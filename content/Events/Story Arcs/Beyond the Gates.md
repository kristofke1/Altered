# Beyond the Gates

**Beyond the Gates** is the first story arc of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], spanning September to December 392 AC. It chronicles the opening of the [[Solstice Gate]], the battle against [[Kraken|The Kraken]], and the reclamation of [[Caer Oorun]]—the first Asgarthan province recovered in over two centuries.

## Overview

After centuries of isolation behind [[The Rampart]], [[Asgartha]] finally launches its great expedition into [[Terra Incognita]]. The arc follows the [[Exalts]] and [[Expeditionary Corps]] as they confront the Kraken—the Leviathan that had devastated Caer Oorun and blocked westward expansion since ~146 AC.

## Timeline

### September 392 AC
- **September 13th**: The [[Solstice Gate]] opens for the first time since the exile of [[Ayxas]], the Mad Kuningas
- [[Tocsin]] sounds his horn as [[Gulrang Oduro]] calls the [[Coalescence]], connecting to all recruits she trained
- The [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] officially launches
- [[The Ouroboros]], converted from the [[Kasirga Clan]]'s Sahanka into a military flagship, serves as mobile command
- The hunt for [[Kraken|The Kraken]] begins across [[Caer Oorun]]

### The Kraken Battle
- Admiral [[Temera Singh]] uses [[The Ouroboros]] itself as bait, racing over churning waves while pursued by the Leviathan
- All six Factions coordinate: [[The Aegis|Aegis]] infantry, [[Ordis]] Scribes, [[Axiom]] sappers, [[Bravos]] fyrds, [[Yzmir]] Battle Mages, [[Muna]] Druids
- [[Muna]] create massive plant cages; [[Yzmir]] attempt a meteor strike
- An unknown saboteur (later revealed as [[Sylas]]) destroys the meteor, freeing the Kraken and killing Battle Mages [[Asta]] and [[Elewa]]
- [[Kaibara]], the whale Leviathan, is summoned by [[Teija]] and engages the Kraken
- [[Kojo Oduro]] gathers massive amounts of [[Mana]], creating localized [[The Tumult|Tumult]] conditions
- [[Afanas Korovinovich]] opens a portal for Kojo to strike from above
- Kojo delivers the killing blow that vaporizes the Kraken entirely

### October 392 AC
- Securing of Oorun, the region directly adjacent to Asgartha
- [[Cebir|The Cebir]] fortress is reclaimed—evidence of esoteric experimentation is discovered within
- [[Wanjiru]] intervenes, leading [[Yzmir]] to quarantine the site

### November 392 AC
- [[Kojo Oduro]] receives the [[Carnelian Crown]], Asgartha's highest honor, from [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]
- [[Gulrang Oduro]] watches her brother's ceremony with pride mixed with fear about his recklessness
- The [[The Age Of Rediscovery Proclamation|Age of Rediscovery]] is officially proclaimed

### December 392 AC
- Systematic exploration of Oorun begins across multiple regions: [[Elikadura Fjords]], [[Strand Region]], [[Irundu]], [[Tilted Steppes]]
- The wreck of [[Mesektet]] is recovered from the Irundu valley by an [[Ordis]] expedition led by [[Gulrang Oduro]]
- Oorun is formally renamed [[Caer Oorun]] as a rightful Asgarthan Province

## Key Revelations

### The Kraken's True Nature
The Kraken left no physical remains after its defeat—no bones, no flesh. [[Suha]] discovered the creature's name originated from a literary source ("Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea") rather than taxonomic classification. In 393 AC, [[Wanjiru]] would propose that the Kraken was not a true [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] but an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoning created by [[The Perjurers]] to prevent Asgarthan expansion.

### The Cebir Secrets
The reclaimed fortress contained evidence of esoteric experimentation hidden for centuries—research protected by the Kraken's presence in the region.

## Aftermath

- Caer Oorun becomes the first province reclaimed in over two centuries
- The Carnelian Crown ceremony marks Kojo as a national hero
- Yzmir Magisters vote 3-2 to add Kraken to their summoning arsenal
- Waru Toowoom begins the lengthy process of reincorporating the province
- The expedition gains a forward base for further westward expansion
