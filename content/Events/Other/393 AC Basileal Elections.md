# 393 AC Basileal Elections

The 393 AC Basileal Elections were held October 17-19, 393 AC to elect a successor to [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]], who resigned after 32 years in office following the launch of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. Over three days, voters cast ballots in six consecutive rounds, with sociologists noting the results marked a potential new paradigm favoring civilian figures over established noble lineages.

## Election Process

Voters cast ballots across six successive rounds, each centered on major themes: exploration, politics, defense, economy, society, and a final candidate preference vote. For each theme, voters chose the idea that resonated most without knowing which candidate proposed it. The winning positions from each ballot became the Republic's official program for the next ten years.

## Candidates

### Somayeh Bahman

Memorialist of the [[Ventauri Clan]]. Advocated for intensifying the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], believing knowledge recovery is "the most precious resource" and that the Endeavor "is not an earthly initiative—it is a spiritual one."

### Bokang Nkoe

[[Muna]] Druid and philosopher of the Issurian school with nearly twenty years of diplomatic experience. Called for pausing the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] until [[Caer Oorun]] became a full Province and establishing citizen council oversight of the [[Expeditionary Corps]].

### Anuncia ruun-Aysun

Direct descendant of [[Aysun]], founder of [[Yzmir]]. Natural successor to [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]], advocating for continued support of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] until the [[Expeditionary Corps]] achieved self-sufficiency. Allied with [[Amaro Arundhani]] as running mate. Supported by the [[Old Nobility]] including [[Vittoria Valiont]] and [[Abenet ruun-Aksoy]].

### Lucas Aveskamp

Direct descendant of [[Geirr Aveskamp]], the fifth Basileus. Fierce opponent of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], advocating for reallocation of [[Expeditionary Corps]] funds to the Asgarthan populace. Gained prominence during the [[Caer Esvander]] suspended mines strike. Chosen as candidate by defectors from [[Amaro Arundhani]]'s camp.

## Ballot Results

### 1st Ballot: Exploration

| Candidate | Position | Votes | Percentage |
|-----------|----------|-------|------------|
| [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] | Continue supporting Endeavor until Corps self-sufficiency | 45 | 39% |
| [[Somayeh Bahman]] | Endeavor is top priority; Asgartha's future lies beyond borders | 40 | 35% |
| [[Lucas Aveskamp]] | Resources allocated to Corps are weakening Asgartha | 17 | 15% |
| [[Bokang Nkoe]] | Pause Endeavor until Oorun becomes full Province | 13 | 11% |

**Winning Position:** Continue supporting the Rediscovery Endeavor until the Expeditionary Corps achieves self-sufficiency within the Tumult.

### 2nd Ballot: Politics

## Adopted Program

The winning positions from each ballot formed the Republic's official program:

- **Exploration:** Continue supporting the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] until the [[Expeditionary Corps]] achieves self-sufficiency within [[The Tumult]]
- **Politics:** Decentralize political and administrative power from [[Arkaster]] to amplify Provincial voices
- **Defence:** Send [[Alterers]] to [[Caer Oorun]] to stabilize the region; the [[The Tumult|Tumult]] remains the true enemy
- **Economy:** Move beyond the monetary system and redefine prosperity as immaterial through education and arts
- **Society:** Environmental protection and ecosystem stabilization; celebration of Rediscovery through grand exhibition

## Election Oversight

[[The Frog Prince]] was appointed by [[Parliament of Ashes|Parliament]] to oversee the elections. All candidates swore an oath before his Eidolon to defend the interests of the [[The Concord|Concord]], [[Asgartha]], and its people.

## Investiture

On October 22, 393 AC, [[Somayeh Bahman]] was sworn in as the fourteenth Basilissa of [[Asgartha]] at [[The Asterion]] in the presence of outgoing Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]].
