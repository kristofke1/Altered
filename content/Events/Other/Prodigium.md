# Prodigium

## Overview

The Prodigium is the grand technological fair of the [[Axiom]] Faction. This recurring exhibition showcases the latest innovations, inventions, and technological achievements developed by the various [[Axiom]] Guilds.

## Purpose

The Prodigium serves multiple functions for the [[Axiom]]:

- Demonstrating new technologies to the public and other Factions
- Celebrating innovation and engineering achievements
- Providing a venue for Guilds to present their work
- Promoting the democratization of technology across [[Asgartha]]

## Upcoming Presentations

### Automata Unveiling

The [[Axiom]] hopes to present their [[Automata]]—humanity's first robotic beings—at the upcoming Prodigium. Several prototypes have already been designed and tested away from public view, with encouraging results. The Faction relies notably on the programming talents of [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] [[Ada Lovelace]] for this ambitious project.

If successful, these new marvels of engineering would mark a significant turning point in the ongoing industrial revolution within the peninsula. [[Automata]] could venture into hostile environments or even within [[The Tumult]] itself, performing tasks too dangerous for humans.

## Significance

The Prodigium represents the [[Axiom]]'s commitment to progress and their belief that technological advancement benefits all of humanity. As the youngest of the six Factions, the [[Axiom]] use the Prodigium to demonstrate their value and establish themselves as equals among the other Factions.

## Relationships

- **[[Axiom]]**: Host Faction organizing the fair
- **[[Ada Lovelace]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] programmer contributing to [[Automata]] development

- **[[The Athanor]]**: Likely venue for the exhibition
