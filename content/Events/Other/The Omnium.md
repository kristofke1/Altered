# The Omnium

## Overview

The Omnium is the annual championship tournament of [[Altrun]], the prestigious competition that determines the best parkour and freerunning team in [[Asgartha]]. The tournament is held in [[Arkaster]] and represents the pinnacle achievement for Altrun practitioners.

## Format

The Omnium features official tournament matches where [[Altrun]] teams compete against each other. The competition culminates in finals where teams vie for the Altrun cup, the highest honor in the sport.

## Significance

Participating in the Omnium is the ultimate goal for Altrun runners. Teams throughout the capital regularly compete in unofficial matches and smaller tournaments, but winning the Omnium brings significant prestige, recognition throughout [[Asgartha]], potential sponsorship opportunities, and status as a top-tier team.

## Notable Competitions

The Jusaka Blaze reached the finals of the Omnium during [[Kojo Oduro]]'s tenure with the team. While they failed to win the cup at that time, team captain [[Carmela]] discussed the possibilities of sponsorship and becoming a top-tier team as their reputation grew.

After [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] departed to join the [[Expeditionary Corps]], he attended the Omnium final as a spectator. On the day of the final, [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] was among the audience watching the competition—he wouldn't have missed the moment for anything in the world.

That day, when [[Carmela]] lifted the Altrun cup in victory, [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] rejoiced and tears of joy ran down his cheeks. He vowed to show the same determination in the wild expanses that his team had shown in winning the championship.

## Timing

The Omnium occurs during the summer season. [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] and [[Booda]] bonded through [[The Musubi]] ceremony the year before his departure, and while his friends competed in the Omnium during that summer, [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] trained relentlessly for his upcoming deployment with the [[Expeditionary Corps]].

## Cultural Impact

The Omnium represents more than just a sporting event—it embodies [[Bravos]] values of daring, athleticism, and pushing boundaries. The championship serves as a proving ground for the faction's young members to demonstrate their skills before potentially joining larger endeavors like the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].
