# The Omnium

## Overview
The Omnium is the annual championship tournament of [[Altrun]], the prestigious competition that determines the best parkour and freerunning team in [[Asgartha]]. The tournament is held in [[Arkaster]] and represents the pinnacle achievement for Altrun practitioners.

## Format
The Omnium features official tournament matches where [[Altrun]] teams compete against each other. The competition culminates in finals where teams vie for the Altrun cup, the highest honor in the sport.

## Significance
Participating in the Omnium is the ultimate goal for Altrun runners. Teams throughout the capital regularly compete in unofficial matches and smaller tournaments, but winning the Omnium brings:
- Significant prestige
- Recognition throughout [[Asgartha]]
- Potential sponsorship opportunities
- Status as a top-tier team

## Notable Competitions
The Jusaka Blaze reached the finals of the Omnium during [[Kojo Oduro]]'s tenure with the team. While they failed to win the cup at that time, team captain [[Carmela]] discussed the possibilities of sponsorship and becoming a top-tier team as their reputation grew.

After [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] departed to join the [[The Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]], he attended the Omnium final as a spectator. On the day of the final, [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] was among the audience watching the competition - he wouldn't have missed the moment for anything in the world.

That day, when [[Carmela]] lifted the Altrun cup in victory, [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] rejoiced and tears of joy ran down his cheeks. He vowed to show the same determination in the wild expanses that his team had shown in winning the championship.

## Timing
The Omnium occurs during the summer season. [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] and [[Booda]] bonded through the [[The Musubi|Musubi]] ceremony the year before his departure, and while his friends competed in the Omnium during that summer, [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] trained relentlessly for his upcoming deployment with the [[The Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]].

## Cultural Impact
The Omnium represents more than just a sporting event - it embodies [[Bravos]] values of daring, athleticism, and pushing boundaries. The championship serves as a proving ground for the faction's young members to demonstrate their skills before potentially joining larger endeavors like the [[The Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]].
