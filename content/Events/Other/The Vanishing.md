# The Vanishing

## Overview
The Vanishing remains one of the most enigmatic mysteries in the history of [[Asgartha]]. In 42 AC, after over forty years of reign, [[Rune]], the first High King of Asgartha, vanished without a trace, as if by magic.

## The Disappearance
His quarters were found empty, his rooms abandoned. The [[Phoenix]] had just perished in one of its cyclical deaths, and its egg, which had reformed from its own ashes, was waiting on its bed of embers. His Consort, [[Ceren]], had no idea where he could be.

At the same time, the absence of three of the Rune Knights, his friends and closest lieutenants, was noted: [[Aksoy]], [[Aldana]], and [[Heeren]] had also disappeared, taking their weapons and armor with them, just like the High King. There was therefore no evidence to suspect any foul play.

## The Investigation
An inquiry commission was appointed under the authority of [[Tamrat]], one of Rune's close companions. [[Tamrat]] sent search parties to all corners of [[Caer Eidos]] to ensure no mishap had befallen the monarch and his close guard. He personally questioned [[Ceren]], [[Aysun]], [[Heshkari]], and [[Caellach]], encouraged the palace staff to provide testimonies or potential leads.

### Tamrat's Findings
Although he did not fully uncover the truth of the events, [[Tamrat]] did not return empty-handed. He discovered that:
- Other individuals had disappeared at the same time as [[Rune]]: a few sailors, as well as soldiers from the [[Elysium|Elysian palace]]
- A masked buyer had purchased a small caravel
- On the morning of the disappearance, a group of about fifteen hooded individuals had set sail westward

[[Tamrat]] revealed his findings to [[Aysun]], Rune's daughter. After their conversation, he went to [[Ceren]] to tell her that her Consort, feeling he was at the twilight of his life, had gone to challenge [[The Tumult]] one final time, never to return.

## The Official Story
The next day, after months of uncertainty, black banners were hoisted on the high towers of the [[Elysium]]. Trumpets and bells resounded in a somber lament. The death of Asgartha's first Kuningas was announced with great pomp. [[Ceren]] and [[Aysun]], dressed in black and in mourning, bowed before an empty urn and invited the populace to mobilize for the upcoming elections.

Several months of mourning were decreed in homage to the hero of [[The Concord]]. After this interval, [[Cwaith]], an administrator trained by [[Caellach]], inherited the heavy burden of succeeding him. In 42 AC, she became the first Kuningatar of Asgartha, the first High Queen of the nation.

## Alternative Theories
Many other rumors challenge the official version of events:
- It is said that [[Rune]] and [[Aysun]] had a violent altercation the night before his disappearance, on the heights of the [[Sekent Isle]]
- It is also said that on his deathbed, [[Tamrat]] begged forgiveness for the secret he took to the grave

## The Phoenix Egg Mystery
Following [[Rune]]'s disappearance, the [[Phoenix Egg]] refused to hatch again. 350 years later, it continues to be incubated within a large brazier, protected by the [[Bravos]].

A persistent legend within the [[Bravos|Faction]] says that the Firebird will only reappear when Rune's true heir is found. This is why many Bravos have volunteered to participate in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. With a bit of luck and perseverance, they hope to find traces of the first sovereign of Asgartha, and who knows, perhaps even his tomb and those of his companions.
