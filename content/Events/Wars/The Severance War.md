# The Severance War

## Overview
The Severance War was a five-year civil war in early 3rd century AC during [[Marcus Sune]]'s reign as Basileus. It pitted the Governors of [[Asgartha]]'s Provinces against the central authority of [[Arkaster]], and was secretly fueled by [[The Isfet]], a chaos sect seeking to destabilize Asgarthan society.

## Background: Rise of the Governors

### Growing Power
The beginning of the third century was marked by the gradual rise of the Governors, who were tasked with developing Asgartha's Provinces. They were granted near full regional powers, which allowed them to amass wealth and influence to the point where they eventually challenged the authority of the Basileus.

### Economic Development
As infrastructures and cities developed and regional economies grew, so did the Governors' personal ambitions.

## Outbreak of War

### Marcus Sune's Reforms
When [[Marcus Sune]], Asgartha's Basileus, sought to reform the government towards a more democratic model, the Governors, feeling threatened, rebelled against the central authority.

### The Governors' Claim
They claimed that Sune's objective was to strip the regional powers of their autonomy and restore [[Arkaster]] as the sole seat of power. This led to a civil war that lasted five years, culminating in the Governors' armies marching on the capital.

## Wanjiru's Investigation

### Initial Position
At the time, when conscription began, [[Wanjiru]] was a young Horomancer stationed in the province of [[Caer Arcolano]]. Although the [[Yzmir]] declared their neutrality in the conflict, Wanjiru pledged allegiance to [[Telmo]], the Governor of Caer Arcolano, whom he knew was driven by noble intentions.

### Suspicions
However, as the situation worsened, Wanjiru realized that something was amiss, as if someone or something was pulling the strings from behind the scenes. He secretly began investigating, patiently listening, watching for signs, and spying when necessary.

### Discovery
As he probed further and further, he uncovered a terrible conspiracy deeply rooted in the political spheres of both the loyalists and the insurgents. A previously unknown faction sought to destabilize Asgarthan society to its core. Wherever possible, its infiltrated agents stoked the flames of discord and violence, worsening the war.

## The Yzmir Intervention

### Alert
Despite his status as an apostate, Wanjiru managed to alert the [[Yzmir]] Magisters, who took the threat more than seriously.

### Halting the Battle
Just as the armies were about to clash on the battlefield, the [[Yzmir]] intervened, showcasing their supremacy by halting the advancing troops.

### Strike Teams
Simultaneously, Wanjiru and small strike teams targeted individuals identified as agitators—high-ranking officials within both the loyalist administration and the rebel coalition.

## The Isfet Conspiracy

### Interrogation
After being interrogated in secret by the [[Ordis]], the conspirators revealed they belonged to [[The Isfet]], a sect devoted to chaos.

### Goals
Their ultimate goal was to let [[The Tumult]] triumph, by fostering disorganization within [[Asgartha]] by any means necessary.

### Resolution
Peace was eventually restored, and the traitors were publicly tried and imprisoned, though not all the details of their actions were fully revealed.

## Aftermath

### Wanjiru's Concerns
Celebrated as a hero and a key figure in the reconciliation, Wanjiru, however, remained uneasy. He came to believe that [[The Isfet]] had been backed by outside forces.

### Foundation of the Qorgan
The Horomancer decided to dedicate his life to uncovering the true masterminds behind the conflict. To protect [[Asgartha]], he founded [[The Qorgan]], an [[Yzmir]] cell specialized in tracking and eliminating the nation's enemies.
