# Battle of Madan Bay

## Overview

The Battle of Madan Bay was a coordinated assault by all six [[Factions]] of the [[Expeditionary Corps]] against [[Kraken|the Kraken]] at [[Caer Oorun]] in September 392 AC. The battle resulted in the Kraken's complete destruction by [[Kojo Oduro]] and the subsequent reclamation of the province, ending over two centuries of Leviathan occupation. The engagement was sabotaged by an unknown attacker later identified as [[Sylas]], who destroyed a [[Yzmir]] meteor strike and freed the Kraken from containment.

## Background

The [[Kraken]] had occupied [[Caer Oorun]] since approximately 146 AC after defeating [[Kaibara]] in a prolonged territorial battle that devastated the province and forced evacuation of its population. The [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] launched in September 392 AC with the opening of the [[Solstice Gate]], and the Kraken's elimination was prerequisite to westward expansion.

## The Battle

Grand Admiral [[Temera Singh]] used [[The Ouroboros]] as bait, racing over churning waves to lure the Kraken into position. All six Factions coordinated in the assault: [[The Aegis|Aegis]] infantry, [[Ordis]] Scribes, [[Axiom]] sappers, [[Bravos]] fyrds, [[Yzmir]] Battle Mages, and [[Muna]] Druids.

[[Muna]] forces created massive plant cages from seed bags deployed in the bay to contain the Kraken while [[Yzmir]] Battle Mages attempted to call down a meteor strike. An unknown saboteur destroyed the meteor, freeing the Kraken from the plant prison. The sabotage killed Battle Mages [[Asta]] and [[Elewa]], and [[Matera]] sacrificed herself protecting [[Afanas Korovinovich]] during the chaos.

[[Teija]] summoned [[Kaibara]], the whale Leviathan, who engaged the Kraken directly. [[Kojo Oduro]] gathered massive amounts of [[Mana]], creating localized [[The Tumult|Tumult]] conditions. [[Afanas Korovinovich]] opened a dimensional portal for Kojo to strike from above. Kojo delivered a blow that vaporized the Kraken entirely, leaving no physical remains—vitrified sand where the Leviathan had stood and nothing else.

## Aftermath

[[Kojo Oduro]] received the [[Carnelian Crown]], Asgartha's highest military honor, from Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] in November 392 AC. The [[The Age Of Rediscovery Proclamation|Age of Rediscovery]] was formally proclaimed the same month. [[Caer Oorun]] was secured and systematically explored, with [[Cebir|the Cebir]] fortress reclaimed in October revealing evidence of esoteric experimentation hidden for centuries.

The Kraken's complete vaporization—leaving no remains—later contributed to [[Suha]]'s investigation concluding the creature was an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] rather than a true [[Leviathans|Leviathan]], a theory supported by [[Wanjiru]] in 393 AC. [[The Qorgan]] maintained surveillance on Kojo due to concerns about the nature of his strike, which [[Suha]] characterized as stripping reality of its essence rather than the transient fire affinity typical of [[Bravos]].
