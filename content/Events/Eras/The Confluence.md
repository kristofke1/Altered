# The Confluence

## Overview

The Confluence was a planetary cataclysm approximately five centuries before 392 AC, born from the collision of reality and the world of imagination. The event leveled entire cities and brought down human nations. It reshaped reality in a chaotic manner, causing widespread destruction.

## Aftermath

In the aftermath, new life forms emerged and new landscapes unfolded—a blend of the real and the imaginary. The world was transformed, rendered strange and often hostile, altered to the point of becoming unrecognizable.

## Legacy

The Confluence left behind [[The Tumult]], a lasting but poorly understood phenomenon that continues to mutate the world. It also led to the discovery of [[Mana]] and the emergence of [[Oases]] as safe zones for human survival. The period following the Confluence became known as [[The Great Wandering]].

## Potential Recurrence

According to [[Sylas]], a second Confluence could be triggered by those who still possess access to the [[The Spheres|Sixth Sphere]]—a banished power that some practitioners have preserved despite its prohibition.
