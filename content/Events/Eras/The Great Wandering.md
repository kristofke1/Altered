# The Great Wandering

## Overview

The Great Wandering was a troubled era lasting between one hundred and one hundred and fifty years following [[The Confluence]]. During this period, few cared to measure time with precision, making exact duration uncertain. The era ended with [[The Concord]] and founding of [[Asgartha]].

## The Tumult Nomads

The few survivors of [[The Confluence]] gathered into large Tribes that roamed the world, traveling from [[Oases|Oasis]] to Oasis, from places shielded from [[The Tumult]] to makeshift shelters. Historians assert that at least eight Tribes existed—eclectic gatherings of peoples and communities brought together by fate.

## Terra Incognita

The Tribes roamed the now fabled [[Terra Incognita]], encountering:
- Wonderful lands and moors of unreal beauty
- Baroque palaces of improbable architecture
- Desolated places gripped by chaos, where ice burns and mountains float above rivers of obsidian

## Discovery of Alteration

The wisest among the survivors began to perceive the flows of [[The Tumult]], gradually discovering the secrets of [[Alteration]]. By developing this ability, some managed to relatively shape their environment and materialize their imagination.

[[Alteration]] could fend off [[The Tumult]] to some extent, providing humanity with new solutions for ensuring its safety. Foremost among these solutions were the [[Eidolons]], creatures from the collective unconscious that [[Alterers]] could manifest in reality to aid them.

## Role of the Oneiroi

The [[Oneiroi]], through dreams, trained the first [[Alterers]] by guiding them from the edge of their subconscious. They ultimately urged humanity towards a great migration, to a promised land, towards rebirth—leading to the founding of [[Asgartha]].
