# Age of Concord

## Overview

The Age of Concord was the era spanning from the founding of [[Asgartha]] in 0 AC to the proclamation of the [[Age of Rediscovery]] in 392 AC. This period marked the blossoming of civilization and its enduring establishment, though it saw dark hours and bloody periods.

## Beginning

The Age of Concord began with [[The Concord]], when [[Rune]] proclaimed the treaty binding all Tumult Nomad tribes into one people. The [[Phoenix]] crowned Rune as first Kuningas at [[Kuningasplatz]], and the [[Trusted Council]] of [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]] was established to govern the new nation.

## Major Periods

### Rune's Reign (0-42 AC)

[[Rune]] governed [[Asgartha]] for over forty years, surrounded by the [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]]. The era ended with [[The Vanishing]] when Rune and three Runic Knights disappeared mysteriously.

### Cwaith's Reign (42-70 AC)

[[Cwaith]], the first Kuningatar, succeeded Rune and focused on strengthening the existing colony rather than expansion. Living conditions for Asgarthans greatly improved during her reign.

### The Torment (70-86 AC)

[[The Torment]] was a dark period of tyrannical rule under [[Ayxas]], marked by the creation of [[The Castigar]] and [[The Magan]], suppression of the Factions, and eventual uprising that restored order.

### Stabilization and Growth (86-392 AC)

Following The Torment, [[Asgartha]] entered a long period of gradual stabilization and growth. The six Factions ([[Axiom]], [[Bravos]], [[Lyra]], [[Muna]], [[Ordis]], [[Yzmir]]) developed their distinct roles in society. Various conflicts occurred including [[The Severance War]], [[The Three Factions War]], and [[The Fratricidal War]].

## End

The Age of Concord ended in 392 AC when [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] proclaimed the [[Age of Rediscovery]], launching the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] to explore [[Terra Incognita]] beyond Asgartha's borders.

## Legacy

The dating system [[Anno Concordiae]] (AC) commemorates this era, with all Asgarthan dates calculated from the founding moment. The Concord itself became the foundation of Asgarthan society—a commitment to cooperation for the common good.
