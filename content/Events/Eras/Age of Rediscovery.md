# Age of Rediscovery

## Overview

The Age of Rediscovery is the current era beginning in 392 AC, proclaimed by [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] with the launch of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. After centuries of isolation within [[Asgartha]], humanity ventures beyond its borders to explore [[Terra Incognita]] and reclaim territories affected by [[The Tumult]].

## Proclamation

In 392 AC, [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] delivered [[The Age of Rediscovery Proclamation]], declaring:

> "Let the horns sound to announce the beginning of the Age of Rediscovery!"

The proclamation followed twenty-four years of preparation for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], involving all six Factions.

## Goals

The [[Expeditionary Corps]] was formed with the following objectives:
- Venture beyond the Known World
- Find other [[Oases]]
- Locate other [[The Oneiroi|Oneiros]]
- Potentially discover other human survivors
- Possibly find the source of [[The Tumult]] to end it

## Timeline

### 392 AC

- **September**: Opening of the [[Solstice Gate]] and launch of the Rediscovery Endeavor; [[Kraken]] defeated
- **October**: Securing of Oorun region; [[Cebir]] reclaimed
- **November**: [[Kojo Oduro]] receives [[Carnelian Crown]]; Proclamation of the Age of Rediscovery
- **December**: Exploration of Oorun begins; [[Mesektet]] wreck recovered; region renamed [[Caer Oorun]]

### 393 AC

- **January**: Expeditionary Corps reaches [[Storhvit]]; [[Belisenki]] attacks begin
- **February**: Exploration of Storvhit and [[Cais Adarra]] mountain range
- **March**: Tumult hurricane threatens forces; discovery of [[Mana Moths]]
- **April**: Discovery of [[The Nilam]], a dead world-tree imprisoning [[Kuraokami]]
- **May**: [[Kuraokami]] freed; Storvhit renamed [[Caer Nilam]]; [[City of Scholars]] located

## Legacy

The Age of Rediscovery represents a turning point for humanity—the transition from survival within protected borders to active exploration and expansion. The era marks renewed hope for understanding and potentially ending [[The Tumult]].
