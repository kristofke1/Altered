# Mesektet Conspiracy

## Overview

The Mesektet Conspiracy refers to a sealed conspiracy connected to the destruction of [[Mesektet]] in approximately 340 AC. While the crash was officially attributed to an attack by [[Cingula]], [[Bai Shan-shu]]'s deathbed confession to his daughter [[Zhen]] revealed that he had caused the ship's fall and was responsible for all the deaths—including that of his wife [[Madelyn Moss]], who died in an explosion on the scientific bridge.

The admiralty has sealed all records concerning both the Mesektet crash and activities at [[Cebir|The Cebir]], where Madelyn conducted [[Chimerae|Chimera]] research from 327-329 AC.

## Known Participants

### Confirmed Visitors

Through [[The Musubi]] with [[Zephyr]], [[Zhen]] witnessed fragmentary memories identifying three high-ranking officials who regularly visited the Moss-Shan-shu home in [[Hadera]] to discuss secret projects behind closed doors:

- **[[Temera Singh]]** - Now Grand Admiral commanding the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]
- **[[de Graaf]]** - Now General serving aboard [[The Ouroboros]]
- **[[Basem Falani]]** - Guildmaster of the [[Lapidaries' Guild]]; remained accessible in [[Asgartha]]

### Central Figures

- **[[Madelyn Moss]]** - Scientist specializing in Chimera research; conducted experiments on [[Zephyr]] involving Mana siphoning; ordered aboard Mesektet to test an invention; died in the crash
- **[[Bai Shan-shu]]** - [[Tagmata]] officer; used Zephyr's abilities to save crew members during the crash, earning the Purple Star; confessed on his deathbed to causing the tragedy

## Timeline

### 327-329 AC - The Cebir Period

[[Madelyn Moss]] and [[Bai Shan-shu]] served at [[Cebir|The Cebir]], an outpost functioning as both military fortress and experimental laboratory. Madelyn conducted research on [[Chimerae|Chimeras]], including [[Zephyr]]. The base was abandoned in 329 AC.

### 329-340 AC - Hadera Period

The couple settled in [[Hadera]], [[Caer Esvander]], where [[Zhen]] was born in 330 AC. Madelyn continued experiments on Zephyr in her workshop, involving Mana siphoning in vats. High-ranking admiralty officials (Singh, de Graaf, Falani) visited regularly to discuss classified projects, creating tension between Madelyn and Shan-shu.

### ~340 AC - The Mesektet Crash

Madelyn received orders to board [[Mesektet]] to test her invention. Shan-shu's [[Tagmata]] squadron escorted the mothership. During an attack attributed to [[Cingula]], the ship went down. Madelyn died in an explosion on the scientific bridge. Shan-shu saved many crew members using Zephyr's abilities.

### Post-Crash - Cover-Up

Shan-shu received the Purple Star, one of the highest Asgarthan military distinctions, and rose to naval high command. The admiralty sealed all records concerning the crash. Shan-shu remained distant from his daughter, drinking heavily after the memorial service.

### 364 AC - Shan-shu Becomes Exalt

[[Avkan ruun-Heshkari|Basileus Avkan]] requested Shan-shu undergo [[The Musubi]] with Zephyr, making him one of the first Exalts. His health declined afterward.

### ~393 AC - Deathbed Confession

On his deathbed, Shan-shu confessed to Zhen that he had caused the Mesektet's crash and was responsible for all the deaths. He stated his life had been lived in imposture and lies, and that he deserved none of the honors he received. When Zhen asked why, he mentioned Madelyn's name but refused to elaborate.

### Post-Confession - Investigation Begins

After Shan-shu's death, Zephyr began dying from the broken Musubi bond. Zhen performed a second Musubi to save the Chimera. Through this bonding, Zephyr's memories transferred to Zhen, revealing the experiments, her father's episodes of despair, and the faces of the conspiracy's visitors.

Zhen applied to become an Exalt and join the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], positioning herself to investigate. Singh now commands the Endeavor with de Graaf at her side, making both difficult to approach. Only Falani remained accessible in Asgartha.

## What Remains Unknown

- The nature of Madelyn Moss's invention
- Why Shan-shu caused the crash
- The full scope of the conspiracy's purpose
- The connection between Madelyn's Chimera research and the sealed projects
- Whether the conspiracy connects to other known plots (such as [[Sylas]]'s experiments at the Cebir)

## Sealed Records

The admiralty has sealed information concerning:
- The Mesektet crash
- Activities at The Cebir
- Details discovered in the Mesektet wreckage (recovered 392 AC)
