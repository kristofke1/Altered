# Ryūkkōsai

## Overview

The Ryūkkōsai is a spring festival organized by the [[Muna]] on the peninsula of [[Kirighai]]. During the festival, [[The Musubi]] bonding ceremonies may be celebrated throughout the peninsula, outside the usual venue of the [[Bark Refuge]]. The festival also honors historical figures including [[Yong-Su Tenzing]] and [[Eru]] for their roles in saving [[Asgartha]]'s capital.

## Relationships

- **[[Muna]]**: Faction that organizes the festival
- **[[Kirighai]]**: Peninsula where the festival takes place
- **[[The Musubi]]**: Bonding ceremony that may be celebrated during the festival
- **[[Yong-Su Tenzing]]**: Historical figure honored during the Ryūkkōsai
- **[[Eru]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] whose statue is honored on the first islet of the [[Mugen]] during the Midori Matsuri processions at the festival
