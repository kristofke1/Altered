# The Age of Rediscovery Proclamation

## Overview

On 392 AC, [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] delivered a proclamation speech announcing the launch of [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] and declaring the beginning of the [[Age of Rediscovery]].

## Context

Twenty-four years prior to this proclamation, the Basileus had requested an extended mandate to realize an ambitious project of unprecedented scale. This speech marked the culmination of those preparations.

## Historical Summary

The Basileus's speech recounted the entire history of [[Asgartha]]:

### The Confluence

Five centuries prior (approximately 367 AC), humanity lived in a golden age with ships connecting distant lands and machines powered by steam and gears. [[The Confluence]] struck suddenly without warning, tearing open reality and unleashing [[The Aether]] from [[The Empyrean]]. This planetary devastation altered everything in its path.

### Age of Tumult

Following the Confluence, [[The Tumult]] was born—destructive currents that relentlessly altered everything. Survivors knew nothing of [[Alteration]] initially. They witnessed loved ones transform uncontrollably, parasitized by volatile ideas. The [[Tumult Nomads]] emerged, learning to detect the arrival of the Tumult and seek shelter in [[Oases]].

### The Great Migrations

[[The Oneiroi]] contacted human communities and offered the secrets of Alteration as a weapon against the Tumult. They convinced survivors to unite. Eight major human tribes converged on what would become [[Asgartha]]. One tribe chose not to stay, another was lost to the Tumult, and the remaining six settled in the peninsula.

### The Concord

Within Asgartha, all tribes bound themselves into a single people under the patronage of the Oneiroi. They proclaimed [[The Concord]]—the unbreakable alliance of human survivors. This became the founding pillar of society.

### Age of Concord

The Age of Concord was the blossoming of civilization and its enduring establishment, though it saw dark hours and bloody periods.

## The Proclamation

The Basileus declared that with Asgartha now stable and secure, it was time to turn outward toward [[Terra Incognita]]. All six [[Factions]] participated in preparing the endeavor:

- [[Bravos]] and [[Ordis]]: Trained heroes and explorers for over twenty years
- [[Axiom]]: Engineers worked on machines to facilitate expeditions
- [[Yzmir]]: Mages prepared to study unknown phenomena
- [[Lyra]] and [[Muna]]: Ensured the adventure could evolve autonomously

## The Mission

The [[Expeditionary Corps]] was formed to venture beyond the Known World, find other [[Oases]], locate other [[The Oneiroi|Oneiroi]], potentially discover other human survivors, and possibly find the source of the Tumult to end it.

## Declaration

The Basileus solemnly proclaimed the launch of the Rediscovery Endeavor and declared the beginning of the [[Age of Rediscovery]].
