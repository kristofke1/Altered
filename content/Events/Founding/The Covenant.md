# The Covenant

## Overview
The Covenant is the treaty through which the [[Factions]] committed to set aside their disputes and cooperate for the common good.

## Significance
This agreement has become the foundation of the Asgarthan civilization: a non-aggression pact providing a sound basis from which to build a society capable of opposing [[The Tumult]].

## Historical Context
In the past, the Factions sometimes violently opposed each other, even threatening the survival of [[Altered/Locations/Nations/Asgartha|Asgartha]]. The Covenant was established to prevent such conflicts from endangering civilization again.

## Impact
Since its promulgation, [[Altered/Locations/Nations/Asgartha|Asgartha]] has entered an era of peace and prosperity.

## Current Tensions
However, the Factions continue to harbor different views on what needs to be accomplished in the medium and long term. The Covenant maintains peace while allowing these philosophical differences to persist.
