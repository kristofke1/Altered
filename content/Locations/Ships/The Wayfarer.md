# The Wayfarer

## Overview

The Wayfarer is a [[Sahanka]] serving as home to the [[Tisdhera Clan]], one of the nine [[Lyra]] Sarabands. The floating city resembles a giant circus or theme park suspended in the sky.

## Architecture

The Wayfarer presents a chaotic jumble of mismatched shacks and colorful roofs. Helium balloons of various shapes and sizes drift from the structure, including a giant clown face so huge it appears almost grotesque, blue whales, clownfish, and ridiculous chicks. At the top of the floating city sits a cluster of mobiles and orreries, crowned with a crescent moon that appears gilded in gold leaf. Massive tents swell like lungs, and gaudy weather vanes glitter under sunlight. The patchwork of structures appears as though a fairground had broken free from gravity itself, accompanied by madcap tunes drifting through the air.

## Transportation

Gondolas resembling wicker baskets descend from The Wayfarer on ropes, allowing passengers and goods to travel between the Sahanka and locations below.

## Artistic Specialty

The [[Tisdhera Clan]] aboard The Wayfarer is known for painters, clowns, tragicomic skits, tightrope acts, goofy scenes, and sleight-of-hand shows. Painters splatter the world with wild pigments during their performances.

## Role in the Rediscovery Endeavor

### 393 AC, July - Arrival at The Screed

The Wayfarer followed a brief opening in [[The Tumult]] from the southeast to join the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] at [[The Screed]]. The Sahanka positioned itself near [[The Ouroboros]], and the two cities exchanged signals of light before gondolas descended from The Wayfarer to the encampment.

### Secret Mission: Phoenix Egg Transport

The Wayfarer serves as the transport vessel for the [[Phoenix Egg]], stolen from [[Haven]] as part of [[Rune's Testament]]. [[Bravos]] loyal to the Testament secretly joined the [[Tisdhera Clan]] aboard the Sahanka, carrying the sacred artifact westward toward the Phoenix's nest.

According to the Testament, the egg must reach the west so the [[Phoenix]] can be rekindled and reborn. The [[Phoenixian Guard]] orchestrated this covert operation, using The Wayfarer's cover as a [[Lyra]] entertainment vessel to mask the true nature of its cargo.

[[Sitina]], a [[Phoenixian Guard]] Blade Dancer, revealed this arrangement in 393 AC to [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Kojo Oduro]] after they discovered [[Rune]]'s scroll in the [[City of Scholars]].

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

The Wayfarer served as one of the four main shielded ships traversing [[The Turmoil]], constantly escorted by the [[Ordis]] flagship vessels [[Mesektet]] and [[Mandjet]]. The Sahanka sustained only minor degradation from mutagenic winds due to its effective shielding.
