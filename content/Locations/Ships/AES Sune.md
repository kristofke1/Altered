# AES Sune

## Overview

AES Sune was an [[Ordis]] cruiser equipped with kelonic engines, turbines, and articulated steel landing struts. The vessel was destroyed by [[Halua]] in the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]] on October 23, 393 AC.

## Design

The ship featured kelonic engines, turbines, and articulated steel landing struts that retracted during flight.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, January - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

AES Sune joined the Asgarthan armada during the fleet launch, taking position alongside fellow [[Ordis]] vessels [[Mandjet]] and [[Mesektet]] in the flotilla formation.

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

AES Sune served as one of the cruisers in the 18-ship armada crossing [[The Turmoil]] under [[Expeditionary Corps]] authority.

### 393 AC, October 23 - Destruction by Halua

[[Halua]] surged from the [[Cloudsea]] and deliberately struck AES Sune with its wing, tearing through the hull and shattering the central tower. The cruiser caught fire and sank slowly into the clouds before exploding upon contact with [[The Tumult]]. More than three hundred people died in the attack, including officers, soldiers, civilians, and families. The destruction prompted [[Sol]] and other [[Bravos]] hunters to rally for a retaliatory hunt against the Leviathan.
