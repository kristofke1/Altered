# Pantagruel

## Overview

The Pantagruel is an [[Axiom]] supertanker used for ore collection and transport during [[Cloud Realm]] mining operations. It requires escort vessels during operations.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Ore Collection in Cloud Realm

The Pantagruel conducted ore collection operations in the [[Cloud Realm]], escorted by [[AES Kurush]] and other vessels.

## Relationships

- **[[AES Kurush]]**: [[Axiom]] vessel that escorted the Pantagruel (393 AC)
- **[[Isaree Thongsuk]]**: [[Axiom]] Exalt who served aboard escort vessels during Pantagruel operations (393 AC)
