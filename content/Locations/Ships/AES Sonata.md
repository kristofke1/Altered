# AES Sonata

## Overview

AES Sonata is a vessel in the Asgarthan Exploration Ship fleet, part of the [[Expeditionary Corps]] armada crossing [[The Turmoil]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Whiplash Crossing

During the fleet's three-hour crossing of [[The Whiplash]], AES Sonata's port side became dangerously exposed to the cyclone's winds, prompting a support request to Admiral [[Temera Singh]] aboard [[The Ouroboros]], who adjusted the fleet's vector to provide cover.

## Relationships

- **[[Expeditionary Corps]]**: Force the vessel was assigned to
- **[[Temera Singh]]**: Admiral who repositioned the fleet to assist (393 AC)
- **[[The Ouroboros]]**: Flagship that adjusted course to shield the Sonata
- **[[The Whiplash]]**: Cyclone during whose crossing the vessel required support
