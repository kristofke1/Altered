# AES Kurush

## Overview

The AES Kurush is an [[Axiom]] vessel that serves escort duty for larger ships during [[Cloud Realm]] operations. It refuels at restocking stations positioned at regular intervals throughout the fleet's operational area.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Pantagruel Escort Mission

The AES Kurush escorted the supertanker [[Pantagruel]] during ore collection operations, docking at a restocking station to refuel [[Kelon]] reserves after several days of escort duty.
