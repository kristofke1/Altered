# AES Concord

## Overview

AES Concord is a cruiser in the Asgarthan Exploration Ship fleet, placed under [[Expeditionary Corps]] authority for the journey toward the second light signal.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

AES Concord suffered severe damage when a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] pocket exploded at the cloud surface during the fleet's crossing of [[The Turmoil]]. The vessel's hull sustained heavy corrosion from the mutagenic burst, forcing it to turn back for repairs while the remaining 17 ships continued toward their destination.
