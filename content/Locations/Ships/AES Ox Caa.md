# AES Ox Caa

## Overview

AES Ox Caa is an escort ship in the [[Asgarthan Navy]] with a distinctive copper hull that gleams like chitin.

## Design

The ship features a copper hull that gleams like the chitin of an immense insect in sunlight.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, January - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

AES Ox Caa served as an escort ship during the Asgarthan armada launch, hovering above [[Mandjet]]'s main deck where [[Tagmata]] troops and Templars stood in formation.

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

AES Ox Caa accompanied the 18-ship armada crossing [[The Turmoil]] as one of the escort ships under [[Expeditionary Corps]] authority, positioned above the heavy ships alongside [[AES Benares]] to serve as observation posts.

## Relationships

- **[[Mandjet]]**: Escorted the Day Barque during fleet launch (393 AC)
- **[[Ordis]]**: Affiliated faction
- **[[The Turmoil]]**: Region crossed with fleet (October 393 AC)
- **[[AES Benares]]**: Fellow escort ship in the armada
