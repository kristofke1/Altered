# Mesektet

## Overview

Mesektet, also known as the Night Barque, is one of two flagship vessels that patrolled the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan]] Protectorate alongside [[Mandjet]], the Day Barque. The ship features an edged profile and served as a mobile defense platform for the [[Ordis]] faction, carrying [[Alterers]] and Tagmata troops to repel [[The Tumult]] and defend against creatures riding its currents.

## The Crash (~340 AC)

The Night Barque was lost during an incursion by [[Cingula]], a Leviathan that regularly harasses the peninsula. Unlike [[Mandjet]], which survived with damage, Mesektet crashed within [[The Tumult]] in the [[Irundu]] region of [[Caer Oorun]] province, approximately 150 miles from [[Arkaster]].

[[Madelyn Moss]], a scientist specializing in [[Chimerae|Chimera]] research, was aboard Mesektet to test an invention when the ship went down. She died in an explosion on the ship's scientific bridge. Her husband [[Bai Shan-shu]], whose [[Tagmata]] squadron escorted the mothership, used [[Zephyr]]'s abilities to save many crew members, earning the Purple Star for his heroism.

Decades later, on his deathbed, Shan-shu confessed to his daughter [[Zhen]] that he had caused the Mesektet's crash and was responsible for all the deaths. The admiralty sealed all records concerning the incident.

## The Wreck

The ship crashed nose-first into a mountainside with stern protruding and debris trailing down the slope. The command deck was partially preserved but half-claimed by jungle vegetation, with the communications room overrun by roots and granite outgrowths. Crew casualties were found in various states—crystallized, mineralized, or overgrown—with humanoid forms fused with plant and stone at consoles. The black box was missing from the cockpit compartment.

During investigation, granite formations on the command deck were initially misidentified as evidence of betrayal, but [[Nosk]] corrected this through [[Coalescence]]—roots had shifted the formations and what appeared to be a blade was geological protrusion. The admiralty has sealed records of what was discovered in the wreckage.

## Recovery Operations (392 AC)

In November 392 AC, [[Gulrang Oduro]] led an [[Ordis]] expedition through [[Irundu]] forest to locate the crashed vessel. [[Nosk]] broadcast the discovery through [[The Gestalt]].

On November 18, 392 AC, [[Waru Toowoom]] examined pre-Confluence city ruins in [[Irundu]], though [[Ordis]] dignitaries prioritized securing Mesektet over archaeological research. On December 11, 392 AC, Waru traveled with dignitaries to join [[Axiom]] forces at the crash site. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] insisted on a security escort.

The Aerolith core had been severely damaged, making the vessel unlikely to be operational for weeks. Recovery was broadcast through [[The Gestalt]] for propaganda purposes.

### 393 AC - Refit and Alteration Maintenance

[[Sylas]] assessed Mesektet's damage as minor, declaring the vessel available to join the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] alongside [[The Ouroboros]], [[The Wayfarer]], and [[Mandjet]]. Technicians at the [[City of Scholars]] outpost began studying the turbines to determine feasibility of converting to a Kelon-[[Sap]] fuel mix. [[Kaaba]] assigned [[Gulrang Oduro]] to maintain the ship as a remote headquarters using [[Alteration]], simulating dormitories, mess halls, and armories, with [[Tocsin]] channeling [[Mana]] from distant sources to sustain the effort.

### 393 AC, January - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

Mesektet led the Asgarthan armada under [[Amiens]]' command during the fleet launch to investigate a pillar of light signal over [[The Tumult]]. The Night Barque's dark hull appeared to absorb light that touched it. [[Zhen]] observed the ship rise from the clouds, identifying it as the site where her mother died and where sealed records of the conspiracy are stored.

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

Mesektet served as one of the four main shielded ships traversing [[The Turmoil]], constantly escorting [[The Ouroboros]] and [[The Wayfarer]] alongside [[Mandjet]]. The Night Barque sustained only minor degradation from mutagenic winds due to its effective shielding.

### 393 AC - Arrival at Asty

Mesektet was among the five vessels armed and prepared to fire during first contact with the [[The Reka|Reka]] people at [[Asty]], alongside [[The Ouroboros]], [[Mandjet]], [[AES Horizon]], and [[AES Tempest|Tempest]].
