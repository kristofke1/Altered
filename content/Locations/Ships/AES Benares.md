# AES Benares

## Overview

AES Benares is an escort ship in the Asgarthan Exploration Ship fleet, placed under [[Expeditionary Corps]] authority for the journey toward the second light signal.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

AES Benares accompanied the 18-ship armada crossing [[The Turmoil]] as one of the escort ships, positioned above the heavy ships alongside [[AES Ox Caa]] to serve as observation posts and shelter the fleet from potential harm.

## Relationships

- **[[Ordis]]**: Affiliated faction
- **[[Expeditionary Corps]]**: Force the vessel was assigned to
- **[[The Turmoil]]**: Region crossed with fleet (October 393 AC)
- **[[AES Ox Caa]]**: Fellow escort ship in the armada
- **[[Mandjet]]**: Flagship the escort ships supported
