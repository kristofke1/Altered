# AES Tempest

## Overview

AES Tempest is the largest military vessel in the Asgarthan Exploration Ship fleet, a renowned battleship placed under [[Expeditionary Corps]] authority for the journey toward the second light signal.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

AES Tempest accompanied the 18-ship armada crossing [[The Turmoil]], serving as the fleet's primary battleship and heaviest military asset during the hazardous journey through the cloud sea.

### 393 AC, October - Halua Trap Operation

AES Tempest served as bait to lure [[Halua]] into a trap at a floating archipelago. The battleship fired salvos to provoke the Leviathan, then led it into a depression while ejecting rows of [[Aerolith]] barrels that deployed as a mine field. [[Maanus]] coordinated the attack from the deck using Morse code signals. After the mines detonated, Tempest fled at maximum speed as a kiloton of stockpiled Aerolith exploded in the hollow.
