# AES Horizon

## Overview

AES Horizon is a destroyer in the Asgarthan Exploration Ship fleet, placed under [[Expeditionary Corps]] authority for the journey toward the second light signal.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October - The Turmoil Crossing

AES Horizon accompanied the 18-ship armada crossing [[The Turmoil]] as the fleet's destroyer, providing military support during the hazardous journey through the cloud sea.

## Relationships

- **[[Ordis]]**: Affiliated faction
- **[[Expeditionary Corps]]**: Force the vessel was assigned to
- **[[The Turmoil]]**: Region crossed with fleet (October 393 AC)
