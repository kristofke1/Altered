# Felsenwel

## Overview
Felsenwel is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], bordered by the [[Bacchanalia]] district to the west. It is a festive district that has always benefited from the aura of [[The Asterion]].

## Character
Considered more distinguished than the [[Bacchanalia]], which is more popular in nature, Felsenwel has always focused on the Arts. The district is home to the lively [[Ruzzante]] sub-district. It is a particularly popular place for affluent individuals, both day and night.

## Restricted Area
The area near [[The Asterion]] is surrounded by a wall, and entry is only possible with a restricted permit. Felsenwel is home to many chancellors and senators, constantly under the watchful protection of [[The Aegis]] sentinels.

## Entertainment
Artists and performers from the neighboring [[Fosa]] district cross the Grand Canal to entertain crowds in Felsenwel.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[The Asterion]]**: Palace whose aura benefits the district
- **[[Bacchanalia]]**: Neighboring popular entertainment district
- **[[Ruzzante]]**: Lively sub-district focused on entertainment
- **[[Fosa]]**: District whose performers entertain in Felsenwel
- **[[The Aegis]]**: Sentinels protecting the district's elite residents
- **[[Kuna]]**: Island overlooking Felsenwel
- **[[Aerolith]]**: Technology that has made the district desirable
