# Nafitera

## Overview
Nafitera is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], established during the reconstruction of the capital following the tsunami of 305 AC, under the direction of chief-architect [[Nanha Niski]].

## Design and Character
Resolutely modern for its time, Nafitera features wide thoroughfares and spacious streets. Some might say the district lacks the typical charm of the rest of [[Arkaster]].

## Kelon Experimental Zone
Along with [[Kabatas]], Nafitera served as an experimental zone to persuade the rest of the capital to fully transition to [[Kelon]] technology.

## Self-Contained City
Nafitera functions as a city within a city, clearly aiming to offer all the services available in the capital, but concentrated in one place.

## Impact on Surrounding Areas
The creation of Nafitera and Kabatas, along with the accompanying new urban dynamic, has caused an explosion of population density in [[Renkairi]]. These modern districts have also drawn residents away from deteriorating areas like [[Sjena]].

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Kabatas]]**: Sister district also designed by Nanha Niski
- **[[Nanha Niski]]**: Chief-architect who designed the district
- **[[Kelon]]**: Technology the district was designed to showcase
- **[[Renkairi]]**: District whose population exploded due to Nafitera's creation
- **[[Sjena]]**: Deteriorating district whose residents migrated to modern districts
- **[[Kladiver]]**: Third district designed by Nanha Niski
