# Huskr

## Overview
Huskr is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], serving as the departure point for the barges and gondolas that navigate the [[Nishaanfjord]].

## Transportation Hub
The boatmen of Huskr transport goods, visitors to the troglodyte cemeteries along the [[Sonowen]] cliffside, or travelers to the distant [[Haven]].

## Ferrymen's Guild
The [[Ferrymen's Guild]] is headquartered in Huskr. Many boatmen live in a network of ships permanently moored side by side, creating a floating residential community.

## The Monolith
Although [[The Monolith]] does not have a fixed position in the sky above [[Arkaster]], it frequently hovers over Huskr to facilitate the ascent of officials living in [[Sonowen]].

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Nishaanfjord]]**: Fjord navigated by Huskr's barges and gondolas
- **[[Ferrymen's Guild]]**: Guild headquartered in the district
- **[[Sonowen]]**: District whose sacred tombs are accessed via Huskr
- **[[Haven]]**: Distant destination reached by Huskr's boats
- **[[The Monolith]]**: Floating structure that frequently hovers over the district
