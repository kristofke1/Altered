# Usku & Koru

## Overview
Usku and Koru are new Permukaan districts of [[Arkaster]], located on the sea arm between the peninsulas of [[Kuna]] and [[Nisan]]. The districts emerged from the waters only recently, at great expense of [[Alteration]].

## Original Purpose
These two areas, still under construction, were intended to provide new residential spaces to alleviate congestion in the old districts of the capital.

## Construction Halt
Following the launch of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] and the resources allocated to this grand project, the construction of these new Permukaan was abruptly halted due to budget constraints and lack of primary resources.

## Rumors
It is rumored that certain fraudulent organizations have established their hideouts in these abandoned zones, using the cover of the half-built structures to conceal loot and goods. However, these rumors might merely be intended to deter visitors, who sometimes explore these quiet, lifeless places for sole leisure purposes.

## Impact on Other Districts
The notable abandonment of Usku and Koru's development has hindered the growth of [[Kladiver]], which was meant to be revitalized by these new districts.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the districts
- **[[Kuna]]**: Peninsula adjacent to the construction site
- **[[Nisan]]**: Peninsula adjacent to the construction site
- **[[Alteration]]**: Magic used at great expense to create the districts
- **[[Rediscovery Endeavor]]**: Project whose budget halted construction
- **[[Kladiver]]**: District whose development was hindered by the abandonment
