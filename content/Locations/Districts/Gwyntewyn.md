# Gwyntewyn

## Overview
Gwyntewyn is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], permanently shrouded in the steamy vapors of [[The Foundry]]. This hot and sticky district is home to numerous artisans, from clockmakers to carpenters, and stonemasons to blacksmiths.

## Streets and Architecture
The streets of Gwyntewyn are crooked, hilly, and sometimes shift when the [[Aerolith]] platforms gently sway.

## The Lungs of the Permukaan
Gwyntewyn is said to be the lungs of the [[Permukaan]], as it seems to breathe with these rhythmic movements of its aerolithe foundations.

## Historical Change
Once the district of stonemasons, the sound of hammers and chisels has alas long fallen silent. The quarries that once dug into [[Kemeri]] have been closed and repurposed into residential areas.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[The Foundry]]**: Industrial complex whose vapors shroud the district
- **[[Aerolith]]**: Technology powering the shifting platforms
- **[[Kemeri]]**: Peninsula whose quarries once supplied the stonemasons
- **[[Vejaskira & Jusaka]]**: Neighboring working-class districts
- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction with nearby Bastion
