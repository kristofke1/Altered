# Kabatas

## Overview
Kabatas is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], established during the reconstruction of the capital following the tsunami of 305 AC, under the direction of chief-architect [[Nanha Niski]].

## Design and Character
Along with [[Nafitera]], Kabatas was designed with a resolutely modern approach, featuring wide thoroughfares and spacious streets. The district served as an experimental zone to persuade the rest of the capital to fully transition to [[Kelon]] technology.

## Self-Contained City
Together with Nafitera, Kabatas functions as a city within a city, offering all the services available in the capital but concentrated in one place.

## Impact on Surrounding Areas
The creation of Kabatas and Nafitera, along with the accompanying new urban dynamic, has caused an explosion of population density in [[Renkairi]].

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Nafitera]]**: Sister district also designed by Nanha Niski
- **[[Nanha Niski]]**: Chief-architect who designed the district
- **[[Kelon]]**: Technology the district was designed to showcase
- **[[Renkairi]]**: District whose population exploded due to Kabatas's creation
- **[[Kladiver]]**: Third district designed by Nanha Niski
