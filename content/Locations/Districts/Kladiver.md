# Kladiver

## Overview
Kladiver is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], the third district whose plans were sketched by [[Nanha Niski]] during the reconstruction following the 305 AC tsunami.

## Geography and Purpose
Kladiver is an extension of [[Sjena]] along [[Sforafjord]]. Over time, the arm of the sea has subsided, forming a narrow and deep rift. To counteract the gradual disappearance of this area, Kladiver was created.

## Development Challenges
Unfortunately, Kladiver's relative remoteness has hindered its development. This was further exacerbated by the notable abandonment of the development of [[Usku]] and [[Koru]], which were meant to revitalize this part of the city.

## Current Function
For now, Kladiver is little more than a stopover for the [[Mistbreakers]] and all the boats heading up to the [[Cierna]].

## Kasirga Clan Haven
During the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], some [[Kasirga Clan]] families decided to stay behind from expeditions into [[The Tumult]], especially those looking after very young children. These families were kindly allocated homes in Kladiver rather than remaining on [[The Ouroboros]] or joining dangerous expeditionary operations.

The separation was difficult for those who departed, including [[Fen]] who deeply missed [[Aruzhan]] and other children who remained behind.
