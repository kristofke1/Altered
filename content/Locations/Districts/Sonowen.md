# Sonowen

## Overview
Sonowen is a Terraferma district of [[Arkaster]], nicknamed the "Dormitorium." It is home to numerous scribes, copyists, and scholars of the [[Ordis]].

## Daily Life
Every day, one can see the district's residents boarding the funiculars to the [[Wright Altiport]], to embark on barges that transport them to [[The Monolith]].

## Character
Like [[Reykur]], Sonowen is a quiet district, far from the bustle of the [[Permukaan]]. However, it is pleasant to stroll through, as there are many small gardens arranged in individual terraces.

## Sacred Shore
On this side of the sea arm, the shore of the [[Nishaanfjord]], otherwise known as the "Kerf Canal," is considered a sacred sanctuary. It is along this cliffside that the tombs and crypts of the deceased are stored. Normally, the tombs can only be accessed by water from the [[Huskr]] district, but some paths lead down from Sonowen.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Ordis]]**: Faction whose scribes and scholars reside here
- **[[The Monolith]]**: Floating structure to which residents commute
- **[[Wright Altiport]]**: Transportation hub accessed via funiculars
- **[[Nishaanfjord]]**: Fjord whose shore contains sacred tombs (also called Kerf Canal)
- **[[Huskr]]**: District from which tombs are normally accessed by water
- **[[Reykur]]**: Fellow quiet Terraferma district
