# Fosa

## Overview
Fosa is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]]. Its name originates from its grim past, when the bodies of victims of the Great Plague of 148 AC were thrown here, when there was nothing else in the vicinity but foaming spray and sharp reefs.

## Residents
Since then, Fosa has been home to the most modest populations, as well as cohorts of artists and performers who entertain the crowd in [[Felsenwel]], on the other side of the Grand Canal.

## Recent Revitalization
The recent creation of the [[Nafitera]] and [[Kabatas]] districts has now revitalized this long-neglected neighborhood.

## Safety
Despite its bohemian temperament, Fosa is a very safe district due to the presence of [[The Kurung]], the military barracks of Arkaster.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Felsenwel]]**: District where Fosa's entertainers perform
- **[[Nafitera]]**: New district whose creation revitalized Fosa
- **[[Kabatas]]**: New district whose creation revitalized Fosa
- **[[The Kurung]]**: Military barracks ensuring the district's safety
- **[[Renkairi]]**: District overlooking the Kurung
