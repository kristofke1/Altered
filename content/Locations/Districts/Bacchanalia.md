# Bacchanalia

## Overview
Bacchanalia is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], located west of [[Felsenwel]]. It is a popular entertainment district.

## Character
While [[Felsenwel]] is considered more distinguished and focuses on the Arts, the Bacchanalia district is more popular in nature, catering to a broader audience seeking entertainment.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Felsenwel]]**: Neighboring distinguished Arts district
