# Porto Novo

## Overview
Porto Novo is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], rising from the ashes of the old harbor that was destroyed by the tsunami of 305 AC. The district focuses on merchant shipping, while its sister district [[Arsenal]] handles military vessels.

## Character
Porto Novo is a bustling district, alive with the cries of fishermen and the constant loading and unloading of ships from dawn until dusk.

## Kelon Transport
Regularly, [[Mistbreakers]] from the distant island of [[Suspira]] can be seen docking here. For several days following their arrival, [[Kelon]] is transported from the docks to the depths of [[The Foundry]], via an unceasing ballet of trains and wagons that travel inland to the island of [[Kemeri]].

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Arsenal]]**: Sister district handling military vessels
- **[[Suspira]]**: Island from which mistbreakers arrive with Kelon
- **[[Mistbreakers]]**: Vessels that dock at Porto Novo
- **[[Kelon]]**: Resource transported from Porto Novo to the Foundry
- **[[The Foundry]]**: Destination for Kelon shipments
- **[[Kemeri]]**: Island to which Kelon is transported
- **[[Karuna]]**: Neighboring bourgeois district
