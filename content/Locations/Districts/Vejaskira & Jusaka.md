# Vejaskira & Jusaka

## Overview
Vejaskira and Jusaka are neighboring Terraferma districts of [[Arkaster]], located on the [[Kemeri]] peninsula. Due to their proximity to the [[Axiom]] Bastion, the [[Arsenal]], and the artisan district of [[Gwyntewyn]], they are predominantly working-class areas.

## Residents
Most of [[The Foundry]] workers live here, as do the blacksmiths of the Arsenal. The districts house the laborers who keep Arkaster's industrial heart beating.

## Architecture and Character
Walking through these districts, one can easily notice the prevalence of metal, from solid beams to various technological enhancements that each family has incorporated into their homes. Vejaskira and Jusaka are a maze of cables and pipes, intertwined with the network of streets.

## Industrial Atmosphere
Day and night, one can hear the hum of wind turbines languidly spinning above the rooftops or feel the heat from chimneys towering overhead. The industrial character permeates every aspect of daily life in these districts.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the districts
- **[[Kemeri]]**: Peninsula on which the districts are located
- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction with nearby Bastion
- **[[The Foundry]]**: Industrial complex where many residents work
- **[[Arsenal]]**: Military shipyard where blacksmiths work
- **[[Gwyntewyn]]**: Neighboring artisan district
