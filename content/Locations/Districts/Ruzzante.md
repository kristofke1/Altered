# Ruzzante

## Overview
Ruzzante is a lively tavern district within [[Felsenwel]] in [[Arkaster]], frequented by university students and intellectuals. The area is known for its seedy bistros and drinking establishments. Felsenwel has always focused on the Arts, and Ruzzante is home to much of its vibrant nightlife.

## University Life
During their time at [[Arkaster University]], [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] often gathered in the evenings at taverns in the Ruzzante to debate their ideas. Despite their disagreements and diametrically opposed views, they engaged in verbal jousting like swordsmen exchanging blows.

When they set aside their debates, the two friends enjoyed talking about poetry, literature, and philosophy. Surrounded by friends and fellow students, they frequented taverns and pubs with merry and intoxicated spirits, sometimes until the merciless early light of dawn.

## The Secret Pact
It was in the seedy bistros of the Ruzzante that Waru and Avkan first conceived their secret alliance. As they raised their glasses together, they became convinced that the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] was an absolute necessity. Waru had always been Avkan's staunchest supporter from these early days, the one willing to make any sacrifice to see his vision realized.

The two friends knew even then that there would be numerous challenges if they ever managed to promote the idea of exploration and ratify it into action. Their late-night conversations in Ruzzante laid the groundwork for the complex political game they would play for years to come.

## Atmosphere
[[Mack]], Waru's ermine Chimera, would rummage around the taverns in the evening, often returning with one or two Florets in his mouth. These coins were enough to pay for the next rounds for the grateful and merry students, adding to the convivial atmosphere of the district's drinking establishments.
