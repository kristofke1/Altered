# Ruzzante

## Overview
Ruzzante is a lively tavern district within [[Felsenwel]] in [[Arkaster]], frequented by university students and intellectuals. The area is known for its seedy bistros and drinking establishments. Felsenwel has always focused on the Arts, and Ruzzante is home to much of its vibrant nightlife.

## University Life
During their time at [[Arkaster University]], [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] often gathered in the evenings at taverns in the Ruzzante to debate their ideas. Despite their disagreements and diametrically opposed views, they engaged in verbal jousting like swordsmen exchanging blows.

When they set aside their debates, the two friends enjoyed talking about poetry, literature, and philosophy. Surrounded by friends and fellow students, they frequented taverns and pubs with merry and intoxicated spirits, sometimes until the merciless early light of dawn.
