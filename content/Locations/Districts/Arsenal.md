# Arsenal

## Overview
The Arsenal is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], rising from the ashes of the old harbor that was destroyed by the tsunami of 305 AC. While [[Porto Novo]] focuses on merchant shipping, the Arsenal handles [[Arkaster]]'s military vessels.

## Restricted Access
This is where the warships of Arkaster are built and stored. Civilians are prohibited from entering this district, especially since the construction of the first two heavy [[Aerolith]] vessels is currently underway.

## Facilities
The Arsenal is an extensive complex comprising the military port and shipyard of its navy. Amidst the cranes, locks, and docks—whether dry docks, construction docks, repair docks, or berthing docks—large vessels constantly jostle for space:
- Fishing trawlers
- [[Bravos]] drakkars
- Battleships guarding the bay
- An increasing number of flying ships utilizing [[Aerolith]], ready to soar into the skies

## Supporting Infrastructure
Surrounding the Arsenal, numerous warehouses dot the Terraferma as well as the lacustrine districts. Factories operate at full capacity to manufacture the components needed by shipbuilders.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Porto Novo]]**: Sister district handling merchant shipping
- **[[Ordis]]**: Faction operating the military facilities
- **[[Bravos]]**: Faction whose drakkars are built and stored here
- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction involved in shipbuilding technology
- **[[Aerolith]]**: Technology enabling new flying vessels
- **[[Vejaskira & Jusaka]]**: Districts where Arsenal blacksmiths live
- **[[Karuna]]**: Neighboring bourgeois district
