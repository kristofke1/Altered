# Karuna

## Overview
Karuna is a Permukaan district of [[Arkaster]], entirely considered a lake district even though it is partially built on a small rocky islet. It is a quintessential bourgeois district.

## Residents
Karuna is home to numerous merchants and shipowners who reside there due to its proximity to the [[Porto Novo]] and [[Arsenal]] districts.

## Expansion
Gradually, the district has expanded over the sea as maritime trade has developed.

## Informal Divisions
The inhabitants of [[Arkaster]] have taken to calling the part situated on the rocky edifice **Karuna Altua**, and the part extending at sea level **Karuna Behera**. However, administratively speaking, the district is not divided.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[Porto Novo]]**: Neighboring merchant port district
- **[[Arsenal]]**: Neighboring military shipyard district
