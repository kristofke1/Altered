# Renkairi

## Overview
Renkairi is a new Terraferma district of [[Arkaster]], overlooking [[The Kurung]] barracks.

## Origins
Renkairi initially housed the families of soldiers and guards who were stationed at the Kurung, whether they belonged to [[The Aegis]] or [[The Damocles]].

## Growth and Expansion
The subsidence of [[Sjena]] has led to a gradual relocation of the inhabitants of that lake district to the stability of Renkairi. New houses are continually sprouting up in the district, which is expanding southwards and into the heights of [[Kuna]].

## Population Boom
The creation of the [[Nafitera]] and [[Kabatas]] districts, along with the accompanying new urban dynamic, has caused an explosion of population density in Renkairi. Many affluent families have settled here to escape the hectic life of the [[Permukaan]].

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the district
- **[[The Kurung]]**: Military barracks overlooked by the district
- **[[The Aegis]]**: Faction whose soldiers' families originally settled here
- **[[The Damocles]]**: Faction whose guards' families originally settled here
- **[[Sjena]]**: Subsiding district whose residents are relocating to Renkairi
- **[[Kuna]]**: Island into whose heights the district is expanding
- **[[Nafitera]]**: New district whose creation spurred Renkairi's growth
- **[[Kabatas]]**: New district whose creation spurred Renkairi's growth
