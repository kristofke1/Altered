# Utval

## Overview
Utval is a mining city located on the rugged coast of [[Suspira]]. It is administered by the [[Axiom]] for the purpose of extracting and refining the abundant [[Kelon]] found in the region.

## Architecture
Utval is a metal citadel resembling an oil platform, made of walkways and tanks battered by winds and black sandstorms.

## Living Conditions
Life here is harsh and difficult, but many Asgarthans brave the elements to harvest the precious mineral.

## Mining Operations
Each day, [[Axiom]] transports head to the [[Kelon]] quarries and return with the valuable energy crystal. The Kelon is then loaded onto armored ships—the [[Mistbreakers]]—which navigate [[Undersea|the Undersea]] and the [[Cierna]] to the capital.

## Transportation
Utval has recently been equipped with an aerodrome to more quickly reach [[Arkaster]] or [[Sadracca]], and in the future, all other cities on the peninsula. This reflects [[Axiom]] Guilds' increasing reliance on air routes to supply themselves with rare ores.

## Maritime Connection
Utval is connected to the capital via the [[Sundström Route]], with [[Dusk]] serving as a maintenance hub for mistbreakers along this route.

## Relationships
- **[[Suspira]]**: Volcanic island where the city is located
- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction administering and operating the city
- **[[Kelon]]**: Primary resource extracted and refined
- **[[Mistbreakers]]**: Armored ships transporting Kelon to capital
- **[[Undersea]]**: Sea navigated by mistbreakers
- **[[Cierna]]**: Sea navigated by mistbreakers
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital destination for Kelon, accessible via aerodrome
- **[[Sadracca]]**: City accessible via aerodrome
- **[[Dusk]]**: Mistbreaker maintenance hub on Sundström Route
- **[[Sundström Route]]**: Maritime route connecting Utval to capital
- **[[Vatigest]]**: Fellow mining city on Suspira with aerodrome
