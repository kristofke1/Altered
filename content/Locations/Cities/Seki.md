# Seki

## Overview
Seki is an oasis city in [[Caer Arcolano]] with a population of approximately 171,000 inhabitants. Nestled in a coastal canyon, the city contrasts with the red desert of [[Sarkans Desert|Sarkans]] with its gleaming white walls and buildings.

## Geography
Protected from the heat and sandstorms by its canyon location, Seki also houses an important port that connects the city to other cities of the Protectorate.

## Economy
Seki's wealth comes from fishing in the fish-rich and tranquil waters of the [[Biela]].

## City of Historians
Seki is known as the City of Historians. Many artifacts and remnants of the Old World are sent there to be analyzed. The city houses a thousand wonders, safely tucked away in its crypts. The [[Historians' Guild]] maintains a significant presence here.

## City of Cats
Seki is also known as the City of Cats. All residents collectively leave water and food for the city's feline population on their doorsteps, maintaining a communal tradition of caring for these animals.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Arcolano]]**: Province containing the city
- **[[Sarkans Desert]]**: Red desert surrounding the canyon
- **[[Biela]]**: Sea providing fishing grounds and port access
- **[[Historians' Guild]]**: Organization analyzing artifacts in Seki
