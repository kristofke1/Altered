# Amorgand

## Overview
Amorgand is a city in [[Caer Kettria]] with a population of approximately 103,000 inhabitants. Rumor has it that Amorgand is the capital of artists and philosophers.

## History
Amorgand was built on the ruins of the old [[Lyra]] Crossroads, which was destroyed during the rebellion of [[Amahle Kalu]].

## Atmosphere
The city is perpetually bathed in a bluish light, creating a serene and melancholic atmosphere.

## Geography
Amorgand is built on a lake, amidst large water lilies and lotus flowers in a thousand shades of blue. Half of the dwellings are barges and ships, and each day, thousands of boats and junks navigate the city's canals.

## Economy
Amorgand is known for its trade in fabrics and silk, as well as its factories producing vibrant blue dyes.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Kettria]]**: Province containing the city
- **[[Lyra]]**: Faction whose old Crossroads the city was built upon
- **[[Amahle Kalu]]**: Rebel whose rebellion destroyed the original Lyra Crossroads
