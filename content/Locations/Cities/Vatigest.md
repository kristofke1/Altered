# Vatigest

## Overview
Vatigest is a mining city on the volcanic island of [[Suspira]].

## Industry
As one of [[Suspira]]'s mining settlements, Vatigest is involved in the extraction of [[Kelon]] and other ores vital to [[Axiom]]'s industrial operations.

## Transportation
Vatigest has recently been equipped with an aerodrome to more quickly reach [[Arkaster]] or [[Sadracca]], and in the future, all other cities on the peninsula. This reflects [[Axiom]] Guilds' increasing reliance on air routes to supply themselves with rare ores.

## Relationships
- **[[Suspira]]**: Volcanic island where the city is located
- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction operating mining operations
- **[[Kelon]]**: Primary resource extracted
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital accessible via aerodrome
- **[[Sadracca]]**: City accessible via aerodrome
- **[[Utval]]**: Fellow mining city on Suspira with aerodrome
