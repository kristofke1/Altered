# Svarograd

## Overview
Svarograd is a city in [[Caer Esvander]] with a population of approximately 9,000 inhabitants. It serves as the gateway to the elevated island of [[Enosha]].

## Ground-Level Interface
Still at ground level, Svarograd serves as the interface between the [[Enosha]] isle and the outside world.

## Porters
The city hosts numerous porters—seasoned climbers who transport supplies and equipment to the fragmented islets of the Province that are not served by the [[Transasgarthan]].

## Transit Point
Svarograd is still considered a transit point today, far less developed than [[Sojourn]] on the other side of the lowlands. It was originally supposed to regulate traffic along the fjord between [[Enosha]] and [[Silg]], but the [[Sundström Route]] has put an end to the use of this channel.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Esvander]]**: Province containing the city
- **[[Enosha]]**: Elevated island to which Svarograd is the gateway
- **[[Sojourn]]**: More developed twin city on other side of lowlands
- **[[Transasgarthan]]**: Railway that doesn't serve all fragmented islets
- **[[Silg]]**: Location at other end of original fjord channel
- **[[Sundström Route]]**: Maritime route that ended use of the fjord channel
