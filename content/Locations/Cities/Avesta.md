# Avesta

## Overview
Avesta is a city in [[Caer Arcolano]] with a population of approximately 78,000 inhabitants. Much like [[Uja]], it serves as an agricultural hub.

## Agriculture
Instead of specializing in wheat or barley cultivation like other agricultural centers, Avesta's wealth comes from its vegetable crops and rice cultivation.

## Threat from the Fagn
Avesta is threatened by [[The Fagn]], which grows larger each year. Many farmers now prefer to send their produce directly to [[Verbera]] rather than braving these strange marshes.

## Transportation
Avesta is known as the location where the junction of the Transasgarthan railway occurred, making it an important node in Asgartha's transport network.

## Muna Gateway
For the [[Muna]], Avesta serves as a gateway to [[Kirighai]] during their pilgrimages within the [[Katkera]] and the [[Silg]].

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Arcolano]]**: Province containing the city
- **[[The Fagn]]**: Expanding marshes threatening the city
- **[[Verbera]]**: Alternative destination for farmers avoiding the Fagn
- **[[Muna]]**: Faction using Avesta as pilgrimage gateway
- **[[Kirighai]]**: Peninsula accessed via Avesta for pilgrimages
- **[[Katkera]]**: Sacred forest destination for Muna pilgrims
- **[[Silg]]**: Mountain region destination for Muna pilgrims
- **[[Uja]]**: Fellow agricultural hub city
