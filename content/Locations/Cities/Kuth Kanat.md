# Kuth Kanat

## Overview

Kuth Kanat is a district in [[Arkaster]], one of the twin districts (along with [[Tesh Kanat]]) known for historic troglodyte dwellings carved into the high cliffs by early Asgarthans after [[The Great Wandering]]. Some of these residences have since become luxurious palaces.

## Relationships

- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital city containing the district
- **[[Tesh Kanat]]**: Twin district with similar troglodyte architecture
- **[[Caer Eidos]]**: Province containing Arkaster
