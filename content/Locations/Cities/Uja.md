# Uja

## Overview
Uja is a city in [[Caer Kettria]] with a population of approximately 95,000 inhabitants. It is one of the main agricultural hubs of [[Asgartha]].

## Agricultural Role
The cereal productions of the [[Arami Lowlands]] are transported to Uja each day before being distributed to the rest of the Peninsula via the Transasgarthan route. The city hosts numerous silos for storing grains from the plains.

## Appearance
Uja is a white city with turquoise roofs and domes, creating a distinctive visual character.

## Tourism
Uja is also a seaside resort town, attracting tourists mainly from the capital who come to enjoy its beaches of pebbles or white sand, as well as its renowned cuisine.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Kettria]]**: Province containing the city
- **[[Arami Lowlands]]**: Agricultural region supplying grain to Uja
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital from which most tourists originate
