# Marsa

Marsa is a pre-Confluence human metropolis located in the [[Tilted Steppes]] region of [[Caer Oorun]], approximately 217 miles from [[Arkaster]]. The city exists in ruins, with notable architectural remains including a theater building that survived [[The Tumult]]'s effects.

## Pre-Confluence History

According to [[Leocardius Sree]], the city was once called Marsa, though it may have gone by other names throughout its history. The settlement functioned as a major urban center during the pre-Confluence era before being lost to [[The Tumult]].

## Architecture and Ruins

### Current State

The ruins of Marsa are not as compelling as the remains found in [[Irundu]], according to [[Waru Toowoom]]'s archaeological assessment. However, certain structures retain enough features to identify their original purposes.

### Theater Building

The ruins of one building caught [[Waru Toowoom]]'s attention despite the scars [[The Tumult]] left on its façade. Based on traces and clues uncovered at the site, the structure was identified as potentially being a theater. This identification suggests Marsa possessed cultural infrastructure associated with performing arts during its active period.

## Location and Geography

Marsa is situated in the [[Tilted Steppes]] region, approximately 217 miles from [[Arkaster]]. The city exists within an area characterized by gravity-defying floating islands and unusual physical phenomena caused by [[The Tumult]]'s influence.

## 393 AC - Exalt Gathering Point

### January 393 AC - Strategic Planning

In January 393 AC, [[Waru Toowoom]] decided to settle in the ruins of Marsa. While he admitted without shame that the site's archaeological appeal was a primary draw, the location served a more immediate strategic purpose.

All Exalts were summoned to Marsa to plan the exploration of next territories. The gathering represented the end of what [[Waru Toowoom]] called the "recess" period—a resumption of active expansion efforts.

### Next Destination Planning

According to the latest rumors, [[The Ouroboros]] high command set their next destination at Marsa. The chosen target was a major geographical landmark: a luminescent mountain to the west of Marsa's position.

Between Marsa and this next waypoint lay the heart of the true [[The Tumult|Tumult]]. The expeditionary forces were finally set to leave the borders of the Known World.

The [[Initiates]] monitoring [[The Tumult]]'s currents predicted a calm period was expected in the coming weeks. The Exalts needed to move quickly to avoid missing the window of opportunity.

### Anticipated Meeting

[[Waru Toowoom]] was informed of [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]'s upcoming visit to Marsa, which he took as a sign that all arrangements for the "tumultuous" succession were complete. [[Waru Toowoom]] made sure he would be aboard [[The Ouroboros]] when [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari|Avkan]] arrived.

## Archaeological Significance

Marsa represents another pre-Confluence settlement providing insights into ancient Asgarthan urban development beyond the Peninsula. The presence of a theater building indicates:
- Cultural sophistication in pre-Confluence cities
- Investment in performing arts infrastructure
- Urban planning that included entertainment venues
- Social organization supporting artistic activities
