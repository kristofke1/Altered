# Hadera

## Overview
Hadera is the chief town of [[Caer Esvander]], a city built on the side of a floating islet. It has a population of approximately 231,000 inhabitants.

## Economy
Hadera's economic strength comes from its open-pit [[Aerolith]] quarries. It is because of this ore that the entire Province, along with [[Caer Alcada]], has risen into the sky.

## Mining Impact
Due to these mining activities, some islets sink and eventually fall to the level of the [[Undersea]]. This constant geological change is a feature of life in the floating Province.

## Transportation
Hadera hosts a fleet of levitating barges that dredge [[Aerolith]] cargo to the rest of the [[Asgartha|Peninsula]].

## Way of Life
Like everywhere else in [[Caer Esvander]], the inhabitants of Hadera have a way of life connected to their aerial habitat.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Esvander]]**: Province of which Hadera is chief town
- **[[Aerolith]]**: Primary resource extracted from quarries
- **[[Caer Alcada]]**: Neighboring Province also elevated by Aerolith
- **[[Undersea]]**: Sea below to which depleted islets sink
- **[[Asgartha]]**: Peninsula supplied by Hadera's Aerolith barges
