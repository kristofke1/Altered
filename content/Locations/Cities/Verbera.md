# Verbera

## Overview
Verbera is a city with a population of approximately 76,000 inhabitants. Although physically located on the [[Anthea]] peninsula, Verbera is directly administered by [[Caer Eidos]] and thus falls under [[Arkaster]]'s jurisdiction.

## Strategic Role
As the interface between the Provinces and [[Arkaster]], Verbera is a strategic city. Its warehouses accommodate goods both coming from the capital and those being transported there, excluding [[Kelon]] which follows separate transport routes.

## Character
Verbera is an industrial city and a commercial port, more functional than aesthetically pleasing. The city prioritizes utility and commerce over architectural beauty.

## Historical Significance
Verbera is famous for being the landing site of [[Arkaster]]'s fleet during the [[The Severance War|Severance War]], where they opposed the armies of the regional Governors.

## Transportation
A railway was built across the shallow [[Muir Concordia]] to connect Verbera directly to [[Arkaster]].

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Eidos]]**: Province that administers the city
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital with direct jurisdiction over Verbera
- **[[Anthea]]**: Peninsula where Verbera is physically located
- **[[Muir Concordia]]**: Lagoon crossed by railway connecting to Arkaster
- **[[The Severance War]]**: Conflict where Arkaster's fleet landed at Verbera
