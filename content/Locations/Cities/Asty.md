# Asty

## Overview

Asty is a vast megalopolis built upon a floating island at the center of [[The Whiplash]]'s eye, home to [[The Reka|the Reka people]]. A gigantic world-tree grows on the island, comparable in scale to [[The Nilam]] or [[The Spindle]], with the city built at its base and extending along its trunk into its branches. The pillar of light that guided the [[Expeditionary Corps]] across [[The Turmoil]] originates from Asty. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] estimated the city's surface area may surpass that of [[Arkaster]] due to its verticality.

## Geography

Asty occupies a floating island drifting at the center of the Whiplash's calm eye. The world-tree dominates the island, with the city colonizing the atoll from ground level up through the tree's canopy. The island is surrounded by the double-funnel cyclone that forms the Whiplash, effectively isolating it from the surrounding [[The Turmoil|Turmoil]].

## Architecture

The city's streets appear carved from white stone and are veined with [[Sap]]. Rivers of golden Sap flow down the world-tree's trunk and cascade to its roots. The city features platforms, docking ports, gardens, bridges, funiculars, and zip lines arranged across a myriad of terraces and levels. Entire districts are fully vegetated. Flocks of birds nest throughout the city.

## Technology

Upon first observation, Admiral Singh assessed Reka technology as equal to, and potentially exceeding, [[Asgartha|Asgarthan]] capabilities. A multitude of flying vessels surrounds the city, along with military craft including what appear to be frigates and destroyers.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Discovery by the Expeditionary Corps

The [[Expeditionary Corps]] armada emerged into the eye of [[The Whiplash]] after a three-hour crossing guided by [[Leviathans]]. The fleet discovered Asty and its living world-tree city, in stark contrast to the abandoned [[City of Scholars]]. [[The Reka|Reka]] warships initially formed a defensive barrier before broadcasting a welcome message identifying themselves and inviting the Asgartha fleet. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] ordered weapons armed during the initial standoff before communication was established.
