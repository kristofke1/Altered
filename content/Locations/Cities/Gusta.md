# Gusta

## Overview
Gusta is a port located on the [[Abelena]] archipelago.

## Maritime Significance
Gusta serves as a stopover point on the [[Sundström Route]], the maritime channel connecting [[Suspira]] to [[Arkaster]] through [[Undersea|the Undersea]]. Along with [[Dusk]], it is one of the obligatory stops for ships navigating this route.

## Relationships
- **[[Abelena]]**: Archipelago where the port is located
- **[[Sundström Route]]**: Maritime route for which Gusta is a stopover
- **[[Dusk]]**: Fellow stopover port on the Sundström Route
- **[[Suspira]]**: Origin point for ore shipments passing through
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Destination for ore shipments passing through
