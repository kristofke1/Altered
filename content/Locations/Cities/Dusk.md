# Dusk

## Overview
Dusk is a city in [[Caer Esvander]] with a population of approximately 2,000 inhabitants. Built beneath the floating buttresses of Caer Esvander, it is a high-altitude town perpetually shrouded in the mists of [[Undersea|the Undersea]].

## Architecture
Troglodyte dwellings coexist with pontoons and [[Aerolith]] buildings. Living there means getting used to the constant creaking of ropes, the gentle swaying of the rocks that form a ceiling obscuring the sky, and above all, enduring the vertigo of a high-altitude existence—almost never seeing the maritime floor far below.

## Economy
Dusk is primarily a small fishing port, but it has also become a maintenance hub for [[Mistbreakers]] when they travel along the [[Sundström Route]] that connects [[Utval]] to the capital.

## The Ouroboros
[[The Ouroboros]] docks at Dusk once a year to resupply and undergo repairs.

## Maritime Significance
Dusk remains an almost obligatory stopover point on the [[Sundström Route]], the maritime channel connecting [[Suspira]] to [[Arkaster]] through the Undersea.
