# Sojourn

## Overview
Sojourn is a city in [[Caer Arcolano]] with a population of approximately 17,000 inhabitants. It is the twin city to [[Svarogard]] on the peninsula of [[Anthea]].

## History

### Fortress Origins
Sojourn was once an impregnable fortress that ensured the safety of the delta before the [[The Covenant|Covenant]] was ratified.

### Naming
During the construction of the Transasgarthan railway, [[Kaja ruun-Aksoy]], then [[Basileus|Basilissa]], established herself in this place to personally oversee the construction of the bridge connecting [[Anthea]] to [[Enosha]], despite the controversy of her self-imposed exile. The city, then a simple enclave, paid tribute to her extended stay by taking the name of Sojourn.

## Current Role
Sojourn still regulates delta traffic today, especially the narrow fjord that connects the [[Biela]] to the [[Muir Concordia]].

## Historical Maritime Role
Before the inauguration of the [[Sundström Route]], the [[Shawl]] passage created such a bottleneck for ships transporting ores from [[Suspira]] that the estuary was completely crowded. This traffic jam forced many vessels to anchor off Sojourn, sometimes for days, before finally embarking on the journey to [[Arkaster]].
