# Sadracca

## Overview
Sadracca is the chief town of [[Caer Alcada]], located in the [[Makonis Heights]], a territory made up of terrestrial coral reefs and desolate moors. It has a population of approximately 189,000 inhabitants.

## Geography
Sadracca was founded in the middle of a canyon formed by hundreds of vertical rocky outcrops. These stone structures have been inhabited by humans, and now resemble a city of bell towers and colorful skyscrapers.

## Economy
The coral and mother-of-pearl harvested here supply numerous artists and artisans in the capital as well as other Provinces.

## Rediscovery Endeavor
In recent decades, Sadracca has become a hub for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. Its shipyards are working on the restoration of [[Mandjet]], a heavy intervention vessel for the [[Ordis]].
