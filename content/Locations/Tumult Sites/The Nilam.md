# The Nilam

## Overview

The Nilam is colossal dead world-tree discovered at heart of [[Cais Adarra]] mountain on April 2, 393 AC, with proportions rivaling [[The Spindle]]. Named for bluish hue of crystallized wood, tree harbors imprisoned [[Oneiros]] [[Kuraokami]] within trunk. Despite death, xylem system continues absorbing vast [[Mana]] amounts rising from ground, contributing to [[Kuraokami]]'s confinement.

Frescos discovered in the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]] refer to world-trees as "Világfa" (also rendered as "égig érő fa," meaning "tree that reaches the heavens"), the term used by inhabitants of the [[City of Scholars]] before their flight to the sky.

## Physical Description

### Structure and Scale

Trunk reaches skyward within shadows of fractured mountain with crown nearly touching summit. Massive branches and roots spread outward piercing and winding through surrounding rock. Entire structure embedded within ravine forming intricate network throughout vast crevice as if fused with stone itself. Proportions rival those of [[The Spindle]], confirming existence of multiple world-trees within Terra Incognita.

### Crystallized State

Fibers frozen and solidified becoming hard as crystal. Limbs long since shed leaves permanently. Entire inner wood glows with iridescent blue light visible throughout structure. Bark exhibits numerous perforations and gashes of non-natural origin penetrating deep into phloem network where [[Kuraokami]] imprisoned. Crystallization process transformed organic wood into mineral-like substance similar to dead world-tree discovered deep in [[Storhvit]] gorge.

## Mana Absorption Properties

Entire xylem system - vessels responsible for drawing nutrients from earth - continues absorbing vast amounts of [[Mana]] rising naturally from ground despite tree's death. High-energy concentration creates continuous [[Mana]] flow through dead wood structure. [[Yzmir]] mages believe concentration partly explains why [[Kuraokami]] remains trapped physically and anagogically at location. Ongoing absorption demonstrates dead world-trees maintain some functional connection to [[The Skein]] and [[Mana]] networks even after losing vitality.

## Kuraokami's Imprisonment

[[Kuraokami]] imprisoned within trunk at location marked by numerous perforations and gashes along bark. Openings penetrate deep into phloem network accessing inner wood structure. [[Oneiros]] confined both physically within tree and anagogically preventing manifestation as [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] in reality. [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]] continue analyses determining confinement mechanism but have only put forth conjectures. Exact method of dual imprisonment remains unclear despite high [[Mana]] concentration theory.

## Scientific Investigation - April 393 AC

### Botanical Analysis

[[Muna]] arborists and [[Axiom]] botanists examined trunk discovering artificial perforations and gashes throughout bark. Investigation revealed inner wood's iridescent blue glow and ongoing xylem system activity. Analysis focused on understanding how dead world-tree maintains [[Mana]] absorption capacity. Comparison with [[The Spindle]] sought to identify commonalities between living and dead world-trees.

### Research Objectives

[[Muna]] arborists requested further study hoping to find clues explaining [[The Spindle|Spindle]]'s current decline. Investigation seeks to determine whether decline caused by soil pathogens, vital nutrient depletion, or inevitable aging processes. Understanding [[The Nilam]]'s death may prevent similar fate befalling [[The Spindle|Katkera]]. Research represents urgent priority given [[The Spindle]]'s importance to [[Muna]] and [[Asgartha]]'s [[Mana]] web.

## Archaeological Discoveries - 393 AC

### Pre-Glaciation Human Settlement

Excavations around [[The Nilam]] conducted by [[Sierra]] revealed signs of human settlement from before glaciation including rusted tools, troglodyte dwellings, fabric remnants, and pottery fragments. Evidence indicates human society once established in shadow of world-tree with colony estimated to have housed several hundred people.

Settlement dated at least two hundred years prior to 393 AC, meaning presence postdates [[The Confluence]]. Provides proof that other human communities survived within [[The Tumult]] well after founding of [[Asgartha]]. Colony appeared abandoned before onset of glacial era despite advanced technological capabilities.

### Advanced Technology

Most astonishing aspect of settlement is technological advancement of community. Long before [[Axiom]] Industrial Revolution, these people developed incredible technical marvels representing technological head start of hundreds of years. Science used to build artifacts far surpasses anything currently accessible in [[Asgartha]].

Mysterious machine found encased in ice at nearby Cobalt Gorge demonstrates settlement's advanced capabilities. Alloy construction incredibly resilient, not matching any Asgarthan materials. Surface covered in grooves etched into metal forming interconnected channel network with ancient organic appearance. Complete absence of power source—not [[Kelon]], coal, oil, or electricity. Engine design features pistons, valves, heads, crankshafts managing fluid like heart without ignition or combustion system.

### Climate Context

Soil analysis revealed [[Cais Adarra]] region and entire [[Storhvit]] enjoyed much milder climate barely century ago, similar to current weather in [[Tilted Steppes]] of [[Caer Oorun]]. Region transformed from once-lush land to polar conditions in few decades through sudden glaciation. Deeper study of sediments and geological formations suggests shift was sudden, possibly using [[Alteration]] to shape arctic landscapes.

Settlement abandonment before glaciation onset suggests inhabitants migrated. Questions remain whether they were descendants of [[The Lost Tribe]] or other [[Tumult Nomads]], and whether descendants still wander world.

## Diplomatic and Political Significance

### Tenth Refuge Proposal

[[Muna]] arborists proposed establishing permanent embassy at location - Tenth Refuge designated as Refuge of Snow. Purpose: maintaining dialogue with [[Winter Folk]] and [[Belisenki]] populations. [[Temera Singh]] inclined to grant contingent ensuring safety while awaiting reinforcements from [[Asgartha]]. Refuge would represent [[Muna]]'s diplomatic presence in newly discovered territory.

### Provincial Incorporation

[[The Asterion]] desires incorporation of [[Storhvit]] region into Asgarthan Protectorate for political and economic reasons. [[Muna]] mediators will remain stationed tempering ambitions of [[Companies|Corporations]], Guilds, and other Factions seeking to exploit region's resources. Once area stabilized, [[Storhvit]] becomes eligible for potential settlement strategy. Explored territory gains official Province status under name [[Caer Nilam]] honoring fallen world-tree.

### Regional Emblem

Cartographers marked atlases with [[The Nilam]]'s location. Heraldists used tree's image designing region's emblem. [[Caer Nilam]] becomes first addition brought forth by [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] following recent reconquest of [[Caer Oorun]]. Development serves as undeniable proof initiative benefits [[Asgartha]]n civilization, significantly strengthening standing among Asgarthan population.

## Seedling Carried by City of Scholars Refugees

Frescos discovered on a floating island in the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]] document that refugees fleeing the [[City of Scholars]] took a fertile Nilam seed with them. The refugees cultivated this seedling during their journey through the quadrants after escaping via a levitating tower-city.

The [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] [[Halua]] was born from this seedling, emerging from its bark. Halua lived alongside the refugees who venerated the growing tree until they began harvesting [[Sap]] from it. When Halua attempted to protect the tree, the refugees captured and imprisoned him before exiling him beyond the cloudline. Halua retains a connection to this tree and continues to feel its pain.

## Comparison to Other World-Trees

### Connection to The Spindle

Both trees exhibit colossal proportions serving as knots in [[The Skein]]. Both connect [[Mana]] web across regions through root and branch networks. [[The Nilam]]'s death provides comparative data for understanding [[The Spindle]]'s withering condition. Shared characteristics suggest common origin or nature among world-trees. Currently, [[The Spindle]] and The Nilam are the only known world-trees.

### Root Network Extent

The Nilam's roots extend deep into [[Storhvit]] gorge, where [[Rin]] and [[Arjun]] encountered crystallized wood transformed into crystal-hard opalescent substance. The root network's vast reach demonstrates the tree's colossal scale, with tendrils penetrating far beyond [[Cais Adarra]] into surrounding terrain.

## Larva Discovery - 393 AC

Researchers found a still-living larva curled dormant inside The Nilam's bark, despite the world-tree's dead state. The caterpillar-like creature was approximately fist-sized when discovered and has since grown to the size of a medium dog after being raised in a laboratory at [[The Screed]]. [[Saskia Averina]] was called in to study the specimen. The discovery of living organic matter within the crystallized dead wood suggests remnants of the world-tree's former ecology may persist within its structure.

## Mana Moths Presence

[[Mana Moths]] circle The Nilam's crystal branches continuously, having done so since before winter arrived according to [[Nauraa]]. The moths' wingbeats create an audible presence that [[Chimerae]] can interpret as emotional expression—[[Nauraa]] described hearing their sorrow and sensing a hollow space where something was missing, "like a void where there should have been absence of void."

## Effect on Chimerae - 393 AC

When [[Teija]] and [[Nauraa]] passed The Nilam en route to [[The Screed]], the dead world-tree triggered prolonged melancholy in [[Nauraa]]. The giant fox stared at the crystallized tree and listened to the [[Mana Moths]]' wingbeats, later describing to [[Teija]] that The Nilam reminded him of [[Katkera]] and the time before their bonding. [[Nauraa]] let out a yelp that was almost a lament. Through their [[The Musubi|Musubi]] bond, [[Teija]] shared the grief and felt anguish at the idea that [[The Spindle]] might wither like The Nilam.
