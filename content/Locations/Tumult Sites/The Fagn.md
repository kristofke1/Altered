# The Fagn

## Overview
The Fagn is a cursed zone within the [[Kiragata Wetlands]] that was created at the end of the 3rd century AC during [[The Three Factions War]]. It is the site where the [[Altered/Concepts/Other/Black Leviathan|Black Leviathan]] was defeated, and remains permanently tainted by the accumulated darkness of war.

## Creation
During [[The Three Factions War]], a [[Altered/Concepts/Other/Black Leviathan|Black Leviathan]] of pure malice and hatred grew in the [[Kiragata Wetlands]], fed by the war's atrocities—death, terror, vengeance, and cruelty. Champions from all four Factions journeyed to the Leviathan's gestation site, plunging into marshes that had become putrid under its influence.

They found the Leviathan, still in its nymph state, having just emerged from its chrysalis. Joining forces with the surviving [[Muna]], the champions battled the creature, and in a last-ditch effort, managed to defeat it, saving [[Asgartha]] in the process.

## The Curse
Unfortunately, despite this victory, all the accumulated darkness had tainted the place with a malevolent aura. Though peace was restored, the area remained cursed.

## Current Status
All the [[Muna]] present swore to watch over this place, to prevent its evil from spreading, and to cleanse it of its curse. Even today, The Fagn remains an island of darkness, ever ready, at the slightest opportunity, to consume everything surrounding it.

## Expansion
The murky bog seems to expand year after year, like an unhealthy tumor spreading through the [[Kiragata Wetlands]]. It is said to harbor strange and terrifying things. This is the center of the territory where humans never venture. [[Muna]] and [[Yzmir]] have settled on the edge of the Fagn to try to freeze its borders and contain its growth.
