# Arami Lowlands

## Overview
The Arami Lowlands are vast expanses of plains and valleys located within [[Caer Kettria]], at the heart of the [[Anthea]] Peninsula. The region is known as [[Asgartha]]'s main granary.

## Geography
A sea of grass and wheat lazily unfolds to the horizon under what looks like an infinite sky. The lowlands are separated from the [[Nutsuwa Highlands]] by a rift nicknamed the "Scar."

## Agriculture
The bulk of [[Asgartha|the Peninsula]]'s agricultural resources come from the Arami Lowlands.

## Settlements
Some strongholds stand atop the hills, but primarily villages along with water mills and windmills dot the landscape.

## Battlefield Remnants
An impressive number of stone swords can be found planted in the ground throughout the region, massive remnants from a giant yet forgotten battlefield.

## Leviathan Carcasses
Most impressive are the carcasses of [[Leviathans]], whose bleached bones stretch out of the ground. The pragmatic inhabitants of Arami have decorated these bones and established dwellings within them.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Kettria]]**: Province containing the lowlands
- **[[Anthea]]**: Peninsula at whose heart the lowlands lie
- **[[Nutsuwa Highlands]]**: Neighboring region separated by the Scar
- **[[Asgartha]]**: Nation for which the lowlands serve as main granary
