# Jayu Region

The Jayu Desert, also known as the Jayu region, is a geographic area within the province of [[Caer Oorun]], located north-west of [[Caer Oorun]]'s central areas and approximately 100 miles from [[Arkaster]]. The region contains a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity manifesting strange and dangerous phenomena.

## Geography

The Jayu Desert is a desert of ocher red, traversed by rocky formations with a tortured aspect. Here and there, salt accumulations cling to the ground, in tumuli or in more extensive dunes. While some areas are covered with a thin film of water reflecting the clouds, most of this biome is arid and desolate.

The region's proximity to [[Arkaster]] makes it one of the closer explored territories during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], yet also one of the most dangerous due to the [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity present.

## Tumult Singularity Phenomena

### Strange Manifestations

The region exhibits disturbing phenomena possibly linked to the presence of a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity. Troops stationed in the Jayu Desert reported multiple anomalous occurrences:

**Rings of Light**: Rings of light appear in the night sky above the desert.

**Transformative Beams**: Beams of light emerge that turn everything they touch into [[Red Salt|red salt]]. This transformation effect poses an extreme hazard to exploratory forces and equipment.

### Ongoing Investigations

As of January 393 AC, investigations into the phenomena were ongoing. The nature and danger level of the manifestations remained under assessment by the [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]].

## 393 AC - Council Deliberations

### January 393 AC - Disturbing Reports

In January 393 AC, disturbing reports reached [[Waru Toowoom]] through his network of informants regarding the exploratory forces stationed in the Jayu Desert. The reports of strange phenomena prompted urgent deliberations about appropriate responses.

### Council of Factions Response

The [[Council of Factions]] hesitated on the nature of forces to deploy to the Jayu Region given the anomalous conditions. [[Temera Singh]] faced numerous requests regarding the deployment while attempting to strike a delicate balance in her decision-making.

[[Temera Singh]] mentioned merit as a factor in her deployment considerations, though the specific implementation of this criterion remained unclear. [[Waru Toowoom]] expressed uncertainty about her intended approach and suggested his correspondent might have insight into her plans.

## Strategic Challenges

The Jayu Region presents unique challenges for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]:
- **Anomalous Threats**: Unpredictable Tumult manifestations requiring specialized responses
- **Force Deployment**: Difficult decisions about appropriate personnel and resources
- **Research vs. Safety**: Balancing investigation of phenomena with protection of troops
- **Unknown Variables**: Ongoing uncertainty about the Singularity's nature and extent
