# Caer Alcada

## Overview
Caer Alcada is one of the autonomous Provinces (Caers) of the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan Protectorate]], occupying the eastern portion of the [[Enosha]] peninsula. The province is also known as the [[Makonis Heights]], positioned high in the clouds.

## Geography
The [[Makonis Heights]] constitute the primary region of this elevated province. The landscape features one of [[Asgartha]]'s most bizarre ecosystems, filled with chaotic biomorphic forms such as mounds of pink moss. Cloud banks frequently drift over the landscape, absorbing the sulfuric fumes from volcanic areas.

## Flora
The grasses, flowers, and groves that grow in Caer Alcada are distinctively pink or purple in coloration. The trees planted in [[The Kadigir]] originate from this region. Unusually, the trees do not exhibit leaves but rather colored bubbles that grow on their branches.

## Volcanic Activity
Some areas of the province are volcanic, with barren ground exhaling toxic vapors.

## Coral Forests
Other regions are covered with coral forests, resembling maritime reefs but existing on solid ground rather than underwater.

## Population
Total provincial population: approximately 34,000 inhabitants.

## Relationships
- **[[Enosha]]**: Peninsula containing the province
- **[[Caer Esvander]]**: Western province of Enosha
- **[[Makonis Heights]]**: Primary region, synonymous with the province
- **[[The Kadigir]]**: Location where trees from this region have been planted
- **[[Auriga]]**: Highest peak of the province
