# Rhodopean Quadrant

## Overview

The Rhodopean Quadrant is one of six regions within the [[Cloud Realm]], characterized by heavily vegetated floating islands. The quadrant's dense vegetation provides natural concealment for both the terrain and its massive inhabitant.

## Geography

The Rhodopean Quadrant features an environment rich with plant life across its floating islands. The heavy vegetation makes it easy for large creatures to go unnoticed within the landscape.

## Fauna

The quadrant serves as the habitat for [[Zaratan]], a reptilian [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] resembling a flying turtle approximately 1,500 meters in diameter. Zaratan's shell supports its own ecosystem of vegetation and nesting birds, allowing it to blend seamlessly into the quadrant's vegetated environment.

## Relationships

- **[[Cloud Realm]]**: Parent region
- **[[Zaratan]]**: Turtle Leviathan inhabiting the quadrant (low risk)
- **[[Expeditionary Corps]]**: Force documenting the quadrant
