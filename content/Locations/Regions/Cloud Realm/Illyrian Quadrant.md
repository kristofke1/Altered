# Illyrian Quadrant

## Overview

The Illyrian Quadrant is one of six regions within the [[Cloud Realm]], characterized by tall rock pillars topped with grassy plateaus scattered into loose colonies. The quadrant's primary activity is livestock farming.

## Geography

The geological formations consist of vertical rock columns with flat, grass-covered summits. These natural structures create impressive cliff faces that serve as ideal terrain for climbing enthusiasts and [[Altrun]] practitioners. The pillars are arranged in loose colonies across the quadrant.

## Strategic Importance

The Illyrian Quadrant is the most stable biome in the [[Cloud Realm]], making it invaluable for fleet operations. The [[Expeditionary Corps]] uses the natural rock formations as lookout points and safe anchorages away from the Sea of Tumult. The quadrant also provides concealment from [[Leviathans]], effectively serving as the expedition's primary exploration hub.

## Activities

### Livestock Farming

[[Muna]] herders use the grassy plateaus to graze their livestock, including woollybacks that were domesticated at [[The Screed]]. The stable conditions and abundant grassland make it suitable for sustained animal husbandry.

### Training Operations

[[Bravos]] Exalts conduct training sessions in the quadrant. [[Kojo Oduro]] was encountered here during one such session by [[Sunniva Vaccarezza]].

### Mining Escort

[[Bravos]] personnel escort [[Axiom]] miners on prospecting expeditions to various islands, assessing Aerolith deposits throughout the quadrant.
