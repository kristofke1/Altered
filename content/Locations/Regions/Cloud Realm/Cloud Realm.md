# Cloud Realm

## Overview

The Cloud Realm is a vast aerial region discovered by the [[Expeditionary Corps]] during their crossing of [[The Turmoil]]. Resembling an ocean with suspended islands where vessels can make port, the region contains floating landmasses, levitating water bubbles, and numerous territories suitable for exploration and resource gathering.

## Geography

The Cloud Realm has been divided into six distinct Quadrants, each with unique geological or topographical characteristics:

- **[[Illyrian Quadrant]]**: Tall rock pillars with grassy plateaus; fleet exploration hub and livestock grazing
- **[[Rhodopian Quadrant]]**: Wooden islands with tropical vegetation; bird colonies and nature preserve
- **[[Thermaic Quadrant]]**: Floating water basins and giant aquariums; main water resupply source
- **[[Macedonian Quadrant]]**: Bone and fossil formations; sealed off by [[Yzmir]] for classified research
- **[[Pelagonian Quadrant]]**: Rich Aerolith deposits; active mining operations
- **[[Thessalian Quadrant]]**: Abandoned terraced rice fields; ancient agricultural zone

Studies conducted by [[Axiom]] researchers using Aerolith dating indicate all six biomes were once part of a single archipelago that gradually drifted apart due to shifting winds. The region contains far more Aerolith-bearing formations than the [[Enosha]] peninsula in [[Asgartha]].

## Ancient Civilization

Evidence of a pre-Tumult civilization exists throughout the Cloud Realm. The [[Pelagonian Quadrant]] contains remnants of ancient mining stations with technology similar to discoveries in [[Storhvit]] and the [[City of Scholars]]. The [[Thessalian Quadrant]]'s extensive terraced rice fields and irrigation systems indicate sophisticated agricultural development, comparable to [[Caer Oorun]]'s historical role in Asgartha.

## Expeditionary Operations

The [[Expeditionary Corps]] uses the Cloud Realm as an operational base during their journey toward the second light signal. The stable [[Illyrian Quadrant]] serves as the primary exploration hub, providing safe anchorage away from the Sea of Tumult and concealment from [[Leviathans]].

Faction activities in the region include:
- **[[Axiom]]**: Aerolith dating research, mining operations, prospecting expeditions
- **[[Muna]]**: Livestock farming, bird-tracking missions, botanical studies
- **[[Yzmir]]**: Classified research in the Macedonian Quadrant
- **[[Lyra]]**: Stationed in the Thessalian Quadrant
- **[[Bravos]]**: Security escorts for mining and research teams
- **[[Prospectors' Guild]]**: Coordinating Aerolith extraction
