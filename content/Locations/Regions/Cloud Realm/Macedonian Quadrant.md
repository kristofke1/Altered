# Macedonian Quadrant

## Overview

The Macedonian Quadrant is one of six regions within the [[Cloud Realm]], sealed off by [[Yzmir]] for classified research. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

## Geography

The quadrant consists of bones or fossils belonging to massive, long-extinct creatures. Two theories exist regarding their origin: some believe the formations constitute a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] graveyard, while others claim they are the remains of ancient titans—beings born with [[The Tumult]] itself before dying out.

## Yzmir Operations

[[Yzmir]] sorcerers conduct experiments within the Macedonian Quadrant and study elemental creatures that have made their homes among the skeletal formations. The specific nature of this research remains classified.

Only a few [[Bravos]] personnel have received permission to enter the quadrant, and information about its contents comes primarily from their accounts.

## Alelo - The Macedonian Leviathan

The primary reason for the embargo is [[Alelo]], an eel-like [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] that dwells among the bone fields. Alelo reacts to any [[Alteration]] of reality, no matter how slight, which has led [[Yzmir]] to forbid all use of Alteration within the region.

## Relationships

- **[[Cloud Realm]]**: Parent region
- **[[Yzmir]]**: Faction controlling the quadrant and conducting classified research
- **[[Bravos]]**: Faction with limited authorized access
- **[[Alelo]]**: Eel-like Aether-hybrid Leviathan inhabiting the quadrant; reacts to any Alteration (high risk)
- **[[Leviathans]]**: Creatures whose remains form the quadrant's bone fields
- **[[Mana Moths]]**: Creatures sharing similar Aether-reality hybrid nature with Alelo
- **[[The Tumult]]**: Force theorized to have birthed the ancient titans
- **[[Sunniva Vaccarezza]]**: Exalt who documented rumors about the quadrant
