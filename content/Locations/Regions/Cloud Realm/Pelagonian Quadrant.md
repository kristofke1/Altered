# Pelagonian Quadrant

## Overview

The Pelagonian Quadrant is one of six regions within the [[Cloud Realm]], naturally rich in [[Aerolith]]—a motherlode of antigravitational ore. The quadrant serves as the primary mining zone for the [[Expeditionary Corps]], though operations carry significant risk due to the material's instability.

## Geography

Unlike the calm [[Illyrian Quadrant]], the Pelagonian airspace exists in constant turmoil. Floating clusters continuously collide and drift, creating a hazardous environment. When two Aerolith veins rub together or crash, exothermic reactions can trigger chain explosions.

## Mining Operations

[[Axiom]] has established extensive mining infrastructure throughout the quadrant:
- Drilling platforms for ore extraction
- Refining plants for processing raw Aerolith
- Transit depots for crystal storage
- Mobile [[Tech Lab Unit|Tech Labs]] for research operations
- Evacuation pods and airbases

The [[Prospectors' Guild]] coordinates extraction efforts, recognizing the unprecedented opportunity presented by the quadrant's deposits—far exceeding anything found in [[Asgartha]]. [[Bravos]] personnel maintain bases in the quadrant and provide security escorts for mining teams.

All operations maintain constant alert status due to the volatile nature of the work.

## Ancient Civilization Evidence

[[Axiom]] researchers have discovered remnants of ancient mining stations within the Pelagonian Quadrant. The technology found at these sites bears similarity to discoveries made in [[Storhvit]] and the [[City of Scholars]], suggesting a sophisticated pre-Tumult civilization once operated mining facilities in this region.

### 393 AC - Floating Island Expedition

A scholarly expedition organized by [[Leocardius Sree]] traveled to a small floating island in the Pelagonian Quadrant aboard an [[Axiom]]-provided mechanical vessel. The island contained ruins sharing architectural style with the [[City of Scholars]], including a large white building with golden geometric lines and walls set with intricate patterns.

Within a structure resembling a place of worship or remembrance, [[Ordis]] linguist [[Yanna]] and the college of linguists translated four fresco panels documenting the [[City of Scholars]] inhabitants' escape to the sky. The scripts bordering the murals share common roots with those of the [[Tumult Nomads|Tribe of the Setting Sun]]. The expedition included historians, architects, ethnologists, sociologists, and linguists, as well as [[Lyra]] Exalt [[Nadir]] and his [[Chimerae|Chimera]] [[Bubbles]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October 23 - Halua Attack

[[Halua]] attacked the Axiom quarry in the Pelagonian Quadrant, destroying mining facilities, the mobile [[Tech Lab Unit|Tech Lab]], [[Axiom]] and [[Bravos]] bases, [[AES Sune]], and two reconnaissance airships. The attack caused heavy human casualties. [[Paju]], piloting a white-shelled mech, sacrificed himself to save [[Isaree Thongsuk]] from a collapsing crane.
