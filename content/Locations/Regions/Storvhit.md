# Storhvit

## Overview

Storhvit is a winter region in the [[Terra Incognita]] beyond [[Caer Oorun]], named by the first scouts of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] exploration forces. The region is perpetually locked in harsh winter and contains six distinct biome environments centered around [[Altered/Locations/Regions/Cais Adarra|Cais Adarra]] mountain.

## Geography and Environment

Storhvit is a snowy expanse perpetually locked in harsh winter. Experts suspect the very idea of winter is deeply entrenched in this region. The mutagenic effects of [[The Tumult]] are mitigated by the persistence of a strong idea near the explored zone: the idea of cold, ice, and winter. This idea emanates from [[Cais Adarra]], a mountain peak that glows with vivid blue light day and night.

The region contains six distinct biome environments: [[Tundra]], [[Taiga]], [[Frozen Lake]], [[Canyon]], [[Caverns]], and [[Spur]].

## Climate and Weather

The weather is highly unpredictable, influenced by [[The Tumult]] sweeping across the land and generating capricious winds. Conditions that seem favorable in the morning can deteriorate within hours. Weather conditions include snow, frost, biting cold, and limited visibility.

## Exploration Challenges

Progress through Storhvit is grueling, slow, and painstaking. Unlike [[Caer Oorun]] which benefited from mild [[The Tumult|Tumult]] currents, areas beyond the rehabilitated Province present harsher conditions. Fatigue is evident among the [[Initiates|Exalts]]. The constant obstacles take a toll on morale.

The harsh weather conditions accompanying the region's stabilizing influence include snow, frost, biting cold, and limited visibility. The goal is to reach [[Altered/Locations/Regions/Cais Adarra|Cais Adarra]] as quickly as possible, as the peak will provide a secure haven against [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities plaguing the lower regions, despite the cold there being even harsher.

## Expeditionary Corps Operations

### January 393 AC Deployment

The vanguard of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] was deployed to Storhvit in January 393 AC. During downtime, many troops listen to teachings of [[Ordis]] scholars or songs of [[Lyra]] skalds. They consult texts brought from the capital.

A recommendation was made to diversify the range of [[Eidolons]] troops can summon, alleviating the burden posed by the region's endemic cold. [[Jack Frost]], the [[Snow Queen]], [[Skaði|Skadi]], and other lesser-used [[Eidolons]] are being explored or rediscovered and adapted to this environment where their abilities prove invaluable.

In coordination with [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] team leaders, a decision was made to deploy contingents of explorers using [[The Ouroboros]] to assist the [[Initiates|Exalts]] when [[The Tumult|Tumult]] currents are sufficiently calm. The [[Initiates|Exalts]] and select [[Alterers]] shield these troops from [[The Tumult]], while the explorers provide relief from the cold and direct support during the journey. [[Axiom]] deployed probes to survey terrain ahead.

## Phenomena

The [[Snow Wraith]], also called Spirit of the Snows, is a disembodied voice phenomenon reported by early explorers of Storhvit.

## Relationship to Other Regions

The [[Tundra]] biome contains coral-like formations similar to [[Makonis Heights]], suggesting the same wave of concepts influenced both this location and the plateaus of the [[Enosha]] peninsula.
