# Caer Arcolano

## Overview
Caer Arcolano is one of the five autonomous Provinces (Caers) of the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan Protectorate]]. It is notable for being divided between two contrasting climates.

## Geography

### Kiragata Wetlands
To the north, the [[Kiragata Wetlands]] form a wet region consisting of marshes and rice fields, many of which are terraced. This area is separated from the desert by the [[Kandu]] region, a chain of low-altitude mountains with white, rounded ridges. While most lands have been populated and exploited for livestock and agriculture, there is a region at the center of the territory where humans never venture: [[The Fagn]]. This murky bog seems to expand year after year, and is said to harbor strange and terrifying things. [[Muna]] and [[Yzmir]] have settled on the edge of the Fagn to try to freeze its borders. Outside of this peril, the rest of the territory is bucolic and pastoral.

### Sarkans Desert
The Province is largely covered by the [[Sarkans Desert]], an immense desert of red sand. Some areas are rugged, traversed by deep canyons of carmine rock. Clusters of [[Kolamusa]]—a living mass crossing between lichen and baobab that could be mistaken for rock—can be found here. The hollow trunks of these organisms provide access to wells that descend into the [[Hypogea]], a titanic underground cavity held in place by root networks, where rivers of milky white waters irrigate the soil and roots produce their own bioluminescence.
