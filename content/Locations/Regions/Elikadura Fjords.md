# Elikadura Fjords Region

The Elikadura Fjords Region is a geographic area within the province of [[Caer Oorun]], located west of [[Caer Oorun]]'s central areas and approximately 174 miles from [[Arkaster]]. The region contains [[Cebir]] fortress and serves as the westernmost explored area during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Geography

Elikadura is a region where mountains are traversed by winding fjords. The slopes of rocky peaks harbor immutable glaciers, and the tortuous sea arms shelter dangerous icebergs. This is where the ruins of [[Cebir]] are located, the citadel built by the joint forces of the [[Bravos]] and the [[Ordis]].

Access to the region requires sailing through increasingly cold waters filled with small icebergs and dangerous ice floes. The fjord system provides natural harbor protection while presenting navigational challenges.

## Strategic Location

The region serves as a gateway to the western coastal territories beyond the [[Turbulent Shores]]. [[Cebir]] fortress was specifically built in this location to serve as a forward base for exploring these western regions.

## 392 AC - Cebir Tensions

In October 392 AC, [[Cebir]] fortress within the Elikadura Fjords Region became the center of tensions between the [[Bravos]] and [[Yzmir]] factions. Admiral [[Temera Singh]]'s directive was to reclaim the fortress to plan exploration of the western coastal region beyond the [[Turbulent Shores]].

Following intervention by [[Wanjiru]], the [[Yzmir]] Faction locked down [[Cebir]], citing a quarantine to seize control of operations. This action complicated [[Bravos]] plans for western exploration.
