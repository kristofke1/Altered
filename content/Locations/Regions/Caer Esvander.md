# Caer Esvander

## Overview

Caer Esvander is one of the autonomous Provinces (Caers) of the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan Protectorate]], occupying the western portion of the [[Enosha]] peninsula.

## Geography

The province contains the [[Vanderun Shards]], a region of floating islands in the southern part of the Enoshan peninsula. The grassy expanses covering these islands are conducive to livestock farming.

## Economy

Caer Esvander is renowned for its livestock, particularly a specific breed of goat. The province's trademark is cheese production from these animals.

## Transportation

Inhabitants resort to various contraptions to move from island to island, including gliders, aerostats, and airships.

## Cities

- [[Hadera]] - 231,000 inhabitants
- [[Sadracca]] - 189,000 inhabitants

Total provincial population: approximately 420,000 inhabitants

## Military Training

The mountains of Caer Esvander serve as training grounds for [[Ordis]] military personnel. [[Zhen]] undertook grueling physical training and survival courses in these mountains while preparing for the officers' academy.

## Timeline

### Pre-393 AC - Suspended Mines Strike

A strike occurred at the suspended mines of Caer Esvander, followed by intervention of private militias. [[Lucas Aveskamp]] was particularly outspoken during this conflict, which increased his political influence.

## Relationships

- **[[Enosha]]**: Peninsula containing the province
- **[[Caer Alcada]]**: Eastern province of Enosha
- **[[Vanderun Shards]]**: Floating island region within the province
- **[[Undersea]]**: Sea beneath the floating portions of the province
- **[[Lucas Aveskamp]]**: Politician who gained prominence during mines strike
- **[[Zhen]]**: [[Ordis]] Exalt who was born in [[Hadera]] and trained in the mountains
- **[[Ordis]]**: Faction that uses the province's mountains for military training
