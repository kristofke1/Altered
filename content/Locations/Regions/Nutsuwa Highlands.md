# Nutsuwa Highlands

## Overview
The Nutsuwa Highlands are tranquil and serene expanses within [[Caer Kettria]]. They are separated from the [[Arami Lowlands]] by a rift nicknamed the "Scar."

## The Scar
Legends say that the Scar was created by a sword stroke from [[Rune]] wielding [[Excalibur]] when he slew the [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity that unfolded in this place.

## Atmosphere
Beyond the Scar, the Highlands are a poetic place with a cottony atmosphere, perpetually bathed in a melancholic bluish glow. The territory appears constantly illuminated by moonlight, and all sounds seem muffled.

## Flora
The Nutsuwa Highlands contain distinctive vegetation including giant flower forests of poisonous beauty, seas of pampas grass swaying their fluffy stems, and forests of pallid trees with ghost-white trunks beneath canopies of blue and luminescent foliage. The trees constantly emit a fluffy pollen that falls like snow.

## Lakes
The lakes are peaceful-looking, surrounded by beaches of fine white sand.

## Inhabitants
Within this peaceful setting, numerous silent creatures can be found: ghostly and semi-translucent beings that roam the area like half-incarnated specters.

## Veil Porousness

The Nutsuwa Highlands differ from other regions of [[Asgartha]] in the porousness of [[The Veil]] separating reality from the realm of imagination. Strange phenomena occur here, bathed in the bluish hue that colors even the underwood air: [[The Aether|Aether]] singularities, ghostly apparitions, and oddities of all kinds. Many [[Yzmir]] settled in the region, far from [[The Kadigir]], to study the permeability of the boundary between worlds and its effects on nature. New species tinged by imagination emerge in the Highlands.

## Settlements

Lumber villages exist deep in the heart of the blue forest, including one roughly halfway between [[Kestia]] and [[Amorgand]] where [[Moyo Chibuye]] was born. [[Tsunoo]] is a settlement where [[Yzmir]] mages are stationed.
