# Keleti

## Overview
Keleti is a region within the Terra Incognita. It is characterized by distant shores where the [[Bravos]] have established an outpost.

## Bravos Presence
The [[Eastern Commandery]], called the Ushungi, stands on the distant shores of the Keleti region. A small contingent of willing [[Bravos]] exiles currently mans this outpost, awaiting the arrival of the main bulk of exploration forces for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].
