# Svarga Station

## Overview
Svarga Station is one of two train stations in [[Arkaster]], located in the west (District 6).

## Purpose
The station is primarily dedicated to freight transport.

## Historical Significance
This was the first station built, established during the reign of [[Altered/Characters/Rulers/Kaja ruun-Aksoy|Kaja ruun-Aksoy]]. It was crucial for the mutual development of the capital and its Provinces, enabling the supply of equipment and seasonal labor on one hand, and essential resources for the city's survival on the other.
