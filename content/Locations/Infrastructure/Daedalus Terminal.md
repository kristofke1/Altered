# Daedalus Terminal

## Overview
Daedalus Terminal is an air terminal located in the lower part of [[Arkaster]] (District 19), named in honor of Daedalus, Icarus' father.

## Purpose
In addition to [[Wright Altiport]], which is dedicated to airships serving the entire Peninsula, this terminal is reserved for short trips.

## Structure
This semi-circular structure features aerolithe embarkation platforms and overlooks numerous maintenance docks and often crowded reception halls.

## Destinations
Through this terminal, the Asgarthans can quickly reach the heights of the islands surrounding the capital, as well as the [[Ordis]] Monolith.
