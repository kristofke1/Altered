# Origamium

## Overview

The Origamium is a facility where [[Paper Herald|paper heralds]] are sorted and dispatched toward their final destinations. Workers known as origamophiles manage the sorting process, releasing the folded paper messages — shaped as dragonflies, grasshoppers, doves, crows, and other forms — to recipients throughout the fleet.

## Operations

Hundreds or thousands of paper heralds may pass through the Origamium at peak times, creating a swirling mass of rustling paper. Each herald bears the name of its intended recipient and is released by origamophiles toward its destination.

## Relationships

- **[[Paper Herald]]**: Messages sorted and dispatched through the Origamium
- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: Awaited election results from Origamium-processed heralds (393 AC)
