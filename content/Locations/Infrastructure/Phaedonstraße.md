# Phaedonstraße

## Overview
Phaedonstraße is a street in [[Arkaster]], likely within the [[Kuth Kanat 1]] district based on the [[Akesha Adhikari|Adhikari]] family's residence there. The street was known for a pastry shop that operated before closing years prior to 392 AC.

## The Pastry Shop

### Description
The shop sold pastries with cream inside. Its logo featured a duck. The establishment became a memorable location for local families visiting the capital.

### Closure
The shop closed years before 392 AC, though it remained a strong memory for former customers.

## Childhood Memories

### Akesha and Kumari Adhikari
[[Akesha Adhikari]] and [[Kumari Adhikari]] visited the pastry shop during their childhood, wandering the capital together and indulging in little joys and wild adventures. The sisters would buy cream pastries from the shop.

Years later, [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]] expressed longing for the pastries during a [[Remanence]] episode at [[The Cloister]]. [[Akesha Adhikari|Akesha]] recreated a pastry from memory using her abilities, including a cardboard box (though she misremembered the logo as a goose rather than duck). Kumari noted the taste was close but slightly saltier than remembered—perhaps due to Akesha's tears while creating it from memory.

The memory represented simpler times before Kumari's accident at [[The Well of Tears]], embodying carefree days of giddy laughter and exploration.
