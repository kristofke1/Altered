# Thule Station

## Overview
Thule Station is one of two train stations in [[Arkaster]], located in the east (District 7).

## Purpose
The station is allocated to passenger transport.

## Recent Development
This more recent station was established simultaneously with the Kabatas district, aiming to create a new economic hub for the city.
