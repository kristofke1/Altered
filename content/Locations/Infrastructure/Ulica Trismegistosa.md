# Ulica Trismegistosa

## Overview

Ulica Trismegistosa is a street in [[Arkaster]] where [[Takao Cordisco]] operated his alchemist shop for nearly twenty years.

## Notable Establishments

### Takao Cordisco's Alchemist Shop

[[Takao Cordisco]], a [[The Kadigir|Kadigiran]] alchemist, operated a storefront on this street. Every Monday he would leave the safety of [[The Kadigir]] to offer passersby brews, liniments, and tonics at cut-rate prices. The shop's display window contained jars, amulets, and occult trinkets. In May 393 AC, Takao's body was discovered in the back room of the shop, the victim of arsenic poisoning.
