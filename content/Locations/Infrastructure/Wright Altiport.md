# Wright Altiport

## Overview
The Wright Altiport is an airship and aerostat transportation hub in [[Arkaster]], located at the base of the rocky promontory of [[The Acus]]. From here, airships and aerostats service the entire [[Asgartha|Peninsula]].

## Connections
Funiculars connect the Altiport to the [[Sonowen]] district. Every day, [[Ordis]] scribes and scholars board these funiculars to reach the Altiport, then embark on barges that transport them to [[The Monolith]].

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the Altiport
- **[[The Acus]]**: Peak at whose base the Altiport is located
- **[[Sonowen]]**: District connected by funiculars
- **[[The Monolith]]**: Destination reached via barges from the Altiport
- **[[Ordis]]**: Faction whose personnel use the Altiport daily
- **[[Reykur]]**: Neighboring district
