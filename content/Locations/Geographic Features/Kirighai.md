# Kirighai

## Overview
Kirighai is one of the four smaller peninsulas of [[Asgartha]]. It is recognized as a neutral and independent zone, separate from the Asgarthan Protectorate's jurisdiction. The peninsula is almost entirely covered by a dense and primal forest, [[Katkera]].

## Access
Kirighai is accessible only by water or via its unique bridge from the island of [[Nisan]], called the [[Mugen]].

## Geography
The Kirighai peninsula includes the [[Katkera]] forest and the [[Silg]] mountain region to the east. The [[Silg]] forms a natural barrier with the island of [[Enosha]] through a chain of sharp mountains and narrow fjords. On these snowy heights resides [[Kaibara]], the tutelary Leviathan of the Asgarthan Peninsula.

Kirighai is connected to other peninsulas by [[Shawl|the Shawl]], a shallow strip of sea.

## Population
Kirighai is sparsely populated by humans. Only the [[Muna]] [[The Refuges|Refuges]] (also called Temples of Serenity) constitute inhabited enclaves.

## Climate
The climate is of the cold oceanic type.

## Political Status
While territories of [[Altered/Locations/Geographic Features/Vagheria|Vagheria]] and [[Altered/Locations/Geographic Features/Suspira|Suspira]] are directly under the control of [[Arkaster]], the Kirighai peninsula is recognized as a neutral and independent zone.

## Relationships
- **[[Katkera]]**: Dense primal forest covering almost the entire peninsula
- **[[Silg]]**: Mountain region to the east forming barrier with Enosha
- **[[Kaibara]]**: Tutelary Leviathan residing in the Silg
- **[[Nisan]]**: Island connected to Kirighai via the Mugen bridge
- **[[Mugen]]**: Unique bridge providing land access from Nisan
- **[[Enosha]]**: Neighboring island separated by the Silg barrier
- **[[Muna]]**: Faction maintaining the only inhabited enclaves
- **[[The Refuges]]**: Muna sanctuaries serving as the peninsula's inhabited areas
