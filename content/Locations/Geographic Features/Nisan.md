# Nisan

## Overview
Nisan is one of the three islands comprising the Province of [[Caer Eidos]], along with [[Sekent]] and [[Kemeri]]. [[Arkaster]] was built at the junction of these three islands. Nisan is connected to the [[Kirighai]] peninsula via the [[Mugen]] bridge.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Eidos]]**: Province to which the island belongs
- **[[Sekent]]**: Sister island of Caer Eidos
- **[[Kemeri]]**: Sister island of Caer Eidos
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital city built at the junction of the three islands
- **[[Kirighai]]**: Peninsula connected via the Mugen bridge
- **[[Mugen]]**: Bridge providing land access to Kirighai
