# Nishaanfjord

## Overview
The Nishaanfjord, also known as the "Kerf Canal," is a fjord in [[Arkaster]]. It splits into two branches at the southern tip of the isle of [[Yeni]], where [[Haven]] is located.

## Sacred Shore
On the [[Sonowen]] side of the fjord, the shore is considered a sacred sanctuary. It is along this cliffside that the tombs and crypts of the deceased are stored. Normally, the tombs can only be accessed by water from the [[Huskr]] district, but some paths lead down from Sonowen.

## Navigation
The [[Huskr]] district serves as the departure point for the barges and gondolas that navigate the Nishaanfjord. The boatmen transport goods, visitors to the troglodyte cemeteries, or travelers to the distant [[Haven]].

## Geography
The steep, grassy slopes of [[Yeni]] island plunge sharply into the two arms of the Nishaanfjord.
