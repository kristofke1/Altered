# Caverns

## Overview

The Caverns are one of six distinct biome environments within the [[Storhvit]] region. Located deep underground beneath the glacier and [[Altered/Locations/Regions/Cais Adarra]] mountain base, they are described as the most mesmerizing biome discovered so far.

## Physical Description

Deep underground lies the most mesmerizing biome discovered so far. Beneath the glacier and the mountain base is a network of crystal caverns—a labyrinth of glowing stalactites and stalagmites radiating phosphorescent pastel hues.

The karstic formations include columns, pillars, draperies, and pools. On the first snapshots of the biome, it resembles a glowing, pastoral forest trapped in an endless night.

## Formation

This protean landscape, with its organic topography, has been shaped by centuries of erosion.
