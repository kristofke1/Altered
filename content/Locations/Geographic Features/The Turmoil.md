# The Turmoil

## Overview

The Turmoil is a vast expanse of clouds that the [[Expeditionary Corps]] flotilla traverses on its journey from [[Sofia]] toward a second signal of light to the southwest. Named by expedition personnel, the region consists of an endless sea of clouds with the [[The Tumult|Tumult]] roaring constantly beneath the cloud cover. The sky above may appear blue or starry, but the underlying volatility poses constant danger to the fleet.

## Geography

The Turmoil manifests as a dense cloud layer separating the clear skies above from the Tumult-infested regions below. "Bubbles and waves of Tumult" periodically rise through updrafts to burst at the cloud surface, creating hazardous conditions for vessels traversing the region.

## Hazards

### Tumult Pockets

Tumult pockets can explode at the cloud surface with devastating effect. The cruiser [[AES Concord]] sustained severe hull corrosion when one such pocket detonated, forcing the vessel to turn back for repairs. The four main shielded ships—[[The Ouroboros]], [[The Wayfarer]], [[Mesektet]], and [[Mandjet]]—sustained only minor degradation due to their more effective shielding against mutagenic winds.

### Health Effects

Prolonged exposure to the volatile [[Mana|Aether]] carried by the Turmoil causes an affliction with symptoms eerily similar to [[Remanence]]. Though temporary, the condition frequently relapses among crew members. The [[Rati]] physicians and their [[Eidolons]] work tirelessly to ease suffering, assisted by [[Muna]] experimenters using leeches capable of siphoning parasitic Aether.

### Potential Leviathan

Multiple lookouts reported seeing a massive shadow beneath the cloud surface following the convoy. The entity may be a known [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] such as [[Terat]] or [[Garuda]], or something previously unencountered. Whether peaceful, curious, or predatory remains unknown.

## Navigation

The flotilla maintains tight formation while crossing the Turmoil, limiting reconnaissance capacity. Constant detours and stoppages slow progress to a crawl. [[Axiom]], [[Ordis]], and [[Yzmir]] collaborate on developing probes capable of piercing the cloud vault to map what lies beneath and identify potential [[Oases]]—suitable landing zones for gathering resources. Early attempts failed, but teams remain confident in designing sensor modules durable enough to endure Tumult conditions.

Lighter vessels that arrived with [[Mandjet]] were positioned above the heavy ships to serve as observation posts and shelter the fleet from potential harm.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October 15 - Fleet Crossing

Admiral [[Temera Singh]] documented the fleet's crossing in her log after nearly two weeks of traversing the Turmoil. The convoy had approximately 150 nautical miles remaining to reach the destination—the source of the second light signal that appeared after [[The Hunger]]'s defeat. The fleet had resupplied above the [[City of Scholars]] before departure.

### 393 AC - The Whiplash Barrier

As the fleet neared the end of its crossing, with the [[pillar of light]] only a few nautical miles away, the armada encountered [[The Whiplash]]—a double-funnel cyclonic tornado with counter-rotating winds exceeding 500 kilometers per hour. The barrier blocked the fleet's final approach, destroying all reconnaissance drones sent through. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] suspected it was deliberately erected as a protective measure.

### 393 AC - Whiplash Crossing and Arrival at Asty

The fleet crossed [[The Whiplash]] in approximately three hours using [[Leviathans]] led by [[Halua]] as a vanguard. Upon emerging into the cyclone's eye, the [[Expeditionary Corps]] discovered [[Asty]], a living megalopolis inhabited by [[The Reka|the Reka people]], marking the completion of the Turmoil crossing and the culmination of the fleet's journey from [[Sofia]].
