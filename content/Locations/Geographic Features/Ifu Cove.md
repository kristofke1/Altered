# Ifu Cove

## Overview
The Ifu Cove is an area of the [[Biela]] sea located within [[Caer Kettria]], shielded from winds and tides.

## Geography
The cove consists of narrow straits and winding sea arms. The bay features numerous stacks rising from the waters, as well as arches projecting into the sea from the towering cliffs.

## Waterfalls
Countless waterfalls can be found in the Ifu Cove, including the majestic [[Sabaax Falls]] that encircle the city of [[Kestia]].

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Kettria]]**: Province containing the cove
- **[[Biela]]**: Sea of which the cove is a sheltered part
- **[[Kestia]]**: City encircled by the Sabaax Falls
- **[[Sabaax Falls]]**: Major waterfall feature encircling Kestia
