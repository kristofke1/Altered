# Tumult Depression

## Overview

The Tumult Depression is a permanent [[The Tumult|Tumult]] storm located southwest of the [[City of Scholars]], blocking the [[Expeditionary Corps]]' path to the second column of light that appeared after [[Sofia]]'s liberation. The depression's mutagenic pressure is approximately sixteen times stronger than any [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity ever studied in [[Asgartha]].

## Characteristics

[[Yzmir]] forecasters at [[Nilam Spires]] have observed and analyzed the depression for months, finding no sign of weakening. The mutagenic currents are strikingly steady, with continuous generation flowing from the west. Toxicity increases the closer one approaches the center.

According to [[Sylas]], approaching the depression's center would eventually reach an edge—a boundary beyond which no law holds sway, where reason yields to madness. Beyond that limit, nothing would make sense, and every certainty would crumble. At the cyclone's heart lies "the true unknown."

## Strategic Challenge

The depression presents a critical obstacle for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. If the exploration forces wish to reach the beam of light that appeared on the horizon after the City's purification, they must somehow cross or circumvent this Tumult zone.

### Proposed Solutions

**Direct Crossing**: Considered impossible. Even Exalts may struggle to withstand the mutagenic pressure at 16x normal Singularity strength.

**Circumnavigation**: Would require months or years given limited resources and funds—deemed impractical.

**Aerial Bypass**: [[Sylas]] proposed flying over the cyclone's eye using four vessels: [[The Ouroboros]], [[The Wayfarer]], [[Mesektet]], and [[Mandjet]]. Satrap [[Zaj Ke Va]] agreed to study this proposal further, noting it would provide [[Yzmir]] valuable research opportunities into the Tumult.

## Possible Origin

The depression may be connected to the second column of light that rose hundreds of kilometers to the southwest when [[The Hunger]] was destroyed. Whether something is creating the depression intentionally—and whether the signal originates from within the cyclone's eye—remains unknown.
