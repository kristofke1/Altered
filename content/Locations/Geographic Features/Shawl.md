# Shawl

## Overview
The Shawl is an elongated and shallow strip of sea connecting the peninsulas of [[Anthea]], [[Kirighai]], and [[Enosha]]. It is located at the crossroads of the Krurafjord and Woswekafjord estuaries.

## Geography
The Shawl widens from the delta to [[Caecaster]] on one side and to [[Hadera]] on the other, at the edge of [[Undersea|the Undersea]].

## Connection to Muir Concordia
Like [[Muir Concordia]], of which it is somewhat an extension, the Shawl is subject to the same unexplained massive evaporation phenomenon.

## Maritime Traffic
The Shawl historically served as a passage for ships transporting ores from [[Suspira]] to [[Arkaster]]. However, the shallows created bottlenecks that crowded the estuary, forcing vessels to anchor off [[Sojourn]] for days before continuing their journey.

- **[[Enosha]]**: Peninsula connected by the Shawl
- **[[Muir Concordia]]**: Lagoon of which the Shawl is an extension
- **[[Caecaster]]**: City at one widening of the Shawl
- **[[Hadera]]**: City at the other widening of the Shawl
- **[[Undersea]]**: Sea at the edge of the Shawl near Hadera
- **[[Sojourn]]**: Port where ships anchored waiting for passage
