# Kandu

## Overview
The Kandu is a mountain region in [[Caer Arcolano]], consisting of a chain of low-altitude mountains with white, rounded ridges. It separates the [[Kiragata Wetlands]] to the north from the [[Sarkans Desert]] to the south.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Arcolano]]**: Province containing the mountain chain
- **[[Kiragata Wetlands]]**: Wet region to the north
- **[[Sarkans Desert]]**: Desert region to the south
