# Vagheria

## Overview
Vagheria is one of the four smaller peninsulas of [[Asgartha]]. It is an austere and rugged peninsula constantly battered by rain, serving as a natural barrier against [[The Tumult]] currents.

## Political Status
The territory of Vagheria is directly under the control of [[Arkaster]], rather than being part of one of the five autonomous Caers.

## Geography and Climate
Vagheria's terrain is rugged and inhospitable, with constant rainfall battering the landscape. This harsh topography made it a natural barrier against Tumult currents, and the peninsula absorbed the majority of the mutagenic flows from the titanic Tumult tsunami of 305 AC.

## The Rampart
When [[The Rampart]] was extended, its architects leveraged the rugged topography of Vagheria to serve as the foundation for this magical wall protecting [[Asgartha]] from [[The Tumult]] and [[Kraken|the Kraken]].

## Population
The peninsula is almost uninhabited, except for [[The Aegis]] sentinels, [[Bravos]] fighters, and [[Yzmir]] mages who have settled in scattered strongholds along the Rampart. These small contingents serve as lookouts and rotate every four months. Each shift change is eagerly awaited due to the harsh conditions.

## Eastern Tumult Zone
The eastern part of Vagheria, located outside the walls, has been deeply affected by the various Tumult currents that have crashed against its steep foothills. A strange grove has unfolded there over the years, featuring disturbing flora:
- Carnivorous plants
- Herbs that mimic human voices
- Flowers with hypnotic movements
- Pollen with soporific properties

Few dare to venture there without an escort, except for certain [[Yzmir]] or [[Muna]].

## Research Laboratory
[[Yzmir]] and [[Muna]] have established a laboratory and greenhouses in the eastern Tumult zone. Their aim is to collect and study the properties of these plants, which have been strongly marked by the Tumult.

## Relationships
- **[[Asgartha]]**: Nation containing the peninsula
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital that directly controls Vagheria
- **[[The Rampart]]**: Magical wall built using Vagheria's terrain as foundation
- **[[The Aegis]]**: Sentinels stationed in strongholds along the Rampart
- **[[Bravos]]**: Fighters stationed in strongholds
- **[[Yzmir]]**: Mages stationed in strongholds and operating the research laboratory
- **[[Muna]]**: Operating the research laboratory alongside Yzmir
- **[[The Tumult]]**: Force against which Vagheria serves as natural barrier
- **[[Muir Concordia]]**: Lagoon bordering Vagheria
