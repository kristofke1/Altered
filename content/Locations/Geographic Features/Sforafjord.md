# Sforafjord

## Overview
Sforafjord is a fjord in [[Arkaster]] that separates the [[Kemeri]] peninsula from [[Kuna]] island. The twin districts of [[Kuth Kanat]] (on the western shore, Kemeri side) and [[Tesh Kanat]] (on the eastern shore, Kuna side) face each other across this waterway.

## Geography
Over time, the arm of the sea has subsided, forming a narrow and deep rift in places. The [[Kladiver]] district extends along the Sforafjord as an extension of [[Sjena]].

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the fjord
- **[[Kemeri]]**: Peninsula on the western shore
- **[[Kuna]]**: Island on the eastern shore
- **[[Kuth Kanat]]**: District on the western shore
- **[[Tesh Kanat]]**: District on the eastern shore
- **[[Kladiver]]**: District extending along the fjord
- **[[Sjena]]**: District of which Kladiver is an extension
