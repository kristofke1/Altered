# Canyon

## Overview

The Canyon is one of six distinct biome environments within the [[Storhvit]] region. It is a narrow pass winding through the glacier that splits the [[Frozen Lake]] in two.

## Physical Description

A narrow pass winds through the glacier, splitting the [[Frozen Lake]] in two. Enveloped in darkness, the canyon echoes with the deep groans of shifting ice. The towering walls are so high that the sky appears as a mere crack above.

While much of the canyon floor is stone and snow, certain sections are submerged, requiring sailboats or barges to traverse.

## Obstacles

Geysers of frozen water occasionally erupt from the walls, forming icy wings that block the path, forcing contingents to carve their way through.

## Natural Aquarium

The most awe-inspiring sight is the silhouette of colossal aquatic beasts swimming behind the icy barriers, like an enormous natural aquarium.

## Relationships

- **[[Storhvit]]**: Parent region containing the Canyon biome
- **[[Frozen Lake]]**: Lake split in two by the canyon
