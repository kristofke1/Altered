# Karuna Island

## Overview
Karuna Island is an island off the coast of [[Asgartha]].

## Ancient Ruins
When the waters recede during the Season of Mists, shifting sand currents sometimes expose the remains of four towers sharp as arrows and domes torn apart off the coast of Karuna Island. [[Ordis]] scholars suggest that a great city existed here before [[The Confluence]].
