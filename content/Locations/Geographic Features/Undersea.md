# Undersea

## Overview
The Undersea is a sea perpetually lost in mist, stretching beneath the elevated Provinces of [[Caer Esvander]] and [[Caer Alcada]] on the floating [[Enosha]] peninsula.

## Dangers
The shallows are numerous, making it very dangerous to venture into. The number of wrecks strewn across this sea, their carcasses impaled on sharp reefs, is countless. Beware of straying from established routes.

## Water Phenomena
Sometimes, gigantic bubbles of water rise from the Undersea and drown certain rocks of the floating [[Vanderun Shards]] above.

## Port of Dusk
A port for light vessels has been installed beneath the suspended city of [[Dusk]]. The city is perpetually shrouded in darkness, built under the floating rocks of [[Caer Esvander]]. Humans live there in troglodyte habitats connected by suspended bridges.

## Sundström Route
A navigable route called the [[Sundström Route]] has been found between [[Suspira]] and [[Arkaster]] that passes through the Undersea. Previously considered too impracticable by pilots, this route now allows ships to drastically reduce travel time when delivering [[Kelon]] to the capital. [[Dusk]] remains an almost obligatory stopover point on this channel.

## Relationships
- **[[Enosha]]**: Floating peninsula above the Undersea
- **[[Caer Esvander]]**: Western floating province above the sea
- **[[Caer Alcada]]**: Eastern floating province above the sea
- **[[Vanderun Shards]]**: Floating islands occasionally struck by rising water bubbles
- **[[Dusk]]**: Troglodyte city with port beneath the floating rocks
- **[[Sundström Route]]**: Maritime route passing through the Undersea
- **[[Suspira]]**: Island connected to Arkaster via the Undersea route
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital connected via the Sundström Route
- **[[Shawl]]**: Sea at the edge of the Undersea near Hadera
