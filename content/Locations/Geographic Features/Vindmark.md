# Vindmark

## Overview
Vindmark is a geographic feature on the eastern frontier of [[Caer Eidos]].

## Location
Vindmark forms the eastern frontier of [[Caer Eidos]]. [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school, a secluded monastery, was located on the edge of Vindmark.

## Historical Significance
In 74 AC, [[The Magan]] attacked and destroyed [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] monastery during [[Ayxas]]'s suppression of independent warrior brotherhoods during [[The Torment]].
