# Taiga

## Overview

The Taiga is one of six distinct biome environments within the [[Storhvit]] region. It is a boreal forest characterized as an ethereal, iridescent dreamscape with otherworldly vegetation and wildlife.

## Geography and Appearance

This boreal forest is an ethereal, iridescent dreamscape containing towering conifers, birch groves with otherworldly hues, crystalline formations, and oaks and ash trees with quartz-like pink foliage. The very air seems tinged with a rosy hue, as if the forest were perpetually bathed in twilight.

Mist often rises, coating the forest floor, and its iridescence resembles enamel.

## Wildlife

The wildlife is equally striking, with white-furred or feathered animals perfectly camouflaged in this luminous, snowy expanse.
