# Kemeri

## Overview
Kemeri is one of the three islands comprising the Province of [[Caer Eidos]], along with [[Sekent]] and [[Nisan]]. [[Arkaster]] was built at the junction of these three islands.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Eidos]]**: Province to which the island belongs
- **[[Sekent]]**: Sister island of Caer Eidos
- **[[Nisan]]**: Sister island of Caer Eidos
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital city built at the junction of the three islands
