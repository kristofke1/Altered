# Auriga

## Overview
The Auriga is the highest point of the [[Enosha]] peninsula, culminating at 4,879 m (16,000 ft).

## Location
This peak takes root in the Enosha region known as [[Makonis Heights|the Makonis Heights]], located in the eastern part of the floating island.
