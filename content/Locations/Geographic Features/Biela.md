# Biela

## Overview
The Biela is a sea located to the south of [[Asgartha]], contrasting with the turbulent [[Cierna]] to the north through its calm, clear, and oily waters.

## Fishing
Every day, fishing fleets traverse the Biela, departing from the cities of [[Seki]], [[Caecaster]], or [[Kestia]], and returning at nightfall with their hauls of fish.

## Features
The [[Ifu Cove]] is a sheltered area of the Biela sea within [[Caer Kettria]], shielded from winds and tides.

## Alternative Route
During the Season of Mists when the [[Shawl]] passage becomes difficult, ships transporting ores from [[Suspira]] would take the Biela and bypass [[Anthea]] to the south, though this extended the journey by several weeks.
