# Silg

## Overview
The Silg is a mountain region located to the north of [[Kirighai]] island, in an area where humanity scarcely has the right to go. A mountain range with sharp relief looms over the [[Katkera]] forest. It is on these snowy heights that [[Kaibara]], the tutelary Leviathan of the Asgarthan Peninsula, resides.

## Geography
The Silg forms a natural barrier between [[Kirighai]] and the island of [[Enosha]] through a chain of sharp mountains and narrow fjords.

## Environment
Invaded by ice, the Silg is an austere place that may seem devoid of life. However, numerous species still inhabit it. Snow-covered high foothills and wind-blown peaks stretch out pristine across the landscape.

## Kaibara's Dwelling
Within this desolate setting, [[Kaibara]] rests in a half-frozen lake dotted with icebergs.

## Ice Formations
The ice of the Silg displays unusual coloration. Many icy formations have a turquoise, semi-transparent hue, while streaks of orange-yellow, pink, or red mark these frozen structures. In the icy seracs, one can sometimes see ideas trapped in the ice, their forms barely discernible.

## Relationships
- **[[Kirighai]]**: Peninsula containing the Silg region
- **[[Katkera]]**: Forest over which the Silg mountains loom
- **[[Kaibara]]**: Tutelary Leviathan dwelling in the region's half-frozen lake
- **[[Enosha]]**: Island separated from Kirighai by the Silg barrier
