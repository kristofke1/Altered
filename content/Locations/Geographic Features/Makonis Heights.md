# Makonis Heights

## Overview
The Makonis Heights is another name for the Province of [[Caer Alcada]], located in the eastern part of the floating [[Enosha]] peninsula, high in the clouds.

## Geography
This region serves as the root of [[Auriga|the Auriga]], the highest peak on [[Enosha]] at 4,879 m (16,000 ft). The Makonis Heights constitute one of [[Asgartha]]'s most bizarre ecosystems, filled with chaotic biomorphic forms such as mounds of pink moss.

## Flora
The grasses, flowers, and groves that grow in the Makonis Heights are distinctively pink or purple in coloration. The trees do not exhibit leaves but rather colored bubbles that grow on their branches. Trees from this region have been planted in [[The Kadigir]].

## Volcanic Areas
Some parts of the Heights are volcanic, with barren ground exhaling toxic vapors. Cloud banks frequently drift over the landscape, absorbing the sulfuric fumes.

## Coral Forests
Other regions are covered with coral forests, resembling maritime reefs but existing on solid ground rather than underwater.

## Relationships
- **[[Enosha]]**: Peninsula containing the Heights
- **[[Caer Alcada]]**: Province synonymous with the Makonis Heights
- **[[Auriga]]**: Highest peak rooted in this region
- **[[The Kadigir]]**: Location where trees from this region have been planted
