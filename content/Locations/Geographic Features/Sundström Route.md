# Sundström Route

## Overview
The Sundström Route is a maritime channel winding through [[Undersea|the Undersea]], connecting [[Suspira]] to [[Arkaster]]. Its inauguration marked a major advancement in Asgarthan maritime trade.

## History
For a long time, ships transporting [[Suspira]]'s ores to the capital faced difficult journeys. The Southwest Passage through [[Shawl|the Shawl]] was a bottleneck, and the Season of Mists forced boats to bypass [[Anthea]] via the [[Biela]], extending journeys by several weeks.

The Undersea was previously considered too impracticable by pilots due to its numerous shallows, persistent mist, and countless wrecks on sharp reefs. However, a navigable route was eventually found and inaugurated as the Sundström Route.

## Impact
The route allowed maritime traffic to finally clear up. Ships could drastically reduce their travel time, and the channel enabled capital shipowners to charter much larger tonnage vessels, the famous [[Mistbreakers]].

## Stopovers
On this channel, [[Dusk]] remains an almost obligatory stopover point, as does the port of [[Gusta]] on the [[Abelena]] archipelago. Travelers are warned against straying from the established path.

## Relationships
- **[[Undersea]]**: Sea through which the route passes
- **[[Suspira]]**: Origin point for ore shipments
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Destination for ore shipments
- **[[Dusk]]**: Obligatory stopover port
- **[[Gusta]]**: Stopover port on the Abelena archipelago
- **[[Abelena]]**: Archipelago where Gusta port is located
- **[[Mistbreakers]]**: Large vessels enabled by this route
- **[[Kelon]]**: Primary cargo transported via this route
