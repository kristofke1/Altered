# Spur

## Overview

The Spur is one of six distinct biome environments within the [[Storhvit]] region. It is the final challenge before reaching the summit of [[Altered/Locations/Regions/Cais Adarra]], consisting of a steep, jagged slope.

## Physical Description

The final challenge before reaching the summit, the Spur is a steep, jagged slope of [[Altered/Locations/Regions/Cais Adarra]]. Its sharp rock formations and sheer cliffs plunge into deep crevasses, making motorized vehicles difficult or nearly impossible to use.

## Ice Formations

In some areas, strange ice bulges of blue topaz emerge from the rocky slopes, forming translucent, ruin-like landscapes. Strangely, the ice appears illuminated from within, as if capturing the essence of the northern lights.
