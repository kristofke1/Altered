# Suspira

## Overview
Suspira is an almost uninhabited volcanic island at the eastern end of [[Asgartha]], made of black stones and sands. The island bathes in an unreal end-of-the-world atmosphere and is called the "whispering island" or the "isle of murmurs," referring to the sulfuric vapors that almost constantly exhale from the ground.

## Climate
The island has a cold oceanic climate.

## Geography
Located far from [[Arkaster]], Suspira features numerous hot springs and sulfur farms. The volcanic nature of the island creates a landscape of black stones and dark sands.

## Political Status
The territory of Suspira is directly under the control of [[Arkaster]], rather than being part of one of the five autonomous Caers.

## Kelon Industry
The main importance of Suspira comes from its high concentration of [[Kelon]], making it an industrial asset for the Asgarthans. [[Axiom]] has established numerous mines and refineries on the island to collect this precious resource.

Suspira is of vital importance to [[Axiom]], which has bet everything on [[Kelon]] to fuel the furnace of its industrial revolution. However, the [[Prospector Guild]] knows that the mines of this precious material are running dry. While current stocks will sustain Asgarthan society for a few years, a solution must be found to maintain the pace in the long term. Despite the general concern, some miners dream of an altered island where vegetation can finally flourish when it is fully stabilized.

## Transportation

### Historical Maritime Routes
For a long time, maritime transport was the main means of transporting Suspira's ores to the capital. Ships initially passed through the Southwest Passage, crossing the shallows of [[Shawl|the Shawl]] and navigating through narrow fjords between [[Anthea]] and [[Kirighai]]. The Season of Mists forced boats to take the [[Biela]] and bypass [[Anthea]] to the south, extending the journey by several weeks. When crossing was possible, the passage created such a bottleneck that the estuary was completely crowded, forcing many vessels to anchor off [[Sojourn]] for days.

### Sundström Route
Maritime traffic cleared up when the [[Sundström Route]] was inaugurated. By winding through [[Undersea|the Undersea]], previously considered too impracticable by pilots, ships could drastically reduce travel time and charter much larger tonnage vessels called [[Mistbreakers]]. On this channel, [[Dusk]] remains an almost obligatory stopover, as does the port of [[Gusta]] on the [[Abelena]] archipelago.

### Air Routes
Today, although maritime routes remain most favored for transporting [[Kelon]] or [[Aerolith|Aerolithe]], [[Axiom]] Guilds increasingly resort to air routes. The mining cities of [[Utval]] and [[Vatigest]] have recently been equipped with aerodromes to more quickly reach [[Arkaster]] or [[Sadracca]], and in future all other cities on the peninsula.
