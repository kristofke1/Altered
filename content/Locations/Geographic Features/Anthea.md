# Anthea

## Overview
Anthea is the largest peninsula in the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan Protectorate]], located south of [[Arkaster|the capital]] beyond the [[Muir Concordia]]. It is connected to other peninsulas by [[Shawl|the Shawl]], a shallow strip of sea.

## Administrative Division
Anthea is divided into two Provinces: [[Caer Kettria]] to the west and [[Caer Arcolano]] to the east.

## Economy
Like [[Enosha]], Anthea is considered an agrarian and pastoral territory. [[Arkaster|The capital]] heavily relies on the food resources from this peninsula to feed its population.

## Relationships
- **[[Caer Kettria]]**: Western province of the peninsula
- **[[Caer Arcolano]]**: Eastern province of the peninsula
- **[[Muir Concordia]]**: Body of water separating Anthea from Arkaster
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Capital city reliant on Anthea's food resources
- **[[Enosha]]**: Fellow agrarian peninsula
