# Cierna

## Overview
The Cierna is a turbulent sea located north of the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan Peninsula]]. Its waters are perilous and treacherous throughout.

## Water Color
The waters of the Cierna have a changing color, ranging from deep blue with turquoise or orange streaks.

## Unique Topography
The Cierna's greatest singularity comes from its topography. Unlike normal seas, it is not entirely flat but structured on many levels. Water holes shape "islands" on the seabed where water cascades down, bypasses the dry area, and then rises on the other side in reverse waterfalls. Elsewhere, water arches swirl in the sky, stirred by powerful currents.

## Relationships
- **[[Asgartha]]**: Peninsula bordered by the Cierna to the north
- **[[Biela]]**: Calmer sea contrasting with the Cierna
