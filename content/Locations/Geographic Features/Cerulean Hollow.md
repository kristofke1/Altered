# Cerulean Hollow

## Overview

Cerulean Hollow is a network of winding tunnels and cavities within [[Storvhit]], discovered during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] in 393 AC. The location serves as the primary habitat for [[Mana Moths]], ethereal moth species composed of pure [[Mana]].

## Discovery

In March 393 AC, a [[Yzmir]] mage exploring the winding tunnels of the Cerulean Hollow happened upon a secluded grotto by pure chance. The hidden cavity, far from the open air, was home to swarms of ethereal [[Mana Moths]]. [[Saskia Averina]] and other scholars were invited to study the creatures and their astonishing properties.

## Physical Description

The Cerulean Hollow consists of:
- **Winding tunnels**: Network of passages through rock or ice
- **Secluded grotto**: Hidden cavity serving as the main Mana Moths habitat
- **Enclosed spaces**: Protected environment far from open air

## Mana Concentration

The cavity housing the [[Mana Moths]] exhibits an exceptionally high concentration of ambient [[Mana]]—comparable almost to that found in a [[Mushs|mush]] nursery. The Moths' presence refines [[Mana]], saturating the air with energy rather than condensing it into droplets.

## Ecological Significance

Cerulean Hollow represents an ideal habitat for [[Mana Moths]] due to its characteristics:
- **Seclusion**: Hidden from sight and protected from the elements
- **Shelter**: Far from open air where predators hunt
- **Protection**: Safe from [[Hakupopo]], which prey on Mana Moth caterpillars in open snow

The enclosed, sheltered nature of the space ensures the survival of the Mana Moths' reproductive cycle.

## Symbiotic Relationship

The discovery of [[Belisenki]] molts in the same areas as [[Mana Moths]] colonies within the Cerulean Hollow supports the theory of symbiotic relationship between the two species. [[Belisenki]] absorb [[Mana]] diffused by the Moths to anchor the concept of cold within themselves, while offering protection to the ethereal butterflies.

## Relationships

- **[[Storvhit]]**: Parent region containing Cerulean Hollow
- **[[Mana Moths]]**: Ethereal species inhabiting the grotto
- **[[Belisenki]]**: Symbiotic species found in same areas
- **[[Saskia Averina]]**: [[Muna]] Naturalist who studied the location (March 393 AC)
- **[[Yzmir]]**: Faction whose mage discovered the site
- **[[Hakupopo]]**: Predators from which the hollow provides protection
