# Acus

## Overview
Acus is a sharp peak in [[Arkaster]], overlooking the [[Reykur]] district. It is the highest point of the entire [[Asgartha|Asgarthan Peninsula]].

## Wright Altiport
At the base of this rocky promontory stands the [[Wright Altiport]], from which airships and aerostats service the entire Peninsula.

## Shadow
Along with [[The Asterion]], the Acus casts shadow over the [[Sjena]] district, keeping it in almost perpetual twilight.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the peak
- **[[Reykur]]**: District overlooked by the Acus
- **[[Wright Altiport]]**: Transportation hub at the base of Acus
- **[[Sjena]]**: District kept in shadow by the peak
- **[[The Asterion]]**: Palace that also casts shadow over Sjena
- **[[Asgartha]]**: Peninsula of which Acus is the highest point
