# Vasanti Park

## Overview

Vasanti Park is a public park in [[Arkaster]] that borders Calle Amsterdam. It serves as the venue for the Ryukkōsai festival celebrating the [[Libellumines]]' annual mating flight.

## Ryukkōsai Festival

During [[Libellumines]] season, the park hosts the Ryukkōsai festival. As shadows grow long on the paths, the insects leave their nymph stage and embark on their ephemeral mating dance. The Midori Matsuri ceremony accompanies the event, with ladies and gentlemen strolling under trees while chefs in hats offer treats.

## Relationships

- **[[Arkaster]]**: City where Vasanti Park is located

- **[[Kuwat Vasanti]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] sharing the park's namesake
