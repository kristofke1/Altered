# Bark Refuge

## Overview
The Bark Refuge is a sanctuary built within the heart of [[The Spindle]], the world tree located in the [[Katkera]]. It serves as the seat of the Spindle and is considered the most important of the nine elemental [[The Refuges|Refuges]] maintained by the [[Muna]].

## Location and Structure
The Refuge is constructed within the massive trunk of [[The Spindle]], utilizing the natural cavity and ecosystem of the world tree. Its entrance opens like a gaping maw, though it appears tiny compared to the immense circumference of the trunk.

The interior features an inner clearing surrounded by moss-covered standing stones, creating a natural ceremonial space. Braziers and lanterns can be positioned around this clearing for rituals.

## Ceremonial Functions

### Musubi Ceremonies
The Bark Refuge is the exclusive location where [[The Musubi]] bonding ceremonies are performed. Only the Muna can celebrate this ceremony. All [[Alterers]] and [[Chimerae]] who wish to bond are invited to the Bark Refuge so that a Muna priest can intertwine their essences.

The Musubi is not only reserved for Alterers and entities of [[The Tumult]]—two individuals can unite for many reasons, whether love, friendship, or mutual interest. A Muna Ollam (venerable priest) officiates these ceremonies.

### Initiation Completion
The Bark Refuge is where the ceremony takes place to officially integrate aspirants into the Muna faction. After completing the [[Nine-Stage Pilgrimage]] and climbing [[The Spindle]] to collect materials, initiates return to the Bark Refuge to weave their [[Namkha Knot]] under the canopy of the world tree.

## Priesthood
The Refuge is maintained by priestesses and priests of the Spindle who perform various ceremonial duties:
- Lighting lanterns and oil lamps with camphor and aromatic herbs (jasmine, sage, magnolia, lavender)
- Bringing baskets of fruits and flowers
- Performing ritual preparations and body paintings
- Officiating bonding ceremonies

## Elemental Affiliation
As one of the nine [[The Refuges|Refuges]], the Bark Refuge is affiliated with the Bark element, representing the living wood and growth aspect of nature's cycle. It serves as a stage within the pilgrimage circuit that connects all Refuges together.
