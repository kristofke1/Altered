# Mugen

## Overview
The Mugen is the unique bridge connecting the island of [[Nisan]] to the [[Kirighai]] peninsula. It provides the only land access to Kirighai, which is otherwise accessible only by water.

## Relationships
- **[[Nisan]]**: Island where the bridge originates
- **[[Kirighai]]**: Peninsula connected by the bridge
- **[[Caer Eidos]]**: Province containing Nisan island
