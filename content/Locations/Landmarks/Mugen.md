# Mugen

## Overview

The Mugen is a sacred wooden bridge linking [[Nisan]] and [[Kirighai]], providing the only land access to the [[Katkera]] forest and the [[Muna]] Refuges. It is regarded as a gateway to the pristine sanctuary of the [[Muna]].

## Structure

The bridge consists of nineteen arched wooden sections hopping between numerous islets of various sizes. On each island stands a torii gate at the foot of which resides an altar dedicated to a specific [[Eidolons|Eidolon]].

## Sacred Significance

The Mugen is regarded as a sacred place. Every visitor is expected to make a stop at each torii to honor the spirits of [[Kirighai]], with each step corresponding to a moment of reflection and communion. This makes crossing the bridge a form of pilgrimage rather than mere transit.

## Origin

The bridge was created by [[Yong-Su Tenzing]], a [[Muna]] Alterer. Yong-Su built the Mugen with the aim of supplying [[Arkaster]] when a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity prevented the capital from being replenished with food and essential resources. What began as a practical solution became one of the most sacred sites in [[Asgartha]].

## Timeline

### 391 AC - Ryukkōsai Festival

During the Ryukkōsai festival, [[Rin]] followed a procession crossing the Mugen bridge. [[Yong-Su Tenzing]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] manifested floating in the air with lotus flowers blooming beneath each step.
