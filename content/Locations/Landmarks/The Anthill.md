# The Anthill

## Overview
The Anthill is the lower part of [[The Monolith]], the white bipyramid Bastion of the [[Ordis]]. It is aptly named, as it teems with life and bureaucratic activity.

## Function and Purpose
Within the Anthill, a myriad of cabinets and offices located all around its perimeter serve as the location where laws and edicts are crafted according to [[The Codex]]. It is the administrative and judicial heart of the [[Ordis]] faction.

## Location and Views
From the Anthill, one can easily contemplate:
- The entire expanse of [[Asgartha]]
- The sea that surrounds the peninsula
- The bustling streets of [[Arkaster]] far below
- The city's markets and port areas

Unfortunately, the clerks and other administrative officers who move about here rarely take the time to appreciate these views, being consumed by their duties.

## Constant Activity
The Anthill buzzes with perpetual motion. Clerks and administrative officers come and go from:
- Offices to counters
- Desks to branches
- Carrying messages, documents, decrees to be signed, and bills to be ratified

This sprawling bureaucracy makes the [[Ordis]] faction a colossus of sometimes excruciating slowness, but it reflects the faction's determination to weigh pros and cons in order to make the right decision regarding any given issue.

## Key Locations Within

### The Oratory
At the very bottom of the pyramid, along walkways suspended over the void, some hurry into the majestic Oratory. Here, rhetoricians:
- Declaim
- Harangue
- Chant

Below the Oratory, beyond the inverted pyramid skylight, the Asgarthi go about their daily activities, visible from above.

### The Assembly
On the upper floors sits the Assembly, where debates continue tirelessly between:
- Marble colonnades
- Benches of a sumptuous amphitheater

The Grand Chancellor arbitrates the deliberations from his lectern. This is where [[The Tetrarchy]]'s recommendations are discussed and refined before being proposed for popular vote.

### The Tribunal
Even higher sits the Tribunal, the body responsible for dispensing justice according to [[The Codex]]. In this vast hall:
- Those who have transgressed are judged
- Verdicts are heard
- Sentences are received
- All proceedings occur under the impartial gaze of the [[Eidolons]]

## Bureaucratic Process
Before recommendations from [[The Tetrarchy]] are proposed for deliberation, they go through a whole process within the Anthill:
- Study and analysis
- Reflection by various departments
- Review from clerk to legate
- Encompassing a whole host of bureaucrats

Once this cautious approach is completed and popular approval secured, the Ordis becomes an unstoppable force of undeniable efficiency.
