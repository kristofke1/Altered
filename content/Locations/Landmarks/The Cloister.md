# The Cloister

## Overview
The Cloister is a medical facility within or near [[The Kadigir]], the [[Yzmir]] Bastion. It serves as a treatment center for those afflicted by magical conditions, particularly those related to [[The Aether|Aether]] exposure and [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities. The facility is staffed by healers including [[Baku]] and [[Caladrius]].

## Purpose and Function
The Cloister specializes in treating cases that involve:
- Magical afflictions caused by [[The Aether|Aether]] exposure
- Conditions related to contact with [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities
- [[Remanence]] and other dangerous magical ailments
- Long-term care and internment for those with unstable conditions

The facility appears to serve as both an acute treatment center and a long-term care facility for [[Yzmir]] members and others who have been affected by the dangerous aspects of magical practice.

### Physical Layout

The Cloister features:
- Inner courtyard with steps
- Balustrade where visitors sit
- Pathways where [[Yzmir]] auxiliaries in red four-pointed star uniforms patrol
- Adjacent to [[The Kadigir]] gardens
- Exposed to seasonal weather (winter brings cold, frost, snow)

Nurses carry bags with red star insignia containing vials and compresses for treating [[Remanence]] episodes. Medical staff maintain watchful presence, ready to intervene during patient crises.

## Treatment of Kumari Adhikari
When young [[Kumari Adhikari]] fell into [[The Well of Tears]] and ingested water containing large amounts of pure [[The Aether|Ether]], she was rescued by an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] and immediately taken to the Cloister for treatment.

Healers [[Baku]] and [[Caladrius]] worked to restore her mental health, but despite their efforts and expertise, they could not fully cure her. [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]] remained subject to visions and bouts of gentle madness, developing [[Remanence]]â€”a dangerous affliction that plunges those affected into trance-like states.

She remained interned within the Cloister at [[The Kadigir]] to ensure her condition did not worsen. Over the years, [[Akesha Adhikari]] would visit her sister during her rare periods of idleness, maintaining their bond despite the tragedy that had separated them.

## Treatment of Oblivion (393 AC)

During the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], the Cloister became a treatment center for [[Oblivion]], an affliction causing progressive memory loss discovered among explorers of the [[City of Scholars]]. [[Sister Mathilde]], an Auxiliary of the Cloister, first identified and documented the condition. Working with [[Rati]] surgeons, healers developed a treatment using memory [[Grafts]]—psychic fragments donated by loved ones that serve as patches for broken minds. These artificial memories allow patients to function, though inconsistencies occur.

## Location Within the Kadigir

The Cloister is situated within or near [[The Kadigir]], allowing it to serve the many [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]] who work with dangerous magical forces. Its proximity to [[The Well of Tears]] and other [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities makes it strategically positioned to provide immediate care for those affected by accidents or exposure to these phenomena.
