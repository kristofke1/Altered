# Yeni

## Overview
Yeni is an isle of [[Arkaster]] located further north than the main urban areas. The island is mostly uninhabited.

## Geography
From Yeni's steep, grassy slopes that plunge sharply into the two arms of [[Nishaanfjord]], one can see the vastness of the [[Cierna]] sea to the north.

## Haven
Only the southern part of Yeni is occupied by [[Haven]], a troglodyte monument co-administered by the [[Bravos]] and the [[Lyra]]. Both a place of residence and a museum of sorts, this citadel hosts members of both Factions during their visits to [[Arkaster]].

### The Sands
Haven also features [[The Sands]], an open-air amphitheater where various competitions, athletic or otherwise, can be held.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City of which Yeni is a constituent island
- **[[Haven]]**: Bravos-Lyra citadel on the southern part of the island
- **[[Nishaanfjord]]**: Fjord into which the island's slopes plunge
- **[[Cierna]]**: Sea visible to the north
- **[[Bravos]]**: Faction co-administering Haven
- **[[Lyra]]**: Faction co-administering Haven
- **[[The Sands]]**: Amphitheater within Haven
