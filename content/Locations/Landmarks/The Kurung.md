# The Kurung

## Overview
The Kurung is the military barracks of [[Arkaster]], housing soldiers of [[The Aegis]] and guards of [[The Damocles]].

## Location
The Kurung is overlooked by the [[Renkairi]] district, which initially housed the families of soldiers and guards stationed at the barracks.

## Impact on Surrounding Areas
Despite the bohemian temperament of the nearby [[Fosa]] district, that area is very safe due to the presence of the Kurung.

## Relationships
- **[[Arkaster]]**: City containing the barracks
- **[[Renkairi]]**: District overlooking the Kurung, originally housed soldier families
- **[[Fosa]]**: District kept safe by the Kurung's presence
- **[[The Aegis]]**: Faction with soldiers stationed at the barracks
- **[[The Damocles]]**: Faction with guards stationed at the barracks
- **[[Ordis]]**: Faction operating the military installation
