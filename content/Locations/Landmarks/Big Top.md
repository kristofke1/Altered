# Big Top

## Overview

The Big Top is a structure aboard [[The Wayfarer]], the [[Sahanka]] of the [[Tisdhera Clan]]. It contains the antechamber where visitors wait before being received by the Clan's Matriarch and the quarters where the Matriarch's representative meets with summoned individuals.

## Function

The Big Top serves as the administrative and ceremonial center for the [[Tisdhera Clan]]'s leadership. When the Matriarch summons clan members through her Shepherd, they wait in the antechamber before being received in the Matriarch's quarters.

## Relationships

- **[[The Wayfarer]]**: Parent Sahanka containing the Big Top
- **[[Tisdhera Clan]]**: Clan whose Matriarch resides here
- **[[Thalia]]**: Shepherdess who represents the Matriarch
- **[[Nadir]]**: Summoned here with family after the Tumult incident
