# The Vivarium

## Overview

The Vivarium is an artificial habitat within the [[Nilam Spires]] at [[The Screed]] outpost where teams of entomologists raise swarms of [[Mana Moths]]. The facility was crafted from [[The Nilam|Nilam]] bark and branches.

## Purpose

The Vivarium enables study of [[Mana Moths]] and their intrinsic duality, hovering at the boundary between reality and illusion. Research at the facility focuses on understanding the creatures' unique properties and potential applications.

## Supervision

The Vivarium operates under the joint supervision of [[Moyo Chibuye]] and [[Saskia Averina]], combining [[Muna]] exobiological expertise with naturalist research on species interactions.
