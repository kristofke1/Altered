# The Refinery

## Overview

The Refinery is an [[Axiom]] facility at [[The Screed]], serving as the centerpiece of the outpost's operations. Established in 393 AC, it processes [[Kelon]] deposits harvested from [[Caer Nilam]] into usable ore for Axiom machinery.

## Location and Construction

The site was carefully selected to serve as a secondary shaft for bringing up goods and resources recovered from the buried [[City of Scholars]]. The facility was constructed near a much smaller opening in the Screed than the [[Crow's Eye]], built atop a partially collapsed annex. The Refinery expanded into a city within the city as the foundry grew with buildings needed for refining and storing Kelon.

## Operations

### Kelon Processing

Axiom energy specialists have expressed concern that the meager Kelon stockpiles recovered from the icy wastes may soon create shortages impacting the technical units of the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. Beyond Kelon processing, Axiom researchers study the amber-hued liquid found in the [[Cais Adarra]] and the upper city murals to determine whether it might serve as an alternative energy source for the Faction's devices.

### Sap Conversion

The Refinery underwent a five-week standstill for reconfiguration from [[Kelon]] processing to [[Sap]] operations. The conversion required the [[Axiom]] community's brightest engineers to check, recalculate, and adjust systems while workers connected, diverted, and reconnected conduits. Upon successful activation, pumps started and fluid flowed through conduits extending to the [[City of Scholars]], producing a distinctive sucking noise before Sap rushed into vats in gloopy eruptions that gradually settled into regular humming.

The converted facility produces Sap fuel in cartridges, cylinders, and tanks that complement or replace Kelon supplies, addressing the expedition's energy shortage.

### Sap Distribution

The converted facility includes a raw-material pumping installation that extends into the [[City of Scholars]] to bring up Sap from collectors placed at strategic locations in [[The Undergrowth]] and [[The Scholars' Tomb]]. Processed Sap is distributed to [[The Farm]], [[The Mess]], airfields, factories, and workshops across [[The Screed]].
