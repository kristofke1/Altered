# Haven

## Overview
Haven is a Bastion and homeport for all [[Bravos]], as well as a troglodyte monument co-administered by the [[Bravos]] and the [[Lyra]]. Located on the southern tip of the isle of [[Yeni]] where the [[Nishaanfjord]] splits into two branches, Haven was built in the heart of the cliff, high above the turquoise waters.

Both a place of residence and a museum of sorts, this citadel hosts members of both Factions during their visits to [[Arkaster]].

## Architecture
When the sun's rays hit the Bastion, its walls can be seen glowing with long frescoes covered in gilded depictions of the exploits of the most famous [[Bravos]] adventurers. The interior of Haven is a labyrinth of corridors and rooms carved into the stone, once again engraved with thousands of evocative scenes.

While one can admire numerous statues and frescoes of past heroes, Haven also features many workshops, halls, dormitories, and training rooms.

## The Sands
Haven features [[The Sands]], an open-air amphitheater where various competitions, athletic or otherwise, can be held.

## Activities
It is not uncommon to see [[Bravos]] sweating profusely, training tirelessly in one of these chambers, toasting near a hearth, recounting their adventures and exploits, or paying homage to their fallen or missing comrades. It is also where they unwind between expeditions, regaining their strength and exchanging with the [[Lyra]] who share this base with them.

The atmosphere is jovial and warm, much like the hearth around which they gather.

## Administrative Status
Despite its relative distance from the capital, Haven is officially considered a district of [[Arkaster]].

## Relationship with Lyra
In gratitude for centuries-old hospitality, the [[Lyra]] frequently lend their pens to record and set to music the bravest deeds of the [[Bravos]].
