# Dead World-Tree at Storvhit

## Overview

Dead World-Tree at [[Storvhit]] is ancient fossilized world-tree discovered by [[Rin]] and [[Arjun]] during 393 AC expedition, located deep within mountain gorge near [[Altered/Locations/Regions/Cais Adarra]]. Tree died long ago with wood transforming into unidentifiable opalescent crystalline substance, serving as focal point for massive [[Mana Moths]] swarms.

## Physical Description

### Crystallized Roots

Massive crystallized roots emerge from ground spanning at least ten feet above surface, six feet across, and twenty feet in length. Interior displays intricate network of vein-like lines tracing curves throughout structure. Roots rise from earth and plunge back down barely skimming rocky ground, creating dense protrusions twisting and snaking through debris. Opalescent material resembles fossilized river made of ice or crystal. Additional roots jut from sheer cliffs or thread through them like caterpillars burrowing through apple flesh. Root system spreads throughout cerulean moraine and rocky gorge pass.

### Cyclopean Trunk

Vast trunk curves along rock face as if growing pushed two cliffs apart to make room within mountain. Trunk located leagues away from initial root discovery sites, filling gorge with network of branches sprawling outward. Heart glows with sapphire color visible through crystalline wood structure. Network creates fractal azure light throughout gorge. Scale described as cyclopean and unbelievable, stretching toward sky and overwhelming observers with magnificence.

### Transformed Wood

Original wood transformed into substance resembling enormous gemstone or opal, far larger than manatee. Material appears translucent allowing viewing of interior structures. Transformation occurred over extended period making identification of substance impossible. Crystalline nature suggests fundamental change in composition from organic to mineral-like state.

## Discovery - 393 AC

[[Arjun]] and [[Rin]] discovered location while following water course through supraglacial lake and moulin passages during [[Storvhit]] expedition. Initial discovery involved massive roots in cerulean moraine scattered with boulders fallen from above. [[Arjun]] identified structures as roots probably belonging to world-tree similar to [[The Spindle]]. [[Rin]] connected to [[The Skein]] attempting to greet ancient entity but felt deep sorrow discovering tree died long ago. [[Arjun]] placed comforting hand on [[Rin]]'s shoulder acknowledging grief while noting importance of understanding what happened to prevent same fate befalling [[The Spindle|Katkera]]. Continued exploration led to discovery of main trunk surrounded by massive [[Mana Moths]] swarms creating murmuration patterns like rivers of wings.

## Ecological Significance

### Mana Moths Relationship

Massive [[Mana Moths]] swarms congregate around dead tree, encircling trunk with heart glowing sapphire color. Swarms create vast murmuration patterns appearing initially like dense foliage before revealing true nature. Discovery revealed [[Mana Moths]] maintain relationship with world-trees even after death, suggesting deep ecological connection. Dead tree serves as focal point for [[Storvhit]]'s [[Mana Moths]] population. Scale of congregation indicates central importance to species distribution and behavior patterns. Observation confirmed [[Saskia Averina]]'s hypothesis about [[Mana Moths]] playing central role in [[Storvhit]] ecosystem.

### Connection to Skein

[[Rin]]'s attempt to connect through [[The Skein]] revealed tree no longer maintains active presence within energetic web. Death occurred sufficiently long ago that wood underwent complete transformation. Absence from [[The Skein]] represents significant loss to [[Mana]] web that would have connected through this world-tree as knot. Discovery raises questions about what caused death and how long ago tree perished.

## Comparison to The Spindle

[[Arjun]] identified structure as world-tree similar to [[The Spindle]], suggesting common origin or nature. Both serve as knots in [[The Skein]] connecting [[Mana]] web across regions. [[Arjun]]'s stated purpose for studying dead tree: understanding what happened to prevent same fate befalling [[The Spindle|Katkera]]. Discovery provides evidence that world-trees can die, making [[The Spindle]]'s withering more concerning. Crystallization and fossilization represent end state not yet reached by [[The Spindle]].

## Location Context

Tree located deep within [[Storvhit]] mountain gorge as if great rift split peak in two. Rocky pass features sheer cliffs stretching toward storm-laden sky with harsh wind whistling through canyon. Area bathed in shades of blue creating ice cavern effect. Cerulean moraine stretches for miles scattered with massive boulders fallen from above. Frozen waterfall clings to fractured cliff crowned with dozens of stalactites. Water courses through supraglacial lake and steep moulin before reaching tree location. Protected somewhat from harsh blizzards by gorge walls though environment remains extremely cold.
