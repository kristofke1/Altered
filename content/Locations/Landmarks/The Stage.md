# The Stage

## Overview

The Stage is a [[Lyra]] mobile performance venue at [[The Screed]], established in 393 AC to boost morale among the [[Expeditionary Corps]] after months of hardship.

## Design and Equipment

The Stage is crafted in the shape of a flying beetle with vibrating wings. It serves as the hub of entertainment, equipped with everything the [[Axiom]] could provide: giant screens, spotlights, blazing neon lights, and pyrotechnic gear. Like a parade float, The Stage can glide above rooftops to shift locations, though its insectoid silhouette drifting across the outpost sometimes annoys those trying to sleep.

## Performances

The venue hosts theater, altavista skits, one-woman and one-man shows, and concerts. Notable performers have included [[Kasirga Clan]] acrobats and [[Jack Frost]]'s ice sculptures. Scheduled upcoming acts include [[Nyala]], [[Auraq]], and the band [[BLISS!]].

## Purpose

The Lyra took on a mission to nourish the spirit alongside the body. After months of hardship, morale among the Expeditionary Corps had reached a low point, and the Faction was determined to address it.
