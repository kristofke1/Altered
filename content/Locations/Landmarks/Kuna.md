# Kuna

## Overview
Kuna is one of the major islands of [[Arkaster]], a massive rock that rises high above the lagoon level. It is steeped in history, as its presence convinced the [[Tumult Nomads]] to settle here, relatively sheltered from the overwhelming [[The Tumult|Tumult]] winds.

## Political Center
The western end of Kuna, overlooking [[Felsenwel]], houses many of the Republic's centers of power. Like a colossal Mont-Saint-Michel, the palace of the [[Basileus]] dominates the bay, visible from almost everywhere in the city.

### Key Structures
- **[[Kuningasplatz]]**: The vast political plaza
- **[[The Asterion]]**: Palace with sharp spires, perched on a protrusion of the island

This part of Kuna, being the administrative and political center of the Peninsula, is constantly under the joint protection of [[The Aegis]] and [[The Damocles]].

## Districts
The [[Tesh Kanat]] district is built along the enormous cliffside of Kuna, home to the [[Old Nobility]]. The heights of the island, extending from the Tesh district, have been covered with large terraced properties—the most coveted residences for the wealthy population of the Peninsula.

The district of [[Renkairi]] is expanding into the heights of Kuna as population density increases.

## Underground Features
Kuna is riddled with a multitude of underground tunnels and caverns, some of which plunge hundreds of meters down to the water level.

## Geography and Features
The island is characterized by:
- Vineyards (frost-covered during [[Season of Mists]])
- Cypresses rising like lances, silhouetted against the misty lagoon
- Views across fjord to [[The Foundry]]'s wind turbines

## ruun-Aysun Villa
The [[House ruun-Aysun|ruun-Aysun family]] maintains a villa retreat on Kuna featuring a deserted courtyard with white stone statues, manicured gardens, and a terrace overlooking the sea. The villa is maintained by [[Asor]] (gardener) and his wife [[Dorota]].

### 392 AC - Conversation Between Siblings
Shortly before the departure of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], [[Sigismar Synd]] retreated to the family villa during the [[Season of Mists]]. His sister [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] found him there and revealed her engagement to [[Amaro Arundhani]], asking Sigismar to remember "those who stay behind."
