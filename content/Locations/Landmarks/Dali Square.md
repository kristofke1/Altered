# Dali Square

## Overview

Dali Square is a public square aboard [[The Wayfarer]], the [[Sahanka]] of the [[Tisdhera Clan]].

## Notable Events

[[Nadir]] used his [[Alteration]] abilities to conjure an entire playground on Dali Square in a single afternoon, creating slides, merry-go-rounds, swings, candy-apple stalls, and carnival rides. This display of demiurgic power drew dismay from the [[Tisdhera Clan]] elders.

## Relationships

- **[[The Wayfarer]]**: Parent Sahanka containing Dali Square
- **[[Tisdhera Clan]]**: Clan whose elders disapproved of Nadir's playground creation
- **[[Nadir]]**: [[Exalts|Exalt]] who conjured a playground here through [[Alteration]]
