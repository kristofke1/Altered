# Musk Refuge

## Overview

The Musk Refuge is one of the nine sanctuaries within [[The Refuges]] system in the [[Katkera]] forest. It is dedicated to the animals of the Peninsula and was the place of residence of [[Eru]], one of [[Niavhe]]'s earliest disciples.

## History

[[Eru]] chose to settle in the [[Katkera]] as a hermit, with only the wild species of the primordial forest as companions. Over time, his place of residence became the Musk Refuge.

## Pilgrimage

Those who undertake the [[Nine-Stage Pilgrimage]] stop at the Musk Refuge for a time. It lies between the Refuges of Wind and Flow on the pilgrimage circuit.

## Eru's Manifestation

During their stay at the Musk Refuge, pilgrims may speak with [[Eru]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]], who still manifests in this place. In every such encounter, Eru advocates harmony among all forms of life, teaching that cooperation is the unmistakable sign of a strong and enduring ecosystem.

## Relationships

- **[[The Refuges]]**: System of nine sanctuaries to which Musk Refuge belongs
- **[[Katkera]]**: Forest where the Musk Refuge is located
- **[[Eru]]**: Oneiros whose Eidolon manifests here; former resident
- **[[Nine-Stage Pilgrimage]]**: Initiatory journey that passes through the Musk Refuge
- **[[Muna]]**: Faction that maintains the Refuge
