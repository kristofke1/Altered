# The Corolla

## Overview

The Corolla is the [[Ordis]] administrative hub at [[The Screed]], also known as the town hall. Established in 393 AC, it handles all matters of oversight for future expeditions and day-to-day administration of the outpost.

## Functions

The facility manages general administration for the [[Expeditionary Corps]], coordinating operations across all Factions present at the outpost. The west wing houses [[The Aegis]] garrison and barracks.

## Relationships

- **[[The Screed]]**: Parent location containing The Corolla
- **[[Ordis]]**: Faction that operates the facility
- **[[Town Square]]**: Central plaza located directly in front of The Corolla
- **[[The Aegis]]**: Military force garrisoned in the west wing
- **[[Expeditionary Corps]]**: Force administered from The Corolla
