# The Cebir (Western Commandery)

## Overview
The Western Commandery, known as the Cebir, is a citadel and outpost built by the [[Bravos]] and [[Ordis]] within the Terra Incognita in the province of [[Caer Oorun]]. The fortress has a troubled history of abandonment, disaster, and eventual reclamation, serving as a symbol of both Asgarthan ambition and the dangers of the [[The Tumult|Tumult]].

## Construction and First Abandonment (327-329 AC)

### Initial Construction
The Cebir was erected in 327 AC within the Terra Incognita as a first outpost and forward base to re-explore the world. The citadel was built through collaboration between:
- [[Bravos]] military forces
- [[Ordis]] architects who actively participated in the project
- Significant investment of Ordis funds

The location was chosen in [[Caer Oorun]], a province to the west of [[Arkaster]], accessible by sailing through increasingly icy waters filled with small icebergs and dangerous ice floes.

### First Abandonment
The living conditions proved so harsh that the [[Bravos]] had to abandon the Cebir only two years after its construction in 329 AC. This surrender was a painful setback and deep dishonor for both the [[Bravos]] faction and the [[Ordis]], which had invested colossal funds in the enterprise.

The failure nearly convinced the Asgarthans that any lasting expedition beyond the borders of the Peninsula was impossible.

## The Second Expedition Disaster (352 AC)

### The Dagger of Esvander
For many [[Bravos]], remaining on such a defeat was unthinkable. In 352 AC, a Bravos Company called the Dagger of Esvander set out to reconquer the fortress, with the approval of other captains from [[Haven]]. The nineteen-year-old [[Basira al-Arshad]] managed to secure a place on one of the faction's longships.

### Discovery of the Laboratory
Upon arrival, the two longships found the citadel's physical integrity had suffered little from the passage of years, despite some Tumult currents altering the walls. However, what they discovered inside sent chills down their spines.

The common hall had been turned into an experimentation laboratory:
- Esoteric diagrams covered the walls
- Cages contained bones of strange creatures
- Tables held vials, alembics, Erlenmeyer flasks, burners, burettes, and test tubes
- A hidden annex contained a cage with a tortured Chimera (later revealed to be [[Kaizaimon]])

### The Attack
While exploring the citadel, the expedition was attacked by infernal creatures - monstrous hybrids with claws and fangs. [[Basira al-Arshad]] identified them as [[Eidolons]]:
- [[Mantichora]] - A creature with a serpent's tail and lion's head and chest
- [[Khimaira]] - A second hybrid monster

These Eidolons, which should have been bound by Yzmir Mandates and impossible for anyone to summon, quickly slaughtered [[Basira al-Arshad|Basira]]'s comrades. She discovered [[Kaizaimon]] imprisoned in a hidden cage and freed him. The Chimera transformed into a tattoo on her body and kept her alive in freezing waters after she was swept away by ship currents.

### Sole Survivor
[[Basira al-Arshad]] was the sole survivor of the expedition. An [[Ordis]] airship rescued her after two days of searching for potential survivors. Upon return to [[Arkaster]], authorities dismissed her claims about [[Eidolons]] as hallucinations induced by cold, fatigue, and stress.

## The Conspiracy

### The Laboratory
The Cebir served as a base camp for a renegade [[Yzmir]] Initiate conducting blasphemous research and experiments. [[Afanas Korovinovich]], who lived at the Cebir for two years during an earlier expedition, suspected that:
- The conspirator controlled the [[Black Leviathan|Kraken]]
- The laboratory was used for heretical magical experiments
- The same individual caused the Tsunami of 305 AC
- The creatures that attacked were deliberately manifested

### The Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock
The conspirator became known as the Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock ([[Sylas]]). The scent of chrysanthemums became associated with the tormentor. When [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]] summoned this scent through [[Alteration]] at the reclaimed Cebir in 392 AC, [[Kaizaimon]] burst forth in rage, recognizing it from his torture.

## Reclamation (392 AC)

### Return of the Expeditionary Corps
In 392 AC, the forces of [[Expeditionary Corps]] successfully reclaimed the Cebir. The citadel was in pitiful state after three-quarters of a century (75 years) of abandonment:
- Half-rotten railings
- Wooden structures in disrepair
- Dark, rumbling storm clouds overhead
- Biting winds and icy conditions

### Multi-Faction Operation
The reclamation involved all major factions:
- **[[Axiom]]** engineers inspected equipment to ensure machines withstood the cold
- **[[Muna]]** quartermasters stacked barrels and crates of provisions
- **[[Ordis]]** overseers meticulously cataloged every resource
- **[[Bravos]]** forces including [[Basira al-Arshad]] returned
- **[[Yzmir]]** representatives including [[Afanas Korovinovich]]

Two Asgarthan ships were anchored among icebergs in the bay, with flotillas of rowboats and dinghies unloading cargo in constant back-and-forth motion. The provisions were sufficient to supply the citadel and stock expeditions for months or even years.

### Alliance Against the Conspirator
At the reclaimed Cebir, [[Basira al-Arshad]] and [[Afanas Korovinovich]] formed an uneasy alliance to hunt the Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock. With the [[Black Leviathan|Kraken]] fallen, the Province of [[Caer Oorun]] seemed poised to rejoin the fold of [[Asgartha]] once and for all.

## Physical Description

### Location and Access
The Cebir is located in the province of [[Caer Oorun]], west of [[Arkaster]]. Access requires:
- Sailing through increasingly cold waters
- Navigating through small icebergs and dangerous ice floes
- Venturing through narrow fjords
- Days of travel in dropping temperatures

### Structure
The citadel features:
- A common hall (later converted to laboratory)
- Inner courtyard for unloading supplies
- Wooden balconies and walkways
- Multiple towers including a highest tower for surveying surroundings
- Annexed spaces and hidden chambers
- An icy bay for ship anchorage
- Wooden beams and railings throughout

### Climate
The Cebir exists in an extremely cold environment:
- Snow-covered peaks visible beyond
- Ice embedded in clothes
- Suffering from cold even for hardy veterans
- Icicles hanging from bent gutters

## Historical Significance

The Cebir represents:
- The first major attempt to establish a permanent presence in Terra Incognita
- A monument to both [[Bravos]] ambition and the dangers of the [[The Tumult|Tumult]]
- Evidence of conspiracy against [[Asgartha]]
- A forward base for [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]
- The site of [[Basira al-Arshad]]'s greatest trauma and eventual return

The fortress stands as both a warning of the Tumult's dangers and a symbol of Asgarthan determination to reclaim the world beyond their borders.
