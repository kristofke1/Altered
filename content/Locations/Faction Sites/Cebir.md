# The Cebir (Western Commandery)

## Overview
The Cebir is a citadel in [[Caer Oorun]] within the Terra Incognita, built by the [[Bravos]] and [[Ordis]] as an outpost for re-exploring the world. The fortress was abandoned twice before being reclaimed in 392 AC.

## Location
The citadel is located in [[Caer Oorun]], west of [[Arkaster]]. Access requires sailing through increasingly cold waters filled with icebergs and dangerous ice floes, then navigating narrow fjords.

## Structure
The citadel contains a common hall, inner courtyard, wooden balconies and walkways, multiple towers, and an icy bay for ship anchorage.

## Timeline

### 327 AC - Construction
The [[Bravos]] and [[Ordis]] erected the Cebir as the first outpost in Terra Incognita. The [[Ordis]] invested significant funds and provided architects for the project.

### 329 AC - First Abandonment
Harsh living conditions forced the [[Bravos]] to abandon the fortress after only two years.

### 352 AC - Second Expedition Disaster
A [[Bravos]] Company called the Dagger of Esvander attempted to reconquer the fortress. [[Basira al-Arshad]], then nineteen years old, joined the expedition. The group discovered the common hall had been converted into an experimentation laboratory containing esoteric diagrams, caged creature remains, and alchemical equipment. A hidden annex contained an imprisoned [[Chimera]] later identified as [[Kaizaimon]].

The expedition was attacked by [[Eidolons]]—[[Mantichora]] and [[Khimaira]]—that killed most of the group. [[Basira al-Arshad]] freed [[Kaizaimon]], who bonded with her as a tattoo and kept her alive in freezing waters. She was the sole survivor, rescued by an [[Ordis]] airship after two days.

### 392 AC - Reclamation
The [[Expeditionary Corps]] reclaimed the Cebir after 75 years of abandonment. All major factions participated: [[Axiom]] engineers inspected equipment, [[Muna]] quartermasters managed provisions, [[Ordis]] overseers cataloged resources, and [[Yzmir]] representatives including [[Afanas Korovinovich]] attended.

At the reclaimed citadel, [[Basira al-Arshad]] and [[Afanas Korovinovich]] formed an alliance to hunt the conspirator responsible for the laboratory—the Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock ([[Sylas]]).

## The Conspiracy
The Cebir served as a base for a renegade [[Yzmir]] Initiate conducting forbidden experiments. [[Afanas Korovinovich]], who lived at the Cebir during an earlier expedition, concluded that the conspirator controlled the [[Black Leviathan|Kraken]] and caused the [[305 AC Tsunami]]. When [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]] summoned the scent of chrysanthemums through [[Alteration]] at the reclaimed Cebir, [[Kaizaimon]] recognized it from his torture and reacted violently.
