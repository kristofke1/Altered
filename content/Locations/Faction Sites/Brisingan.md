# Brisingan

## Overview
Brisingan was a [[Lyra]] Caravanserai located in [[Anthea]]. It served as the base of operations for [[Amahle Kalu]]'s uprising during [[The Fratricidal War]]. The caravanserai was dismantled following the defeat of the Prophet's forces.

## Role in the Fratricidal War
From Brisingan, the man who called himself the Prophet coordinated militias skilled in guerrilla warfare. [[Amahle Kalu]]'s intentions from this location were clear: [[Anthea]] was to be placed under the supervision of the [[Lyra]] Clan [[The Nine Matriarchs|Matriarchs]].

His fiery sermons claimed that [[The Nifir]] was a divine punishment for colluding with the [[Oneiroi]]. His only solution was for [[Asgartha]] to withdraw from the southern peninsula and for the so-called [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] cult to ultimately fade away.

Brisingan sheltered the rebel armies throughout the years of simmering conflict as [[Amahle Kalu]]'s forces engaged in guerrilla warfare against the combined [[Factions]].

## Dismantling
After years of conflict, the Prophet's loyalists were finally cornered by a grand opposition in the plains of Cathor-Firi. When the Council of Four's forces swept over them, the enemy ranks were swiftly overpowered. [[Amahle Kalu]] was captured on the battlefield and handed over to the justice of [[The Nine Matriarchs|the Matriarchs]].

Brisingan, which had sheltered the rebel armies, was dismantled as part of the resolution to [[The Fratricidal War]].
