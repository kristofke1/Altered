# Brisingan

## Overview
Brisingan was a [[Lyra]] Caravanserai located in [[Anthea]]. It served as the base of operations for [[Amahle Kalu]]'s uprising during [[The Fratricidal War]] and was dismantled following the defeat of the Prophet's forces.

## Role in the Fratricidal War
[[Amahle Kalu]], who called himself the Prophet, coordinated militias skilled in guerrilla warfare from Brisingan. His goal was to place [[Anthea]] under the supervision of the [[Lyra]] Clan [[The Nine Matriarchs|Matriarchs]].

He preached that [[The Nifir]] was divine punishment for colluding with the [[Oneiroi]], demanding that [[Asgartha]] withdraw from the southern peninsula and that the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] practices cease.

Brisingan sheltered the rebel armies throughout the years of conflict as [[Amahle Kalu]]'s forces engaged in guerrilla warfare against the combined [[Factions]].

## Dismantling
The Prophet's loyalists were cornered by a combined opposition in the plains of Cathor-Firi and defeated. [[Amahle Kalu]] was captured and handed over to [[The Nine Matriarchs|the Matriarchs]] for judgment. Brisingan was dismantled as part of the war's resolution.
