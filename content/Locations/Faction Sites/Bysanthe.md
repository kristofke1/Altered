# Bysanthe

## Overview
Bysanthe is a citadel located north of the [[Vagheria]] peninsula, serving as one of the sixteen strongholds along [[The Rampart]]. It is one of the two most important strongholds, alongside the [[Solstice Gate]].

## Strategic Location
The fortress oversees the open waterway between the [[Muir Concordia]] and the [[Elikadura Fjords]]. This passage, nestled between high majestic walls, serves as a critical maritime corridor.

## Historical Use
The waterway overlooked by Bysanthe was used by the [[Bravos]] and the [[Ordis]] to transport materials for the construction of [[Cebir]], as well as to launch perilous ventures off the [[Caer Oorun]] coasts.

## Structure
Like other strongholds along the Rampart, Bysanthe is topped with a floating tower housing [[Kelon]] cores that power the magical turbines pushing [[The Tumult]] currents outward.
