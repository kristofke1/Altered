# The Megalith

## Overview
The Megalith is a titanic structure that floats in the air within [[The Well of Tears]], deep beneath [[The Kadigir]]. It is suspected to be a relic of the Confluence—a mass of [[The Aether|Aether]] that has been trapped between the two worlds.

## Physical Description
The Megalith presents as:
- A titanic floating structure
- Emitting violet reflections
- Vibrating with power
- Seemingly suspended between worlds
- Hovering above the underground lake's waters

The structure defies normal physics, floating without visible support in the natural cavity beneath the Yzmir Bastion.

## Nature and Origin
The [[Yzmir]] suspect the Megalith to be a relic of the Confluence—the cataclysmic event that brought [[The Tumult]] into the world. It appears to be a concentrated mass of [[The Aether|Aether]] that became trapped between the dimension of reality and [[The Empyrean]], the world of imagination.

## Current Status
While the Megalith is currently dormant, the [[Yzmir]] must constantly monitor it as one of two persistent [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities within [[The Kadigir]]. There is nothing to say it couldn't awaken one day and jeopardize [[Arkaster|the capital]].

## Yzmir Theory
The [[Yzmir]] predict that if the barrier between worlds managed to close in this place where the Megalith is trapped, then the still-open breaches elsewhere can be sealed. This could be a way to silence [[The Tumult]].

However, many occult theorists believe that true stability will only occur when the two dimensions have fully merged, suggesting the Megalith represents an incomplete transition between states of reality.

## Source of Megalith Fragments
The Megalith serves a crucial function in Yzmir initiation rituals. When aspirants become full [[Initiates]], they are allowed access to [[The Well of Tears]] to:
1. Take a small hammer and chisel
2. Carve out a [[Megalith Fragment]]
3. Have it set into a jewel to carry at all times

This extremely rare material acts as a catalyst for magical power, allowing [[Initiates]] to manifest imagination with greater efficiency when practicing [[The Naali]].

## Protective Containment
The Megalith is one of the dangerous phenomena that required protection and containment for the safety of the population of [[Arkaster|the First City]]. This was one reason [[Aysun]] founded [[The Kadigir]] at this location—someone had to monitor and control such potentially catastrophic forces.

## Significance
The Megalith represents:
- A direct physical remnant of the Confluence
- A potential key to understanding or reversing [[The Tumult]]
- A source of immense magical power
- An ongoing threat requiring constant vigilance
- Evidence of the incomplete barrier between dimensions
