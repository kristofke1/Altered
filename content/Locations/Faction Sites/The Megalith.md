# The Megalith

## Overview
The Megalith is a titanic structure that floats within [[The Well of Tears]], deep beneath [[The Kadigir]]. The [[Yzmir]] suspect it to be a relic of [[The Confluence]]—a mass of [[The Aether|Aether]] trapped between reality and [[The Empyrean]].

## Physical Description
The Megalith emits violet reflections and vibrates with power. It hovers above the underground lake's waters without visible support.

## Monitoring
The [[Yzmir]] constantly monitor the Megalith as one of two persistent [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities within [[The Kadigir]]. The structure is currently dormant. Its presence was one reason [[Aysun]] founded [[The Kadigir]] at this location.

## Yzmir Theory
The [[Yzmir]] theorize that if the barrier between worlds closed where the Megalith is trapped, the still-open breaches elsewhere could be sealed. Some occult theorists believe true stability will only occur when the two dimensions have fully merged.

## Megalith Fragments
The Megalith is the source of [[Megalith Fragment|Megalith Fragments]]. When aspirants become full [[Initiates]], they descend to [[The Well of Tears]], carve out a small fragment with hammer and chisel, and have it set into a jewel. This material acts as a catalyst for magical power, improving efficiency when practicing [[The Naali]].
