# The Kadigir

## Overview
The Kadigir, also known as the Occult College, is the Bastion of the [[Yzmir]] faction. It serves as the center of magical learning and research for [[Initiates]] who practice [[The Naali]]. The Kadigir sits at the summit of the rocky promontory in [[Kuth Kanat]], with levitating elevators connecting the fjord waters below to the hanging gardens above.

## Location and Founding
At the summit of the rocky promontory in [[Kuth Kanat]] sits the Kadigir, the Alteration School of the [[Yzmir]] Sorcerers. An impressive irrigation system channels water from a high-altitude lake, [[Ayna]], to the verdant terraces, before supplying the workshops of the [[Axiom]] Bastion below. Levitating elevators connect the fjord waters to the hanging gardens above.

Founded by [[Aysun]], daughter of the first High King of [[Asgartha]], the Yzmir Faction chose this location for several reasons:
- Its secluded position from the capital
- Its position atop high cliffs bordering the lacustrian city
- The presence of [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities that needed careful monitoring

The Kadigir houses dangerous phenomena like [[The Well of Tears]] and [[The Black Wave]] that require protection and containment for the safety of the population of the First City.

## Structure and Layout
The Kadigir is a vast complex consisting of:
- A series of esplanades and temples
- Hanging gardens of unparalleled magnificence
- A building for each of [[The Spheres|the five Spheres]] of [[The Naali]]
- Numerous buildings dedicated to specialized Lodges

## The Hanging Gardens
Overlooking the city of [[Arkaster]] from high above, the hanging gardens are composed of a hilly maze featuring:
- Shaded alleys and terraces
- Ancient trees: cypresses, cedars, maples
- Glasshouses and orangeries
- Fountains and artificial streams irrigating all green spaces
- Elegant colonnades
- Temples
- Observatories for studying the night sky or [[The Tumult]]

The gardens function as a true arboretum where the [[Yzmir]] often meditate, study, or instruct young [[Initiates]]. The serenity of the place provides an ideal environment for contemplation and learning.

## The Five Sphere Temples
Each of [[The Spheres|the five Spheres]] has its own dedicated temple within the Kadigir:
1. Temple of [[Eidomancy]]
2. Temple of [[Horomancy]]
3. Temple of [[Phonomancy]]
4. Temple of [[Kinemancy]]
5. Temple of [[Gephyromancy]]

Each temple serves as a learning place for [[Initiates]], hosting teachers and disciples devoted to that particular magical discipline.

## Tumult Singularities
The Kadigir contains two persistent singularities that must be constantly monitored:

**[[The Well of Tears]]**: An underground lake with a floating [[The Megalith|Megalith]], suspected to be a relic of the Confluence. While dormant, it could potentially awaken and jeopardize the capital.

**[[The Black Wave]]**: A plain of black grass constantly bathed in twilight darkness. Monstrosities sometimes burst forth unexpectedly and must be defeated before they threaten the population.

## Purpose and Mission
The Kadigir serves as:
- The center of Yzmir magical education
- A research facility for studying [[The Aether|Aether]] and [[Alteration]]
- A defensive position protecting [[Arkaster]] from occult threats
- The seat of [[The Magisterium]], the High Council of the Yzmir

The [[Yzmir]] do not boast of their protective role; they keep it secret to prevent the people of Asgartha from succumbing to panic about the dangers lurking at the edge of existence.
