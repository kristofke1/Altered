# Nexus

The Nexus is a sphere of concentrated [[Kelon]] that serves as the energy core of [[The Athanor]]. It floats in the air at the center of the facility, powering all the equipment.

## Function

The Nexus serves as:
- Primary power source for all equipment in [[The Athanor]]
- Energy source for [[Constructs]] creation
- Central focal point of the facility

## Construct Creation

It is near the Nexus energy core that [[Constructs]] are created, utilizing the concentrated [[Kelon]] energy to empower these mechanical artifacts that allow non-[[Alterers]] to manipulate reality.
