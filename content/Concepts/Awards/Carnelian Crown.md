# Carnelian Crown

## Overview

The Carnelian Crown is the highest honor in [[Asgartha]], taking the form of a laurel wreath. It is bestowed personally by the [[Basileus]] upon distinguished champions and explorers.

## Description

The Crown is a laurel wreath with a reddish hue. The award ceremony involves the recipient ascending a dais before the Basileus, who places the Crown upon the recipient's head.

## Known Recipients

- **[[Abelen Sundström]]**: Historical recipient
- **[[Aroro Niski]]**: Historical recipient
- **[[Kojo Oduro]]**: Awarded in November 392 AC for his role in defeating the [[Kraken]]
