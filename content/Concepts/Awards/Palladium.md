# Palladium

## Overview

The Palladium is the highest honor a Leviathan hunter can earn. Recipients wear the Palladium at their collar.

## Known Recipients

- **[[Sol]]**: Commander of [[The Ascalon]]

## Relationships

- **[[Bravos]]**: Faction associated with Leviathan hunting and the Palladium

- **[[The Ascalon]]**: Company whose commander holds the Palladium
