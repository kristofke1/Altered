# The Seven Blades

## Overview
The Seven Blades are legendary mythical swordsmen from the [[The Lost Tribe|Lost Tribe]]. According to legends of the first Nomads, these illustrious sword fighters protected the rear guard during [[The Great Wandering]], covering the advance of the seven other tribes toward the place that would become [[Asgartha]].

## Historical Role
Before the founding of [[Asgartha]], the [[The Lost Tribe|Lost Tribe]] remained behind while the other seven tribes advanced. The Seven Blades served as protectors, ensuring the safe passage of the migrating tribes through the [[The Tumult|Tumult]].

Their sacrifice allowed the seven tribes to reach the Peninsula and establish the First City, but the fate of the Lost Tribe and the Seven Blades remains unknown.

## Connection to The Drifter

According to memories preserved in the [[City of Scholars]], The Seven Blades accompanied a mysterious figure known as [[The Drifter]] who visited the city in ancient times. The Drifter asked at least one of the Seven Blades, [[Fiore dei Liberi]], to remain behind as a sacrifice—imprisoned in the city's memory library for centuries until a worthy challenger would come to free him.

## Lost Names
Three of the Seven Blades have been identified by Atsadi through his research:
1. **[[Fiore dei Liberi]]** - Italian fencing master
2. **[[Julie d'Aubigny]]** - French opera singer and duelist  
3. **[[Joseph Bologne]]** - Chevalier de Saint-Georges, composer and swordsman

Four of the Seven Blades remain unidentified, their names still lost to history. The passage of centuries since [[The Great Wandering]] has eroded the specific details of their identities from historical record.

## Atsadi's Quest
[[Atsadi]], the One-Armed Swordsman, seeks to identify and unearth all Seven Blades. He must:
1. Find their identities through research and investigation
2. Learn enough about each swordsman to materialize them as [[Eidolons]] through [[Alteration]]
3. Challenge them in combat
4. Defeat them using his [[Galisgi]] style

By 392 AC, Atsadi had discovered the names of three of the Seven Blades: Fiore dei Liberi, Julie d'Aubigny (also known as Mademoiselle Maupin), and Joseph Bologne (also known as the Chevalier de Saint-Georges). These names became a mantra in his mind during training sessions. Atsadi worked intensively to manifest these three Eidolons so he could challenge and defeat them, though the process of learning enough about them to successfully materialize them through Alteration proved difficult.

This quest is essential to [[Atsadi]]'s goal of proving himself worthy of wielding [[Excalibur]]. By defeating the legendary protectors of the [[The Lost Tribe|Lost Tribe]], he demonstrates his mastery of the sword and his right to be considered the greatest swordsman in the world.

## Significance
The Seven Blades represent the ultimate test for [[Atsadi]]. While he has already defeated legendary warriors like [[Tomoe Gozen]], [[Achilles]], and [[Sun Wukong]], the Seven Blades hold special significance as defenders of humanity during its most vulnerable migration.

Their connection to [[The Lost Tribe|Lost Tribe]] and [[The Great Wandering]] makes them symbols of sacrifice, duty, and martial excellence. Defeating them would not only prove [[Atsadi]]'s skill but also honor their memory by demonstrating that their legacy lives on in worthy successors.

### 393 AC - Fiore dei Liberi Defeated

[[Atsadi]] located [[Fiore dei Liberi]] imprisoned in the [[City of Scholars]] memory library and defeated him in a duel. After his victory, Fiore honored their wager by confirming [[Julie d'Aubigny]] and [[Joseph Bologne]] as Atsadi's next targets among the Seven Blades.

## Mystery
The deliberate obscurity surrounding the Seven Blades - their forgotten names, unknown fates, and lost histories - adds to their legendary status. They exist at the boundary between history and myth, making [[Atsadi]]'s quest to discover and challenge them all the more difficult.

Whether they perished during [[The Great Wandering]], were transformed by the [[The Tumult|Tumult]], or met some other fate remains one of the mysteries of Asgarthan history.
