# The Lost Tribe

## Overview
The Lost Tribe was the eighth of [[The Eight Tribes]] that comprised the [[Tumult Nomads]]. It was lost body and soul before reaching the convergence point, earning its name after its demise.

## The Mystical Call
Eons ago, before the birth of [[Asgartha]]'s civilization, the [[Tumult Nomads]] roamed the world divided into [[The Eight Tribes]]. All received a mystical call to converge towards the same place. While seven Tribes arrived safely, the eighth was lost.

## Current Mystery
The [[Lyra]] wish to know what happened to the Lost Tribe. Did they survive? If not, what happened to them? The [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] is their opportunity to find out.
