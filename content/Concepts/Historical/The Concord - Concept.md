The Concord is the founding treaty of Asgartha, proclaimed by Rune in Year 1 AC, through which all the Tumult Nomad tribes agreed to bind themselves into a single unified people. It represents the fundamental covenant upon which Asgarthan civilization was built and remains the cornerstone of the nation's identity.

After long and perilous journeys across the transformed world, survivor communities from all corners of the globe—Africa, Europe, Asia, and beyond—converged on the same peninsula in response to mystical visions of a Firebird. However, resource scarcity, harsh conditions, and the relentless Tumult immediately threatened to tear apart this fragile gathering. Civil war loomed as various tribal leaders prepared to seize power by force.

When the Phoenix crowned Rune at Kuningasplatz, his first act as Kuningas was to decree the Concord. Using the metaphor of rivers converging into one stream, the treaty bound all tribes into a single people. Like separate waterways joining together, the diverse communities mingled to become one nation, sharing a common identity and purpose.

The Concord established several key principles:
- **Non-aggression pact**: Tribes committed to setting aside their disputes
- **Cooperation for common good**: United effort against the Tumult and for mutual survival
- **Inclusive governance**: All communities represented, not just the powerful
- **Shared destiny**: Recognition that humanity needed unity to survive

The treaty became so central to Asgarthan identity that the calendar system itself was named in its honor: AC stands for "Anno Concordiae" (Year of the Concord), with Year 1 marking the proclamation of this founding covenant.

Throughout Asgarthan history, the Concord has been invoked during times of crisis as a reminder of the original commitment to unity. During the Severance War, the central authority viewed allowing secession as violating the very concept of Concord—the first step toward future divisions that could not be tolerated. The Covenant between the six Factions (established much later) echoes the principles of the original Concord.

The Concord represents more than a political agreement; it embodies the existential recognition that humanity's survival depends on cooperation rather than conflict, on unity rather than division. It is the promise that, despite differences, all Asgarthans are one people with a shared commitment to endure and thrive together.
