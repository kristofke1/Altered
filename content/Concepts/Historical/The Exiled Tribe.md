# The Exiled Tribe

## Overview
The Exiled Tribe was one of [[The Eight Tribes]] that comprised the [[Tumult Nomads]]. Even before the foundation of [[Asgartha]], this Tribe decided not to remain on the peninsula and to continue leading a nomadic existence.

## History
Eons ago, before the birth of [[Asgartha]]'s civilization, the [[Tumult Nomads]] roamed the world divided into [[The Eight Tribes]]. All received a mystical call to converge towards the same place. While seven arrived safely, and one ([[The Lost Tribe]]) was lost, the Exiled Tribe arrived but chose not to stay.

## Current Mystery
The [[Lyra]] wish to know what happened to the Exiled Tribe. Did they survive? If not, what happened to them? The [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] is their opportunity to find out.
