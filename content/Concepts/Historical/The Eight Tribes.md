# The Eight Tribes

## Overview
Eons ago, before the birth of [[Asgartha]]'s civilization, the [[Altered/Concepts/Historical/Tumult Nomads|Tumult Nomads]] roamed the world. They were divided into eight Tribes, each originating from a distinct region of the world.

## The Mystical Call
All eight Tribes received a mystical call to converge towards the same place. While they all received this call, only seven arrived safely.

## Fate of the Tribes
- Seven Tribes arrived safely and eventually became the ancestors of the [[Lyra]]
- One Tribe, [[The Lost Tribe]], was lost body and soul before reaching the convergence point
- One Tribe, [[The Exiled Tribe]], decided not to remain on the peninsula even before the foundation of [[Asgartha]] and continued leading a nomadic existence

## Current Status
The [[Lyra]] wish to know what happened to [[The Lost Tribe]] and [[The Exiled Tribe]]. The [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Rediscovery Endeavor]] is their opportunity to find out.
