Kuningas is the title for the High King of Asgartha, first held by Rune after the Phoenix crowned him during the founding moment in Year 1 AC. The title represents the supreme executive authority of the Asgarthan nation and carries both political and symbolic weight as the chosen leader of a united humanity.

The title was established when Rune became the first sovereign of Asgartha, crowned by divine selection rather than conquest or election. The Phoenix's choice of Rune—placing a crown of fire upon his brow that left an indelible mark—established the Kuningas as a leader with mystical legitimacy beyond mere political power.

The Kuningas holds several key responsibilities and powers:
- **Executive authority**: Leading the nation and making crucial decisions
- **Symbol of unity**: Representing all Asgarthans, not just a single faction
- **Commander**: Directing military and defensive forces
- **Arbiter**: Settling disputes and providing final judgment
- **Guardian**: Protecting the nation from external and internal threats

However, the title was never intended to be absolute. Rune established the Trusted Council specifically to advise and challenge the Kuningas's decisions, recognizing that even a divinely chosen leader needed counsel and accountability.

The feminine equivalent is Kuningatar (High Queen). Cwaith became the first Kuningatar in 42 AC, succeeding Rune and establishing that the title could pass to capable leaders regardless of gender.

The line of Kuningas and Kuningatar continued through several rulers until 130 AC, when the reign of Ekaterina ruun-Caellach ended and the political system transitioned to the Council of Archons (130-194 AC). Later periods saw the title replaced by "Basileus" (masculine) and "Basilissa" (feminine), though these represented a different conception of leadership within evolved governmental structures.

The Kuningas represents the ideal of a leader chosen not by force or birthright alone, but by destiny and divine will, accountable to both the people and their advisors, committed to serving all rather than advancing a single faction's interests.
