# Chimerae

## Overview
These living beings have become accustomed to living within [[The Tumult]], constantly mutating as a result. They are hybrid creatures, with extremely varied forms, intrinsically marked by the Tumult.

## Characteristics
When a Chimera emerges from the Tumult, its appearance settles into one form, until it returns to it. Some are peaceful, others hostile to humans, but all have acquired the ability to absorb [[Mana]] to sustain themselves.

## Relationship with Alterers
When a Chimera bonds with an [[Alterers|Alterer]] through [[The Musubi]], its appearance stabilizes, as the human retains a memory of the form it is supposed to assume in their eyes.

## Bonded Powers
When an Alterer forms such a bond with a Chimera, the duo develops powers that defy comprehension. Through this connection, the Alterer and their Alter Ego share their abilities, making them particularly apt at venturing into the Tumult.

These bonded pairs are called [[Exalts]], and their existence offers new perspectives for humanity.
