# Mushs

## Overview
Mushs are a family of mushrooms that have the unique characteristic of being sentient. They were crucial in discovering the cure for [[The Nifir]] epidemic.

## Characteristics

### Sentience
These conscious mushrooms possess awareness and can form social structures. They live in forest or underground colonies and have various "customs."

### Social Behavior
Over time, the Mushs accepted [[Themba Hekekia]] into their colonies, allowing him to study their ecosystems and behaviors.

## Mana Filtering Ability

### Discovery
[[Themba Hekekia]] was astonished to discover that Mushs had the ability to filter [[Mana]] from the environment and living beings.

### Process
Mushs transform unstable Mana into appeased Mana by passing it through their bodies. Using their mycelium, they could extract unstable Mana and offer purified Mana in return.

## Role in Curing the Nifir

### The Breakthrough
This Mana-filtering discovery was crucial in curing [[The Nifir]]. The disease was linked to unstable Mana, which the Mushs could purify.

### Distribution
Numerous mush spores were sent to all corners of the Peninsula. Over time, they grew into adult Mushs that helped eradicate the epidemic.

### Success
Within a few years, thanks to this fortuitous discovery, [[The Nifir]] was eradicated from [[Asgartha]].
