# Noosh Burrowers

## Overview

Noosh Burrowers are burrowing creatures employed by the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] for excavation purposes. They are capable of digging tunnels through rock and are utilized during exploration of [[The Crow's Eye]] and [[The Undergrowth]].

## Physical Characteristics

Noosh Burrowers possess cauliflower-shaped tails that they occasionally flick. Their coats are plant-like in nature, allowing vegetation to grow directly from their fur. When resting frequently among vegetable plots, daisies and small gourds can sprout from their coats.

## Role in Expedition

During descent operations at [[The Crow's Eye]] in 393 AC, Noosh Burrowers accompanied [[Muna]] mediators and [[Hellequin]] trackers. Their ability to dig through rock makes them valuable for tunnel excavation and accessing blocked passages within the [[City of Scholars]].
