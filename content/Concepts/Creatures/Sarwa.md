# Sarwa

## Overview
The Sarwa are mythical guardian spirits of nature actively sought by the [[Muna]] faction. These creatures were glimpsed by some primitive peoples during their migration to [[Asgartha]] and are said to reign over the wild expanses, far from human enclaves.

## Nature and Mystery
No one knows the true nature of the Sarwa—whether they are still unknown entities or beings similar to the [[Leviathans]]. Their existence remains largely legendary, with only fragmentary sightings and ancient stories providing evidence of their presence.

## Muna Belief and Objectives
The Muna believe that the Sarwa could be the missing piece to achieve lasting stabilization of the world. If, as legends say, the Sarwa preside over ecosystems, granting them the gift of [[Alteration]] could accelerate the appeasement of the world.

This belief connects to a fundamental question the Muna have long pondered: why are humans the only species capable of Alteration? The Muna wonder what could be achieved if other creatures awakened to this faculty.

## Connection to Ecosystems
According to legend, each Sarwa is tied to specific wild expanses and ecosystems, serving as their guardian and maintaining their natural balance. This aligns with the Muna philosophy of harmony between all living things and their environment.

## Current Search
The Muna actively seek to rediscover these particular creatures, viewing their potential awakening to Alteration as crucial to their long-term goals of stabilizing [[The Tumult]] and expanding [[The Skein]] to encompass all biomes.
