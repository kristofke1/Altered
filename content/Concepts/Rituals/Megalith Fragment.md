# Megalith Fragment

## Overview
A Megalith Fragment is an extremely rare piece of material carved from [[The Megalith]], the titanic floating relic suspended within [[The Well of Tears]]. These fragments serve as catalysts for magical power, allowing [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]] to manifest imagination with greater efficiency.

## Acquisition Ritual
The rare chosen ones who attain the position of Yzmir mage are allowed access to [[The Well of Tears]], the heart of the Bastion. Above the underground lake infused with magic floats [[The Megalith]], vibrating with power and seemingly suspended between worlds.

There, future mages:
1. Take a small hammer and chisel
2. Carefully carve out a Fragment of the Megalith
3. Have the Fragment set into a jewel
4. Keep it on their person at all times

## Function as Catalyst
The Megalith Fragment acts as a catalyst for magical power by:
- Amplifying the practitioner's connection to [[The Aether|Aether]]
- Enabling more efficient manifestation of ideas
- Enhancing the practice of [[The Naali]]
- Facilitating the channeling of concepts from the collective unconscious

## Extreme Rarity
The material is described as extremely rare, which is fitting given that:
- Only a few dozen aspirants per year become Initiates
- Each Initiate carves only a single small Fragment
- [[The Megalith]] itself is a unique relic from the Confluence
- The process requires access to the most sacred part of the Yzmir Bastion

## Significance in Yzmir Tradition
Receiving permission to carve a Megalith Fragment represents the culmination of the rigorous selection process for becoming a Yzmir mage. After confirming receptivity to [[Alteration]] and proximity to [[The Spheres|one or more Spheres]] of magic, and passing bizarre and enigmatic tests, only then can an aspirant access [[The Well of Tears]] to claim their Fragment.

The Fragment serves as both a practical tool for enhanced magical practice and a symbol of acceptance into the inner circle of [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]].
