The Martengale is the eleventh sign of the New Zodiac, active from October 23 to November 22. The sign is depicted as a winged marten with long ears and symbolizes instability and impermanence.

## Symbolism

The Martengale's element is air. The sign is depicted as a winged marten with long ears.

## Personality Traits

Those born under the Martengale sign are characterized by contradictions and paradoxes:

### Core Contradictions
- Independent but dislike being alone
- Like innovation but maintain strong sense of tradition  
- Venture off beaten track but always return to starting points
- Show strong determination yet can be stubborn when situations require it

### Behavioral Characteristics
Martengales are elusive by nature and full of contradictions. They are adaptable individuals who display strong determination and can become stubborn when circumstances demand it. Rather than wallowing in misfortune, they prefer bouncing back quickly.

### Reputation for Luck
Martengales are often said to be lucky, though this reputation stems more from their resilience than genuine fortune. Their ability to adapt and recover quickly from setbacks creates an impression of consistent good luck. They refuse to sit around feeling sorry for themselves, instead choosing active recovery.

### Need for Freedom
Terrified of boredom, Martengales display a relentless need for freedom. This can lead them to raise stakes to levels that defy all odds. They will take significant risks rather than accept stagnation or predictable routines.

## Cultural Context

The New Zodiac emerged after the Confluence as a reimagined astrological system for post-Confluence Asgartha. Traditional zodiac signs were adapted or replaced to reflect new cultural realities and philosophical perspectives.
