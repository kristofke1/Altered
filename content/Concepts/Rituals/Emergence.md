# Emergence

## Overview
Emergence is an initiation rite undergone by those who wish to become [[Lyra]] [[Alterers]]. The ritual is not without danger.

## The Ritual
Only those who demonstrate exceptional artistic abilities within the [[Lyra]] are invited to become [[Alterers]]. Applicants must undergo Emergence, which involves entering a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity and allowing themselves to be transformed by it. An [[Alterer]] always accompanies them to limit the extent of the metamorphosis, but the process is inherently risky.

## Physical Alterations
That's why the vast majority of the [[Lyra]] visibly bear a physical alteration of their being. It can be a simple change in hair color, a parasitism by the idea of an element, or even the appearance of crow wings on their backs. There are countless different mutations.

While most are cosmetic and non-functional, a small part of the [[Lyra]] inherit talents from this exposure, such as increased longevity, the ability to breathe underwater, or regenerate their limbs.

## Variability
The extent of the marks varies, mainly depending on the violence of [[The Tumult]] and the degree of control exercised by their guide.
