# Midori Matsuri

## Overview

The Midori Matsuri is a [[Muna]] festival during which processions pay their respects to the statues of the [[Oneiroi]] of nature along the [[Mugen]] bridge.

## Honoring Eru

[[Eru]]'s statue is the first to be honored on the first islet of the [[Mugen]] during the Midori Matsuri. His statue stands alongside those of other Oneiroi of nature as the processions pass.

## Relationships

- **[[Mugen]]**: Sacred bridge where the festival processions take place
- **[[Eru]]**: Oneiros whose statue is the first honored during the festival
- **[[Muna]]**: Faction that celebrates the Midori Matsuri
- **[[Oneiroi]]**: Nature spirits honored during the festival
- **[[Ryukkōsai]]**: Related Muna festival
