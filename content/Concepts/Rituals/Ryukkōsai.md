# Ryukkōsai

## Overview

The Ryukkōsai is a spring festival organized by the [[Muna]] Faction. During this festival, [[The Musubi]] ceremony can be celebrated throughout the Peninsula, allowing [[Alterers]] and their Alter Egos to bond.

## The Musubi

While [[The Musubi]] can be celebrated year-round at the [[Bark Refuge]], the Ryukkōsai allows the ceremony to take place throughout the Peninsula. This union is not taken lightly, as it causes two beings to share their minds in an indelible and immutable way.

## Honoring the Saviors of Arkaster

People often honor [[Yong-Su Tenzing]] during the Ryukkōsai festival for his role in saving [[Arkaster]], though [[Eru]]'s crucial contribution in rallying the fauna of [[Kirighai]] is frequently forgotten.

## Timeline

### 391 AC - Procession on the Mugen

During the Ryukkōsai festival, [[Rin]] followed a procession crossing the [[Mugen]] bridge. [[Yong-Su Tenzing]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] manifested floating in the air with lotus flowers blooming beneath each step.
