# The Musubi

## Overview
Also known as the Bonding Ceremony, the Musubi is a recent ritual that allows two beings to link their essences.

## Basic Effects
Through this magical union, the linked beings can share their minds, thoughts, feel each other's presence, or experience their counterpart's emotions even at a great distance.

## Alterer-Chimera Bonds
When an [[Alterers|Alterer]] forms such a bond with a [[Chimerae|Chimera]], the duo develops powers that defy comprehension. Through this connection, the Alterer and their Alter Ego share their abilities, making them particularly apt at venturing into [[The Tumult]].

## Exalts
These pairs are called [[Exalts]], and their existence offers new perspectives for humanity. Thanks to the Exalts, humanity can now aspire to explore the expanses affected by the Tumult.

## Stabilization Effect
When a Chimera bonds with an Alterer, its appearance stabilizes, as the human retains a memory of the form it is supposed to assume in their eyes. This prevents the Chimera from constantly mutating while bonded.
