# Ollam

An Ollam is a venerable [[Muna]] priest who officiates [[The Musubi]] bonding ceremonies at [[Bark Refuge]]. During the ritual, the Ollam weaves and intertwines the spiritual threads of two beings through [[The Skein]], binding their essences into a permanent union.

The Ollam who presided over [[Teija]] and [[Nauraa]]'s ceremony anointed each participant with a drop of sap before beginning the intertwining process. [[Kauri]] later imitated an Ollam's gestures when performing a field Musubi between [[Sol|Soledad]] and [[Halua]] at [[The Screed]].

- **[[Muna]]**: Faction whose priesthood includes the Ollam role
