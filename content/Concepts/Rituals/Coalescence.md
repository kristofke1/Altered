# Coalescence

## Overview
Coalescence is the process by which [[Ordis]] followers link their minds together through the [[Glyph]] tattoo, forming [[The Gestalt]] network. It represents the pooling of consciousness that the Ordis readily embrace in exchange for collective benefits.

## Function
Through Coalescence, Ordis members can:
- Link their minds to converse telepathically
- See through another's eyes
- Share perceptions and knowledge
- Transmit emotions to support one another
- Pool their [[Alteration]] abilities for enhanced effects

## Requirements
Coalescence requires:
- The [[Glyph|Ordis Glyph]] tattoo as an interface
- Voluntary authorization from both parties
- Physical proximity (limited by distance)

## Philosophy of Union
The Ordis tends to readily sacrifice individual privacy and autonomy for the many advantages offered by this pooling of consciousness. By linking through Coalescence, followers affirm that:
- Their individuality fades in favor of the collective
- Personal interests will never outweigh those of the organization
- The whole is greater than the sum of its parts

## Collective Enhancement
The pooling of minds through Coalescence allows [[Alterers]] to deploy [[Heka]] abilities that a single individual would struggle to implement. This collective application of power makes the Ordis formidable in their manipulation of reality through written glyphs.

## Social Benefits
Through perpetual Coalescence:
- Ordis followers are rarely lonely, remaining connected to others
- Emotions can be transmitted to reassure, support, encourage, or soothe
- Knowledge flows freely among all connected members
- Information exchange reaches unstoppable velocity

## The Price of Severance
The deep integration created by Coalescence means that being cut off from [[The Gestalt]] constitutes a terrible tear for Ordis members. The disconnection is compounded by an exacerbated sense of longing, making isolation particularly painful for those accustomed to perpetual connection.
