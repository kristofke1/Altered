# The Creed

## Overview
The Creed is a code of honor and morality by which the [[Bravos]] dedicate themselves to others. As individualistic as they may be, the [[Bravos]] adhere strictly to this code.

## The Ten Edicts
The [[Bravos]] gathered at the [[Parliament of Ashes]] after the foundation of [[Asgartha]] and first enunciated the ten edicts of the Creed, which can be summarized in ten principles:
1. Rectitude
2. Courage
3. Compassion
4. Respect
5. Honesty
6. Honor
7. Duty
8. Composure
9. Wisdom
10. Fellowship

## Purpose
Through the Creed, the [[Bravos]] dedicate themselves boldly, courageously, and with a bit of luck to serve those who cannot fight, those who bear the brunt of the universe's hostility. This is why they are often regarded as heroes.
