# Floret

The Floret is the currency of [[Asgartha]]. By 393 AC, plans were underway to discontinue the Floret and transition to an alternative system for fairer resource distribution.

## Planned Discontinuation

Following the [[393 AC Basileal Elections]], [[Fabia Valesquia]] reported that one of the major challenges facing new Basilissa [[Somayeh Bahman]] was preparing for the discontinuation of the Floret and finding a viable alternative to currency to ensure fairer resource distribution.

The election's winning economic platform called for moving beyond the monetary system entirely and redefining prosperity as immaterial, emphasizing access to ideas through education and arts rather than currency-based wealth.

## Relationships

- **[[Asgartha]]**: Nation where the Floret serves as currency
- **[[Somayeh Bahman]]**: Basilissa tasked with overseeing Floret discontinuation (393 AC)
