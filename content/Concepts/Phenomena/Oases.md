# Oases

## Overview
Within [[The Tumult]] exist pockets of reality where the laws of nature and physics still apply to some extent. These "Oases," where it was possible to subsist, played a crucial role in the survival of humanity.

## Historical Significance
Human tribes traveled from Oasis to Oasis to escape the ravages of [[The Tumult]]. This period of migration is known as [[The Great Wandering]].

## Notable Oases
[[Altered/Locations/Nations/Asgartha|Asgartha]] is the most significant known Oasis—a peninsula that became the new cradle and home of burgeoning human civilization. Through efforts and sacrifices, the humans who settled there succeeded in creating a stabilized region, almost beyond the reach of the Tumult.
