# Remanence

## Overview
Remanence is a form of alienation that afflicts [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]] who practice [[The Naali]]. It represents one of the most serious consequences of directly channeling [[The Aether|Aether]] from the collective unconscious.

## Symptoms
Remanence plunges even the most powerful Yzmir into a trance-like state characterized by:
- Visions
- Recitations in an unknown language
- Long periods of deep sleep
- Loss of conscious control
- Coughing fits that expel immaterial ideas as ephemeral motes floating in air
- Eyes developing strange sheen like iridescent oil spreading across corneas
- Specific ideas expelled during episodes (examples include: butterfly, snowman, cutting board, inhibition, echo, parachute, kit, hunger)
- Physical fragility, trembling, crying during episodes

Expelled ideas must be captured (such as in bubbles) to prevent them from imprinting on the world. Some [[The Cloister]] residents interpret expelled ideas as divination game to find meaning in affliction.

## Primary Danger
The greatest danger of Remanence lies in the fact that those afflicted with it can at any moment begin to regurgitate Aether into reality. This spontaneous release risks:
- Erratic alterations to the world around them
- Dangerous and unpredictable manifestations
- Potential creation of [[The Tumult|Tumult]] pockets
- Harm to those nearby

## Cause
Remanence appears to be connected to the contamination inherent in practicing [[The Naali]]. When [[Yzmir]] mages directly channel ideas from [[The Aether|Aether]], these ideas tend to seep into and parasitize those who manipulate them. Over time, this contamination accumulates until it manifests as Remanence.

## Current Status
Faced with this unexplained phenomenon, [[The Magisterium|the Council]] has asked sorcerers to somewhat restrain their use of magic while awaiting the discovery of a cure. This represents a significant limitation on Yzmir activities, as their magical practice is central to their mission.

Treatment at [[The Cloister]] involves nurses with red star insignia bags containing vials and compresses administered during episodes. Multiple [[Eidolons]] including [[Baku]], [[Caladrius]], and [[Tooth Fairy]] provide temporary relief through various methods, though no permanent cure exists.

## Experimental Treatments

### Leech Therapy (393 AC)

[[Saskia Averina]] developed experimental leeches capable of draining [[Remanence]] effects before symptoms fully manifest. During a field incident in 393 AC, [[Akesha Adhikari]] used these leeches from a vial to treat a [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] pendekar suffering an acute attack, applying them to the victim's forehead while ideas erupted from tear ducts, nose, and mouth like an inferno of pure concepts.

## Priority for Treatment
Treating this illness is a priority for the [[Yzmir]], as without a cure, they will not be able to continue their experiments with the necessary peace of mind required for their mission. The faction's ability to study the secret arcana of the world and protect [[Asgartha]] from hidden threats depends on resolving this affliction.
