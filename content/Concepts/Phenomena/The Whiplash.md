# The Whiplash

## Overview

The Whiplash is a double-funnel cyclonic tornado encountered by the [[Expeditionary Corps]] armada during its crossing of [[The Turmoil]] in 393 AC. It stands between the fleet and the [[pillar of light]] that appeared after [[The Hunger]]'s defeat, blocking passage despite the fleet being only a few nautical miles from its destination. The phenomenon consists of two nested counter-rotating vortices capable of shearing winds exceeding 500 kilometers per hour, destroying all drones and probes sent to investigate. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] suspects the barrier was deliberately erected as a protective measure.

## Characteristics

The Whiplash consists of an outer cyclonic tornado with a flared vortex and an inner tornado nested within it, driven by counter-rotating winds. The double-funnel structure grinds material between its opposing rotations. Reconnaissance drones sent into the outer funnel were destroyed by the inner counter-rotating winds. The combined forces exceed what any vessel in the fleet can withstand.

Passage beneath the Whiplash is impossible due to [[The Tumult|Tumult]] winds of unprecedented magnitude at lower altitudes. Passage above is equally untenable, as the winds are most violent at the top of the funnel. Scholars and scientists consulted by Admiral Singh were unable to identify a solution.

## The Eye

The Whiplash contains a calm eye at its center, where the hurricane forms a wide ring around a stable zone. Within this eye sits a floating island bearing the city of [[Asty]], home to [[The Reka|the Reka people]]. The pillar of light that guided the [[Expeditionary Corps]] across [[The Turmoil]] originates from the world-tree growing on this island.

## Possible Artificial Origin

The Whiplash's double-funnel configuration appears to defy natural meteorological behavior. Admiral Singh recorded suspicion that the barrier was deliberately constructed as a protective measure. The discovery of Asty at the cyclone's center confirms the Whiplash functions as a shield around the Reka civilization.

## Crossing

The [[Expeditionary Corps]] crossed the Whiplash by using [[Leviathans]] as a vanguard, led by [[Halua]]. The Leviathans consume [[Mana]], creating a void that attracts less [[The Tumult|Tumult]] and generating a brief lull in wind speed behind them. [[Exalts]] aboard a skiff at the front of the formation protected an unnamed caller who lured the Leviathans through. The [[Yzmir]] focused their efforts on countering the Tumult specifically. The crossing took approximately three hours, during which the fleet sustained superficial structural damage.
