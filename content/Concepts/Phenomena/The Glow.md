# The Glow

## Overview

The Glow is one of several [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities that exist within [[Asgartha]] and require constant monitoring by the [[Yzmir]]. Along with [[The Megalith]], [[The Fagn]], and [[The Black Wave]], it represents one of the persistent phenomena that must be controlled, regulated, and kept dormant to protect Asgarthan civilization.

## Yzmir Surveillance

At any given moment, [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]] watch over The Glow along with the other singularities. Their duties include controlling the singularity, regulating its activity, ensuring it remains dormant, and preventing dangers from manifesting into reality.

The existence and monitoring of The Glow is kept secret from the general population. The rest of the population remains oblivious to these dangers, living their lives as if nothing were amiss.
