# The Spheres

## Overview
The Spheres are five complementary disciplines of [[Alteration]] that comprise [[The Naali]], the [[Yzmir]] style of magic. Each Sphere represents a distinct approach to channeling and manipulating [[The Aether|Aether]].

## The Five Spheres

### 1. Eidomancy
[[Eidomancy]] is the invocation of ideas using Effigies. This Sphere focuses on manifesting and embodying concepts through physical representations.

### 2. Horomancy
[[Horomancy]] is divination through the study of idea currents present in the world via Irises. This Sphere allows practitioners to perceive and interpret the flow of ideas across reality.

### 3. Phonomancy
[[Phonomancy]] is the manipulation of ideas through voice and sound, using techniques called the Psalms. This Sphere harnesses the power of spoken and sung words to shape reality.

### 4. Kinemancy
[[Kinemancy]] is the mastery of primal and elemental forces through gesture, employing movements called the Signs. This Sphere channels power through physical motion and symbolic gestures.

### 5. Gephyromancy
[[Gephyromancy]] is the magic of idea transfers and reinforcement through Sigils. This Sphere focuses on creating bridges and connections between concepts.

## Schools and Temples
The [[Yzmir]] distinguish themselves by their sectorization into Schools of Alteration. Each School has a Temple within [[The Kadigir]] that serves as a learning place for [[Initiates]], hosting a wide range of teachers and disciples.

At the head of each School sits a Magister, a prominent representative of the magical discipline over which they preside. These five Magisters sit on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council that guides the entire Faction.

## Practitioner Approaches
While there are [[Alterers]] capable of mastering all five Spheres, others prefer different approaches:

**Specialists**: Focus on one domain to develop a single primary expertise
**Generalists**: Practice two or three disciplines simultaneously
**Masters**: Rare individuals who achieve competency across all five paths

Most commonly, Alterers practice two or three disciplines, as it is challenging to devote oneself to the study of all these fields simultaneously.

## The Sixth Sphere

Beyond the five known Spheres exists a Sixth Sphere that has been "banished"—its practice forbidden and its knowledge suppressed. According to [[Sylas]], some individuals still possess access to this power, which is capable of triggering a second [[The Confluence|Confluence]]. The nature and methods of the Sixth Sphere remain unknown, but its potential for world-scale devastation makes it a subject of concern for entities like [[Maw]], whose nature compels them to hunt those who could cause such catastrophes.

## Purpose and Philosophy
The division into five Spheres allows the [[Yzmir]] to systematically explore and master all aspects of [[The Aether|Aether]] manipulation. Each Sphere provides a different lens through which to understand and interact with the fabric of ideas that underlies reality.
