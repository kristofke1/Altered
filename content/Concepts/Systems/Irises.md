# Irises

## Overview

Irises are a perception technique practiced within [[Horomancy]], one of the five [[The Spheres|Spheres]] of [[The Naali]]. When activated, the practitioner's eyes take on a strange hue and their pupils shift to display geometric patterns, opening their sight to the metaphysical dimension of reality.

## Capabilities

Through Irises, a practitioner can see beneath the physical form of beings and things to perceive the intertwining [[The Aether|Aether]] and [[Quintessence]], as well as the pulsing [[Mana]] anchoring them to the world. This allows practitioners to distinguish [[Eidolons]] from mortals, perform diagnostic examinations to identify parasitic ideas grafted onto individuals, track opponents through camouflage, and analyze observed techniques for reproduction.

## Limitations

The [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] has developed methods to materialize [[Eidolons]] that are completely undetectable even to those with the sharpest Irises, representing a known limit of the technique's perceptive reach.

## Enhancement

Irises can be enhanced with [[Arcolano Milk]], which provides a heightened extrasensory state. In [[Lindiwe]]'s case, Arcolano Milk awakened Iris-like perception in someone who was physically blind, allowing her to perceive through ideas rather than through her senses. [[Yzmir]] Initiates determined that she perceived not through sight but by directly welcoming into her mind the ideas resonating around her.
