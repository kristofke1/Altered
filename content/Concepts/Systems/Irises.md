# Irises

## Overview

Irises are the perception technique through which [[Horomancy]] practitioners study idea currents present in the world. [[Horomancy]] is one of the five [[The Spheres|Spheres]] of [[The Naali]], focusing on divination via Irises.

## Function

Through Irises, practitioners can:
- Perceive and interpret the flow of ideas across reality
- Perform diagnostic examinations to identify parasitic ideas grafted onto individuals
- Track opponents through camouflage
- Analyze observed techniques for reproduction

## Enhancement

Irises can be enhanced with [[Arcolano Milk]] to provide deeper perception.
