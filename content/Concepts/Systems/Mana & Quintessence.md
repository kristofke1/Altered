# Mana & Quintessence

## The Two Substances
If [[The Aether]] is the substance of [[The Empyrean]], Quintessence is that of reality. It is only when Aether imbues itself onto Quintessence that the idea it carries becomes physically incarnate in reality.

## The Relationship
- **Aether** is the immaterial idea, the concept of a thing (like a plan or a scheme)
- **Quintessence** is the substance (the material) that allows ideas to become tangible, to exist within the world
- **[[Mana]]** is the cement that binds the two, the energy that keeps Aether and Quintessence in a coherent whole

## Creation of Mana
When [[The Tumult]] blows over the world, Aether is sometimes torn from Quintessence like paint can be peeled off a canvas. If this violent process disperses orphaned ideas into the world, it leaves behind a blank canvas that other nomadic ideas can in turn cover. This tearing also leaves in its wake droplets of energy with a bluish glow: Mana.

## Applications
It is this energy that [[Muna]] collect and redistribute in nature, with the aim of strengthening the world. The more a place is saturated with Mana, the more resistant it becomes to the flows of the Tumult. The ideas it contains take root deep in the texture of reality.

## Formation of Oases
Ultimately, a place saturated with Mana can even become an [[Oases|Oasis]], capable of repelling the Tumult like an anticyclone repels certain meteorological phenomena.
