# Horomancy

## Overview
Horomancy is one of the five [[The Spheres|Spheres]] of [[The Naali]], the [[Yzmir]] alteration style. It focuses on divination through the study of idea currents present in the world via Irises.

## Method
Horomancy practitioners use specialized techniques called Irises to perceive and interpret the flow of ideas across reality. This Sphere allows them to observe the currents of [[The Aether|Aether]] as they move through the world, enabling divination and foresight.

By studying these idea currents, Horomancers can:
- Perceive patterns in the flow of concepts
- Anticipate future developments
- Understand hidden connections between events
- See beneath the surface of apparent reality

## School and Temple
Within [[The Kadigir]], there is a dedicated Temple for Horomancy that serves as a learning place for [[Initiates]]. The School is headed by a Magister who represents this magical discipline on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council of the Yzmir.

## Place Among the Spheres
Horomancy is one of five complementary disciplines:
- [[Eidomancy]]: Invocation via Effigies
- **Horomancy**: Divination via Irises
- [[Phonomancy]]: Manipulation via Psalms
- [[Kinemancy]]: Mastery via Signs
- [[Gephyromancy]]: Transfers via Sigils

Practitioners may specialize solely in Horomancy or combine it with other Spheres as part of their magical education.
