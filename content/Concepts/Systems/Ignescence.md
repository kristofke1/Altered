# The Ignescence

## Overview
The Ignescence is the unique [[Alteration]] style of the [[Lyra]]. The [[Lyra]] draw [[Mana]] directly from within themselves, or harvest all the energy they spark around them when performing in front of an audience.

## Characteristics
The Ignescence revolves around creativity, whether it's artistic, architectural, or artisanal expression. It's a raw style, charged with emotions, and as powerful as the feelings they evoke around them. It's an instinctive, intuitive style, and perhaps inconsistent, but it allows them to alter the world with a scope unmatched by any other Faction.

## Proximity to the Tumult
The [[Lyra]] are arguably the [[Alterers]] considered closest to [[The Tumult]]. Indeed, their way of altering the world through singing, music, or even daring constructions closely resembles the way [[The Tumult|Tumult]] winds transform it. [[Alterers]] from other Factions feel this similarity viscerally, which often unsettles them.

This proximity even extends to their beliefs: many [[Lyra]] believe that [[The Tumult]] is not a phenomenon to be entirely banished, but to be mastered. For them, it is the expression of nature in its most fierce and unrestrained form, the very embodiment of the concept of change.

## The Inner Fire
The Ignescence is an inner fire that burns with incredible intensity. Born from the purest inspiration, this flame that inhabits them and takes root in the depths of their soul is like a spark of [[The Tumult|Tumult]] that continues to grow throughout their lives. It is through this flame that the [[Lyra]] achieve a thousand feats.

Expressing this inner fire is natural for them, as it is an integral part of their being. But it is also a matter of survival.

## Dangers
If they cannot release it through their [[Alteration]], their flesh begins to char, their skin flakes into pale ashes, as if they were slowly being consumed. Conversely, pouring out too much of their fire can leave them empty and drained, prone to lethargy and depression.

But this condition does not frighten the members of the [[Lyra]] Faction. It is part of their identity, as death is part of life.

## The Legend of Paramours
There is a persistent legend of [[Paramours]]: soulmates capable, by their mere presence, of neutralizing the harmful effects of Ignescence by absorbing a portion of this inner fire, or feeding it in return.
