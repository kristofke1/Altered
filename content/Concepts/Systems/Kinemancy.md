# Kinemancy

## Overview
Kinemancy is one of the five [[The Spheres|Spheres]] of [[The Naali]], the [[Yzmir]] alteration style. It focuses on the mastery of primal and elemental forces through gesture, employing movements called the Signs.

## Method
Kinemancy practitioners channel power through physical motion and symbolic gestures. The Signs—specific hand movements, body postures, and ritual gestures—allow them to command primal and elemental forces drawn from [[The Aether|Aether]].

Through Kinemancy, practitioners can:
- Command elemental powers (fire, water, earth, air)
- Channel primal forces of nature
- Direct energy through precise gestures
- Embody concepts through physical movement

## School and Temple
Within [[The Kadigir]], there is a dedicated Temple for Kinemancy that serves as a learning place for [[Initiates]]. The School is headed by a Magister who represents this magical discipline on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council of the Yzmir.

## Place Among the Spheres
Kinemancy is one of five complementary disciplines:
- [[Eidomancy]]: Invocation via Effigies
- [[Horomancy]]: Divination via Irises
- [[Phonomancy]]: Manipulation via Psalms
- **Kinemancy**: Mastery via Signs
- [[Gephyromancy]]: Transfers via Sigils

Practitioners may specialize solely in Kinemancy or combine it with other Spheres as part of their magical education.
