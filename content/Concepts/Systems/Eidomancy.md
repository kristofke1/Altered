# Eidomancy

## Overview
Eidomancy is one of the five [[The Spheres|Spheres]] of [[The Naali]], the [[Yzmir]] alteration style. It focuses on the invocation of ideas using Effigies.

## Method
Eidomancy practitioners use physical representations—Effigies—to manifest and embody concepts from [[The Aether|Aether]]. These Effigies serve as vessels or anchors for ideas, allowing them to be invoked and brought into reality.

## School and Temple
Within [[The Kadigir]], there is a dedicated Temple for Eidomancy that serves as a learning place for [[Initiates]]. The School is headed by a Magister who represents this magical discipline on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council of the Yzmir.

## Place Among the Spheres
Eidomancy is one of five complementary disciplines:
- **Eidomancy**: Invocation via Effigies
- [[Horomancy]]: Divination via Irises
- [[Phonomancy]]: Manipulation via Psalms
- [[Kinemancy]]: Mastery via Signs
- [[Gephyromancy]]: Transfers via Sigils

Practitioners may specialize solely in Eidomancy or combine it with other Spheres as part of their magical education.
