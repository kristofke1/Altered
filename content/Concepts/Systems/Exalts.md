# Exalts

## Overview

Exalts are bonded pairs consisting of an [[Alterers|Alterer]] and a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who have undergone [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Through this connection, the Alterer and their Alter Ego share their abilities, making them particularly apt at venturing into [[The Tumult]].

## Role in the Rediscovery Endeavor

Thanks to the Exalts, humanity can now aspire to explore the expanses affected by the Tumult. Exalts are sent as scouts and vanguards of the exploration forces during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. By capitalizing on their abilities, humanity hopes to stabilize the regions still subjected to the Tumult.
