# Alterers

## Definition
Those who choose to specialize in [[Alteration]] are called Alterers. These individuals have become masters in the art of making their imagination tangible.

## Historical Role
Throughout the history of [[Altered/Locations/Nations/Asgartha|Asgartha]], they have been invaluable in structuring society, enabling it to endure in hostile environments. It is largely thanks to them that humanity has managed to survive until today.

## Factional Affiliations
While some Alterers claim no particular affiliation, the majority of them choose to belong to one of the six [[Factions]] of Asgartha. Each Faction welcomes anyone who shares their convictions, not just Alterers.

## Advanced Capabilities
When an Alterer forms a bond with a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] through [[The Musubi]], the duo develops powers that defy comprehension. These bonded pairs are called [[Exalts]], and they are particularly apt at venturing into [[The Tumult]].
