# Alterers

## Overview

Alterers are individuals who specialize in [[Alteration]], having developed their ability to influence the world by taming the unstable energy of [[Mana]]. While all humans possess the capacity for Alteration, Alterers have made it their specialty through innate talent or relentless effort (see [[Alteration#Accessibility]]).

## Factional Affiliations

While some Alterers claim no particular affiliation, the majority choose to belong to one of the six [[Factions]] of [[Asgartha]]. Each faction has developed its own distinctive Alteration style (see [[Alteration#Faction Styles]]).

## Bonding with Chimerae

When an Alterer forms a bond with a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] through [[The Musubi]], the resulting pair is called an [[Exalts|Exalt]], gaining abilities suited to venturing into [[The Tumult]].

## Vulnerabilities

The [[Oblivion]], an affliction documented at the [[City of Scholars]] in 393 AC, can sever Alterers' connection to their [[Eidolons]], causing them to forget beings they had always been able to summon (see [[Eidolons#Vulnerabilities]]).

## Relationships

- **[[Alteration]]**: Technique that Alterers specialize in
- **[[Mana]]**: Unstable energy that Alterers must tame to perform Alteration
- **[[Factions]]**: Organizations that most Alterers join to channel their abilities
- **[[Exalts]]**: Bonded Alterer-Chimera pairs formed through the Musubi
- **[[Eidolons]]**: Conscious beings that Alterers can summon
- **[[Oblivion]]**: Affliction that can sever Alterers' connection to Eidolons
