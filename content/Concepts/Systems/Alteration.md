# Alteration

## Overview
By using [[Altered/Concepts/Systems/Mana|Mana]], the very energy of [[The Tumult]], humanity discovered that it could materialize its imagination and memories.

## Accessibility
Although all human beings are capable of this, certain individuals, known as [[Altered/Concepts/Systems/Alterers|Alterers]], have made it their specialty.

## Mechanics
While most things created through Alteration are ephemeral, allocating large amounts of [[Altered/Concepts/Systems/Mana|Mana]] to them can make them more resilient, allowing them to be more strongly anchored in reality.

## Applications
Through the use of Alteration, it is possible to materialize:
- Physical objects and constructs
- [[Altered/Concepts/Systems/Eidolons|Eidolons]] - beings drawn from memories, myths, tales, or human history

## Societal Impact
Alteration has been invaluable in structuring Asgarthan society, enabling it to endure in hostile environments. The six [[Factions]] of [[Altered/Locations/Nations/Asgartha|Asgartha]] each have their own philosophy on how Alteration should be used to stabilize the world.
