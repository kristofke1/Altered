# Eidolons

## Overview
Through the use of [[Alteration]], it is possible to materialize not only things but also beings known as "Eidolons."

## Nature
These characters, drawn from memories, myths, tales, or even human history, can come to the assistance of human beings. When they appear, these "thought-forms" are colored by the imagination of the person who has summoned them.

## Manifestation Properties
The same Eidolon can be materialized multiple times, simultaneously and in different locations, taking on different appearances depending on the vision of its summoner. This means that an Eidolon's form is not fixed, but rather reflects the unique perspective and imagination of the [[Alterers|Alterer]] who calls it forth.
