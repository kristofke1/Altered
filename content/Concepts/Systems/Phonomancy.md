# Phonomancy

## Overview
Phonomancy is one of the five [[The Spheres|Spheres]] of [[The Naali]], the [[Yzmir]] alteration style. It focuses on the manipulation of ideas through voice and sound, using techniques called the Psalms.

## Method
Phonomancy practitioners harness the power of spoken and sung words to shape reality. Through carefully crafted Psalms—vocal utterances, chants, and songs—they can manipulate [[The Aether|Aether]] and alter the world around them.

The Psalms work by:
- Encoding ideas into sound
- Channeling concepts through vocal vibrations
- Shaping reality through spoken and sung words
- Resonating with the fundamental frequencies of ideas

## School and Temple
Within [[The Kadigir]], there is a dedicated Temple for Phonomancy that serves as a learning place for [[Initiates]]. The School is headed by a Magister who represents this magical discipline on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council of the Yzmir.

## Place Among the Spheres
Phonomancy is one of five complementary disciplines:
- [[Eidomancy]]: Invocation via Effigies
- [[Horomancy]]: Divination via Irises
- **Phonomancy**: Manipulation via Psalms
- [[Kinemancy]]: Mastery via Signs
- [[Gephyromancy]]: Transfers via Sigils

Practitioners may specialize solely in Phonomancy or combine it with other Spheres as part of their magical education.
