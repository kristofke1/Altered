# Recycling Centers

Recycling Centers are facilities established by the [[Axiom]] Faction as part of their resource management strategy. These centers collect faulty or obsolete tools and reintegrate them into the production lines in one form or another.

## Purpose

Due to the difficulty of accessing abundant resources, the [[Axiom]] has centered its operation on a perfectly honed logic of recycling. The Recycling Centers are the physical manifestation of this philosophy.

## Function

The Recycling Centers:
- Collect faulty equipment and tools
- Process obsolete devices
- Reintegrate materials into production lines
- Enable resource efficiency across [[Axiom]] operations

## Traceability

It is not uncommon to see old stamps appearing on new creations, allowing the origin of all parts to be traced. This system provides transparency and accountability in the [[Axiom]]'s manufacturing processes.

## Distribution

Numerous Recycling Centers have sprung up here and there throughout [[Axiom]]-controlled territories, creating a network of resource recovery.

## Strategic Context

The [[Axiom]] knows that these recycling processes are only temporary measures. If they want to ensure their longevity as a Faction, they must find new resource veins to maintain their current growth and yield. 

This recognition is one reason why [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] is a crucial issue for them - the hope of discovering new resources in the Terra Incognita could reduce dependence on recycling and enable even greater technological advancement.

## Philosophy

The Recycling Centers embody the [[Axiom]]'s commitment to:
- Reasoned progress
- Environmental consciousness
- Efficient resource utilization
- Accessibility across all social classes
