# Primordiae

The Primordiae are four legendary elemental materials that, according to persistent legends among the [[Axiom]], could accomplish wonders. Each Primordia is associated with one of the classical elements.

## The Four Primordiae

According to the legends:

1. **Fire**: [[Kelon]] is said to be the Primordia of Fire
2. **Air**: [[Altered/Concepts/Technology/Aerolith]] is believed to be the Primordia of Air
3. **Water**: The Primordia of Water remains yet to be discovered
4. **Earth**: The Primordia of Earth remains yet to be discovered

## Status and Debate

The existence and nature of the Primordiae is a subject of debate within the [[Axiom]]:
- Some Axiom scientists question the truth of these rumors
- Others have come to dream of finding these mythical materials
- The question remains: what new feats would the Faction be capable of if they obtained such resources?

## Connection to Exploration

Many [[Axiom]] members have joined the Expeditionary Corps specifically to be the first to uncover the missing Primordiae, or at least to verify if these legends are founded.

This quest represents both:
- A practical goal (obtaining powerful materials)
- A philosophical pursuit (testing the boundaries between legend and reality)

## Confirmed Primordiae

Of the four, only [[Kelon]] has been confirmed to exist and is extensively exploited by the [[Axiom]]. The status of [[Altered/Concepts/Technology/Aerolith]] and the other two Primordiae remains uncertain.

## Significance

The Primordiae legend embodies the [[Axiom]]'s character:
- Curiosity about natural phenomena
- Desire to harness powerful resources
- Willingness to pursue ambitious goals
- Balance between skepticism and wonder

The search for Primordiae reflects the Faction's commitment to [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] and their hope that the Terra Incognita holds resources that could revolutionize their technological capabilities.
