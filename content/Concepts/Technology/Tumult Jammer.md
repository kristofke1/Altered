# Tumult Jammer

## Overview

Tumult Jammer is device used by Expeditionary Corps to keep [[The Tumult|Tumult]] emanations at bay during operations beyond [[Asgartha]]. Device features rotating antenna like enormous weathervane that prevents landscape warping, reality twisting, and mutagenic effects while active.

## Physical Description

Device mounted on armored expedition vehicles with large antenna that rotates continuously above vehicle like weathervane. Antenna visible from distance, marking protected zone around vehicle.

## Function and Operation

### Protection Capabilities

While Tumult Jammer runs, it:
- Keeps Tumult emanations at bay
- Prevents landscape warping
- Prevents reality twisting  
- Protects against mutagenic effects
- Creates safe zone around vehicle

As long as jammer active, personnel within range remain safe from Tumult's transformative influence.

### Observable Effects

When jammer pulses active, wisps of rosy clouds (Tumult manifestations) are driven back from slopes. In jammer's wake, stone transforms into smooth saffron-colored striations. Concept of wind subtly manifests forming brief gusts only to be interwoven with other ideas before jammer disperses them.

### Alteration Integration

Jammer can have concepts channeled into it through [[Alteration]]. During 393 AC tsunami event, [[Sierra]] channeled concept of "Sealing" into jammer attempting to protect vehicle from incoming wave, demonstrating device can be augmented through Alteration techniques.

## Role in Expeditions

Tumult Jammer essential equipment for Expeditionary Corps operations in Tumult-affected regions. Device allows personnel to establish temporary safe zones, conduct mineral surveys, and claim territory without succumbing to Tumult's chaotic transformations.

Crew members work alongside jammer to maintain vehicles and equipment, as parasitic ideas still accumulate on surfaces over time requiring ongoing maintenance and stabilization.

## Limitations

### Range Constraints

Protection limited to zone around vehicle. [[Sierra]] needed to scout ahead beyond jammer's range, requiring her to monitor parasitic ideas on personal display and navigate carefully to avoid contamination.

### Durability

During 393 AC tsunami from [[Altered/Locations/Geographic Features/Storvhit]], despite Sierra channeling "Sealing" concept into jammer, device could not prevent massive wintry wave from overwhelming vehicle defenses. Parasitic ideas manifested inside cabin after impact, suggesting jammer either failed or was damaged by extreme Tumult surge.

## 393 AC Expedition Usage

### Mineral Survey Operations

[[Sierra]]'s expedition used Tumult Jammer to protect armored vehicle with caterpillar tracks during survey of territories identified by [[Prospector Guild]] as rich in [[Kelon]] and rare minerals. Jammer allowed crew to approach regions that would otherwise be immediately hostile to human presence.

### Tsunami Event

When tsunami struck from [[Altered/Locations/Geographic Features/Storvhit]], jammer could not withstand overwhelming surge of Tumult energy. Vehicle flipped, parasitic ideas manifested inside cabin despite jammer's presence, and crew suffered injuries though all survived.

## Relationships

- **[[The Tumult]]**: Phenomenon jammer designed to suppress
