# Khyalo Alphabet

The Khyalo Alphabet is a symbol system developed by the [[Axiom]] for use with [[Constructs]]. It brings together all the symbols necessary for manipulating these mechanical artifacts.

## Nature

Each symbol in the Khyalo Alphabet represents a concept, functioning like a hieroglyph. The symbols are inscribed on [[Constructs]] and serve as the interface between the [[Operators|Operator]] and the device.

## Function

When using [[Constructs]]:
1. [[Operators]] combine symbols from the Khyalo Alphabet
2. These combinations can be created by pressing keys or making symbols associate in space
3. The combinations shape complex ideas
4. The [[Kelon]] core within the Construct allows these ideas to materialize within reality

## Accessibility

A key feature of the Khyalo Alphabet is its accessibility. Everyone is capable of learning this alphabet and thus manipulating [[Constructs]], without necessarily having an innate talent for [[Alteration]].

This democratization of knowledge aligns with the [[Axiom]]'s philosophy of making technology and progress accessible to all social classes without exception.

## Significance

The Khyalo Alphabet represents an innovation in how [[Alteration]] can be performed, creating a standardized symbolic language that bridges the gap between human thought and reality manipulation through mechanical means rather than innate talent.
