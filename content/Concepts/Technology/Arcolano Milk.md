# Arcolano Milk

## Overview

Arcolano Milk is a psychotropic substance harvested in [[Hypogea]], the underground ecosystem beneath [[Caecaster]]. The substance appears as a sweet, intoxicating liquid with a milky appearance. [[Alterers]] use it to enhance their demiurgic abilities, and it has unique properties related to perception and interaction with [[The Aether]].

## Harvesting

Specialized workers harvest Arcolano Milk in the perpetual night of [[Hypogea]]'s depths. Harvesters like [[Nesiwe]] work in underground conditions characterized by subterranean rivers, chilly air contrasting with the arid desert above, and humidity laden with mineral scents.

## Properties and Effects

### Alteration Enhancement

[[Alterers]] use Arcolano Milk to enhance their demiurgic abilities. The psychotropic substance amplifies or facilitates connection with [[The Aether]], making it valuable for magical practitioners.

### Eidomantic Vision

The substance can enable a unique form of perception through [[Eidomancy]]. [[Lindiwe]], who was born blind, gained the ability to perceive her surroundings after exposure to Arcolano Milk. [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]] determined she did not see through her physical senses but by directly welcoming into her mind the ideas that resonated around her—perceiving through the prism of her mind alone.

### Risks

The [[Yzmir]] warn that abuse of Arcolano Milk can have serious side effects on the psyche if not properly disciplined. The substance requires careful management and training to use safely.

## Connection to Lindiwe's Bond with Maw

Arcolano Milk was present during [[Lindiwe]]'s transformative encounter with [[Maw]]. When she touched a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] worm in [[Hypogea]] and lost consciousness, she fell into a puddle of the substance. During this time she experienced her first vision and formed an unprecedented bond with [[Maw]]—a connection that occurred without [[The Musubi]] ceremony.
