# Paper Herald

Paper Herald is a communication and correspondence system used for transmitting written messages. The system employs [[Butterfly Folding]] as a preparation method and [[Rhombus Categorization]] for organizing and prioritizing correspondence.

## System Components

### Butterfly Folding

[[Butterfly Folding]] is the method used to prepare messages for transmission through the Paper Herald system. The specific folding technique serves functional purposes in the correspondence process.

### Rhombus Categorization

[[Rhombus Categorization]] provides a priority classification system for Paper Herald correspondence. Messages are assigned priority levels ranging at minimum from Priority 6 to potentially higher urgency classifications.

The categorization system allows recipients to assess message importance and organize response priorities accordingly.

## 392-393 AC - Waru's Correspondence

### Usage by Waru Toowoom

[[Waru Toowoom]] utilized the Paper Herald system for his correspondence during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] from October 392 AC through January 393 AC. His letters were prepared using Butterfly Folding and categorized as Rhombus Priority 6.

### Correspondence Headers

[[Waru Toowoom]]'s correspondence included standardized headers:
```
Waru Toowoom
Paper Herald - Butterfly Folding
Rhombus Categorization / Priority 6
```

## Relationship to Other Systems

### The Talaria

Paper Herald messages were transmitted through [[The Talaria]], suggesting the Paper Herald system provides the formatting and preparation method while [[The Talaria]] handles the actual transmission and delivery.

The increasing difficulty [[Waru Toowoom]] experienced getting dispatches through [[The Talaria]] suggests the transmission system faced operational challenges or surveillance during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] period.

### The Gestalt

While [[The Gestalt]] provides instantaneous communication for [[Ordis]] faction members through their Rhombus tattoos, the Paper Herald system offers an alternative correspondence method. The use of "Rhombus Categorization" in the Paper Herald system may reference [[The Gestalt]]'s Rhombus symbolism while functioning as a separate physical correspondence system.

## Strategic Value

The Paper Herald system serves important functions:
- **Secure Correspondence**: Physical messages less vulnerable to Gestalt monitoring
- **Priority Management**: Rhombus Categorization enables efficient message triage
- **Cross-Faction Communication**: Not limited to Gestalt-connected Ordis members
- **Record Keeping**: Physical letters provide permanent documentary evidence
- **Diplomatic Communications**: Suitable for sensitive political correspondence
