# Grafts

Grafts are artifacts created by the [[Axiom]] that obey the will of their holder by the sheer force of thought. They represent an advanced form of prosthetic and enhancement technology.

## Creation Method

By infusing an object with an idea of oneself, it is possible to link one's mind to it so that the object becomes a part of the bearer's identity. It is through this method that members of the [[Axiom]] have created Grafts.

## Types and Forms

Grafts can serve various purposes:
- Additional limbs
- Prosthetics (replacement limbs)
- Other enhancements

A wide variety of models have been developed, each with a range of utilities.

## Construction

Grafts are equipped with:
- Pistons
- Cogwheels
- Mechanical joints

These Grafts strap onto the body and are easily controlled once one becomes accustomed to their presence, as if they were another part of the body.

## Operation

The key feature of Grafts is their mind-link. Once attached and attuned to the user, they respond directly to thought, becoming an extension of the user's will and body.

## Current Status and Goals

These artifacts are becoming increasingly widespread in [[Asgartha]], and the [[Axiom]] hope to expand these devices to the rest of the Known World as it is rediscovered through [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Memory Grafts

In 393 AC, healers from [[The Cloister]] working with [[Rati]] surgeons developed a non-mechanical application: memory grafts. These psychic fragments, donated by loved ones, are grafted onto the minds of patients suffering from [[Oblivion]]—an affliction that causes progressive memory loss. The artificial memories serve as patches for broken minds, allowing patients to function despite inconsistencies in their reconstructed recollections.

## Significance

Grafts represent the [[Axiom]]'s commitment to improving human lives through practical technology, offering solutions for those who have lost limbs or who wish to enhance their physical capabilities.
