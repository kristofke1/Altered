# FarFetch 2.0

## Overview

FarFetch 2.0 is a modified maintenance rig converted by [[Treyst]] and [[Rossum]] to house [[Paju]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]], allowing the deceased pilot to explore autonomously without requiring continuous manifestation.

## Design and Origin

The original machine was a non-Asgarthan maintenance rig, likely designed for handling [[Aerolith]]. It featured prehensile claws for grabbing crystals from a safe distance, shatterproof glass for explosion protection, and a cushioned pilot cabin. [[Treyst]] and [[Rossum]] modified the rig with [[Kelon|Kelonic]] convectors that produce enough energy to keep an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] incarnated for hours.

## Purpose

[[Treyst]] created FarFetch 2.0 as a final gift for [[Paju]], fulfilling a dream to let his friend fly freely and explore at leisure without requiring [[Treyst]]'s presence to maintain the manifestation.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Construction and Activation

[[Treyst]] and [[Rossum]] completed modifications to the maintenance rig, installing [[Kelon|Kelonic]] convectors in the cockpit and using [[Alteration]] [[Constructs]] to summon [[Paju]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] into the machine.
