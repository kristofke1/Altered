# Constructs

Constructs are the signature alteration style and technology of the [[Axiom]] Faction. They are mechanical artifacts that enable human populations to protect themselves from the ravages of [[The Tumult]] without requiring the presence of an [[Alterers|Alterer]].

## Purpose and Philosophy

The [[Axiom]] have made Constructs their trademark, embodying their philosophy that technology can democratize protection against [[The Tumult]]. Thanks to the Constructs they develop, human populations can defend themselves without needing an Alterer present.

This reality is particularly prevalent in areas of the peninsula where [[Mana]] is still somewhat unstable. While Constructs cannot replace the versatility and flexibility of [[Alterers]], they provide a valuable alternative where the influence of [[Asgartha]], and consequently the speed of intervention of Alterers, is drastically reduced.

## Physical Form

Constructs are mechanical artifacts that can take various forms:
- Three-dimensional puzzles
- Cylindrical cryptexes
- Other configurations

All Constructs are covered in symbols from the [[Khyalo Alphabet]].

## Operation

Each symbol on a Construct represents a concept, like a hieroglyph. The process works as follows:

1. [[Operators]] combine symbols by pressing keys or making them associate in space
2. These combinations shape complex ideas
3. The [[Kelon]] core contained within the Construct allows these ideas to express themselves and materialize within reality

## Accessibility

A key feature of Constructs is their accessibility. Everyone is capable of learning the [[Khyalo Alphabet]] that brings together all these symbols, and thus manipulating Constructs, without necessarily having an innate talent for [[Alteration]].

This democratization of alteration power aligns with the [[Axiom]]'s philosophy of making technology available to all social classes.

## Creation

Constructs are created at [[The Athanor]], near the [[Nexus]] energy core, where the concentrated [[Kelon]] energy can be properly integrated into the devices.
