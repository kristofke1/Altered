# Mistbreakers

## Overview
Mistbreakers are large tonnage vessels used for maritime transport in [[Asgartha]], particularly for shipping ores from [[Suspira]] to [[Arkaster]].

## Development
The inauguration of the [[Sundström Route]] through [[Undersea|the Undersea]] enabled capital shipowners to charter much larger tonnage vessels than previously possible. These vessels became known as mistbreakers, named for their ability to navigate the perpetually misty waters of the Undersea.

## Function
Mistbreakers are primarily used for transporting [[Kelon]] and [[Aerolith|Aerolithe]] from [[Suspira]]'s mines to the capital. Their larger cargo capacity made ore transport significantly more efficient compared to the smaller vessels that previously navigated the difficult [[Shawl]] passage.

## Relationships
- **[[Sundström Route]]**: Maritime route that enabled mistbreaker usage
- **[[Undersea]]**: Misty sea where mistbreakers operate
- **[[Suspira]]**: Origin point for mistbreaker cargo
- **[[Arkaster]]**: Destination for mistbreaker cargo
- **[[Kelon]]**: Primary cargo transported
- **[[Aerolith]]**: Secondary cargo transported
