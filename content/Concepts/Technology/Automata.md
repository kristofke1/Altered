# Automata

Automata are humanity's first robotic beings, representing one of the [[Axiom]]'s flagship projects. The Faction is on the verge of realizing this ambitious goal.

## Development Status

Several prototypes have already been designed and tested away from the public eye, with results that are more than encouraging. The project relies notably on the programming talents of [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] [[Ada Lovelace]].

## Potential Unveiling

With a bit of luck, the Automata may be presented at the upcoming [[Prodigium]], the grand technological fair of the [[Axiom]].

## Intended Purpose

The [[Axiom]] hopes that these new marvels of engineering will mark a new turning point in the ongoing industrial revolution within the peninsula. The key advantage of Automata is that they could go where humanity struggles to venture, whether it be:
- Hostile environments
- Within [[The Tumult]] itself

## Significance

The Automata project represents the cutting edge of [[Axiom]] innovation, combining:
- Mechanical engineering
- [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] programming expertise
- [[Kelon]] power systems
- Advanced automation

This project embodies the [[Axiom]]'s philosophy of using technology to overcome the limitations that [[The Tumult]] has imposed on humanity, allowing exploration and work in areas too dangerous for human presence.

## Key Figures

- [[Ada Lovelace]] - Eidolon providing programming expertise for the project
