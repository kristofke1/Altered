# Transasgarthan Railway

## Overview

The Transasgarthan Railway is a major railway network connecting [[Kestia]] in the east to [[Sadracca]] in the west, spanning the entire [[Asgartha]]n peninsula. Construction began in 209 AC under the direction of [[Kaja ruun-Aksoy]] and was completed in 234 AC when the two construction sites converged at [[Avesta]].

## Construction Timeline

### 209 AC - Project Launch
[[Kaja ruun-Aksoy]] launched the joint construction of [[Thule Station]] and [[Svarga Station]], announcing the ambitious Transasgarthan project that would connect [[Kestia]] to [[Sadracca]].

### 214 AC - Construction Begins
The two construction sites began at opposite ends of the planned route amidst general euphoria and fierce competition between the eastern and western teams.

### 222 AC - Arkaster Connection
[[Asgartha]] was connected by railway to [[Verbera]], on the border of the Provinces of [[Caer Kettria]] and [[Caer Arcolano]].

### 234 AC - Completion
After years of hard work, the two construction sites finally converged in [[Avesta]], completing the main trunk line.

### 238 AC - Extension to Seki
The railroad extended all the way to [[Seki]], marking the full completion of the network.

## Significance

The Transasgarthan Railway represented a major engineering achievement and transformed travel and commerce across the peninsula. The project unified distant regions and facilitated the movement of goods, people, and resources between the major population centers of [[Asgartha]].

## Related Infrastructure

The railway connects to [[The Athanor]], the [[Axiom]]'s architectural jewel, which has direct access to the Altiport with a marshaling yard and a dozen railway tracks. Trains transport machines to aerostats that disseminate technology throughout the peninsula.

## Relationships

- **[[Kaja ruun-Aksoy]]**: Architect and project director who launched construction (209 AC)
- **[[Thule Station]]**: Northern terminus station
- **[[Svarga Station]]**: Station along the route
- **[[Avesta]]**: Location where eastern and western construction sites met (234 AC)
- **[[The Athanor]]**: [[Axiom]] facility with direct railway access
