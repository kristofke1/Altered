# Altrun

## Overview
Altrun is a discipline practiced in [[Asgartha]] that combines parkour, freerunning, and acrobatics with [[Alteration]] magic. Practitioners, known as Altrunners, traverse urban environments by jumping from roof to roof, performing somersaults, sliding on tiles, and using Alteration to create temporary structures as holds, supports, or shortcuts.

## Technique
Altrunners use [[Alteration]] to perform improbable feats and create various structures out of nothing:
- Stone pylons
- Wooden gangways
- Glass slides

These manifestations serve as holds, supports, or shortcuts to optimize their routes through the city. The discipline requires both physical prowess in acrobatics and skill in Alteration magic to manifest structures mid-run.

## Urban Practice
In [[Arkaster]], Altrunners can be seen descending from roof to roof with incredible agility. They:
- Perform somersaults
- Slide on scorching tiles
- Cling to gutters
- Use chimneys, walls, beams, and other vantage points

The runners know the city intimately and frequently change locations to vary their routes, gathering on rooftops to warm up before runs.

## Competitions
Altrun features both official and unofficial competitions. Teams within the capital regularly compete against each other. The ultimate goal is to participate in official tournaments held at the Colosseum.

Matches involve sprinters racing from starting point to finish line, navigating the urban landscape using their Alteration skills and acrobatic abilities. The first to reach the finish line wins.

## The Omnium
[[The Omnium]] is the annual championship of Altrun, representing the pinnacle of the sport. Teams compete for the cup, and winning brings significant prestige and potential sponsorship opportunities.

## Social Aspect
Altrun culture centers around teams and their headquarters, typically taverns like the Speedster's Den. Runners form tight-knit communities, training together, competing together, and celebrating victories as a group. Team mascots and emblems are common, such as [[Booda]] serving as the mascot for the Jusaka Blaze.

## Connection to Bravos Values
The discipline embodies [[Bravos]] values of daring, athleticism, and pushing boundaries. The sport's emphasis on speed, risk-taking, and exploration of urban environments aligns with the faction's adventurous spirit.
