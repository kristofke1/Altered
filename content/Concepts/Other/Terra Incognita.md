# Terra Incognita

## Overview

Terra Incognita refers to the unknown lands beyond [[Asgartha]]'s borders, territories still affected by [[The Tumult]] that humanity has not yet explored or reclaimed.

## Geography

The Terra Incognita encompasses the vast territories outside the stabilized [[Asgartha|Asgarthan]] Peninsula. According to accounts from the [[The Great Wandering|Great Wandering]], these lands contain wonderful lands, moors of unreal beauty, baroque palaces of improbable architecture, and desolated places gripped by chaos where ice burns and mountains float above rivers of obsidian.

## The Great Wandering

During [[The Great Wandering]], the Tumult Nomad tribes roamed the Terra Incognita for one hundred to one hundred and fifty years following [[The Confluence]]. They traveled from [[Oases|Oasis]] to Oasis, from places shielded from the Tumult to makeshift shelters. The legends passed down by these nomads speak of cities, palaces, and enclaves where survivors awaited rescue.

## The Rediscovery Endeavor

In 392 AC, Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] proclaimed the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], declaring it was time to turn outward toward the Terra Incognita. The stated mission objectives include discovering other [[Oases]] and reinforcing them, finding other [[The Oneiroi|Oneiroi]] allies, locating potential human survivors, and finding the source of [[The Tumult]].

## Known Regions

Several regions of the Terra Incognita have been identified through exploration:

- **[[Caer Oorun]]**: The first province reclaimed during the Rediscovery Endeavor
- **[[Storvhit]]**: A winter region perpetually locked in harsh cold, centered on [[Cais Adarra]] mountain
- **[[City of Scholars]]**: An ancient settlement referenced in Tumult Nomad records
