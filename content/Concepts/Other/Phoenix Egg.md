# Phoenix Egg

## Overview

The Phoenix Egg is a sacred artifact of the [[Bravos]] Faction, containing the dormant form of the Asgarthan [[Phoenix]]. When [[Rune]] disappeared in 42 AC, the Phoenix consumed itself and reformed into this egg, which has refused to hatch for over 350 years. The egg is central to [[Rune's Testament]] and the true purpose of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Origins

When Rune disappeared into [[The Tumult]] at the twilight of his reign, the Phoenix consumed itself, leaving behind the egg. The [[Bravos]] have carefully watched over it since time immemorial, awaiting the emergence of the Phoenix once again.

## Location at Haven

The egg was kept in a central brazier with everlasting flames at the [[Parliament of Ashes]], the heart of [[Haven]]. [[Tiny Jinn]] maintained the flames, serving as eternal guardians. The [[Phoenix Chamber]] deep beneath Haven also housed the egg, where it was incubated in connection to the site of Rune's coronation and the enactment of [[The Creed]].

## The Legend

A persistent legend within the [[Bravos]] Faction holds that the Firebird will only reappear when Rune's true heir is found. This belief motivated many Bravos to volunteer for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], hoping to find traces of the first sovereign and perhaps his tomb.

## Theft from Haven (393 AC)

In 393 AC, the Phoenix Egg was stolen from [[Haven]]. Initial reports caused Haven to be sealed off—even from the [[Lyra]]—with only [[Bravos]] permitted to circulate freely within its walls. An armorer named [[Tessalit Girum]], who had been appointed to modernize the Bravos' training dummies, was taken in for questioning regarding her involvement in the theft.

The theft was reported in the [[Arkaster Echo]] newspaper, which [[Waru Toowoom]] read while stationed at [[Storvhit]].

## Transport Westward

The theft was revealed to be part of a larger plan connected to [[Rune's Testament]]. [[Bravos]] loyal to the Testament secretly joined the [[Tisdhera Clan]] aboard [[The Wayfarer]], carrying the Phoenix Egg westward. According to the Testament, the egg must be taken to the Phoenix's nest in the west, where it can be rekindled and the Firebird reborn.

[[Sitina]], a [[Phoenixian Guard]] Blade Dancer, revealed this truth to [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Kojo Oduro]] after they discovered Rune's scroll in the [[City of Scholars]]. The Testament declared: "The Firebird must be rekindled, reborn. Only then can the world rise from its ashes."

## Significance to Rune's Testament

The Phoenix Egg represents the true objective of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]—not the fabricated Source of [[The Tumult]], but the rebirth of the Phoenix. Rune foresaw that the Source would be revealed as a mirage, and left instructions for the egg to be transported westward to its nest. The [[Phoenixian Guard]] serve as keepers of this secret, bearing golden tracings on their skin that mark their role as Testament stewards.
