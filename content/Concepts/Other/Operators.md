# Operators

## Overview

Operators are individuals who handle and manipulate [[Constructs]], the mechanical artifacts developed by the [[Axiom]] Faction. They serve as the human interface with Constructs, trained to use these mechanical artifacts to protect against [[The Tumult]] without requiring innate [[Alteration]] talent.

## Training Requirements

To become an Operator, an individual must learn the [[Khyalo Alphabet]]—the symbol system inscribed on Constructs—and understand how to combine symbols to shape complex ideas. Operators master the physical manipulation of Constructs by pressing keys and associating symbols in space.

## Function

When an Operator uses a [[Constructs|Construct]], they combine symbols from the [[Khyalo Alphabet]]. These combinations shape complex ideas, and the [[Kelon]] core within the Construct allows these ideas to express themselves and materialize within reality.

## Accessibility

The role of Operator is designed to be accessible to anyone capable of learning the [[Khyalo Alphabet]], without necessarily requiring innate talent for [[Alteration]]. This reflects the [[Axiom]]'s philosophy of making protection and technological advancement available to all social classes.

## Relationships

- **[[Constructs]]**: Mechanical artifacts Operators manipulate
- **[[Khyalo Alphabet]]**: Symbol system Operators must learn
- **[[Kelon]]**: Energy source within Constructs that materializes Operator commands
- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction that developed the Operator system
- **[[Alteration]]**: Magical ability that Operators do not require
