# Operators

Operators are individuals who handle and manipulate [[Constructs]], the mechanical artifacts developed by the [[Axiom]] Faction.

## Role

Operators serve as the human interface with [[Constructs]]. They are trained to use these mechanical artifacts to protect against [[The Tumult]] without requiring innate [[Alteration]] talent.

## Skills and Training

To become an Operator, an individual must:
- Learn the [[Khyalo Alphabet]], the symbol system inscribed on Constructs
- Understand how to combine symbols to shape complex ideas
- Master the physical manipulation of Constructs (pressing keys, associating symbols in space)

## Function

When an Operator uses a [[Constructs|Construct]]:
1. They combine symbols from the [[Khyalo Alphabet]]
2. These combinations shape complex ideas
3. The [[Kelon]] core within the Construct allows these ideas to express themselves and materialize within reality

## Accessibility

The role of Operator is designed to be accessible to everyone. The [[Axiom]] have created a system where anyone capable of learning the [[Khyalo Alphabet]] can manipulate [[Constructs]], without necessarily having an innate talent for [[Alteration]].

This democratization reflects the [[Axiom]]'s philosophy of making protection and technological advancement available to all social classes.

## Significance

Operators represent the [[Axiom]]'s vision of a future where protection from [[The Tumult]] and the ability to manipulate reality are not limited to those born with [[Alteration]] talent, but are accessible to anyone willing to learn.
