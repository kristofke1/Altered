Anno Concordiae (abbreviated AC, meaning "Year of the Concord") is the calendar system established by Rune when he founded Asgartha in Year 1 AC. The dating system commemorates the proclamation of the Concord, the founding treaty that bound all Tumult Nomad tribes into one people, making this moment the cornerstone of Asgarthan chronology and identity.

The choice to name the calendar system after the Concord was deliberate and symbolic. By marking time from the moment of unity rather than from the Confluence or some other cataclysmic event, Rune emphasized that Asgartha's true beginning was not defined by destruction but by cooperation, not by catastrophe but by covenant.

Year 1 AC marks the specific moment when:
- The Phoenix crowned Rune at Kuningasplatz
- Rune proclaimed the Concord
- The Tumult Nomad tribes bound themselves into one people
- The nation of Asgartha was founded
- The Trusted Council was established

All dates in Asgarthan history are calculated from this founding moment. For example, the current year as of the Rediscovery Endeavor's launch is 392 AC—392 years since the Concord was proclaimed.

The abbreviation AC appears in all official documents, historical records, and everyday usage throughout Asgartha. It serves as a constant reminder of the treaty that made civilization possible and the principle that unity is the foundation of survival.

The calendar represents more than a practical chronological system; it embodies Asgarthan values and historical consciousness. Every time an Asgarthan writes a date, they invoke the memory of the Concord and the commitment to cooperation over conflict. The dating system itself becomes a form of civic ritual, reinforcing the covenant that binds the people together.

The period before Year 1 AC—the time of the Great Wandering, the Age of Tumult, and the ancient world before the Confluence—exists outside this system or is referenced as "before the Concord," marking it as prehistory to the unified nation.
