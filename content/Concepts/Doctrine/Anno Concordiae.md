# Anno Concordiae

## Overview

Anno Concordiae (abbreviated AC, meaning "Year of the Concord") is the calendar system established by [[Rune]] when he founded [[Asgartha]] in Year 1 AC. The dating system commemorates the proclamation of [[The Concord - Concept|the Concord]], the founding treaty that bound all [[Tumult Nomads|Tumult Nomad]] tribes into one people.

## Year 1 AC

Year 1 AC marks the moment when the [[Phoenix]] crowned [[Rune]] at [[Kuningasplatz]], Rune proclaimed the Concord, the Tumult Nomad tribes bound themselves into one people, the nation of [[Asgartha]] was founded, and [[The Trusted Council]] was established.

## Usage

All dates in Asgarthan history are calculated from this founding moment. The abbreviation AC appears in all official documents, historical records, and everyday usage throughout [[Asgartha]]. The current year as of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]'s launch is 392 AC.

The period before Year 1 AC—the time of [[The Great Wandering]], the Age of [[The Tumult|Tumult]], and the ancient world before [[The Confluence]]—is referenced as "before the Concord."

## Relationships

- **[[The Concord - Concept]]**: Treaty commemorated by the calendar system
- **[[Rune]]**: First Kuningas who established the dating system
- **[[Phoenix]]**: Entity that crowned Rune at the founding moment
- **[[Kuningasplatz]]**: Location of the founding events
- **[[Asgartha]]**: Nation whose history is measured in AC
