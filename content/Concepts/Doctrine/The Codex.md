# The Codex

## Overview
The Codex is the comprehensive body of laws developed and maintained by the [[Ordis]] faction. It serves as the foundation upon which human society must be rebuilt according to Ordis philosophy.

## Purpose and Philosophy
For the [[Ordis]], justice is the foundation upon which human society must be rebuilt. To constitute a solid base, this justice must be unwavering in its impartiality. The framework of the social structure must be inflexible and resilient to solidify the entire edifice.

## Development and Maintenance
The Ordis takes special care in developing and maintaining the Codex. Laws and edicts are crafted within [[The Anthill]], the lower part of [[The Monolith]], where a myriad of cabinets and offices work tirelessly on legal matters.

The process involves:
- Clerks and administrative officers carrying messages and documents
- Decrees to be signed and bills to be ratified
- Constant refinement and evolution of legal frameworks

## Evolution and Adaptation
Influenced by the precepts of the [[Lyra]], the Ordis knows that order must constantly evolve and adapt, like a reed bending to endure the hurricane. The Codex is therefore not static but continuously refined to meet changing circumstances while maintaining its core principles of impartiality and permanence.

## Role in Governance
The Codex serves as the legal framework through which [[The Tetrarchy]] implements its decisions. After major orientations are determined and passed through popular vote, they are concretized into official decrees that become part of the Codex.

## Judicial Application
Within [[The Anthill]], the Tribunal uses the Codex to dispense justice. In this vast hall, those who have transgressed are judged, hear verdicts, and receive sentences under the impartial gaze of the [[Eidolons]], all according to the principles enshrined in the Codex.
