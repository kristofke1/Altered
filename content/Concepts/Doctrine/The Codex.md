# The Codex

## Overview

The Codex is the comprehensive body of laws developed and maintained by the [[Ordis]] faction. It serves as the legal framework through which [[The Tetrarchy]] implements its decisions.

## Development and Maintenance

The Ordis develops and maintains the Codex within [[The Anthill]], the lower part of [[The Monolith]], where a myriad of cabinets and offices work on legal matters. Clerks and administrative officers carry messages and documents while decrees are signed and bills ratified.

Influenced by the precepts of the [[Lyra]], the Ordis holds that order must constantly evolve and adapt. The Codex is therefore not static but continuously refined to meet changing circumstances.

## Judicial Application

Within [[The Anthill]], the Tribunal uses the Codex to dispense justice. In this vast hall, those who have transgressed are judged, hear verdicts, and receive sentences under the impartial gaze of the [[Eidolons]].

After major orientations are determined by [[The Tetrarchy]] and passed through popular vote, they are concretized into official decrees that become part of the Codex.

## Relationships

- **[[Ordis]]**: Faction that develops and maintains the Codex
- **[[The Tetrarchy]]**: Governing body whose decisions become Codex decrees
- **[[The Anthill]]**: Location where the Codex is developed and justice dispensed
- **[[The Monolith]]**: Ordis headquarters containing the Anthill
- **[[Eidolons]]**: Oversee judicial proceedings under the Codex
