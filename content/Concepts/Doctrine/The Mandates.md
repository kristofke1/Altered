# The Mandates

## Overview
The Mandates are a set of strict conventions and rules regarding the use of magical talents imposed by the [[Yzmir]] upon themselves. They serve as ethical guidelines to prevent abuse of the considerable power accumulated by [[Initiates]].

## Purpose and History
The [[Yzmir]], aware of the power they accumulate through [[The Naali]], know they must exercise restraint. The history of the Faction, particularly in its early years, was marked by sorcerers who abused their powers and used them for their own benefit.

The Mandates were established to prevent such abuses from recurring and to ensure that the dangerous practice of directly channeling [[The Aether|Aether]] serves the greater good rather than personal gain.

## The First Mandate
The first Mandate is not trivial and serves as the foundation for all others:

**Initiates must never use their gifts for personal gain, but always for a cause or an ideal in which they believe.**

This principle ensures that the considerable power of Yzmir magic remains directed toward higher purposes rather than selfish ends.

## Enforcement and Philosophy
The Mandates represent a form of self-regulation by the [[Yzmir]]. Rather than having external oversight, the Faction chose to impose these restrictions upon itself, recognizing that with great power comes the necessity for great responsibility.

By adhering to the Mandates, [[Initiates]] demonstrate:
- Commitment to ideals over personal benefit
- Restraint in the face of temptation
- Understanding of the dangerous nature of their powers
- Dedication to protecting [[Asgartha]] rather than exploiting it

## Connection to Yzmir Mission
The Mandates align with the broader Yzmir mission of studying the secret arcana of the world to protect humanity. Through hermetic experiments, they gain power to protect [[Asgartha]], so that the rest of humanity can continue to live oblivious to the dangers lurking at the edge of existence. The Mandates ensure this power remains protective rather than exploitative.
