# The Mandates

## Overview

The Mandates are a set of strict conventions and rules regarding the use of magical talents imposed by the [[Yzmir]] upon themselves. They serve as ethical guidelines to prevent abuse of the considerable power accumulated by [[Initiates]].

## History

The history of the [[Yzmir]] Faction, particularly in its early years, was marked by sorcerers who abused their powers and used them for their own benefit. The Mandates were established to prevent such abuses from recurring.

## The First Mandate

The first Mandate serves as the foundation for all others:

> **Initiates must never use their gifts for personal gain, but always for a cause or an ideal in which they believe.**

## Bound Eidolons

Certain [[Eidolons]] are bound by the Mandates and cannot be summoned under normal circumstances. [[Mantichora]] and [[Khimaira]] are examples of bound Eidolons—their illegal manifestation during the [[Cebir]] massacre in 352 AC indicated the involvement of a renegade [[Yzmir]] mage who violated the Mandates.

## Relationships

- **[[Yzmir]]**: Faction that imposes the Mandates upon itself
- **[[Initiates]]**: Yzmir practitioners bound by the Mandates
- **[[The Naali]]**: Power accumulation that necessitates the Mandates
- **[[Mantichora]]**: Eidolon bound by Mandates
- **[[Khimaira]]**: Eidolon bound by Mandates
- **[[Sylas]]**: Renegade mage suspected of violating Mandates
