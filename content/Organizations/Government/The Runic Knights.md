# The Runic Knights

## Overview

The Runic Knights were the legendary advisors who formed the first [[The Trusted Council|Trusted Council]], serving [[Rune]], the first [[Kuningas]] of [[Asgartha]]. This group of eight individuals became the mythical founding heroes of the nation. Their descendants became the [[Old Nobility]]—the aristocratic class closely associated with power throughout Asgarthan history.

## Composition

The Runic Knights consisted of both [[Rune]]'s original allies who helped prevent civil war and the former opposition leaders who reconciled after the [[Phoenix]]'s divine choice.

### Original Allies
- [[Heshkari]] (champion, later founded [[Bravos]])
- [[Caellach]] (later founded [[Ordis]])
- [[Tamrat]]
- [[Aksoy]]
- [[Heeren]]
### Reconciled Barons
- [[Balram Kasun]] (Tribe of the East)
- [[Cilistro Tighe]] (Tribe of the West)
- [[Ainara Aldana]] (Tribe of the Lower Banks)

This composition demonstrated [[Rune]]'s commitment to inclusive governance. By appointing both allies and former enemies, he ensured [[The Trusted Council|the Trusted Council]] represented all survivor communities rather than just a victorious faction. This act of reconciliation became a founding principle of Asgarthan unity.

## Role and Authority

The Runic Knights advised the [[Kuningas]] on all matters of state, with the authority to challenge his decisions if necessary. Their wisdom and diverse perspectives helped establish the early policies, structures, and principles of Asgarthan civilization.

## The Vanishing

Three of the Runic Knights—[[Aksoy]], [[Ainara Aldana|Aldana]], and [[Heeren]]—vanished alongside [[Rune]] in 42 AC in the mysterious event known as [[The Vanishing]]. The surviving Knights continued to serve subsequent rulers, though the formal organization's status after Rune's disappearance is unclear.

## Legacy

The Runic Knights' legacy endures through their descendant houses: [[House ruun-Heshkari|ruun-Heshkari]], [[House ruun-Caellach|ruun-Caellach]], [[House ruun-Tamrat|ruun-Tamrat]], [[House ruun-Aksoy|ruun-Aksoy]], [[House ruun-Heeren|ruun-Heeren]], [[House ruun-Kasun|ruun-Kasun]], [[House ruun-Tighe|ruun-Tighe]], and [[House ruun-Aldana|ruun-Aldana]]—eight of the nine patrician dynasties known as the [[Old Nobility]]. The ninth house, [[House ruun-Aysun|ruun-Aysun]], descends from [[Rune]] through his daughter.
