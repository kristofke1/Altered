# Privy Council

## Overview

The Privy Council is an executive body within the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan]] government that advises the [[Basileus]] and votes on key appointments and policy decisions. It is composed of representatives from the executive branch, including members of the [[Conclave]] and the [[Chancellery]].

## Known Actions

In 79 AC, [[Ayxas]] presented a motion of no confidence against the [[Factions]] to the Privy Council, leading to the unilateral dissolution of the [[Bravos]] and [[Ordis]] by royal decree and the subjugation of the [[Yzmir]] to the [[Castigar]].

In approximately 390 AC, the Privy Council voted in favor of [[Temera Singh]] among proposed candidates to lead the [[Expeditionary Corps]], with [[Abelen]] putting forward her name.
