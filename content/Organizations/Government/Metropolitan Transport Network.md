# Metropolitan Transport Network

## Overview
The Metropolitan Transport Network (MTN) is the organization responsible for managing the capital's transport lines in [[Arkaster]].

## Responsibilities
The MTN oversees:
- Maintenance of railways and rail infrastructure
- Maintenance of runways and takeoff platforms
- Coordinating and managing the logistics of all public transport vehicles:
  - Trains
  - Trams
  - Airships
  - Other aircraft

## Exceptions
The MTN does not manage anything related to maritime transport, despite maritime transport being the most widespread means of transportation in Asgartha.
