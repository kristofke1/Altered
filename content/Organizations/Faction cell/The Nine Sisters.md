# The Nine Sisters

## Overview
The Nine Sisters are [[Eidolons]] who serve as tutelary patrons to the [[Lyra]] Clans. Each of the nine [[Saraband|Sarabands]] has one of the Nine Sisters as their patroness, functioning as a Muse for their respective Clan.

## Relationship with the Matriarchs
Each Matriarch of the nine [[Saraband|Sarabands]] represents an exclusive interlocutor for one of the Nine Sisters. This connection forms the spiritual and organizational foundation of [[Lyra]] authority, as [[The Nine Matriarchs]] serve as the voices through which the Nine Sisters communicate with their respective Clans.

## Connection to Mnemosyne
The Matriarchs are each the voice of a Muse, and each Muse is the patroness of a [[Lyra]] Clan. The Nine Sisters are understood to be the daughters of [[Mnemosyne]], the goddess of memory and mother of the Muses. [[Mnemosyne]] holds authority over the Nine Sisters, making her theoretically capable of imposing her will upon them.

## Role in Lyra Governance
The Nine Sisters rarely exercise direct authority over the [[Lyra]]. Instead, they prefer to allow [[The Nine Matriarchs]] to watch over their processions like shepherds over their flocks. However, when tradition is violated or significant decisions must be made, the Nine Sisters can act through the Matriarchs to enforce their will.

## The Kasirga Clan Exile
When the [[Kasirga Clan]] elected an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] as their Shepherd, breaking with the tradition that only a mortal could serve in this role, the Nine Sisters responded through the Matriarchs. According to tradition, the role of Shepherd was reserved for a mortal who could become the vessel of the Matriarch's voice, not an imaginary being.

The Matriarchs declared that if the [[Kasirga Clan]] persisted in pursuing this path, it would be banished from the [[Lyra]] alliance. When the Clan elders remained steadfast in their decision, the Nine Sisters had no choice but to exile them, a dishonor the Clan had to endure forever.
