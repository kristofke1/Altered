# The Nine Sisters

## Overview

The Nine Sisters are [[Eidolons]] who serve as tutelary patrons to the [[Lyra]]. Each of the nine [[Saraband|Sarabands]] has one of the Nine Sisters as their patroness, functioning as a Muse for their respective Clan. They are the daughters of [[Mnemosyne]], the goddess of memory.

## Connection to Mnemosyne

The Nine Sisters are understood to be the daughters of [[Mnemosyne]], the goddess of memory and mother of the Muses. Mnemosyne holds authority over the Nine Sisters, making her theoretically capable of imposing her will upon them.

## Relationship with the Matriarchs

Each Matriarch of [[The Nine Matriarchs]] serves as the exclusive interlocutor for one of the Nine Sisters. This connection forms the spiritual and organizational foundation of [[Lyra]] authority—the Matriarchs are the voices through which the Nine Sisters communicate with their respective Clans.

## Role in Lyra Governance

The Nine Sisters rarely exercise direct authority over the [[Lyra]]. They prefer to allow [[The Nine Matriarchs]] to watch over their processions. However, when tradition is violated or significant decisions must be made, the Nine Sisters can act through the Matriarchs to enforce their will.

## The Kasirga Clan Exile

When the [[Kasirga Clan]] elected [[Esmeralda]], an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]], as their Shepherd, the Nine Sisters responded through the Matriarchs. According to tradition, the role of Shepherd was reserved for a mortal who could become the vessel of the Matriarch's voice—not an imaginary being.

The Matriarchs declared that if the Kasirga Clan persisted, it would be banished from the Lyra alliance. When the Clan elders remained steadfast in their decision, the Nine Sisters had no choice but to exile them—a dishonor the Clan must endure forever.

## Known Members

One of the Nine Sisters has been identified:

- **[[Thalia]]**: One of the Greek Muses, now exists as an [[Eidolons|Oneiros]]. Serves as Shepherdess of the [[Tisdhera Clan]].
