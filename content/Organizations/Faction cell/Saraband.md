# Sarabands

## Overview
A Saraband is a grouping of [[Lyra]] caravans that share the same ancestry and a common set of customs. These Sarabands are nine in number.

## Organization
Each [[Lyra]] procession, from the most modest to the most imposing, is necessarily part of a Saraband. Each Saraband has a Matriarch at their helm, an elder who ensures the respect of traditions and the memory of the [[Lyra]] Faction.

## Leadership
These Matriarchs constitute [[The Nine Matriarchs]], the supreme authority among the [[Lyra]], as each represents an exclusive interlocutor for the Nine Sisters, the [[Eidolons]] who serve as their tutelary patrons. However, they rarely use this preeminence, preferring instead to watch over their procession like a shepherd over his flock.
