# Khamsin Clan

## Overview

The Khamsin Clan is one of the nine [[Lyra]] [[Saraband|Sarabands]]. The Clan is known for maintaining skilled healers who provide medical aid to other Lyra in need.

## Healing Practices

When the [[Tisdhera Clan]]'s caravan was struck by a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] anomaly, the Khamsin responded to treat the injured. [[Sierra]], then a child of the Tisdhera Clan, had her legs mutated by exposure to a Tumult pool. The Khamsin healers worked to save them but could not reverse the damage — the chaotic mutations had entirely compromised the limbs, and amputation was required. The Clan provided lodging to Sierra and [[Efrén]] during the recovery period.

## Relationships

- **[[Efrén]]**: Lodged with the Khamsin Clan during Sierra's recovery
- **[[Lyra]]**: Parent faction
- **[[Sierra]]**: Treated by Khamsin healers after Tumult mutation; later joined [[Axiom]]
- **[[Tisdhera Clan]]**: Clan the Khamsin aided after Tumult anomaly
