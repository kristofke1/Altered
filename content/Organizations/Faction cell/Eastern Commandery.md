# Eastern Commandery

## Overview
The Eastern Commandery, called the [[Ushungi]], is an outpost built by the [[Bravos]] within the Terra Incognita. It stands on the distant shores of the [[Keleti]] region.

## Current Status
A small contingent of willing exiles currently mans the Eastern Commandery, awaiting the arrival of the main bulk of the exploration forces.

## Purpose
Eager to further explore the world, the [[Bravos]] built this outpost as one of two [[Commanderies]] within the Terra Incognita. The [[Bravos]] hope this remote Commandery, erected with their blood, will serve as a future base for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].
