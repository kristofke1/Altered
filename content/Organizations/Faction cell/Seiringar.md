# Seiringar

## Overview
The Seiringar are a group within the [[Bravos]] Faction composed of veterans who, after years of tribulations, prefer to support the new recruits of the Faction within [[Haven]].

## Purpose
The Seiringar dedicate themselves to helping new recruits of the [[Bravos]] Faction fully realize their potential through training and support.

## Location
The Seiringar operate within [[Haven]], the [[Bravos]] homeport in [[Caer Eidos]].
