# Iju Clan

## Overview

Clan Iju is one of the [[Lyra]] Clans that has managed to live in symbiosis with a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]].

## Symbiosis with Scarab

Clan Iju lives in symbiosis with [[Scarab]], the Beetle Leviathan. The [[Lyra]] negotiated with [[Kaibara]], the Whale Leviathan and protector of [[Asgartha]], to allow Scarab to continue roaming the Peninsula in exchange for ensuring that it does not cause trouble within the [[Oases|Oasis]].
