# Iju Clan

## Overview

The Iju Clan is one of the nine [[Lyra]] [[Saraband|Sarabands]]. The Clan has achieved the rare distinction of living in symbiosis with a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]].

## Symbiosis with Scarab

The Iju Clan lives in symbiosis with [[Scarab]], the Beetle Leviathan. The [[Lyra]] negotiated with [[Kaibara]], the Whale Leviathan and protector of [[Asgartha]], to allow Scarab to continue roaming the Peninsula in exchange for ensuring it does not cause trouble within the [[Oases|Oasis]].

## Relationships

- **[[Lyra]]**: Parent faction
- **[[Scarab]]**: Beetle Leviathan with whom the Clan lives in symbiosis
- **[[Kaibara]]**: Whale Leviathan who permitted Scarab's presence in Asgartha
- **[[Ventauri Clan]]**: Fellow Lyra Clan also living in Leviathan symbiosis
