# Ossonoya Clan

## Overview

The Ossonoya Clan is one of the nine [[Lyra]] [[Saraband|Sarabands]]. [[Itoro]] of the [[Kasirga Clan]] characterized the Ossonoya as a group that prizes secrecy above all else. Their Sahanka is [[Mist]], a tracked vehicle of immense scale that moves across open terrain within a perpetual bank of self-generated fog.

## Known Members

- **[[Nevenka]]**: Graffiti artist born within Mist, later banished by the Clan's Shepherd for her disruptive behavior; became a [[Lyra]] [[Exalts|Exalt]]
- **[[Blotch]]**: [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who became Nevenka's companion and Alter Ego after testing her patience for 64 days

## Relationships

- **[[Blotch]]**: Nevenka's Alter Ego
- **[[Itoro]]**: [[Kasirga Clan]] Inkcaster who noted the Ossonoya's secretive reputation
- **[[Lyra]]**: Parent faction
- **[[Mist]]**: The Clan's Sahanka
- **[[Nevenka]]**: Former member, banished by the Shepherd
