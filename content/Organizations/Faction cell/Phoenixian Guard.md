# Phoenixian Guard

## Overview

The Phoenixian Guard is an elite military organization within the [[Bravos]] Faction. Members have received "the gift of the Phoenix," granting them the ability to control the element of fire. Beyond their martial role, the Guard serves as the secret keepers of [[Rune's Testament]].

## The Gift of the Phoenix

Members of the Phoenixian Guard possess fire-based abilities that distinguish them from other [[Bravos]] warriors. Their blades can take on crimson gleam, leaving red trails like glowing ribbons during combat. The Guard includes specialists such as Blade Dancers who combine precise swordsmanship with dance-like movements.

## Keepers of Rune's Testament

The Phoenixian Guard has preserved [[Rune's Testament]] across generations—secret instructions left by the first Kuningas revealing the true purpose of exploration. They have always known that the Source of [[The Tumult]] was a mirage, and that the real mission was to sail westward and rekindle the [[Phoenix]].

Guard members bear golden tracings on their skin—patterns identical to the archaic script carved throughout the [[City of Scholars]]. These markings identify them as Testament stewards.

## The Phoenix Egg Operation (393 AC)

The Guard orchestrated the theft of the [[Phoenix Egg]] from [[Haven]] and its transport aboard [[The Wayfarer]]. [[Bravos]] loyal to the Testament secretly joined the [[Tisdhera Clan]] to carry the sacred artifact westward toward the Phoenix's nest.

When [[Waru Toowoom]] discovered [[Rune]]'s scroll in the [[City of Scholars]], [[Sitina]]—a Phoenixian Blade Dancer—revealed the Testament's existence. She declared: "The Firebird must be rekindled, reborn. Only then can the world rise from its ashes. So he spoke, at the dawn of a new age. Such is the mission entrusted to us."
