# The Nine Matriarchs

## Overview
The Nine Matriarchs constitute the supreme authority among the [[Lyra]]. They are the leaders of the nine [[Altered/Organizations/Faction cell/Saraband|Sarabands]], groupings of caravans that share the same ancestry and common customs.

## Role
Each Matriarch is an elder who ensures the respect of traditions and the memory of the [[Lyra]] Faction. Each Matriarch represents an exclusive interlocutor for the Nine Sisters, the [[Eidolons]] who serve as their tutelary patrons.

## Leadership Style
The Matriarchs rarely use their preeminence, preferring instead to watch over their procession like a shepherd over his flock.
