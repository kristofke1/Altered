# The Reapers

## Overview

The Reapers are an elite subgroup within the [[Hellequin]] hunters of the [[Muna]] faction. Members bear a distinctive mark identifying them as Reapers.

## Role in Rediscovery Endeavor

During the descent into [[The Crow's Eye]] in 393 AC, several Hellequin hunters bearing the mark of the Reapers accompanied the expedition team, indicating their involvement in the most dangerous exploration operations.

## Relationships

- **[[Hellequin]]**: Parent organization
- **[[Muna]]**: Faction to which Reapers belong
- **[[The Crow's Eye]]**: Location where Reaper-marked hunters descended (393 AC)
