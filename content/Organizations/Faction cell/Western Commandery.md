# Western Commandery

## Overview
The Western Commandery, called the [[Cebir]], is an outpost built by the [[Bravos]] within the Terra Incognita. It has been reluctantly abandoned due to the countless dangers it faced.

## Status
The Western Commandery was abandoned, though the [[Bravos]] hope this remote Commandery, erected with their blood, will serve as a future base for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Purpose
Eager to further explore the world, the [[Bravos]] built this outpost as one of two [[Commanderies]] within the Terra Incognita, intending it to serve as a base for exploration forces.
