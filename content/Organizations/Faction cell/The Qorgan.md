# The Qorgan

## Overview
The Qorgan is an [[Yzmir]] cell specialized in tracking and eliminating the nation's enemies. It was founded by [[Wanjiru]] in the aftermath of [[The Severance War]].

## Founding

### Wanjiru's Decision
After uncovering [[The Isfet]] conspiracy during [[The Severance War]], [[Wanjiru]] remained uneasy despite being celebrated as a hero and a key figure in the reconciliation. He came to believe that [[The Isfet]] had been backed by outside forces.

### Purpose
The Horomancer decided to dedicate his life to uncovering the true masterminds behind the conflict. To protect [[Asgartha]], he founded The Qorgan, an [[Yzmir]] cell specialized in tracking and eliminating the nation's enemies.

## Function
The Qorgan operates as a counter-intelligence organization within the [[Yzmir]] Faction, focused on identifying and neutralizing threats to [[Asgartha]]'s security. While under [[Yzmir]] jurisdiction, the organization maintains resources and operatives embedded across all six Factions.

## Operations

### Ariadne Surveillance Network (393 AC)

[[Yanna]] operates a surveillance system at [[The Screed]] using [[Ariadne]], the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who serves as central knot for expedition threads into the [[City of Scholars]]. A hidden [[Glyph|Rhombus of the Gestalt]] placed under Ariadne's tunic allows monitoring of expedition movements through a secret [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]] frequency whenever [[Ordis]] members are present. Secret laws were passed by carefully selected individuals to ensure maximum [[The Aegis|Aegis]] support for expedition teams, increasing Gestalt coverage for Qorgan surveillance.
