# Ventauri Clan

## Overview

The Ventauri Clan is one of the nine [[Lyra]] [[Saraband|Sarabands]]. The Clan has achieved the rare distinction of living in symbiosis with a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]], residing among the hollow scales of [[Meander]], the Serpent Leviathan.

## Symbiosis with Meander

The Ventauri Clan lives in symbiosis with [[Meander]], the Serpent Leviathan. Clan members dwell among the hollow scales of the creature. The [[Lyra]] negotiated with [[Kaibara]], the Whale Leviathan and protector of [[Asgartha]], to allow Meander to continue roaming the Peninsula in exchange for ensuring it does not cause trouble within the [[Oases|Oasis]].

## Known Members

- **[[Nittaya Vorona]]**: Dancer born into the Ventauri Clan; became concubine of Basilissa [[Annuska Valiont]] (325-335 AC); one of the [[Paramours]] of Seki
- **[[Somayeh Bahman]]**: Skald and memorialist of the Ventauri Clan; elected fourteenth Basilissa of [[Asgartha]] (393 AC)

## Relationships

- **[[Lyra]]**: Parent faction
- **[[Meander]]**: Serpent Leviathan with whom the Clan lives in symbiosis
- **[[Kaibara]]**: Whale Leviathan who permitted Meander's presence in Asgartha
- **[[Iju Clan]]**: Fellow Lyra Clan also living in Leviathan symbiosis
- **[[Nittaya Vorona]]**: Former member who left the Clan to join Basilissa Annuska Valiont
- **[[Somayeh Bahman]]**: Memorialist who became Basilissa of Asgartha
