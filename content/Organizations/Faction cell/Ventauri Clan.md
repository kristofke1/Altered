# Ventauri Clan

## Overview

Clan Ventauri is one of the [[Lyra]] Clans that has managed to live in symbiosis with a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]].

## Symbiosis with Meander

Clan Ventauri lives in symbiosis with [[Meander]], the Serpent Leviathan. The [[Lyra]] negotiated with [[Kaibara]], the Whale Leviathan and protector of [[Asgartha]], to allow Meander to continue roaming the Peninsula in exchange for ensuring that it does not cause trouble within the [[Oases|Oasis]].
