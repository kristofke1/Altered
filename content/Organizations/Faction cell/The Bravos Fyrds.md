# The Bravos Fyrds

## Overview
The Bravos Fyrds are military assemblies connected to [[Bravos]].

## Composition
Fyrds are assemblies of Companies.

## Role
[[Bravos]] have the ability to summon Fyrds that can provide support to the military forces of [[Asgartha]] when needed.

## Frequency
However, this opportunity has materialized only on rare occasions.
