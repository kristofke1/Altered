# Companies

## Overview
Companies are organizational units within the [[Bravos]] Faction. When [[Bravos]] are not solitary, they are organized into Companies of varying sizes.

## Areas of Expertise
[[Bravos]] within Companies can dedicate themselves to various areas of expertise, including:
- Personal protection
- Firefighting
- Artistic endeavors
- Navigation
- Exploration

Many are lured by the call of adventure and migrate towards the horizon in search of challenges, prestige, or freedom. It is through these navigators and argonauts that [[Asgartha]] has managed to reach new shores conducive to exploration, even if for the moment they have only managed to index them while awaiting more serious expeditions.

## Organization
At least one Company is permanently stationed within the [[Commanderies]] spread across the peninsula. In the past, this number could easily rise to three or four. This tradition has been maintained to guard against any eventuality, even in the current peace context.
