# Factions

## Overview
Factions are groups of individuals who gather around a common philosophy and set of objectives. Each Faction has a specific idea of how the world should be stabilized to better overcome [[The Tumult]].

## Membership
Factions welcome anyone who shares their convictions, not just [[Alterers]]. While some Alterers claim no particular affiliation, the majority of them choose to belong to one of the six Factions.

## The Six Factions
1. [[Axiom]]
2. [[Bravos]]
3. [[Lyra]]
4. [[Muna]]
5. [[Ordis]]
6. [[Yzmir]]

## Historical Conflicts
In the past, the Factions have sometimes violently opposed each other, even threatening the survival of [[Altered/Locations/Nations/Asgartha|Asgartha]], which led to the establishment of [[The Covenant]].

## Current Status
Since the promulgation of the [[The Covenant]], Asgartha has entered an era of peace and prosperity. However, the Factions continue to harbor different views on what needs to be accomplished in the medium and long term.
