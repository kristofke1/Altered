# Artificers' Guild

## Overview

The Artificers' Guild is one of the founding guilds of the [[Axiom]] Faction, specializing in engineering and invention. The Guild is credited with the creation of [[Constructs]], mechanical artifacts that allow [[Alteration]] without requiring an [[Alterers|Alterer]].

## Notable Members

- **[[Edire Niski]]**: Engineer who invented [[Constructs]] and helped unify the technical Guilds under the [[Axiom]] banner

## Historical Significance

### Constructs Invention

The Artificers' Guild, through [[Edire Niski]]'s work, developed [[Constructs]]—modules that allow [[Operators]] to control [[Alteration]] using the [[Khyalo Alphabet]]. This innovation became the trademark of the [[Axiom]] Faction.

### Capital Rebuilding (305 AC)

Following the [[305 AC Tsunami]], [[Edire Niski]] of the Artificers' Guild partnered with [[Vaike Swiftwater]] to rebuild [[Arkaster]]. Edire invited Vaike to the Guilds' headquarters to lead the transition of infrastructure—trams, lighting, and more—to [[Kelon|kelonic]] energy.

### Axiom Unification

The partnership between the Artificers' Guild's [[Constructs]] technology and [[Vaike Swiftwater]]'s kelonic energy enabled the technical Guilds to unify under the [[Axiom]] banner, establishing it as a full-fledged Faction.
