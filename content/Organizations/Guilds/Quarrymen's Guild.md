# Quarrymen's Guild

## Overview

The Quarrymen's Guild is an [[Axiom]]-affiliated guild involved in [[Aerolith]] mining and trade operations in [[Hadera]]. The guild's shareholders hold significant economic and political influence in [[Caer Esvander]].

## Operations

The guild operates mining facilities including the [[Enosha Mines]] and maintains interests in aerial transit routes and airship fleet construction. Members include shipowners who transport Aerolith cargo across [[Asgartha]].

## 384 AC Enosha Strikes

During the [[384 AC Enosha Strikes]], the Quarrymen's Guild shareholders controlled the [[Enosha Mines]] management. When miners demanded an inquiry into safety system cost-cutting following a disaster that killed approximately twenty workers, management refused and blamed human error. [[Meredith]], a guild-affiliated shipowner, was elected board chair during the crisis and led opposition to striker demands. The guild hired private security forces and later mercenaries to break the strike, leading to violent confrontations before [[The Aegis]] intervened.

[[Sunisa]] witnessed the conflict as a child and recalled the strikes as a confrontation "between the Quarrymen's Guild and the miners."
