# Wood Guild

## Overview
The Wood Guild is a professional organization for woodworkers and artisans in [[Asgartha]]. Members specialize in various aspects of woodworking, including luxury marquetry and fine carpentry.

## Membership and Specializations
[[Tehea]], mother of [[Gulrang Oduro]] and [[Kojo Oduro]], is a member of the Wood Guild. She specializes in luxury marquetry work, particularly with ebony. Her expertise includes using [[Alteration]] in her craft to enhance the quality and precision of her woodwork.

## Labor and Production
Guild members work in factory settings while maintaining their specialized skills. Tehea continued her factory work even while pregnant with Kojo, demonstrating the demanding nature of artisan labor in Asgartha.

Members of the Wood Guild show their children the techniques of their craft. Tehea demonstrated woodworking methods to Gulrang, passing down knowledge of the trade even though her daughter ultimately chose a military career instead.

## Role in Asgarthan Society
The Wood Guild represents one of the many professional guilds that organize skilled labor in Asgartha's economy. These guilds provide structure for craftspeople and maintain standards for specialized trades.
