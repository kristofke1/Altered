# Lapidaries' Guild

## Overview

The Lapidaries' Guild is an [[Axiom]] guild specializing in cutting and faceting minerals, prisms, and [[Kelon]] crystals. The guild operates from [[The Foundry]] in [[Arkaster]].

## Work

Guild members cut prisms for [[Ordis]] Espars and process [[Kelon]] crystals. Mist-breaker ships arriving at [[Porto Novo]] bring precious minerals for the guild to cut and facet. Members also work as gemsmiths, training apprentices in lapidary techniques and jewelry enhancement through [[Alteration]].

## Political Influence

The guild holds significant political weight within the [[Axiom]] Assembly. In 367 AC, the guild commanded approximately thirteen percent of Assembly support, enough to sway general opinion on major votes. [[Juraj]] of [[The Sanctum]] approached Guildmaster [[Basem Falani]] to secure the guild's endorsement for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], recognizing that the Lapidaries and their allies had not yet taken a stance on the project.
