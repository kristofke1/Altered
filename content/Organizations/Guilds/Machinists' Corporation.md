# Machinists' Corporation

## Overview

The Machinists' Corporation is one of the Guilds within the [[Axiom]] Faction, specializing in mechanical engineering. The Corporation participates in the Guild Assembly where masters deliberate on economic, political, and social issues.

## Known Members

- **[[Hao Fen]]**: Supervisor of the Machinists' Corporation (367 AC)

## Guild Assembly Participation

In 367 AC, [[Hao Fen]] attended a contentious Guild Assembly session where supporters and opponents of [[Basileus]] [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] clashed over the proposed [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. During the chaotic proceedings, Hao Fen chose to read a thick report unrelated to the ongoing debates rather than participate in the unproductive arguments.
