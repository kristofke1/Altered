# Prospector Guild

The Prospector Guild is one of the many guilds within the [[Axiom]] Faction, specializing in the search and extraction of [[Kelon]] deposits.

## Purpose

The Prospector Guild continually searches for [[Kelon]] deposits to supply the [[Axiom]]'s energy needs. This work has become increasingly challenging as deposits become scarcer in [[Asgartha]].

## Suspira Concerns

The Prospector Guild knows that the [[Kelon]] mines on [[Suspira]] are running dry. While current stocks will sustain Asgarthan society for a few years, a solution must be found to maintain the pace in the long term. Despite general concern, some miners dream of an altered island where vegetation can finally flourish when Suspira is fully stabilized.

## Importance to Axiom

The Prospector Guild plays a crucial role in the [[Axiom]]'s strategy of Kelon democratization. No one has thrown themselves into the exploitation and use of this energy source as fervently as the Axiom, and the Prospector Guild is at the forefront of securing new sources.

## Context

The Faction's engineers are forsaking alternative energies like coal in favor of [[Kelon]] because none can compete with its:
- Energy yield
- Variety of forms (gaseous, liquid, or solid)
- Storage capacity (canisters, tanks, crates, or barrels)

For the [[Axiom]], the flourishing of the Faction goes hand in hand with the widespread adoption of [[Kelon]], making the Prospector Guild's work essential to their objectives.

## Structure

Like other [[Axiom]] guilds, the Prospector Guild likely follows the hierarchical structure of:
- A master at the head
- Numerous journeymen
- Apprentices learning the trade

Guild masters sit in the assembly where current issues are shared and legislated.
