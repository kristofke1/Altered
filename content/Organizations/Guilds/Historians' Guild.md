# Historians' Guild

The Historians' Guild is an academic organization dedicated to historical research and archaeological investigations. The Guild participates in expeditions to excavate and study pre-Confluence sites discovered during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Purpose and Activities

The Historians' Guild conducts scholarly research into [[Asgartha]]'s historical past, particularly focusing on pre-Confluence civilizations and settlements. Guild scholars possess expertise in:
- Archaeological excavation techniques
- Historical analysis and interpretation
- Pre-Confluence urban development
- Ancient Asgarthan culture and practices

## 392 AC - Calida Expedition

### November 392 AC - Strand Region Deployment

In November 392 AC, scholars from the Historians' Guild participated in an expedition to [[Calida]] in the [[Strand Region]] of [[Caer Oorun]]. The expedition was led jointly by Guild scholars and prospectors from the [[Axiom]] faction.

Approximately forty explorers participated in the mission, with the Guild scholars providing historical expertise and research methodology. The expedition was established based on information [[Waru Toowoom]] gathered at [[The Sanctum]] regarding the existence of a hot spring station called [[Calida]].

### Mission Objectives

The Historians' Guild scholars worked toward multiple objectives at [[Calida]]:
- Unearthing potential ruins of the ancient hot spring station
- Investigating pre-Confluence settlement patterns
- Researching daily life and ritual practices in ancient baths
- Setting up an advanced base for exploring coastal regions of the Cierna
- Documenting architectural and cultural findings

### Research Questions

Guild scholars engaged with specific historical questions during the [[Calida]] expedition. [[Leocardius Sree]] and [[Waru Toowoom]] had lengthy discussions about daily life in the baths, debating whether bathing was a daily ritual or reserved for liturgical occasions.

## Collaboration with Other Organizations

The Historians' Guild works in collaboration with other organizations during [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] expeditions:

**[[The Sanctum]]**: Provides historical references and archival materials for Guild research

**[[Axiom]]**: Provides prospectors and technical expertise for joint expeditions

This collaborative approach combines historical scholarship with practical exploration capabilities, enabling more effective investigation of archaeological sites.

## Strategic Role

The Historians' Guild serves a crucial role in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] by:
- Providing scholarly context for discovered sites
- Conducting systematic archaeological research
- Preserving and documenting pre-Confluence heritage
- Supporting understanding of ancient Asgarthan civilization
- Contributing to knowledge base for territorial reclamation
