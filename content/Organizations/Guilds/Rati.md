# Rati

## Overview

The Rati are surgeons affiliated with the [[Yzmir]] faction, specializing in medical procedures involving psychic and magical treatments.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Memory Graft Development

Working alongside healers from [[The Cloister]], the Rati surgeons developed a treatment for [[Oblivion]]—an affliction causing progressive memory loss discovered among explorers of the [[City of Scholars]]. The treatment involves grafting artificial memories—psychic fragments donated by loved ones—onto patients' minds. These [[Grafts|memory grafts]] serve as patches for broken minds, allowing afflicted individuals to function despite inconsistencies in their reconstructed recollections.

## Relationships

- **[[Grafts]]**: Technology applied in memory graft treatments
