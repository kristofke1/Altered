# The Parallax

## Overview

The Parallax is one of the Guilds within the [[Axiom]] Faction. The Guild participates in the Guild Assembly where masters deliberate on economic, political, and social issues.

## Known Members

- **[[Celsa]]**: Spokesperson for the Parallax (367 AC)

## Guild Assembly Participation

In 367 AC, [[Celsa]] represented the Parallax at a contentious Guild Assembly session where supporters and opponents of [[Basileus]] [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] clashed over the proposed [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. During the chaotic proceedings, Celsa chose to sleep through the debates rather than engage in the unproductive arguments.
