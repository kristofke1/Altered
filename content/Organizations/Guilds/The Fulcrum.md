# The Fulcrum

## Overview

The Fulcrum is an [[Axiom]] Guild responsible for developing technical equipment used by the Expeditionary Corps. The Guild is led by Guildmaster [[Shiramun]] and serves as the Faction's branch dedicated to the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Purpose

The Fulcrum develops and manufactures technical equipment that explorers use in their missions into the Terra Incognita.

## Membership and Selection

### 381 AC - Competition Process

Entry into the Fulcrum is competitive with only a handful of spots available. In 381 AC, [[Sierra]] participated in a competition for spots in the Guild. Candidates worked on machine design and assembly diagrams in a classroom setting with only two hours to finish. The [[Three Little Pigs]] Eidolons supervised the competition in place of a Journeyman. Despite one of the Eidolons nodding approvingly at Sierra's work, she was not selected to join the Fulcrum.

## Aeronaval Contingent

The Fulcrum includes an aeronaval contingent that operates aerodynes. [[Treyst]] and [[Paju]] were selected to join the Axiom's aeronaval contingent and incorporated into the Fulcrum after completing their training as navigator and pilot respectively.
