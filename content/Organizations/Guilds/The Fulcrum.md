The Fulcrum is an Axiom Guild responsible for developing technical equipment used by the Expeditionary Corps. The Guild is led by Guildmaster Shiramun.

## Purpose and Function

The Fulcrum serves as the Faction's branch dedicated to the Expeditionary Corps. The Guild develops and manufactures technical equipment that explorers use in their missions into the Terra Incognita.

## Membership and Selection

### 381 AC - Competition Process

Entry into the Fulcrum is competitive with only a handful of spots available. In 381 AC, Sierra participated in a competition for spots in the Guild. Candidates worked on machine design and assembly diagrams in a classroom setting with only two hours to finish. The Three Little Pig Eidolons supervised the competition in place of a Journeyman. Despite one of the Eidolons nodding approvingly at Sierra's work, she was not selected to join the Fulcrum.

## Known Members

- **Shiramun**: Guildmaster of the Fulcrum
- **Tessalit**: Foundry Armorer (372 AC)
- **Treyst**: Aeronaval contingent member who was incorporated into the Fulcrum
- **Paju**: Aeronaval contingent member who was incorporated into the Fulcrum alongside Treyst

## Aeronaval Contingent

The Fulcrum includes an aeronaval contingent that operates aerodynes. Treyst and Paju were selected to join the Axiom's aeronaval contingent and incorporated into the Fulcrum after completing their training as navigator and pilot respectively.
