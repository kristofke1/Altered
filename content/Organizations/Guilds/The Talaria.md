# The Talaria

## Overview

The Talaria is a guild of messengers and couriers that handles postal and delivery services throughout [[Asgartha]].

## Urban Messengers

In cities, Talaria messengers can often be seen swiftly traversing the streets and rooftops, adept at parkour, leaping from building to building, utilizing chimneys, walls, beams, and other vantage points to optimize their routes. They know the city intimately: its alleys, corners, market days, festive occasions, and places to avoid at certain times.

## Services

Customers can call them to their homes or drop letters and parcels at various offices scattered throughout the city.

## Warehouse Operations

The Talaria operates shipping warehouses where [[Nisse]] workers handle folding, wrapping, and packing of parcels. [[Axiom]] installed mechanical conveyor belts to optimize the shipping process.

## Remote Deliveries

The Talaria also ensures delivery of mail to the farthest reaches of the Peninsula, where communication networks are rudimentary. In the wild expanses, urban acrobats are replaced by seasoned heralds who are accustomed to navigating any climate or terrain.

- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction that installed mechanical conveyor belts in warehouses
- **[[Nisse]]**: Workers who handle packaging operations in warehouses
- **[[Kojo Oduro]]**: [[Bravos]] Exalt who worked as Talaria courier before becoming Exalt
- **[[Santa Claus]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who visits warehouses for deliveries
