# Ferrymen's Guild

## Overview

The Ferrymen's Guild is an organization in [[Arkaster]] responsible for operating the barges and gondolas that navigate the city's waterways, particularly the [[Nishaanfjord]].

## Headquarters

The Ferrymen's Guild is headquartered in [[Huskr]]. Many of its boatmen live in a network of ships permanently moored side by side, creating a floating residential community within the district.

## Services

The guild's boatmen provide essential transportation services including transporting goods throughout the city, ferrying visitors to the troglodyte cemeteries along the [[Sonowen]] cliffside, and transporting travelers to the distant [[Haven]].

## Relationships

- **[[Arkaster]]**: City where the guild operates
- **[[Haven]]**: Distant destination reached by guild boats
- **[[Huskr]]**: District where the guild is headquartered
- **[[Nishaanfjord]]**: Primary waterway navigated by guild members
- **[[Sonowen]]**: District whose sacred tombs are accessed via guild boats
