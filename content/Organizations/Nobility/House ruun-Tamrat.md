# House ruun-Tamrat

## Overview

House ruun-Tamrat is one of the nine patrician dynasties of the [[Old Nobility]], descended from [[Tamrat]], one of [[Rune]]'s original allies among [[The Runic Knights]].

## Location

House ruun-Tamrat is established in the [[Tesh Kanat 1]] district of [[Arkaster]].

## Notable Members

- **[[Carnelian ruun-Tamrat]]**: [[Basileus]] during the Republic period (355-361 AC)

## Relationships

- **[[Tamrat]]**: Founder, original Runic Knight
- **[[The Runic Knights]]**: Advisory council from which the house descends
- **[[Old Nobility]]**: Aristocratic class to which the house belongs
- **[[Carnelian ruun-Tamrat]]**: Basileus (355-361 AC)
