# House ruun-Heeren

## Overview

House ruun-Heeren is one of the nine patrician dynasties of the [[Old Nobility]], descended from [[Heeren]], one of [[Rune]]'s original allies among [[The Runic Knights]]. [[Heeren]] vanished alongside Rune in 42 AC during [[The Vanishing]], but the house continued through descendants.

## Location

House ruun-Heeren is established in the [[Tesh Kanat 1]] district of [[Arkaster]].

## Notable Members

- **[[Itzamna ruun-Heeren]]**: Member of the house
- **[[Odran ruun-Hereen]]**: Member of the house

## Relationships

- **[[Heeren]]**: Founder, original Runic Knight who vanished with [[Rune]] (42 AC)
- **[[The Runic Knights]]**: Advisory council from which the house descends
- **[[Old Nobility]]**: Aristocratic class to which the house belongs
