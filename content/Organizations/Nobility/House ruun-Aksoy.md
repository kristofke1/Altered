# House ruun-Aksoy

## Overview

House ruun-Aksoy is one of the nine patrician dynasties of the [[Old Nobility]], descended from [[Aksoy]], one of [[Rune]]'s original allies among [[The Runic Knights]]. [[Aksoy]] vanished alongside Rune in 42 AC during [[The Vanishing]], but the house continued through descendants.

## Location

House ruun-Aksoy is established in the [[Tesh Kanat 1]] district of [[Arkaster]].

## Notable Members

- **[[Kaja ruun-Aksoy]]**: [[Basileus]] during the Federation period (208-224 AC); built [[Svarga Station]]
- **[[Ausra ruun-Aksoy]]**: Member of the house

## Relationships

- **[[Aksoy]]**: Founder, original Runic Knight who vanished with [[Rune]] (42 AC)
- **[[The Runic Knights]]**: Advisory council from which the house descends
- **[[Old Nobility]]**: Aristocratic class to which the house belongs
- **[[Kaja ruun-Aksoy]]**: Basileus (208-224 AC)
