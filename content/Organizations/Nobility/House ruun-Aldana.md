# House ruun-Aldana

## Overview

House ruun-Aldana is one of the nine patrician dynasties of the [[Old Nobility]], descended from [[Ainara Aldana]], Baron of the Tribe of the Lower Banks. Aldana was one of the three reconciled barons who joined [[The Runic Knights]] after the [[Phoenix]]'s divine choice of [[Rune]]. [[Ainara Aldana|Aldana]] vanished alongside Rune in 42 AC during [[The Vanishing]], but the house continued through descendants.

## Location

House ruun-Aldana is established in the [[Tesh Kanat]] district of [[Arkaster]].

## Relationships

- **[[Ainara Aldana]]**: Founder, Reconciled Baron and Runic Knight who vanished with [[Rune]] (42 AC)
- **[[The Runic Knights]]**: Advisory council from which the house descends
- **[[Old Nobility]]**: Aristocratic class to which the house belongs
