# Old Nobility

## Overview
The Old Nobility consists of nine patrician dynasties descended from the [[The Runic Knights]]. These ancient families have, over time, become the aristocratic class of [[Asgartha]].

## Composition
The nine dynasties include explicitly mentioned houses:
- [[House ruun-Heshkari]]
- [[House ruun-Caellach]]
- [[House ruun-Tighe]]
- [[House ruun-Aysun]] (lineage of the first Kuningas)

In addition to these explicitly named houses, there are eight families descended from Rune Knights, with the lineage of the first Kuningas continued by his daughter Aysun making a total of nine houses.

## Political Influence
The ruun-Heshkari, ruun-Caellach, and other ruun-Tighe Houses have always been closely associated with power, regardless of the ruling regime or political system of the time.

## Location
The nine major Houses are established in the [[Tesh Kanat 1]] district of [[Arkaster]].

## Relationship with New Exchequer
The Old Nobility are opposed by the [[New Exchequer]], which consists of around forty families. Today, the influence and wealth of the New Exchequer rival that of the Old Nobility.

## Current Status (392 AC)
Both the Old and New Nobility are preparing for the resignation of Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]. He has promised to relinquish power once his ambitious project, the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], is finally launched. As the preparations for his departure have intensified, so too have the political maneuvers and intrigues.

However, there is little true animosity between the noble Houses. In fact, they have affectionately nicknamed their diplomatic conflict the "Fair Game."
