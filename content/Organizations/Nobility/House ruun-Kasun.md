# House ruun-Kasun

## Overview

House ruun-Kasun is one of the nine patrician dynasties of the [[Old Nobility]], descended from [[Balram Kasun]], Baron of the Tribe of the East. Kasun was one of the three reconciled barons who joined [[The Runic Knights]] after the [[Phoenix]]'s divine choice of [[Rune]].

## Location

House ruun-Kasun is established in the [[Tesh Kanat]] district of [[Arkaster]].

## Relationships

- **[[Balram Kasun]]**: Founder, Reconciled Baron and Runic Knight
- **[[The Runic Knights]]**: Advisory council from which the house descends
- **[[Old Nobility]]**: Aristocratic class to which the house belongs
