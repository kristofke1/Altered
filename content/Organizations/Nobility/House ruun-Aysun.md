# House ruun-Aysun

## Overview
House ruun-Aysun is the lineage of the first Kuningas, continued by his daughter [[Aysun]], who is the founder of the [[Yzmir]] Faction. This house forms part of the [[Old Nobility]]'s nine patrician dynasties.

## Founder
The house was founded by [[Aysun]], daughter of [[Rune]], the first Kuningas of [[Asgartha]].

## Political Stance
The ruun-Aysun have long since abandoned any political ambitions, choosing instead to devote themselves entirely to scholarship and knowledge.

## Affiliation
The house is closely associated with the [[Yzmir]] Faction, which was founded by Aysun.

## Location
House ruun-Aysun is established in the [[Tesh Kanat 1]] district of [[Arkaster]] as part of the Old Nobility.

## Relationships

- **[[Aysun]]**: Founder, daughter of [[Rune]], founded [[Yzmir]]
- **[[Rune]]**: First [[Kuningas]], father of Aysun
- **[[Old Nobility]]**: Aristocratic class to which the house belongs
- **[[Yzmir]]**: Faction founded by [[Aysun]] with which the house is affiliated
