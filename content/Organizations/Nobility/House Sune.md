# House Sune

## Overview
House Sune is one of the venerable names among the [[New Exchequer]], the collection of around forty families forming part of [[Asgartha]]'s minor nobility.

## Ancestry
Despite being part of the minor Houses, House Sune has no reason to feel inferior in terms of ancestry and is recognized as a venerable name.

## Location
House Sune has settled on the slopes of [[Kuth Kanat]], opposite to the [[Old Nobility]]'s [[Tesh Kanat]] district.

## Strategic Investment
By investing in the [[Axiom]], House Sune and other minor Houses made a shrewd move. Recently, these families have seen their prestige grow significantly. Today, the influence and wealth of the [[New Exchequer]] rival that of the [[Old Nobility]].
