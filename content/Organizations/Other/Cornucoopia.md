# Cornucoopia

## Overview
Cornucoopia is a grocery and food service organization founded by [[Hermagoras Whittle]] in 294 AC with the aim of ensuring the population's access to essential goods.

## Expansion
Cornucoopia groceries have since become commonplace in every district of [[Arkaster]].

## Products and Services

### Original Focus
Initially focused on transporting essential resources from the Peninsula's Provinces to urban centers:
- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Condiments
- Spices
- Meats

### Diversification
The Cornucoopia stores later diversified to include processed products from [[Athanor|the Athanor]]:
- Soaps
- Clothing
- Some alchemical remedies

### Dining Service
About fifteen years ago, Cornucoopia began offering a supplementary dining service, featuring bakery and grilling sections, aiming to provide high-quality street food.

## Emblem
The emblem of the brand is a stylized cornucopia.

## Store Owners
[[Yawo]], husband of [[Tehea]] and father of [[Gulrang Oduro]] and [[Kojo Oduro]], owns a Cornucoopia-branded convenience store in [[Arkaster]]. His store provides neighborhood grocery service, and he asks his son Kojo to make deliveries for emergencies and to assist elderly customers.

## Expedition Support

Cornucoopia provides food provisions to the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] forces operating in the Terra Incognita. Supplies are delivered via zeppelin air drops to expedition positions, with crates bearing the Cornucoopia logo parachuted to designated drop points.
