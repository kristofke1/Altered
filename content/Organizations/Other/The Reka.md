# The Reka

## Overview

The Reka are a human civilization inhabiting the city of [[Asty]], a megalopolis built upon a floating island and a world-tree at the center of [[The Whiplash]]'s eye. They are the source of the pillar of light that guided the [[Expeditionary Corps]] across [[The Turmoil]]. Upon first contact with the Asgarthan fleet in 393 AC, the Reka addressed the arrivals as "lost sons and daughters of humanity," indicating awareness of a shared human heritage.

## First Contact

When the [[Expeditionary Corps]] emerged into the Whiplash's eye, Reka vessels immediately responded. One craft boarded the [[Exalts]]' skiff while another moved to intercept the [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] [[Halua]]. Warships formed a defensive barrier between the Asgarthan fleet and Asty. After a tense standoff during which Admiral [[Temera Singh]] ordered weapons armed, a Reka representative broadcast a welcome message. The message was spoken in an unfamiliar language and translated aboard [[The Ouroboros]] by a [[Horomancy|Horomancer]] using [[Irises]].

## Military Capabilities

The Reka possess a fleet of flying vessels including craft resembling frigates and destroyers. Their technology appears equal to or potentially beyond Asgarthan capabilities based on Admiral Singh's initial assessment.
