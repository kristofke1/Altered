# The Isfet

## Overview
The Isfet is a sect devoted to chaos that orchestrated a conspiracy during [[The Severance War]]. Previously unknown, they infiltrated both loyalist and insurgent political spheres to destabilize [[Asgartha]].

## Discovery
[[Wanjiru]], a young Horomancer, uncovered a terrible conspiracy deeply rooted in the political spheres of both the loyalists and the insurgents during the civil war. As he probed further, he discovered this previously unknown faction sought to destabilize Asgarthan society to its core.

## Methods
Wherever possible, The Isfet's infiltrated agents stoked the flames of discord and violence, worsening [[The Severance War]]. High-ranking officials within both the loyalist administration and the rebel coalition were identified as agitators.

## Goals
After being interrogated in secret by the [[Ordis]], the conspirators revealed they belonged to The Isfet, a sect devoted to chaos. Their ultimate goal was to let [[The Tumult]] triumph, by fostering disorganization within [[Asgartha]] by any means necessary.

## Exposure and Aftermath

### Resolution
Peace was eventually restored, and the traitors were publicly tried and imprisoned, though not all the details of their actions were fully revealed.

### Outside Forces
Celebrated as a hero and a key figure in the reconciliation, [[Wanjiru]], however, remained uneasy. He came to believe that The Isfet had been backed by outside forces. The Horomancer decided to dedicate his life to uncovering the true masterminds behind the conflict.
