# The Perjurers

## Overview

The Perjurers are ancient conspiracy of body-hopping parasites who possess human hosts across centuries to destabilize [[Asgartha]] and prevent its outward expansion. Members serially inhabit different bodies, abandoning hosts when strategically necessary and assuming new identities. Through [[Irises]], they appear as parasites beneath host skin: translucent venomous slugs, multicolored leeches, invasive crustaceans.

## Nature and Capabilities

### Body-Hopping Parasites

Perjurers are parasitic entities who possess human hosts across centuries. Each member inhabits series of bodies sequentially:
- [[Sylas]] previously inhabited body called "Qaasin" (pre-356 AC), currently inhabits "Sylas"
- [[Saskia Averina]] called "Eugenist" previously inhabited Parisa, EugÃ©nie, Rojin, Hayley (all dead), currently inhabits "Saskia Averina"

When viewed through [[Irises]], parasites are visible moving on and beneath host's opaline skin as translucent venomous slugs, multicolored leeches, and invasive crustaceans.

### Host Selection Preferences

Each Perjurer has preferences for host types:
- [[Sylas]] prefers hosts with natural permeability to [[The Aether]]
- [[Saskia Averina]] delights in selecting hosts with alluring appearances to better ensnare victims

### Self-Description

Perjurers embrace their parasitic nature, self-describing as parasites. [[Saskia Averina]] compared their infiltration strategy to "tick on dog." When [[Sylas]] asked "We're all parasites. Have you forgotten?", Saskia replied asking whether he meant "humanity in general, or us in particular" - rhetorical acknowledgment of their nature.

## Goals and Methods

### Primary Objectives

1. **Destabilize Asgarthan Regime**: After [[Amahle Kalu]]'s betrayal, [[Sylas]] spent nearly century working to undermine [[Asgartha]]'s stability and prevent outward expansion.

2. **Infiltrate Rediscovery Endeavor**: By 392 AC, Perjurers planning to infiltrate Expeditionary Corps to ensure Asgarthan actions don't undermine conspiracy interests.

3. **Set Trap**: [[Sylas]] claimed to be setting trap for [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] that would "snap shut" with all attention turned outward.

### Operational Methods

**Create Puppets**: [[Sylas]] created [[Kraken]] as puppet to allow [[Saskia Averina]] to conduct experiments unhindered. By 392 AC, Kraken had "outlived its purpose."

**Maintain Cover Identities**: Members infiltrate [[Yzmir]] and [[Muna]] factions, maintaining credible identities within Asgarthan society.

**Stage Deaths**: [[Sylas]] staged death of "Qaasin" identity in 356 AC to assume new identity, deceiving even close associates like [[Afanas Korovinovich]] for decades.

**Long-term Isolation**: Decades passed without inter-member communication before 392 AC meeting, suggesting cells operate independently for extended periods.

## Historical Activities

### The Kraken Operations

[[Sylas]] created [[Kraken]] as puppet serving multiple purposes:
- Allowed [[Saskia Averina]] to carry out experiments unhindered
- Contributed to destabilization efforts
- "Incident with the Strix" occurred where Sylas failed to deliver something promised to Saskia, though he later atoned

### Post-Betrayal Mission

After [[Amahle Kalu]]'s betrayal, conspiracy restructured:
- [[Sylas]] tasked with destabilizing Asgarthan regime
- Nearly century of operations (approximately 292-392 AC)
- [[Saskia Averina]] questioned whether recent attempts lacked vigor
- [[Sylas]] assured patience, claiming trap was set

By 392 AC, Perjurers had not communicated in decades. [[Saskia Averina]] visited [[Sylas]]'s workshop for "courtesy call" to coordinate:
- Infiltration of Expeditionary Corps
- Division of targets and responsibilities
- Confirmation that [[Kelsang]] would remain at [[The Monolith]]
- Acknowledgment that "day of reckoning" was approaching

## Wanjiru's Investigation (243-393 AC)

### 150-Year Counter-Intelligence Operation

[[Wanjiru]], [[Oneiros]] founder of [[The Qorgan]], conducted secret investigation into [[The Perjurers]] for approximately 150 years. Worked with "tight circle of trusted individuals to keep things quiet" rather than exposing conspiracy publicly.

By May 393 AC, investigation produced comprehensive theory about Perjurer operations:
- Organization active since [[Amahle Kalu|Kalu's Revolt]]
- Orchestrating periodic unrest to stall [[Asgartha]]'s expansion
- Goal: Keep Asgarthans "tethered to our little corner of the world, with no goal beyond survival"
- Infiltrators hidden in nearly every Faction "twisting ideals from within"

### Methodological Signatures

Wanjiru identified "certain identifiable traits, almost like signatures" that Perjurers leave behind. Methods go "beyond mere tactics"—patterns emerge when examining their operations across decades. Like fingerprints, these signatures suggest immortal operatives ([[Eidolons]] or body-hoppers) maintaining consistent approaches.

### Per-Faction Infiltration Evidence

Wanjiru's investigation documented infiltration across all six Factions:
- **[[Yzmir]]:** Secrets stolen, [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]] identified as embedded agent
- **[[Ordis]]:** Information vanishing from records
- **[[Bravos]]:** Failed expeditions with suspicious patterns
- **[[Muna]]:** [[Saskia Averina]] confirmed as operative
- Other Factions: Similar patterns of interference and sabotage

### The Kraken Theory

By 393 AC, [[Wanjiru]] theorized [[Kraken]] was not [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] but [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoning—"insane amount of [[Mana]]" required but "not entirely impossible." Victory seemed "little too easy" for genuine Leviathan.

Purpose: "To stop us from looking beyond our own walls"—another ruse keeping Asgarthans focused on survival rather than expansion into [[The Tumult|Terra Incognita]].


### Wanjiru's Approach

[[Wanjiru]] kept investigation hidden for 150 years with "tight circle of trusted individuals to keep things quiet." Admitted to [[Afanas Korovinovich]] at [[Cais Adarra]] that he "deliberately fueled their disbelief and mockery" regarding CCW claims to prevent premature action that might drive warlock underground: "If hierarchy took your claims seriously, he might've gone to ground, never to resurface."

### Recruitment of Afanas

In May 393 AC, immediately after [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation at [[Cais Adarra]], [[Wanjiru]] approached [[Afanas Korovinovich]] with alliance offer. Revealed [[The Qorgan]] had:
- Developed undetectable [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] materialization for espionage
- Identified [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]] as Perjurer agent
- Deliberately fueled disbelief of CCW's existence to prevent him going to ground

Offered Afanas role as "bloodhound, judge, and executioner" against CCW. [[The Qorgan]] would flush out warlock, Afanas would deliver punishment. Afanas tentatively accepted, weighing opportunity for vengeance against risk of manipulation.
