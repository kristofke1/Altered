# The Perjurers

## Overview

The Perjurers are an ancient conspiracy of body-hopping parasites who possess human hosts across centuries to destabilize [[Asgartha]] and prevent its outward expansion. Members serially inhabit different bodies, abandoning hosts when strategically necessary and assuming new identities. Through [[Irises]], they appear as parasites beneath host skin: translucent venomous slugs, multicolored leeches, and invasive crustaceans.

## Nature and Capabilities

Perjurers are parasitic entities who possess human hosts across centuries. Each member inhabits a series of bodies sequentially. [[Sylas]] previously inhabited a body called "Qaasin" (pre-356 AC) and currently inhabits "Sylas." [[Saskia Averina]], called "Eugenist," previously inhabited Parisa, Eugénie, Rojin, and Hayley (all dead), and currently inhabits "Saskia Averina."

Each Perjurer has preferences for host types. [[Sylas]] prefers hosts with natural permeability to [[The Aether]], while [[Saskia Averina]] delights in selecting hosts with alluring appearances to better ensnare victims.

Perjurers embrace their parasitic nature. [[Saskia Averina]] compared their infiltration strategy to a "tick on dog." When [[Sylas]] asked "We're all parasites. Have you forgotten?", Saskia replied asking whether he meant "humanity in general, or us in particular"—rhetorical acknowledgment of their nature.

## Known Members

### Active Members

**[[Sylas]]** operates as a [[Yzmir]] Battlemage within [[The Kadigir]]. He previously inhabited a body called "Qaasin" (pre-356 AC) before staging that identity's death in 356 AC. He created the [[Kraken]] as a puppet for conspiracy operations and was tasked with destabilizing the Asgarthan regime after [[Amahle Kalu]]'s betrayal. He maintains a workshop with thaumaturgical tools, forbidden relics, and bound tomes, wearing an enamel mask concealing glowing eyes. He planned to join the Expeditionary Corps (392 AC).

**[[Saskia Averina]]**, called "Eugenist," operates as a [[Muna]] Naturalist. She conducted experiments during Kraken operations and planned to join the Expeditionary Corps (392 AC). Her parasitic manifestations include a brightly colored snail that drains life from plants.

**[[Kelsang]]** is expected to "hole up in [[The Monolith]] as usual" during Expeditionary Corps departure, remaining in [[Asgartha]] to continue oversight operations.

A **"prodigal child"** of unknown identity and location was mentioned by Saskia as separate from other members. Additional Perjurers are described as "numerous enough to oversee things here in Asgartha," though their identities remain unknown.

### Former Member

**[[Amahle Kalu]]** betrayed The Perjurers. After her betrayal, [[Sylas]] was tasked with destabilizing the Asgarthan regime for nearly a century.

## Goals and Methods

The Perjurers pursue three primary objectives. First, they work to destabilize the Asgarthan regime—after [[Amahle Kalu]]'s betrayal, [[Sylas]] spent nearly a century working to undermine [[Asgartha]]'s stability and prevent outward expansion. Second, they planned to infiltrate the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] by 392 AC to ensure Asgarthan actions don't undermine conspiracy interests. Third, [[Sylas]] claimed to be setting a trap for the Rediscovery Endeavor that would "snap shut" with all attention turned outward.

Their operational methods include creating puppets ([[Sylas]] created the [[Kraken]] to allow [[Saskia Averina]] to conduct experiments), maintaining cover identities within [[Yzmir]] and [[Muna]] factions, staging deaths to assume new identities (as Sylas did in 356 AC), and operating independently for extended periods without inter-member communication.

## Historical Activities

### The Kraken Operations

[[Sylas]] created the [[Kraken]] as a puppet serving multiple purposes: allowing [[Saskia Averina]] to carry out experiments unhindered, contributing to destabilization efforts, and maintaining fear that kept Asgarthans from venturing outward. An "incident with the Strix" occurred where Sylas failed to deliver something promised to Saskia, though he later atoned. By 392 AC, the Kraken had "outlived its purpose."

### Post-Betrayal Mission (~292-392 AC)

After [[Amahle Kalu]]'s betrayal, the conspiracy restructured. [[Sylas]] was tasked with destabilizing the Asgarthan regime over nearly a century of operations. [[Saskia Averina]] questioned whether recent attempts lacked vigor, but [[Sylas]] assured patience, claiming the trap was set.

### 392 AC Coordination

By 392 AC, Perjurers had not communicated in decades. [[Saskia Averina]] visited [[Sylas]]'s workshop to coordinate infiltration of the Expeditionary Corps, division of targets and responsibilities, and confirmation that [[Kelsang]] would remain at [[The Monolith]]. Saskia concluded the conversation by noting "the day of reckoning is upon us," suggesting the conspiracy was approaching a critical phase.

## Wanjiru's Investigation (243-393 AC)

[[Wanjiru]], [[The Oneiroi|Oneiros]] founder of [[The Qorgan]], conducted a secret investigation into The Perjurers for approximately 150 years, working with a tight circle of trusted individuals rather than exposing the conspiracy publicly.

By May 393 AC, the investigation produced a comprehensive theory: the organization had been active since [[Amahle Kalu|Kalu's Revolt]], orchestrating periodic unrest to stall [[Asgartha]]'s expansion and keep Asgarthans "tethered to our little corner of the world, with no goal beyond survival." Infiltrators were hidden in nearly every Faction "twisting ideals from within."

Wanjiru identified "certain identifiable traits, almost like signatures" that Perjurers leave behind—patterns emerging when examining their operations across decades. Like fingerprints, these signatures suggest immortal operatives maintaining consistent approaches. Evidence documented infiltration across all six Factions, with secrets stolen from [[Yzmir]], information vanishing from [[Ordis]] records, failed [[Bravos]] expeditions with suspicious patterns, and [[Saskia Averina]] confirmed as a [[Muna]] operative.

Wanjiru theorized the [[Kraken]] was not a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] but an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoning requiring an "insane amount of [[Mana]]" but "not entirely impossible." The victory seemed "a little too easy" for a genuine Leviathan, serving instead as "another ruse keeping Asgarthans focused on survival rather than expansion."

In May 393 AC, immediately after [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation at [[Cais Adarra]], [[Wanjiru]] approached [[Afanas Korovinovich]] with an alliance offer, revealing that [[The Qorgan]] had identified the [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]] as a Perjurer agent and offering Afanas a role as "bloodhound, judge, and executioner" against the warlock.
