# Jusaka Blaze

## Overview
The Jusaka Blaze is an [[Altrun]] team based in [[Arkaster]]. The team is known for reaching the finals of [[The Omnium]], the annual Altrun championship, and eventually winning the cup. Their mascot and emblem is [[Booda]], the flame-furred cheetah Chimera belonging to team member [[Kojo Oduro]].

## Headquarters
The Jusaka Blaze uses the Speedster's Den as their headquarters. This tavern's terrace serves as a gathering place for the team to meet, plan routes, and socialize.

## Team Members
The core team consists of:
- **[[Carmela]]** - Team captain, known for giving advice and eventually leading the team to Omnium victory
- **[[Kojo Oduro]]** - Former courier who joined the team and became a skilled runner before leaving for the Expeditionary Corps
- **[[Uju]]** - Welcoming member who insisted on giving [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] a chance when others doubted him
- **[[Cathal]]** - Initially critical member who eventually became supportive
- **[[Gault]]** - Provided constant emotional support and encouragement, romantic partner of [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]]

## Team Culture
The Jusaka Blaze functions as a second family for its members. The team demonstrated strong mutual support:
- [[Carmela]] stayed late evenings to give [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] advice during his training
- [[Uju]] gave [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] new shoes to help him improve
- [[Cathal]] covered for [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] at work to allocate more training time
- [[Gault]] provided constant comfort and congratulations

Members celebrated victories together and supported each other through defeats and frustrations.

## Competitive History
The Jusaka Blaze reached the finals of [[The Omnium]], the annual Altrun championship. While they failed to win the cup initially during [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]]'s tenure with the team, they eventually achieved victory.

When [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] attended the Omnium final as a spectator (having joined the [[The Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]]), he witnessed [[Carmela]] lifting the Altrun cup to the crowd's celebration.

## Mascot
[[Booda]], [[Kojo Oduro]]'s Chimera companion, served as the mascot and emblem of the Jusaka Blaze. The powerful feline with fur made of cold flames became a distinctive symbol for the club. As an adult, [[Booda]] could even follow the team during their frantic runs.

## Reputation
The team's reputation grew over the years through competitions - both official and unofficial. [[Carmela]] discussed potential sponsors and the possibility of becoming a top-tier team as their success increased.

## Support for Kojo's Departure
When [[Kojo Oduro]] decided to join the [[The Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]], the Jusaka Blaze fully supported his decision:
- [[Gault]] went to great lengths ensuring [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]]'s application was accepted
- The team defended him when [[Gulrang Oduro|Gulrang]] tried to dissuade him
- They helped sway the opinion of the [[The Aegis|Aegis]] captain
- [[Carmela]] joked "good riddance" but revealed she always knew he had potential to be much more than just a runner

The entire team attended [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]]'s ceremony to receive the [[Carnelian Crown]], cheering from the stands. [[Carmela]] raised her fist proudly, [[Uju]] whistled at the top of her lungs, [[Cathal]] teased with a sly grin, and [[Gault]] placed a hand on his heart gazing at [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] with warmth and encouragement.
