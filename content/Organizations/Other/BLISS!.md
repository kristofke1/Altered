# BLISS!

## Overview

BLISS! is a renowned musical band associated with the [[Lyra]] faction, known for performing concerts at major [[Expeditionary Corps]] events.

## Name Philosophy

According to [[Fen]], the group's name derives from the philosophy that it takes courage to be wise because wisdom means admitting you don't know. Ignorance itself is not a virtue, but admitting you know nothing leads to true bliss—and also to constant torment, because of awareness of one's own condition.

## Members

- **[[Fen]]**: Lead vocalist, draws on [[Ignescence]] during performances
- **[[Crowbar]]**: Guitarist, [[Chimerae|Chimera]] with scarecrow form and pumpkin head
- **[[Orbec]]**: Keytar and kelonic piano
- **[[Tamati]]**: Drums, toms, and cymbals
- **[[Boona]]**: Bass

## Performance Style

BLISS! performs high-energy concerts featuring electric guitar, kelonic keyboards, and powerful vocals. Their music includes emotionally charged songs about fate and circumstance.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - The Screed Concert

BLISS! performed at [[The Stage]] at [[The Screed]] outpost, with the concert broadcast through [[The Gestalt]] under [[Nosk]]'s direction. The performance was attended by large crowds from the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. During the concert, [[Fen]] experienced a sudden episode where she forgot her lyrics mid-song, causing visible distress and confusion among the band members.
