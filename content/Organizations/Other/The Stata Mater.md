# The Stata Mater

## Overview
The Stata Mater is an intervention brigade that includes several [[Alterers]], with the important task of controlling fires and extinguishing any that may break out within [[Arkaster|the capital]].

## Organization
Divided into nine divisions, each with its own dedicated station, it covers the entire urban territory.

## Recent Expansion
A tenth division of firefighters has recently been created to support [[Expeditionary Corps|the Expeditionary Corps]].
