# The Arkaster Echo

## Overview
The Arkaster Echo is a tri-weekly newspaper covering [[Asgartha]], now nearly fifty years old (as of approximately 396 AC).

## Distribution
The tri-weekly gazette is primarily distributed in:
- Train stations
- Main squares of the city
- Cornucoopia stores

## Publication Schedule
Published on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays.

## Staff
The Echo employs:
- Nearly two hundred sworn journalists
- Correspondents on each peninsula or island of the Asgarthan Peninsula
- Special correspondents deployed to field locations such as the [[Expeditionary Corps]]

### Reporters

- **[[Emerson Swanson]]**: Covers political affairs including Assembly debates and election campaigns
- **[[Avdel Mezdar]]**: Covers local Arkaster news

### Special Correspondents

- **[[Vera Valesquia]]**: Deployed to [[Expeditionary Corps]] to document the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]; daughter of founder [[Fabia Valesquia]]

## Broadcasting

Political coverage from the Echo is broadcast live via [[ATTROG|Radio Guild]], enabling real-time reporting of Assembly debates and public speeches.

## Founding

Fabia Valesquia founded the Arkaster Echo in 346 AC alongside the democratization of printing.
