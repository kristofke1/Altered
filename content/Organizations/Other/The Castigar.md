# The Castigar

## Overview
The Castigar was an Alteration Faction affiliated with political power and loyal only to [[Ayxas]]. It was founded in 74 AC as part of Ayxas's consolidation of power during [[The Torment]].

## Founding (74 AC)
In 74 AC, [[Ayxas]] secluded himself for several months in his [[Elysium|Elysian]] quarters, where he devised a plan to restructure [[Asgartha]]'s power structures. He first founded The Castigar as an instrument of his authoritarian rule.

## Purpose and Function
The Castigar served to:
- Control Alteration practices within [[Asgartha]]
- Reclaim functions from independent Factions, particularly the [[Yzmir]]
- Enforce loyalty to Ayxas's regime

In 79 AC, the [[Yzmir]] were instructed to submit to The Castigar and hand over all their esoteric research.

## Reign of Terror
Alterers who refused to join The Castigar were hunted, imprisoned, or eliminated. The organization became a tool of oppression during Ayxas's dictatorship.

## Defense of the Regime (86 AC)
In 86 AC, when insurgents stormed the [[Elysium]] with the help of certain royal guards, the conscripted forces of The Castigar opposed the rioters but were eventually overthrown by the coalition of rebel forces.

## Dismantling
The Castigar was ultimately dismantled after Ayxas's overthrow in 86 AC, as part of the restoration of civil liberties and administrative institutions in [[Asgartha]].
