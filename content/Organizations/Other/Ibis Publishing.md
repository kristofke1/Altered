# Ibis Publishing

## Overview

Ibis Publishing is an Asgarthan publishing house. It published [[Jiruu Kiet]]'s *Treatise of the Demiurge, or The Death of Philosophy*.

## Relationships

- **[[Jiruu Kiet]]**: Author whose treatise was published by Ibis Publishing
