# The Conclave

## Overview
The Conclave is one of three organs of the executive branch (the [[Trusted Council]]) of [[Asgartha]]'s government.

## Composition
The Conclave gathers representatives from each [[Factions|Faction]] - the annually elected Archons.

## Role
These spokespersons advise the [[Basileus]] based on the perspective of their sponsoring organization.
