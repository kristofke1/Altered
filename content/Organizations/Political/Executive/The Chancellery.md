# The Chancellery

## Overview
The Chancellery is one of three organs of the executive branch (the [[Trusted Council]]) of [[Asgartha]]'s government.

## Purpose
The Chancellery was created to serve as a counterbalance to the [[Factions]]. This assembly of politicians and scholars, who are not affiliated with any Faction, advises the [[Basileus]] in the interest of the people and society at large.

## Composition
Each member of the Chancellery is assigned to a specific area of expertise, often related to the Archon they represent as a citizen counterpart.
