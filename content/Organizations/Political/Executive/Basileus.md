# Basileus

## Overview
The Basileus is the elected monarch and executive leader of [[Asgartha]], serving as the head of the [[Trusted Council]].

## Creation of the Title
The position of Basileus was created during the Regional Development period (Stochastic Federation era), replacing the earlier title of [[Kuningas]] (High King) or [[Kuningas|Kuningatar]] (High Queen).

## Role and Powers
The Basileus, elected for a usual term of ten years, arbitrates the sessions of the [[Trusted Council]]. In cases of tie votes, the Basileus has the authority to decide the direction they deem most appropriate.

The Basileus is advised by:
- [[The Conclave]] - representatives from each [[Factions|Faction]]
- [[The Chancellery]] - politicians and scholars not affiliated with any Faction

## Security
The [[The Kuninguard|Kuninguard]] serves as the personal guard of the Basileus, loyal to the office rather than the individual.

## Current Basileus
[[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] currently holds the office, having served since 361 AC (31 years as of 392 AC).

## Historical Context
The title evolved through Asgartha's political periods:
- **Foundation Era:** [[Kuningas]] (High King/Queen)
- **Settlement Era:** [[Kuningas|Kuningatar]] delegated power to Factions
- **Federation Era:** Basileus title created with random appointment system
- **Republic Era:** Basileus became elected position with 10-year terms
