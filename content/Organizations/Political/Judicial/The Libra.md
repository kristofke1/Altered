# The Libra

## Overview
The Libra is one of three justice organs within the judicial branch of [[Asgartha]]'s government. It is overseen by [[Ordis]].

## Role
The Libra is tasked with administering justice throughout the Peninsula. It is before the Libra, whether in the Tribunal or other Courts of Justice, that violators of the order are judged.

The Libra serves as prosecutors in trials.
