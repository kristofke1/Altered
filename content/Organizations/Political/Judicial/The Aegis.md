# The Aegis

## Overview
The Aegis is one of three law enforcement organs within the judicial branch of [[Asgartha]]'s government. It is overseen by [[Ordis]].

## Role
The Aegis is the organization responsible for ensuring the security of the citizens of Asgartha. They constitute a law enforcement force.

## Expeditionary Deployment

At [[The Screed]] outpost (393 AC), Aegis contingents are garrisoned in the west wing of [[The Corolla]], where their barracks are located. They regularly emerge to parade through [[Town Square]] or carry out training drills while onlookers gather, awaiting deployment into the depths of the [[City of Scholars]].
