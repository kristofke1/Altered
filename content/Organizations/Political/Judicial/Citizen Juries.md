# Citizen Juries

## Overview
Citizen Juries are one of three justice organs within the judicial branch of [[Asgartha]]'s government.

## Composition
Every significant trial involves a Citizen Jury, randomly selected from the population.

## Role
This panel is responsible for rendering a verdict after hearing arguments from [[Altered/Organizations/Political/Judicial/The Libra]] prosecutors and [[The Misericordia]] defense advocates.
