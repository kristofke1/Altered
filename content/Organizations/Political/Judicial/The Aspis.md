# The Aspis

## Overview
The Aspis is one of three law enforcement organs within the judicial branch of [[Asgartha]]'s government.

## Composition
These citizen militias are composed of volunteers.

## Role
The Aspis can take up arms to defend [[Asgartha]] when the need arises.
