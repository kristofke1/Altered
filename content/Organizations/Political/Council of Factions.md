# Council of Factions

## Overview

The Council of Factions is a deliberative body that coordinates major deployment decisions during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. The Council brings together representatives from all six [[Factions]] to deliberate on strategic matters affecting the [[Expeditionary Corps]].

## Function

The Council serves as the political coordination mechanism for the Rediscovery Endeavor, working alongside the military command structure led by Grand Admiral [[Temera Singh]]. While the Admiral holds operational authority, the Council deliberates on matters requiring inter-faction consensus.

## 393 AC - Jayu Region Deliberations

In January 393 AC, the Council of Factions deliberated on the appropriate response to disturbing reports from the [[Jayu Desert|Jayu Region]]. Strange phenomena including rings of light in the night sky and beams that transformed objects into [[Red Salt]] prompted difficult discussions about force deployment.

The Council hesitated on the nature of forces to deploy given the anomalous conditions. [[Temera Singh]] faced numerous requests regarding deployment while attempting to strike a delicate balance in her decision-making.

## Relationship to Other Bodies

The Council of Factions appears to function as a field-level deliberative body for the Rediscovery Endeavor, distinct from [[The Conclave]] which gathers Faction representatives to advise the [[Basileus]] on domestic governance in [[Arkaster]].

## Relationships

- **[[Temera Singh]]**: Grand Admiral who works with Council on deployment decisions
- **[[Expeditionary Corps]]**: Military force whose deployments the Council deliberates
- **[[Rediscovery Endeavor]]**: Parent expedition the Council coordinates
- **[[Jayu Desert]]**: Region where Council deliberated on force deployment (393 AC)
- **[[The Conclave]]**: Related body advising Basileus on domestic matters
- **[[Factions]]**: Six organizations whose representatives comprise the Council
