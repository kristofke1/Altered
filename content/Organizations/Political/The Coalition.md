# The Coalition

## Overview

The Coalition was a political alliance formed in 242 AC by the Governors of [[Caer Kettria]], [[Caer Esvander]], and [[Caer Alcada]]. The alliance was created for cooperation between [[Asgartha]]n Provinces and represented an attempt by regional powers to limit the authority of the central government in [[Caer Eidos]].

## Formation and Expansion

### 242 AC - Founding
The three Governors of [[Caer Kettria]], [[Caer Esvander]], and [[Caer Alcada]] created the Coalition as an alliance for cooperation between their Provinces.

### 244 AC - Caer Arcolano Joins
[[Caer Arcolano]] joined the Coalition, expanding its membership to four Provinces.

### 248 AC - Rejection by Caer Eidos
The Governors proposed that [[Caer Eidos]] join the Coalition as a full member. However, Basileus [[Marcus Sune]] refused, provoking anger among the regional leaders. The Governors aimed to reduce [[Caer Eidos]] to a mere Province rather than the central seat of power. Sune, by contrast, harbored ambitions of handing power to the people and making [[Asgartha]] a true democracy.

## The Severance War

### 252 AC - War Begins
The tension between the Coalition and the central authority erupted into open conflict. An assassination attempt on [[Marcus Sune]] marked the beginning of [[The Severance War]], a civil war between the Provinces and [[Arkaster]].

### 257 AC - Resolution
The civil war ended after five years of conflict, largely through the intervention of [[Wanjiru]] and the [[Yzmir]], who uncovered [[The Isfet]]'s role in stoking the flames of war.

## Legacy

The Coalition represented a significant challenge to central authority during a period of growing provincial power. Its conflict with [[Caer Eidos]] and subsequent dissolution during [[The Severance War]] shaped the political landscape of [[Asgartha]] for generations. The failed secession attempt demonstrated the dangers of internal division and contributed to the later establishment of more balanced relations between central and regional powers.
