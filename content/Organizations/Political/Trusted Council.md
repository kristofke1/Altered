# Trusted Council

## Overview
The Trusted Council is the executive power of [[Asgartha]]'s government.

## Responsibilities
The executive power, or Trusted Council, is responsible for:
- Enforcing the laws passed by the legislative branch
- Implementing the decisions of the people
- Making necessary choices to achieve set objectives

## Structure
Like the legislative branch, the Trusted Council is divided into three distinct organs:

### Monarch (Basileus)
The [[Basileus]], elected for a usual term of ten years, arbitrates the sessions of the Council. In cases of tie votes, the Basileus has the authority to decide the direction deemed most appropriate.

### [[The Conclave]]
The Conclave gathers representatives from each [[Factions|Faction]] - the annually elected Archons. These spokespersons advise the Basileus based on the perspective of their sponsoring organization.

### [[The Chancellery]]
The Chancellery was created to serve as a counterbalance to the Factions. This assembly of politicians and scholars, who are not affiliated with any Faction, advises the Basileus in the interest of the people and society at large.
