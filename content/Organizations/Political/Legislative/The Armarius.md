# The Armarius

## Overview
The Armarius is one of three organs of the legislative branch of [[Asgartha]]'s government. It is a branch of [[Ordis]].

## Role
Once the votes are cast and counted, the ratified laws are drafted by the Armarius. The Armarius refines the texts and ensures that the laws properly integrate into the legal corpus.
