# Tagmata

## Overview

The Tagmata are the special forces of the Asgarthan navy, serving as elite troops within the [[Ordis]] faction. Armed contingents of the Tagmata stand ready aboard vessels like [[Mandjet]] and [[Mesektet]] to repel [[The Tumult]] and defend against creatures riding its currents.

## Role and Function

The Tagmata serve as the first line of defense against abominations threatening human communities. They operate from [[Ordis]] sky vessels, deploying to intervene where needed. Tagmata squadrons escort motherships during dangerous missions.

## Notable Units

- **[[The Raptors]]**: Elite platoon commanded by [[Zhen]], known for exceptional loyalty and the motto "I am your shield, you are my spear"

## Notable Members

- **[[Bai Shan-shu]]**: Tagmata officer whose squadron escorted [[Mesektet]]; received Purple Star for heroic rescue
- **[[Zhen]]**: Rose through Tagmata ranks to command the Raptors, later becoming Rear Admiral
