# The Raptors

## Overview

The Raptors are an elite [[Tagmata]] platoon within the [[Ordis]] faction, commanded by [[Zhen]]. Under her leadership, the unit became known as the elite of the elite, distinguished by exceptional loyalty and esprit de corps.

## Motto

"I am your shield, you are my spear."

## Command Structure

[[Zhen]] obtained command of the Raptors after a few years of service in the [[Tagmata]]. She pushed the platoon to excellence, creating an exceptional military formation where loyalty to one's brothers and sisters in arms went beyond the usual bounds of Ordis camaraderie.

## Training and Operations

Zhen accompanied the Raptors in all their training, shared their bivouacs, and led assaults alongside them. This hands-on leadership style fostered mutual respect between the officer and her soldiers, contributing to the unit's reputation.

The Raptors' performance earned Zhen promotion to Commodore under Rear Admiral Gaj, then to Rear Admiral herself.

## Equipment and Capabilities

Raptors use origami-style paper wings created through seals entrusted to them by [[Zhen]]. These wings unfurl from their shoulder blades when activated by pressing [[Glyph|Glyphs]] to their chests, allowing aerial reconnaissance and flight. The wings fold back into loose sheets that scatter when the seals are removed. Members also deploy paper heralds for communication purposes.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

The Raptors conducted aerial reconnaissance over [[The Tumult]] ahead of the [[Expeditionary Corps]] fleet launch, with [[Zephyr]] leading the scouting squadron through the clouds. Upon returning to [[Mandjet]], [[Osoyo]] reported stable Tumult winds to [[Zhen]]. As tradition, the entire squadron snapped to attention and pressed their Glyphs to their chests, unfurling paper wings toward the heavens in salute before Zhen issued the fleet-wide command to proceed.
