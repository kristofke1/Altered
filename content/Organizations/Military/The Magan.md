# The Magan

## Overview
The Magan was a military academy dedicated to the arts of war, created by [[Ayxas]] in 74 AC as part of his consolidation of power during [[The Torment]]. It served as the military arm of his authoritarian regime.

## Founding (74 AC)
In 74 AC, [[Ayxas]] secluded himself for several months in his [[Elysium|Elysian]] quarters, where he devised a plan to restructure [[Asgartha]]'s power structures. Alongside [[The Castigar]], he created The Magan, a military academy loyal only to him.

## Purpose and Function
The Magan served to:
- Train military forces loyal to Ayxas
- Reclaim military functions from the [[Bravos]] and other independent groups
- Enforce Ayxas's military control over [[Asgartha]]

Gradually, Ayxas reclaimed social, military, and economic functions from the Factions and reassigned them to offices he had personally established, including The Magan.

## Consolidation of Power (74-79 AC)
For five years, Ayxas passed laws and decrees, patiently restructuring the state apparatus. To many bureaucrats, his reforms made sense: the [[Bravos]] couldn't hold all the nation's military forces.

### 74 AC - Suppression of Warrior Brotherhoods
The Magan enforced Ayxas's decree outlawing independent warrior brotherhoods. In 74 AC, envoys arrived at [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school bearing royal decree outlawing monastery's existence. When [[Walela]], the Grandmaster, refused Kuningas's "request," attack came following dawn. Entire Magan contingent descended upon monastery. Flames consumed dwellings as monks fought fiercely. Resistance slowly crumbled. [[Suddran]] fell speared from all sides. [[Nadra]] was killed by enemy archers. [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] was pierced by arrows and left for dead. Monastery became smoldering ruins.

## Military Control (79 AC)
In 79 AC, when Ayxas presented a motion of no confidence against the Factions, all independent military groups protecting the borders of [[Caer Eidos]] were required to disarm and submit to The Magan.

## Defense of the Regime (86 AC)
In 86 AC, when insurgents stormed the [[Elysium]] with the help of certain royal guards, the conscripted forces of The Magan opposed the rioters but were eventually overthrown by the coalition of rebel forces.

## Dismantling
The Magan was ultimately dismantled after Ayxas's overthrow in 86 AC, as part of the restoration of civil liberties and administrative institutions in [[Asgartha]].
