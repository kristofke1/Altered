# Afena

## Overview

Afena is a [[Bravos]] [[Companies|Company]]. [[Odwar Ouma]] is a hunter in the Company.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October - Volunteering for Halua Hunt

Following the destruction of [[AES Sune]] by [[Halua]], Afena volunteered to join the hunt when [[Sol]] rallied hunters in the mess hall.
