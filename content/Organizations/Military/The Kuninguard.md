# The Kuninguard

## Overview
The Kuninguard is the personal guard of the [[Basileus]].

## Loyalty
The Kuninguard is loyal to the office rather than the individual.

## Role
It is responsible for the security of the leader of the Republic and acts solely on the Basileus's orders.
