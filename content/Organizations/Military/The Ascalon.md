# The Ascalon

## Overview

The Ascalon is a [[Bravos]] Company specializing in Leviathan hunting, commanded by [[Sol]]. The Company bears two notches on their armor, marking two Leviathan kills. The Ascalon serves in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], safeguarding aerial convoys against titanic aberrations in the cloud sea. Sol seeks to recruit [[Basira al-Arshad]] as the Company's [[Alterers|Alterer]], as the Ascalon requires one now that they contend with [[The Tumult]].

## Membership

The Ascalon consists of veterans organized into specialized roles:

**Command**
- **[[Sol]]** - Commander, [[Palladium]] recipient, [[Exalts|Exalt]] bonded with [[Halua]]

**Harpooners/Lancers**
- **[[Veora]]** - Lancer and harpooner; Sol's acrobatic combat partner
- **[[Priya]]** - Lancer and signaler; locates Leviathans through cloud cover; guided [[AES Tempest]] to ambush site

**Wingsuit Jumpers/Fliers**
- **[[Magda]]** - Wingsuit jumper and flier who dives upon targets with the precision of a hawk
- **[[Zeke]]** - Wingsuit jumper
- **[[Chalbo]]** - Wingsuit jumper

**Beaters/Spotters**
- **[[Maanus]]** - Beater and spotter; former woodcutter; uses conch horn for signals and morning reveille

**Scouts**
- **[[Sae]]** - Scout
- **Yejun** - Scout/lancer
- **Tokota** - Scout/lancer
- **Salbaan** - Scout/lancer

There are two types of harpooners in the Company: those who stay on ships and arm turrets, and acrobats like Sol and Veora who use mobility and [[Alteration]] to summon ammunition. Wingsuit jumpers use [[Aerolith]] belts, jet packs, and parachutes to act as both bait and beaters.

## Hunting Tactics

The Ascalon employs [[Aerolith]] barrel charges against Leviathans. Barrels of Aerolith are chained together on ejection rails in groups of six, weighted to plummet toward the earth. Lancers guide them toward the Leviathan in freefall, then drop ballast and fire propulsion javelins to anchor the charges to the beast. A series of detonations triggers the mineral's exothermic properties. The Company drilled this maneuver extensively, destroying dozens of balloons in exercises.

## Equipment

The Company operates from transport barges equipped with kelonic sconces for lighting, firing slits along the fuselage, ballistae at harpooner stations, rear loading ramps for deployment, and ejection rails for Aerolith barrel charges.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October - Flying Dockyard Base

The Ascalon established operations at a [[Bravos]] advance base—a former bastion covered in inactive [[Sap]] and [[Aerolith]]. After two failed clashes with [[Halua]], [[Sol]] proposed a new strategy. Other captains made clear this was the Ascalon's last chance.

### 393 AC, October - Halua Hunt Rally

Following the destruction of [[AES Sune]] by [[Halua]], [[Sol]] rallied hunters in the mess hall to hunt the Leviathan, with the Ascalon leading the effort alongside [[Afena]], [[Requiem of Seti]], and [[Gift of Nuur]].

### 393 AC, October - Halua Trap Operation

The Ascalon led a coordinated trap against [[Halua]] using [[AES Tempest]] as bait to lure the Leviathan into an archipelago hollow. Lancers deployed in freefall, firing kelonic-propulsion spears to detonate an [[Aerolith]] mine field, followed by a kiloton of stockpiled Aerolith. Despite the massive explosion, Halua survived. [[Kauri]] mounted on [[Puff]] intervened to block Sol's subsequent attack.
