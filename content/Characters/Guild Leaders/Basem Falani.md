# Basem Falani

Basem Falani is a former Guildmaster of the Lapidaries who joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Background

Falani was approached about Sun [[Kelon]] to join the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Research at The Screed

By 393 AC, Falani has authority over one of the [[Tech Lab Unit|Tech Lab Units]] at [[The Screed]], conducting research alongside other [[Axiom]] scientists including [[Treyst]].

## Relationships

- **[[Axiom]]**: Affiliated faction
- **[[Rediscovery Endeavor]]**: Expedition Falani joined
- **[[Tech Lab Unit]]**: Mobile laboratory under Falani's authority (393 AC)
- **[[Treyst]]**: Fellow researcher operating another Tech Lab Unit (393 AC)
