# Basem Falani

Basem Falani is the Guildmaster of the [[Lapidaries' Guild]] and a master gemsmith who joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. He operated a workshop in [[Arkaster]] and is an old friend of [[Rama Thongsuk]]. Falani is a member of a cabal connected to the [[Mesektet]] crash and is a target of [[Zhen]]'s investigation.

## Background

Falani ran a gemsmith workshop at [[The Foundry]] in [[Arkaster]]. Following the [[384 AC Enosha Strikes]], he took on [[Isaree Thongsuk]] as an apprentice at the request of his friend [[Rama Thongsuk]], teaching her lapidary work and encouraging her to use [[Alteration]] to enhance her jewelry.

Prior to the Rediscovery Endeavor, Falani was among the high-ranking officials who regularly visited the home of [[Madelyn Moss]] and [[Bai Shan-shu]] in [[Hadera]] to discuss secret projects. Through [[The Musubi]] with [[Zephyr]], [[Zhen]] later witnessed fragmentary memories identifying Falani as one of the participants in a cabal — alongside [[Temera Singh]] and [[de Graaf]] — whose activities were connected to the [[Mesektet]] crash. The information surrounding the crash and the [[Cebir]] was subsequently sealed. Unlike Singh and de Graaf, who departed with the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], Falani initially remained in [[Asgartha]] and was the only cabal member still accessible to Zhen's investigation.

Falani knew [[Zhen]] from childhood, having visited the Moss-Shan-shu estate regularly. He remembered her as a young girl of about ten, and recalled their studies and long conversations on the patio — a time before the founding of the [[Axiom]].

## Endorsement of the Rediscovery Endeavor

In 367 AC, during a contentious session of the Guild Assembly presided over by [[Boro]], Falani was approached by [[Juraj]], Master-Archivist of the [[The Sanctum|Sanctum]]. Juraj presented him with a dull crystal with golden reflections — a fragment of Sun [[Kelon]] found in the wake of a [[The Tumult|Tumult Singularity]]. Although inert and drained of energy, the specimen's quality was unmistakable. Juraj argued that the [[Suspira]] mines would soon be exhausted and that supporting the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] would allow the [[Axiom]] to discover Sun Kelon within the [[The Tumult|Tumult]], securing the faction's future energy supply. With his thirteen percent influence, Falani's endorsement could sway the Assembly's vote in favor of the project. After deliberation, Falani accepted the crystal and began considering mobilizing his supporters.

## Research at The Screed

By 393 AC, Falani has authority over one of the [[Tech Lab Unit|Tech Lab Units]] at [[The Screed]], conducting research alongside other [[Axiom]] scientists including [[Treyst]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Departure from the City of Scholars

[[Zhen]] identified Falani among the expedition fleet as the armada departed the [[City of Scholars]], noting her intent to track him down during the voyage.

### 393 AC, June-September - Aerolith Processing and Zhen's Letter

Falani worked as a lapidary aboard an expedition vessel, polishing and faceting [[Aerolith]] crystals using specialized equipment capable of absorbing residual energy to prevent dangerous exothermic reactions, and received a letter from [[Zhen]] bearing [[Ordis]] and [[Tagmata]] seals.
