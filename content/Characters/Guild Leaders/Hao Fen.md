# Hao Fen

Hao Fen supervises the Machinists' Corporation within the [[Axiom]] Faction.

## Role

As Supervisor of the Machinists' Corporation, Hao Fen oversees mechanical engineering operations and holds a seat at the [[Axiom]] Assembly alongside other Guild Masters.

## Timeline

### 367 AC - Assembly Session at The Foundry

Hao Fen attended a chaotic Assembly session at [[The Foundry]] where Guild Masters clashed over political divisions. While other Masters argued and brawled, Hao Fen read a thick report unrelated to the ongoing debates.
