# Celsa

Celsa is the spokesperson for [[The Parallax]], a Guild within the [[Axiom]] Faction.

## Role

As spokesperson for The Parallax, Celsa represents the Guild's interests at the [[Axiom]] Assembly alongside other Guild Masters.

## Timeline

### 367 AC - Assembly Session at The Foundry

Celsa attended a chaotic Assembly session at [[The Foundry]] where Guild Masters clashed over political divisions. While other Masters argued and brawled, Celsa slept through the clamor.
