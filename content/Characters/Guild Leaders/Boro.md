# Boro

Boro is the President of the [[Axiom]] Assembly and a virtuoso engineer.

## Role

As President of the Assembly, Boro presides over meetings where Guild Masters gather to debate and legislate on economic, political, and social issues affecting the [[Axiom]] Faction.

## Background

Boro possesses sharp intellect and genius for mechanics. He is regarded as an admirable person and maintains good relations with fellow Guild Masters.

## Timeline

### 367 AC - Assembly Session at The Foundry

Boro presided over a chaotic Assembly session at [[The Foundry]] during which Guild Masters clashed over political divisions regarding the Basileus. Despite powerful speakers, Boro struggled to establish order as Masters argued, threw agendas into the air, and drowned out debate. He eventually dropped his microphone in frustration while conferring with secretaries on the dais.
