# Boro

## Overview

Boro is the President of the [[Axiom]] Assembly and an engineer. As President, he presides over sessions at [[The Foundry]] where Guild Masters gather to debate and legislate on economic, political, and social issues affecting the Faction.

## Timeline

### 367 AC - Assembly Session at The Foundry

Boro presided over an Assembly session at [[The Foundry]] during which Guild Masters were deeply divided over the [[Basileus]]. The session devolved into open conflict, with Masters shouting over one another, throwing agendas, and preventing opposing speakers from being heard. Boro attempted repeatedly to call for silence but was unable to restore order, eventually conferring with his secretaries on the dais while the chaos continued.
