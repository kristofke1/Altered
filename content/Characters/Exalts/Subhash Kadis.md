# Subhash Kadis

^b447d5

## Overview

Subhash Kadis, known as Bash, is an [[Axiom]] Exalt from [[Suspira]] who worked for eight years in the island's Kelon quarries before joining the Expeditionary Corps vanguard. He is bonded to [[Marmo]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] appearing as a wounded dog, through [[The Musubi]]. ^35986d

## Early Life

Subhash was born as fourth child in family of six children on [[Suspira]], the island at the eastern end of [[Asgartha]]. His mother worked in construction management and recommended him for mine work when old enough. His father worked as nurse who treated [[Marmo]]'s wounds and maintained plantations amidst black sand, often speaking about possibility of [[Suspira]] becoming paradise through proper terraforming. [[Suspira]] is a desolate land almost entirely covered in black sand and razor-sharp rocks, with harmful fumes of sulfur, methane, and ammonia. Subhash grew up in [[Utval]], a mining colony battered by winds and dust. Family members appearing in Subhash's 393 AC memory projections include [[Kavuri Kadis]], [[Navin Kadis]], and [[Sunder Kadis]].

## Finding Marmo

During a forbidden excursion among dark mounds of [[Suspira]], Bash heard a lament and found a severely wounded puppy missing its two hind legs and lacerated with cuts. Bash brought the animal home, and his father treated its wounds. Bash rigged up a wheelchair for it so it could move around and taught it to follow the sound of his voice and whistles. Bash named the dog [[Marmo]], and over the years they became inseparable. [[Marmo]] began eating [[The Tumult|Tumult]] effluvia, and one day transformed into a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] while protecting Bash from a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity.

## Career in the Mines

Bash was hired at the mine on a recommendation from his mother when old enough to work. He worked for eight years in the Kelon quarries, doing excavation work, salvaging operations, and prospecting. His experience with [[Suspira]]'s harsh conditions and [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities made him valuable to the Expeditionary Corps. He joined the [[Prospector Guild]], which specialized in locating and extracting rare materials.

## Leaving Suspira

After eight years of work in the mines, Bash joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] as part of the vanguard. His skills in mining, salvaging, and dealing with [[The Tumult|Tumult]] conditions made him well-suited for exploration work in the Terra Incognita.

Subhash worked under a demanding Lead Salvager in recycling operations on [[Suspira]] as mines became depleted, sorting and collecting materials for reuse to keep up the pace for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

Subhash operated a motorized crane at the Recyclery on [[Suspira]], loading scrap metal onto a mobile Reprocessor's conveyor belt while [[Marmo]] settled under his seat.

### 393 AC - Comforting Nyala

Subhash summoned [[Little Match Girl]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to comfort the traumatized [[Nyala]], with the red-haired Eidolon striking matches whose sparks transformed into metal butterflies and fireflies to distract her from her ordeal.

### 393 AC - Rocket Puffin Reconnaissance

Subhash used his [[Constructs|Construct]] to summon a puffin [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] equipped with [[Icarus]]'s jetpack to pierce the fog blanketing the frozen lake and locate [[Nyala]]'s [[Lyra]] caravan, with [[Marmo]] bringing the portable monitor to display the topographical readings that also revealed a massive canyon crossing the ice.

### 393 AC - Investigating Cold with Lord Kelvin

Subhash observed [[Lord Kelvin]] experimenting on the nature of cold at [[Storvhit]], with the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] freezing and liberating [[Mushs]] to investigate his theory that the region's cold was artificial and being generated by something rather than occurring naturally.

### 393 AC - Sitar Performance and Meeting Vera Valesquia at Storvhit

Subhash played sitar equipped with [[Alteration]] module working like [[Constructs|Construct]], with each note acting as Khyalo symbol triggering specific memories tied to melodies. Performance of Prakash Ruggeveen's RWV 416 "The Lament" summoned ghostly figures including [[Kavuri Kadis]], [[Navin Kadis]], [[Marmo]], Chatur, and Sunder from memories. [[Vera Valesquia]], journalist from Arkaster Echo, witnessed performance from bunk bed in dorm and introduced herself offering coffee from personal stash. [[Vera Valesquia|Vera]] requested interview for "Faces of Rediscovery" profile series, offering to send copy to Subhash's family. She revealed [[The Ouroboros]] moved upstream to investigate topographic anomaly - circular structure possibly containing human ruins - suggesting potential survivors nearby. [[Vera Valesquia|Vera]] noted deployment imminent for Subhash, connecting recent relic discovery to possibility of other human settlements.

### 393 AC - Petitioning for Sap Research

Subhash persistently lobbied a [[Kelon]] distribution supervisor at [[The Refinery]] for permission to begin [[Sap]] research, arguing for weeks until the supervisor—facing a worsening [[Kelon]] shortage—finally agreed, at which point Subhash summoned [[Little Match Girl]] to deliver a tube of Sap for examination.

### 393 AC - Brassbug Repair

Subhash repaired a damaged [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] he found immobile in his workshop, using a [[Sap]] gun as glue to restore its cracked protective backplate in a technique the Ancients called kintsugi.

### 393 AC - Refinery Conversion Activation

Subhash was present at [[The Refinery]] control room during the successful activation of the Kelon-to-Sap conversion, covered in oil and grime from requisitioned maintenance operations, while [[Marmo]] greeted [[Treyst]] and [[Rossum]].

### 393 AC - Fluid Research at The Screed

Subhash discussed his research on the mysterious fluid discovered beneath the [[City of Scholars]] with [[Sierra]] at his workbenches near [[The Screed]]'s chasm elevators. He explained the fluid had confirmed energy properties and was stored in underground silos and tanks in huge quantities. Despite having one working machine, they could not replicate it, and adapting it to expedition gear proved slower than expected. Bash mentioned rumors the fluid might be organic in origin and suggested recruiting [[Muna]] botanists, specifically [[Arjun]], to help analyze it. He initially theorized the fluid might be Sun Kelon but remained uncertain of its true nature.
