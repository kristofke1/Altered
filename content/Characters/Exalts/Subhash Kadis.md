# Subhash Kadis

^b447d5

## Overview

Subhash Kadis (known as Bash), born circa 352 AC, is an [[Axiom]] Exalt from [[Suspira]] who worked for eight years in the island's Kelon quarries before joining the Expeditionary Corps vanguard. He is bonded to [[Marmo]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] appearing as a wounded dog, through [[The Musubi]]. ^35986d

## Background

Subhash was born as fourth child in a family of six children on [[Suspira]], the island at the eastern end of [[Asgartha]]. [[Suspira]] is a desolate land almost entirely covered in black sand and razor-sharp rocks, with harmful fumes of sulfur, methane, and ammonia. Subhash grew up in [[Utval]], a mining colony battered by winds and dust. His mother worked in construction management and recommended him for mine work when old enough. His father worked as a nurse who treated [[Marmo]]'s wounds and maintained plantations amidst black sand. Family members include [[Kavuri Kadis]], [[Navin Kadis]], [[Sunder Kadis]], and [[Chatur Kadis]].

During a forbidden excursion among the dark mounds of [[Suspira]], Bash heard a lament and found a severely wounded puppy missing its two hind legs and lacerated with cuts. Bash brought the animal home, and his father treated its wounds. Bash rigged up a wheelchair for it so it could move around and taught it to follow the sound of his voice and whistles. Bash named the dog [[Marmo]], and over the years they became inseparable. [[Marmo]] began eating [[The Tumult|Tumult]] effluvia, and one day transformed into a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] while protecting Bash from a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity.

Bash was hired at the mine on a recommendation from his mother. He worked for eight years in the Kelon quarries, doing excavation work, salvaging operations, and prospecting. His experience with [[Suspira]]'s harsh conditions and [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities made him valuable to the Expeditionary Corps. He joined the [[Prospector Guild]], which specialized in locating and extracting rare materials, and subsequently joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] as part of the vanguard.

## Timeline

### 368 AC - Reprocessor Operations
Bash worked on ore reprocessing to filter impurities and extract precious materials needed for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

### 370 AC - Kelon Elemental Prospecting
Subhash used a [[Kelon Elemental]] in [[Suspira]] mines a thousand feet underground, releasing the axolotl-like creature from its cage to locate [[Kelon]] veins while working in sulfur-saturated air with other suited miners.

### 371 AC - Tinker Bell Equipment Repair
Subhash summoned [[Tinker Bell]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to repair his damaged gauntlet, using [[Alteration]] to shrink his toolbox for her to use while enduring her dragon-like temper and tinkling displeasure.

### 373 AC - Salvager Recycling Operations
Subhash worked under a demanding Lead Salvager in recycling operations on [[Suspira]] as mines became depleted, sorting and collecting materials for reuse for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

### 374 AC - Reprocessor Crane Operations
Subhash operated a motorized crane at the Recyclery on [[Suspira]], loading scrap metal onto a mobile Reprocessor's conveyor belt while [[Marmo]] settled under his seat.

### 378 AC - Brassbug Hub Discovery
Subhash explored vast caverns behind [[The Foundry]] to investigate why [[Brassbugs]] were moving slowly, discovering their self-constructed charging stations—enormous metal spheres distributed throughout the hive with workers marching to and from them in single file.

### 378 AC - Brassbug Hive and Queen Discovery
Subhash discovered the [[Brassbugs]] Queen in a large honeycombed sphere, observing her reproductive cycle as she ejected blue pellets that workers gathered while others fed her liquid [[Kelon]].

### 386 AC - Scrambler Antenna Deployment
Subhash climbed a mountain summit with the Jammer to set up Scrambler antennas, connecting the equipment to [[Kelon]] cylinders while [[Marmo]] waited comfortably on a rock, then camped overnight before continuing to three more installation sites.

### 391 AC - FarFetch Testing Incident
[[Sierra]]'s FarFetch [[Automata|Automaton]] test caused an explosive disaster that burned Subhash's beard after a [[Kelon]] cylinder reached saturation while the machine attempted to pull an oversized load.

### 392 AC - Kelon Mining Cave Exploration
Bash explored cave tunnels with [[Marmo]], using a dorsal antenna to part [[The Tumult|Tumult]] mist and summoning a [[Kelon Elemental]] to detect Kelon deposits.

### 392 AC - Armored Jammer Deployment
Subhash deployed shielded [[Tumult Jammer|Jammers]] alongside [[Treyst]] operating an armored tank beyond [[The Rampart]] of [[Asgartha]], covering the advance of [[Ordis]] and [[Bravos]] troops while avoiding frontline combat.

### 392 AC - Gambling on the Ouroboros
Bash gambled in the casino aboard [[The Ouroboros]] where [[Mack]], [[Waru Toowoom]]'s ermine Alter Ego, observed him gathering intelligence.

### 393 AC, January-April - Frozen Lake Rescue
Subhash sprinted across breaking ice on a frozen lake at [[Storhvit]] after a full day of marching when [[Nyala]], a young [[Lyra]], fell through weakened ice, using his [[Constructs|Construct]] to envelop [[Marmo]] in a heat shell before sending him into the water and summoning a Walrus [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] with sonar rig to locate her.

### 393 AC, January-April - Comforting Nyala
Subhash summoned [[Little Match Girl]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to comfort [[Nyala]], with the red-haired Eidolon striking matches whose sparks transformed into metal butterflies and fireflies.

### 393 AC, January-April - Rocket Puffin Reconnaissance
Subhash used his [[Constructs|Construct]] to summon a puffin [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] equipped with [[Icarus]]'s jetpack to pierce the fog blanketing the frozen lake and locate [[Nyala]]'s [[Lyra]] caravan, with [[Marmo]] bringing the portable monitor to display the topographical readings that also revealed a massive canyon crossing the ice.

### 393 AC, January-April - Investigating Cold with Lord Kelvin
Subhash observed [[Lord Kelvin]] experimenting on the nature of cold at [[Storhvit]], with the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] freezing and liberating [[Mushs]] to investigate his theory that the region's cold was artificial and being generated by something rather than occurring naturally.

### 393 AC, January-April - Sitar Performance at Storvhit
Subhash played sitar equipped with [[Alteration]] module working like a [[Constructs|Construct]], with each note acting as Khyalo symbol triggering specific memories tied to melodies. Performance of Prakash Ruggeveen's RWV 416 "The Lament" summoned ghostly figures including [[Kavuri Kadis]], [[Navin Kadis]], [[Marmo]], [[Chatur Kadis]], and [[Sunder Kadis]] from memories. [[Vera Valesquia]], journalist from [[Arkaster Echo]], witnessed the performance and introduced herself, requesting an interview for her "Faces of Rediscovery" profile series.

### 393 AC, January-April - Intelligence from Vera Valesquia
[[Vera Valesquia]] informed Subhash that [[The Ouroboros]] had moved upstream to investigate a topographic anomaly—a circular structure possibly containing human ruins—suggesting potential survivors nearby, and noted deployment was imminent for Subhash.

### 393 AC, June-September - Sap Research Authorization
Subhash lobbied a [[Kelon]] distribution supervisor at [[The Refinery]] for permission to begin [[Sap]] research until the supervisor agreed, at which point Subhash summoned [[Little Match Girl]] to deliver a tube of Sap for examination.

### 393 AC, June-September - Brassbug Dropship Delivery
Subhash waited on a floating rock veined with [[Aerolith]] with a broken drill when a [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] dropship capsule arrived deploying Brassbugs in single file to assist with repairs.

### 393 AC, June-September - Brassbug Nursery Discovery
Subhash discovered a [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] Nursery in a crater where the creatures had developed self-replication capability without their Queen, using a Kelonic generator to power incubators producing embryos.

### 393 AC, June-September - Brassbug Repair
Subhash repaired a damaged [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] using a [[Sap]] gun as glue to restore its cracked protective backplate in a technique the Ancients called kintsugi.

### 393 AC, June-September - Refinery Conversion Activation
Subhash was present at [[The Refinery]] control room during the successful activation of the Kelon-to-Sap conversion alongside [[Treyst]] and [[Rossum]].

### 393 AC, June-September - Fluid Research at The Screed
Subhash discussed his research on the mysterious fluid discovered beneath the [[City of Scholars]] with [[Sierra]] at his workbenches near [[The Screed]]'s chasm elevators. He explained the fluid had confirmed energy properties and was stored in underground silos and tanks in huge quantities. Despite having one working machine, they could not replicate it, and adapting it to expedition gear proved slower than expected. Bash mentioned rumors the fluid might be organic in origin and suggested recruiting [[Muna]] botanists, specifically [[Arjun]], to help analyze it.

## Abilities

Subhash is an experienced miner and prospector specializing in [[Kelon]] extraction, salvaging, and [[The Tumult|Tumult]] navigation. He uses [[Constructs]] to enhance equipment, including enveloping [[Marmo]] in heat shells and equipping [[Eidolons]] with technological augmentations such as jetpacks and sonar rigs. He plays the sitar with an [[Alteration]] module that triggers memory projections through Khyalo symbols. He can summon [[Eidolons]] including [[Tinker Bell]], [[Little Match Girl]], [[Lord Kelvin]], and [[Icarus]]. Through [[The Musubi]], he is bonded with [[Marmo]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who can navigate [[The Tumult|Tumult]] conditions.
