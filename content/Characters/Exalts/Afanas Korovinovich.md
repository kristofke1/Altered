# Afanas Korovinovich

## Overview

Afanas Korovinovich is [[Yzmir]] Exalt and Battle Mage specializing in [[Kinemancy]]. Bonded with [[Senka]], night bird [[Chimerae|Chimera]], through [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Joined [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] seeking vengeance against Chrysanthemum Cloak Warlock who murdered family.

## Early Life and Training

### Childhood at Caer Kettria

[[Korovin]] was [[Bravos]] veteran who faced many Leviathans. Father trained eldest son in martial arts with rigorous pace including early rises, endurance runs, and demanding katas. When [[Bravos]] announced expedition beyond [[Asgartha]]'s walls, [[Korovin]] volunteered to emigrate with family to [[Cebir]], fortress barely built in lands subject to [[The Tumult]].

### 305 AC - Kraken Attack and Family Death

Family embarked from [[Arkaster]] toward [[Caer Oorun]]. During night voyage, violent blow struck frigate as [[Kraken]] attacked. [[Korovin]] pierced Kraken's arm with harpoon but tentacle surged and struck him down. Ship split in two, splinter struck Afanas's eye. While struggling in icy waters, Afanas saw mage floating with [[Kraken]] standing behind without attacking—figure wore alabaster mask and cloak covered with golden chrysanthemums. Afanas pulled metal coffin which split open, releasing sinuous shadow that sank talons into arms and lifted him to soar. Entire family perished except Afanas.

### Rescue by Qaasin

[[Sylas|Qaasin]] ([[Yzmir]] mage) routed [[Kraken]] and retrieved Afanas's unconscious body. Night bird with plumage of shadows and mask of lunar pallor remained at Afanas's bedside—creature Afanas had freed from steel prison. Both Afanas and bird had lost eye where mask split. Afanas named bird "[[Senka]]" and cared for it. [[Qaasin]] taught meditations and mantras during convalescence. [[Qaasin]] brought Afanas (age 10) to [[The Kadigir]] for initiation. Through perseverance and determination, Afanas caught up and exceeded expectations, specializing in [[Kinemancy]] as combat mage.

## Quest for Chrysanthemum Cloak Warlock

Afanas investigated warlock who caused family's death, gathering clues over years. When [[Bravos]] expedition failed 23 years later (328 AC), began suspecting sorcerer established lair within abandoned [[Cebir]]. Studied statements of lone survivor, delving into experiments conducted there. Pursued vengeance through adulthood into old age. Could have sought Magister title of [[Kinemancy]] Sphere but cared little for honors or politics.

### Musubi Bond with Senka

When [[Muna]] recreated [[The Musubi]] ceremony, Afanas volunteered to bond with [[Senka]] to traverse [[The Tumult]] searching for warlock. Through bond, looked through [[Senka]]'s eyes as bird flew over altered lands. Visited [[Cebir]] through bird and made sense of experiments—mage engaged in esoteric and unholy research flouting Faction restrictions. Became certain enemy had cryptic sinister objective against Asgarthan civilization interests.

### Warlock Confrontation via Senka

During [[Senka]]'s exploration, bird came face-to-face with warlock. Masked figure turned toward [[Senka]], spreading arms in greeting. Afanas had proof warlock existed. Warlock addressed Afanas directly through [[Senka]], voice booming in mind, urging him to join in [[The Tumult]].

[[Senka]] wrapped night feathers around Afanas, leaping through vortex traveling thousand cable lengths to estuary where they found [[Sylas|Qaasin]]'s cold lifeless body.

### 366 AC - Training Sewati
Afanas trained [[Sewati]] in Sign choreography, observing student summon water idea from Empyrean, create whirlpool, then solidify into glass spiral.

### 386 AC - Nimue Summoning
Afanas summoned [[Lady of the Lake|Nimue]] holding [[Excalibur]] to seal trembling Megalith by plunging sword into Aether mass.

### 386 AC - Flamel Initiation Ceremony

### 386 AC - Matera's Aether Shard
[[Matera]] struck Megalith with hammer causing fragment to fall, but Megalith trembled furiously requiring Afanas to summon guardian.

### 389 AC - Hecate Conjuring Seal
Afanas and [[Ilinka]] maintained [[Hecate]]'s Strophalos Conjuring Seal, plunging hand through crack to pull ideas from Empyrean while arm was ravaged.

### 392 AC - Kraken Battle
Afanas commanded from promontory during [[Kraken|Kraken]] battle at [[Caer Oorun]], coordinating with [[Sigismar Synd]].

### 392 AC - Informing Fasano
Afanas found [[Fasano]] carrying books in occult library at [[The Kadigir]]; without speaking, young Disciple understood elder sister wouldn't return.

### 392 AC - Tooth Fairy Treatment
Afanas placed tooth on summoning circle; [[Tooth Fairy]] planted barb in forearm, venom purging Remanence's parasitic ideas while poisoning flesh.

### 392 AC - Standing Stone Circle
Afanas created circle of standing stones anchored by [[Mana]] from [[Senka]]'s Tumult journeys, with amethyst glow and organic stone texture; [[Senka]] drank blood from his arm as offering then hunted ideas above the site.

### 392 AC - Bureaucratic Obstruction
[[Waru Toowoom]] confiscated esoteric works requested by Afanas and [[Lindiwe]].

### 392 AC - Basira Encounter at Cebir
When [[Expeditionary Corps]] reclaimed [[Cebir]], Afanas approached [[Basira al-Arshad]] to share information about the [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]]; summoned chrysanthemum scent which triggered [[Kaizaimon]] to attack, confirming warlock's connection to the oni's torture.

### 393 AC, February - Belisenki Battle at Storvhit
Afanas led [[Yzmir]] expedition at [[Storvhit]] with objective of pulling device from [[The Aether]] to detect ideas swirling in region (Tumult Observatory), with [[Sunisa]] serving as aide-de-camp. When [[Belisenki]] swarm attacked shared camp, Afanas performed complex katas while spectral arms appeared tracing patterns, accumulating [[Mana]] pressure. Rose into air with arms spread, eyes pulsing, violet flames dancing as skin took ghostly translucent appearance. Maelstrom unleashed shockwave hurling expedition members into air, making wind weapon that scattered [[Belisenki]]. Display created collateral damage catching [[Sunisa]] and rescued scout, flinging them through air with scout swept into darkness.

### 393 AC, February - Belisenki Tracking Techniques
Trained Irises to track [[Belisenki]] furtive movements and see through camouflage. Observed [[Akesha Adhikari]] manifesting [[Mana Moths]] causing [[Belisenki]] to avoid contact, then deconstructed technique for reproduction.

### 393 AC, February - Storm Manifestation
Felt parasitic ideas (scaly skin twisting) becoming channel for entity's rage, merging with storm. [[Aroro Niski]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] appeared unsummoned, stringing Gestures and covering in Sigils to contain attacks until storm ebbed.

### 393 AC, February - Combined Sphere Attack
Combined [[Phonomancy]] and [[Kinemancy]] to detect [[Belisenki]] by isolating wind sounds, then used [[Eidomancy]] to manifest ghostly limbs. Tamed storm using [[Mana Moths]] at bottlenecks but hurricane escaped control, fed by chain reaction.

### 393 AC, February - Ice Magic Analysis
At frozen clearing, used [[Kinemancy]] and [[Horomancy]] to analyze spell mechanism. Successfully reproduced ice phenomenon by infusing green branch, causing ice to climb wood with transparent rime.

### 393 AC, February - Icebound Pass Incident
Explored Icebound Pass with giant ice walls imprisoning past life. Spotted semi-transparent Glyph in ice wall, used [[Gephyromancy]] to absorb ideas, but triggered earthquake causing walls to split.

### 393 AC, May - Liberation of Kuraokami at The Nilam
During coordinated [[Exalts|Exalt]] assault on [[The Nilam]], Afanas landed beside [[Sigismar Synd]] showing signs of exhaustion with residual electricity crackling across skin and purple blotches from [[Remanence]] effects. Admitted he could no longer cast but offered to transfer remaining power to [[Sigismar Synd|Sig]], stating: "Save your strength if you still can... I think I've pushed mine too far." Transferred untainted [[The Aether|Aether]] from Empyrean through clasped hands, warning: "Don't waste it, and don't expect this to become habit."

### 393 AC, May - Conversation with Kuraokami and Wanjiru
Immediately after [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation, Afanas summoned dragon as [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to learn truth—dragon appeared as alabaster serpent coiling around him with fury. [[Kuraokami]] revealed [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]] imprisoned him using "unholy artifice" at dead world-tree, siphoned essence to forge 305 AC tsunami that devastated [[Arkaster]], and explained triple-walled prison (dead world-tree matter, cold wasteland, [[Belisenki]] guardians). After dragon departed, [[Wanjiru]] (materialized [[The Oneiroi|Oneiros]] with vessel [[Suha]]) approached offering alliance. Revealed [[The Qorgan]] investigated [[The Perjurers]] conspiracy for 150 years, organization working since [[Amahle Kalu|Kalu's Revolt]] to stall [[Asgartha]]'s expansion through infiltrators. Offered Afanas role as "bloodhound, judge, and executioner" against CCW, admitting deliberately fueled disbelief of CCW's existence to prevent warlock going to ground.

### 393 AC - Qorgan Informant at Axiom Warehouse
Afanas followed magical trail to building near [[The Refinery]] where [[Chinam]], [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] Initiate, revealed an [[Axiom]] stockpile of [[Sap]]-filled artifacts.

### 393 AC - Sap Infusion Ritual with Fasano
Afanas prepared [[Sap]]-enriched tea using techniques learned from [[Sylas|Qaasin]], training [[Fasano]] in the ritual before they traveled to the [[City of Scholars]].

### 393 AC - Nilam Spires Laboratory Visit
Afanas visited [[Nilam Spires]] dimensional pocket laboratory with [[Fasano]], where researchers studied a maggot specimen from frozen [[The Nilam|world-tree]] bark.

### 393 AC - Scholars' Vault Investigation
Afanas investigated the [[Scholars' Vault]] through [[Senka]]'s mind, discovering [[Axiom]]'s [[Sap]] veinstones hidden among fossilized memory monoliths.

## Abilities

### Alteration Spheres
- **[[Kinemancy]]**: Primary specialization for movement analysis and spatial manipulation
- **[[Phonomancy]]**: Sound isolation and battlefield detection
- **[[Eidomancy]]**: Ghostly limb manifestation for multi-gesture casting
- **[[Horomancy]]**: Limited knowledge for magical force analysis
- **[[Gephyromancy]]**: Glyph idea absorption

### Combat Capabilities
Enhanced vision (Irises) trained to track stealthy opponents through camouflage and analyze techniques for reproduction. Combines multiple Spheres simultaneously for devastating attacks. Risks losing control when channeling excessive [[Mana]]. Capable of reproducing observed phenomena after analytical study. Creates prism-like shields. Teleports through space using [[Senka]]'s feathers.
