# Blotch

## Overview
Blotch, whose full name is Kutrabalamashablotchara (meaning "sun lance fragmented through the sublime iridescence of celestial waves"), is a paint dragon [[Chimerae|Chimera]] and the Alter Ego companion of [[Nevenka]]. He serves as her sensei and traveling companion.

## Appearance
Blotch appears as a colorful mass swirling in the clouds - like a serpent made of rainbow colors. He is described as a dragon of hues and shades, as if clouds had turned into paint, and that paint had become a dragon creature. His form is ethereal and composed of oily, colorful textures.

## First Encounter with Nevenka
While [[Nevenka]] wandered beneath the mighty chimneys of [[Mist]], she saw Blotch swirling around the smokestacks. To get closer to him, she climbed dizzying ladders and stepped over protective railings.

When a maintenance platform gave way beneath her feet and she plummeted into the void, Blotch rushed to save her. Using his oily texture, he managed to slow her fall and place her on a terrace below, though she ended up covered from head to toe in colorful gouache.

## The Testing Period
After saving [[Nevenka]], Blotch attempted to lose her, but she pursued him relentlessly. She followed him through swamps, found his trail in rocky plains, stole a boat to follow him above a lagoon. When she was attacked by a thirsty lion or got stuck in mud as the tide rose, Blotch saved her each time, increasing her sense of debt to him.

After 64 days, Blotch stopped on a rocky prominence and revealed his true name: Kutrabalamashablotchara. However, [[Nevenka]] only registered "Blotch," and that became his official name, much to his chagrin.

Blotch had been testing [[Nevenka]] for months to get a sense of her patience and perseverance. She passed brilliantly, so he agreed to become her sensei.

## Role as Sensei
During the following months, Blotch trained [[Nevenka]] in [[Alteration]]. She meditated under waterfalls, learned to stand still on a reed, until she could split a torrent with a simple hand gesture under his guidance.

## Personality
Blotch is described as having a joking nature. He likes to quip that he "tolerated her presence like a dog forgets about a tick" when describing his relationship with [[Nevenka]]. Despite this teasing, he grew genuinely attached to her over time.

He can be grumpy and serious when situations demand it, showing a no-nonsense side when [[Nevenka]] needs to focus on her duties.

## Relationship with Nevenka
Over time, Blotch got used to Nev tailing him, and they started traveling together. Despite quarrels and gratuitous jabs, they became inseparable companions. Blotch made Nev his sidekick, though he maintains his sardonic sense of humor about their partnership.

Their relationship is characterized by:
- Mutual respect despite constant banter
- Deep loyalty to one another
- Blotch's protective instincts toward [[Nevenka]]
- [[Nevenka]]'s persistent determination that won him over

## Abilities
As a paint dragon [[Chimerae|Chimera]], Blotch possesses:
- Flight capabilities
- An oily, colorful texture that can be manipulated
- The ability to slow falls and cushion impacts
- Significant strength (able to carry [[Nevenka]] while flying)
- [[Alteration]] abilities related to his paint-like nature

## Role as Alter Ego
Blotch serves as [[Nevenka]]'s Alter Ego in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], accompanying her as a [[Lyra]] Exalt. He transports her when needed and provides guidance, though he isn't always patient with her antics.
