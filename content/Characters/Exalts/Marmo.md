# Marmo

## Overview

Marmo is a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who serves as [[Subhash Kadis]]'s Alter Ego, bonded through [[The Musubi]]. Originally found as a severely wounded puppy on [[Suspira]], Marmo transformed into a Chimera after consuming [[The Tumult|Tumult]] effluvia and protecting Bash from a Tumult singularity.

## Origin

During a forbidden excursion among the dark mounds of [[Suspira]], [[Subhash Kadis]] heard a lament and found a severely wounded puppy missing its two hind legs and lacerated with cuts. Bash brought the animal home, and his father, a nurse, treated its wounds. Bash rigged up a wheelchair for Marmo so the dog could move around and taught it to follow the sound of his voice and whistles. Over the years, they became inseparable.

## Transformation

Marmo began eating [[The Tumult|Tumult]] effluvia on [[Suspira]]. One day, while protecting Bash from a Tumult singularity, Marmo transformed into a [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. Following this transformation, Marmo and Subhash bonded through [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

## Abilities

Marmo possesses a dorsal antenna that can part [[The Tumult|Tumult]] mist, useful during cave exploration and prospecting operations.
