# Crowbar

## Overview

Crowbar is a scarecrow [[Chimerae|Chimera]] with a pumpkin head who serves as the Alter Ego companion of [[Fen]]. She is a virtuoso guitarist and member of [[BLISS!]]. [[Fen]] notes that Crowbar's scarecrow appearance is "just a stage outfit that she puts on when she feels like it."

## Appearance

Crowbar appears as a scarecrow with a pumpkin head. Her eyes light up when she "wakes up" or becomes alert. She can set her pumpkin head aflame during performances.

## Role in BLISS!

Crowbar plays guitar in [[BLISS!]]:
- [[Fen]] on vocals
- [[Tamati]] on percussion
- [[Boona]] on bass
- [[Orbec]] on keyboard
- Crowbar on guitar

[[Fen]]'s operatic vocals blend with Crowbar's guitar solos. Through [[Fen]]'s [[The Ignescence|Ignescence]] and [[Alteration]], their concerts became multi-sensory experiences.

## Role as Alter Ego

Crowbar serves as [[Fen]]'s Alter Ego in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], accompanying her as a [[Lyra]] Exalt from the [[Kasirga Clan]]. She offers counsel when needed, once advising [[Fen]] that "there was nothing better than aiming as high as possible, because those who don't aim for the stars have no chance of reaching them."

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Lyra Festival Performance
Crowbar played electric guitar during festival in [[Arkaster]] while [[Fen]] drew on [[Ignescence]] and summoned [[Asmodeus]], [[The Sandman]], and [[Cloth Dancer]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Snowman Building at Storvhit
Crowbar built snowman with [[Fen]] at [[Storvhit]], engaged in snowball fight, then observed [[Living Snowman]] examine their creation and wave goodbye.

### 393 AC, June-September - BLISS! Concert Crisis
During [[BLISS!]] performance at [[The Screed]], Crowbar suffered migraine causing missed note simultaneously with [[Fen]]'s memory loss, then approached [[Fen]] telepathically but received only white noise.

### 393 AC, June-September - Concert Recovery and Encore
Crowbar convinced [[Orbec]] and [[Tamati]] to support [[Fen]] after her memory loss, then led marathon encore culminating in flaming pumpkin head solo and crowd surf.
