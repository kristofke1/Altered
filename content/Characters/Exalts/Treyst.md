# Treyst

## Overview

Treyst, born 363 AC, is an [[Axiom]] Exalt and engineer who serves in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]]. He is paraplegic due to a crash that killed his best friend [[Paju]] and requires Kelonic batteries implanted in his spine to walk. He is bonded to [[Rossum]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] accidentally trapped in an [[Automata|Automaton]] body, through [[The Musubi]].

## Background

Treyst grew up as a ward in the [[Kurung]] orphanage in [[Arkaster]] alongside [[Paju]]. Lacking natural affinity for [[Alteration]], they applied to the [[Axiom]] and trained at [[The Foundry]]. Paju became a pilot while Treyst, rejected due to faulty vision, became a navigator. They joined the aeronaval contingent and were incorporated into [[The Fulcrum]]. A crash during a reconnaissance mission killed Paju and left Treyst paraplegic, requiring Kelonic spinal implants to walk. Treyst subsequently worked in [[Grafts|Graft]] manufacturing before collaborating with [[Frankenstein]] on an Eidolon-[[Automata|Automaton]] fusion project that accidentally created [[Rossum]].

## Timeline

### 370 AC - Apprentice Work
Treyst and Paju transported Kelon cylinders between shipyard and charging stations as Apprentices.

### 372 AC - Repair Training
Treyst and Paju helped [[Tessalit Girum]] repair an airship using kelonic bolt-poppers.

### 375 AC - Aerolith Training
[[Jian Lam]] taught Treyst about [[Aerolith]] properties and airship altitude adjustment.

### 378 AC - Achieving Flight
Paju became pilot and Treyst became navigator; they joined the aeronaval contingent.

### 379 AC - Fatal Crash
During a reconnaissance mission, a creature's tentacle struck their aerodyne. Treyst summoned [[Amelia Earhart]] to stabilize the ship, but an explosion caused a crash. Paju died and Treyst's spinal column fractured along with both legs and one arm.

### 380 AC - Kelonic Recovery
The Grems activated a Kelonic Generator to charge Treyst's mechanical vertebra, enabling him to walk using Kelon batteries.

### 382 AC - Graft Manufacturing
Treyst worked at [[The Foundry]] alongside [[Ganesha]], fitting patients with prostheses.

### 390 AC - Frankenstein's Proposal
[[Frankenstein]] proposed using [[Grafts]] to create [[Automata]] by projecting memories inside them, referencing Paju.

### 391 AC - Studying Coppélia
[[Frankenstein]] showed Treyst [[Coppélia]] at [[The Athanor]]—a porcelain doll with a mechanical Kelon heart that stabilized Eidolons.

### 391 AC - Rossum's Creation
Treyst's attempt to pair Paju's Eidolon with an Automaton triggered a Tumult Singularity, trapping a Chimera in the metal shell instead.

### 392 AC - Waru's Budget Denial
[[Waru Toowoom]] denied Treyst a budget for improving his [[Automata|Automatons]] as part of his campaign obstructing [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] operations.

### 392 AC - Armored Jammer Deployment
Treyst operated an armored tank beyond [[The Rampart]] while [[Subhash Kadis]] deployed shielded [[Tumult Jammer|Jammers]] to cover the advance of [[Ordis]] and [[Bravos]] troops.

### 392 AC - Tumult Expedition
Treyst and [[Rossum]] crossed shifting ground beyond [[The Rampart]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Frozen Lake Rescue at Storvhit
Treyst remained on shore with his armored vehicle during a rescue operation at [[Storhvit]], unable to venture onto breaking ice while [[Subhash Kadis]] sprinted across to reach [[Nyala]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Snowy Pass with Vaike Swiftwater
Treyst traveled through snowy pass at [[Storhvit]] with [[Vaike Swiftwater]] leading the party using her heat cannon, and converted a kelonic heater into a heat gun with her help.

### 393 AC, January-April - Cliff Ascent with Mechanical Spider
Treyst used his mechanical spider to scale vertical rock face at [[Storhvit]] while [[Rossum]] wore scarf and mittens given by [[Fen]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Frozen Delivery Supply Drop
Treyst received supply drop from zeppelin at [[Storhvit]] containing [[Cornucoopia]]-branded provisions and [[Axiom]] gear, navigating his spider carefully while [[Rossum]] clung to the vehicle to avoid moisture damage.

### 393 AC, January-April - Kelonic Heater at Night Camp
Treyst arrived at night camp at [[Storhvit]] with mechanical spider slowed by ice and brought a kelonic heater to shivering engineers.

### 393 AC, January-April - Icebound Hollow Exploration
Treyst explored cave at [[Storhvit]] with a porter, discovering walls of gypsum, ice, and pyrite with bioluminescent glow, before noticing rising water on the floor.

### 393 AC, June-September - Sap Sniffer Delivery
Treyst observed a prospector and his [[Sap Sniffer]] delivering Sap containers to his workshop.

### 393 AC, June-September - Dredger Drone Observation
Treyst visited a dig site with [[Rossum]] and observed [[Dredger Drone|Dredger Drones]] performing coordinated excavation work with hive-like precision.

### 393 AC, June-September - Sap Delivery from Aberdeen
[[Aberdeen]] delivered Sap samples from [[Sierra]] to Treyst's workshop while Treyst mapped vein locations from her descriptions.

### 393 AC, June-September - Refinery Conversion Activation
Treyst witnessed the successful activation of [[The Refinery]]'s Kelon-to-Sap conversion alongside [[Subhash Kadis]] and other engineers after a five-week standstill.

### 393 AC, June-September - City of Scholars Excavation
During excavation at the [[City of Scholars]], Treyst operated drilling equipment to extract the [[Sap]] from sealed reservoirs. [[Arjun]] revealed the substance was likely the Sap of a world-tree that the ancient inhabitants had drained, causing the city's decline. When drilling triggered a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity, [[Yzmir]] Initiates appeared through portals to evacuate personnel. [[Rossum]] tackled Treyst to the ground, saving him from a falling block, then helped him escape as his Kelonic braces smoked from the strain.

### 393 AC, September - Destruction of The Maze
Treyst participated in the Exalt operation against [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]]. His role was to trigger explosive charges as a last resort if the psychic contact attempt failed. When The Hunger shifted to aggression and [[Atsadi]] and [[Kojo Oduro]] engaged in combat, someone shouted Treyst's name. He slammed the detonator button, triggering a chain reaction of successive explosions that spread from the upper levels into the depths. The charges shattered the floating memory blocks and struck their [[Aerolith]] cores, causing every [[Tesseracts|Tesseract]] to detonate in a furious chain reaction. Treyst glimpsed [[Akesha Adhikari]] slowing the collapse as [[Yzmir]] mages opened portals to evacuate the Exalts before the cataclysm buried them.

### 393 AC, June-September - Bessie Coleman Training Observation
Treyst watched [[Bessie Coleman]] perform aerobatic maneuvers to train cadets in [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] evasion tactics.

### 393 AC, June-September - Paju Eidolon Simulation
Treyst manifested [[Paju]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] in [[Rossum]]'s workshop, where Paju described flight experiences before the simulation ended.

### 393 AC, June-September - FarFetch 2.0 Construction
Treyst and [[Rossum]] modified a non-Asgarthan maintenance rig into [[FarFetch 2.0]], installing Kelonic convectors to allow [[Paju]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to explore autonomously without continuous manifestation.

## Abilities

Treyst is a trained engineer and navigator specializing in [[Kelon]] technology, [[Grafts|Graft]] manufacturing, and [[Automata]] construction. He operates a mechanical spider vehicle for traversing difficult terrain and maintains a [[Tech Lab Unit]] for prosthetics research. He can summon [[Eidolons]] including [[Amelia Earhart]] and [[Paju]]. Through [[The Musubi]], he is bonded with [[Rossum]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] trapped in an [[Automata|Automaton]] body.
