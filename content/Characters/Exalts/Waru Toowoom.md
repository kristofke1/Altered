# Waru Toowoom

## Overview

Waru Toowoom is an [[Ordis]] Exalt, politician, and historian who publicly serves as chief opponent to Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]], while secretly collaborating with him to ensure the success of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. He is bonded with [[Mack]], an ermine [[Chimerae|Chimera]] named after Machiavelli, through [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

## Background

Waru and [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] met at university where they became close friends and intellectual rivals. Despite diametrically opposed views, they gathered evenings at a tavern in the Ruzzante to debate. Waru had a passion for history while Avkan had an interest in tales of great explorers. [[Mack]] was a gift from Waru's father when he enrolled in his political curriculum.

When certain indicators hinted at future troubles—growing population, limited resources, declining mines—Avkan was convinced solutions required looking beyond the Peninsula's borders, while Waru believed solutions existed in [[Asgartha]]. One evening, Avkan likened Asgartha to a colony of mice that would collapse upon itself; Waru left the tavern. When they graduated, a chasm had opened between them.

In truth, they made a secret pact: they would pretend to be opposed in every way, with Waru appearing as Avkan's chief detractor. By positioning himself correctly, Waru would appear as a weighty ally for Avkan's opponents while weaving his conspiratorial web among the shadows. Together, they would lead Asgartha toward its destiny beyond the Peninsula's borders.

Partly thanks to [[Mack]], Waru built a network of spies and informants. Once linked through [[The Musubi]], Mack could share what he saw and heard. Despite their machinations, Avkan was elected Basileus given his growing popularity. In the end, they could only delay the inevitable, securing certain concessions including Waru's appointment as Exalt.

### 393 AC - Rati Diagnosis
[[Rati]] doctors diagnosed Waru with the same illness affecting [[City of Scholars]] labyrinth explorers, though his symptoms had begun before arriving at the City. Waru adopted routine mental exercises in response.

### 393 AC, June-September - Physical Reaction to Rune's Scroll
Upon reading [[Rune]]'s scroll, Waru experienced an intense physical reaction including migraine and dropped the document.

## Abilities

Waru is skilled in Heka [[Alteration]], using [[Glyph|Glyphs]] to summon [[Eidolons]], reconstruct ruins with ghost-images, and create constructs. Through [[The Musubi]], [[Mack]] serves as his intelligence-gathering extension, sharing what the ermine sees and hears across Waru's spy network.
