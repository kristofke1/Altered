# Zephyr

## Overview

Zephyr is a swan [[Chimerae|Chimera]] bonded with [[Zhen]] as her Alter Ego. Originally a research specimen at [[Cebir|The Cebir]], Zephyr is unique among Alter Egos for having undergone [[The Musubi]] twice—first with [[Bai Shan-shu]] in 364 AC, then with Zhen after Shan-shu's death. The second bonding saved Zephyr's life but transferred traumatic memories of experiments and conspiracy to Zhen.

## Physical Form

Zephyr takes the form of a majestic swan. The Chimera can whistle and trumpet, producing a plaintive cry as lamentation.

## Early History

### The Cebir

Zephyr was held as a specimen at [[Cebir|The Cebir]], an outpost serving as both military fortress and experimental laboratory. [[Madelyn Moss]], a scientist studying Chimeras, conducted research on the creature between 327 and 329 AC.

### Departure from the Cebir

When the remote base was abandoned in 329 AC, Madelyn was permitted to keep Zephyr as the only Chimera specimen she could bring. The swan accompanied the Moss family to [[Hadera]] in [[Caer Esvander]].

## Life with the Moss Family

### Bond with Infant Zhen

At [[Zhen]]'s birth in 330 AC, Zephyr wrapped its wings around the small baby to keep her warm, as if wanting to brood over her. As Zhen grew, Zephyr became her constant companion and playmate. Zhen considered the Chimera a full member of the family.

### Experimental Subject

[[Madelyn Moss]] regularly brought Zephyr to her workshop for experiments, using the Chimera to assist with her research. These sessions involved Mana siphoning in vats, leaving Zephyr exhausted and forced to rest for days afterward.

## The Mesektet Tragedy

When orders came for Madelyn to board [[Mesektet]] to test her invention, Zephyr accompanied [[Bai Shan-shu]]'s [[Tagmata]] squadron escorting the mothership. During the attack by [[Cingula]], Shan-shu used Zephyr's abilities to save many crew members, earning the Purple Star for his heroism. Madelyn died in the ship's explosion.

## First Musubi with Bai Shan-shu

In 364 AC, [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari|Basileus Avkan]] requested that Shan-shu undergo [[The Musubi]] with Zephyr, as his closeness to the Chimera and past heroism made him an ideal candidate. Shan-shu became one of the first Exalts, proving the Musubi ceremony was reproducible. However, his health suddenly declined afterward, making him unfit for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Shan-shu's Death and Near-Death

When [[Bai Shan-shu]] died, Zephyr was found in the veranda, curled near an artificial pond. The Chimera was weak, without even the will to live. The Musubi bond proved so strong that Zephyr was letting himself die now that his other half had passed. The swan lost feathers as its body wasted away irreversibly.

## Second Musubi with Zhen

[[Zhen]] sought counsel from a [[Muna]] enclave about saving Zephyr. The druids mentioned the possibility of a second Musubi, warning that if the Chimera were to disappear, it could affect Zhen to her core. Zhen accepted the risk.

During the ceremony, as their beings mingled, Zephyr's memories transferred to Zhen. Through the Chimera's eyes, she witnessed the experiments where Zephyr's Mana was siphoned, the vats, and her parents' faces. The memories showed her father's suffering, his episodes of despair, his guilt, and his wish to end it all. She also saw the faces of those who had visited the family home as part of a conspiracy: [[de Graaf]], [[Basem Falani|Falani]], and [[Temera Singh|Singh]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC, January - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

Zephyr led [[The Raptors]] scouting squadron home from reconnaissance over [[The Tumult]], breaching the clouds with wings spread wide. [[Zhen]] sensed Zephyr's presence before seeing him. After landing on [[Mandjet]], Zephyr lowered his head onto Zhen's shoulder in greeting. [[Gaj]] observed that a part of [[Bai Shan-shu]] lives on through the Chimera.
