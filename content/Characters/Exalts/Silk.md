# Silk

## Overview

Silk is a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] larva discovered dormant inside [[The Nilam]]'s bark in 393 AC. Named by [[Moyo Chibuye]] for its ability to secrete hardening fiber similar to a silkworm, Silk has developed a bond with Moyo and is considered a potential candidate for [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

## Discovery and Growth

Researchers found Silk curled dormant inside The Nilam's fossilized trunk, approximately fist-sized when discovered. The larva had endured the frost in hibernation and awoke when exposed to milder temperatures at [[Nilam Spires]]. The creature's growth rate proved exponential—by the time of [[Zaj Ke Va]]'s inquiry, Silk had reached the size of a farm pig.

## Physical Characteristics

Silk possesses unquestionably chimeric nature but displays unusual stability—it has not undergone the series of mutations typical of [[Chimerae]]. Its form has remained consistent since awakening, only increasing in size. The larva secretes a tough fiber from its glands, saliva that hardens when exposed to air, earning its name.

## Diet and Nature

[[Mana]] forms part of Silk's daily diet, yet despite this magical sustenance, the creature has maintained its original form without mutation. [[Moyo Chibuye]] theorized that Silk's existence was no coincidence—its idea lingered in the fossilized trunk while its physical shell endured the frost.

## Bond with Moyo Chibuye

Since Silk's awakening, [[Moyo Chibuye]] has been the one feeding it. The creature displays attachment to Moyo like a dog to its master—reacting differently in Moyo's presence, seeking out their touch, and showing what appears to be affection. This bond prompted [[Yzmir]] leadership to consider Silk as a potential candidate for serving as Moyo's Alter Ego through [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

## Possible Connection to Mana Moths

When questioned about a connection between Silk and [[Mana Moths]], Moyo could only respond "perhaps," leaving the relationship uncertain.
