# Basira al-Arshad

## Overview

Basira al-Arshad is a veteran [[Bravos]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]], known as the Last Survivor of [[Cebir]] and a Seiringar instructor. She is bonded with her Alter Ego [[Kaizaimon]], an oni [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. Basira seeks vengeance against [[Cingula]] for the deaths of her comrades and family.

## The First Cebir Disaster and Early Life

Basira was young when the [[Bravos]] mounted an expedition to reclaim [[Cebir]], a citadel erected in 327 AC within the Terra Incognita. During the expedition in 329 AC, Basira's company was attacked by infernal creaturesâ€”monstrous hybrids with claws and fangs that slaughtered her comrades. She later identified these as [[Eidolons]]: [[Mantichora]] and [[Khimaira]], which should have been bound by [[Yzmir]] Mandates. During the attack, Basira discovered [[Kaizaimon]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] imprisoned and tortured by the attackers, and freed him. When swept away by currents, her armored body plummeted like a stone, but the freed [[Chimerae|Chimera]] transformed into a tattoo on her body, emitting waves of heat that kept her alive until an [[Ordis]] airship rescued her. Basira was the sole survivor of the expedition. The authorities dismissed her claims about [[Eidolons]] as hallucinations. Recovery was long and arduous, with psychological scars remaining raw. After accepting the tragedy was not her fault, Basira left [[Arkaster]] to lead a quasi-solitary existence, offering her services to communities in need and spending years in [[Caer Kettria]].

## Family Life

Basira started a family with [[Filiki]], a [[Muna]] who was her opposite in temperamentâ€”where she was irascible, he was calm and patient. They had four children together. For many years, Basira set aside her pain and fury. Time passed with the slow passage of seasons, and the young adventurer became a wise fighter. Basira's family was taken by [[Cingula]], a Leviathan, in circumstances paralleling [[Afanas Korovinovich]]'s loss.


## Filiki's Death and Return to the Field

As the departure date for exploration forces approached, [[Filiki]] died. With his death, it was as if an enchanted parenthesis closed, leaving Basira once again facing herself and her failures. Though surrounded by her children and a large family, Basira announced her resignation as an instructor. She still had scores to settle with life. Basira knew she had one last chance to make amends and challenge [[The Tumult]] again, knowing that [[Cingula]] was still waiting somewhere.
