# Basira al-Arshad

## Overview

Basira al-Arshad, born 333 AC, is a veteran [[Bravos]] [[Exalts|Exalt]] and [[Alterers|Alterer]], known as the Last Survivor of [[Cebir]] and a Seiringar instructor. She is bonded with her Alter Ego [[Kaizaimon]], an oni [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. Basira seeks vengeance against [[Cingula]] for the deaths of her comrades and family.

## Background

At nineteen years old, Basira joined a [[Bravos]] Company called the Dagger of Esvander on an expedition to reclaim [[Cebir]], the citadel in [[Caer Oorun]] that had been abandoned since 329 AC. The company discovered the fortress had been converted into a laboratory containing esoteric diagrams, caged creature remains, and alchemical equipment. They were attacked by [[Eidolons]] — [[Mantichora]] and [[Khimaira]] — that slaughtered most of the group. During the attack, Basira discovered [[Kaizaimon]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] imprisoned and tortured within a hidden annex, and freed him. Swept away by currents, her armored body sank, but [[Kaizaimon]] transformed into a tattoo on her body and emitted waves of heat that kept her alive until an [[Ordis]] airship rescued her two days later. The authorities dismissed her claims about the [[Eidolons]] as hallucinations.

After a long recovery, Basira left [[Arkaster]] and spent years in [[Caer Kettria]], offering her services to communities in need. She started a family with [[Filiki]], a [[Muna]] who was her opposite in temperament. They had four children together. Basira's family was later taken by [[Cingula]], a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]], in circumstances paralleling [[Afanas Korovinovich]]'s loss. When [[Filiki]] died as the departure date for exploration forces approached, Basira announced her resignation as instructor, determined to challenge [[The Tumult]] again and confront [[Cingula]].

## Timeline

### 352 AC - Cebir Expedition Disaster

Basira joined the Dagger of Esvander's expedition to reclaim [[Cebir]] and was the sole survivor of the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] attack, rescued after two days in freezing waters. She bonded with [[Kaizaimon]] during the disaster.

### 368 AC - Return to Haven as Seiringar

The Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] personally asked Basira to return to the capital to train recruits who would take part in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. She accepted and settled in [[Haven]] as a Seiringar, devoting herself to training the next generation of fighters. [[Kaizaimon]]'s appearance alone was enough to motivate the troops.

### 368 AC - Physical Training at Kurung

Basira walked through [[Kurung]] training grounds with bundle over shoulder, greeting instructors and watching cadets.

### 374 AC - Chiron Treats Carmela

[[Carmela]] sprained her ankle showing off, and Basira summoned [[Chiron]] who treated and carried her back while giving advice.

### 385 AC - Issun-Bōshi Training Demonstration

New recruits mocked [[Issun-Bōshi]]'s size, but the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] taught them never to underestimate an insignificant opponent.

### 390 AC - Training Sitina in Blade Dance

Basira trained [[Sitina]] in the Phoenixian Blade Dance through three progressive stages, observing her enter the duende trance state with crimson-gleaming blades.

### 392 AC - Bravos Vanguard Expedition

Basira escorted cartographer [[Aoife]] on an elevation charting mission during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], putting her decade of preparation to field use.

### 392 AC - Passing the Torch

After the expedition, Basira experienced her first real aches acknowledging age, and [[Aoife]] helped her stand.

### 392 AC - Atlas Transportation

Basira summoned [[Atlas]] who tore off the rocky outcrop she stood on and transported her leagues in a few steps.

### 392 AC, September - Battle of Madan Bay

During the [[Battle of Madan Bay]], Basira positioned herself and [[Bravos]] soldiers on a cliff while [[Kaizaimon]] solidified above the bluff, waiting for [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]] on griffon to signal the [[Kraken]]'s approach.

### 392 AC - Cebir Reunion with Afanas

At [[Cebir]], Basira met [[Afanas Korovinovich]], a [[Yzmir]] Battle Mage and veteran who revealed he had lived at the fortress as a child. [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]] validated her account of the Eidolon attacks and explained their parallel histories — his family had been taken by the [[Kraken]] just as hers was taken by [[Cingula]]. He suspected a renegade [[Yzmir]] Initiate, [[Sylas]] (the Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock), was responsible for the [[Cebir]] laboratory, the summoning of [[Mantichora]] and [[Khimaira]], and the control of the [[Kraken]]. To confirm, [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]] summoned the scent of chrysanthemums; [[Kaizaimon]] burst forth in violent rage, recognizing the scent of their tormentor. [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]] invited Basira to join forces, but she preferred pursuing vengeance independently.

### 393 AC, January-April - Shahram Eidolon Morale Boost

Basira summoned [[Shahram]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to boost troops' morale by fire, with the old Seiringar boasting about exploits and warming listless adventurers through swagger.

### 393 AC, January-April - Avalanche Probe Search

Basira searched an avalanche site with a probe, sinking it repeatedly into snow before finding resistance and whistling for [[Chaghagan]] to dig with a shovel.

### 393 AC, January-April - Trailblazer Rescue

[[Chaghagan]] dug up a white-furred animal then revealed a Trailblazer with pink hair beneath.

### 393 AC, January-April - Catriona Warming Victims

Basira observed [[Catriona]] summoning heat to warm an [[Ordis]] recruit after an avalanche.

### 393 AC, January-April - Akhlut Ice Breaking Journey

Basira rode [[Akhlut]] across an ice pack as the creature broke through with its snout until reaching the shoreline.

### 393 AC, June-September - Escorting Jubal's Excavation

Basira escorted [[Jubal Okech]] and prospectors during the [[City of Scholars]] excavation, observing Jubal's rapport with rank-and-file despite his surly personality.

### 393 AC, June-September - Shadow Entity Attack and Sidqi's Sacrifice

Basira led an expedition through the [[City of Scholars]] when shadow entities attacked. [[Sidqi]] sacrificed himself and [[Aoife]] rushed to help while [[Kaizaimon]] fought alongside.

### 393 AC, June-September - Witnessing Aoife's Phoenix Manifestation

Basira witnessed [[Aoife]] manifest the gift of the Phoenix — blades, hair, and eyes catching fire like [[Sitina]]'s — and held [[Kaizaimon]] back to let Aoife banish the shadows.

### 393 AC, June-September - Hestia's Kitchen at The Mess

Basira visited [[The Mess]] to order food for [[Aoife]]'s inauguration celebration, observing [[Hestia]]'s strict kitchen management and [[Sap]]-enhanced cuisine.

### 393 AC, June-September - Aoife's Inauguration Celebration

Basira celebrated [[Aoife]]'s [[Phoenixian Guard]] induction at [[The Mess]] with [[Jubal Okech]], drinking [[Sap]] beer and reminiscing about [[Filiki]]'s sayings about moderation.

### 393 AC, October - Refusing to Lead Hunt Against Halua

Following the destruction of [[AES Sune]] by [[Halua]], [[Sol]] rallied hunters in the mess hall and called on Basira to lead a retaliatory hunt. Basira refused, warning that charging in headfirst would get people killed and accusing Sol of using collective grief to feed a personal vendetta. When Sol accused her of cowardice, Basira chose restraint over vengeance.

### 393 AC - Receiving the Phoenix Egg

[[Sitina]] and the [[Phoenixian Guard]] entrusted Basira with the [[Phoenix Egg]] from the [[Parliament of Ashes]], instructing her to find someone worthy to carry it.

### 393 AC - Traveling by Phileas Fogg's Balloon

Basira rode in [[Phileas Fogg]]'s balloon to a battlefield where the [[The Bravos Fyrds|fyrds]] were gathering, with [[Kaizaimon]] clinging to her during the six-minute journey.
