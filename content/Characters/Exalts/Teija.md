# Teija

## Overview

Teija, born 364 AC, is a [[Muna]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who works to combat the corrupting effects of [[The Fagn]] in the [[Kiragata Wetlands|Kiragata Marshes]]. Through [[The Musubi]] ceremony at the [[Bark Refuge]] within [[The Spindle]], they bonded with [[Nauraa]], a giant fox who serves as their Alter Ego. Teija departed with the other Exalts for the Terra Incognita as part of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Background

Teija trained as a [[Muna]] [[Alterers|Alterer]], learning to perceive [[The Skein]], the great tapestry that binds all things together. They developed skills in detecting intangible ideas that plague creatures and objects—concepts like "pain," "destruction," "impulse," "acid," and "gigantism." Using an ivory blade, Teija learned to excise these parasitic ideas from corrupted beings and graft them onto others or release them into the environment. Their skills caught the attention of [[Bokang Nkoe]], a mentor and druid who guided them toward [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

Teija underwent the ceremony at the [[Bark Refuge]] sanctuary within [[The Spindle]], wearing ceremonial attire with hakama, obi, and headdress with bells. Inside [[The Spindle]]'s trunk, thousands of natural niches buzzed with life—birds, rodents, bears, wolves, sentient plants, and insects. Teija knelt in the center of a circle of moss-covered standing stones as priestesses extinguished braziers and lanterns, beginning the ceremony that bound their spirit to [[Nauraa]], a giant fox [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. Through [[The Musubi]], their senses synchronized, allowing telepathic communication.

The [[Kiragata Wetlands|Kiragata Marshes]] have been struck by the dark effluvia of [[The Fagn]]. What was once a bucolic landscape has transformed into a dark, misty environment with gigantic corals illuminating the area with a reddish glow. Teija works methodically through the marshes, healing corrupted fauna and flora bit by bit, plot by plot. While working in the marshes, Teija and [[Nauraa]] encountered a black [[Chimerae|Chimera]] corrupted by [[The Fagn]], with a snake tail, panther torso, and badger head with jet-black scales. Teija excised "impulse" from the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] and grafted it onto [[Nauraa]], then removed "acid" and "gigantism" from the creature. The "gigantism" accidentally landed on [[Nauraa]], causing the fox to grow as tall as a village steeple. After removing "pain" and "destruction," Teija absorbed the pain and stored the destruction in a flask.

Over the years, many sentient species have come to rely on Teija to defend and rescue them from [[The Fagn]]'s spread. Despite ongoing efforts, the plague is gaining ground.

Teija and [[Nauraa]] brought sticky rice sweets to Fox Spirit altar, where [[Kitsune]] appeared and gave gift wrapped in oak leaf.

Teija performed first service with taiko drums and bell instrument, summoning [[Inari]] to bless crops while dancing together at pagoda.

### 378 AC - Leaving Earth Refuge

Teija left Earth Refuge with [[Muna]] Druid [[Bokang Nkoe]] as instructor, leaving parents behind to begin apprenticeship.

### 381 AC - Arriving at The Spindle

Teija and [[Bokang Nkoe]] reached [[The Spindle]], marking end of pilgrimage before fasting, collecting roots, creating [[Namkha]] knot, and preparing for [[The Musubi]].

### 382 AC - Treating with Sneezer Shroom

Teija treated young girl affected by Tumult singularity, summoning [[Sneezer Shroom]] to purify destabilized [[Mana]] through mycelial network.

### 384 AC - Addressing Human Encroachment

Dracaena led Teija to trampled marsh where community took land without permission, requiring Teija to relieve tensions with inhabitants.

### 385 AC - Negotiating with Coniferal Coneman

Teija prostrated before angry Coniferal Coneman to show respect, offering [[Mana]]-charged water to discuss village's displacement by [[The Fagn]].

### 387 AC - Witnessing Hellequin Hunt

Teija found a deer herd massacred by a Fagn-corrupted beast and joined [[Hellequin]] Hunters—recognizable by their antler headdresses—who drove the prey with requiem melodies blown through horns before a Huntress bearing [[The Reapers|Harvester]] initials delivered the coup de grâce, shedding ritual tears of duty for the life taken.

### 388 AC - Verdantback Delegation to Arkaster

Teija escorted centuries-old verdantback turtle through [[Arkaster]] streets to request government aid against [[The Fagn]]'s spread.

### 392 AC - Swamp Stabilization and Recovery

Teija and [[Nauraa]] rested after escaping a swamp they had stabilized, fishing in a temporary paradise of dragonflies and water spiders that would be destroyed if [[The Tumult]] passed through before the [[Expeditionary Corps]] arrived.

### 393 AC, January-March - Fleeing Belisenki at Storvhit

Teija manifested [[Eidolons]] and [[Simbi]] to aid [[Nauraa]]'s escape through [[Storhvit]] forest while pursued by hordes of [[Belisenki]], attempting unsuccessfully to reach the creatures through [[The Skein]] to soothe their hostility.

### 393 AC, January-April - Leshy Bridge Crossing

When expedition halted at wide crevasse spanning miles that was too broad for ropes and grappling hooks, [[Leshy]] emerged from the gorge and stretched enormous tree-trunk arms across the gap, creating a makeshift bridge for Teija and the expedition to cross.

### 393 AC, January-April - Demeter Climate Conference

Teija attended meeting where [[Demeter]] conferred with Admiral [[Temera Singh]] about permanently changing [[Storhvit]]'s climate, and after hearing arguments from those demanding terraforming and those seeking compromise, Demeter concluded that humanity must adapt to nature rather than the reverse.

### 393 AC, January-April - Hostile Forest Encounter

When frozen taiga gave way to wooded expanses free of snow, Teija found the reawakened forest hostile and bitter through [[The Skein]], discovering [[Three Little Pigs]] [[Eidolons]] struggling with voracious roots in a dark clearing before driving back the lashing plant limbs while the pig engineers fled.

### 393 AC, January-April - Magic Beans Experimentation

Before departing on expedition, Teija received packet of magic beans from [[Arjun]] that could sprout ideas into reality, testing them to create plant-based containers and tents while saving remaining seeds for critical moments.

### 393 AC, June-September - Ryukkōsai Festival Attendance

Teija attended [[Libellumines]] season at [[Vasanti Park]] in [[Arkaster]], watching the insects' ephemeral mating dance during the Ryukkōsai festival with its accompanying Midori Matsuri ceremony.

### 393 AC, June-September - Summoning Arawn for Missing Expedition

Teija summoned [[Arawn]] and his [[Cŵn Annwn]] hounds to track a missing expedition that had not reported for two days and was supposed to rendezvous with the [[Mesektet]] at the entrance to the [[City of Scholars|Scholars' Vault]].

### 393 AC, June-September - Minotaur and Mythology Discussion

Teija observed their [[Minotaur]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] meditating beneath tree foliage and discussed mythological parallels between Asterion legends and Asgarthan civilization with historian [[Abiram Okech]].

### 393 AC, June-September - Waiting at The Mess

Teija waited at [[The Mess]] for [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]], who was held up by administrative formalities at city hall. Teija observed the nearly empty canteen and the few families with school-age children who had been granted exemptions for key personnel, noting that tutors complained children had been skipping school since arriving at [[The Screed]] after months confined to [[The Ouroboros]]. 

### 393 AC, June-September - Psychic Interference with Lernaean Hydra

During shadow attack in [[City of Scholars]] caverns, Teija psychically intervened through [[The Skein]] when [[Atsadi]] attempted to summon a bloodthirsty [[Lernaean Hydra]], implanting alternative ideas into his imagination and altering the manifestation.

### 393 AC, June-September - Descent into The Crow's Eye

Teija recommended to Admiral [[Temera Singh]] that exploration of [[City of Scholars|the City]] be postponed because [[The Skein]] reacted strangely near [[The Crow's Eye]], as if its network avoided going too deep. Teija descended into the [[The Crow's Eye|Crow's Eye]] with [[Nauraa]] and a team of [[Hellequin]] hunters, some bearing marks of [[The Reapers]], accompanied by [[Noosh Burrowers]] for tunnel excavation. During descent via [[Axiom]] freight elevator, Teija observed [[The Undergrowth]]—a partially buried jungle of tangled roots, gigantic stems, pink mosses, and dark blue geometric blocks veined with gold embedded in vegetation. Teija sensed ripples disturbing perception of [[The Skein]], like interference, and temporarily severed connection to focus.

### 393 AC, September - Psychic Contact with The Hunger

Teija participated in the Exalt confrontation with [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]]. While [[Axiom]] machines flooded an arena with [[Sap]] to lure the creature, Teija meditated atop one of the floating memory blocks with [[Fen]] at their side and [[Nauraa]] providing protection. Through [[The Skein]], Teija extended consciousness toward the entity, attempting to reach whatever remained within. The dendrites of [[The Skein]] spread around Teija like sea anemone tentacles. Together with [[Fen]], Teija helped establish contact with [[Sofia]], the child consciousness trapped within the monster.

### 393 AC, post-September - Witnessing Tāne Restore Explosion Site

Teija encountered a biome in the [[Cloud Realm]] flattened by an [[Axiom]]-[[Bravos]] experiment that overloaded an [[Aerolith]] fragment with vibrations to measure its explosive properties, destroying branches, shelters, and nests for miles around ground zero. The [[Eidolons|Oneiros]] [[Tāne]] walked among the bare trunks, and where his fingers brushed the bark his color rubbed off onto it, rising to branches that flowered as if experiencing new spring, while grass shot up in concentric waves around his feet and flocks of birds alighted on his arms. Teija bowed to the Eidolon.

### 393 AC, post-September - Communicating with Zaratan

Teija stood before the [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] Zaratan in ceremonial robes, declining bodyguards from the Tagmata offered by [[Sigismar Synd|Sig]]. Through [[The Skein]], Teija felt the creature's curiosity and joy, and their dendrites tangled as Teija shared key concepts of human nature. The Zaratan asked if they came from below the clouds, and when told they came from much further away, revealed that visitors had come before the archipelago broke up and dispersed—before "the red wing" made them abandon these shores.

## Abilities

Teija practices [[Alteration]] through [[The Skein]], perceiving and manipulating the intangible ideas that compose reality. They can detect concepts plaguing creatures or objects—such as "pain," "destruction," "impulse," "acid," and "gigantism"—and excise these ideas using an ivory blade, grafting them onto another entity or releasing them into the environment. Teija collects [[Mana]] droplets that float in areas recently affected by [[The Fagn]] and can transform terrain to make it easier to traverse. Through [[The Musubi]], Teija and [[Nauraa]] communicate telepathically and share synchronized senses. Teija can also psychically intervene through [[The Skein]] to alter other Alterers' manifestations.
