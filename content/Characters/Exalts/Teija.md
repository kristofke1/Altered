# Teija

## Overview

Teija is a [[Muna]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who works to combat the corrupting effects of [[The Fagn]] in the [[Kiragata Wetlands|Kiragata Marshes]]. Through [[The Musubi]] ceremony at the [[Bark Refuge]] within [[The Spindle]], they bonded with [[Nauraa]], a giant fox who serves as their Alter Ego. Teija is preparing to depart with the other Exalts for the Terra Incognita as part of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Becoming a Fagn Healer and Bond with Nauraa

Their skills caught the attention of [[Bokang Nkoe]], a mentor and druid who guided them toward [[The Musubi]] ceremony. ## Abilities and Skills

Teija practices [[Alteration]], using [[The Skein]] to perceive and manipulate the intangible ideas that compose reality. They can detect concepts plaguing creatures or objects, such as "pain," "destruction," "impulse," "acid," and "gigantism." Using an ivory blade, Teija can excise these ideas from one entity and graft them onto another, or release them into the environment. Teija collects [[Mana]] droplets that float in areas recently affected by [[The Fagn]] and can transform terrain to make it easier to traverse.

## Bond with Nauraa

Teija and [[Nauraa]] are connected through [[The Musubi]] ceremony, which synchronized their senses and allows them to communicate telepathically. [[Nauraa]]'s voice can thunder in Teija's head, and they can feel each other's presence and needs.

## Work in the Kiragata Marshes

The [[Kiragata Wetlands|Kiragata Marshes]] have been struck by the dark effluvia of [[The Fagn]]. What was once a bucolic landscape has transformed into a dark, sinister, misty environment with gigantic corals illuminating the area with a reddish glow. Teija works methodically through the marshes, healing corrupted fauna and flora by kneeling to pick up small animals slumped in the nauseating mud. The process is long and tedious, conducted bit by bit, plot by plot.

## Encounter with Corrupted Chimera

While working in the marshes, Teija and [[Nauraa]] encountered a black [[Chimerae|Chimera]] corrupted by [[The Fagn]]. The creature had a snake tail, panther torso, and badger head with jet-black scales. Teija excised "impulse" from the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] and grafted it onto [[Nauraa]], then removed "acid" and "gigantism" from the creature. The "gigantism" accidentally landed on [[Nauraa]], causing the fox to grow as tall as a village steeple. After removing "pain" and "destruction," Teija absorbed the pain and stored the destruction in a flask.

## Preparation for Departure

Teija will leave for the Terra Incognita as part of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. Over the years, many sentient species have come to rely on them to defend and rescue them. Despite ongoing efforts, the plague is gaining ground.

## Timeline

### 373 AC - Offering to Kitsune

Teija and [[Nauraa]] brought sticky rice sweets to Fox Spirit altar, where [[Kitsune]] appeared and gave gift wrapped in oak leaf.

### 375 AC - First Service Summoning Inari

Teija performed first service with taiko drums and bell instrument, summoning [[Inari]] to bless crops while dancing together at pagoda.

### 378 AC - Leaving Earth Refuge

Teija left Earth Refuge with [[Muna]] Druid [[Bokang Nkoe]] as instructor, leaving parents behind to begin apprenticeship.

### 381 AC - Arriving at The Spindle

Teija and [[Bokang Nkoe]] reached [[The Spindle]], marking end of pilgrimage before fasting, collecting roots, creating [[Namkha]] knot, and preparing for [[The Musubi]].

### 382 AC - Treating with Sneezer Shroom

Teija treated young girl affected by Tumult singularity, summoning [[Sneezer Shroom]] to purify destabilized [[Mana]] through mycelial network.

### 384 AC - Addressing Human Encroachment

Dracaena led Teija to trampled marsh where community took land without permission, requiring Teija to relieve tensions with inhabitants.

### 385 AC - Negotiating with Coniferal Coneman

Teija prostrated before angry Coniferal Coneman to show respect, offering [[Mana]]-charged water to discuss village's displacement by [[The Fagn]].

### 387 AC - Witnessing Hellequin Hunt

Teija found a deer herd massacred by a Fagn-corrupted beast and joined [[Hellequin]] Hunters—recognizable by their antler headdresses—who drove the prey with requiem melodies blown through horns before a Huntress bearing [[The Reapers|Harvester]] initials delivered the coup de grâce, shedding ritual "tears of duty" for the life taken.

### 388 AC - Verdantback Delegation to Arkaster

Teija escorted centuries-old verdantback turtle through [[Arkaster]] streets to request government aid against [[The Fagn]]'s spread.

### 392 AC - Swamp Stabilization and Recovery

Teija and [[Nauraa]] rested after escaping a swamp they had stabilized, fishing in a temporary paradise of dragonflies and water spiders that would be destroyed if [[The Tumult]] passed through before the [[Expeditionary Corps]] arrived.

### 393 AC - Ryukkōsai Festival Reflection

Teija recalled attending [[Libellumines]] season at [[Vasanti Park]] in [[Arkaster]], watching the insects' ephemeral mating dance during the Ryukkōsai festival with its accompanying Midori Matsuri ceremony, understanding it as acceptance of existence's brevity and rebellion against [[The Tumult]].

### 393 AC - Summoning Arawn for Missing Expedition

Teija summoned [[Arawn]] and his [[Cŵn Annwn]] hounds to track a missing expedition that had not reported for two days and was supposed to rendezvous with the [[Mesektet]] at the entrance to the [[City of Scholars|Scholars' Vault]].

### 393 AC - Minotaur Meditation and Mythology Discussion

Teija observed their [[Minotaur]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] meditating beneath tree foliage and reflected on conversations with historian [[Abiram Okech]] about mythological parallels between Asterion legends and Asgarthan civilization.

### 393 AC - Waiting at The Mess

Teija waited at [[The Mess]] for someone at city hall, observing families with children who had been granted exemptions for key personnel, and noting [[Hestia]]'s generous portions for children while [[Nauraa]] mentally agreed with her philosophy that proper nutrition starts with a full belly.

### ~393 AC - Fleeing Belisenki at Storvhit

Teija manifested [[Eidolons]] and [[Simbi]] to aid [[Nauraa]]'s escape through [[Storvhit]] forest while pursued by hordes of [[Belisenki]], attempting unsuccessfully to reach the creatures through [[The Skein]] to soothe their hostility.

### ~393 AC - Leshy Bridge Crossing

When expedition halted at wide crevasse spanning miles that was too broad for ropes and grappling hooks, [[Leshy]] emerged from the gorge and stretched enormous tree-trunk arms across the gap, creating a makeshift bridge for Teija and the expedition to cross.

### ~393 AC - Demeter Climate Conference

Teija attended meeting where [[Demeter]] conferred with Admiral [[Temera Singh]] about permanently changing [[Storvhit]]'s climate, and after hearing arguments from those demanding terraforming and those seeking compromise, Demeter concluded that humanity must adapt to nature rather than the reverse.

### ~393 AC - Hostile Forest Encounter

When frozen taiga gave way to wooded expanses free of snow, Teija found the reawakened forest hostile and bitter through [[The Skein]], discovering [[Three Little Pigs]] [[Eidolons]] struggling with voracious roots in a dark clearing before driving back the lashing plant limbs while the pig engineers fled.

### ~393 AC - Magic Beans Experimentation

Before departing on expedition, Teija received packet of magic beans from [[Arjun]] that could sprout ideas into reality, testing them to create plant-based containers and tents while saving remaining seeds for critical moments.

### 393 AC, June-September - Psychic Interference with Lernaean Hydra
During shadow attack in [[City of Scholars]] caverns, Teija psychically intervened through [[The Skein]] when [[Atsadi]] attempted to summon a bloodthirsty [[Lernaean Hydra]], implanting alternative ideas into his imagination and altering the manifestation.

### 393 AC - Descent into The Crow's Eye

Teija recommended to Admiral [[Temera Singh]] that exploration of [[City of Scholars|the City]] be postponed because [[The Skein]] reacted strangely near [[The Crow's Eye]], as if its network avoided going too deep. Teija descended into the [[Crow's Eye]] with [[Nauraa]] and a team of [[Hellequin]] hunters, some bearing marks of [[The Reapers]], accompanied by [[Noosh Burrowers]] for tunnel excavation. During descent via [[Axiom]] freight elevator, Teija observed [[The Undergrowth]]—a partially buried jungle of tangled roots, gigantic stems, pink mosses, and dark blue geometric blocks veined with gold embedded in vegetation. Teija sensed ripples disturbing perception of [[The Skein]], like interference, and temporarily severed connection to focus.

### 393 AC - Psychic Contact with The Hunger

Teija participated in the Exalt confrontation with [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]]. While [[Axiom]] machines flooded an arena with [[Sap]] to lure the creature, Teija meditated atop one of the floating memory blocks with [[Fen]] at their side and [[Nauraa]] providing protection. Through [[The Skein]], Teija extended consciousness toward the entity, attempting to reach whatever remained within. The dendrites of [[The Skein]] spread around Teija like sea anemone tentacles. Together with [[Fen]], Teija helped establish contact with [[Sofia]], the child consciousness trapped within the monster.
