# Arjun

## Overview

Arjun is a [[Muna]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who specializes in agriculture and cultivation. Through [[The Musubi]] ceremony, they bonded with [[Spike]], a sentient cactus [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who serves as their Alter Ego. Arjun proposed the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] to find a cure for the dying [[The Spindle|Spindle]] and volunteered to lead the expedition beyond [[Asgartha]]'s borders.

## Background and Agricultural Expertise

Arjun comes from a farming family with extensive agricultural lands including niece Flore, brother, grandfather, and various cousins, aunts, and uncles. They are an expert farmer with deep knowledge of multiple agricultural disciplinesÃ¢â‚¬"they grasp handfuls of earth and inhale the humus, identifying good soil that is fertile and fruitful. Arjun uses [[Alteration]] to help crops flourish by extending their perception through [[The Skein]]. When closing their eyes, they can feel the energetic web vibrating with life as it unfurls through them and their garden. Through [[The Skein]], Arjun can sense how different plants interact and detect when plants are under pressure from neighbors. They work with numerous crops including wheat, flax, spelt, endives, alfalfa, tomatoes, zucchini, kale, and many others. Their daily work follows the rhythm of natureÃ¢â‚¬"digging, planting, harvesting, and sometimes taking naps in the shade of weeping willows.

Arjun hums [[Muna]] nursery rhymes as personal ritual whenever planting seeds. They view seed planting as a sacred moment requiring careful preparation of soil furrows to provide nutrients and protection. When soil is ready, Arjun adds touch of [[Mana]] without forcing nature's magic, witnessing germination process as cotyledons open, radicle emerges, and plumule unfurls with golden sparkle. Arjun has shared seeds with [[Teija]], and [[Rin]] found seeds in bottom of Arjun's pots when taking down floral tent before striking camp, deciding to share them as Arjun had done with [[Teija]].

## The Dying Spindle and Meeting Spike

For years, Arjun watched [[The Spindle]] withering away despite all efforts to save it. Arjun tried to find a remedy, traveling across the peninsula in search of a treatment. They invited mushs to spread mycelium on [[The Spindle]]'s bark and applied powerful ointments, but the tree appeared to be missing something. Arjun knew that [[The Spindle]] was a knot in [[The Skein]], and that the entire [[Mana]] web of the peninsula was connected to this tree. Arjun met [[Spike]] while wandering under the canopy of treesÃ¢â‚¬"the small [[Chimerae|Chimera]] was trying to climb an apple tree. Arjun struck the trunk with a stick and an apple fell next to the sentient cactus. After that encounter, [[Spike]] didn't leave Arjun's side, following them and eventually climbing on their shoulders. Over time, [[Spike]] got into the habit of dozing there or in Arjun's hood.

## Bond with Spike and Proposing the Endeavor

During their long wanderings, [[Spike]] provided great comfort to Arjun. Bonding through [[The Musubi]] was an obvious outcome, almost a necessity. One evening during a fireside gathering, Arjun stood up to speakÃ¢â‚¬"to help [[The Spindle]] come back to life, there was no choice but to venture beyond the borders of [[Asgartha]]. Through the veins of [[The Skein]], Arjun felt the unanimous blessing of their peers. Many years separated Arjun's proposal from the great departure, and Arjun went to the capital to help the Expeditionary Corps prepare, providing expertise in hydroponic farming, permaculture, soil regeneration, and biomimicry.

## Preparation for the Expedition

Before leaving, Arjun insisted on taking a few months to see their family again and share in their life. They would humbly take care of turning the soil, planting, and harvesting. They might go with niece Flore to take care of yaks or help Qaaje repair his barn before the rainy season. They would distribute gifts to their family before departure.

## Timeline

### 379 AC - Summoning Mowgli
Arjun set down basket of offerings at old forest threshold and summoned [[Mowgli]], the wild child [[Eidolons|Eidolon]].

### 380 AC - Encounter with Parvati
While bathing in river, [[Parvati]] appeared and placed hand on Arjun's cheek, projecting images showing essences joining.

### 382 AC - Encountering Multi-Headed Hydracaena
While climbing hill, Arjun encountered imposing dracaena creature with multiple heads looking hungry for Arjun's apple.

### 384 AC - Traveling with Kimo
Arjun helped [[Kimo]], a traveling [[Cornucoopia]] merchant, whose carriage wheel got stuck in mud. [[Kimo]] suggested visiting [[The Spindle|Spindle]] Tree for official training.

### 388 AC - Summoning Daughter of Yggdrasil
Village faced food crisis, and Arjun summoned [[Daughter of Yggdrasil]], a sentient tree [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] whose canopy covered in flowers then fruits.

### 392 AC - Creating Hot Spring with Aloe Vera
Arjun tapped into [[Mana]] bits [[Spike]] gathered and materialized natural pool with sulfur-tinged water. [[Aloe Vera]] appeared and played with [[Spike]] in the water.

### 392 AC, October-December - Territorial Dispute with Lindiwe
Arjun wanted to transform scrubland into orchard where [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] could harvest fruit. According to Treaty of Aysun, area was site of Indus Tribe's last camp. [[Lindiwe]] prevented Arjun from claiming the land to protect the sacred burial ground.

### 393 AC, January-March - Caring for Sick Spike
[[Repotter Otter]] repotted fevered [[Spike]] in greenhouse with clay, bark, and soil, removing wilted flowers so new buds could bloom.

### 393 AC, January-March - Rescue Mission with Yeti
[[Spike]] led Arjun on rescue mission where they discovered [[Yeti]] sheltering cold birds against chest in snow.

### 393 AC, January-April - Rescuing Rin and Nilam Discovery at Storvhit
During [[Storvhit]] expedition, [[Spike]] shed form to ride [[The Tumult|Tumult]] currents after summit became singularity. Arjun spotted [[Rin]] stumbling through blizzard toward a sérac edge and rescued her with [[Orchid]]'s help, providing shelter in a grown floral tent. After [[Rin]]'s recovery, Arjun led exploration through cerulean moraine, discovering massive crystallized roots—the fossilized remains of a dead world-tree similar to [[The Spindle]]. Following water course, they discovered the cyclopean trunk of [[The Nilam]], surrounded by [[Mana Moths]] swarms dancing around its sapphire-glowing heart.

### 393 AC, January-April - Seed Sharing Discovery
[[Rin]] found seeds in Arjun's pots when taking down floral tent, and shared them as Arjun had with [[Teija]].

### 393 AC, June-August - Collecting Samples in The Undergrowth
Arjun collected plant samples in [[The Undergrowth]] for [[The Farm]]'s greenhouse and herbarium, gathering fragrant berries and exotic species from thickets and flower beds with petals never seen before, wrapping specimens carefully to avoid damage.

### 393 AC, June-August - Fair Fox Encounter
[[Fair Fox]] appeared to Arjun in [[The Undergrowth]], presenting its Hoshi-No-Tama artifact with outstretched paws—indicating [[Teija]] was nearby and needed help. Arjun mentally informed [[Spike]] they would need to reschedule plans, accepted the artifact, and followed the fox.

### 393 AC, June-August - Escorting Cuddly Corgi to Nilam Spires
Arjun escorted [[Cuddly Corgi]]—one of [[Arawn]]'s hounds sent by [[Teija]] from [[The Crow's Eye]]—to [[Nilam Spires]] to request [[Yzmir]] aid, providing regular [[Mana]] infusions during the journey. Teija's voice had carried fear, a confession urging Arjun to leave the abyss quickly, warning something was "not right" down there.

### 393 AC, June-August - Exchange with Flore at The Farm
Arjun visited [[The Farm]] and met with [[Flore]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] in her vegetable patch, delivering bulbs and plants gathered from [[The Undergrowth]]. In exchange, Flore gave Arjun [[Sap]]-engorged strawberries wrapped in cloth.

### 393 AC, June-August - Cart Ride from The Farm
A gardener offered Arjun a ride in a cart laden with [[Sap]]-cultivated produce—oranges, pomegranates, pumpkins, mushrooms, and flower bouquets—destined for [[The Mess]] and the morning market at the [[Ordis]] town hall square.

### 393 AC, June-September - Sap Discovery in City of Scholars
While taking a break at the [[City of Scholars]] excavation site with [[Spike]] curled up beside them, Arjun conversed with [[Treyst]] about the mysterious substance being drilled. Arjun theorized that the substance was the Sap of the world-tree, which the ancient inhabitants had channeled and exploited until the tree died. Arjun noted a fresco on the wall depicting the world-tree connected to the city below and observed that the Sap's finite supply meant a similar fate awaited any civilization overly dependent on it. When drilling triggered a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity, Arjun was present during the emergency evacuation.
