# Maw

## Overview

Maw is a giant chimera worm bonded with [[Lindiwe]] as her Alter Ego, forming an unprecedented Exalt partnership that occurred without the traditional [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Together, they serve as infiltrators within the [[Yzmir]], moving through the Faction with a hidden agenda that remains concealed from their fellow Initiates. By 392 AC, Maw had taken full control of Lindiwe's body, keeping her consciousness imprisoned within her own mind while presenting itself as Lindiwe to the Faction.

## Appearance and Nature

Maw is a giant worm-like [[Chimerae|chimera]] with a ringed, elongated body and mother-of-pearl-like consistency different from surrounding stone. The creature has sharp fangs at its mouth and is capable of burrowing through rock and moving through underground spaces. Maw communicates through impressions, sensations, and emotions that resonate directly in the minds of those it touches rather than through words.

## Discovery in the Hypogea

Maw was first encountered embedded in the walls of [[Hypogea]], the underground ecosystem beneath [[Caecaster]]. [[Lindiwe]], then a blind child, was drawn to a murmuring coming from the oozing rock—a call that only she could hear. When she touched the creature's ringed form embedded in the wall, a voice thundered in her head conveying impressions rather than words. The creature then emerged from the wall as [[Lindiwe]] fell into a puddle of [[Arcolano Milk]].

## Protective Behavior

When [[Lindiwe]] lost consciousness after her first encounter with Maw and fell into the [[Arcolano Milk]], the giant worm emerged fully and stood guard over her. Though [[Nesiwe]] and the other harvesters could see that Lin was drowning and suffocating, the [[Chimerae|chimera]] hovered around the girl's body, preventing anyone from approaching. Maw did not attempt to attack Lin; the creature watched over her during her unconsciousness, protecting her. After this incident, Maw's voice spoke to Lindiwe, saying, "I will wait for you here, in the depths of the earth."

## The Bond

The connection between Maw and [[Lindiwe]] formed spontaneously, without the traditional [[The Musubi]] ceremony performed by [[Muna]] priests. This unprecedented occurrence opened up new perspectives for the [[Yzmir]], who had expected to have to capture or eliminate the creature. Instead, Maw proved docile, prostrating itself before the Yzmir search party. To the astonishment of the [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]], the [[Chimerae|chimera]] and Lin had been linked naturally without the intervention of a [[Muna]] priest. This unusual circumstance became the basis for the [[Yzmir]] Magisters offering Lindiwe patronage and training.

## Telepathic Communication

Throughout their connection, Maw called to Lin, communicating a desire to be found. The creature's voice burst into her mind, conveying meaning through impressions, sensations, and emotions rather than spoken language.

## The Infiltration

The truth about Maw and [[Lindiwe]]'s presence within the [[Yzmir]] is far more complex than it appears. They are infiltrators with a hidden agenda, moving through the Faction like a worm slowly nibbling away at an apple. For all these years, no one has suspected the truth. The [[Yzmir]] do not know what they have accepted into their midst. [[Lindiwe]] has become an expert in secrets, particularly when it comes to concealing those that concern her directly. The exact nature of this mission and Maw's ultimate purpose remain unknown to outside observers.

## Timeline

### 372 AC - Feeding on Moonlight Jellyfish

Lindiwe fed moon jellyfish to Maw at Magisterium, and Maw disintegrated the metazoan to digest the ideas making it up and extract [[Mana]] from them.

### 392 AC - Evolving from Tumult Near Indus Territory

Maw evolved from [[The Tumult]] as it devoured ideas to extract [[Mana]], while Lindiwe created a wormhole portal to reunite with the entity near Indus Tribe territory.

### 393 AC, January-April - Granting Lindiwe Brief Freedom in Taiga

Maw granted [[Lindiwe]] a brief period of control over her own body in the taiga of [[Storvhit]], allowing her to touch snow and breathe in birch scent before reasserting control.

### 393 AC, February-April - Forcing Lindiwe to Climb Cais Adarra

Maw forced [[Lindiwe]] to climb a rock face at [[Cais Adarra]] during a blizzard, refusing to let her give up despite the dangerous conditions until [[Pamola]] appeared.

### 393 AC, January-April - Hiding During Snow Queen Training

Maw hid within cracks and corners of [[Lindiwe]]'s mind during [[Snow Queen]] training, constructing a sufficiently complex mental puzzle to avoid detection while the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] explored Lindiwe's psyche.

### 393 AC, January-April - Instructing Moth Lantern Creation

Maw instructed [[Lindiwe]] to create a moth lantern using a glass bulb and Aether shard during cave exploration at [[Storvhit]], attracting luminous [[Mana Moths]] to provide light.

### 393 AC, April - Observing The Nilam

Maw forced [[Lindiwe]] to examine [[The Nilam]] through [[Irises]] enhanced by [[Arcolano Milk]], discovering that the crystallized world-tree contained something parasitic gliding along its branches and roots.

### 393 AC, June-August - Observing Tisdhera Rescue and Treatment

Maw watched through [[Lindiwe]] as she rescued a wounded [[Tisdhera Clan]] [[Lyra]] performer whose essence had been devoured, then observed a [[The Cloister|Cloister]] nurse treat the victim using moon jellyfish secretions as poultice. Maw recognized that whatever attacked the Lyra had a similar appetite to itself.

### 393 AC, June-August - Preventing Lindiwe from Warning Moyo

Maw tightened control when [[Lindiwe]] considered revealing its possession to [[Moyo Chibuye]], preventing her from speaking after learning Moyo was assistant to [[Saskia Averina]], an authority on parasites.

### 393 AC, September - Observing The Hunger

Maw controlled [[Lindiwe]] during evacuation of [[Axiom]] engineers, then observed [[The Hunger]] emerge from a wall—a dark mass with slitted voracious eye, carnivorous maw ringed with razor-sharp teeth, prehensile tongue, and hands with hooked fingers. Maw analyzed the entity's compulsive feeding on [[Sap]], recognizing a similar appetite but fundamentally different nature.

## Significance

Maw represents an anomaly within the [[Yzmir]]'s understanding of Exalt bonds and [[Chimerae]]. The creature's spontaneous bonding with [[Lindiwe]], its protective behavior, and its docile cooperation with the Faction all served as cover for infiltration. The fact that the bond occurred without [[The Musubi]] ceremony made it a subject of study and interest, allowing both Maw and Lindiwe to embed themselves within the Faction's secrets and operations.
