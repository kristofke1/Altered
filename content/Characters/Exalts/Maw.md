# Maw

## Overview

Maw is a parasitic worm entity bonded with [[Lindiwe]] as her Alter Ego, forming an unprecedented Exalt partnership that occurred without the traditional [[The Musubi]] ceremony. By 392 AC, Maw had taken full control of Lindiwe's body, keeping her consciousness imprisoned within her own mind while presenting itself as Lindiwe to [[Yzmir]]. According to [[Sylas]], Maw is in a larval state, feeding on its current host before eventually shedding to colonize a new one—a process Sylas described as digesting Lindiwe's psyche. Maw hunts those who could trigger world-scale devastation, and its stated role is to pierce illusions and dispel them.

## Appearance

Maw is a giant worm-like [[Chimerae|chimera]] with a ringed, elongated body and mother-of-pearl-like consistency different from surrounding stone. The creature has sharp fangs at its mouth and is capable of burrowing through rock and moving through underground spaces.

## Background

### Discovery and First Encounter

Maw was first encountered embedded in the walls of [[Hypogea]], the underground ecosystem beneath [[Caecaster]]. [[Lindiwe]], then a blind child, was drawn to sounds coming from the rock—sounds that only she could hear. When she touched the creature's ringed form, a voice communicated in her head, conveying impressions, sensations, and emotions rather than words. Lindiwe lost consciousness and fell into a puddle of [[Arcolano Milk]].

The giant worm emerged fully and positioned itself over her. Though [[Nesiwe]] and the other harvesters could see that Lin was drowning and suffocating, Maw remained around the girl's body, preventing anyone from approaching. [[Nesiwe]] later concluded the creature had watched over Lindiwe rather than attempted to attack her. After this incident, Maw's voice spoke to Lindiwe: "I will wait for you here, in the depths of the earth."

### The Bond with Lindiwe

The [[Yzmir]] organized a hunt lasting many days through uncharted areas of [[Hypogea]]. Maw was waiting for them, calling to Lin throughout, and prostrated itself before the search party. To their astonishment, the [[Chimerae|chimera]] and Lin had been linked without passing through [[The Musubi]] rite. This unprecedented natural bond prompted the [[Yzmir]] Magisters to offer Lindiwe patronage to study the case.

### True Nature and Purpose

[[Sylas]] identified Maw as a "Worm" researching the [[The Spheres|Sixth Sphere]]—a banished power that could trigger a second [[The Confluence|Confluence]]. Maw's stated role is "to pierce illusions and to dispel them." The [[Yzmir]] do not know what they have accepted into their midst, though [[Wanjiru]]'s followers in [[The Qorgan]] are tracking Maw without yet identifying its true nature.

## Timeline

### 372 AC - Feeding on Moonlight Jellyfish

Lindiwe fed moon jellyfish to Maw at Magisterium, and Maw disintegrated the metazoan to digest the ideas and extract [[Mana]].

### 392 AC - Evolving from Tumult Near Indus Territory

Maw evolved from [[The Tumult]] as it devoured ideas to extract [[Mana]], while Lindiwe created a wormhole portal to reunite with the entity.

### 393 AC, January-April - Granting Lindiwe Brief Freedom in Taiga

Maw granted [[Lindiwe]] a brief period of control over her own body in the taiga of [[Storhvit]], allowing her to touch snow and breathe in birch scent before reasserting control.

### 393 AC, February-April - Forcing Lindiwe to Climb Cais Adarra

Maw forced [[Lindiwe]] to climb a rock face at [[Cais Adarra]] during a blizzard, refusing to let her give up despite the dangerous conditions until [[Pamola]] appeared.

### 393 AC, January-April - Hiding During Snow Queen Training

Maw hid within cracks and corners of [[Lindiwe]]'s mind during [[Snow Queen]] training, constructing a mental puzzle to avoid detection while the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] explored Lindiwe's psyche.

### 393 AC, January-April - Instructing Moth Lantern Creation

Maw instructed [[Lindiwe]] to create a moth lantern using a glass bulb and Aether shard during cave exploration at [[Storhvit]], attracting luminous [[Mana Moths]] to provide light.

### 393 AC, April - Observing The Nilam

Maw forced [[Lindiwe]] to examine [[The Nilam]] through [[Irises]] enhanced by [[Arcolano Milk]], discovering that the crystallized world-tree contained something parasitic gliding along its branches and roots.

### 393 AC, June-August - Observing Tisdhera Rescue and Treatment

Maw watched through [[Lindiwe]] as she rescued a wounded [[Tisdhera Clan]] [[Lyra]] performer whose essence had been devoured, then observed a [[The Cloister|Cloister]] nurse treat the victim using moon jellyfish secretions as poultice. Maw recognized that whatever attacked the Lyra had a similar appetite to itself.

### 393 AC, June-August - Preventing Lindiwe from Warning Moyo

Maw tightened control when [[Lindiwe]] considered revealing its possession to [[Moyo Chibuye]], preventing her from speaking after learning Moyo was assistant to [[Saskia Averina]], an authority on parasites.

### 393 AC, September - Observing The Hunger

Maw controlled [[Lindiwe]] during evacuation of [[Axiom]] engineers, then observed [[The Hunger]] emerge from a wall—a dark mass with slitted eye, carnivorous maw, prehensile tongue, and hooked fingers. Maw analyzed the entity's feeding on [[Sap]], recognizing a similar appetite but fundamentally different nature.

### 393 AC - Confrontation with Sylas

[[Kali]] performed choreography with krises and chains while a masked mage approached. Maw reared up, revealing rows of teeth, but the mage identified himself as [[Sylas]] and proposed an alliance against [[Wanjiru]]'s followers in [[The Qorgan]]. Sylas demonstrated knowledge of Maw's research into the [[The Spheres|Sixth Sphere]] and identified Maw as a "Worm" in larval state that would eventually shed its current host. When [[Lindiwe]] briefly seized control and attempted to use [[Shiva]]'s destructive third eye against Sylas, Maw regained control, commanded her to "return to the limbo," and erected extensive mental barriers.
