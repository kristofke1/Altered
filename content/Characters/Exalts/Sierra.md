# Sierra

## Overview

Sierra is an [[Axiom]] Exalt, engineer, and [[Alterers|Alterer]] specializing in [[Constructs]] who serves as vanguard in the Expeditionary Corps. Born within a [[Lyra]] caravan of the Tisdhera Clan, she lost both legs to [[The Tumult|Tumult]] exposure during childhood and now uses prosthetic [[Grafts]]. She is bonded to Oddbal, a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] drone, through [[The Musubi]].

## Early Life and Discovery of Engineering

Sierra was born within a [[Lyra]] caravan, traveling from city to city with her father Efrén, who managed a small shadow theater troupe. While Sierra's caravan traveled the roads of [[Asgartha]], a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] anomaly erupted and Sierra stepped into a pool of it. Her legs chaotically mutated and both had to be amputated. She remained bedridden for many weeks before her father carried her outside again. Efrén brought Sierra to [[Verbera]] during the [[Prodigium]], the [[Axiom]]'s World Exposition, where she was fitted with rudimentary racing blade [[Grafts]] at prosthetist Hamisu's workshop. The [[Prodigium]] captivated her with its technical wonders, particularly a mechanical doll performing dance steps. Sierra visited Hamisu daily to learn about mechanics, pyrotechnics, and kelonic batteries. Hamisu taught Sierra the basics to qualify as an Apprentice, then she traveled to [[Arkaster]] and secured a position at [[The Foundry]].

## Career at the Foundry

Sierra arrived at [[The Foundry]] as a new Apprentice. She participated in a competition for spots in [[The Fulcrum]] but was not selected despite one of the Three Little Pig [[Eidolons]] nodding approvingly at her work. Following rejection, Sierra worked to learn metalworking and [[Alteration]]. After six years, Sierra joined the Parallax, the [[Axiom]] Guild working on [[Alteration]]. After eleven years of apprenticeship, she achieved Journeyman status by presenting [[Automata]] as tribute to her father's puppeteer profession.

## Bond with Oddbal

Sierra met Oddbal when the drone transformed into a [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. Studying the creature awakened curiosity about what existed beyond [[Asgartha]]'s borders. They connected through [[The Musubi]] to deepen their friendship.

## Timeline

### 379 AC - Arriving at The Foundry
Sierra arrived at [[The Foundry]] on tram. The structure was anchored to cliff with metal beams and workshops filled with clanging forges.

### 383 AC - Working with Ogun
Sierra worked with [[Ogun]] on metalworking, raising temperature using [[Alteration]] while he forged metal, and he told her she had talent for [[Alteration]].

### ~383 AC - Brassbug from Gibil
Sierra observed [[Gibil]] working with lava at [[The Foundry]] furnaces, and when the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] restored a dying [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] by forging new parts from molten metal, he placed the reborn creature in her arms to care for.

### 385 AC - Operator Duel
Sierra participated in [[Operators|Operator]] duel testing [[Constructs|Construct]] manipulation, creating lightning rod, circular saw on rail, before opponent's Kelon burst dematerialized her creation.

### 386 AC - Drone Transformation into Oddbal
Sierra sent drone into [[The Tumult]] for data collection, and when Irun connected damaged drone to charging station, it screeched, molted metal, and cavorted around Sierra like puppy.

### 388 AC - Ada Lovelace and Automata Operating System
Sierra summoned [[Ada Lovelace]] to review assembly plans for using [[Alteration]] modules as [[Automata]] operating system, and Ada tested module successfully on robotic head.

### 389 AC - Racing Blade Design Workshop

Sierra worked on assembly plans for new racing blade prosthetics in her workshop while listening to [[Ankita Struna]]'s music, assisted by a curious [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] that analyzed her blueprints and provided tools from its dorsal compartments.

### 390 AC - FarFetch Mk-1 Prototype Testing
Sierra tested FarFetch Mk-1 [[Automata]] with telescopic grapples, and it self-corrected after missing target with first grapple by hitting mark with second.

### 392 AC - Workshop Leadership with Aberdeen
Sierra ran her own workshop at [[The Foundry]] with Apprentice Aberdeen, investigating FarFetch malfunction that burned [[Subhash Kadis]]'s beard during explosive test.

### 392 AC - Field Operations with Oddbal
During [[The Tumult]] expedition, Sierra crossed oily lake separated from Oddbal and created Salvager [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] at his location through bond to restore damaged armor.

### 393 AC - Expedition into The Tumult
Sierra participated in Expeditionary Corps mission into [[The Tumult]] regions hundreds of miles beyond [[Asgartha]], traveling in armored vehicle with caterpillar tracks toward regions rich in [[Kelon]] and rare minerals. She operated in competitive dynamic with [[Akesha Adhikari]] who sought to claim territory for [[Yzmir]]. Sierra used [[Tumult Jammer]] device to monitor parasitic ideas on her display while scouting ahead on her running blades, imbuing herself with concept of "Speed" through [[Alteration]].

### 393 AC - Discovery of Storvhit
Sierra discovered [[Storvhit]], a towering mountain with snow-gleaming summit encircled by grand halo and strange light emanating from fissure near peak. The mountain was suffused with ideas of "Snow," "Frost," "Ice" - possibly an Oasis. The mountain's name resonated with her own.

### 393 AC - Reprocessor Repair with Vishvakarma
Sierra summoned [[Vishvakarma]] to repair a Reprocessor armored vehicle damaged by falling ice stalactites and scree at [[Storvhit]], with the four-armed [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] scanning assembly plans and working in a shower of sparks while she reconfigured her [[Constructs|Construct]] to power the manifestation.

### 393 AC - Automaton Salvage at Storvhit Gorge
Sierra retrieved her [[Automata|Automaton]] after it tumbled down a gorge at [[Storvhit]], salvaging parts from an old armored Scrambler while [[Brassbugs]] welded a new chest plate and she promised to give the revived machine a proper name once fully repaired.

### 393 AC - Tumult Tsunami Survival
[[Oddball]] warned Sierra of approaching tsunami through Morse code via their spiritual bond. Massive wintry wave originated from [[Storvhit]], and Sierra summoned [[Skadi]] and created grappling gauntlet through [[Alteration]] to reach her vehicle before impact. The tsunami struck the vehicle violently, flipping it, and parasitic ideas manifested inside the cabin. Sierra fired distress flare, and [[Akesha Adhikari]] responded from few kilometers away. Sierra summoned [[Little Match Girl]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to banish cold while awaiting rescue.

### 393 AC - Avalanche Rescue Operation
When an avalanche buried [[The Aegis|Aegis]] soldiers at [[Storvhit]], Sierra dispatched [[Oddball]] to drop location beacons on victims while emitting distress signals, then transformed her prostheses into snowblades using [[Alteration]] and set off to assist rescue efforts.

### 393 AC - Reprocessor Cliff Stabilization
Sierra used [[Alteration]] to apply the concept of sturdiness to cracking rock and materialize metallic struts when the Reprocessor armored vehicle nearly slid off a cliff edge at [[Storvhit]], buying time for the [[Three Little Pigs]] [[Eidolons]] steering the vehicle to reverse to safety.

### 393 AC - Mysterious Relic Examination
Sierra collaborated with [[Treyst]] examining human-origin machine extracted from ice at Cobalt Gorge. Conducted scans revealing no internal circuitry, electric networks, or kelonic systems. Analyzed organic metal fusion properties and surface channel architecture suggesting external liquid fuel flow system with yellowish residue. Provided technical insights during [[Treyst]]'s disassembly process.

### 393 AC - Archaeological Research and Letter to Efrén
While resting at [[Storvhit]] Oasis, Sierra conducted soil analysis of Cais Adarra revealing milder climate century ago similar to [[Tilted Steppes]], with sudden glaciation possibly using [[Alteration]]. Excavations around [[The Nilam]] revealed pre-glaciation human settlement including rusted tools, troglodyte dwellings, fabric, and pottery dating at least two hundred years prior, postdating [[The Confluence]]. Colony of several hundred people abandoned site before glaciation despite advanced technology surpassing [[Axiom]] Industrial Revolution by hundreds of years. Mysterious relic analysis revealed resilient alloy not matching Asgarthan materials, surface channels, mysterious cavities, and engine managing fluid without combustion system. Sierra wrote letter to father [[Efrén]] via [[The Talaria]] detailing discoveries and mentioning caravan companions Faarsan and Argiris. Letter expressed excitement about potential meeting with descendants of [[The Lost Tribe]] or other [[Tumult Nomads]].

### 393 AC - Fluid Research Discussion with Subhash
Sierra met [[Subhash Kadis]] at his workbenches near [[The Screed]]'s chasm elevators to discuss research on the mysterious fluid discovered beneath the [[City of Scholars]]. Bash explained the fluid had confirmed energy properties and was stored in underground silos and tanks, but they possessed only one working machine and could not replicate it. He mentioned rumors the fluid might be organic and suggested recruiting [[Muna]] botanists, specifically [[Arjun]], to help analyze it.

### 393 AC - Arrival of The Wayfarer and Reunion with Efrén
While speaking with [[Subhash Kadis]] at [[The Screed]], Sierra spotted [[The Wayfarer]], the Sahanka of the [[Tisdhera Clan]], emerging from behind clouds above the crater cliffs. As gondolas descended, someone called her name—a voice she recognized immediately. Sierra embraced her father [[Efrén]] tightly, emotions overwhelming her after ten months apart. She noticed details like the curls of his mustache and a few more white strands in his hair.

### 393 AC, June-August - Creating Designer Drone Assistant
Missing [[Aberdeen]] both personally and professionally, Sierra created a jellyfish-like assistant drone with "Magnum 6" cryptex—the most complex she had ever developed—capable of analyzing blueprints and indicating areas for improvement.

### 393 AC, June-August - Grems Sabotage at The Refinery
Sierra replaced [[Subhash Kadis]] at [[The Refinery]] owing him a favor, and discovered three [[The Grems|Grems]] attacking a regulator panel that had shut down [[Sap]] delivery for hours.

### 393 AC, June-August - Enhanced Prototype Development
Sierra worked on an [[Automata|Automaton]] prototype requiring proto-consciousness, replacing [[Storvhit]] parts with new components and adding a stabilizer that enabled it to run.

### 393 AC, June-August - Sap Overflow Extraction
Sierra located [[Sap]] beyond a wall in [[The Undergrowth]] using sonar and directed [[Oddball]] to fire a focused ray, rupturing the wall and releasing a [[Sap]] geyser that teams collected with pumps.

### 393 AC, June-August - Preparing Bug-Out Bag for Depths
Sierra prepared a bug-out bag for deep exploration, reflecting on [[Treyst]]'s revelation from [[Arjun]] that [[Sap]] came from a world-tree, while a [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] hid in the bag to accompany her.

### 393 AC, June-August - Exploring City of Scholars with Efrén
Sierra descended into the [[City of Scholars]] on a one-wheeler while [[Efrén]] kept pace using wires, discovering her father's powerful [[Ignescence]] and ability to summon combat-equipped puppet [[Eidolons]] he had stored long ago.


## Skills and Abilities

Sierra is a goldsmith whose hands work with precision to craft and create. She is a talented [[Alterers|Alterer]] expert in manipulating [[Constructs]], the modules used by [[Axiom]] to control [[Alteration]]. She can rearrange her [[Constructs|Construct]] to combine symbols, creating complex effects. During [[Operators|Operator]] duels, she demonstrated ability to create defensive and offensive constructs.

Sierra can imbue herself with concepts through [[Alteration]], such as "Speed" to lengthen strides and blur landscape. She can channel concepts into devices like "Sealing" into [[Tumult Jammer]] or "Dampening" for protection. She creates complex constructs through [[Alteration]] including grappling gauntlet with hook, gears, cogs, and steel plates that interlock seamlessly.

She uses monitor device to track parasitic Tumult ideas in environment. Her running blade prosthetics allow her to traverse difficult rocky terrain with rhythmic clicking sound.
