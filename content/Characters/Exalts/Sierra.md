# Sierra

## Overview

Sierra, born 368 AC, is an [[Axiom]] Exalt, engineer, and [[Alterers|Alterer]] specializing in [[Constructs]] manipulation who serves as vanguard in the Expeditionary Corps. Born within a [[Lyra]] caravan of the [[Tisdhera Clan]], she lost both legs to [[The Tumult|Tumult]] exposure during childhood and now uses prosthetic [[Grafts]]. She is bonded to [[Oddball]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] drone, through [[The Musubi]].

## Background

Sierra was born within a [[Lyra]] caravan, traveling from city to city with her father [[Efrén]], who managed a small shadow theater troupe. While the caravan traveled the roads of [[Asgartha]], a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] anomaly erupted and Sierra stepped into a pool of it. Her legs chaotically mutated and both had to be amputated. She remained bedridden for many weeks before her father carried her outside again. [[Efrén]] brought Sierra to [[Verbera]] during the [[Prodigium]], the [[Axiom]]'s World Exposition, where she was fitted with rudimentary racing blade [[Grafts]] at prosthetist Hamisu's workshop. Sierra visited Hamisu daily to learn about mechanics, pyrotechnics, and kelonic batteries. When the caravan prepared to depart, [[Efrén]] offered Sierra the choice to continue with the caravan or pursue technical apprenticeship—she chose to stay. Hamisu taught Sierra the basics to qualify as an Apprentice, then she traveled to [[Arkaster]] and secured a position at [[The Foundry]].

Sierra participated in a competition for spots in [[The Fulcrum]] but was not selected despite one of the [[Three Little Pigs]] [[Eidolons]] nodding approvingly at her work. Following rejection, Sierra worked to learn metalworking and [[Alteration]]. After six years, Sierra joined [[The Parallax]], the [[Axiom]] Guild working on [[Alteration]]. After eleven years of apprenticeship, she achieved Journeyman status by presenting [[Automata]] as tribute to her father's puppeteer profession.

Sierra met [[Oddball]] when her drone transformed into a [[Chimerae|Chimera]], and they connected through [[The Musubi]].

## Timeline

### 379 AC - Arriving at The Foundry
Sierra arrived at [[The Foundry]] on tram, the structure anchored to cliff with metal beams and workshops filled with clanging forges.

### 383 AC - Working with Ogun
Sierra worked with [[Ogun]] on metalworking, raising temperature using [[Alteration]] while he forged metal, and he told her she had talent for [[Alteration]].

### 383 AC - Brassbug from Gibil
[[Gibil]] placed a restored [[Brassbugs|Brassbug]] in Sierra's arms to care for after forging new parts from molten metal at [[The Foundry]] furnaces.

### 385 AC - Operator Duel
Sierra participated in [[Operators|Operator]] duel testing [[Constructs|Construct]] manipulation, creating lightning rod and circular saw on rail before opponent's [[Kelon]] burst dematerialized her creation.

### 386 AC - Drone Transformation into Oddball
Sierra's drone transformed into [[Oddball]] after returning damaged from [[The Tumult]] data collection—it screeched, molted metal, and cavorted around Sierra when connected to charging station.

### 388 AC - Ada Lovelace and Automata Operating System
Sierra summoned [[Ada Lovelace]] to review assembly plans for using [[Alteration]] modules as [[Automata]] operating system, and Ada tested module successfully on robotic head.

### 389 AC - Racing Blade Design Workshop
Sierra worked on assembly plans for new racing blade prosthetics in her workshop while listening to [[Ankita Struna]]'s music.

### 390 AC - FarFetch Mk-1 Prototype Testing
Sierra tested FarFetch Mk-1 [[Automata]] with telescopic grapples, and it self-corrected after missing target with first grapple by hitting mark with second.

### 392 AC - Workshop Leadership with Aberdeen
Sierra ran her own workshop at [[The Foundry]] with Apprentice [[Aberdeen]], investigating FarFetch malfunction that burned [[Subhash Kadis]]'s beard during explosive test.

### 392 AC - Field Operations with Oddball
During [[The Tumult]] expedition, Sierra crossed oily lake separated from [[Oddball]] and created Salvager [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] at his location through bond to restore damaged armor.

### 393 AC, January - Expedition into The Tumult
Sierra participated in Expeditionary Corps mission into [[The Tumult]] regions hundreds of miles beyond [[Asgartha]], traveling in armored vehicle with caterpillar tracks toward regions rich in [[Kelon]] and rare minerals. She operated alongside [[Akesha Adhikari]], who was claiming territory for [[Yzmir]]. Sierra used [[Tumult Jammer]] device to monitor parasitic ideas while scouting ahead on her running blades.

### 393 AC, January - Discovery of Storvhit
Sierra discovered [[Storhvit]], a towering mountain with snow-gleaming summit encircled by grand halo and strange light emanating from fissure near peak. The mountain was suffused with ideas of "Snow," "Frost," "Ice"—possibly an Oasis.

### 393 AC, January-April - Reprocessor Repair with Vishvakarma
Sierra summoned [[Vishvakarma]] to repair a Reprocessor armored vehicle damaged by falling ice stalactites and scree at [[Storhvit]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Automaton Salvage at Storvhit Gorge
Sierra retrieved her [[Automata|Automaton]] after it tumbled down a gorge at [[Storhvit]], salvaging parts from an old armored Scrambler while [[Brassbugs]] welded a new chest plate.

### 393 AC, March - Tumult Tsunami Survival
[[Oddball]] warned Sierra of approaching tsunami through Morse code via their spiritual bond. Massive wintry wave originated from [[Storhvit]], and Sierra summoned [[Skaði]] and created grappling gauntlet through [[Alteration]] to reach her vehicle before impact. The tsunami struck violently, flipping the vehicle, and parasitic ideas manifested inside the cabin. Sierra fired distress flare and [[Akesha Adhikari]] responded from few kilometers away. Sierra summoned [[Little Match Girl]] to banish cold while awaiting rescue.

### 393 AC, January-April - Avalanche Rescue Operation
When an avalanche buried [[The Aegis|Aegis]] soldiers at [[Storhvit]], Sierra dispatched [[Oddball]] to drop location beacons on victims while emitting distress signals, then transformed her prostheses into snowblades using [[Alteration]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Reprocessor Cliff Stabilization
Sierra used [[Alteration]] to apply the concept of sturdiness to cracking rock and materialize metallic struts when the Reprocessor nearly slid off a cliff edge at [[Storhvit]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Mysterious Relic Examination
Sierra collaborated with [[Treyst]] examining human-origin machine extracted from ice at Cobalt Gorge, conducting scans revealing no internal circuitry, electric networks, or kelonic systems.

### 393 AC, April-May - Archaeological Research and Letter to Efrén
While resting at [[Storhvit]] Oasis, Sierra conducted soil analysis of Cais Adarra revealing milder climate a century ago similar to [[Tilted Steppes]], with sudden glaciation possibly through [[Alteration]]. Excavations around [[The Nilam]] revealed pre-glaciation human settlement including rusted tools, troglodyte dwellings, fabric, and pottery dating at least two hundred years prior. Colony of several hundred people abandoned site before glaciation despite advanced technology surpassing [[Axiom]] Industrial Revolution. Sierra wrote letter to father [[Efrén]] via [[The Talaria]] detailing discoveries.

### 393 AC, June-September - Fluid Research Discussion with Subhash
Sierra met [[Subhash Kadis]] at his workbenches near [[The Screed]]'s chasm elevators to discuss research on the mysterious fluid discovered beneath the [[City of Scholars]].

### 393 AC, July - Arrival of The Wayfarer and Reunion with Efrén
While speaking with [[Subhash Kadis]] at [[The Screed]], Sierra spotted [[The Wayfarer]], the Sahanka of the [[Tisdhera Clan]], emerging from behind clouds above the crater cliffs and was reunited with her father [[Efrén]].

### 393 AC, June-August - Creating Designer Drone Assistant
Sierra created a jellyfish-like assistant drone with "Magnum 6" cryptex capable of analyzing blueprints and indicating areas for improvement.

### 393 AC, June-August - Grems Sabotage at The Refinery
Sierra discovered three [[The Grems|Grems]] attacking a regulator panel at [[The Refinery]] that had shut down [[Sap]] delivery for hours.

### 393 AC, June-August - Enhanced Prototype Development
Sierra worked on an [[Automata|Automaton]] prototype requiring proto-consciousness, replacing [[Storhvit]] parts with new components and adding a stabilizer that enabled it to run.

### 393 AC, June-August - Sap Overflow Extraction
Sierra located [[Sap]] beyond a wall in [[The Undergrowth]] using sonar and directed [[Oddball]] to fire a focused ray, rupturing the wall and releasing a [[Sap]] geyser.

### 393 AC, June-August - Preparing for Deep Exploration
Sierra prepared a bug-out bag for deep exploration after learning from [[Treyst]] that [[Arjun]] had revealed [[Sap]] came from a world-tree.

### 393 AC, June-August - Exploring City of Scholars with Efrén
Sierra descended into the [[City of Scholars]] on a one-wheeler while [[Efrén]] kept pace using wires and summoned combat-equipped puppet [[Eidolons]] powered by his [[Ignescence]].

### 393 AC, June-September - Lucan Attack on Aerolith Convoy
During [[Lucan]]'s attack on an [[Aerolith]] convoy, Sierra raced to convince the captain to dump the ore cargo targeted by the [[Leviathans|Leviathan]].

## Abilities

Sierra specializes in [[Constructs]] manipulation and [[Alteration]], using these disciplines to engineer [[Automata]], modify equipment, and apply conceptual properties to materials (such as sturdiness to rock). She operates prosthetic racing blade [[Grafts]] that can be transformed via [[Alteration]] into snowblades or other configurations. Through [[The Musubi]] bond with [[Oddball]], she can create [[Eidolons]] at his remote location. She can summon [[Eidolons]] including [[Ada Lovelace]], [[Ogun]], [[Skaði]], [[Little Match Girl]], [[Vishvakarma]], [[Gibil]], and [[Three Little Pigs]].
