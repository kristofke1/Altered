# Akesha Adhikari

## Overview

Akesha Adhikari (called "Kesh"), born 375 AC, is a [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates|Initiate]] and hero who specializes in [[Eidomancy]] and [[Horomancy]], with additional proficiency in [[Kinemancy]]. She is bonded with [[Taru]], a luminescent octopus-like creature from [[The Tumult]], through [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Akesha seeks a cure for [[Remanence]], the affliction that has stricken her younger sister [[Kumari Adhikari]].

## Background

### Early Life in Kuth Kanat

Akesha was born in an ornate troglodyte palazzo in the aristocratic district of [[Kuth Kanat]]. She was trained from a young age for a political vocation, attending classes taught by tutors in music, dance, history, management, and etiquette. With the birth of [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]], three years her junior, Kesh became her sister's primary caregiver while their parents were occupied with diplomatic duties. The two girls would sneak out of the palazzo and climb [[The Kadigir]]'s enclosure wall, exploring magical gardens and becoming known to [[The Magisterium]]'s teaching staff.

### The Well of Tears Accident

During one escapade, the sisters stumbled upon a breach in rock and followed carved chutes where water flowed upward, unknowingly entering [[The Well of Tears]], the sacred sanctuary of [[Yzmir]]. Beneath the underground lake, luminescent shapes moved—images that intertwined and combined. [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]] stepped toward the edge to touch the spectral emanations but slipped into the dark iridescent waters. Kesh rushed forward grabbing her sister's hand, but her own steps skidded on mossy walls. The [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] [[Nimue]] appeared and seized Kumari, pulling her from the icy waters.

[[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]] had ingested water, absorbing large amounts of pure [[The Aether|Ether]]. Neither [[Baku]] nor [[Caladrius]] could fully restore her mental health, and she remained subject to visions and bouts of madness, interned within [[The Cloister]] at [[The Kadigir]].

### Joining the Yzmir

Akesha joined the ranks of [[Yzmir]] Disciples after the accident, dedicating herself to the study of esoteric arts. She had the support of [[Kuwat Vasanti]], an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] and former Magister of the [[Eidomancy]] Sphere, who became her official mentor. Under Vasanti's patronage, she trained while reserving periods to visit her interned sister. She became the youngest apprentice to receive a master's robe at fourteen years old.

### Meeting Taru

During an experiment using [[The Aether|Aether]], the veil between worlds thinned and a creature slipped through—[[Taru]], a luminescent octopus with translucent tentacles. [[Taru]] was injured, and Akesha helped heal the creature. Over weeks, they bonded through [[The Musubi]], synchronizing their essences. Through this bond, [[Taru]] can sense Akesha's emotions and intentions.

## Timeline

### 378 AC - Meeting Lindiwe Through Taru

[[Taru]] sensed presence through Veil and introduced Akesha to [[Lindiwe]], who was using [[Eidomancy]] to help her see despite blindness.

### 383 AC - Exploring Kadigir Gardens with Kumari

Akesha and [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]] snuck into [[The Kadigir]] gardens, hiding from students and teachers while exploring groves and orchards.

### 384 AC - Seeking Alchemist's Cure

Akesha brought saved Florets to Kadigiran alchemist [[Takao Cordisco]], describing [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]]'s illness and requesting a potion or ointment to heal her.

### 385 AC - Meeting Alice

After [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]]'s crisis, [[Alice]] appeared through wall and dragged Akesha through ground, showing her the [[The Aether|Aether]] world.

### 387 AC - Magical Training with Aether Fragment

Akesha held [[The Aether|Aether]] fragment against chest to summon ideas from collective unconscious, attempting to summon [[White Rabbit]] to steal time for [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]].

### 391 AC - Monitoring Black Wave with Baba Yaga

Akesha was assigned to monitor [[The Black Wave]] alongside [[Baba Yaga]] from isba on chicken legs.

### 391 AC - Summoning Sakarabru

When [[The Black Wave]] quivered and aberrations emerged, Akesha summoned [[Sakarabru]] who roared and scattered chimeric monsters.

### 391 AC - Banishing Chimaera Through Portal

[[Taru]] wrapped sticky legs around monster's head while Akesha traced Signs to slow time, summoned portal, and banished aberration.

### 392 AC, September - Underwater Rescue of Aegis Soldiers

Akesha dove beneath surface during [[Kraken]] battle, summoned portal to dry land, and rescued [[The Aegis|Aegis]] soldiers swallowed by waves.

### 392 AC, September - Observing Celestial Blast Technique

Akesha observed [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]] and [[Senka]]'s technique of making fire rain from sky.

### 392 AC, October-December - Bureaucratic Obstruction

[[Waru Toowoom]] blocked [[The Kadigir]] Mages' authority to maintain [[The Mandates|Mandates]] order aboard [[The Ouroboros]].

### 393 AC - Caladrius Triage After Mining Accident

Akesha maintained composure as a carrier delivered wounded to [[The Ouroboros]]'s crowded deck after a mining accident. She watched medics perform triage, sorting patients by injury severity. [[Caladrius]] flew between critical patients giving verdicts—if it looked away, nothing could be done; if it stared, doctors treated the victim. Akesha held the hand of a dying miner whose breathing grew raspy after Caladrius departed, staying by his side while [[Taru]] sent kind thoughts as he breathed his last.

### 393 AC, February - Mana Moths Chrysalides Deployment

Akesha arrived through portal during [[Belisenki]] night assault on [[Ordis]] convoy at [[Storhvit]], bringing [[Initiates]] and [[Eidolons]] as reinforcements. She unfurled cloth revealing collection of empty [[Mana Moths]] cocoons made of ice/crystal with traces of blue liquid, instructing soldiers including [[Gulrang Oduro]] to consume the chrysalides quickly. After consumption, [[Belisenki]] ceased attacking and departed, no longer perceiving expedition members as intruders due to absorbed pheromones.

### 393 AC, February - Belisenki Pheromone Hypothesis Testing

Akesha tested the hypothesis that [[Belisenki]] would protect beings exuding moth pheromones by ingesting a [[Mana Moths|chrysalis]] and exposing herself to a Belasenka, confirming the protection mechanism worked.

### 393 AC, February - Greenhouse Research Visit

Akesha visited multi-biome greenhouse at [[Storhvit]] base to deliver new plant specimen. She encountered [[Saskia Averina]] conducting [[Mana Moths]] research with [[Moyo Chibuye]], observing chrysalides attached to branches and witnessing moth hatching under glass dome. [[Saskia Averina|Saskia]] explained moths emit [[Mana]] continuously after emerging from cocoon and described moths as creatures of pure idea with [[Mana]] dissipating over brief lifespan.

### 393 AC, February - Magpeng Theft and Icy Containment

After magpeng stole maple syrup supplies, duty officer's hat, and [[Fen]]'s scarf, Akesha followed webbed footprints to confront thief and blasted it with icy gust sealing creature in thick layer of ice.

### 393 AC, February - Mana Moths Attraction

At [[Storhvit]], Akesha observed [[Mana Moths]] swirling in flight diffusing [[Mana]] into air, then raised hand with [[Mana]] drop allowing moth to land and sip liquid.

### 393 AC, February - Summoning Aroro During Storm

During [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]]'s blizzard attack at [[Storhvit]], Akesha watched as something took possession of him, then summoned [[Aroro Niski]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to contain devastation.

### 393 AC, February - Mana Moths Manifestation Technique

Akesha combined [[Kinemancy]] with [[Taru]] using ballet movements to manifest swarm of moths by studying their behavior patterns and anatomy using Irises, with each pirouette gathering more moths into compact swarms crafted from ideas and [[Mana]].

### 393 AC, March 26 - Letter to Kumari from Storvhit Ascent

Akesha wrote letter to [[Kumari Adhikari]] from deep within [[Storhvit]] heart describing conditions and expedition progress. She noted proximity to [[Oases|Oasis]] center where expedition would shelter until [[The Tumult|Tumult]] hurricane subsides, and mentioned spending time talking with [[Saskia Averina]] who might help find cure for [[Kumari Adhikari]]'s condition.

### 393 AC, June-August - Encounter with Sylas at Nilam Spires

While searching for [[Saskia Averina]] at [[Nilam Spires]], Akesha pushed open a workshop door and found [[Sylas]] sitting in the center of the room covered in Sigils while a [[Gephyromancy|Gephyromancer]] tattooist applied [[The Aether|Aether]] patterns. Sylas spread his arms in welcome; Akesha left and encountered Saskia outside.

### 393 AC, June-August - Crow's Eye Descent Advice

Three nights before [[Teija]]'s descent into [[The Crow's Eye]], Akesha told Teija "You never know what you'll find when you fall down the rabbit hole."

### 393 AC, June-August - BLISS! Concert and Cure Lead

Akesha sat with [[Saskia Averina]] on terrace at [[The Screed]] watching [[BLISS!]] perform. She learned [[Moyo Chibuye]] left [[Nilam Spires]] to descend into [[City of Scholars]] to test connection with [[Mana Moths]]. Saskia revealed researchers found still-living larva inside [[The Nilam]]'s bark, now grown from fist-size to medium dog after being raised in lab. Saskia indicated she may have found lead to help heal [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]].

### 393 AC, June-September - Merlin's Involuntary Manifestation

[[Merlin]] materialized before Akesha in the [[City of Scholars]] labyrinth by siphoning her memories without permission and delivered a prophecy: "Destiny is in motion... The king will awaken, and you will play your part."

### 393 AC, June-September - Treating Remanence Attack in Labyrinth

While traveling with two [[Bravos]] warriors (covert [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] agents) left by [[Afanas Korovinovich]], Akesha applied experimental leeches from [[Saskia Averina]] to treat a pendekar suffering a [[Remanence]] attack.

### 393 AC, September - Slowing The Maze's Collapse

Akesha participated in the Exalt operation against [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]]. When [[Treyst]] triggered explosive charges that detonated the [[Aerolith]] cores of the [[Tesseracts]] in a chain reaction, Akesha used her abilities to slow the collapse while [[Yzmir]] mages opened portals to evacuate the Exalts.
