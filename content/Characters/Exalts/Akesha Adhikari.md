# Akesha Adhikari

## Overview

Akesha Adhikari, affectionately called "Kesh," is [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates|Initiate]] and hero who specializes in [[Eidomancy]] and [[Horomancy]]. Bonded with [[Taru]], luminescent octopus-like creature from [[The Tumult]], forming Exalt partnership. Driven by mission to find cure for [[Remanence]], affliction that has stricken younger sister [[Kumari Adhikari]].

## Early Life and the Well of Tears Accident

Akesha was born in an ornate troglodyte palazzo in the aristocratic district of [[Kuth Kanat 1]]. Trained from young age for political vocation, attending classes taught by tutors in music, dance, history, management, and etiquette. With birth of [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]], three years her junior, Kesh became her sister's primary caregiver, as their parents were occupied with diplomatic duties.

Two girls would sneak out of palazzo and climb [[The Kadigir]]'s enclosure wall, exploring magical gardens, gradually becoming darlings of [[The Magisterium]]'s teaching staff. During one escapade, stumbled upon breach in rock and followed carved chutes where water flowed upward. Unknowingly had entered [[The Well of Tears]], sacred sanctuary of [[Yzmir]].

Beneath underground lake, luminescent shapes danced languidlyâ€”evanescent and hypnotic images that intertwined and combined. [[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]] stepped toward edge to touch spectral emanations but slipped into dark iridescent waters. Kesh rushed forward grabbing sister's hand but own steps skidded on mossy walls. Presence appearedâ€”woman of great beauty holding immense swordâ€”[[Eidolons|Eidolon]] effortlessly seized little Kumari, pulling her from icy waters.

[[Kumari Adhikari|Kumari]] had ingested water, absorbing large amounts of pure [[The Aether|Ether]]. Neither [[Baku]] nor [[Caladrius]] could fully restore mental health, and she remained subject to visions and bouts of gentle madness, interned within [[The Cloister]] at [[The Kadigir]].

## Joining the Yzmir and Rapid Advancement

Akesha joined ranks of [[Yzmir]] Disciples after the accident, fervently dedicating herself to study of esoteric arts. Had support of [[Kuwat Vasanti]], [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] and former Magister of [[Eidomancy]] Sphere, who became official mentor. Under Vasanti's patronage, trained hard, reserving rare periods to visit interned sister. Became youngest apprentice to receive master's robe at just fourteen years old. Many saw her quickly ascending to [[The Magisterium]] thanks to tact and impeccable manners.

[[Taru]] was injured and terrified, but Akesha helped heal creature. Over weeks, built trust, and eventually bonded through [[The Musubi]], synchronizing essences. Through this bond, [[Taru]] could sense Akesha's emotions and intentions.

## Abilities

### Alteration Spheres

Specializes in [[Eidomancy]] and [[Horomancy]], allowing glimpses into future and manipulation of time itself. Made time specialization trademark, consistently giving slight edge over peers. Can trace Signs in air to slow down time, summon portals, teleport beings, and channel pure ideas from collective unconscious.

Combines [[Kinemancy]] with [[Taru]] for manifestation techniques. Uses Irises (enhanced vision from [[Horomancy]]) to analyze and deconstruct observed techniques for reproduction.

### Combat and Support Techniques

Can summon [[Eidolons]] including [[Sakarabru]] and [[Aroro Niski]]. Creates portals for rescue operations and rapid deployment. Traces Signs to slow time during combat. Combines dance choreography with [[Kinemancy]] to manifest [[Mana Moths]]. Manipulates ice through [[Kinemancy]] for containment purposes.
