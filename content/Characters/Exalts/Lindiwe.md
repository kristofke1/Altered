# Lindiwe

## Overview

Lindiwe, often called "Lin," is a [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates|Initiate]] who was born blind but gained the ability to perceive the world through [[Eidomancy]] by directly welcoming ideas into her mind. She is bonded with [[Maw]], a giant chimera worm, in an unprecedented Exalt partnership that formed without the traditional [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

By 392 AC, [[Maw]] had taken full control of Lindiwe's body, manipulating her through a psychic connection while keeping her consciousness imprisoned within her own mind. [[Maw]] presents itself as Lindiwe to the [[Yzmir]], operating as an infiltrator with a hidden agenda. The actual Lindiwe remains trapped, unable to call for help or reveal the true nature of their bond.

Prior to this complete possession, Lindiwe had built a reputation as an expert in secrets, hunting plots while protecting the Faction's mysteries.

## Early Life and Discovery of Eidomantic Sight

Lindiwe grew up in [[Caecaster]], born blind into a world of sounds, smells, textures, and temperatures. Her mother [[Nesiwe]] worked in [[Hypogea]], the underground world beneath [[Caecaster]], harvesting [[Arcolano Milk]], a psychotropic substance [[Alterers]] used to enhance abilities. One day in [[Hypogea]], Lin was drawn to murmuring from the oozing rock and discovered a worm embedded in the wall with sharp fangs. A voice thundered in her head with impressions and emotions, then darkness enveloped her. She fell into a puddle of [[Arcolano Milk]] and saw flashes of light for the first time. The worm protected her body from others while she was unconscious. When she deliberately drank [[Arcolano Milk]] again, she confirmed she could see—[[Yzmir]] [[Initiates]] revealed she perceived through [[Eidomancy]], welcoming ideas directly into her mind rather than using her eyes. The [[Yzmir]] offered to train her if she helped them track the chimera worm.

## The Bond with Maw

The hunt for the worm lasted many days through uncharted areas of [[Hypogea]]. The worm was waiting for them, calling to Lin, and prostrated itself seeking protection. To their astonishment, the [[Chimerae|chimera]] and Lin had been linked without passing through [[The Musubi]] rite. The [[Yzmir]] Magisters offered Lindiwe patronage to study this unprecedented case. The worm was given the name [[Maw]].

## Role within the Yzmir

Lindiwe has become an expert in secrets, hunting down plots while keeping the Faction's secrets protected. She must contend with [[The Mandates]], restrictions on [[Alteration]] use, though circumventing them has proven useful. She operates in a gray area within the Faction.

## Timeline

### 372 AC - Moonlight Jellyfish at Magisterium

Lindiwe fed a moon jellyfish to [[Maw]], who digested the ideas within it to extract [[Mana]].

### 378 AC - Kuwat Vasanti at College of Mages

[[Kuwat Vasanti]] appeared before the college of mages with a disgraced reputation as [[Ayxas]]'s former adviser.

### 381 AC - Evading Spy at Svarog Village

A spy followed Lindiwe through Svarog Village. She conjured the idea of "Break" on a cart's axle, used the commotion to alter her appearance, and escaped.

### 385 AC - Treating Young Initiate with Baku

Lindiwe assisted [[Baku]] in treating [[Kumari Adhikari]], a young girl suffering from Remanence affliction who leaked ideas with no control.

### 391 AC - Saskia's Visit at Baba's Isba

[[Saskia Averina]], a [[Muna]] member, visited [[Baba Yaga]]'s isba while Lindiwe was there.

### 392 AC - Watching Kraken Battle via Seer

Through a Seer's crystal ball, Lindiwe watched [[Kraken]] battle and imprinted its image in memory.

### 392 AC - Kraken Summoning Authorization Vote

[[Chambulla]] cast the deciding vote in favor of adding [[Kraken]] to [[Yzmir]]'s summoning arsenal after debate among the Magisters.

### 392 AC - Waru's Political Opposition Activities

[[Waru Toowoom]] confiscated esoteric works Lindiwe requested aboard [[The Ouroboros]] as part of his campaign to obstruct [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] operations.

### 392 AC - Murder of Matera During Kraken Battle

Lindiwe watched [[Matera]] fall to [[Kraken]] through a crystal ball, then tore apart [[Matera]]'s essence and projected the [[Mana]] to [[Afanas Korovinovich]].

### 392 AC - Confronting Arjun Over Indus Tribe Territory

Lindiwe prevented [[Arjun]] from claiming scrubland that was the Indus Tribe's last camp site, protected by the Treaty of Aysun.

### 392 AC - Insight in Maw

[[Maw]] evolved from appearing as a simple organism to an entity with complex cognition capable of human manipulation, driven to consume [[Mana]] and Aether endlessly. [[Maw]] referenced "others like me," suggesting other parasitic worm entities. [[Maw]] maintains total control while actual Lindiwe remains trapped, unable to call for help.

### 393 AC, January-April - Brief Freedom in Taiga

[[Maw]] granted Lindiwe a brief period of control over her own body in the taiga of [[Storvhit]], during which she touched snow, breathed in birch scent, and used her gathered [[Mana]] to hatch a [[Mana Moths|Mana moth]] chrysalis.

### 393 AC, February-April - Climbing Cais Adarra

Lindiwe climbed a rock face at [[Cais Adarra]] during a blizzard using great quantities of [[Mana]] to maintain body temperature, until [[Pamola]] appeared on an outcrop and extended his hand toward her.

### 393 AC, January-April - Training with Snow Queen

Lindiwe trained with the [[Snow Queen]] alongside other disciples in mental stasis techniques for capturing ideas from [[The Empyrean]] while limiting [[Remanence]], using a prism construct to filter and refine ideas.

### 393 AC, January-April - Creating Moth Lantern

Lindiwe created a moth lantern during cave exploration by blowing [[Mana]] into a glass bulb containing an Aether shard, attracting luminous [[Mana Moths]] that revealed magnificent crystal formations.

### 393 AC, April - Observing The Nilam

Lindiwe examined [[The Nilam]] through [[Irises]] enhanced by [[Arcolano Milk]], observing the crystallized world-tree's scarred bark and discovering something parasitic contained within its branches and roots.

### 393 AC, June-August - City of Scholars Singularity Evacuation

Lindiwe appeared through a portal at the [[City of Scholars]] excavation site when a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity began forming during drilling operations, guiding [[Maw]] through the breach while accompanying other [[Yzmir]] mages to assist in the emergency evacuation of [[Axiom]] personnel.

### 393 AC, June-August - Rescuing Wounded Tisdhera Lyra

Lindiwe discovered a wounded [[Lyra]] performer from [[Tisdhera Clan]] whose spiritual essence was leaking through lacerations as if something had wanted to devour her. She applied Sigil compresses to stop the flow of ideas and collected escaped ones in vials, feeding some to [[Maw]]. She then opened a portal to [[Nilam Spires]] for treatment at [[The Cloister]].

### 393 AC, June-August - Observing Yzmir Nurse Treatment

[[Maw]] controlled Lindiwe while observing a [[The Cloister|Cloister]] nurse treat the wounded Tisdhera [[Lyra]], noting the nurse used moon jellyfish secretions as a poultice and that the patient would suffer after-effects from having memories devoured.

### 393 AC, June-August - Encountering Moyo Chibuye

Lindiwe encountered [[Moyo Chibuye]], a [[Yzmir]] xenobiology specialist carrying a moth lantern and wearing a wide hat hung with bluish [[Mana Moths]] chrysalids. Lindiwe considered revealing [[Maw]]'s possession but was prevented from speaking by her captor's control.

### 393 AC, September - Observing The Hunger

[[Maw]] controlled Lindiwe during the evacuation of [[Axiom]] engineers through portals, then observed [[The Hunger]] emerge from a wall—a dark mass with slitted eye, carnivorous maw ringed with razor-sharp teeth, prehensile tongue, and hooked fingers. Maw analyzed the entity's compulsive feeding on [[Sap]], recognizing a similar appetite but different nature.
