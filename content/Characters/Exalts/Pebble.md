# Pebble

## Overview

Pebble is a golem-like [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who serves as the Alter Ego of [[Isaree Thongsuk]], bonded through [[The Musubi]]. The name is ironic given the creature's massive bulk.

## Appearance

Pebble is a humanoid figure standing nearly three meters tall with a massive form and stone-like skin. Veins of [[Aerolith]] run across its body, and it has round, glassy eyes set in rose-colored sockets. The creature possesses pudgy arms capable of twisting metal with precision.

## Abilities

Pebble can cause [[Aerolith]] crystals to sprout from stone by placing its hand on rock surfaces. The creature can levitate and rise through vertical shafts. Pebble can sense [[Aerolith]] veins even when deeply buried in rock and appears to generate a magnetic or antigravitational field that causes [[Aerolith]]-veined stones to part before it. Through the [[The Musubi|Musubi]] bond, Pebble communicates telepathically with simple words.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Aerolith Vein Sensing

Pebble assisted [[Axiom]] operations by locating profitable [[Aerolith]] veins buried deep in rock that other methods could not detect.

### 393 AC - Navigating Aerolith Reef

Pebble navigated through a belt of suspended [[Aerolith]]-veined rocks in the [[Cloud Realm]], with the floating stones parting before the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] as if affected by a field it generated.

### 393 AC - Restocking Station Visit

Pebble accompanied [[Isaree Thongsuk]] aboard [[AES Kurush]] during a docking at a restocking station to refuel [[Kelon]] reserves.

### 393 AC, June-September - First Encounter with Isaree

During a prospecting mission for the [[Expeditionary Corps]], [[Isaree Thongsuk]] was conducting safety analyses on a mineral vein when Pebble emerged from the rock wall behind her, demonstrating its crystal-generating ability by causing Aerolith to sprout from the cavern wall.

### 393 AC, October 23 - Halua Attack on Pelagonian Quarry

While exploring abandoned tunnels in the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]], Pebble carried [[Isaree Thongsuk]] through an elevator shaft when [[Halua]]'s attack caused the mine to collapse. Pebble shielded Isaree from falling debris and levitated her to safety before they witnessed the destruction of [[AES Sune]].
