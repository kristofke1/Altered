# Auraq

## Overview

Auraq is a [[Lyra]] [[Alterers|Alterer]] and Exalt known for their provocative and extravagant stage persona. Auraq is the alter ego and stage name of Awmar, a performer from a [[Lyra]] theatrical troupe. Their companion is [[Kibble]], an ethereal rabbit or cat-like [[Chimerae|Chimera]].

## Early Life and Mother's Death

During childhood, Awmar accompanied his parents to their performances. He loved watching the setup, a habit dating back to childhood. He adored wandering amidst the clutter of crates, cables, and metal frameworks. He loved watching actors don costumes and watching his mother perform.

Awmar's father worked as a costume maker for the troupe, maintaining a studio with fantastical clothing designs. During childhood, Awmar and his friend [[Nyala]] would sneak into the studio to take these fantastical designs. Together they dreamed up performances, forming a duo. They vowed to create their first show as team. They spent hours devising scenarios, practicing steps, reciting lines, and juggling. Awmar only left Nyala's side to watch his mother perform onstage at night.

[[Nyala]] developed her [[Alteration]] powers first and teased Awmar about his slower progress. For Awmar, Alteration had not been a priority until his mother's illness consumed their lives. Over time, as the illness claimed his mother, Alteration became both distraction and escape. He caught up to Nyala in mastering the art and surpassed her.

Drawing on her [[Ignescence]], Awmar's mother captivated crowds with hypnotic voice and elegant gestures, but her performances left her drained. During a rehearsal, Awmar's mother lost consciousness and sank into a languor that nothing could alleviate. With no one to replace the lead female role, Awmar volunteered to stand in for her and delivered the performance to thunderous applause. When he opened the door to their trailer afterward, he saw his father's eyes bathed in tears and his mother's motionless body. He collapsed on the trailer steps, feeling guilty that he had stolen her glory and hadn't been there when she drew her last breath.

The friendship with Nyala faltered after his mother's death. Awmar withdrew into guilt. Nyala, unsure how to help, kept her distance. A mutual unease grew into a wall between them. Their paths diverged. Nyala began staging small shows independently while Auraq stepped into her mother's role. They observed each other from afar without sharing the stage. After finishing her studies, Nyala attempted to reconnect with Auraq, but found Auraq had vanished without trace.

## Career in Arkaster

Auraq moved to [[Arkaster]] to perform at venues like [[The Opera]], [[The Astoria]], [[The Nightingale]], and [[The Spark]]. Her performances of [[Alteration]] can bring the house down and set venues ablaze with creative energy. Before treading the stage, she always gives a thought to her mother.

## Alteration Abilities

Through her mother's teachings and her own development, Auraq possesses [[Alteration]] abilities connected to performance and [[Ignescence]]. Her performances can set venues ablaze with creative energy.

During afternoon rehearsal, Auraq observed Nyala's portal demonstration from backstage shadows but accidentally revealed presence by catching feet in ropes, leading Awmar to remove mask and perform [[Lyra]] ballad while Nyala watched.
