# Auraq

## Overview

Auraq is a [[Lyra]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who performs at venues including [[The Opera]], [[The Astoria]], [[The Nightingale]], and [[The Spark]] in [[Arkaster]]. Auraq is the stage name of Awmar, a performer from a [[Lyra]] theatrical troupe. Their companion is [[Kibble]], an ethereal rabbit or cat-like [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. Auraq's [[Alteration]] abilities are connected to [[Ignescence]].

## Background

During childhood, Awmar accompanied his parents to their performances, watching the setup and wandering amidst the clutter of crates, cables, and metal frameworks. He watched actors don costumes and watched his mother perform. Awmar's father worked as a costume maker for the troupe, maintaining a studio with fantastical clothing designs. Awmar and his friend [[Nyala]] would sneak into the studio to take these designs. Together they formed a duo and vowed to create their first show as a team, spending hours devising scenarios, practicing steps, reciting lines, and juggling. [[Nyala]] developed her [[Alteration]] powers before Awmar did.

Awmar's mother drew on [[Ignescence]] to captivate crowds with her voice and gestures, but her performances left her drained. During a rehearsal, she lost consciousness and did not recover. With no one to replace the lead female role, Awmar volunteered to stand in for her and delivered the performance to thunderous applause. When he returned to their trailer afterward, he found his mother had died during the performance. Awmar and Nyala drifted apart after the death. Nyala began staging small shows independently, while Auraq stepped into his mother's role in the troupe.

When the Clan journeyed to a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity to entrust his mother's body to its transforming currents, Awmar joined the procession. A [[Chimerae|Chimera]] emerged from [[The Tumult]]—a cat or rabbit-like creature with an ethereal, translucent body. The entity approached him and placed its paw on his arm. The [[Chimerae|Chimera]] remained close to him afterward, following them in their wanderings. He named the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] [[Kibble]].

After [[Kibble]]'s arrival, Awmar took on the roles left vacant by his mother. His performances gained visibility, and critics praised his work. Over time, his repertoire became more burlesque, and the persona of Auraq emerged as his stage identity. One evening, Auraq invoked her mother's [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to converse with her, and the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] kissed her on the forehead.

## Abilities

Auraq draws on [[Ignescence]] to fuel performances, absorbing ideas from the air and channeling them into [[Alteration]]. Auraq can summon multiple [[Eidolons]] including [[Hathor]], [[Tanuki]], [[The Sandman]], [[Heimdall]], and [[Will-o'-the-Wisp]]. Working with [[Kibble]], Auraq can release explosive transformations of surrounding areas through combined [[Mana]] discharge.
