# Senka

## Overview
Senka is a night bird [[Chimerae|chimera]] bonded with [[Afanas Korovinovich]] through [[The Musubi]] ceremony, serving as his Alter Ego in their Exalt partnership. The creature serves as Afanas's eyes and wings, tirelessly scouting [[The Tumult]] in search of the Chrysanthemum Cloak Warlock.

## Bond with Afanas
When the [[Muna]] managed to recreate [[The Musubi]] ceremony, [[Afanas Korovinovich|Afanas]] was one of the first to volunteer to bond with his [[Chimerae|chimera]]. He needed Senka to traverse [[The Tumult]] in search of the Chrysanthemum Cloak Warlock who had murdered his family and conducted forbidden experiments at [[Cebir]].

## Abilities and Function
As a night bird, Senka possesses the ability to fly tirelessly over the altered lands. Through the bond created by [[The Musubi]], Afanas can look through Senka's eyes, allowing him to scout vast territories and dangerous areas that would be impossible or deadly for a human to navigate.

This connection enables Afanas to:
- Survey [[The Tumult]] from above
- Search for clues about the warlock's whereabouts
- Visit dangerous locations like [[Cebir]] safely
- Investigate experiments and sites from a distance

## Role in the Hunt
Through Senka, Afanas visited [[Cebir]] and made sense of the experiments conducted there by the Chrysanthemum Cloak Warlock. The bird's tireless flights and aerial perspective provided Afanas with crucial information about the mage's esoteric and unholy research that flouted the Faction's [[The Mandates|Mandates]].

## The Confrontation
During one of Senka's explorations, the bird came face to face with the warlock. The masked figure turned toward Senka, spreading his arms in a greeting. Through the bird's eyes, miles away, Afanas watched. After all these years, he finally had proof that the warlock existed, that he had not dreamt it.

The warlock addressed Afanas directly through Senka, his voice booming in Afanas's mind. He urged him to join him in [[The Tumult]] if he dared, promising to be there waiting to offer him his due. This confrontation confirmed Afanas's long-held belief and solidified his determination to hunt down the warlock, even if it meant joining the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] to pursue him into the Terra Incognita.
