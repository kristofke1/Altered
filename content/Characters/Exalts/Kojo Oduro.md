# Kojo Oduro

## Overview

Kojo Oduro, born 366 AC, is a [[Bravos]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] known as the Slayer of [[Kraken]] and Hero of [[Asgartha]]. He is bonded with his Alter Ego [[Booda]], a cheetah [[Chimerae|Chimera]], through [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Kojo received the [[Carnelian Crown]] from Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] for defeating [[Kraken|the Kraken]] at the [[Battle of Madan Bay]].

## Background

### Early Life

Even as a child, Kojo was known as a daredevil. His mother [[Tehea]] was a skilled woodworker from the Wood Guild specializing in luxury marquetry, and his father [[Yawo]] owned a small convenience store under the [[Cornucoopia]] brand. His older sister [[Gulrang Oduro|Gulrang]] was his opposite—stern and devoid of humor. His father asked him to deliver groceries to help the elderly, which suited him as he explored the sprawling alleys of the capital. When he came of age, Kojo applied to [[The Talaria]], becoming a courier.

### Discovery of Booda

Kojo discovered his [[Chimerae|Chimera]] [[Booda]] by pure chance—one evening while walking the heights of the city, he spotted a shimmering [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity, and as the cloud disappeared, found a small cheetah whose fur was made of cold flames. Finding no parent Chimera nearby, he took in the animal. The [[Bravos]], desperate to add another Exalt to their ranks, allowed Kojo to skip the traditional squire training entirely and bypass the [[Imhallat]] ceremony. This unconventional path left Kojo feeling like an impostor—technically a Bravos, but not truly one of them.

### Altrun Career

Like many kids, Kojo's hero was [[Rune]], and he heard from [[Venka]] about the prophecy that whoever found Rune in [[The Tumult]] would be his successor. One afternoon while climbing slopes, Kojo saw acrobats using [[Alteration]] to create stone pylons and glass slides—his introduction to [[Altrun]]. He began training in early mornings, studying old competition archives.

After approaching runners at the Speedster's Den tavern, Kojo met [[Uju]], [[Cathal]], [[Carmela]], and [[Gault]]. The reception was initially cold but [[Uju]] insisted on giving him a chance. When Kojo's first match came, [[Gault]] gave him a discreet nod and Kojo won his first race. That evening, [[Gault]] kissed Kojo. Years passed as the team's reputation grew.

Kojo realized his childhood dream had never left him—to find [[Rune]]. [[Gault]] supported his decision and helped process his file at the Monolith. The following year, Kojo and [[Booda]] bonded through [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

### The Carnelian Crown

The [[Carnelian Crown]] had been worn before by illustrious champions including [[Abelen Sundström]] and [[Aroro Niski]]. When Kojo received the honor, his friends from the [[Jusaka Blaze]]—[[Carmela]], [[Uju]], [[Cathal]], and [[Gault]]—were in the stands.

During urgent postal delivery, Kojo traversed rooftops using [[Alteration]] to create stone steps, materializing [[Ratatoskr]] to gain speed through slipstream effect.

### 388 AC - Haven Warrior Training
Kojo sparred with unnamed female warrior at [[Haven]] during training for Expeditionary Corps entry.

### 389 AC - Bravos Pathfinder Training
Kojo climbed cliff with [[Sunniva Vaccarezza]] to reach halfway point before facing Chasm's maze of landings, pulleys, and mechanisms.

### 391 AC - Altrun Final at Colosseum
Kojo watched [[Carmela]] race in [[Altrun]] final at Colosseum where teams' [[Alterers]] modified field.

### 392 AC - Tilted Steppes Mission
Kojo traversed floating islands where gravity switched directions, planning to meet [[Booda]] and use collected [[Mana]] to stabilize area for [[Bravos]].

### 392 AC, September - Battle of Madan Bay

During the [[Battle of Madan Bay]], [[Teija]] summoned [[Kaibara]] to engage the [[Kraken]]. Kojo leaped onto Kaibara's skin gripping tree branches while the whale Leviathan attacked. After [[Afanas Korovinovich]] opened a dimensional portal, Kojo launched himself above the weakened Kraken, forming a glowing [[Mana]] sphere between his hands, and delivered a blow that vaporized the Leviathan entirely.

### 392 AC, November - Carnelian Crown Ceremony
Kojo received the [[Carnelian Crown]] from Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] at the Asterion amphitheater before thousands of cheering citizens.

### 392 AC, November - Eat Me Energy Bar Commercial
After defeating [[Kraken|the Kraken]], Kojo was recruited to star in advertisements for "Eat Me" energy bars.

### 393 AC, January-April - Skiing Training with Skadi
[[Skaði|Skadi]] trained Kojo in skiing at [[Storhvit]], though he crashed into a snowdrift after missing a bump jump.

### 393 AC, January-April - Inspecting Skadi's Technical Boots
Kojo tried on [[Skaði|Skadi]]'s technical boots featuring retractable crampons and an ice-skate flame blade before being caught by the returning [[Eidolons|Eidolon]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Geyser Hot Springs Visit
Kojo visited hot springs created by [[Atsadi]] during a [[Belisenki]] battle, where [[Lyra]] had converted the battlefield's boiling geysers into an open-air pool.

### 393 AC, February-March - Belisenki Assault Combat
During [[Belisenki]] night assault at [[Storhvit]], Kojo insisted on participating despite [[Gulrang Oduro]]'s orders, citing his training with [[Atsadi]], with [[Booda]] accompanying him into the fray until [[Akesha Adhikari]] distributed [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides and the Belisenki ceased attacking.

### 393 AC, June - Following Atsadi and Witnessing Fiore Duel
Kojo followed [[Atsadi]] through the [[City of Scholars]] tunnels hoping to convince him to take him as apprentice, pulled Atsadi back to consciousness when he became lost in a memory library chamber's stored memories, then witnessed and analyzed [[Atsadi]]'s duel with [[Fiore dei Liberi]], noting Atsadi held back from using his full [[Alteration]] abilities.

### 393 AC, June - Deciding to Become Atsadi's Squire
After watching [[Atsadi]] duel [[Fiore dei Liberi]] and comparing their fighting styles, Kojo resolved to pursue becoming Atsadi's squire despite considering whether Fiore's flamboyant style suited him better.

### 393 AC, June-August - Discovery of Rune's Scroll
Kojo accompanied [[Waru Toowoom]]'s expedition into the [[City of Scholars]] depths with [[Phoenixian Guard]] members led by [[Sitina]], where Waru discovered [[Rune]]'s scroll confirming the Source of [[The Tumult]] was a fabrication and [[Sitina]] revealed [[Rune's Testament]] and the [[Phoenix Egg]]'s transport westward.

### 393 AC, June-August - Reunion with Sunniva at The Mess
Kojo reunited with [[Sunniva Vaccarezza]] at [[The Mess]] after her convalescence, ordering Sap borscht and fondue while catching up about their respective journeys since briefly meeting at [[Caer Oorun]].

### 393 AC, June-August - Sap Soda Delivery in the Depths
While exhausted from days of underground exploration, Kojo received [[Sap]] Soda from a Talarian courier who emerged from a side passage to resupply the demoralized expedition.

### 393 AC, June-August - Visiting The Mess
Kojo visited [[The Mess]] and observed [[Basira al-Arshad]] clinking a toast with [[Treyst]], reminded of Basira's refusal to coach him.

### 393 AC, September - Confrontation with The Hunger
Kojo participated in the Exalt operation against [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]], rushing forward with blazing fist when the creature shifted to aggression until [[Atsadi]] shoved him back to prevent [[The Hunger]]'s mandibles from snapping shut on him.

### 393 AC, October-November - Training with Kaw
Kojo trained with [[Kaw]], 376 AC [[Altrun]] champion, who threw a [[Jumping Frog]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] at him and instructed him to study its chaotic jumps, sudden leaps, and wall-sticking ability to improve his reflexes.

### 393 AC, October-November - Training with Gault
Kojo summoned [[Gault]] as an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] in the [[Illyrian Quadrant]], training together and reconnecting emotionally while separated from his teammates in [[Arkaster]].

### 393 AC - Distracted State During Expedition

Kojo appeared worried, distracted, and uncharacteristically silent during meals at the expedition mess hall, a change noticed by his sister [[Gulrang Oduro]]. His demeanor suggested a hidden concern connected to knowledge shared with [[Waru Toowoom]] regarding [[Rune's Testament]] and the [[Phoenix Egg]].

### 393 AC, October-November - Waru's Assessment of Four Strengths
[[Waru Toowoom]] identified four strengths in Kojo—[[Booda]] as greatest asset, agility, speed, and an unknown fourth—advising him to exploit others' weaknesses rather than dwell on his own.

### 393 AC, October-November - Racing The Hare
Kojo challenged [[The Hare]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to a race with a kelonic Floret at stake, seeking to exploit its predictable story-dictated overconfidence after falling behind.

### 393 AC, October-November - Training in the Cloud Realm
Kojo conducted training sessions in the [[Illyrian Quadrant]] of the [[Cloud Realm]], where he was encountered by [[Sunniva Vaccarezza]] during one of her escort missions.

### 393 AC - Atsadi Accepts Squire Request
After repeatedly approaching [[Atsadi]] seeking mentorship, Kojo admitted his impostor feelings—having found his Chimera by chance, skipped squire training, and never undergone the [[Imhallat]]. He argued that inexperience was the difference between living and dying in the expedition's dangers. Atsadi, revealing he too had been a simple herdsman who fought his way up, agreed to take Kojo as his squire with the condition that Kojo keep up with his demanding standards.
