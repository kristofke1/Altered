# Gulrang Oduro

## Overview

Gulrang Oduro (also known as Gul) is an [[Ordis]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who serves as a captain in [[The Aegis]]. She is bonded with her Alter Ego [[Tocsin]], a sentient Golem [[Chimerae|Chimera]], through [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Gulrang is the older sister of [[Kojo Oduro]], the Slayer of [[Kraken|The Kraken]].

## Musubi Bond and Family

Over time, Gulrang and [[Tocsin]] increasingly aspired to understand each other. [[The Musubi]] naturally imposed itself on them, and [[Tocsin]] accepted without hesitation. Through the Hive Mind, others could converse with [[Tocsin]]. When she introduced [[Tocsin]] to her family, she discovered [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] had also taken in a [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. During the ceremony where [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] received the [[Carnelian Crown]], Gulrang teased him about his recklessness.

### 382 AC - Aedyt's Singularity Rescue

Three Exalt duos (Gulrang/[[Tocsin]], [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]]/[[Wingspan]], [[Waru Toowoom|Waru]]/[[Mack]]) were chosen by four [[Ordis]] branch representatives: [[Kakoba]], [[Frog Prince]], [[Cestimir]], [[Nassefa]].

### 387 AC - Learning Glyphs from Baxsan
[[Baxsan]] taught Gulrang new [[Glyph|Glyphs]] including "Shackle," "Darkness," "Passage," and others, then moved to practical dueling creating insulating suit.

### 389 AC - Waiting at Council Chamber
Gulrang encountered [[Maibritt]], Templar guard with gold chain and three sealed scrolls, standing before closed Council Chamber door during negotiations.

### 391 AC - Recruit Assessment

Gulrang assessed ranks of new recruits during inspection attended by Admiral [[Temera Singh]], Commodore [[Jin-Hee Jeanty]], and [[Sigismar Synd]], evaluating [[Sunisa]], [[Amenisa]], and [[Osoyo]] as officer material while other instructors placed bets on top performers.

### 392 AC - Opening Solstice Gate
Heavy doors of Solstice Gate opened and [[Tocsin]] blew powerful horn note, then Gulrang called [[Coalescence]] connecting to all recruits she trained.

### 392 AC - Coordinating Kraken Battle
During [[Kraken|The Kraken]] battle, [[Tocsin]] blew horn signaling lancers while Gulrang channeled [[Glyph|Glyphs]] "Coordination," "Arrangement," "Harmonization" through conch to coordinate troubadours.

### 392 AC - Kojo's Carnelian Crown Ceremony
Gulrang watched [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] kneel before [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] who placed [[Carnelian Crown]] on his head, feeling pride mixed with fear about brother's recklessness.

### 392 AC - Building Telamon Beacons
Gulrang protected Telamon architects using [[Alteration]] to summon [[Eidolons]] as workers building beacon towers to connect her Rhombus to [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]].

### 392 AC - Mesektet Recovery Expedition
Gulrang led [[Ordis]] expedition through Irundu forest to locate crashed [[Mesektet]], discovering wreckage on mountainside while [[Nosk]] broadcast through [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]].

### 393 AC - Belisenki Night Assault at Storvhit

Gulrang commanded [[Ordis]] forces during [[Belisenki]] night assault on convoy traveling toward [[Storvhit]] base camp, coordinating through [[The Gestalt]] while issuing orders and tracing [[Heka]] symbols. She engaged approximately 40 [[Belisenki]] attacking from northern sector using hyperbola formation, deploying shield walls and interception teams while unstable terrain prevented [[Axiom]] artillery support. During individual combat, Gulrang parried Belasenka attack and witnessed creature use minor [[Alteration]] creating jagged ice spikes, responding with [[Heka]] symbols solidifying snow into marble columns trapping enemy and amplifying shrillness to scatter swarm. [[Akesha Adhikari]] arrived through portal bearing [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides, instructing soldiers to consume them. After consuming chrysalis containing bitter syrupy essence, Gulrang observed [[Belisenki]] ceased attacking and departed, no longer perceiving expedition members as intruders due to absorbed pheromones. During battle, [[Kojo Oduro]] insisted on fighting despite Gulrang's orders to take cover, with [[Tocsin]] coordinating through [[The Gestalt]] and managing [[Kojo Oduro]]'s position.

### ~393 AC - Tundra Operations Briefing

Gulrang attended briefing at rocky foothills marking the tundra's edge, where an NCO commander announced reinforcements being sent to address scout harassment by [[Belisenki]] and assigned Gulrang's unit to join the advance guard.

### ~393 AC - Belisenki Defense at Tundra Foothills

Gulrang led shield defense during all-night [[Belisenki]] assault, filling gaps in formation as troops fell while [[Tocsin]] used horn blasts to repel attackers; reinforcements arrived at dawn from the western ridge.

### ~393 AC - Mobile Armory Resupply

Gulrang received supplies from an [[Aerolith]]-powered floating mobile armory on the ice pack, inventorying medical supplies, cold-weather equipment, climbing kits, and autocrawlers while loading troops' letters to families for return transport.

### ~393 AC - Summoning Benzaiten

After [[The Gestalt]] ordered rapid reinforcement of eastern contingents, Gulrang traced [[Glyph|Glyphs]] in snow to summon [[Benzaiten]], whose giant serpent mounts transported the entire squadron across treacherous tundra terrain.

### ~393 AC - Rallying Call at Earthen Fort

Gulrang stood beside [[Tocsin]] at an earthen fort grown using [[Alteration]] as the Golem sounded the great horn and recited rallying verses to approximately 50 soldiers before [[Belisenki]] assault approached through the mist.

### 393 AC, June-September - Receiving Kaaba at Crow's Eye
Gulrang prepared troops to break camp at [[The Crow's Eye]] when Templar [[Kaaba]] arrived as expedition leader, anticipating conflict due to Kaaba's ambition and recklessness.

### 393 AC, June-September - Mesektet Escape and Mass Healing
Gulrang piloted [[Mesektet]] out of [[The Crow's Eye]] after an attack that left her with memory loss, then summoned [[Simurgh]] above [[The Screed]] to heal over 200 soldiers in critical condition.

### 393 AC, June-September - Maintaining Mesektet Through Alteration
Gulrang used [[Alteration]] to maintain [[Mesektet]]'s appearance as a functional headquarters, simulating engine rooms and facilities while [[Tocsin]] channeled [[Mana]] from distant sources—an assignment [[Kaaba]] gave to sideline her from frontline operations.

## Personality and Skills

Gulrang is stoic and martial in demeanor, contrasting sharply with her exuberant brother [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]]. She shows perseverance, dedication, and determination. She is skilled in Heka [[Alteration]], able to inscribe [[Glyph|Glyphs]] into stone to create marble spikes and other constructs. Despite her stern exterior, she cares for her family and rose to captain in [[The Aegis]] through her own merit.
