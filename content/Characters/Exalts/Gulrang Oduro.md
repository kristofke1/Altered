# Gulrang Oduro

## Overview

Gulrang Oduro (also known as Gul), born 354 AC, is an [[Ordis]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who serves as a captain in [[The Aegis]]. She is bonded with her Alter Ego [[Tocsin]], a sentient Golem [[Chimerae|Chimera]], through [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Gulrang is the older sister of [[Kojo Oduro]], the Slayer of [[Kraken|The Kraken]].

## Background

Gulrang comes from a working family in [[Arkaster]]—her mother [[Tehea]] is a skilled woodworker from the Wood Guild specializing in ebony, and her father [[Yawo]] owns a small grocery store. As a child, Gulrang would accompany her mother to the factory and rush to the window when [[The Aegis|Aegis]] sentinels departed on patrol. [[Venka]], a veteran injured fighting [[The Tumult]], visited for dinner and told anecdotes about service. At twelve, Gulrang decided to become an [[The Aegis|Aegis]] sentinel. By fourteen, when [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] was barely two, she heard about the Expeditionary Corps from street criers. She convinced her parents to let her join by promising to take care of the house and [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] for four years. [[Venka]] began training her—rising at dawn, preparing breakfast, escorting [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] to school, attending classes, managing meals, and training for the [[Ordis]] armed forces. She increased muscle mass, trained in weapon handling, studied laws, and learned Heka [[Glyph|Glyphs]].

Gulrang's progression within the [[Ordis]] hierarchy was rapid—from cadet to recruit in months, then official Trooper in two years. She was marked with the Faction's Diamond, opening her to the Hive Mind. During a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] surge at [[The Rampart]], two [[Chimerae|Chimeras]] materialized—one infused into a stone statue (Golem) and another took the form of a griffin. A rock block hurtled toward Gulrang, but the animated statue raised its arm to protect her. The griffin pounced on the Golem, and Gulrang used Heka to create marble spikes that pierced the griffin's flank. The Golem was captured, and Gulrang visited it in its cell. Using Heka [[Glyph|Glyphs]], they initiated conversation. After weeks of dialogue, Gulrang brought it a trumpet, and the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] produced cacophonous notes—she gave it the name [[Tocsin]].

Gulrang and [[Tocsin]] underwent [[The Musubi]] ceremony and bonded as Alter Egos. Through the Hive Mind, others could converse with [[Tocsin]]. When she introduced [[Tocsin]] to her family, she discovered [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] had also taken in a [[Chimerae|Chimera]].

## Timeline

Gulrang watched recruit [[Aedyt]] struggle in training until [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity formed at [[Kurung]], and [[Aedyt]] threw herself into winds to save [[Alterers|Alterer]].

Three Exalt duos (Gulrang/[[Tocsin]], [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]]/[[Wingspan]], [[Waru Toowoom|Waru]]/[[Mack]]) were chosen by four [[Ordis]] branch representatives: [[Kakoba]], [[Frog Prince]], [[Cestimir]], [[Nassefa]].

### 387 AC - Learning Glyphs from Baxsan
[[Baxsan]] taught Gulrang new [[Glyph|Glyphs]] including "Shackle," "Darkness," "Passage," and others, then moved to practical dueling creating insulating suit.

### 389 AC - Waiting at Council Chamber
Gulrang encountered [[Maibritt]], Templar guard with gold chain and three sealed scrolls, standing before closed Council Chamber door during negotiations.

### 391 AC - Recruit Assessment
Gulrang assessed ranks of new recruits during inspection attended by Admiral [[Temera Singh]], Commodore [[Jin-Hee Jeanty]], and [[Sigismar Synd]], evaluating [[Sunisa]], [[Amenisa]], and [[Osoyo]] as officer material while other instructors placed bets on top performers.

### 392 AC - Opening Solstice Gate
Heavy doors of Solstice Gate opened and [[Tocsin]] blew powerful horn note, then Gulrang called [[Coalescence]] connecting to all recruits she trained.

### 392 AC, September - Battle of Madan Bay
During the [[Battle of Madan Bay]], [[Tocsin]] blew horn signaling lancers while Gulrang channeled [[Glyph|Glyphs]] "Coordination," "Arrangement," "Harmonization" through conch to coordinate troubadours.

### 392 AC - Kojo's Carnelian Crown Ceremony
Gulrang watched [[Kojo Oduro|Kojo]] kneel before [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] who placed [[Carnelian Crown]] on his head, and teased him afterward about his recklessness.

### 392 AC - Building Telamon Beacons
Gulrang protected Telamon architects using [[Alteration]] to summon [[Eidolons]] as workers building beacon towers to connect her Rhombus to [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]].

### 392 AC - Mesektet Recovery Expedition
Gulrang led [[Ordis]] expedition through Irundu forest to locate crashed [[Mesektet]], discovering wreckage on mountainside while [[Nosk]] broadcast through [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]].

### 393 AC, February - Belisenki Night Assault at Storvhit
Gulrang commanded [[Ordis]] forces during [[Belisenki]] night assault on convoy traveling toward [[Storhvit]], coordinating through [[The Gestalt]] while deploying shield walls and interception teams against approximately 40 attackers from the northern sector. During combat, she witnessed a Belisenka use minor [[Alteration]] to create jagged ice spikes, and responded with [[Heka]] symbols solidifying snow into marble columns. [[Akesha Adhikari]] arrived through portal bearing [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides; after soldiers consumed the chrysalides, the [[Belisenki]] ceased attacking and departed, no longer perceiving expedition members as intruders due to absorbed pheromones.

### 393 AC, January-April - Tundra Operations Briefing
Gulrang attended briefing at rocky foothills marking the tundra's edge, where an NCO commander announced reinforcements and assigned Gulrang's unit to the advance guard.

### 393 AC, January-March - Belisenki Defense at Tundra Foothills
Gulrang led shield defense during all-night [[Belisenki]] assault, filling gaps in formation as troops fell while [[Tocsin]] used horn blasts to repel attackers; reinforcements arrived at dawn from the western ridge.

### 393 AC, January-April - Mobile Armory Resupply
Gulrang received supplies from an [[Aerolith]]-powered floating mobile armory on the ice pack, inventorying medical supplies, cold-weather equipment, climbing kits, and autocrawlers while loading troops' letters to families for return transport.

### 393 AC, January-April - Summoning Benzaiten
After [[The Gestalt]] ordered rapid reinforcement of eastern contingents, Gulrang traced [[Glyph|Glyphs]] in snow to summon [[Benzaiten]], whose giant serpent mounts transported the entire squadron across treacherous tundra terrain.

### 393 AC, January-March - Rallying Call at Earthen Fort
Gulrang stood beside [[Tocsin]] at an earthen fort grown using [[Alteration]] as the Golem sounded the great horn and recited rallying verses to approximately 50 soldiers before [[Belisenki]] assault approached through the mist.

### 393 AC, June-September - Receiving Kaaba at Crow's Eye
Gulrang prepared troops to break camp at [[The Crow's Eye]] when Templar [[Kaaba]] arrived as expedition leader.

### 393 AC, June-September - Mesektet Escape and Mass Healing
Gulrang piloted [[Mesektet]] out of [[The Crow's Eye]] after an attack that left her with memory loss, then summoned [[Simurgh]] above [[The Screed]] to heal over 200 soldiers in critical condition.

### 393 AC, June-September - Maintaining Mesektet Through Alteration
Gulrang used [[Alteration]] to maintain [[Mesektet]]'s appearance as a functional headquarters, simulating engine rooms and facilities while [[Tocsin]] channeled [[Mana]] from distant sources—an assignment [[Kaaba]] gave to sideline her from frontline operations.

## Abilities

Gulrang is skilled in Heka [[Alteration]], able to inscribe [[Glyph|Glyphs]] into stone to create marble spikes, protective barriers, and constructs. She can channel multiple Glyphs simultaneously through instruments such as a conch for battlefield coordination. She rose from cadet to captain in [[The Aegis]] through her own merit.
