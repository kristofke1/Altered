# Rin

## Overview

Rin is a [[Muna]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who was found as an infant and raised by the fisherman [[Yonago]]. Through [[The Musubi]] ceremony, she bonded with [[Orchid]], a mantis [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who had originally brought her to [[Yonago]]'s doorstep. Rin joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] to seek answers about her origins beyond [[Asgartha]]'s borders.

## Background

[[Yonago]] found Rin on his doorstep one rainy evening as a tiny infant wrapped in vines and bluish foliage in a cocoon lined with moss. He had seen the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] that brought her—a human-sized praying mantis with a rosy hue like an orchid. [[Yonago]] raised her as his own daughter, teaching her to walk, talk, and decipher the language of nature. At least once a season, the mantis would return to the edge of the forest to see if Rin had grown well. Rin grew up helping [[Yonago]] with fishing work, always careful to give some fish to [[Ukai]], their domestic cormorant. Since she was very young, Rin ventured into the diaphanous forest where she explored the undergrowth in search of mushrooms, wild berries, or edible herbs. She watched the beaver family build a shelter, played hide and seek with white wolf cubs, and rummaged in streams for shells or crayfish. Twice a week, they would wake before dawn and load bins onto their yak to reach the floating city of [[Amorgand]].

One day, Rin set off alone toward the forest, determined to find the only being who might know where she came from. She ventured farther into the spectral forest than ever before, where bluish foliage undulated in an unreal breeze. As night fell, two yellow eyes began to shine in the darkness, and the mantis emerged from the thickets with blade-like legs shining in the moonlight. The [[Chimerae|Chimera]] moved slowly with delicacy, and Rin reached out until she touched its chitinous abdomen. From that day on, Rin returned every day to see the [[Chimerae|Chimera]], whom she named [[Orchid]]. [[Yonago]] revealed that through [[The Musubi]], Rin could bond with the creature and communicate. At the next market, he introduced her to [[Turuun]], a venerable [[Muna]] priestess. [[Yonago]] told her that nature had been generous to him and if nature gave, it was necessary to give back. Rin would have to journey to the [[Katkera]] and perhaps become a [[Muna]] herself.

The envisaged months turned into years. Rin and [[Orchid]] accompanied [[Turuun]] to [[The Spindle]] and the [[Bark Refuge]]. Through [[The Musubi]], they became an Exalt and joined the [[Muna]] family. During this long absence, Rin sent letters to her father. One day, [[Yonago]] saw a frail figure raise its arm from the shore, accompanied by a more massive insectoid form. [[Orchid]] revealed something to Rin—the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] had come from beyond the borders of [[Asgartha]], tossed by the tumultuous flows. [[Orchid]] had taken in a baby from a distant Oasis to keep her safe. [[Yonago]] offered his blessing—of course, she had to find answers about her origins. Rin joined the Expeditionary Corps to venture beyond [[Asgartha]]'s borders.

## Timeline

### 387 AC - Kodama Encounter
Rin traveled through [[Katkera]] forest with [[Turuun]]. Group of [[Kodama]] spirits greeted Rin with little wave from branch lookout.

### 388 AC - Observing Spindle Harvesters
Rin climbed [[The Spindle]] Tree secured by rope and observed tiny rodents descending trunk with claws sinking deep in bark.

### 388 AC - Cernunnos Death Ritual
Rin observed death ritual for Valpas, eldest elk. [[Cernunnos]] emerged from fire and laid hand on old elk as [[The Skein]]'s strands rose from soil.

### 389 AC - Learning Herbal Medicine from Aja
Rin watched [[Aja]] the Orisha [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] prepare salves while cauldron boiled, mimicking each movement to make own ointment.

### 389 AC - Observing Muna Caregiver with Wyvern
Rin watched [[Muna]] healer carefully moving dragon eggs while scaly wyvern creature watched, and wyrmling emerged as healer presented newborn to dragon.

### 390 AC - Son of Yggdrasil Transportation
Rin climbed onto [[Son of Yggdrasil]]'s hand as he lifted them onto shoulder, stepping over hills and crossing forest to reach [[Amorgand]].

### 391 AC - Yong-Su Manifestation
Rin followed procession crossing Mugen bridge during Ryukkōsai festival where [[Yong-Su Tenzing]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] manifested floating in air with lotus flowers blooming beneath steps.

### 392 AC - Forest Exploration with Orchid
Rin and [[Orchid]] explored dense forest where [[Orchid]] cut through undergrowth with sickle-like forelegs while Rin released [[Mana]] back into wounded plants, discovering strange fruit with tart grassy flavor and emerald juice.

### 393 AC, January-April - Hypothermia and Rescue at Storvhit
During blizzard at [[Storhvit]], Rin fell from height through storm and a layer of fresh powder broke her fall. [[Arjun]] took nearly an hour to reach Rin through the blizzard, long enough for her body temperature to drop noticeably. [[Arjun]] and [[Orchid]] rescued Rin from powder snow, with [[Arjun]] providing shelter using [[Alteration]] to conjure fire while [[Orchid]] hunted for food.

### 393 AC, January-April - Discovering Seeds from Arjun's Pots
Rin found seeds in bottom of [[Arjun]]'s pots when taking down floral tent before striking camp, and they decided to share seeds as [[Arjun]] had done with [[Teija]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Summoning Persephone in Frozen Taiga
Rin summoned [[Persephone]] in frozen northern forest where dead tree trunks suggested winter had swept into once-temperate region, causing snowbanks to melt and awakening old tree spirits including oak, chestnut, and beech manifestations who approached the goddess.

### 393 AC, January-April - Scolded by Saskia Averina
[[Saskia Averina]] scolded Rin for carelessly summoning [[Persephone]] without respect for [[Storhvit]] ecosystem, calling it a "rookie mistake" that disrupted balance the [[Winter Folk]] and [[Hakupopo]] depended on, then comforted Rin about missing her father.

### 393 AC, January-April - Encountering the Snow Maiden
Rin followed a melancholy song through ice-covered forest to discover [[Snow Maiden]] sculpting ice animals including rabbits, fawns, and foxes that came to life and frolicked around her, noting the unusual frequency of unsummoned [[Eidolons]] manifesting at [[Storhvit]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Sensing Belisenki in Icebound Taiga
Rin sensed [[Belisenki]] watching expedition through [[The Skein]] while traveling through taiga where something formidable had passed leaving wake of intense cold, warning companions to stay on the trail.

### 393 AC, June-August - Cooking for Arjun
Rin regularly cooked meals for [[Arjun]] to thank him for saving her during the [[Cais Adarra]] ascent.

### 393 AC, June-August - Watching Pastry Chef at The Mess
Rin watched [[Hestia]]'s brigade pastry chef create lemon meringue and a layer cake with [[Sap]]-infused raspberry mousse that turned sparkling golden.

### 393 AC, June-August - Working at The Farm
Rin worked at [[The Farm]] spraying [[Sap]] on plants alongside other gardeners, discovering that Sap amplifies flavor, scent, and texture.

### 393 AC, June-August - Attempting to Forage Gran Bwa's Orchard
Rin tried to forage from [[Gran Bwa]]'s orchard but found the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] surrounded by bumblebeets, and received an apple from a young shepherd.

### 393 AC, June-August - Tracking Noosh to Golden Tuber
Rin chased a [[Noosh Burrowers|Noosh]] through stone maze into [[The Scholars' Tomb]], following the creature as it unearthed an extremely rare golden tuber.

### 393 AC, June-August - Exploring The Undergrowth
Rin explored [[The Undergrowth]] and discovered through [[The Skein]] that the biotope was an extension of the [[City of Scholars]]'s consciousness, sensing a presence deep within the network.

## Abilities

Rin practices [[Alteration]] through [[The Skein]], allowing her to connect to her surroundings, sense presences, and project her mind to search the surrounding area. She can conjure climbing plants that blossom with white flowers and release [[Mana]] back into wounded plants. Through her [[The Musubi|Musubi]] bond with [[Orchid]], she can share tastes and sensations with her Alter Ego. Rin is also a skilled cook and herbalist, trained in preparing salves and ointments.
