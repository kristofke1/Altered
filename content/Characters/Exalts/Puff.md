# Puff

## Overview

Puff is a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] that takes the form of a massive ram, bonded to [[Kauri]] as his Alter Ego through [[The Musubi]]. Puff serves alongside Kauri as an [[Exalts|Exalt]] with the [[Expeditionary Corps]] at [[The Screed]].

## Appearance and Behavior

Puff manifests as an unusually large ram with prominent horns and misty, downy wool. He moves among the sheep flocks at [[The Screed]], disguising his true nature by "parading in ovine form." Only [[Kauri]] recognizes that Puff is a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] rather than an ordinary sheep—through his [[The Skein|Skein]] sensitivity, Kauri alone could perceive Puff's true nature hidden among the flock.

Puff displays protective behavior toward the flock, watching over ewes and lambs. When Kauri freed a lamb caught in rope at the fence, Puff observed from nearby, glaring to ensure the lamb wasn't mistreated. After Kauri released the lamb, Puff turned away. Puff bleats when sensing Kauri's distress and nudges him with insistent headbutts when guiding him.

Aboard [[The Ouroboros]], Puff was initially distrustful of [[Kauri]] but gradually relaxed in his presence as Kauri fed the animals and filled grain bowls each morning.

### Armored Bird Form

Puff can manifest as a gigantic armored bird with jade and emerald coloration. In this form, Puff possesses plated feathers and spreads wings large enough to block an attacker's path. The mineral plumage can withstand lances, arrows, and rockets.

### Protective Behavior

When [[Kauri]] is injured, Puff enters a berserk state and attacks aggressors with horns and hooves. In this state, Puff cannot be easily stopped and will charge directly into enemy formations.

### Skein Shielding

Puff can shield [[Kauri]] from emotional overflow during [[The Skein|Skein]] contact with powerful entities such as [[Leviathans]]. Puff absorbs the emotional torrent through his vaporous fleece, which is consumed but reforms using [[Mana]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Woollyback Transhumance

[[Puff]] grunted alongside [[Kauri]] to keep the [[Woollyback]] flock moving as they began transhumance from [[The Ouroboros]] across the pastures of [[The Screed]].

### 393 AC - Defending Flock from Wyvern

During a severe storm at [[The Screed]], Puff charged a wyvern that swooped down on the [[Woollyback]] flock, buying [[Kauri]] time to lead the sheep to the pen.

### 393 AC - Wounds Healed by Kauri

[[Kauri]] drained [[Mana]] from surrounding plants and directed it toward Puff to heal wounds sustained defending the flock from the wyvern.

### 393 AC - Musubi Ceremony

Puff and [[Kauri]] underwent [[The Musubi]] bonding ceremony presided over by [[Eru]], with Kauri's parents and woodland animals present as witnesses.

### 393 AC, October - Intervening to Protect Halua

During the [[Bravos]] trap operation against [[Halua]], Puff carried [[Kauri]] in armored bird form through hunter crossfire. Puff's mineral plumage absorbed lances, arrows, and rockets as they weaved between islets to shield Halua from the hunters' line of fire.

### 393 AC, October - Harpoon Strike and Berserk Charge

A harpoon struck Puff and [[Kauri]], causing Kauri to fall and lose control of his [[Alteration]]. Puff entered a berserk state and charged into a [[Bravos]] unit, attacking hunters with horns and hooves.

### 393 AC, October - Shielding Kauri During Skein Contact

When [[Kauri]] extended his [[The Skein|Skein]] to make contact with [[Halua]], Puff shielded Kauri from the Leviathan's overwhelming consciousness. Puff absorbed the emotional torrent through his vaporous fleece, allowing Kauri to experience Halua's memories without being consumed by them.
