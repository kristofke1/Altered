# Nadir

## Overview

Nadir, born 383 AC, is a [[Lyra]] [[Exalts|Exalt]] and [[Alterers|Alterer]] with demiurgic abilities, specializing in painting-based [[Ignescence]]. Bonded to [[Bubbles]], a carp-like [[Chimerae|Chimera]], he serves as vanguard in the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. Nadir was found as a newborn on his adoptive parents' doorstep in [[The Wayfarer]] following a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity collision, and his true origins remain unknown.

## Background

### Origin and Adoption

Approximately nine years before Nadir became an Exalt, the [[Lyra]] Sahanka [[The Wayfarer]] collided with a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity. That same event deposited a naked, silent newborn on the doorstep of [[Ghamam]] and [[Kinta]], two painters of the [[Tisdhera Clan]]. [[Kinta]] wrapped him in cloth and blanket, and the couple raised him as their own son.

Explaining the baby's sudden arrival to neighbors required considerable deception. As Nadir grew, his parents invented increasingly far-fetched excuses for the bizarre phenomena that followed in his wake. The Lyra's tolerance for the strange aided their efforts to conceal his unusual nature.

### Childhood in The Wayfarer

Nadir grew up in [[The Wayfarer]], the [[Sahanka]] of the [[Tisdhera Clan]]. The family resided in a house with a capricious, temperamental personality—its sliding workshop door jammed during plastering or painting work, its stove refused to function during certain meals, and its bathroom played hide-and-seek after extended water usage.

From earliest childhood, Nadir spent time in his parents' painting studio, which had large bay windows and stored solvents and paints. He filled notebooks with pastel and wax-crayon drawings, progressing to charcoal, colored pencils, gouache, and watercolor as he matured. He developed from simple doodles to elaborate portraits, still lifes, and landscapes. While classmates engaged in stunts and pranks, Nadir sat in corners sketching, his fingers routinely stained with pigment or charcoal.

Nadir's quiet demeanor and solitary behavior prompted teachers to summon his parents frequently. [[Kinta]] maintained this was simply his nature. [[Ghamam]] considered leaving [[The Wayfarer]] so Nadir would not have to endure the clowning that characterized the [[Tisdhera Clan]], but Kinta insisted moving would be worse. Other children teased Nadir because he did not talk much, but he communicated primarily through drawings.

### Training Under Zahira

When Nadir began painting the world through [[Ignescence]], the [[Tisdhera Clan]] Matriarch placed him in Zahira's care. [[Zahira]] was a mapmaker who served as the [[The Wayfarer|Wayfarer]]'s Inkwell—the cartographer responsible for drawing maps of explored territories. At first Nadir saw no connection between cartography and painting, but over time recognized both were ways of depicting the world.

Zahira insisted Nadir start with fundamentals: reproducing, imitating, and copying to improve technique before developing personal style. Nadir initially saw little value in the exercises but noticed his ability to identify and express his artistic intent improved as he practiced painting the external world. Zahira called this development "mastering your imagination." Through this discipline, [[Bubbles]]'s traits also became more refined—training Nadir understood was designed to prepare them for becoming an Exalt.

### Bond with Bubbles

Nadir first introduced [[Bubbles]] to his parents as an imaginary friend; they could also see the creature. Ghamam and Kinta immediately recognized Bubbles as a [[Chimerae|Chimera]], though they never learned where Nadir had found the large carp. Initially appearing daily to play, Bubbles eventually came to live with the family permanently.

The house reacted to Bubbles's presence with jammed doors and malfunctioning fixtures. Bubbles's size caused damage—vases, shelves, and most notably the family's porcelain collection suffered from tail and fin collisions.

### Manifestation of Alteration Powers

Around the time Bubbles joined the household, Nadir began displaying exceptional [[Alteration]] abilities. Paper and canvas no longer sufficed; he began painting directly onto reality. One evening, his parents returned from the Painthouse to find the living room transformed—Nadir had turned it into a pool of paint so Bubbles could swim.

Nadir frequently reshaped his surroundings through [[Ignescence]]. In a single afternoon, he conjured an entire playground on Dali Square—slides, merry-go-rounds, swings, candy-apple stalls, and carnival rides. The [[Tisdhera Clan]] elders reprimanded the family. Weeks later, he transformed the school into a zoo, bringing to life every animal his classmates had sketched for him on paper. Another time he manifested a pirate ship crewed entirely by sailor anteaters in the middle of a street.

After each incident, Ghamam and Kinta discussed boundaries with Nadir. [[Ignescence]] should not be silenced or suppressed, only channeled to a canvas broad enough to express itself without causing chaos.

### The Tumult Incident and Recognition as Exalt

While [[The Wayfarer]] drifted over the sea at the edge of the [[Terra Incognita]], a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] storm struck. The [[Sahanka]] lurched as the city shifted shape in uncontrolled spasms. Nadir and his parents sheltered in a neighborhood bunker with other residents, the heavy hatch sealed behind them.

During the night, while Ghamam and Kinta slept, Nadir went to the armored hatch and began opening it. Other adults failed to stop him in time. When the bolts slid back and the [[The Tumult|Tumult]] surged inward, its currents engulfed Nadir and [[Bubbles]]. Those inside braced for impact, but the winds did not flood the shelter. Nadir stood at the threshold as Tumult currents wrapped around him in rose-colored patterns. His form shifted repeatedly but returned to its original shape each time. The storm subsided, and a wave of colors radiated from Nadir across the entire city.

When survivors emerged from shelters, they found the city intact and untouched by the Tumult, save for locations transformed into art—trees made of pigment, fountains flowing with watercolor, buildings turned to woodblock prints.

The [[Tisdhera Clan]] Matriarch learned of Nadir's actions and summoned the family through her Shepherd. After waiting in the antechamber of the Big Top, they were received by the Matriarch's representative of [[Thalia]]. The Matriarch offered them cakes and tea. Without removing her mask, she studied Nadir and questioned him at length about what he had experienced facing pure Tumult and whether he had heard its call.

At the interrogation's end, the Matriarch declared that Nadir and Bubbles would become an Exalt. She would ensure they received education and training needed to join the vanguard of the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. When Ghamam protested that a child could not be sent to face the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]'s perils, the Matriarch remained firm. She stated Nadir was no ordinary boy—his demiurgic abilities would only grow and must be channeled accordingly.

When [[The Wayfarer]] set out toward the unknown, Nadir departed with the expedition.

## Abilities

Nadir specializes in painting-based [[Ignescence]], using [[Alteration]] to reshape reality through artistic vision. He can transform environments, manifest entities from paintings, and summon [[Eidolons]] including [[Nereid]], [[Oread]], and [[Nuit]]. His demiurgic abilities are capable of protecting an entire [[Sahanka]] from a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] storm. Until he achieves complete control over his [[Alteration]], Nadir is required to be supervised by an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] during field operations. Through [[The Musubi]], he is bonded with [[Bubbles]], with whom he exhibits perfect osmosis.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Painting in the Painthouse at City of Scholars
Nadir painted flying islands and clouds in the Painthouse while [[Bubbles]] floated under the glass roof, working through artistic phases from ukiyo-e to impressionism to fauvism, with Art Nouveau and Klimt increasingly catching his eye.

### 393 AC - Painting Nuit in the Painthouse
Nadir painted a depiction of Nuit, an Egyptian goddess [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] hugging the sun and cradling it beneath a cloak of stars, working in the Painthouse while planning to add golden Egyptian patterns and an eclipse.

### 393 AC - Forest Nymph Guidance
Nadir collected dead wood for a fire while a dryad guided him through a forest, and manifested leaves of gouache to flutter around her as thanks.

### 393 AC - Mapmaking with Zahira
Nadir worked on surveying sketches under Zahira's supervision while she drew a map of the [[Thessalian Quadrant]], taking readings with tripod, poles, sighting station, and reflector prisms alongside explorers.

### 393 AC - Crossing Water with Nereid
Nadir summoned a Nereid [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to transform a lake into a Japanese woodblock-style theater set with removable panels, allowing him to walk across sandy ground between static rollers.

### 393 AC - Mountain Descent with Oread
Nadir called on an Oread [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to surf a controlled landslide down a mountain, painting rocks in a style inspired by August Macke—required to be supervised by an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] until achieving complete control over his [[Alteration]].

### 393 AC - Watching Kasirga Aerialists
Nadir watched [[Kasirga Clan]] aerialists descend from heights with colorful ribbons while [[The Ouroboros]] and [[The Wayfarer]] traveled side by side with frequent shuttles between the two [[Sahanka|Sahankas]].

### 393 AC - Observing Koi in the Thessalian Quadrant
Nadir watched [[Koi]], a carp [[Leviathans|Leviathan]], dance above the rice fields of the [[Thessalian Quadrant]] while [[Bubbles]] played with it, and sketched the aerial ballet in his notebook to show Zahira.

### 393 AC - Rescuing Three Little Pigs in Thermaic Quadrant
Nadir and [[Bubbles]] rescued the [[Three Little Pigs]] [[Eidolons]] from gravity currents in the [[Thermaic Quadrant]] that threatened to sweep them away, jumping in despite knowing it would earn a scolding.

### 393 AC - Pelagonian Quadrant Expedition
Nadir and [[Bubbles]] exhibited perfect osmosis during an expedition to the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]] organized by [[Leocardius Sree]], examining frescoes in ruins with architecture similar to the [[City of Scholars]] while [[Yanna]] of [[The Qorgan]] surveilled them and concluded Nadir was not a [[The Perjurers|Perjurer]].
