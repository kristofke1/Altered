# Fen

## Overview

Fen, born 373 AC, is a [[Lyra]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] from the [[Kasirga Clan]]. She is bonded with [[Crowbar]], a scarecrow [[Chimerae|Chimera]] with a pumpkin head. Fen joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] seeking [[The Song of One]] and [[Mnemosyne]] to lift the banishment of the [[Kasirga Clan]] from the [[Lyra]] alliance.

## Background

Fen's grandfather was [[Cayrat Steinn]], the great Caravan Shepherd of the [[Kasirga Clan]]. She grew up on [[The Ouroboros]], the ring-shaped [[Sahanka]] of the [[Kasirga Clan]], waking to contemplate the horizon from bay windows. She would climb access ramps, greet troubadours and herdsmen, and walk along the circus throwing smiles to aerialists and clowns. One day, Fen found a small frail bird on an endless plain of withered grass and brought it to [[Berchad]] the pigeon fancier. When she returned home, many people were waiting with tearful eyes. [[Cao]] led her to her grandfather's room where [[Cayrat Steinn]] lay with eyes closed, not moving. That night, Fen took refuge in the pigeon loft, stroking the feathers of the small bird she named [[Juju]].

[[Cayrat Steinn]] had named [[Esmeralda]] (an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]]) to take his place as Shepherd, and the elders agreed. However, the Matriarch opposed this decision. The Matriarchs of all the Clans conferred and condemned the decision, proclaiming that if the [[Kasirga Clan]] persisted, it would be banished from the [[Lyra]] alliance. Fen tried to convince their Matriarch to reconsider, reminding them there had always been nine Clans among the [[Lyra]] and they had sung [[The Song of One]] together. [[Esmeralda]] decided to entrust the fate to the [[Kasirga Clan|Kasirga]] elders, and they insisted she take the reins. The banishment was proclaimed. [[The Ouroboros]] set course for [[Arkaster]] to stay there until the Expeditionary Corps were ready to depart.

Fen attempted to have the sentence lifted, but no one listened. [[The Song of One]] had been forgotten, its words lost. With friends, she founded a music group: Fen on vocals, [[Tamati]] on percussion, [[Boona]] on bass, [[Orbec]] on keyboard, and [[Crowbar]] on guitar. Together, they scoured bars and concert halls, making a name for themselves. During one performance, Fen met [[Nevenka]] who had also been expelled from her Clan. Together, they roamed the streets, Fen keeping watch while Nev graffitied on walls, then fled laughing to take refuge in a tavern. When Fen asked [[Nevenka]] why she had joined the exploration forces, Nevenka turned the question back. Fen told her about her Clan's situation and the search for [[The Song of One]]. Nev suggested finding [[Mnemosyne]], the goddess of memory and mother of the Muses.

Fen became a [[Lyra]] Exalt and joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] to find [[The Song of One]] and locate [[Mnemosyne]], whose authority over [[The Nine Sisters]] might compel them to sway [[The Nine Matriarchs]] and lift the [[Kasirga Clan]]'s banishment.

[[Crowbar]] played electric guitar with [[Boona]] on bass and [[Tamati]] on drums while Fen sang, performing last concert in [[Arkaster]] before departure.

### 392 AC - Performance with Crowbar
Fen drew from [[Ignescence]] while [[Crowbar]] played powerful riff across distance, summoning [[The Sandman|Sandman]], [[Cloth Dancer]], and singing to transform world with music and [[Asmodeus]]'s laughter.

### 392 AC - Waru's Dismissal
[[Waru Toowoom]] dismissed Fen's claims with single gesture as part of campaign obstructing [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] operations.

### 393 AC, January-April - Hot Spring Discussion at Storvhit
Fen bathed in hot springs at [[Storhvit]] alongside [[Auraq]] and [[Nevenka]]. Fen outlined her plan to [[Auraq]]: find [[The Song of One]] to reunite the [[Lyra]] [[The Nine Matriarchs|Matriarchs]] as [[Chiara Caceres]] once did, and seek [[Mnemosyne]] whose authority as mother of [[The Nine Sisters|the Muses]] could sway the Matriarchs. During the conversation, Fen heard voices carried by the Wind describing a trapped entity of lightning, rain, and ice, and declared it was an [[Oneiros]] that needed help.

### 393 AC, April-May - Conversing with Kuraokami
Fen conversed with [[Kuraokami]], an imprisoned primordial dragon [[Oneiros]] at the heart of [[Cais Adarra]], using her affinity with the Wind. She projected her voice to the entity's dwelling and heard its words with far greater clarity than previous scouts—an actual exchange of intelligible speech rather than mere echo. [[Kuraokami]] claimed to be an [[Oneiros]] imprisoned by a human seeking to harness its power, embodying rain and snow, and commanded Fen to set it free. Fen's successful communication provided [[Temera Singh]] and the expedition with critical intelligence about the source of [[Belisenki]] hostilities.

### 393 AC, June-September - Pre-Concert Preparation at The Stage
Fen prepared for [[BLISS!]] concert at [[The Stage]], applying makeup and warming up voice while the band flew over camp throwing confetti to announce the show.

### 393 AC, June-September - Sap Duende Rehearsal
Fen summoned [[Ole Bull]], [[Ethel Waters]], and [[Baishan]] during rehearsal, with [[Boona]] preparing [[Sap]]-coated mandolin for [[Baishan]]'s synesthetic performance.

### 393 AC, June-September - BLISS! Concert Memory Loss
Fen performed with [[BLISS!]] at [[The Screed]], drawing on [[Ignescence]] to maintain energy despite exhaustion from a week of daily descents into the [[City of Scholars]]. Mid-performance, Fen and [[Crowbar]] simultaneously suffered violent episodes—Fen lost the ability to recall lyrics and stammered before the audience while [[Crowbar]] experienced a migraine causing missed notes. [[Tamati]] attempted to cover by finishing the verse while [[Orbec]] improvised a solo. Fen revealed to [[Crowbar]] that she could no longer recall the song's words.

### 393 AC, June-September - Investigating Memory Loss at City of Scholars
[[Nevenka]] brought Fen into the [[City of Scholars]] corridors to search for her lost memories. Fen described the experience as words being removed entirely rather than simply forgotten, requiring [[Tamati]] to repeat information and reviewing notes to re-learn everything. While [[Nevenka]] activated black memory stones with [[Ignescence]], Fen absorbed songs and lamentations from the sealed fragments—expressions from a lost age. The stones contained sealed memories from another time, preserved within the rock.

### 393 AC, September - Reaching Sofia Within The Hunger
Fen participated in the Exalt operation against [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]]. While [[Teija]] meditated atop a memory block with [[Nauraa]] shielding them, Fen sat at Teija's side and extended her consciousness into the entity. She broke through layers of accumulated memories that had encrusted the creature over ages. Deep within, Fen discovered [[Sofia]], a honey-gold child suspended in a golden bubble, dreaming in fetal position. Fen projected memories of companionship—fireside gatherings on [[The Ouroboros]], the roar of crowds after concerts, [[Nevenka]]'s antics—and when Sofia opened her eyes and reached back, the child remembered and spoke her own name.

### 393 AC - Groundhog Weather Scout
Fen materialized a groundhog [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] equipped with an umbrella and climbing gear to scout weather conditions after rain forced the group to shelter in a cave.

## Abilities

Fen possesses an affinity with the Wind that allows her to hear voices and communicate across great distances, as demonstrated in her conversation with [[Kuraokami]]. She draws on [[Ignescence]] to fuel her performances and [[Alteration]]. She can summon multiple [[Eidolons]] including [[Anansi]], [[The Sandman]], [[Cloth Dancer]], [[Ole Bull]], [[Ethel Waters]], [[Baishan]], and [[Asmodeus]]. She is also capable of projecting her consciousness into other entities, as she did when reaching [[Sofia]] within [[The Hunger]]. Fen is a vocalist and frontwoman of the band [[BLISS!]].
