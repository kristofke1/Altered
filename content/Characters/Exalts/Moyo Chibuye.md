# Moyo Chibuye

## Overview

Moyo Chibuye is a [[Yzmir]] [[Initiates|Initiate]] and exobiological researcher who has developed a bond with [[Silk]], a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] larva discovered in [[The Nilam]]. This bond has prompted [[Yzmir]] leadership to consider Moyo as a candidate for [[The Musubi]] ceremony.

## Appearance

Moyo carries a gnarled staff on his shoulder and a [[Mana Moths|Moth]] lantern in his other hand to illuminate underground shadows. He wears a wide hat, and on its brim and on his cloak lapel hang countless bluish cocoons—diaphanous, glowing moth chrysalids. Some hatch during travel, releasing moths that fly around him before disappearing in collisions of dust.

## Background

Moyo specializes in xenobiology and was taken under [[Saskia Averina]]'s wing as an assistant. Many believe him to be an expert on insects and parasites. His weakness is his youth and the naivety that comes with it, making him approachable and open when discussing his career and mentor.

## Research Work

### 393 AC - Mana Moths Study at Storvhit

Moyo led exobiological research on [[Mana Moths]], an endemic species at [[Storvhit]], working with [[Saskia Averina]] who assisted the research efforts. Research conducted at a greenhouse facility in the arctic section, studying chrysalides attached to branches and observing moth hatching processes. [[Akesha Adhikari]] visited during the research phase, witnessing detailed observations of moth lifecycle and [[Mana]] emission patterns. Study investigated reproductive cycles, metamorphosis processes, and the ecological role of [[Mana Moths]] within the [[Storvhit]] ecosystem. Moyo's exobiological focus complemented [[Saskia Averina]]'s broader species interaction research including symbiosis and parasitism. Research contributed to understanding [[Belisenki]]'s symbiotic relationship with [[Mana Moths]] and pheromone-based protection mechanism.

### March 26, 393 AC - Theories Enable Belisenki Territory Passage

Moyo's theories about [[Mana Moths]] and [[Belisenki]] relationship enabled the [[Expeditionary Corps]] to successfully traverse [[Belisenki]] territory without incident. [[Temera Singh]] credited Moyo's work in a March 26, 393 AC log entry documenting establishment of makeshift camp at [[Cais Adarra]] foothills. Practical application of research findings neutralized [[Belisenki]] threat through understanding of pheromone-based protection mechanism.

### 393 AC - Moth Reproductive Cycle Short-Circuit Technique

Working in the refrigerated greenhouse of [[The Ouroboros]], Moyo demonstrated an ability to bypass the [[Mana Moths]] reproductive cycle entirely by placing a moth egg on fingertip, flooding it with ideas drawn from [[The Aether|Aether]] currents beyond [[The Veil]], then blowing the egg onto a manifested [[Eidolons|Eidolon]]—the egg consumed the Eidolon's ideas to instantaneously form an adult moth, eliminating the normal caterpillar and cocoon stages.

### 393 AC - Vivarium Supervision at The Screed

At [[The Screed]] outpost, Moyo jointly supervised [[The Vivarium]] with [[Saskia Averina]], an artificial habitat within [[Nilam Spires]] where teams of entomologists raised swarms of [[Mana Moths]]. The habitat was crafted from [[The Nilam|Nilam]] bark and branches.

### 393 AC - City of Scholars Descent

Moyo left [[Nilam Spires]] to descend into [[City of Scholars]] to test connection with [[Mana Moths]], continuing research into species behavior patterns.

### 393 AC - Bond with Silk and Musubi Consideration

Moyo attended a meeting at [[Nilam Spires]] observatory where [[Zaj Ke Va]] questioned them about the [[Silk]] larva. Since discovering the creature in [[The Nilam]]'s bark, Moyo had been the one feeding it. The larva—now pig-sized and growing exponentially—displayed attachment to Moyo like a dog to its master, reacting differently in Moyo's presence and seeking out their touch.

Moyo theorized that Silk's existence was no coincidence—its idea lingered in the fossilized trunk while its physical shell endured the frost. When questioned about a connection to [[Mana Moths]], Moyo could only respond "perhaps." Moyo also harbored suspicions that the [[Storvhit]] ecosystem seemed artificial, almost manufactured, as if its laws had been imposed rather than evolving naturally—though they chose to discuss this with Saskia rather than the assembly.

After the meeting, [[Sylas]] approached Moyo cryptically, implying that Moyo would soon understand what their bond with Silk meant—hinting at a potential [[The Musubi|Musubi]] ceremony.
