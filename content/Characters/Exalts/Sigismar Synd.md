# Sigismar Synd

## Overview

Sigismar Synd (also known as Sig) is an [[Ordis]] Exalt and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who serves as a Paladin of [[The Aegis]]. He is bonded with his Alter Ego [[Wingspan]], a griffin [[Chimerae|Chimera]], through [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Sigismar descends from [[Ayxas]], the third Kuningas of [[Asgartha]], and carries the weight of his ancestor's infamous legacy.

## Family Background and Childhood Stigma

Sigismar comes from a wealthy noble family residing in an opulent palazzo in [[Arkaster]]. His lineage traces back to [[Ayxas]], the third Kuningas of [[Asgartha]], whose name is infamous throughout all history books for turning the kingdom into a tyrannical regime. His father is a relentless and draconian politician who worked late in the corridors of the Basileus's palace, infiltrating diplomatic circles through intimidation and deception. His mother pampered him with gifts and indulged his every whim, though these came with the expectation of studying etiquette, music, eloquence, rhetoric, history, and sciences. 

As a child, Sigismar became acutely aware of the stigma that weighed on himÃ¢â‚¬â€adults prevented their children from playing with him, and his birthday parties were deserted. His father ordered him to stand tall, enduring the loneliness and exclusion. Whenever he managed to escape his tutors' watchful eyes, Sigismar would wander across the island, hiding among the vineyards, escaping his isolation by imagining a thousand adventures and playing with the [[Eidolons]] he summoned, who never judged him.

One instructor named [[Kadri]] (whom Sigismar affectionately called "old wolf") took him under his wing and taught him that children were not responsible for the sins of their parents. [[Kadri]] taught Sigismar the basics of [[Alteration]], and with time he touched a certain inner peace. Sigismar wanted to break the wheel of fate once and for all.

When Sigismar was seven years old, his sister [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] was born after a difficult labor. His father forbade him from seeing his mother during the confinement, and Sigismar felt a selfish anger at not being able to join the celebrations. When finally allowed inside, he saw his mother extend her hand, radiant with happiness despite exhaustion, holding the tiny newborn. Seven years separated the siblings, creating a certain reservation between them. [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun|Anuncia]] benefited from their father's leniency while Sigismar bore the burden of succession, and when he joined the [[Ordis]], their father shifted all attention to [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun|Anuncia]].

Sigismar possesses a ring with a diamond band, connected to his romantic relationship with [[Hasret]].

## Joining the Ordis and Meeting Wingspan

The [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] resonated with Sigismar as a chance to wipe the slate clean and make a name for himself. He decided to leave his clerkship and join the exploration forces, enlisting in the [[Ordis]] to pursue a military career. His father persuaded the military hierarchy to grant his son a dispensation for a political career instead, but as Sigismar was being escorted out of [[The Rampart]], bells rang and alarms sounded. He rushed to the fortifications and saw soldiers grappling with [[The Tumult|Tumult]] creatures. A defeated [[Chimerae|Chimera]] resembling a griffin crashed near him, pregnant and dying. He extracted a scrawny but still living griffin from its belly, and the creature curled up in his arms. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] granted his request to join the exploration forces on the spot. Sigismar named the griffin [[Wingspan]].

## Training and Musubi Bond

Sigismar took care of [[Wingspan]] every day, and the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] grew in size and strength, becoming the darling of their regiment. Sigismar asked if [[Kadri]] could become his designated instructor for [[Alteration]] and was trained daily in weapons handling by [[Eidolons]] like [[Jeanne d'Arc]], [[Bedivere]], and [[Robin Hood]]. Through [[The Musubi]] ceremony, Sigismar and [[Wingspan]] became Alter Egos, their souls bound together.

## Quest for Truth

Secretly, Sigismar dreams of shedding light on [[Ayxas]]'s actions. His ancestor had uttered a curse during his exile in [[The Tumult]], decreeing that humanity was not ready to face what lurked there. In his spare time, Sigismar combs through old grimoires in search of clues, though nothing concrete has emerged. He hopes that when he walks on [[Caer Oorun]], he will find preserved tomes recounting [[Ayxas]]'s story more accurately.

## Role in the Rediscovery Endeavor

As a Paladin of [[The Aegis]], Sigismar serves in the Expeditionary Corps. During the battle against [[Kraken]], he flew on [[Wingspan]] serving as [[The Aegis]]'s banner bearer, conjuring a banner using [[Alteration]].

## Timeline of Activities

### 368 AC - Consoled by Ozma
After bullying at school for his lineage, [[Ozma]] appeared and comforted Sigismar, stroking his hair and reassuring him he bore no responsibility for ancestors' sins.

### 371 AC - Carriage Ride to Monolith
Sigismar rode in carriage past [[Arkaster]] streets gazing at [[The Monolith]], wanting to visit Sanctum library but mother postponed.

### 386 AC - Training with Jeanne d'Arc
Sigismar sparred with [[Jeanne d'Arc]] who taught him that [[The Aegis|Aegis]] soldiers must be instruments dedicated to completing mission bigger than themselves.

### 387 AC - Chess with Kakoba
Sigismar played chess with Legion Commander [[Kakoba]], opening with queen pawn while sizing up opponent's strategy and personality through game.

### 390 AC - Cadets Meeting Wingspan
Sigismar landed [[Wingspan]] at Kurung courtyard where cadets abandoned training to pet griffin, and Sigismar answered their questions as Exalt figurehead.

### 390 AC - Hasret's Archive Theft
Spy [[Hasret]] stole scroll from Asterion archives about [[Ayxas]], containing attack pamphlet written by [[Vissentes Narses]] that might explain ancestor's offenses.

### 391 AC - Studying Ordis Carrier
Sigismar studied transporter at altiport with [[Sunisa]], memorizing details and associating Heka [[Glyph|Glyphs]] needed to summon it within [[The Tumult]].

### 391 AC - Recruit Inspection
Sigismar attended recruit inspection alongside Admiral [[Temera Singh]] and Commodore [[Jin-Hee Jeanty]], scrutinizing each recruit and introducing [[Nosk]] to Jeanty while instructor [[Gulrang Oduro]] assessed candidates for officer potential.

### 392 AC - Kraken Battle Coordination
Sigismar flew on [[Wingspan]] as banner bearer using [[Coalescence]] to coordinate assault vectors with regimental leaders, creating diversion for [[Muna]] Druids and [[Yzmir]] Mages.

### 392 AC - Conversation with Anuncia at Kuna
During Season of Mists shortly before departure, Sigismar visited family villa at [[Kuna]] where [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] found him brooding with ring, and they discussed his departure and her engagement to [[Amaro Arundhani]].

### 392 AC - Bureaucratic Frustrations Aboard Ouroboros
[[Waru Toowoom]] blocked various [[Ordis]] initiatives and confiscated materials, creating tensions for Sigismar.

### 393 AC - Receiving Tumult Warning at Ice Palace
Sigismar received [[Paper Herald]] from Admiral [[Temera Singh|Singh]] at ice palace warning that [[The Ouroboros]] detected approaching [[The Tumult|Tumult]] waves, ordering immediate evacuation to [[Cais Adarra]] Oasis, and he rushed to inform General [[Sekifu]].

### 393 AC - Capturing Jinn at Mountain Pass
Sigismar captured a furious [[Eidolons|Jinn]] blocking a narrow mountain pass by using [[Glyph|Glyphs]] written on paper to create a paper cage trap, while his troops wagered [[The Isura|Florets]] on the outcome.

### 393 AC - Hunting Thieving Magpeng
Sigismar used [[Heka]] to summon bloodhounds by stamping a [[Glyph]] on a birch trunk, tracking a [[Magpengs|Magpeng]] that had stolen items from camp including a ring from [[Hasret]], on assignment from [[Fen]] to locate the creature's nest.

### 393 AC - Crossing Frozen Lake
Sigismar led his troops in single file across a frozen lake with bioluminescent activity and massive silhouettes visible in the crystal-clear ice below, when a beast erupted through the surface at the vanguard position.

### 393 AC - Mushroom Forest Patrol
Sigismar patrolled through forest with mushroom-covered tree trunks and drifting spores with new aide-de-camp [[Nina]], detecting suspicious rustling that proved to be a knee-high [[Living Snowman]] [[Chimerae|Chimera]] carrying camping gear on its back.

### 393 AC - At The Screed Before Crow's Eye Descent
Sigismar spoke with [[Teija]] at [[The Screed]] before Teija's descent into [[The Crow's Eye]], cautioning them about sealed lower levels and potential [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity at the bottom, and patted [[Nauraa]]'s neck as he would his griffon [[Wingspan]].

### 393 AC - Confrontation with The Hunger
Sigismar participated in the Exalt operation against [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]]. He watched [[Teija]] meditate atop a memory block attempting psychic contact with the entity while waiting for his signal to unleash an attack. Through [[Heka]], Sigismar summoned the Gestalt's Rhombus and pressed it to a memory block to monitor Teija's safety from a distance. When [[The Hunger]] shifted to aggression and [[Atsadi]] and [[Kojo Oduro]] engaged in combat, Sigismar held back his forces until [[Treyst]] triggered the detonation that destroyed [[The Maze]].

### 393 AC, June-August - Ordis Roll Call at The Screed
Sigismar endured a cockatoo Ordis Crier reading names at [[The Screed]] while jays unfurled the roll call, mentally connecting with [[Sunisa]] via [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]] as she ranted about cooking the annoying bird.

### 393 AC, June-August - Receiving Special Assignment
Sigismar waited in the staff clerk's tent at [[The Screed]], observing the map of the Abyss covered with urgent reinforcement requests, before receiving a cryptic "special assignment" that shocked him when he read the orders.

### 393 AC, June-August - Recruitment Video Flub
Sigismar filmed a recruitment video for the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] with [[Nosk]] operating camera, but flubbed the closing line by saying "needs... me" while [[Sunisa]] watched and laughed.

### 393 AC - Woollyback Herding Assignment

Sigismar was assigned to watch over herds of Woollybacks as punishment after his failed recruitment speech.

## Abilities

Sigismar practices Heka [[Alteration]], using [[Glyph|Glyphs]] to create protective barriers, make his blade incandescent, and conjure banners. He serves as a Paladin of [[The Aegis]] rather than pursuing the political career his father intended.
