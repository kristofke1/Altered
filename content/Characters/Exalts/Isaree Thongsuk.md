# Isaree Thongsuk

## Overview

Isaree Thongsuk, born 366 AC, is an [[Axiom]] [[Exalts|Exalt]] and [[Alterers|Alterer]] who specializes in geology and lapidary work. She is bonded with [[Pebble]], a golem-like [[Chimerae|Chimera]] with [[Aerolith]] veins, through [[The Musubi]] ceremony. Born into a wealthy family in [[Hadera]], Isaree played a central role in the [[384 AC Enosha Strikes]] before fleeing to [[Arkaster]] and joining the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. She conducts secret research on [[Aerolith]]'s electromagnetic properties while working on mining operations in the [[Cloud Realm]].

## Background

Isaree was born in 366 AC to [[Rama Thongsuk]], a geologist and philosopher, and [[Meredith]], a shipowner affiliated with the [[Quarrymen's Guild]]. Her father frequently descended into the [[Enosha Mines]] ostensibly to collect [[Aerolith]] samples, but used these visits to assist miners with correspondence and bureaucratic problems. He brought young Isaree to the mine gates and taverns where workers gathered, and she became known among the staff as "the professor's kid." Her childhood friend [[Jethro]] was among those she grew up with around the mines.

In 384 AC, the [[384 AC Enosha Strikes]] erupted after a mine disaster killed approximately twenty miners. Isaree organized a field hospital during rescue efforts, then joined the strike committee as delegate. Her mother [[Meredith]], elected board chair, ordered Isaree to withdraw and threatened disinheritance; Isaree refused and left the family home. The conflict escalated into violent confrontation before [[The Aegis]] intervened. Isaree was beaten while trying to block the shafts.

Following the violence, Isaree fled [[Hadera]] as a stowaway on one of her family's airships. She reached [[Arkaster]] and found refuge at [[The Foundry]], accepting work as an apprentice to Master [[Basem Falani]], an old friend of her father who ran a gemsmith workshop. She discovered talent for lapidary work and began using [[Alteration]] to enhance her jewelry.

She earned degrees in geology and specialized in [[Aerolith]] studies, following her father's academic path. She sent her earnings to miners' families through the former head of the Lapidaries' Guild and maintained correspondence with [[Rama Thongsuk]]. [[Meredith]]'s influence continued to follow her—funding disappeared and doors closed whenever Isaree attempted major projects. She applied to the [[Expeditionary Corps]], beginning as a maintenance agent for [[The Ouroboros]]'s [[Aerolith]] core. When the Corps discovered new deposits of floating stone, her expertise led to assignment organizing mining preparations.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, June-September - First Encounter with Pebble

During a prospecting mission to assess a vein for mining safety, Isaree conducted preliminary analyses when a humanoid figure nearly three meters tall emerged from the rock wall behind her. The golem-like creature, later named [[Pebble]], had veins of [[Aerolith]] across its stone skin and round, glassy rose-colored eyes. As Isaree retreated toward the cavern entrance, the creature placed its hand on the rock wall and caused [[Aerolith]] crystals to sprout from the stone.

### 393 AC, June-September - Gavroche Collecting Aerolith in Ruins

Isaree observed [[Gavroche]] collecting [[Aerolith]] nuggets from ruins in the [[Cloud Realm]] for energy specialists, singing about Voltaire and Rousseau while tossing fist-sized crystals into his basket.

### 393 AC, June-September - Demolition Operations with Angus

Isaree worked alongside [[Angus]], a one-armed demolition expert, as he placed charges along [[Aerolith]] deposits to reveal veins without triggering chain reactions.

### 393 AC, June-September - Titan Operation Observation

Isaree observed [[Vassili]] operating a [[Constructs|Titan]] at an [[Aerolith]] quarry using a steering gauntlet that reproduced his movements in the machine.

### 393 AC, June-September - Aerolith Quarry Operations

Isaree returned to an open-pit [[Aerolith]] quarry in the [[Cloud Realm]] that had been established during her absence, with cranes, excavators, refineries, and a drone factory deployed.

### 393 AC, June-September - Discovering Pebble's Field Generation

Isaree observed [[Pebble]] navigating floating rocks and noted the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] appeared to generate a field that caused [[Aerolith]]-veined stones to part before it, potentially manipulating the direction of the levitation field.

### 393 AC, June-September - AES Kurush Escort Mission

Isaree served aboard [[AES Kurush]] escorting the supertanker [[Pantagruel]] during ore collection, docking at a restocking station to refuel [[Kelon]] reserves.

### 393 AC, June-September - Secret Aerolith Research with Marie Curie

Isaree conducted secret research on [[Aerolith]]'s electromagnetic properties with [[Marie Curie]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]], using materials obtained from [[Kelon]] reserves.

### 393 AC, June-September - Aerolith Energy Potential Assessment

After witnessing the detonation of [[Aerolith]] deposits, Isaree began considering Aerolith's potential as an alternative energy source—noting that in an era of plentiful [[Kelon]] there had been no need for alternatives, and that [[Sap]] was too finite, but Aerolith's exothermic properties might be harnessed with current technology.

### 393 AC, June-September - Medusa Summoning During Leviathan Attack

During a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] attack near [[Aerolith]] veins, Isaree summoned [[Medusa]] to petrify [[Bravos]] [[Eidolons]] whose presence endangered the site, enabling evacuation.

### 393 AC, June-September - Exploring Abandoned Depot

Isaree explored an abandoned depot in the [[Cloud Realm]] with architectural similarities to [[City of Scholars]] and [[Sofia]], concluding the structures were built permanently rather than as temporary migration stations.

### 393 AC, October 23 - Halua Attack on Pelagonian Quarry

While exploring abandoned tunnels within an ancient [[Aerolith]] mine in the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]], Isaree discovered advanced technology far exceeding current Asgarthan capabilities—drills, extractors, and empty [[Sap]] canisters designed to hold vaporized Sap for rationed use. When [[Halua]] attacked the quarry, Isaree and [[Pebble]] escaped from the collapsing mine shaft. A child pilot in a white-shelled mech saved her from a falling crane but was crushed in the collapse. From a rocky summit, Isaree witnessed the [[Axiom]] and [[Bravos]] bases burning and [[AES Sune]] approaching before [[Halua]] dove and struck the cruiser.

## Abilities

Isaree specializes in geology and lapidary work, using [[Alteration]] to enhance her jewelry craftsmanship. She holds degrees in geology with a specialization in [[Aerolith]] studies. She can summon [[Eidolons]] including [[Medusa]] and [[Gavroche]]. Through [[The Musubi]], she is bonded with [[Pebble]], who can generate a field that causes [[Aerolith]]-veined stones to part, can sense buried veins even when deeply buried in rock, and can cause [[Aerolith]] crystals to sprout from stone.
