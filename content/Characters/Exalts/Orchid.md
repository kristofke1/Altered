# Orchid

## Overview

Orchid is a mantis [[Chimerae|Chimera]] who serves as the Alter Ego to [[Rin]] through their [[The Musubi|Musubi]] bond. She originally brought [[Rin]] as an infant from a distant [[Oases|Oasis]] beyond [[Asgartha]]'s borders, leaving her with [[Yonago]] to raise. Orchid's existence serves as proof that life is possible beyond the limits of the Peninsula, though she no longer remembers the exact location due to the chaotic nature of the world outside. She accompanies [[Rin]] on the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Physical Appearance

Orchid is a human-sized praying mantis with a rosy hue that mimics the delicate petals of an orchid flower. She has blade-like legs that shine in the moonlight, a shiny chitinous abdomen, yellow eyes that shine in darkness, and mandibles that click when she moves or communicates. Her prehensile appendages can be raised or lowered. She possesses iridescent wings that tremble and buzz frenetically like a hummingbird's in blustery wind. In arctic environments, her colors dull from vibrant pink to harsh cold white, becoming almost translucent.

## Origins and Early Bond with Rin

Orchid came from beyond [[Asgartha]]'s borders, tossed by tumultuous flows from a place where enormous flowers spring from the ground. She had taken in a baby ([[Rin]]) from that distant [[Oases|Oasis]] to keep her safe. Orchid brought [[Rin]] to [[Yonago]]'s doorstep one rainy evening, wrapping the infant in vines and bluish foliage in a cocoon lined with moss, then looked at [[Yonago]] for a long time before departing.

At least once a season, Orchid would return to the edge of the forest to see if [[Rin]] had grown well. [[Yonago]] would greet her with a nod before she disappeared back into the trees. When [[Rin]] eventually ventured into the spectral forest searching for her, Orchid emerged from the thickets and took [[Rin]] in a tender embrace. From that day, [[Rin]] returned daily to see Orchid, and they would catch fish together on riverbanks or clear thorny bushes to pick berries.

## Musubi Bond

Orchid and [[Rin]] bonded through [[The Musubi]] ceremony facilitated by [[Turuun]], accompanying her to [[The Spindle]] and [[Bark Refuge]]. Through the bond, they can converse through thoughts and share sensory experiences—when [[Rin]] eats, Orchid clicks her mandibles in response to the shared taste.

## Behavior

Orchid sometimes stays in gardens on the edge of gatherings rather than attend urban festivities. She is protective and caring, watching over [[Rin]] with benevolence. Orchid can display protective jealousy when others comfort [[Rin]], directing feelings of animosity toward those who provide maternal care.

## Timeline

### 388 AC - Spindle Tree Climb Support
Orchid watched [[Rin]] during difficult [[The Spindle|Spindle]] Tree climb, ready to spread wings to catch her in midair if needed.

### 390 AC - Son of Yggdrasil Transportation
Orchid accompanied [[Rin]] on [[Son of Yggdrasil]]'s shoulder, revealing during the journey that [[Rin]]'s origin was a place where enormous flowers spring from ground—meaning other [[Oases]] existed in Terra Incognita.

### 392 AC - Forest Path Clearing
Orchid led through dense forest using forelegs like sickles to prune thorny bushes while [[Rin]] released [[Mana]] to heal wounded plants.

### 393 AC - Rin's Rescue and Hunting at Storvhit
During [[Storvhit]] expedition, Orchid's colors dulled from vibrant pink to harsh cold white becoming almost translucent due to harsh environment. When [[Rin]] suffered hypothermia and fell into powder snow near sérac edge, Orchid assisted [[Arjun]] in digging out and rescuing bonded partner. After [[Arjun]] provided shelter in floral tent, Orchid went hunting for rabbit or marmot to help [[Rin]] recover from exposure. Orchid chittered warning with clicking mandibles before [[Rin]]'s fall, attempting to alert partner to danger. While [[Rin]] recovered, Orchid insisted on finding food to aid recovery process.

### ~393 AC - Jealousy Toward Saskia
When [[Saskia Averina]] comforted [[Rin]] after scolding her about carelessly summoning [[Persephone]], Orchid directed feelings of animosity toward the naturalist, causing [[Rin]] to pull away wondering if her [[Chimerae|Chimera]] was getting jealous.
