# Nevenka

## Overview

Nevenka, commonly called "Nev," is a [[Lyra]] [[Alterers|Alterer]] and Exalt from the [[Ossonoya Clan]]. She is a graffiti artist described as the "queen of fibs, the empress of hoaxes, the goddess in the making of fabrication, mystification, and inaccuracy." Her companion is [[Blotch]], a paint dragon [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. [[Esmeralda]] described Nevenka as a troublemaker capable of the best and the worst. Described as unpredictable and rebellious, she is unconcerned with taboos and lives for excess and transgression. She experiences persistent voices that she cannot silence. She enjoys chewing gum, creamy pea soup, graffiti and street art, and cotton candy.

## Early Life and Meeting Blotch

Nevenka was born within [[Mist]], the [[Sahanka]] of the [[Ossonoya Clan]]. From a young age, she struggled with boredom and killed time by bombarding walls with graffiti, collecting insults along the way. Her gang was entirely composed of [[Eidolons]], and she told her stories with paint bombs or brushes. Her mother is bedridden and nostalgic for her days as a stage diva, while her father has been absent most of the time since her birth. While wandering beneath the mighty chimneys of [[Mist]], Nevenka saw a colorful serpent dragon swirling in the clouds. She climbed ladders to get closer, but a maintenance platform gave way and she plummeted—the [[Chimerae|Chimera]] rushed to save her using its oily texture. She leaped from the base of the [[Sahanka]] and began to stalk the dragon, pursuing him for 64 days. The dragon revealed his name as Kutrabalamashablotchara, but Nev registered only "[[Blotch]]." [[Blotch]] had tested her to get a sense of her patience and perseverance, then agreed to become her sensei and taught her to meditate and control water.

## Banishment from Ossonoya Clan

According to rumors mentioned by [[Esmeralda]], Nevenka was banished from [[Mist]] by its Shepherd. The [[Ossonoya Clan]] is described as being fond of secrets.

## Relationship with Blotch

Over time, [[Blotch]] got used to Nev tailing him, and they started traveling together. He made Nev his sidekick, or as he jokes, "tolerated her presence like a dog forgets about a tick." Despite quarrels and gratuitous jabs, they became inseparable companions.

## Friendship with Fen

During one of [[Fen]]'s performances in [[Arkaster]], Nevenka met her. She too had been expelled from her Clan and was going to leave with her [[Chimerae|Chimera]] into [[The Tumult]] expanses. Despite their notable differences, they hit it off. Together, they roamed the streets, [[Fen]] keeping watch while Nev graffitied on walls, then they fled laughing to take refuge in a tavern. When [[Fen]] asked why she had joined the exploration forces, Nevenka turned the question back. Upon hearing about [[Fen]]'s situation and dream of finding [[The Song of One]], Nev suggested she apply to be an Exalt too and suggested finding [[Mnemosyne]], the goddess of memory.

## Role as Exalt

Nevenka joined the exploration forces and became a [[Lyra]] Exalt. She participates in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] with her companion [[Blotch]].

## Timeline

### 390 AC - Pickpocket Encounter
Young girl tried stealing from Nevenka, but Nevenka walked through walls to corner thief in dead-end and played dice game for trinket.

### 390 AC - Graffiti Chase and Paint Prison
After graffiti on Basileus's palace wall, [[The Aegis|Aegis]] agents pursued Nevenka with [[Eidolons]], and [[Blotch]] grabbed [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] from wall and transformed it into mural.

### 391 AC - First Visit to The Ouroboros
Nevenka explored [[The Ouroboros]] with [[Fen]], navigating gangways through construction areas filled with taverns, gambling houses, and big top circus.

### 391 AC - Casino Croupier Card Game
Nevenka played cards against croupier at casino, considering using [[Alteration]] to transform cards while losing track of strategy.

### 391 AC - Asmodeus Bargain
Two brutes caught Nevenka cheating with loaded dice, and [[Asmodeus]] appeared to bargain and strike deal with her.

### 391 AC - Chaos with Loki
Nevenka discussed with [[Loki]] about chaos being world's true nature, planning to kick hornet's nest and wake sleeping giants.

### 391 AC - All In Disaster
Nevenka lost all savings at casino and went all in at craps, accidentally using [[Alteration]] to make dice show "6" on all sides of first die and "1" on all sides of second.

### 392 AC - Contest with Sierra and Bifröst Creation
During contest with [[Sierra]], Nevenka used spray paint to create [[Bifröst]] rainbow road and equipped [[Hatter]] with skates and [[Tanuki]] with a sand yacht using [[Alteration]] so they could reach [[Blotch]].

### 393 AC, January-April - Talaria Warehouse Infiltration
Nevenka infiltrated [[The Talaria]] postal warehouse to mail a letter, then attempted to steal packages while [[Blotch]] kept lookout before speeding up the [[Axiom]]-installed conveyor belt as a diversion.

### 393 AC, January-April - Encounter with Santa Claus
[[Santa Claus]] caught Nevenka leaving the warehouse with stolen presents and gestured for her to load them into his sleigh, which she did before the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] departed with his reindeer team into the clouds.

### 393 AC, January-April - Joyride with Stolen Sleigh
Nevenka took [[Santa Claus]]'s sleigh on a joyride with children aboard, bringing the figurehead to life without reindeer, carving grooves into lake ice until motion sickness forced her to stop and the children released the handbrake, stranding her.

### 393 AC, January-April - Sleigh Obsession
Nevenka became fixated on acquiring [[Santa Claus]]'s red sleigh with its gold handlebars, velvet cushions, and aerodynamic design, considering various schemes before abandoning planning in favor of improvisation.

### 393 AC, June-August - Observing The Wayfarer's Arrival
Nevenka observed [[The Wayfarer]] hovering over [[The Screed]] with its helium balloons, noting that the [[Tisdhera Clan]]'s arrival was no coincidence.

### 393 AC, June-August - BLISS! Concert at The Screed
Nevenka waited in line at [[The Mess]] for stew during [[BLISS!]] concert preparations at [[The Screed]], nearly joined [[Nosk]] for Katsubichi sake but remembered obligation to attend [[Fen]]'s performance and ran off.

### 393 AC, June-August - Gambling with Lakshmi
Nevenka summoned [[Lakshmi]], goddess of wealth, and wagered with [[Sap]]-infused elongated cuboid dice the goddess provided, but lost—acknowledging she should not have challenged a goddess of luck.

### 393 AC, June-August - X Marks the Spot
Nevenka spray-painted X markers in the [[City of Scholars]] to help [[Tamati]] navigate, and while fleeing heard humming from a black stone block like a music box with no lid or key.

### 393 AC, June-August - Revealing the City's Nature
Nevenka brought [[Fen]] into the [[City of Scholars]] corridors to search for her lost memories. Using [[Ignescence]], Nevenka activated black polyhedron memory stones, causing shimmering bubbles to emerge and burst into golden dust forming ghostly afterimages of past inhabitants—children listening to singers, washerwomen at work, mothers humming lullabies. Nevenka revealed insight about the City: it is sentient and immortal, eternally in pain, and steals memories and imagination to dream and think of something else—like a drug to soothe itself, though Aether provides no true nourishment, making the comfort meaningless.
