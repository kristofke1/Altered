# Tamrat

## Overview

Tamrat was one of the founding heroes of [[Asgartha]] and a member of the [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]]. Served as one of [[Rune]]'s closest companions. Led the investigation into [[The Vanishing]] in 42 AC.

## Background

Tamrat was one of Rune's secret allies who worked alongside [[Heshkari]], [[Caellach]], [[Aksoy]], and [[Heeren]] to gather sympathizers from the reunited Tumult Nomad tribes. When the [[Phoenix]] crowned Rune at [[Kuningasplatz]], Tamrat became one of the Runic Knights serving on the [[Trusted Council]].

## Timeline

### 0 AC - The Concord

Tamrat became one of the founding [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]] following the Phoenix's crowning of Rune at [[Kuningasplatz]].

### 42 AC - Investigation of The Vanishing

Tamrat led the inquiry commission investigating Rune's disappearance. The investigation uncovered evidence of a planned departure: approximately fifteen hooded individuals had purchased a caravel and sailed westward into [[The Tumult]]. [[Aksoy]], [[Heeren]], and [[Ainara Aldana]] vanished alongside Rune with their weapons and armor.

On his deathbed, Tamrat reportedly begged forgiveness for a secret he took to the grave, suggesting knowledge about Rune's fate that he never revealed.
