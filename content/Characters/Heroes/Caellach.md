# Caellach

## Overview

Caellach was one of the founding heroes of [[Asgartha]] and founder of the [[Ordis]] Faction. Served as one of [[Rune]]'s closest companions and became one of the [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]].

## Background

Caellach was one of Rune's secret allies who worked to gather sympathizers from the reunited Tumult Nomad tribes. When the [[Phoenix]] crowned Rune at [[Kuningasplatz]], Caellach was the second person to bend the knee, immediately following [[Heshkari]].

Caellach served on Rune's [[Trusted Council]] and later founded the [[Ordis]] Faction, which became responsible for law, justice, administration, and preservation of knowledge. He trained [[Cwaith]], the first Kuningatar, as an administrator.

## Timeline

### 0 AC - The Concord

Caellach became one of the founding [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]] following the Phoenix's crowning of Rune at [[Kuningasplatz]]. Was second to bend the knee after [[Heshkari]].
