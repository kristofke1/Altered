# Aksoy

## Overview

Aksoy was one of the founding heroes of [[Asgartha]] and a member of the [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]]. Served as one of [[Rune]]'s closest companions. Vanished alongside Rune in 42 AC.

## Background

Aksoy was one of Rune's secret allies who worked alongside [[Heshkari]], [[Caellach]], [[Tamrat]], and [[Heeren]] to gather sympathizers from the reunited Tumult Nomad tribes. When the [[Phoenix]] crowned Rune at [[Kuningasplatz]], Aksoy became one of the Runic Knights serving on the [[Trusted Council]].

## Timeline

### 0 AC - The Concord

Aksoy became one of the founding [[The Runic Knights|Runic Knights]] following the Phoenix's crowning of Rune at [[Kuningasplatz]].

### 42 AC - The Vanishing

Aksoy was one of three Runic Knights who vanished alongside Rune. Disappeared with [[Ainara Aldana]] and [[Heeren]], taking weapons and armor. [[Tamrat]]'s investigation discovered that approximately fifteen hooded individuals had purchased a caravel and sailed westward.
