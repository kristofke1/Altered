# Tong

## Overview

Tong is a veteran [[Ordis]] soldier with expertise in mechanical systems and historical knowledge. He served in [[Gulrang Oduro]]'s unit during the 392 AC [[Mesektet]] recovery expedition, where his knowledge of airship schematics proved valuable.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Mesektet Recovery Expedition

During the expedition to recover the crashed Night Barque [[Mesektet]] in [[Caer Oorun]], Tong spotted granite formations that appeared to show one figure striking another with a blade, triggering accusations of betrayal through [[The Gestalt|the Coalescence]]—though Gulrang later proved the formations were geological distortions. Tong then assisted Gulrang in locating the black box compartment in the cockpit, identifying the small hatch on a control panel; when they discovered the black box had been stolen, Gulrang grabbed Tong's hand to prevent him from broadcasting the theft through his Coalescence connection.

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: Unit commander during Mesektet expedition; relied on Tong's expertise; prevented Tong from broadcasting black box theft (392 AC)
