# Dorota

Dorota works at the [[Kuna]] villa retreat of the ruun-Aysun family ([[Sigismar Synd]] and [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]]'s family). She is married to [[Asor]], the gardener.

## Role

Dorota assists with maintaining the villa on the island of [[Kuna]]. During the [[Season of Mists]], Dorota typically travels to town along with her husband [[Asor]], leaving the villa largely unattended.

## Relationships

- **[[Asor]]**: Husband, gardener at [[Kuna]] villa
- **[[Sigismar Synd]]**: Villa belongs to his family
- **[[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]]**: Villa belongs to her family
- **[[Kuna]]**: Island location where she works
