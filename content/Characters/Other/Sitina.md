# Sitina

## Overview

Sitina is a Phoenixian Blade Dancer and member of the [[Phoenixian Guard]] who practices the rare and prestigious art of blade dancing. She wields dual swords and can enter the duende state during combat—a transcendent condition where the dancer becomes one with their art. She serves as one of the keepers of [[Rune's Testament]].

## Fighting Style

### Blade Dancing
Sitina's combat technique combines precise swordsmanship with dance-like movements. Her sequences are meticulous and her pacing impeccable. As a Blade Dancer, she maintains calm, controlled breathing before combat, starts movements slowly with blades tracing arabesques, accelerates into spinning motions, and executes flawless twirling with confident steps.

### Dual Sword Technique
Sitina wields two swords simultaneously with exceptional skill. She maintains precise spacing between her blades and keeps her head tilted slightly during guard stance.

### Duende State
The duende is a transcendent artistic state where the performer loses self-consciousness and becomes the art itself. When Sitina enters duende, her dance becomes trance-like, her body moves with perfect fluidity, her blades take on crimson gleam leaving red trails, and she can replicate complex movements instinctively without delay.

## Timeline

### 390 AC - Training with Basira al-Arshad
[[Basira al-Arshad]] trained Sitina through three progressive stages—slow arabesques, accelerated spinning, and unleashed duende—using finger snaps as cues until Sitina replicated her complex katas perfectly.

### 393 AC - Escorting Waru's Expedition
Sitina led a cohort of [[Phoenixian Guard]] escorting [[Waru Toowoom]]'s expedition into the [[City of Scholars]] depths. When seismic tremors struck following Waru's [[Tesseracts]] probe, she urged retreat but deferred to the Exalt's judgment.

### 393 AC - Revealing Rune's Testament
After [[Waru Toowoom]] discovered [[Rune]]'s scroll confirming the Source of [[The Tumult]] was a fabrication, Sitina revealed the truth the [[Phoenixian Guard]] had always known. The Source was a mirage; the Guard were keepers of [[Rune's Testament]]; the true purpose was to sail westward and rekindle the [[Phoenix]]; other [[Bravos]] had secretly joined the [[Tisdhera Clan]] aboard [[The Wayfarer]] carrying the [[Phoenix Egg]] stolen from [[Haven]].

## Physical Characteristics

Sitina bears golden tracings on her skin—patterns identical to the archaic script carved throughout the [[City of Scholars]]. These markings identify her as a Testament steward.
