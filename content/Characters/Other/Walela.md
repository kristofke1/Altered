# Walela

## Overview
Walela was Grandmaster of [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school, a secluded monastery on edge of Vindmark, eastern frontier of [[Caer Eidos]]. In 74 AC, she refused [[Ayxas]]'s royal decree outlawing monastery's existence. She was killed during [[The Magan]]'s attack that followed.

## Defiance of Ayxas (74 AC)

In 74 AC, envoys of [[Ayxas]] arrived at [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school bearing royal decree that outlawed monastery's existence as part of suppression of independent warrior brotherhoods during [[The Torment]]. Walela thanked emissaries for their message but politely refused the Kuningas's "request."

Attack came following dawn as entire [[The Magan|Magan]] contingent descended upon monastery. Walela was killed during assault that destroyed school and killed most monks including [[Suddran]] and [[Nadra]].
