# Orbec

## Overview

Orbec is a keytarist and member of [[BLISS!]] from the [[Kasirga Clan]]. He plays keytar with skilled fingers capable of drawing complex melodies. He wears shades constantly and reads cheap airport novels or studies sheet music in private.

## Recruitment

Orbec was the hardest member to recruit for [[BLISS!]], refusing two separate invitations before [[Fen]] courted him aggressively enough to earn his interest. He arrived at [[The Ouroboros]] with a long list of conditions and requirements. Despite [[Tamati]]'s frustrations with his demands, the two now perform together.

## Role in BLISS!

Orbec plays keytar in [[BLISS!]]:
- [[Fen]] on vocals
- [[Tamati]] on percussion
- [[Boona]] on bass
- Orbec on keyboard/keytar
- [[Crowbar]] on guitar

He signals the band's start with a single note, counting "One, two. One, two; one two" before the group begins playing.

## Timeline
