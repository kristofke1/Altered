# El-Amin

El-Amin was an Asgarthan writer known for theoretical texts.

## Legacy

El-Amin's writings were studied by [[Leocardius Sree]] during his formative years as a scholar. Sree, who would later become Rector of [[The Sanctum]], studied El-Amin's works alongside those of other great Asgarthan theorists including [[Aurica]] and [[Fenn]].
