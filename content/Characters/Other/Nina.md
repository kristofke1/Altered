# Nina

Nina is an [[Ordis]] warrant officer who serves as aide-de-camp to [[Sigismar Synd]] during operations in the [[Storvhit]] region. She wields a dual lance as her weapon.

## Military Service

Nina serves as [[Sigismar Synd]]'s new aide-de-camp following [[Sunisa]]'s reassignment to support [[Afanas Korovinovich]].

## Personality

Nina's temperament is the opposite of her predecessor [[Sunisa]]. Where Sunisa was cheeky and never at a loss for words, Nina is discreet, even deferential. Sigismar noted this as a radical change, though he believed it might be a matter of time until Nina felt comfortable enough to express her personality.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Mushroom Forest Patrol

Nina accompanied [[Sigismar Synd]] through a forest with mushroom-covered tree trunks and drifting spores, taking defensive position with dual lance when Sigismar detected suspicious rustling that proved to be a [[Living Snowman]] [[Chimerae|Chimera]].

### 393 AC - Jinn Capture at Mountain Pass

Nina brought Sigismar's equipment when he captured a furious [[Eidolons|Jinn]] blocking a narrow mountain pass using a paper cage trap.

### 393 AC - Frozen Lake Crossing

Nina followed directly behind [[Sigismar Synd]] during a frozen lake crossing when a beast erupted through the ice at the vanguard position.

### 393 AC - BLISS! Concert Backstage Coordination

Nina provided backstage updates to [[Nosk]] through [[The Gestalt]] during [[BLISS!]] concert at [[The Screed]], reporting on stage activities including [[Fen]] warming up her voice, the drummer tapping snare, keyboardist playing kelonic piano, and [[Crowbar]] fine-tuning instruments.
