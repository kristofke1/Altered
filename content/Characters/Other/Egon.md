# Egon

## Overview

Egon is the [[Yzmir]] Magister of the Sphere of [[Kinemancy]], one of five Magisters who sit on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council that guides the Yzmir Faction.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Vote

Egon participated in the Magisterium debate over whether the [[Kraken]] should be added to the Yzmir's summoning arsenal or prohibited. He voted in favor of inclusion, offering a cynical yet incisive comment: "If a weapon exists, it must be used. It's not worth fighting against it. On the contrary, we should understand it as soon as possible so we can harness its power." The vote passed 3-2, with [[Malefane]] and [[Nasir]] opposed, and Egon, [[Tarjani]], and [[Chambulla]] in favor.

## Relationships

- **[[Chambulla]]**: Fellow Magister (Horomancy) who cast the deciding vote in the Kraken debate (392 AC)
- **[[Malefane]]**: Fellow Magister (Eidomancy) who opposed the Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
- **[[Nasir]]**: Fellow Magister (Gephyromancy) who opposed the Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
- **[[Tarjani]]**: Fellow Magistra (Phonomancy) who advised caution but voted in favor (392 AC)
