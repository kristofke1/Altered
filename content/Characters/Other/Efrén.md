## Overview

Efrén is a [[Lyra]] shadow theater performer and combat puppeteer who managed a small shadow theater troupe within the [[Tisdhera Clan]] caravan. He is the father of [[Sierra]], whom he raised while traveling from city to city across [[Asgartha]]. Beyond his theatrical performances, Efrén possesses powerful [[Ignescence]] abilities and can summon combat-equipped puppet [[Eidolons]]—capabilities he kept hidden from Sierra until their reunion at [[The Screed]] in 393 AC.

## Background

### Shadow Theater and Caravan Life

Efrén managed a small shadow theater within the [[Lyra]] caravan troupe, performing stories of princesses and ancient heroes, fables and tales from ages past. His shows referenced mythical cities including Madrid, Venice, and the City of Light. Sierra helped him manipulate threads, lights, silhouettes, and sets behind the narrow backstage.

Efrén raised Sierra while following the [[Tisdhera Clan]] caravan from city to city. He often teased Sierra by saying she could have easily become a [[Bravos]].

### The Tumult Incident

While the caravan traveled Asgarthan roads, a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] anomaly erupted from the ground. Efrén and Sierra jumped from their mobile home to avoid pools of Tumult as terrain transformed and collapsed around them. When Sierra stepped into a pool and her legs began mutating, Efrén pulled her to safety. Her left leg liquefied into scattering pebbles and her right leg transmuted into knotted wood sprouting feathers and rusty nails. Both legs were later amputated by healers from the [[Khamsin Clan]].

### Recovery and Journey to Verbera

After the amputation, Efrén cared for Sierra and tried to occupy her mind by asking her to help rebuild his theater, sculpt, and paint new puppets. He eventually carried her to [[Verbera]], on the edge of the [[Muir Concordia]], to find a mobility device. The visit coincided with the [[Prodigium]], the [[Axiom]]'s World Exposition.

At prosthetist [[Hamisu]]'s workshop, Efrén could only afford rudimentary devices rather than the advanced [[Grafts]] that responded to their wearer's will. Sierra was fitted with racing blades. During the stay, Efrén finalized the Clan's new caravan and performed shows in the city to fund his projects.

### Sierra's Departure

Sierra began building smoke machines, spark projectors, and visual effects to accompany Efrén's plays. Though Efrén preferred simplicity in his art, he accepted Sierra's technical additions. He went to [[Hamisu]] and asked him to sponsor Sierra within the [[Axiom]]. Hamisu agreed on the condition it was something Sierra truly desired.

Efrén offered Sierra the choice to continue with the caravan or remain in [[Verbera]] for a technical apprenticeship. Sierra chose to stay, and Efrén promised to visit whenever the caravan passed near the capital. Sierra then traveled to [[Hamisu]]'s workshop and the station that would take her to [[Arkaster]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Letter from Sierra

Efrén received a letter from [[Sierra]] via [[The Talaria]] courier while traveling with the caravan. Sierra's letter detailed discoveries at [[Storhvit]] including climate analysis of the Cais Adarra region, archaeological findings around [[The Nilam]] revealing pre-glaciation human settlement, and technical analysis of a mysterious ancient machine. Sierra mentioned caravan companions [[Faarsan]] and [[Argiris]], asking Efrén to give them hugs and remind them about chores.

### 393 AC, June-August - Reunion with Sierra at The Screed

Efrén arrived at [[The Screed]] aboard [[The Wayfarer]], the [[Tisdhera Clan]]'s [[Sahanka]], after the vessel followed a brief opening in the [[The Tumult|Tumult]]. As gondolas descended from the vessel, he called out to [[Sierra]], and they reunited after ten months apart.

### 393 AC, June-August - Exploring City of Scholars with Sierra

Efrén accompanied [[Sierra]] into the depths of the [[City of Scholars]], using wires to swing from block to block while she sped ahead on her one-wheeler. He revealed his [[Ignescence]] and combat puppeteer abilities by summoning old mannequins he had sculpted and stored away years earlier, equipped for battle and artifact retrieval.
