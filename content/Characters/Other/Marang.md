# Marang

## Overview

Marang is a young child living aboard [[The Ouroboros]] as part of the [[Kasirga Clan]].

## Timeline

### 381 AC - Storytime with the Sandman

Marang clung to [[Auraq]] during the evening storytime session in the children's dormitory while [[The Ouroboros]] drifted toward [[Arkaster]] on the path of exile. Auraq summoned the [[Sandman]] [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to help tell the story of [[Chiara Caceres]] and the [[Song of One]], with ghostly sand figures depicting palaces including [[The Asterion]], cathedrals of glass and greenery, and battlefields. The children gradually fell asleep as the Sandman's golden dust took effect.

## Relationships

- **[[Kasirga Clan]]**: Clan to which Marang belongs
- **[[Auraq]]**: Storyteller whom Marang clung to during storytime (381 AC)
