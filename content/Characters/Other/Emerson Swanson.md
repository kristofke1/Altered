# Emerson Swanson

Emerson Swanson is a reporter for [[Arkaster Echo]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC, May 28 - "Baby Steps" Article

Emerson authored article titled "Baby Steps" for [[Arkaster Echo]] reporting on [[Expeditionary Corps]] reaching estimated location of [[City of Scholars]] after four-month journey across [[Caer Nilam]] (approximately 250 miles from [[Caer Oorun]] borders). Article covered Admiral [[Temera Singh]]'s decision to delay exploration of the buried city and the political debate it generated, including campaign speeches by Senator [[Muttunbaal]] criticizing the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], defense of the Endeavor by Senator [[Noya|Noya Kagama]], response by [[Amaro Arundhani]], and interruption by Senator [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun|Anuncia Synd]] during Radio Guild broadcast defending expedition members.

### 393 AC, August 6 - "Shockwave at the Asterion" Article

Emerson authored article titled "Shockwave at the Asterion" for [[Arkaster Echo]] reporting on the joint announcement by [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] and [[Amaro Arundhani]] at [[The Asterion]] formalizing their political union and candidacy as a couple for Basileus and Basilissa in the upcoming election.

### 393 AC, October 15 - "Break-In at the Well of Tears" Article

Emerson authored article for [[Arkaster Echo]] Issue #289 reporting on the discovery of [[Kennus]], a lost cat from [[Kuth Kanat]] district, inside [[The Well of Tears]] beside the Lady of the Lake ([[Nimue]]).
