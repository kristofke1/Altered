# Tamati

## Overview

Tamati is a drummer and member of [[BLISS!]] from the [[Kasirga Clan]]. He is an [[Alterers|Alterer]] who can infuse his drumsticks with [[Alteration]] to make them glow. He has thick white hair and plays with fierce energy combined with subtlety and nuance.

## Transition

Tamati was previously known as Tammy. With surgical intervention and the [[The Rati|Rati]]'s attentive care, she transitioned and became he. For a long time, he couldn't decide between the names Tamati or Tameem, but both suited him equally well.

## Playing Style

Even during breaks, Tamati fiddles with his gear—twirling drumsticks, tapping on toms, hitting hi-hat, kicking bass drum. His instrument is an extension of himself. He infuses drumsticks with [[Alteration]] to make them glow.

## Role in BLISS!

Tamati plays drums, toms, and cymbals in [[BLISS!]]:
- [[Fen]] on vocals
- Tamati on percussion
- [[Boona]] on bass
- [[Orbec]] on keyboard/keytar
- [[Crowbar]] on guitar

## Relationship with Fen

[[Fen]] and Tamati lived close to each other on [[The Ouroboros]]. When [[Fen]] experienced total amnesia at the [[City of Scholars]], Tamati helped her recover by repeating lost information and going through her notes.
