# Tascha

## Overview

Tascha is the [[Muna]] mother of [[Moyo Chibuye]]. She lived with her husband [[Abu]] in a lumber village in the [[Nutsuwa Highlands]], roughly halfway between [[Kestia]] and [[Amorgand]].

## Influence on Moyo

Moyo inherited his love of small creatures from Tascha. She often said Moyo was, in a way, the forest itself made flesh: mysterious, with his head always in the clouds.
