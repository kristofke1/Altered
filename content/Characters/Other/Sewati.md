# Sewati

## Overview

Sewati is a [[Yzmir]] Mage-Dancer at [[The Kadigir]] who trained under [[Afanas Korovinovich]] in [[Kinemancy|Sign]]-based [[Alteration]].

## Background

Mage-Dancers perform elaborate choreographies while casting, though they function more as fishermen than dancers — each [[Kinemancy|Sign]] they trace acts as a lure for ideas on the other side of [[The Veil]], drawing them to a precise location. By creating small incisions in the barrier between worlds, Mage-Dancers allow ideas from [[The Empyrean]] to cross into reality and take physical form.

## Timeline

### 366 AC - Water-to-Glass Kata

During a training exercise observed by [[Afanas Korovinovich]], Sewati performed a three-Sign kata that summoned water from [[The Empyrean]], shaped it into a whirlpool fed by [[The Aether|Aether]], and solidified the spout into a glass spiral, after which he exhaled to dispel parasitic ideas that had crossed [[The Veil]] during the casting.
