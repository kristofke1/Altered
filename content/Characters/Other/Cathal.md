Cathal is a member of the Jusaka Blaze who attended Kojo Oduro's Carnelian Crown ceremony in 392 AC and participated in the Storvhit expedition in 393 AC.

## Jusaka Blaze Membership

Cathal belongs to the Jusaka Blaze, serving alongside friends including Carmela, Uju, and Gault. As part of this group, Cathal shared a close bond with Kojo Oduro before his rise to prominence as the Kraken Slayer.

During [[Storhvit]] expedition, someone ordered Cathal to run when [[Amarok]] wolf [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] appeared, causing giant creature to lose interest in [[Belisenki]] and chase Cathal through pines before Cathal leapt onto wolf's fur.

## Carnelian Crown Ceremony

In 392 AC, Cathal attended the ceremony where Kojo received the Carnelian Crown from Basileus Avkan ruun-Heshkari. During the momentous occasion, as Kojo climbed the stairs to receive Asgartha's highest honor, Cathal provided moral support from the stands.

Cathal teased Kojo with a sly, mocking grin, demonstrating the playful familiarity between longtime friends. This gentle ribbing occurred alongside more overt displays of support from other Jusaka Blaze members - Carmela raising her fist proudly, Uju whistling loudly, and Gault offering warm encouragement.

The presence of his Jusaka Blaze friends, including Cathal's characteristic teasing, helped buoy Kojo's confidence as he approached the Basileus, questioning whether he was truly worthy of such an honor.
