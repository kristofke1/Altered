# Chambulla

## Overview

Chambulla is the Magistra of the Sphere of [[Horomancy]] within the [[Yzmir]] Faction, sitting on [[The Magisterium]] High Council.

## Role

As Magistra of [[Horomancy]], Chambulla presides over one of [[The Spheres|five Spheres]] of [[The Naali]], the [[Yzmir]] alteration style. Horomancy focuses on divination through the study of idea currents present in the world via Irises. Chambulla oversees the Horomancy Temple within [[The Kadigir]] and represents the discipline on the High Council that guides the entire Faction.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Summoning Vote

Following the defeat of the [[Kraken]], the [[Yzmir]] Magisters debated whether the creature should be added to the Faction's summoning arsenal or list of prohibitions. With the vote tied 2-2, Chambulla cast the deciding vote in favor of inclusion after remaining silent for a long time, as if considering several possible futures. She gave a naive explanation that the Rediscovery Endeavor required arming with weapons up to the challenge. The vote passed 3-2, with [[Nasir]], [[Egon]], and Chambulla voting in favor, while [[Malefane]] and [[Tarjani]] opposed.
