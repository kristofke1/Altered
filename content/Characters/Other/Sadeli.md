# Sadeli

## Overview

Sadeli is the Little Shepherd of [[The Wayfarer]], the [[Sahanka]] of the [[Tisdhera Clan]]. Although the Little Shepherd traditionally holds the Inkwell duties—the cartographic and record-keeping responsibilities—aboard a [[Sahanka]], Sadeli is described as not very skilled with her hands, and these duties are instead performed by Zahira.

## Relationships

- **Zahira**: Mapmaker who handles the Inkwell duties that would normally fall to Sadeli
