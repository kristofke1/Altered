# Luka

## Overview

Luka is a child living aboard [[The Ouroboros]] as part of the [[Kasirga Clan]].

## Timeline

### 386 AC - Dormitory Incident

Luka was among a group of children who snuck out of the dormitory during a puppet show performed by [[Auraq]] and [[Sierra]]. [[Aruzhan|Aru]] motioned for [[Phong]] and Luka to return to their beds. Aruzhan then sang a nursery rhyme to help the children fall asleep.

## Relationships

- **[[Kasirga Clan]]**: Clan to which Luka belongs
- **[[Phong]]**: Fellow child caught sneaking out of dormitory (386 AC)
- **[[Aruzhan]]**: Singer who directed Luka back to bed and sang the children to sleep (386 AC)
- **[[Auraq]]**: Performer whose puppet show Luka watched (386 AC)
