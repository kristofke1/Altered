# Kaw

## Overview

Kaw is a [[Bravos]] [[Altrun]] champion whose team won the championship in 376 AC. Her competitive style relies heavily on [[The Avgrunn]] to gain additional strength.

## Relationships

- **[[Kojo Oduro]]**: [[Bravos]] Exalt who trains with Kaw to learn from her techniques (393 AC)
- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: Fellow elder whose white hair Kojo contrasts with Kaw's reaction to being called "elder"
