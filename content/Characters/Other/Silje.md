# Silje

## Overview

Silje is a [[Bravos]] bouncer at [[Haven]] responsible for controlling access to restricted areas within the Bastion. She is known for her massive, muscular build and unwavering adherence to duty.

## Timeline

### 390 AC - Security Confrontation

During Admiral [[Temera Singh]]'s visit to Haven, Silje blocked a [[Phoenixian Guard]] messenger carrying an urgent missive, categorically refusing to let her pass despite arguments. [[Venka]], on leave at the time, attempted to intervene but Silje stared her down, and Venka backed off.

## Relationships

- **[[Venka]]**: Haven colleague; Silje intimidated Venka when she tried to intervene during security confrontation (390 AC)
- **[[Temera Singh]]**: Admiral whose visit prompted the security incident (390 AC)
