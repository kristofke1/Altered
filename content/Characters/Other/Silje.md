Silje is a Haven bouncer responsible for controlling access to restricted areas within the Bravos bastion. She is known for her massive build, muscular torso, unwavering adherence to duty, and reputation for holding grudges.

## Security Role

Silje serves as a bouncer at Haven, emerging from alcoves to block access when unauthorized personnel attempt to pass. She takes her security responsibilities seriously and refuses to make exceptions, even when confronted by important messengers or fellow Bravos personnel.

Her approach to security is categorical—when she decides someone cannot pass, she crosses her arms on her muscular torso and refuses to step aside regardless of circumstances or arguments.

## Incidents

### 390 AC - Confrontation with Phoenixian Guard Messenger

During Admiral Temera Singh's visit to Haven, a Phoenixian Guard messenger with paint on her cheeks rushed through the halls carrying an important message. The messenger tried to pass through a corridor, but Silje emerged from an alcove to block her path.

The Phoenixian Fighter argued with Silje, trying desperately to get around her to deliver the urgent missive. However, Silje crossed her arms on her muscular torso and categorically refused to step aside, maintaining the security cordon.

Venka, who was on leave, approached and adopted an impassive expression to help subdue the situation. However, Silje stared Venka down with nostrils flaring like a bull about to charge. She glowered at Venka as if Venka were a nuisance preventing her from doing her job. Venka felt bowels liquefy before Silje's closed expression and realized the attempt to interfere had been an overstep.

## Physical Description

Silje is described as a massive figure with a muscular torso. Her imposing physical presence reinforces her role as an effective bouncer. When angry or determined, her nostrils flare like a bull about to charge.

## Reputation

Fellow Bravos personnel recognize Silje as someone who takes her job seriously and does not forgive interference. Her reputation for holding grudges and her physical prowess in arm wrestling contests make her someone to avoid offending.
