# Abracosa

## Overview

Abracosa was a painter who created "The Apostate's Diatribe," a fresco depicting [[Wanjiru]] that hangs in [[The Conclave]]'s great hall.

## Relationships

- **[[The Conclave]]**: Location where Abracosa's fresco is displayed
- **[[Wanjiru]]**: Subject of Abracosa's fresco "The Apostate's Diatribe"
