# Hasret

Hasret is a skilled thief and spy, recognized as one of most skilled of her generation. She conducts infiltration operations stealing documents from secure locations.

## Thief Skills

Hasret is one of most skilled thieves of her generation. She wouldn't come back empty-handed from mission as matter of pride. She specializes in stealing documents from archives and secure facilities.

## Infiltration Methods

### 390 AC - Breaking into Sigismar's Home

Hasret broke into Sigismar's home leaving deliberate signs of intrusion designed to catch attention. She left half-open window with curtains flapping, velvet gloves on mantelpiece, shawl nonchalantly dropped on sofa, and half-filled glass on desk. Scene warned of intrusion while showing no danger.

Hasret stole liqueur from Sigismar's cabinet and used his shower. When Sigismar arrived home and sat on bed, Hasret emerged from bathroom wearing only towel. Her hair was jet black and shoulders still wet from shower. She feigned surprise but had heard him come in.

## Document Theft

### 390 AC - Asterion Archives Infiltration

Hasret walked past Sigismar and stole his glass while pointing to scroll placed on chest of drawers. She had found something in Asterion archives. Sigismar averted eyes to let her dress.

Scroll she stole mentioned Sigismar's ancestor Ayxas. It was attack pamphlet written by Vissentes Narses, contemporary of Ayxas. Scroll might hold clue to help Sigismar understand why ancestor committed offenses.
