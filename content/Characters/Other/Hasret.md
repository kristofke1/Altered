# Hasret

## Overview

Hasret is an [[Ordis]] spy and thief, recognized as one of the most skilled of her generation. She specializes in infiltration operations and stealing documents from secure locations. She is the former romantic partner of [[Sigismar Synd]], and they share a connection symbolized by a ring with a diamond band.

## Timeline

### 390 AC - Asterion Archives Theft

Hasret infiltrated [[The Asterion]] archives and stole a scroll about [[Sigismar Synd]]'s ancestor [[Ayxas]]—an attack pamphlet written by [[Vissentes Narses]] that might explain Ayxas's offenses.

## Relationships

- **[[Sigismar Synd]]**: Former romantic partner; stole Ayxas pamphlet from Asterion archives for him (390 AC); ring with diamond band connected to their relationship
- **[[Vissentes Narses]]**: Author of the attack pamphlet about Ayxas that Hasret stole
- **[[Ayxas]]**: Subject of the pamphlet Hasret stole for Sigismar
