# Sam

## Overview

Sam is a female member of a joint [[Yzmir]]-[[Muna]] expedition encountered during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. She was present during [[Taima]]'s combat against a mutated [[Mana Moths|Mana Moth]] in 393 AC, standing ready to intervene before Taima signaled the situation was under control.

## Relationships

- **[[Taima]]**: [[Yzmir]] Exalt whom Sam supported during the Mana Moth encounter (393 AC)
