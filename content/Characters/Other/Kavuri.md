# Kavuri

## Overview

Kavuri is an associate of the Kadis family from [[Suspira]], likely a sibling or close friend of [[Subhash Kadis]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Remembered in Storvhit

While playing his sitar in the [[Storhvit]] dormitory, [[Subhash Kadis]] conjured ghostly figures of his memories through Alteration. Among them appeared Kavuri playing with [[Navin Kadis]] and [[Marmo]], Bash's Chimera companion.

## Relationships

- **[[Subhash Kadis]]**: Family member or close friend; Bash conjures memories of Kavuri through his sitar (393 AC)
- **[[Navin Kadis]]**: Fellow memory figure seen playing with Kavuri and Marmo (393 AC)
- **[[Marmo]]**: Subhash's Chimera companion; Kavuri played with Marmo in Bash's memories
