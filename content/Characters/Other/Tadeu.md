# Tadeu

## Overview

Tadeu is a former resident of the [[Vanderun Shards]] in [[Caer Esvander]] who was a childhood friend of [[Kauri]]. Following an earthquake that devastated the region, Tadeu relocated to [[Svarograd]].

## Childhood

Tadeu grew up on the Shards alongside [[Kauri]], [[Arib]], and [[Fania]]. After completing everyday chores, the children would gather for their "second, truant school"—informal play and exploration among the floating islands.

## Displacement

When a major earthquake struck the [[Vanderun Shards]], opening fissures and causing widespread destruction, authorities declared entire zones unstable and uninhabitable. Tadeu was the first of the childhood friends to leave, heading for [[Svarograd]]. The scattering of the community separated the childhood friends.
