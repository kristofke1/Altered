# Eugen

## Overview

Eugen is an [[Axiom]] drilling operations manager stationed at [[The Screed]] outpost, overseeing underground excavation operations at the [[City of Scholars]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Drillmole Test Incident

[[Eugen]] oversaw the first field test of the [[Drillmole]] mobile drilling vehicle at the [[City of Scholars]], activating the machine before observing scientists, but the vehicle failed to return after successfully drilling into rock and the remote control became unresponsive.

- **[[Axiom]]**: Affiliated faction
