# Vera Valesquia

## Overview

Vera Valesquia is journalist from [[Arkaster Echo]] who joined [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] to report on expedition and write profiles of Corps members. She maintains personal coffee stash with portable percolator and kelonic grinder, working on "Faces of Rediscovery" profile series.

## Physical Appearance

Vera has long tousled cream-colored hair. She wears beanie and scarf even indoors. Her fingers are stained with ink from writing work.

## Reporting Work on Expedition

### 393 AC, May 28 - "Remnants" Article

Vera authored article titled "Remnants" as special correspondent for [[Arkaster Echo]] reporting from [[The Screed]], location of [[Expeditionary Corps]]' first forward outpost around [[Crow's Eye]] chasm. Article described the crater's human-made structures on concentric terraces as evidence of former civilization, covered architectural assessment by [[Axiom]] engineers noting technological sophistication without [[Kelon]], discussed theories about population's disappearance (estimated over 100,000 at peak), and documented each Faction's construction: [[Ordis]] Town Hall, [[Axiom]] Refinery, [[Muna]] Farm. Reported [[Waru Toowoom]]'s statement about the frustrating encounter with a dead civilization and Admiral [[Temera Singh]]'s rationale for delaying underground exploration. Noted Axiom operators were deploying drones and probes despite exploration delay, with early rumors of buried megastructures and fractal architecture.

### 393 AC - Interview with Subhash Kadis at Storvhit

Vera witnessed [[Subhash Kadis]] playing sitar with [[Alteration]] module creating ghostly memory projections in dorm at [[Storvhit]]. After performance of Prakash Ruggeveen's RWV 416 "The Lament," she introduced herself and offered coffee from personal stash including kelonic grinder and portable percolator. Requested interview for "Faces of Rediscovery" profile series, offering to personally ensure family receives copy of paper. Revealed [[The Ouroboros]] moved upstream to investigate topographic anomaly - circular structure possibly containing human ruins - suggesting potential survivors nearby following recent relic discovery. Noted [[Subhash Kadis|Subhash]] would be deployed again soon, connecting multiple human-origin findings as evidence of other settlements.

## Personality

Vera demonstrates hospitality offering coffee to those she meets. She maintains friendly demeanor with slight slyness, using big eyes described as having magnetic quality used to extract information from people. Shows professional dedication maintaining coffee supplies and equipment despite expedition's harsh conditions. Displays careful observation skills noting details about Corps members for profile work.
