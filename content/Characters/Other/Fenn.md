# Fenn

Fenn was an Asgarthan writer known for philosophical treatises.

## Legacy

Fenn's writings were studied by [[Leocardius Sree]] during his formative years as a scholar. Sree, who would later become Rector of [[The Sanctum]], studied Fenn's works alongside those of other great Asgarthan theorists including [[Aurica]] and [[El-Amin]].
