# Zenith

## Overview

Zenith is a Venerable One of the [[Kasirga Clan]], one of the elders who supplies [[Mana]] to sustain [[Esmeralda]]'s permanent incarnation as an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]].

## Timeline

### 381 AC - Planning the Journey to Arkaster

Zenith attended a meeting aboard [[The Ouroboros]] with other Venerable Ones, [[Itoro]], and newly-appointed Shepherdess [[Esmeralda]]. During the meeting, [[Fen]] served herbal tea to the attendees, though Zenith refused his cup. The Venerable Ones channeled Mana to fuel Esmeralda's incarnation while she traced the route to [[Arkaster]] on a map: from [[Avesta]], around the [[Fagn]], through the [[Arami Lowlands]], to [[Verbera]], across the [[Muir Concordia]], and finally to the capital to request an audience with the [[Basileus]] about joining the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].
