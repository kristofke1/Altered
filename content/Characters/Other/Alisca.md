# Alisca

## Overview

Alisca is a companion of [[Nadir]] aboard [[The Wayfarer]], the [[Sahanka]] of the [[Tisdhera Clan]]. [[Nadir]] records Alisca's jokes in his surveying notes alongside field observations, and these annotations frequently amuse his mentor Zahira.

## Relationships

- **[[Nadir]]**: Companion whose jokes Nadir records in his surveying notes
- **Zahira**: Mapmaker who reads and is amused by Alisca's jokes in Nadir's annotations
