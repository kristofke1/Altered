# Fasano

## Overview

Fasano is a young [[Yzmir]] Disciple training under [[Afanas Korovinovich]]. He previously worked in the occult library at [[The Kadigir]]. His elder sister [[Matera]] died during the Battle of the Kraken with the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] in 392 AC. By 393 AC, Fasano accompanied Afanas to the [[City of Scholars]].

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Learning of Sister's Death

[[Afanas Korovinovich]] found Fasano at [[The Kadigir]]'s occult library carrying a heavy pile of grimoires, rushing around to prove his worth. When Fasano recognized Afanas, his smile vanished as he noticed the pained expression. Afanas did not need to speak; Fasano understood that [[Matera]] would not return from the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. His knees buckled and his hands released all the grimoires.

### 393 AC - Training Under Afanas

Fasano experienced visible growth spurts and increasing self-confidence from training. He accepted punishments without complaint, showing stubborn determination. [[Afanas Korovinovich]] observed him flipping through spell books he could barely carry a year prior. Afanas caught Fasano with tears at an improvised altar placing flowers, and gave him space for bereavement. Afanas trained Fasano in [[Sap]]-enriched tea ritual before they traveled to the [[City of Scholars]].

## Relationships

- **[[Afanas Korovinovich]]**: [[Yzmir]] Exalt who delivered news of sister's death (392 AC); observed Fasano's growth and bereavement, trained in Sap infusion ritual (393 AC)
- **[[Matera]]**: Elder sister, [[Yzmir]] Initiate killed during [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] (392 AC)
