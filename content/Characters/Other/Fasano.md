# Fasano

## Overview

Fasano is a young [[Yzmir]] Disciple who works in the occult library at [[The Kadigir]]. His elder sister [[Matera]] died during the Battle of the Kraken with the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] in 392 AC.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Learning of Sister's Death

[[Afanas Korovinovich]] found Fasano at [[The Kadigir]]'s occult library carrying books. Afanas did not need to speak; Fasano understood from his expression that [[Matera]] would not return from the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

### 393 AC - Sap Infusion Training

[[Afanas Korovinovich]] trained Fasano in [[Sap]]-enriched tea ritual before they traveled to the [[City of Scholars]].

## Relationships

- **[[Afanas Korovinovich]]**: [[Yzmir]] Exalt who delivered news of sister's death (392 AC); trained Fasano in Sap infusion ritual (393 AC)
- **[[Matera]]**: Elder sister, [[Yzmir]] Initiate killed during [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] (392 AC)
- **[[The Kadigir]]**: [[Yzmir]] Bastion where Fasano works in occult library
- **[[City of Scholars]]**: Destination Fasano accompanied Afanas to (393 AC)
