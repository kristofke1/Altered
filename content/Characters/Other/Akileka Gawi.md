# Akileka Gawi

## Overview

Akileka Gawi was the personal [[Alteration|Alterer]] of Basilissa [[Annuska Valiont]]. Gawi is known for striking down [[Acantha Shah]] during the tragic events of [[The Paramours of Seki]], an act that triggered [[Nittaya Vorona]]'s catastrophic Ignescence and the creation of [[The Viper-Tree]].

## Timeline

### 325–335 AC - The Paramours of Seki

When [[Annuska Valiont]] confronted [[Nittaya Vorona]] in [[Seki]], [[Acantha Shah]] stepped forward and manifested her [[Alteration]] to push back the Basilissa's guards. Gawi, fearing the young [[Ordis]] Alterer might harm the ruler, struck Acantha down. The killing triggered Nittaya's uncontrolled [[Ignescence]], which transformed both lovers into [[The Viper-Tree]]. Gawi removed Annuska from the chambers for safety.

## Relationships

- **[[Acantha Shah]]**: [[Ordis]] Alterer whom Gawi killed during the confrontation in [[Seki]] (325–335 AC)
- **[[Annuska Valiont]]**: Basilissa whom Gawi served as personal Alterer (325–335 AC)
- **[[Nittaya Vorona]]**: [[Lyra]] dancer whose Ignescence erupted after Gawi struck down [[Acantha Shah]] (325–335 AC)
