# Aruzhan

## Overview

Aruzhan, nicknamed "Aru," is a child singer of the [[Kasirga Clan]]. She possesses an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that can manifest her presence to others. Aruzhan stayed behind with other [[Kasirga Clan]] families in [[Kladiver]] when the expedition departed, as she was among those looking after very young children.

## Relationship with Fen

Aruzhan has close bond with [[Fen]], sometimes snuggling up against her during night vigils. [[Fen]] deeply misses Aruzhan after the separation when Clan families stayed behind. [[Fen]] also watches over other children including [[Qannik]] and [[Marang]], wondering if Aruzhan might be scolding [[Qannik]] or taking care of [[Marang]].

## Eidolon Manifestation

Aruzhan possesses an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] capable of manifesting her presence across distance. During [[Fen]]'s journey through [[Storvhit]] taiga, [[Fen]] felt Aruzhan's [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] presence snuggling against her when her [[Ignescence]] fired up while harmonizing with nature's sounds. [[Fen]] kept eyes closed to avoid the sensation fading.

Aruzhan's [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] manifested to [[Fen]] in [[Storvhit]] taiga when [[Fen]]'s [[Ignescence]] activated while harmonizing with nature, felt snuggling against [[Fen]] like during past night vigils.
