# Ehuang

## Overview

Ehuang is a [[Lyra]] cloth dancer and acrobat of the [[Kasirga Clan]]. She performs aerial acts using [[Alteration]] to manifest long coils of fabric that serve as her trapeze and safety equipment.

## Timeline

### 385 AC - Circus Performance

Ehuang performed under the Kasirga big top, introduced by [[Auraq]] as "the incredible, the extraordinary, the astounding Ehuang." She launched herself from a pole without net or harness, catching a trapeze mid-air. When the ropes of a second trapeze deliberately snapped as part of the act, she used Alteration to manifest fabric coils that wrapped around the poles, slowing her fall into a graceful spinning descent. She then performed an aerial ribbon routine, walking in mid-air and circling above the audience. The act concluded with Ehuang using her ribbons to capture a [[Tanuki]] summoned by Auraq, wrapping the Eidolon in a cloth cocoon as part of their hero-villain theatrical performance.

## Relationships

- **[[Auraq]]**: Fellow Kasirga performer who introduced Ehuang's act and played the villain in their theatrical routine (385 AC)
- **[[Kasirga Clan]]**: Lyra clan to which Ehuang belongs
- **[[Tanuki]]**: Eidolon who served as Auraq's sidekick in the performance; captured by Ehuang's ribbons (385 AC)
