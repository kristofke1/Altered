# Nadra

## Overview
Nadra was a musician and apprentice swordsman at [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] monastery. She was friend of [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] and taught him lute during their time at the school. She was killed by enemy archers during [[The Magan]]'s attack on the monastery in 74 AC.

## Life at Ugetsu-Fenshen (60-74 AC)

Nadra lived at [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school on edge of Vindmark, eastern frontier of [[Caer Eidos]]. In evenings, she played lute while [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] listened, occasionally teaching him her craft. She joined apprentice swordsmen's ranks one year after Gericht became disciple (65 AC).

## Death During Monastery Attack (74 AC)

In 74 AC, [[The Magan|Magan]] contingent attacked [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] after [[Walela]] refused [[Ayxas]]'s decree outlawing monastery. During battle, Nadra was wounded and retreating. [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] broke ranks attempting to lead her to safety. Enemy archers struck both with arrows. Nadra was killed. [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] was pierced but survived, left for dead.

## Legacy

Nadra's lute miraculously survived flames despite scorch marks on wood. [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] took it from ruins and carried it throughout rebellion. During rare moments between battles, he played lute with tenderness and skill in her memory.
