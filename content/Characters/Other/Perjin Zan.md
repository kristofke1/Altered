# Perjin Zan

## Overview

Perjin Zan was the daughter of [[Peredur Zan]], the [[Yzmir]] [[Phonomancy|Phonomancer]] who served as personal advisor to Basileus [[Manssur]]. During [[The Three Factions War]], after [[Affan Cathal]] surrendered to secure an armistice, Perjin executed him during the negotiations. She also killed [[Bravos]] General [[Kelia Mordad]] when Mordad attempted to intervene. The [[Bravos]] immediately turned on Perjin and killed her with arrows.

## Timeline

### ~290 AC - Executions at Armistice Negotiations
When [[Ordis]] Templar [[Affan Cathal]] surrendered to end the fighting between [[Ordis]], [[Bravos]], and [[Yzmir]] forces, Perjin executed him during the armistice negotiations. [[Bravos]] General [[Kelia Mordad]] attempted to intervene but was also killed by Perjin. The [[Bravos]] responded by killing Perjin with arrows, and the resulting tragedy escalated the conflict into total war.

- **[[Affan Cathal]]**: Ordis Templar and her father's assassin whom Perjin executed during armistice negotiations (~290 AC)
- **[[Kelia Mordad]]**: Bravos General killed by Perjin when she attempted to intervene during Cathal's execution (~290 AC)
- **[[Peredur Zan]]**: Father; Yzmir Phonomancer and advisor to [[Manssur]], assassinated by [[Affan Cathal]]
