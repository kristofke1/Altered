# Potaba

Potaba is a fox with white feathers who lives in the forests near Yonago's cabin. When Rin passed by during her quest to find Orchid, Potaba invited her to stop, but Rin had to decline. Potaba emitted a deep growl of disappointment in response. The fox is one of the forest creatures living in the area near the Katkera.
