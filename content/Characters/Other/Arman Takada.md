# Arman Takada

Arman Takada is a renowned fashion designer in [[Arkaster]].

## Arkaster Fashion Week (393 AC)

In 393 AC, Arman Takada was granted the rare honor of opening Arkaster Fashion Week with a runway show set in the Gardens of [[The Kadigir]]. His collection was inspired by the cycles of nature, particularly a hike through [[Makonis Heights]] National Park where he claimed to have encountered a two-hundred-year-old hydracaena shimmering with autumn colors.

## Relationships

- **[[The Kadigir]]**: Location of Fashion Week runway show (393 AC)
- **[[Makonis Heights]]**: National park that inspired Takada's collection
