# Vera Velasquia

## Overview

Vera Velasquia is a journalist for the [[Arkaster Echo]] and daughter of the newspaper's founder [[Fabia Velasquia]]. She joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] to document the expedition, working on a profile series called "The Faces of Rediscovery." She has long tousled cream-colored hair, wears a beanie and scarf even indoors, and has ink-stained fingers from her writing work.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, May 28 - "Remnants" Article from The Screed

Vera authored an article titled "Remnants" as special correspondent reporting from [[The Screed]], describing the crater's human-made structures as evidence of a former civilization. The article covered [[Axiom]] engineers' assessment of technological sophistication achieved without [[Kelon]], theories about the population's disappearance, each Faction's construction projects, [[Waru Toowoom]]'s statement about encountering a dead civilization, and Admiral [[Temera Singh]]'s rationale for delaying underground exploration.

### 393 AC - Interview with Subhash Kadis at Storvhit

Vera witnessed [[Subhash Kadis]] playing sitar with [[Alteration]] module creating memory projections in a dorm at [[Storhvit]]. She introduced herself, offered coffee from her personal stash including kelonic grinder and portable percolator, and requested an interview for her "Faces of Rediscovery" series, offering to ensure his family receives a copy.

### 393 AC - BLISS! Concert Broadcast

Vera requested [[Nosk]] to broadcast the [[Altered/Organizations/Other/BLISS!|BLISS!]] concert through [[The Gestalt]], coordinating coverage from multiple positions around the stage.
