# Taima

## Overview

Taima is a [[Yzmir]] alchemist and [[Kinemancy|Kinemancer]] who succeeded [[Takao Cordisco]] as the [[The Kadigir|Kadigiran]] alchemist after his death in 393 AC. She fights using needles and pins wielded like a rapier, threading chimeran silk from her Alter Ego Silk through an automated spool to anchor and immobilize targets.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Combating Mutated Mana Moth

Taima fought a mutated [[Mana Moths|Mana Moth]] above rippling waters during a joint [[Yzmir]]-[[Muna]] expedition, piercing its wing with a needle threaded with Silk's chimeran filament and pinning it to a rocky wall to protect a frozen [[Muna]] member nearby.
