# Telmo

Telmo was Governor of [[Caer Arcolano]] during [[The Severance War]] in the early 3rd century AC.

## Role in the Severance War

When the Governors of [[Asgartha]]'s Provinces rebelled against Basileus [[Marcus Sune]]'s democratic reforms, Telmo was among those who challenged central authority. However, unlike some of his peers, Telmo was known to be driven by noble intentions.

[[Wanjiru]], a young [[Yzmir]] Horomancer stationed in [[Caer Arcolano]] at the time, recognized Telmo's integrity. Although the [[Yzmir]] declared neutrality in the conflict, Wanjiru pledged allegiance to Telmo specifically because he knew the Governor acted from genuine principle rather than personal ambition.
