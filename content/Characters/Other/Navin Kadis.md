# Navin Kadis

## Overview

Navin Kadis is a member of the Kadis family from [[Suspira]], likely a sibling of [[Subhash Kadis]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Remembered in Storvhit

While playing his sitar in the [[Storhvit]] dormitory, [[Subhash Kadis]] conjured ghostly figures of his memories through Alteration. Among them appeared Navin playing with [[Kavuri]] and [[Marmo]], Bash's Chimera companion.

## Relationships

- **[[Subhash Kadis]]**: Family member (likely sibling); Bash conjures memories of Navin through his sitar (393 AC)
- **[[Kavuri]]**: Fellow memory figure seen playing with Navin and Marmo (393 AC)
- **[[Marmo]]**: Subhash's Chimera companion; Navin played with Marmo in Bash's memories
