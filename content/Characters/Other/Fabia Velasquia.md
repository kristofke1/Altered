# Fabia Velasquia

## Overview

Fabia Velasquia is the founder of the [[Arkaster Echo]] newspaper, which she established in 346 AC alongside the democratization of printing. She is the mother of [[Vera Velasquia]], a journalist who joined the [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] to document the expedition.

## Timeline

### 346 AC - Founded Arkaster Echo

Fabia Velasquia founded the [[Arkaster Echo]] alongside the democratization of printing.

### 393 AC, October 15 - "A Page Turns" Article

Fabia authored the lead article "A Page Turns" for [[Arkaster Echo]] Issue #289, profiling the four candidates in the basileal elections: [[Lucas Aveskamp]], [[Somayeh Bahman]], [[Bokang Nkoe]], and [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]].

### 393 AC, October 22 - "New Challenges" Article

Fabia authored an article titled "New Challenges" on the day of [[Somayeh Bahman]]'s swearing-in as Basilissa, outlining the challenges facing the new leader: preparing for discontinuation of the [[Floret]] currency, stabilizing [[Caer Oorun]], and continuing the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] while granting more authority to regional councils.
