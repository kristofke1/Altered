# Themba Hekekia

## Overview

Themba Hekekia was a [[Muna]] botanist specializing in rare plants who discovered the cure for [[The Nifir]], a plague that had ravaged [[Asgartha]] for nearly seventy-five years. Following in his father's footsteps as a naturalist, he dedicated himself to finding a cure after his father died from the disease.

## Background

### Discovery of the Cure

Hekekia stumbled upon the cure almost by accident while studying the [[Mush|mushs]], a family of sentient mushrooms. Over time, these conscious fungi accepted him into their forest and underground colonies, allowing him to study their ecosystems and customs. He discovered that mushs could filter [[Mana]] from the environment and living beings, transforming unstable Mana into appeased Mana by passing it through their bodies.

This discovery proved crucial in curing the Nifir. Mush spores were distributed across the Peninsula, growing into adult mushs whose mycelium could extract unstable Mana and return purified Mana. Within a few years, the Nifir was eradicated from Asgartha.

## Relationships

- **Hekekia's father**: Naturalist who died from the Nifir, inspiring Themba's quest for a cure
- **[[Mush]]**: Sentient mushrooms whose properties Hekekia discovered and used to cure the Nifir
