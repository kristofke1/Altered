# Fabia Valesquia

## Overview

Fabia Valesquia is founder of [[Arkaster Echo]] newspaper. Shares Valesquia surname with [[Vera Valesquia]], journalist who joined [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] in 393 AC, suggesting family connection.

## Arkaster Echo Foundation

Fabia founded [[Arkaster Echo]], newspaper organization based in [[Arkaster]]. Publication continues operations with [[Vera Valesquia]] serving as reporter covering [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] activities during 393 AC expedition.

## Relationships

- **[[Vera Valesquia]]**: Shares Valesquia surname suggesting family relationship, reporter for [[Arkaster Echo]] (393 AC)
- **[[Arkaster Echo]]**: Newspaper organization Fabia founded
