# Nyala

## Overview

Nyala is a [[Lyra]] [[Alterers|Alterer]] and performer known for skating tricks combined with portal generation abilities. She was a childhood friend of [[Auraq]] (Awmar) before their estrangement following the death of Auraq's mother.

## Early Life

During childhood, Nyala and Awmar (later known as [[Auraq]]) would sneak into Awmar's father's costume studio to take fantastical clothing designs. Together they dreamed up performances, forming a duo and vowing to create their first show as a team. They spent hours devising scenarios, practicing steps, reciting lines, and juggling.

Nyala developed her [[Alteration]] powers first and teased Awmar about his slower progress. However, after Awmar's mother died, their friendship faltered. Nyala was unsure how to help as Awmar withdrew into guilt, and she kept her distance. A mutual unease grew into a wall between them, and their paths diverged.

While [[Auraq]] stepped into her mother's role, Nyala began staging small shows independently. They observed each other from afar without sharing the stage. After finishing her studies, Nyala attempted to reconnect with Auraq but found Auraq had vanished without trace.

## Performance Style

Nyala performs skating tricks with [[Mana]] rippling at her fingertips, generating portals that enable spectacular relocation abilities. [[Auraq]] dismisses her techniques as "country fair conjuring tricks" despite their visual impact.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Caer Oorun Performance

Nyala performed at the [[Kasirga Clan]] celebration marking [[Caer Oorun]] reclamation and expeditionary departure. During the evening performance, she lost control of a summoned flaming wyvern [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that ignited stage drapes. [[Auraq]] intervened by singing haunting baroque opera while communicating with the creature through syncopated gestures. After an exchanged glance and nod, Nyala created a dimensional portal behind Auraq allowing her to command the wyvern into the gateway. Both performers collapsed from depleted [[Ignescence]] as the audience erupted believing it was a planned duet. They departed backstage separately without speaking.

### 393 AC - Ice Performance at Storvhit

Nyala performed skating tricks at [[Storvhit]] with Mana rippling at fingertips generating portals. [[Auraq]] watched the performance but dismissed the techniques despite their spectacular relocation abilities.

### 393 AC - Lake Ice Incident

During the frozen lake incident at [[Storvhit]], [[Auraq]] chose to save children from breaking ice rather than warning Nyala who was skating. Nyala fell through the weakened ice. [[Subhash Kadis]] rescued her by sending [[Marmo]] into the water and summoning a Walrus [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] with sonar rig to locate her. Afterward, Subhash summoned [[Little Match Girl]] to comfort the traumatized Nyala.

### 393 AC - Scheduled at The Stage

Nyala was scheduled to perform at [[The Stage]] at [[The Screed]] outpost alongside [[Auraq]] and the band [[BLISS!]].
