# Zyran Rostag

Zyran Rostag is a ghostwriter who co-authored "The Three Circles" with [[The Frog Prince]]. The book is a political-fiction adventure essay exploring the role of compassion within the political intelligentsia, published by Les Éditions de l'Orée.

## Relationships

- **[[The Frog Prince]]**: Eidolon with whom Zyran co-authored "The Three Circles"
