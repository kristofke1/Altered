# Nasir

## Overview

Nasir is the [[Yzmir]] Magister of the Sphere of [[Gephyromancy]], one of five Magisters who sit on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council that guides the Yzmir Faction.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Vote

Nasir participated in the Magisterium debate over whether the [[Kraken]] should be added to the Yzmir's summoning arsenal or prohibited. He made appeals in favor of inclusion, which [[Malefane]] attempted to counter. The vote passed 3-2, with Nasir, [[Egon]], and [[Chambulla]] voting in favor, while Malefane and [[Tarjani]] opposed.

## Relationships

- **[[Chambulla]]**: Fellow Magistra (Horomancy) who cast the deciding vote in favor of Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
- **[[Egon]]**: Fellow Magister (Kinemancy) who voted in favor of Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
- **[[Malefane]]**: Fellow Magister (Eidomancy) who countered Nasir's appeals and voted against (392 AC)
- **[[Tarjani]]**: Fellow Magistra (Phonomancy) who voted against Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
