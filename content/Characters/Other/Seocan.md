# Seocan

## Overview

Seocan is an [[Ordis]] soldier serving in [[Gulrang Oduro]]'s squad during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Belisenki Night Assault at Storvhit

Seocan was assigned to interception mode during [[Belisenki]] night assault on convoy traveling toward [[Storhvit]] base camp. After [[Akesha Adhikari]] distributed [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides, the [[Belisenki]] ceased attacking.

## Relationships

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: Squad commander during [[Storhvit]] operations (393 AC)
- **[[Akesha Adhikari]]**: [[Yzmir]] mage who brought [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides solution (393 AC)
