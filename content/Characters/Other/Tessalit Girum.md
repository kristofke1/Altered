# Tessalit Girum

## Overview

Tessalit Girum is a Foundry Armorer and member of [[The Fulcrum]], the Guild responsible for preparing vehicles and equipment for the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. She specializes in repairing and armoring airships. In 393 AC, she was appointed to modernize the [[Bravos]]' training dummies at [[Haven]] and was subsequently taken in for questioning regarding the [[Phoenix Egg]] theft.

## Timeline

### 372 AC - Airship Repairs with Apprentices
Tessalit worked on repairing a damaged airship, hammering armor back into shape and realigning cabin panels. [[Brassbugs]] worked alongside her on the fuselage, welding and cutting. The foreman assigned [[Treyst]] and [[Paju]] to assist her for a full day, with Treyst using his kelonic bolt-popper to remove rivets so Tessalit could access the vehicle interior. Tessalit shared her flask with Treyst during the work.

### 393 AC - Phoenix Egg Theft Investigation
Tessalit had been appointed to modernize the [[Bravos]]' training dummies at [[Haven]]. Following the theft of the [[Phoenix Egg]] from the [[Parliament of Ashes]], authorities took her in for questioning to determine her involvement. Haven was sealed off during this period, with only Bravos permitted to circulate freely within its walls.
