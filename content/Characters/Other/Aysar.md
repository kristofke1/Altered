# Aysar

Aysar was a son of [[Aysun]] and twin brother of [[Ayxas]]. Unlike his brother who became the tyrannical third High King of [[Asgartha]], Aysar chose humility and oblivion.

## Legacy

A portrait of Aysar hangs in the Basileus's bedchamber at [[The Asterion]], opposite that of his twin [[Ayxas]]. [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] kept both portraits as reminders of the temptation and corruption any ruler might succumb to during their reign—Ayxas representing tyranny, Aysar representing the alternative path of humility.

## Relationships

- **[[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]**: Basileus who kept Aysar's portrait as reminder
- **[[Ayxas]]**: Twin brother who became tyrannical High King
- **[[Aysun]]**: Mother; founder of [[Yzmir]]
- **[[Rune]]**: Grandfather; first Kuningas of Asgartha
