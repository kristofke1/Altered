# Kennus

Kennus is a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] cat from the [[Kuth Kanat]] district of [[Arkaster]].

## Discovery

In December 392 AC, Kennus went missing and posters lined the streets: "LOST: Kennus, 4 years old." After weeks of searching, the owners resigned themselves to the worst.

In October 393 AC, an emergency alert at [[The Well of Tears]] in [[The Kadigir]] prompted deployment of a rapid-response unit including an [[Exalts|Exalt]]. The team expected to face a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] Singularity or dangerous creature but instead found Kennus calmly sitting beside [[Nimue|the Lady of the Lake]].

How Kennus entered the Well and survived the dangerous waters remains unknown. Veterinarians confirmed the cat was a previously undetected Chimera.
