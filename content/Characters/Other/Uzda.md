# Uzda

## Overview

Uzda is an [[Ordis]] soldier serving as a scout in [[Gulrang Oduro]]'s squad during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Belisenki Night Assault at Storvhit

Uzda was initially assigned to interception mode during [[Belisenki]] night assault on convoy traveling toward [[Storvhit]] base camp. When [[Tocsin]] called for retreat, [[Gulrang Oduro]] ordered [[Viri]] and Uzda to lead evacuation. The shield wall parted to let them pass, and they lit torches to guide retreating troops toward escape vector while archers provided cover fire shielding them from aerial threats. The two scouts returned with the first wounded soldiers. After [[Akesha Adhikari]] distributed [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides, the [[Belisenki]] ceased attacking.

## Relationships

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: Squad commander during [[Storvhit]] operations (393 AC)
- **[[Viri]]**: Fellow scout who led evacuation alongside Uzda (393 AC)
- **[[Akesha Adhikari]]**: [[Yzmir]] mage who brought [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides solution (393 AC)
