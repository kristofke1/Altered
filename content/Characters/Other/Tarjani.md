# Tarjani

## Overview

Tarjani is the [[Yzmir]] Magistra of the Sphere of [[Phonomancy]], one of five Magisters who sit on [[The Magisterium]], the High Council that guides the Yzmir Faction.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Vote

Tarjani participated in the Magisterium debate over whether the [[Kraken]] should be added to the Yzmir's summoning arsenal or prohibited. Speaking with a clear and confident voice, she advised caution as usual and voted against inclusion. The vote passed 3-2, with [[Nasir]], [[Egon]], and [[Chambulla]] voting in favor, while [[Malefane]] and Tarjani opposed.

## Relationships

- **[[Chambulla]]**: Fellow Magistra (Horomancy) who cast the deciding vote in favor of Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
- **[[Egon]]**: Fellow Magister (Kinemancy) who voted in favor of Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
- **[[Malefane]]**: Fellow Magister (Eidomancy) who voted against Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
- **[[Nasir]]**: Fellow Magister (Gephyromancy) who voted in favor of Kraken inclusion (392 AC)
