# Turuun

## Overview

Turuun is a venerable [[Muna]] priestess versed in natural ways and a diplomat who has dedicated her life to serving as a bridge between the human world and that of beasts. She receives visitors at the Muna temple in [[Amorgand]], built on a converted schooner turned sanctuary.

## Background

[[Yonago]] described Turuun as a venerable Muna who would know how to proceed with the [[The Musubi|Musubi]] ceremony when [[Rin]] wanted to bond with [[Orchid]]. When Rin presented herself at the temple in Amorgand, Turuun received her and listened attentively, then spoke to her about Musubi and the Muna. Everything Turuun said resonated with Rin's being.

The envisaged months of training turned into years as Rin and Orchid accompanied Turuun to [[The Spindle]] and the [[Bark Refuge]]. Through Turuun's guidance, they underwent the Musubi ceremony and became an Exalt, joining the Muna family.

## Relationships

- **[[Rin]]**: Guided through Musubi ceremony and years of training to become an Exalt
- **[[Orchid]]**: Rin's Chimera whom Turuun helped bond with Rin through Musubi
- **[[Yonago]]**: Fisherman who recommended Turuun to Rin
