# Boona

## Overview

Boona is a [[Chimerae|Chimera]] bassist and member of [[BLISS!]], currently residing with the [[Kasirga Clan]]. He has a punk chimpanzee appearance and erratic behavior patterns.

## Appearance and Nature

Boona appears as a punk chimpanzee, though [[Fen]] notes this look reflects the combined memories of those around him rather than necessarily his true form. Like all [[Chimerae]], Boona is extremely variable in nature. He has a habit of picking lice off himself in public and chewing on roots that others find unappetizing. He bares his fangs while playing bass.

## Background

Few [[Chimerae]] are permitted to roam free in Asgartha, but many who do end up with the [[Lyra]]. Boona found work with a circus before joining [[BLISS!]]. [[Fen]] had initial misgivings about bringing another [[Chimerae|Chimera]] into the group (alongside [[Crowbar]]), but seeing Boona and Crowbar interact convinced her it was the right move. The two engage in playful jabbing and cursing.

Boona displays erratic moods, sometimes showing lack of motivation or going on eccentric flights of fancy. However, once the band starts playing, he always grabs his bass and joins in. The band adapts their set list to his erratic behavior.

## Role in BLISS!

Boona plays bass in [[BLISS!]]:
- [[Fen]] on vocals
- [[Tamati]] on percussion
- Boona on bass
- [[Orbec]] on keyboard/keytar
- [[Crowbar]] on guitar

He also prepares instruments, coating lute strings with [[Sap]] for performances.

## Presence at Cayrat's Death

Boona was present when [[Fen]] discovered her grandfather [[Cayrat Steinn]] had died, waiting on the doorstep and staring at the ground.
