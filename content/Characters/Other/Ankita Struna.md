# Ankita Struna

Ankita Struna is a musician whose work is popular among [[Asgartha]]n citizens.

## Music

Struna's music is described as rhythmic yet ethereal. Her recordings are listened to by workers and engineers, including [[Axiom]] personnel at [[The Foundry]].
