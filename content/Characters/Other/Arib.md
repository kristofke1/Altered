# Arib

## Overview

Arib is a resident of the [[Vanderun Shards]] in [[Caer Esvander]] who was a childhood friend of [[Kauri]].

## Childhood

Arib grew up on the Shards alongside [[Kauri]], [[Fania]], and [[Tadeu]]. After completing everyday chores, the children would gather for their "second, truant school"—informal play and exploration among the floating islands.
