# Kavuri Kadis

## Overview

Kavuri Kadis is member of Kadis family lineage from [[Suspira]], part of [[Subhash Kadis]]'s extended family or close social circle. Appears in [[Subhash Kadis|Subhash]]'s memory projections created through sitar [[Alteration]] module during 393 AC performance at [[Storvhit]].

## Memory Appearance - 393 AC

Kavuri appeared as ghostly figure during [[Subhash Kadis]]'s performance of Prakash Ruggeveen's RWV 416 "The Lament" at [[Storvhit]] expedition dorm. Memory showed Kavuri playing with [[Marmo]] alongside [[Navin Kadis]], suggesting close relationship from [[Suspira]] mining community days. Projection created through sitar's [[Alteration]] module functioning like [[Constructs|Construct]], with each note acting as Khyalo symbol triggering specific memories tied to melodies. Appearance in performance of "The Lament" - piece associated with mourning and loss - suggests emotionally significant relationship with [[Subhash Kadis|Subhash]].
