# Reggie C. Bones

Reggie C. Bones is the creator of REDEALT, a collectible card game that became a phenomenon among students in [[Arkaster]] by 393 AC. The game was traded "faster than fresh pastries" among students and became so popular that school principals sometimes confiscated the cards.

## Relationships

- **[[Luis Tidix]]**: School principal who noted REDEALT's popularity (393 AC)
