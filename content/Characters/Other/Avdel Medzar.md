# Avdel Mezdar

## Overview

Avdel Mezdar is a reporter for [[Arkaster Echo]].

## Reporting Work

### 393 AC, May 28 - "Aurific Discovery" Article

Avdel authored article titled "Aurific Discovery" for [[Arkaster Echo]] reporting on death of alchemist [[Takao Cordisco]], whose body was discovered at his shop on [[Ulica Trismegistosa]] the previous day. Article covered details of Takao's nearly twenty-year career, his reputation for quiet generosity, and the apparent arsenic poisoning that authorities were investigating.

- **[[Arkaster Echo]]**: Newspaper for which Avdel reports
