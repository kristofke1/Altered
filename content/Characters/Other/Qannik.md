# Qannik

## Overview

Qannik is a child living aboard [[The Ouroboros]] as part of the [[Kasirga Clan]].

## Timeline

### 386 AC - Dormitory Incident

Qannik was among a group of children who snuck out of the dormitory during a puppet show performed by [[Auraq]] and [[Sierra]]. [[Fen]] tugged Qannik along by the ear to return to bed. [[Aruzhan]] then sang a nursery rhyme to help the children fall asleep.

## Relationships

- **[[Kasirga Clan]]**: Clan to which Qannik belongs
- **[[Fen]]**: Exalt who tugged Qannik by the ear back to bed (386 AC); Fen later wondered if Aruzhan might be scolding Qannik (393 AC)
- **[[Aruzhan]]**: Singer who sang the children to sleep (386 AC); may scold Qannik
- **[[Auraq]]**: Performer whose puppet show Qannik watched (386 AC)
