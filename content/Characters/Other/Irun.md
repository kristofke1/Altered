Irun is a Foundry Engineer within the Axiom who was born in Lyra. Irun underwent the metamorphosis ceremony and gained scales and a tail.

## Background

Irun was born in Lyra before joining the Axiom as an engineer at the Foundry.

## Physical Transformation

Irun underwent the metamorphosis ceremony, which resulted in gaining scales and a tail.

## Work at the Foundry

Irun works as an engineer at the Foundry. In 386 AC, when Sierra's drone returned damaged from a Tumult expedition, Irun connected it to a charging station. The drone began to screech and molt, shedding pieces of metal from its shell before transforming into the sentient Chimera Oddbal.

## Relationships

- **Sierra**: Close friend and fellow engineer at the Foundry
