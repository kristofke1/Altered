# Hanta

## Overview

Hanta was a [[Bravos]] warrior and comrade of [[Basira al-Arshad]]. His ashes are interred in the [[Parliament of Ashes]] at [[Haven]] alongside other fallen Bravos heroes.

## Relationships

- **[[Basira al-Arshad]]**: Fellow Bravos who considered Hanta a comrade and friend (pre-368 AC)
