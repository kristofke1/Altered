# Jiruu Kiet

## Overview

Jiruu Kiet is a contemporary theorist who authored the *Treatise of the Demiurge, or The Death of Philosophy*, published by [[Ibis Publishing]]. The work advances the hypothesis that [[Aurica|Aurican]] thought, through its omnipresence, has inadvertently imposed its structure upon the world itself. Kiet's interpretation, already hotly contested, argues that the observer generates reality and conceals its true nature behind their own conceptualization, making truth perpetually inaccessible behind myths, beliefs, and faith. She urges a change of perspective, advocating self-knowledge as the path to discovering the world's truth.

## Relationships

- **[[Aurica]]**: Foundational philosopher whose dominance Kiet challenges
- **[[Ibis Publishing]]**: Publisher of the Treatise of the Demiurge
