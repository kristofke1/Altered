# Kaaba

## Overview

Kaaba is an [[Ordis]] Templar who served as expedition leader at [[The Crow's Eye]] in 393 AC. She rides a giant lizard mount and is accompanied by a squire. [[Gulrang Oduro]] considers her ambitious and reckless.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, June-September - Arrival at Crow's Eye
Kaaba arrived at [[The Crow's Eye]] as expedition leader, descending the shaft walls on her giant lizard mount while [[Gulrang Oduro]] prepared troops to break camp.

### 393 AC, June-September - Sidelining Gulrang
Kaaba assigned [[Gulrang Oduro]] to create a remote headquarters using [[Mesektet]], effectively removing Gulrang from frontline operations.

## Relationships

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: [[Ordis]] captain who disapproves of Kaaba's ambition and recklessness (393 AC)
- **[[The Crow's Eye]]**: Location of Kaaba's expedition command
