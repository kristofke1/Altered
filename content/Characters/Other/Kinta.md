# Kinta

## Overview

Kinta is a [[Lyra]] painter of the [[Tisdhera Clan]] who resides in [[The Wayfarer]]. Together with her husband [[Ghamam]], she adopted [[Nadir]] after finding the infant on their doorstep following a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity collision with their metropolis.

## Family and Home

Kinta and [[Ghamam]] maintain a painting studio in their home aboard [[The Wayfarer]]. Their house possesses a capricious, temperamental personality—its sliding workshop door jams during plastering or painting work, its stove refuses to function during certain meals, and its bathroom plays hide-and-seek after extended water usage.

Kinta paints landscapes in the studio, which has large bay windows and stores solvents and paints.

## Adoption of Nadir

When the [[Lyra]] metropolis collided with a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity, a naked, silent newborn appeared on the couple's doorstep. Kinta took him in her arms to warm him, wrapping him in cloth and blanket. The couple raised the child as their son, naming him [[Nadir]].

Explaining the baby's sudden arrival to neighbors required considerable deception. As Nadir grew and bizarre phenomena followed in his wake, Kinta and Ghamam invented increasingly far-fetched excuses to conceal his unusual nature. The couple swore to keep the secret of Nadir's true origins.

## Response to Nadir's Behavior

When teachers frequently summoned the couple to discuss Nadir's solitary behavior and detachment from other children, Kinta maintained this was simply who he was and that he only needed acceptance. She dismissed concerns about his quiet, sketching-focused nature.

When [[Ghamam]] considered leaving [[The Wayfarer]] so Nadir would not have to endure the clowning characteristic of the [[Tisdhera Clan]], Kinta insisted that moving would be worse.

When [[Bubbles]] joined their household, Kinta and Ghamam immediately recognized the carp as a [[Chimerae|Chimera]], though they never learned where Nadir had found the creature.
