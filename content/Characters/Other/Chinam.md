# Chinam

## Overview

Chinam is an [[Initiates|Initiate]] and member of [[The Qorgan]] who operates under [[Wanjiru]]'s direction, using [[Axiom]] affiliation as cover.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Revealing Axiom Sap Stockpile

Chinam lured [[Afanas Korovinovich]] to a warehouse near [[The Refinery]] where [[Axiom]] had secretly stockpiled [[Sap]]-filled artifacts, revealing herself holding an artifact after he entered through a wall with modified porosity.

- **[[Wanjiru]]**: [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] leader whom Chinam serves
