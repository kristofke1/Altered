# Nagi de Santis

## Overview

Nagi de Santis was a sculptor best known for creating "The Night Watcher," a statue of [[Leocardius Sree]] displayed in the [[Emir Garfagnini Pavilions]].

## Timeline

### Pre-322 AC - The Night Watcher

Nagi de Santis sculpted "The Night Watcher" in the likeness of [[Leocardius Sree]]. She created the piece because Leocardius had been the only one to support her when everyone else turned their backs on her.

## Relationships

- **[[Leocardius Sree]]**: Subject of her sculpture "The Night Watcher"; the only person who supported her when others abandoned her
