# Asor

Asor is the gardener at the [[Kuna]] villa retreat of the ruun-Aysun family ([[Sigismar Synd]] and [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]]'s family).

## Role

Asor maintains the gardens and grounds of the family villa on the island of [[Kuna]]. During the [[Season of Mists]], Asor typically travels to town along with his wife [[Dorota]], leaving the villa largely unattended.

## Relationships

- **[[Dorota]]**: Wife, also works at [[Kuna]] villa
- **[[Sigismar Synd]]**: Villa belongs to his family
- **[[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]]**: Villa belongs to her family
- **[[Kuna]]**: Island location where he works as gardener
