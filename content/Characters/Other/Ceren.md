# Ceren

## Overview

Ceren was the Consort of [[Rune]], the first Kuningas of [[Asgartha]]. She had no idea where Rune could be when he mysteriously vanished in 42 AC.

## Family

Ceren was mother to [[Aysun]], who continued Rune's lineage and founded the [[Yzmir]] Faction.

## Timeline

### 42 AC - The Vanishing

When [[Rune]] disappeared, Ceren was questioned by [[Tamrat]] as part of the inquiry commission investigating the disappearance. After Tamrat's investigation and his consultation with [[Aysun]], he came to Ceren with the official explanation: her Consort, feeling he was at the twilight of his life, had gone to challenge [[The Tumult]] one final time, never to return.

Ceren and Aysun, dressed in black and in mourning, bowed before an empty urn at the ceremonial funeral. They invited the populace to mobilize for the upcoming elections to decide Rune's successor. Several months of mourning were decreed in homage to the hero of [[The Concord]].

## Relationships

- **[[Aysun]]**: Daughter who founded the [[Yzmir]] Faction
- **[[Rune]]**: Consort; first Kuningas of Asgartha who vanished in 42 AC
- **[[Tamrat]]**: Rune Knight who delivered the official explanation of Rune's fate to her (42 AC)
