# Flore

## Overview

Flore is [[Arjun]]'s niece and a [[Muna]] farmer who remains in [[Anthea]] while Arjun participates in the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. An [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] of Flore—summoned from Arjun's memories of her—tends the vegetable gardens at [[The Farm]] on [[The Screed]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC, June-September - Exchange with Arjun at The Farm
[[Arjun]] visited [[The Farm]] and met with Flore's [[Eidolons|Eidolon]], who appeared in her vegetable patch with watering can in hand, whistling as she walked through crops of oversized tomatoes and mulberry trees resembling hydrangeas. Arjun delivered bulbs and plants gathered from [[The Undergrowth]], which Flore tucked into her apron pocket, and in exchange she gave Arjun [[Sap]]-engorged strawberries wrapped in cloth.

### 393 AC, June-September - Bumblebeet Dream
Flore dreamed of [[Bumblebeet]]s emerging from vegetable beds at [[The Farm]] and flying off in buzzing circles, planning to tell [[Celestino]] about the strange vision.
