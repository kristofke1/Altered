# Isikeli

## Overview

Isikeli is a researcher present at the [[Nilam Spires]] dimensional pocket laboratory in 393 AC.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Nilam Spires Laboratory Research

Present at the dimensional pocket laboratory at [[Nilam Spires]]' seventh branch alongside [[Kuwat Vasanti]], [[Moyo Chibuye]], and [[Saskia Averina]], studying a [[Silk|maggot specimen]] that had survived dormant inside frozen [[The Nilam|world-tree]] bark.
