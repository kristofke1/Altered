# Zahira

## Overview

Zahira, known affectionately as Zaza, is a [[Lyra]] mapmaker and cartographer serving as the Inkwell of [[The Wayfarer]], the [[Sahanka]] of the [[Tisdhera Clan]]. She mentored [[Nadir]] in artistic technique and the discipline of "mastering imagination."

## Background

The role of Inkwell—the keeper of cartographic records—is typically held by the Little Shepherd aboard a [[Sahanka]], but on [[The Wayfarer]], Zahira holds this responsibility instead of [[Sadeli]], the ship's Little Shepherd. Zahira works from her workshop, creating maps from data gathered by others rather than joining field expeditions herself.

The [[Tisdhera Clan]] Matriarch placed [[Nadir]] under Zahira's care when his [[Ignescence]] abilities began manifesting, recognizing that her cartographic discipline could channel his talents. Zahira's teaching philosophy emphasized fundamentals—reproducing, imitating, and copying to improve technique before developing personal style. Nadir initially found this approach tedious but gradually recognized that painting the external world improved his ability to identify and express artistic intent. Zahira termed this development "mastering your imagination."
