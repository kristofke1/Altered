# Mahmoud

Mahmoud is an [[Ordis]] health inspector who works for [[Waru Toowoom]], conducting inspections and sharing observations via [[The Gestalt]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - The Mess Inspection

Mahmoud conducted a health inspection of [[The Mess]] on [[Waru Toowoom]]'s orders, documenting unsatisfactory infrastructure and cleanliness issues while [[Hestia]] glared at him and offered meringue pie in what he noted as possible bribery.

## Relationships

- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: Superior who orders inspections and receives reports via [[The Gestalt]] (393 AC)
- **[[The Gestalt]]**: Psychic network through which Mahmoud shares observations with Waru
- **[[Hestia]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who glared at Mahmoud during [[The Mess]] inspection (393 AC)
- **[[The Mess]]**: Facility Mahmoud inspected (393 AC)
