# Nassefa

## Overview

Nassefa is an [[Ordis]] representative of [[The Armarius]], the political branch of the Ordis responsible for drafting ratified laws.

## Timeline

### 386 AC - Exalt Selection Meeting

Nassefa attended a meeting at [[The Monolith]] alongside representatives from the other three branches of the [[Ordis]]: [[Kakoba]] (Legion Commander of [[The Aegis]]), the [[Frog Prince]] (representing [[The Libra]]), and [[Cestimir]] (Chief Curator of [[The Sanctum]]). The four representatives deliberated to confirm the selection of the first three Exalt pairs to begin the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] ahead of the [[Expeditionary Corps]]: [[Gulrang Oduro]] and [[Tocsin]], [[Sigismar Synd]] and [[Wingspan]], and [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Mack]].
