Jubal Okech is a [[Bravos]] relic hunter serving as lead excavator aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. He is the twin brother of [[Abiram Okech]], an [[Ordis]] historian.

## Background

Jubal and his twin brother [[Abiram Okech]] pursue complementary careers despite belonging to different factions. Jubal retrieves relics in the field while Abiram analyzes artifacts, forming what Jubal describes as a working partnership.

## Role in Rediscovery Endeavor

Jubal serves as lead excavator under the oversight of [[Leocardius Sree]]. His responsibilities include retrieving artifacts and relics from expedition sites. During excavations, he handles physical recovery while Sree determines the significance of discoveries.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Crow's Eye Excavation Assignment

Admiral [[Temera Singh]] summoned Jubal aboard [[The Ouroboros]] positioned above [[The Crow's Eye]] to discuss the upcoming excavation of the underground city. Jubal expressed eagerness to begin work and outlined his approach: retrieving promising items for [[Leocardius Sree]] to analyze. He inquired about claiming items not directly relevant to the operation and expressed motivation to leave his mark on the discoveries.

### 393 AC, June-September - Excavation Under Basira's Escort

Jubal led excavation in [[City of Scholars]] under [[Basira al-Arshad]]'s escort, emerging from near-death experience with bag crammed full of fossilized [[Sap]] and relics. Despite surly and miserly personality, Jubal's habit of buying rounds of [[Sap]] beer and spending time with rank-and-file earned respect from fellow excavators.

### 393 AC, June-September - Aoife's Inauguration Celebration

Jubal bought drinks at [[The Mess]] to celebrate [[Aoife]]'s [[Phoenixian Guard]] induction alongside [[Basira al-Arshad]], resulting in an eye-watering bill.
