# Osoyo

Osoyo is an [[Ordis]] soldier and member of [[The Raptors]], an elite [[Tagmata]] platoon commanded by [[Zhen]]. Before joining the Raptors, Osoyo had prior experience working alongside an [[Exalts|Exalt]], and [[Gulrang Oduro]] both supported his candidacy for the [[Tagmata]] and rescued him during a descent into [[The Crow's Eye]]. As a recruit, Gulrang assessed Osoyo as having the most room for growth among his cohort.

## Military Training

### 391 AC - Recruit Assessment

[[Gulrang Oduro]] assessed Osoyo as having the most room for growth among officer candidates, noting his patience, discipline, quiet strength, and the hardness of steel in his eyes.

### 393 AC - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

Osoyo participated in [[The Raptors]]' reconnaissance mission over [[The Tumult]], flying with [[Zephyr]] leading the squadron. Upon landing on [[Mandjet]], he snapped to attention and reported to [[Zhen]] that Tumult winds were stable with nothing to report. Osoyo then launched paper heralds that scattered into the wind.

### 393 AC - Calming Aedyt Through the Gestalt

Osoyo used [[The Gestalt]] to calm [[Aedyt]] with a mental caress before a flight against a Leviathan, steadying her nerves as she prepared to deploy her paper wings.
