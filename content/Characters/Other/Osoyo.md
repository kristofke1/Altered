# Osoyo

Osoyo is an [[Ordis]] recruit whom instructor [[Gulrang Oduro]] assessed as having the most room for growth among his cohort.

## Military Training

### 391 AC - Recruit Assessment

[[Gulrang Oduro]] assessed Osoyo as having the most room for growth among officer candidates, noting his patience, discipline, quiet strength, and the hardness of steel in his eyes.

## Personality

Osoyo demonstrates patience, discipline, and quiet determination. While not the most eloquent or talented recruit, his strength is understated rather than flashy. He keeps his eyes constantly on objectives and displays a drive rarely seen in someone so young, qualities that marked him as officer material in [[Gulrang Oduro]]'s assessment.
