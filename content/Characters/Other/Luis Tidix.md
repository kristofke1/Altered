# Luis Tidix

Luis Tidix is the principal of a middle school in [[Arkaster]]. In 393 AC, he commented on the popularity of the REDEALT collectible card game among students, noting he sometimes had to confiscate the cards.

## Relationships

- **[[Reggie C. Bones]]**: Creator of REDEALT card game popular among Tidix's students
