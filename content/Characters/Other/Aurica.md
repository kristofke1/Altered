# Aurica

Aurica was an Asgarthan writer known for philosophical treatises.

## Legacy

Aurica's writings were studied by [[Leocardius Sree]] during his formative years as a scholar. Sree, who would later become Rector of [[The Sanctum]], studied Aurica's works alongside those of other great Asgarthan theorists including [[Fenn]] and [[El-Amin]].
