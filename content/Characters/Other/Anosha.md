# Anosha

## Overview

Anosha is an [[Ordis]] soldier serving as an archer in [[Gulrang Oduro]]'s squad during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Belisenki Night Assault at Storvhit

Anosha was assigned to rear support during [[Belisenki]] night assault on convoy traveling toward [[Storhvit]] base camp. When [[Belisenki]] swarms dove toward the shield wall, Anosha fired arrows alongside [[Eigil]] in their wake. After [[Akesha Adhikari]] distributed [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides, the [[Belisenki]] ceased attacking.

## Relationships

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: Squad commander during [[Storhvit]] operations (393 AC)
- **[[Eigil]]**: Fellow archer who provided covering fire during [[Belisenki]] assault (393 AC)
- **[[Akesha Adhikari]]**: [[Yzmir]] mage who brought [[Mana Moths]] chrysalides solution (393 AC)
