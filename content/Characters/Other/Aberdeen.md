# Aberdeen

Aberdeen, known as Aby, is an [[Axiom]] Apprentice at [[The Foundry]] who also works as a Sap courier at [[The Screed]].

## Training and Career

By 392 AC, Aberdeen works as [[Sierra]]'s Apprentice at Sierra's workshop in The Foundry. Sierra plans to promote Aberdeen to Journeyman in a few months and transfer the workshop to her.

## Work on FarFetch

Aberdeen works with Sierra on iterations of the FarFetch [[Automata|Automaton]]. After a previous test caused an explosive disaster that burned [[Subhash Kadis]]'s beard, Aberdeen and Sierra investigated the new iteration to determine if a [[Kelon]] cylinder had overloaded, if there was a bad connection, or a short circuit.

## Sap Courier Work

At [[The Screed]], Aberdeen works for the Asgarthan postal services as a Sap courier, transporting [[Sap]] samples between locations on a unicycle. She carries an enormous delivery cooler on her back and navigates the labyrinthine streets of the [[City of Scholars]] to collect samples from Sap veins located by [[Sap Sniffer|Sniffers]].

Aberdeen prefers being at The Screed where the action is rather than in [[Arkaster]], despite the dangers lurking in the underground city.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Sap Delivery to Treyst

Aberdeen delivered Sap samples to [[Treyst]]'s workshop on [[Sierra]]'s behalf, nearly colliding with him on her unicycle while transporting a [[Sap Sniffer]] in her delivery cooler.
