# Phong

## Overview

Phong is a child living aboard [[The Ouroboros]] as part of the [[Kasirga Clan]].

## Timeline

### 386 AC - Dormitory Incident

Phong was among a group of children who snuck out of the dormitory during a puppet show performed by [[Auraq]] and [[Sierra]]. [[Aru]] motioned for Phong and [[Luka]] to return to their beds. [[Aruzhan]] then sang a nursery rhyme to help the children fall asleep.

## Relationships

- **[[Kasirga Clan]]**: Clan to which Phong belongs
- **[[Auraq]]**: Performer whose puppet show Phong watched (386 AC)
- **[[Luka]]**: Fellow child caught sneaking out of dormitory (386 AC)
- **[[Aru]]**: Adult who directed Phong back to bed (386 AC)
- **[[Aruzhan]]**: Singer who sang the children to sleep (386 AC)
