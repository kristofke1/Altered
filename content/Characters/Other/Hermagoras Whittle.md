# Hermagoras Whittle

## Overview

Hermagoras Whittle was the founder of [[Cornucoopia]], a grocery chain he established in 294 AC with the aim of ensuring the population's access to essential goods.

## Timeline

### 294 AC - Founded Cornucoopia

Hermagoras Whittle founded [[Cornucoopia]] to transport essential resources from the Peninsula's Provinces to urban centers, primarily fruits, vegetables, condiments, spices, and meats.

## Relationships

- **[[Cornucoopia]]**: Grocery chain Whittle founded (294 AC)
