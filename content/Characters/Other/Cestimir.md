# Cestimir

## Overview

Cestimir is the Chief Curator of [[The Sanctum]], the labyrinthine library occupying the upper section of [[The Monolith]], the [[Ordis]] Bastion. As Chief Curator, Cestimir holds a senior administrative position within the Sanctum and represents the institution in faction-level proceedings.

## Timeline

### 386 AC - Representing Sanctum at Exalt Selection

Cestimir served as one of four [[Ordis]] branch representatives who selected the three Exalt duos during Teamwork Training at [[The Monolith]], alongside [[Kakoba]] representing [[The Aegis]], [[Frog Prince]] representing [[The Libra]], and [[Nassefa]] representing [[The Armarius]].
