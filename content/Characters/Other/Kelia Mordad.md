# Kelia Mordad

## Overview

Kelia Mordad was a [[Bravos]] General during [[The Three Factions War]]. When [[Perjin Zan]] executed [[Ordis]] Templar [[Affan Cathal]] during armistice negotiations, Mordad attempted to intervene and was killed by Perjin as well. The deaths of both Cathal and Mordad caused the [[Bravos]] to turn on Perjin and escalated the conflict into total war.

## Timeline

### ~290 AC - Death at Armistice Negotiations
During negotiations following [[Affan Cathal]]'s surrender, [[Perjin Zan]] executed Cathal without warning. Mordad attempted to intervene but was also killed by Perjin. The [[Bravos]] responded by killing Perjin with arrows, and news of the tragedy prompted the Bravos to encircle [[The Kadigir]] and [[The Asterion]], seizing control of the capital.

## Relationships

- **[[Affan Cathal]]**: Ordis Templar whose execution by [[Perjin Zan]] prompted Mordad's intervention (~290 AC)
- **[[Perjin Zan]]**: Daughter of [[Peredur Zan]] who killed both Cathal and Mordad during armistice negotiations (~290 AC)
