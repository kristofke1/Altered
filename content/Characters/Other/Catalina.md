# Catalina

## Overview
Catalina is a member of the [[Kasirga Clan]] aboard [[The Ouroboros]].

## Cayrat Steinn's Death
Catalina was present on the day of [[Cayrat Steinn]]'s death, among those gathered to mourn the loss of their beloved Shepherd.

## Relationships

- **[[Cayrat Steinn]]**: Shepherd of [[Kasirga Clan]] whose death Catalina mourned
- **[[Sunisa]]**: [[Ordis]] soldier who spent time with Catalina's group at [[The Mess]] (393 AC)
