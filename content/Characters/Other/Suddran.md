# Suddran

## Overview
Suddran was aging swordsman at [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] monastery who served as mentor to [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]]. He subjected young orphan to monastic life of extreme rigor for four years before declaring him ready to become disciple. He was killed during [[The Magan]]'s attack on monastery in 74 AC.

## Role at Ugetsu-Fenshen (60-74 AC)

When [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]]'s parents perished in [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity in 60 AC, five-year-old Gericht was entrusted to Suddran at [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school on edge of Vindmark, eastern frontier of [[Caer Eidos]]. Suddran was determined to harden young orphan at any cost through monastic life of extreme rigor and endless labor.

After four years under this regime, Suddran declared Gericht ready to become disciple (64 AC). Training transformed to strengthening body and mind, learning blade techniques, and dueling.

## Death During Monastery Attack (74 AC)

In 74 AC, [[The Magan|Magan]] contingent attacked [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] after [[Walela]] refused [[Ayxas]]'s decree outlawing monastery. During battle, Suddran fell to his knees, speared from all sides. [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] witnessed his old master's death.
