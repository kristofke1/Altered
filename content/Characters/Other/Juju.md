# Juju

## Overview
Juju is a small bird found by [[Fen]] and cared for by [[Berchad]], the pigeon fancier of the [[Kasirga Clan]]. The bird became [[Fen]]'s companion after [[Cayrat Steinn]]'s death and serves as a scout during expeditions, signaling when no danger is nearby.

## Discovery
[[Fen]] found Juju as a small, frail bird on an endless plain of withered grass. It was so frail, as if it had just fallen from its nest, though there were no trees around. It chirped weakly as she nestled it against her chest. She brought it to [[Berchad]] for help.

## Naming
After [[Cayrat Steinn]]'s death and funeral ceremony, [[Fen]] hesitated for a long time before giving her little charge a name, but decided on Juju. That's what the Wind had whispered to her.

## Character
The bird flapped its wings and caressed [[Fen]]'s hand with its crest during her grief, as if trying to comfort her. [[Berchad]] had somewhat revived it and cared for it in the pigeon loft of [[The Ouroboros]].
