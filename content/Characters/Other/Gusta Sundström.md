# Gusta Sundström

Gusta Sundström was a [[Bravos]] messenger and the parent of [[Abelen Sundström]], the most celebrated explorer in Asgarthan history.

## Legacy

Gusta's child [[Abelen Sundström]] became renowned as the most fearless explorer [[Asgartha]] has ever known. Accompanied by [[Lyra]] boatswain [[Maya Vastag]], Abelen discovered an island far off the coast of the Peninsula and found a new passage between [[Suspira]] and the capital through the [[Undersea]]. Abelen used wealth and fame to launch further expeditions, particularly to the east, mapping new shores named "[[Keleti]]".

The Sundström family's exploits inspired generations, including Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]], whose childhood admiration for explorers like Sundström and [[Rifat Asdrubal]] helped give birth to the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].
