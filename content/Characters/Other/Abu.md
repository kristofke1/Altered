# Abu

## Overview

Abu is the father of [[Moyo Chibuye]]. He lived with his wife [[Tascha]] in a lumber village in the [[Nutsuwa Highlands]], roughly halfway between [[Kestia]] and [[Amorgand]].

## Role in Moyo's Life

Abu gave Moyo great freedom to roam the countryside. After one evening spent searching for Moyo past nightfall, Abu strapped an enormous straw hat onto his son's head for visibility. When other children trampled, tore, or smeared the hat with mud, Abu would patch it up again after Moyo returned home bruised from defending it.
