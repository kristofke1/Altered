# Faarsan

## Overview

Faarsan is member of [[Efrén]]'s [[Lyra]] caravan from Tisdhera Clan. Mentioned by [[Sierra]] in 393 AC letter to father as caravan companion helping [[Efrén]] with chores and daily activities.

## Caravan Life

As of 393 AC, Faarsan travels with [[Efrén]]'s caravan alongside [[Argiris]]. [[Sierra]] sent message through letter asking [[Efrén]] to give Faarsan hug and remind them about importance of helping with chores, playfully threatening they would "hear from her" if they skipped out on responsibilities again.

## Relationships

- **[[Efrén]]**: Caravan leader, shadow theater performer
- **[[Argiris]]**: Fellow caravan companion
- **[[Sierra]]**: [[Efrén]]'s daughter who remembers Faarsan from caravan days
