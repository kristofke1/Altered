# Oleneo

## Overview

Oleneo is a child living aboard [[The Ouroboros]] as part of the [[Kasirga Clan]], noted for frequently getting into mischief.

## Timeline

### 386 AC - Dormitory Incident

Oleneo was among a group of children who snuck out of the dormitory during a puppet show performed by [[Auraq]] and [[Sierra]]. [[Crowbar]] lit up his eyes to scare Oleneo, who was "up to no good as usual." [[Aruzhan]] then sang a nursery rhyme to help the children fall asleep.

## Relationships

- **[[Kasirga Clan]]**: Clan to which Oleneo belongs
- **[[Crowbar]]**: Fen's Chimera who scared Oleneo back to bed (386 AC)
- **[[Aruzhan]]**: Singer who sang the children to sleep (386 AC)
- **[[Auraq]]**: Performer whose puppet show Oleneo watched (386 AC)
