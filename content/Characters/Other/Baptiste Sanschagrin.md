# Baptiste Sanschagrin

## Overview

Baptiste Sanschagrin is a 68-year-old farm worker stationed at [[The Farm]], where his tasks include watering crops and handling [[Sap]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Sap-Induced Memory Treatment

Baptiste was treated by [[Moyo Chibuye]] for increasingly invasive dreams and parasitic memories caused by [[Sap]] exposure, with [[Silk]] surgically removing the intrusive memories while [[Saskia Averina]] secured them in a container.

## Relationships

- **[[Moyo Chibuye]]**: [[Yzmir]] Exalt who treated Baptiste for Sap-induced symptoms (393 AC)
- **[[Silk]]**: [[Chimerae|Chimera]] that performed surgical removal of intrusive memories (393 AC)
- **[[Saskia Averina]]**: Researcher who secured extracted memories in container (393 AC)
- **[[The Farm]]**: Workplace where Baptiste handled crops and [[Sap]]
