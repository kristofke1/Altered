Sunniva Vavcarezza, known as Sunn, is a Bravos Pathfinder trainee who serves as a scout for Atsadi. She achieved top rankings in multiple training areas including reconnaissance, orientation, and survival, earning recognition as the instructors' favored student. She grew up in tiny village near [[Kandu Range]] and joined [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] motivated by desire to see the world beyond her childhood home.

## Training Excellence

Sunniva excelled in pathfinding training and achieved top-ranked status in several different areas of expertise. The instructors considered her their pet student due to her consistent high performance. Her approach to challenges differed from typical Bravos competitive attitudesÃ¢â‚¬â€she pursued excellence for personal growth rather than to prove superiority over others.

This mindset proved effective, as her technique worked better than the perpetual showdown mentality adopted by many other trainees. While other students often languished in the middle of the pack or at the bottom, Sunniva consistently ranked at the top.

## Background and Motivation

### Childhood at Kandu Range

Sunniva spent entire childhood in tiny village near [[Kandu Range]], which she described as "most boring place imaginable." Limited opportunities and isolation of rural mountain life motivated her to seek wider horizons.

### Joining Rediscovery Endeavor

When asked about her reasons for joining [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], Sunniva stated simply: "I think I just wanted to see the world." She acknowledged this motivation lacked nobility compared to other recruits' reasons, describing it as potentially reckless rather than noble cause according to her mother's likely perspective.

She found deep satisfaction in exploration experience: "Sleeping under the stars, waking up every morning to landscapes no one's ever seen beforeâ€”that's what keeps me going." Despite calling herself less noble than others, she expressed fascination with watching [[Exalts]] shape world through [[Alteration]]: "Watching the Exalts shape the world around them is something that will never stop fascinating me."

### Personality Traits and Habits

Sunniva maintained relaxed approach to exploration work, finding thrill when plans came together. She carried sugared candies in her pocket (raspberry, strawberry, and other flavors identified by taste in darkness) and shared them with fellow expedition members. She broke energy bars in half to share with companions during camp downtime.

## Training Exercises

### 389 AC - The Chasm with Kojo

Sunniva participated in climbing training at the Chasm alongside Kojo. The training location was a vertiginous canyon with a maze of landings, pulleys, and mechanisms designed to challenge pathfinders. Yzmir had placed Sigils throughout the area to slow falls, preventing fatal drops when trainees made mistakes.

During the climb, Sunniva and Kojo reached a small ledge at the halfway point where they paused to catch their breath. The fjord already seemed far below while the peak remained far above. Sunniva sat beside Kojo, sucking on a mint lollipop, and handed him a tube of sugared almondsÃ¢â‚¬â€an unconventional choice compared to typical dried fruit and granola bars.

The two discussed the upcoming challenge. Sunniva had previously made it almost three-quarters of the way through the Chasm's mechanisms during a past attempt, while Kojo had only managed one-third. The canyon required navigating through nasty mechanisms where every wrong step could result in a fall, though the Yzmir Sigils provided safety measures.

## Expeditionary Corps Service

### 393 AC - Storvhit Expedition

Sunniva served as scout for [[Atsadi]] during [[Storvhit]] expeditions. Her role included reconnaissance, setting up camp, cooking, and other support tasks that allowed [[Exalts]] to conserve [[Mana]] for critical [[Alteration]] use. She explained philosophy: "Our main role is to help the Exalts retain their energy so they can use their Mana only when absolutely necessary."

Despite describing tasks as cooking and camp setup, Sunniva emphasized importance: "Just surviving the cold drains a huge amount of their energy." She rejected characterization of work as menial, finding fulfillment in daily discoveries and watching [[Exalts]] reshape environment.

### Betting Culture

Sunniva participated in betting competitions with other expedition scouts, wagering on which [[Exalts]] would reach objectives first. She admitted being "down two Florets" but planned comeback on next expedition. Betting started immediately when [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] mobilized from [[Caer Oorun]] to assist [[Exalts]], providing entertainment during long expeditions.

### Expedition Objectives

During February 393 AC expedition, Sunniva's group worked to build high-altitude shelter stable enough for future explorers to use. She compared this favorably to previous expedition where [[Lyra]] summoned hot springs from ground, which she enjoyed despite failure to meet objectives.

### Belisenki Attack - February 393 AC

During night at shared expedition camp, [[Belisenki]] triggered [[Yzmir]] alert wards set around perimeter. Sound of countless tiny bells signaled breach. Soldiers emerged from tents hastily dressing and gearing up. [[Muna]] mages clutching staves took positions at perimeter, some levitating to form circle meters above ground probing shadows.

Sunniva helped fasten brigandine armor in response. When [[Belisenki]] swarm attacked, temperature dropped several degrees instantly. Wind rose with their arrival sending snow swirling through air, making it difficult to hold weapons steady.

During attack, Sunniva fought back-to-back with [[Yzmir]] Initiate, drifting few meters from main group. Their isolation worried observers. When Sunniva's companion was swept away into tempest, Sunniva continued fighting.

When another scout was struck and dazed with [[Belisenki]] approaching for kill, Sunniva intervened. She slammed into creature with large targe shield, sending it tumbling into snow. She stood over fallen scout extending hand to help them up.

Before rescue could complete, [[Afanas Korovinovich]] unleashed massive [[Alteration]] display. Shockwave hurled Sunniva and scout into air. Sunniva lost from sight in chaos as torrents of snow blasted in all directions. [[Yzmir]] mages raised magical barriers to shield themselves from carnage.

Sunniva sustained leg injury from [[Afanas Korovinovich]]'s shockwave blast, requiring extended hospital recovery period during which [[Sunisa]] made multiple visits.

### 393 AC - Hospital Recovery and Friendship with Sunisa

While recovering from leg injury in yurt hospital, Sunniva received visit from [[Sunisa]], [[Ordis]] aide-de-camp who smuggled blueberry candy under nurses' noses. Sunniva joked about escaping in wheelchair, causing [[Sunisa]] visible emotional range from surprise to embarrassment to considering feasibility of escape plan. She admitted honestly feeling tired of sitting around doing nothing, while [[Sunisa]] promised they would "raise hell" once Sunniva back on feet. [[Sunisa]] made multiple visits despite no obligation, providing welcome distraction Sunniva was grateful for. When [[Sunisa]] mentioned [[Exalts]] needing backup, Sunniva recognized underlying message sounded like "not sure we're coming back," causing her jaw to tighten as no cast had ever felt so suffocating. Sunniva appeared carefree on outside but embers still burned hot at heart of soul.

### 393 AC, June-September - Reunion with Kojo at The Mess

After completing convalescence, Sunniva reunited with [[Kojo Oduro]] at [[The Mess]], meeting him in line for reservations. The two caught up over Sap borscht with fried eggs and fondue, with Sunniva describing her [[Storvhit]] journey while Kojo shared his recent difficulties. Sunniva admitted feeling jealous of Kojo's Exalt status. She demonstrated her characteristic appetite, gulping down a fried egg whole from her second helping of borscht.

## Personality

Sunniva demonstrated relaxed and non-competitive companionship style. Unlike other pairs who maintained constant rivalry, she approached challenges without perpetual showdown mentality. Her company was pleasant during expeditions, and she showed thoughtfulness in bringing sweets to share during breaks.

She maintained friendly demeanor even in difficult conditions, initiating conversation with expedition members from other factions. She grinned and scrunched nose when amused, scratched head when puzzled, and leaned on ground when finally warm by fire.
