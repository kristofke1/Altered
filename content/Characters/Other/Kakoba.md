# Kakoba

Kakoba is Legion Commander and well-rounded soldier-scholar. He is strong supporter of Avkan ruun-Heshkari and serves as wise, bold, and stubborn politician.

## Military and Political Role

Kakoba holds position of Legion Commander in Ordis forces. He combines military expertise with scholarly knowledge, making him well-rounded leader. He is recognized as strong supporter of Basileus Avkan ruun-Heshkari.

## Political Character

Kakoba is described as wise, bold, and stubborn politician. His temperament includes tendency to provoke opponents and lie low to ambush them. He triggers traps by relying on opponents' tactical mistakes.

## Interview Methods

### 387 AC - Chess Match with Sigismar

Kakoba met with Sigismar for chess game serving as interview and audition. Pieces were lined up neatly on chessboard. Kakoba extended hand and Sigismar sat across from him while Wingspan lay near window.

Sigismar went on offensive moving pawn to d4 (queen pawn opening). Kakoba responded with knight to f6 to stop e4 center control - Indian Defense, classic response. Kakoba considered opening not intimidated.

Sigismar moved pawn to c4. Kakoba immediately responded with pawn to e6. After Sigismar answered with knight to c3, Kakoba moved pawn to c5. They sized each other up, sparring after first strikes.

Chess game was more than mere match - it was way for them to introduce themselves and show who they really were. Kakoba's strategy reflected his character: well-rounded, provocative, patient in setting ambushes.
