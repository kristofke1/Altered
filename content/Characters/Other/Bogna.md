# Bogna

## Overview

Bogna is a [[Yzmir]] dance teacher whose training in classical ballet techniques enabled [[Akesha Adhikari]] to develop her [[Mana Moths]] manifestation method combining [[Kinemancy]] with choreographed movement.

## Role

Bogna teaches dance within the [[Yzmir]] faction. Her instruction covers classical ballet techniques including ballonné, déboulés, pas de bourrées, arabesques, and pirouettes.

## Relationships

- **[[Akesha Adhikari]]**: Student whose [[Mana Moths]] manifestation technique was enabled by Bogna's dance training
