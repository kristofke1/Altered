[[Basira al-Arshad]] was looking for Nanshe when visiting the [[Kurung]] in 368 AC

Nanshe is a Mesopotanian Godess, associated with the the sea, marshlands, the animals inhabiting these biomes, namely bird and fish, as well as divination, dream interpretation, justice, social welfare, and certain administrative tasks.

Mostly associated with her symbolic animal, a bird. Currently compared to a Pelican.
