# Cuddly Corgi

## Overview

Cuddly Corgi is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] taking the form of a playful corgi with big eyes. Despite her unassuming appearance—prancing, getting distracted by dragonflies and butterflies—she is one of [[Arawn]]'s hounds, a tracker bred for his hunts.

## Nature

The corgi possesses a [[Muna]] aspect that some [[Chimerae]] find unsettling. She requires regular infusions of [[Mana]] to maintain energy over long distances. When receiving [[Mana]] through physical contact, she becomes lively and alert.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, June-September - Escort Mission to Nilam Spires
[[Teija]] sent Cuddly Corgi from [[The Crow's Eye]] depths to seek help from the [[Yzmir]] at [[Nilam Spires]], with [[Arjun]] escorting the Eidolon and providing regular [[Mana]] infusions for the journey. At the Spires, the [[Yzmir]] mages authorized use of [[Merlin]], and both Eidolons disappeared through a portal leading to the depths of the Eye.
