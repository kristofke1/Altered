# Tengu

Tengu is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] manifested by [[Sol]] through [[The Avgrunn]].

## Appearance

Tengu manifests as a winged demon with a red face contorted in rage and an elongated nose.

## Relationship with Sol

Sol summons Tengu when channeling rage and frustration. The Eidolon reflects Sol's inner conflict about her motivations, observing that her mission "pretends to be a noble cause, but it's no more than vengeance at its core."

## Relationships

- **[[Sol]]**: Summoner who manifests Tengu through [[The Avgrunn]]
- **[[The Avgrunn]]**: Manifestation method
