# Medusa

## Overview

Medusa is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] Gorgon with snakes on her head that drip venom. Her gaze can transform those she looks upon into amphibolite statues. She can be summoned using a [[Constructs|Construct]] and [[Kelon]] battery.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Petrifying Bravos Eidolons

[[Isaree Thongsuk]] summoned Medusa during a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] attack near [[Aerolith]] veins; Medusa petrified [[Bravos]] [[Eidolons]] whose presence endangered the site, enabling evacuation.

## Relationships

- **[[Isaree Thongsuk]]**: [[Axiom]] Exalt who summoned Medusa during Leviathan attack (393 AC)
