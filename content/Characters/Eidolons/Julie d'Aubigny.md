# Julie d'Aubigny

## Overview

Julie d'Aubigny, also known as Mademoiselle Maupin, is an [[Eidolon]] swordmaster and one of [[The Seven Blades]]—the legendary warriors of [[The Lost Tribe]] who protected the rear guard during [[The Great Wandering]]. She was a French opera singer and duelist whose individual name was lost to history for centuries until rediscovered by [[Atsadi]].

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Identified by Atsadi

[[Atsadi]] successfully identified Julie d'Aubigny as one of [[The Seven Blades]] through research and investigation. By 392 AC, the name "d'Aubigny" had become part of a mantra in Atsadi's mind alongside the two other discovered names: Dei Liberi and Bologne. Atsadi worked to learn enough about her to materialize the [[Eidolon]] through [[Alteration]] and challenge her using the [[Galisgi]] style.

### 393 AC - Named as Target

After [[Atsadi]] defeated [[Fiore dei Liberi]] in the [[City of Scholars]], Fiore revealed Julie d'Aubigny's name as one of Atsadi's next targets. Fiore noted that "Maupin" usually fights with rapiers.
