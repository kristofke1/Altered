Julie d'Aubigny, also known as Mademoiselle Maupin, is an Eidolon swordmaster and one of the Seven Blades, the legendary warriors of the Lost Tribe who protected the rear guard during the Great Wandering. Julie d'Aubigny was a French opera singer and duelist whose name was lost to history for centuries until rediscovered by Atsadi.

## Historical Identity

Julie d'Aubigny was a French opera singer renowned for both her vocal performances and her exceptional skill as a duelist. The historical figure's mastery of the sword made her worthy of inclusion among the Seven Blades, the mythical protectors who covered the advance of the seven tribes toward what would become Asgartha.

D'Aubigny was also known by the stage name Mademoiselle Maupin during her opera career. Her combination of artistic talent and martial prowess distinguished her as one of the illustrious sword fighters selected to protect the Lost Tribe.

Like the other members of the Seven Blades, Julie d'Aubigny's individual name was forgotten over the centuries following the Great Wandering. Only the collective title "The Seven Blades" survived in legends of the first Nomads.

## Rediscovery by Atsadi

### 392 AC - Training Target

Atsadi successfully identified Julie d'Aubigny (d'Aubigny) as one of the Seven Blades through research and investigation. By 392 AC, the name "d'Aubigny" had become part of a mantra in Atsadi's mind alongside the two other discovered names: Dei Liberi and Bologne.

Atsadi worked intensively to learn enough about Julie d'Aubigny to materialize the Eidolon through Alteration. The goal was to challenge and defeat d'Aubigny using the Galisgi style, as part of Atsadi's quest to prove himself worthy of wielding Excalibur.

During training sessions, Atsadi attempted to manifest Julie d'Aubigny while sparring with Surge. However, successfully materializing the Eidolon required substantial knowledge about the historical figure, making the process difficult and requiring continued study.

## Role in Atsadi's Quest

As one of the Seven Blades, Julie d'Aubigny represents one of the ultimate tests for Atsadi. The One-Armed Swordsman must:
1. Learn sufficient historical details to successfully materialize Julie d'Aubigny as an Eidolon
2. Challenge the legendary French duelist in combat
3. Defeat her using the Galisgi style

Defeating all Seven Blades, including Julie d'Aubigny, would demonstrate Atsadi's mastery of the sword and prove him worthy to be considered the greatest swordsman in the world—the only one who can draw Excalibur from its sheath.

### 393 AC - Named as Target

After [[Atsadi]] defeated [[Fiore dei Liberi]] in the [[City of Scholars]], Fiore revealed Julie d'Aubigny's name as one of Atsadi's next targets. Fiore noted that "Maupin" (d'Aubigny's stage name) usually fights with rapiers.

## Significance

Julie d'Aubigny's connection to the Lost Tribe and the Great Wandering makes her a symbol of sacrifice, duty, and martial excellence. Her dual nature as both artist and warrior adds additional significance to her legend. By identifying and challenging d'Aubigny, Atsadi honors the memory of the Lost Tribe's protectors while seeking to prove his own worthiness to join their legendary ranks.
