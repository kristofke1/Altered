# Phileas Fogg

## Overview

Phileas Fogg is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who manifests differently depending on which faction summons him. For [[Axiom]], he appears in a methodical, mechanical form; for [[Bravos]], he appears as an adventurer who disregards borders and established limits.

## Appearance

Phileas Fogg travels in a wicker basket suspended by an old fabric balloon. He carries a pocket watch which he checks impatiently, similar to the [[White Rabbit]].

## Relationships

- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction for whom Fogg appears in methodical, mechanical form
- **[[Basira al-Arshad]]**: [[Bravos]] Exalt who rode in Fogg's balloon to battlefield (393 AC)
- **[[Bravos]]**: Faction for whom Fogg appears as adventurer form
- **[[White Rabbit]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] whose pocket watch behavior Fogg resembles
