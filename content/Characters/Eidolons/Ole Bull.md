# Ole Bull

## Overview

Ole Bull is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] violinist with bushy eyebrows, based on the historical Norwegian virtuoso violinist (1810-1880).

## Summoning History

### 393 AC, June-September - BLISS! Rehearsal
[[Fen]] summoned Ole Bull during [[BLISS!]] rehearsal at [[The Screed]] alongside [[Ethel Waters]], then dismissed them to summon [[Baishan]].

## Relationships

- **[[Fen]]**: [[Lyra]] Exalt who summoned Ole Bull (393 AC)
- **[[Ethel Waters]]**: Fellow [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] performer summoned alongside Ole Bull
- **[[Baishan]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who performed after Ole Bull was dismissed
