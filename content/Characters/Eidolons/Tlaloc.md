# Tlaloc

## Overview

Tlaloc is a nature deity revered by the [[Muna]] Faction. Stylized statues of Tlaloc can be found within the [[Bark Refuge]] sanctuary inside [[The Spindle|the Spindle Tree]], where offerings of flower garlands and trinkets are placed.

The [[Muna]] honor Tlaloc alongside other nature deities including [[Inari]], [[Cernunnos]], [[Freyja]], and [[Yong-Su Tenzing|Yong-Su]].

## Relationships

- **[[Muna]]**: Faction that worships Tlaloc
- **[[The Spindle]]**: Location where Tlaloc statues are found
- **[[Bark Refuge]]**: Sanctuary containing Tlaloc statues
- **[[Inari]]**, **[[Cernunnos]]**, **[[Freyja]]**, **[[Yong-Su Tenzing|Yong-Su]]**: Fellow nature deities honored by Muna
