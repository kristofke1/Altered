# Winged Monkey

## Overview

Winged Monkeys are [[Eidolons]] associated with [[Dorothy Gale]], appearing as hordes of flying creatures with furiously flapping wings and whipping tails that descend upon enemies to bolster defensive ranks.

## Appearances

### 393 AC - Moyo's Nightmare

[[Moyo Chibuye]] dreamed of hordes of Winged Monkeys descending to reinforce defenders led by [[Dorothy Gale]] and [[Ozma]] against a dark presence resembling [[The Hunger]].

## Relationships

- **[[Dorothy Gale]]**: Commander who signals Winged Monkey troops
- **[[Ozma]]**: Ally present alongside Winged Monkey forces
- **[[Moyo Chibuye]]**: [[Yzmir]] Exalt who dreamed of Winged Monkeys during nightmare (393 AC)
