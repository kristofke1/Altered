Tinker Bell is an Eidolon summoned for precision repair work. Despite small size, Tinker Bell has dragon-like temper and makes displeasure known through loud tinkling sounds.

## Appearance and Behavior

Tinker Bell constantly emits tinkling sounds as she flits around rooms. She alights on workbenches to examine damaged equipment. She crosses her arms with air of disapproval when viewing destroyed tools, tinkling as loud as possible to show displeasure.

## 371 AC - Repairing Subhash's Gauntlet

Subhash summoned Tinker Bell to repair his damaged gauntlet after a day of drilling and excavating. While he could handle routine repairs himself, he needed assistance with precision work. He rearranged the glyphs on his Construct's surface, and Tinker Bell appeared, emitting tinkling sounds as she flitted around the room.

Tinker Bell alighted on the workbench and examined the destroyed gauntlet. She crossed her arms and tinkled loudly to show displeasure. Despite Subhash's apology, the Eidolon remained displeased and haughtily pointed at his toolbox. Subhash used Alteration to shrink the toolbox to accommodate Tinker Bell's small size, then handed it to her.

## Skills and Abilities

Tinker Bell demonstrates expertise in:
- Precision repair work
- Small-scale tool repair
- Gauntlet maintenance
- Fine mechanical work

## Equipment

Requires miniaturized toolbox shrunk through Alteration to accommodate small size.

## Relationships

- **Subhash Kadis**: Summoned Tinker Bell for precision repair work despite her difficult temperament (371 AC)
