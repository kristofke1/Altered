# Nuit

## Overview

Nuit is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] based on the Egyptian sky goddess who brings nightfall. [[Nadir]] painted a depiction of Nuit to prepare for summoning her—the painting depicts Nuit hugging the sun, cradling it beneath a cloak of stars and darkness. Nadir planned to add golden patterns recalling Egyptian gods and an eclipse to make the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] more vivid and clearly identifiable to viewers.

To summon Nuit and make night fall, Nadir must remember every detail of the scene: the constellations, the starlight, and the moons.
