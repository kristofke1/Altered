# Aurora

## Overview
Aurora is an [[Eidolon]] and goddess, daughter of Hyperion, sister of the Sun and Moon, and herald of the new day. She was the romantic companion of [[Atsadi]] and is currently imprisoned in a crystal cocoon created by a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity near [[Dusk]]. Before her imprisonment, she channeled her power through a cicada to create [[Surge]] and save Atsadi's life.

## Divine Nature
Aurora is identified as:
- Daughter of Hyperion (the Titan of light)
- Sister of the Sun and Moon
- Herald of the new day
- A goddess representing the dawn

As an [[Eidolon]], Aurora exists detached from the physical world and can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those who know her story and can imagine her form. [[Atsadi]]'s father told him Aurora's story to explain what light was.

## Relationship with Atsadi
When [[Atsadi]] was old enough to watch over his family's goat herds alone in [[Dusk]], he invoked Aurora to play and ward off loneliness. He gave the Eidolon the appearance of a child his age. She was always there to play hide and seek among rocks, run and help gather goats, and lie in the grass watching clouds.

As years passed, Aurora became his accomplice and confidante. As Atsadi grew, her appearance changed to reflect his own age - adapting her form to match his development. They would huddle together sharing her warmth, strolling arm in arm and exchanging dreams and experiences.

A cicada constantly accompanied them, buzzing around and later landing on Atsadi's shoulder, vibrating its abdomen as if to bless their union.

## Romance
One day as they napped together, [[Atsadi]] sat up on his elbow to look at her face and gaze into her eyes. As naturally as could be, he kissed her lips and she returned the kiss. Initially shy and awkward, their embrace became passionate and fiery. Despite the difference in their conditions - her eternal as an Eidolon, him mortal and condemned to eventual disappearance - they imagined a lifetime together.

Aurora was Atsadi's happiness and light. She taught him [[Alteration]], and in return, he fashioned marvelous groves and lakes with clear, limpid water in which their emotions were reflected. They wanted more than anything to enjoy the present day, believing they had a whole lifetime to find a solution to their differing natures.

## The Crystal Prison
One night, as they slept nestled together on a boat, [[Atsadi]] was roused by the cicada's chiming. The world around them glowed with strange iridescent light. The grass turned pink before crystallizing, blades chiming as they clashed and shattered, while particles rose like tourmaline dust. Their boat became trapped in a quartz cocoon.

Aurora's tranquil eyes began to petrify, covered with gleaming facets. The boat, caught in a growing vice of topaz, began to overturn and sink. Water enveloped the Eidolon before freezing like ice around her. [[Atsadi]] grabbed her hand and pulled with all his might to free her, but his arm was ensnared in turn as thousands of splinters pierced his flesh.

## Saving Atsadi
Through the translucent crystal barrier, Aurora stared intensely at [[Atsadi]]. In her final moments of freedom, light emanated from her, spreading vigorously in the crystalline membrane until it burst out of the cocoon and struck the cicada that had always accompanied them.

Aurora managed to imbue the cicada with a portion of her power and light, successfully shaping a Chimera by relying on the insect. She succeeded in this final effort before the light in her eyes faded, her gaze becoming dull and lost in the distance.

The cicada's carapace tore from containing too much energy, and out emerged [[Surge]] - a new being fashioned from crackling light. Surge severed Atsadi's ensnared arm and cauterized the wounds, saving his life and alerting his family.

## Current Status
Aurora remains encased in crystal at the glass lake on the heights of [[Dusk]]. [[Atsadi]] has struck her cocoon with his valid hand, but no tool or weapon can break it. He has summoned [[Eidolons]] through [[Alteration]] to ask for help, but none could breach the crystal prison.

[[Surge]] revealed that only [[Excalibur]], the mythical sword wielded by the heir of the King of Kings, could crack the crystal jail. This sword can slice through mountain peaks and cleave the waters of the sea.

## The Quest to Free Her
[[Atsadi]] made a vow before the [[Lady of the Lake]] to become the world's greatest swordsman so he could be deemed worthy to wield [[Excalibur]]. He seeks to obtain the legendary blade and use it to free Aurora from her torpor so that a new dawn may finally rise on their love.

After following [[Bedivere]]'s teachings and traveling the world studying under numerous masters, Atsadi developed the [[Galisgi]] sword style. He defeats legendary warriors and seeks to identify and challenge [[The Seven Blades]] of the [[The Lost Tribe|Lost Tribe]].

Atsadi hopes that by covering himself in sufficient glory, his reputation will allow him to endure as an [[Eidolon]] beyond death. Thus, he could access eternity to spend it with Aurora, who still fills and animates his heart.

## Symbolism
Aurora represents the liminal space between night and day, shadow and light. [[Atsadi]] would position himself where a band of light struck his face at dawn and call out to her. During this moment between night and day, at the border of shadow and light, she would come to keep him company.

Her imprisonment in crystal represents light trapped and frozen, unable to shine - fitting for the herald of the new day now caught in eternal twilight.
