# Gavroche

## Overview

Gavroche is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who participated in the [[384 AC Enosha Strikes]] at the [[Enosha Mines]] and later assisted [[Axiom]] operations in the [[Cloud Realm]].

## Timeline

### 384 AC - Enosha Strikes Messenger

Gavroche joined the demonstrators alongside other [[Eidolons]] during the [[384 AC Enosha Strikes]], serving as a messenger carrying communications between different resistance positions as strikers defended the occupied mine against private security forces.

### 393 AC - Aerolith Collection in Cloud Realm

Gavroche collected [[Aerolith]] nuggets from ruins in the [[Cloud Realm]] for energy specialists, placing them in a basket as directed.

## Relationships

- **[[384 AC Enosha Strikes]]**: Labor conflict where Gavroche served as messenger
- **[[Enosha Mines]]**: Location of the strikes
- **[[Isaree Thongsuk]]**: [[Axiom]] Exalt whom Gavroche supported during strikes and later worked alongside in Cloud Realm (393 AC)
