# Alice

Alice is Eidolon with blond hair who emerges from walls and transports individuals through ground portals. She carries bizarre creature resembling cross between caterpillar and water bear.

## Appearance

Alice has blond hair that she fixes after emerging. She wears garments with neckline where she stores bizarre creature. Creature appears as cross between caterpillar and water bear.

## Manifestation

Alice appears through walls preceded by oddly colored soap bubbles bursting from dividing walls. She leaps out of wall landing on rear end as if catapulted several feet. She rubs bruised buttocks after landing.

## Portal Creation

Alice plunges hand down neckline to pull out bizarre creature. She holds out hand to individuals inviting them to join her. When hand is taken, she falls backwards dragging companions with her. Ground opens beneath them and swallows them whole, transporting them elsewhere.

## Card Lore Timeline

### 385 AC - Rescuing Akesha from Despair

Young Akesha burst into tears on doorstep after Initiate asked her to leave so sister Kumari could rest. Akesha blamed self for sister's illness. No cures worked - poultices, balms, pills all failed. She swore she'd lose mind if she didn't do anything to help.

Light appeared from nowhere making Akesha look up at wall. Oddly colored soap bubbles burst out of dividing wall. Figure leaped out landing on rear. She rubbed bruised buttocks then realized Akesha was staring wide-eyed.

Alice smiled instantly, then plunged hand down neckline pulling out bizarre creature (cross between caterpillar and water bear). She quickly fixed blond hair, dusted self off, jumped up before holding out hand.

Akesha took hand though had no idea where Alice wanted to take her. Alice picked up Akesha and without letting go, let self fall backwards dragging Akesha down. Ground opened beneath them and swallowed them whole.
