# Nereid

## Overview

A Nereid is a sea nymph [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoned by [[Nadir]] to assist with water crossings. When summoned, the Nereid emerges from water like a coral reef coming to life, solidifying waves around her into an optical illusion.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Transforming Lake for Nadir
[[Nadir]] summoned a Nereid to transform a lake into a Japanese woodblock-style theater set with removable panels sliding from right to left as if pulled by invisible ropes, allowing him to walk across sandy ground between static rollers resembling the works of Hokusai and Hiroshige.

## Relationships

- **[[Nadir]]**: [[Lyra]] Exalt who summoned the Nereid to cross a body of water (393 AC)
