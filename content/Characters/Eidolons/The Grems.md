Grems are Eidolons who operate Kelonic Generators to charge Kelon-powered medical devices. They are known for giggling mischievously while working with machinery.

## Appearance and Behavior

Grems giggle mischievously while activating machines. They work together to operate Kelonic Generators, activating the equipment that charges Kelon crystals used in medical prosthetics.

## Timeline

### 380 AC - Charging Treyst's Mechanical Vertebra

Grems activated the Kelonic Generator while [[Treyst]] sat in his wheelchair with hands gripping the armrests. The energy tank hummed while inside, the Kelon crystal of Treyst's mechanical vertebra charged. The Grems giggled as they worked, though not maliciously.

### 393 AC, June-August - Sabotaging The Refinery

Three Grems attacked a regulator panel at [[The Refinery]], causing a malfunction that shut down [[Sap]] delivery for hours. [[Sierra]] discovered them while investigating the cause of the shutdown, having taken over for [[Subhash Kadis]].
