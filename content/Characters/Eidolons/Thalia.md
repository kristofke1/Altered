# Thalia

## Overview

Thalia is one of the [[The Nine Sisters|Nine Sisters]], the Muse [[Eidolons]] who serve as tutelary patrons of the [[Lyra]] Faction. She is the Matriarch of the [[Tisdhera Clan]], whose members specialize in painting and clowning, and who reside aboard the [[Wayfarer]] Sahanka.

## Timeline

### 384 AC - Meeting with Nadir's Family

After [[Nadir]] inadvertently stopped a Tumult singularity that threatened the [[Wayfarer]], Thalia's Shepherd summoned his family to the Big Top. Rather than scold them, Thalia offered cakes and tea, studied Nadir intently, and questioned him at length about his experience facing the pure Tumult. She then declared that Nadir and [[Bubbles]] would become an Exalt, insisting she would personally ensure they received proper training despite his parents' protests.
