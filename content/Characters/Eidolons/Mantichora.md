# Mantichora

## Overview

Mantichora is an [[Eidolon]] creature normally bound by the [[Mandates]] of the [[Yzmir]] Faction. The creature has a serpent's tail and lion's head and chest. Under normal circumstances, Mantichora should not be able to be summoned.

## Timeline

### 352 AC - Cebir Massacre

During a [[Bravos]] expedition to reclaim [[Cebir]], Mantichora was manifested alongside [[Khimaira]] and attacked the exploring company. The Eidolons slaughtered [[Basira al-Arshad]]'s comrades, with Basira as the sole survivor. Authorities later dismissed her claims about the Eidolon attacks as hallucinations induced by cold, fatigue, and stress.

### 392 AC - Investigation

[[Afanas Korovinovich]] revealed to [[Basira al-Arshad]] that both Mantichora and [[Khimaira]] are Eidolons normally bound by [[Yzmir]] [[Mandates]], meaning no one should be able to summon them. The fact that they were manifested suggests the work of a renegade [[Yzmir]] Mage—the [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]].
