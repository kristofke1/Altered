# Aloe Vera

Aloe Vera is a graceful plant Eidolon with long slender legs who displays playful, childlike behavior. She is summoned by Arjun as a companion for Spike.

## Appearance

Aloe Vera appears as a graceful plant with long slender legs. She is small enough to play in water with Spike and can sink her legs into hot spring water.

## Behavior

Aloe Vera displays playful, childlike behavior. She becomes delighted when seeing her playmate Spike and joins him in water play. She responds to Spike's repeated motions encouraging her to join him in activities. The two play in water together like children.

## Summoning

### 392 AC - Mountain Hot Spring

During a mountain expedition before departing for the Tumult, Arjun created a temporary onsen (hot spring) using Alteration. After materializing a natural pool surrounded by waterfalls with sulfur-tinged water at comfortable temperature, Arjun embedded the illusion within reality using the Skein so it would persist.

Spike rushed to the hot springs and dove in, splashing around. Spike tugged on bond with Arjun and their Alteration powers. Arjun called little Vera from their mind. The graceful plant appeared not far from the pond.

Delighted to see her playmate again, Vera sank her long slender legs into the steaming water. Spike motioned repeatedly for her to join him. Arjun watched them play in the water like children while fog settled around them as warm and cold temperatures collided.

## Relationships

- **[[Arjun]]**: Summoned Aloe Vera to play with Spike at hot spring (392 AC)
- **[[Spike]]**: Playmate who encouraged Vera to join water activities, the two play together like children
