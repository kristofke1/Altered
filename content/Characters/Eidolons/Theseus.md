# Theseus

## Overview

Theseus is an [[Eidolon]] who guides expeditions through the underground labyrinth beneath the [[City of Scholars]].

## Role

When [[Section 57]] of the [[Expeditionary Corps]] became lost in the network of tunnels and labyrinthine passages with stairways and walkways going in every direction, Theseus appeared to guide them through. His presence lifted the soldiers' spirits and they set off with renewed energy. Once the unit reached their campsite, Theseus vanished as quickly as he had appeared.

## Relationships

- **[[City of Scholars]]**: Location where Theseus provides guidance
- **[[Section 57]]**: Military unit Theseus guided through the labyrinth (393 AC)
