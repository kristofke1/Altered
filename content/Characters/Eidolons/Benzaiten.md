# Benzaiten

Benzaiten is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who commands giant snakes and serves as a strategic transport asset for [[Ordis]] forces during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Appearance

Benzaiten is a tall woman with eight arms. She rides a colossal serpent and holds a biwa (traditional lute) and a sword among her many arms. Her serpent mounts have bluish-purple scaly surfaces with golden sparkles.

## Summoning

Benzaiten can be summoned by tracing [[Glyph|Glyphs]] in snow, which creates a whirlwind that transforms into giant snakes. The serpents curl around the summoner's forces before Benzaiten appears riding one of them.

## Transport Capabilities

Benzaiten's serpents serve as rapid transport for [[Ordis]] troops across dangerous terrain. Each serpent head can carry three riders. She instructs the colossal reptiles where to go through an aerial dance timed to her biwa's rhythm. By changing her melody, different vibrations run through the group of serpents.

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Tundra Transport Mission

[[Gulrang Oduro]] summoned Benzaiten when [[The Gestalt]] ordered rapid reinforcement of eastern contingents. The Eidolon's serpents loaded Gulrang's troops for fast, safe transport across treacherous tundra terrain.

## Relationships

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: Ordis Exalt who summoned Benzaiten for troop transport
