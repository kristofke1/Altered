# Nisse

## Overview

Nisse are small [[Eidolons]] who work in [[The Talaria]] postal warehouses handling package preparation and shipping operations.

## Warehouse Operations

Nisse workers perform folding, wrapping, and packing duties in Talaria shipping warehouses. They work alongside [[Axiom]]-installed mechanical conveyor belts, though some have difficulty operating the automated equipment.

## Relationships

- **[[The Talaria]]**: Postal guild employing Nisse workers in warehouses
- **[[Axiom]]**: Faction that installed conveyor belt systems Nisse work with
- **[[Santa Claus]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who receives packages from Talaria warehouses
