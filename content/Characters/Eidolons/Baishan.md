# Baishan

## Overview

Baishan is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] musician of legendary power, known for playing that could transform listeners. He appears wearing a gold-embroidered tunic and favors dark glasses. [[Fen]] refers to him as the "Sap Duende" when he plays instruments coated with [[Sap]].

## Abilities

According to legend, one of Baishan's notes could split the world wide open and another could bring waves of light surging from his fingers. He possesses synesthetic abilities—every sound he makes gives rise to whirls of color and shapes spinning through the air like swirling flocks of birds, which he calls "murmurations." When playing [[Sap]]-coated instruments, the Sap blends with his playing as his eyes light up with flame.

## Summoning History

### 393 AC, June-September - BLISS! Rehearsal
[[Fen]] summoned Baishan at [[The Screed]] after [[Boona]] prepared a [[Sap]]-coated mandolin, and the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] played with such intensity that synesthetic murmurations of color filled the air.
