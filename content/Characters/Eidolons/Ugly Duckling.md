# Ugly Duckling

## Overview

The Ugly Duckling is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoned by [[Sigismar Synd]] through [[Heka]]. It is a small creature with large eyes that fits in the palm of a hand.

## Summoning

Sigismar summons the Ugly Duckling by writing words in a notebook. The Eidolon holds personal significance for Sigismar, who identifies with the fairy tale character's story of rejection and transformation.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Summoned During Election Wait

[[Sigismar Synd]] summoned the Ugly Duckling aboard ship while awaiting [[Arkaster Echo]] election results, calmly petting its head as he contemplated the outcome's implications for his sister [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]].

- **[[Sigismar Synd]]**: Summoner via [[Heka]] (393 AC)
