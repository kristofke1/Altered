# Kali

## Overview

Kali is a goddess [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] bonded to [[Sylas]], the body-hopping parasite and member of [[The Perjurers]]. She is bound to Sylas by psychic chains and wields knives that gleam with red spectral light.

## Appearance and Abilities

Kali manifests as a goddess wielding knives that produce a red spectral glow. She responds to Sylas's psychic commands through heavy chains that bind her to him—a single thought allows him to position her protectively or offensively.

## Relationships

- **[[Sylas]]**: Master who controls Kali through psychic chains
- **[[Maw]]**: Entity Kali was positioned against during confrontation (393 AC)
- **[[Lindiwe]]**: [[Yzmir]] Initiate possessed by [[Maw]] whom Kali faced (393 AC)
- **[[Shiva]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who manifested defensively against Kali during confrontation (393 AC)
