# Akhlut

## Overview

Akhlut are shapeshifting mammals capable of transforming between aquatic sea creature and terrestrial land animal forms. Native to [[Storvhit]] arctic region, they serve as versatile mounts for expeditionary forces, able to traverse both ice pack and shoreline terrain. Akhlut travel in packs and undergo dramatic physical transformation when transitioning between environments.

## Physical Characteristics

### Aquatic Form

In aquatic form, Akhlut appear as sea mammals with:
- Smooth skin covering body
- Fins for propulsion through water
- Powerful snout capable of breaking through ice
- Ability to deliver violent blows to break ice pack

The creature's aquatic form is optimized for navigating frozen seas and breaking through ice barriers with its snout, though this creates challenging riding conditions for mounted passengers.

### Terrestrial Form

Transformation from aquatic to terrestrial form involves dramatic physical changes:
- Head becomes slimmer when shaking after reaching shore
- Smooth skin gradually develops patches of bushy hair
- Hair slowly turns into dense black and white fur
- Fins extend and become muscular legs with claws
- Creature stands up and begins walking across snow

The transformation process is gradual enough to be observed in detail, presenting fascinating spectacle for witnesses.

## Behavior

### Pack Structure

Akhlut travel in packs, with all members transforming together when transitioning between environments. When pack climbs onto shore, entire group undergoes metamorphosis from aquatic to terrestrial forms simultaneously, creating coordinated transformation display.

### Domestication and Use

Akhlut can be guided and used as mounts by expedition forces. They accept riders in saddles and respond to direction from guides who pet their muzzles and provide care. The creatures demonstrate patience with mounted passengers despite violent ice-breaking required during aquatic travel.

## Use at Storvhit

### 393 AC - Expedition Transport

During [[Storvhit]] expedition, Akhlut pack served as mounts for transport across ice pack. [[Basira al-Arshad]] rode Akhlut across ice pack, enduring constant torture as creature broke through ice with violent blows from its snout. She stretched and massaged lower back to relieve soreness, her whole body tense from trying to maintain balance during ice-breaking journey.

When guide pointed to shoreline, Basira felt certain relief and caught herself sighing. She anticipated feeling good to drop bag on shore after sea voyage. With final ominous crack, Akhlut climbed onto shore. Basira needed no encouragement to climb down from saddle, cracking her back and taking few steps to loosen aching thighs.

As Basira petted neck of her mount, she watched its head becoming slimmer as it shook. She stepped back fascinated, watching transformation slowly occur. Smooth skin of sea mammal gradually developed patches of bushy hair which slowly turned into dense black and white fur. Fins extended and became muscular legs with claws. It stood up and started to walk across snow. All around, rest of pack also transformed from aquatic creatures to land mammals.

Basira muttered to herself that she shouldn't be getting absorbed in spectacle - she had work to do. She noted this seemed like bad habit [[Filiki]] had passed on to her. She went to find guide who was petting muzzle of his own Akhlut. The animal raised its head as she approached. She reached into bag for sextant that would help calculate route taken by [[The Ouroboros]].

## Relationships with Expeditionary Forces

Akhlut demonstrate working relationship with human guides and riders, accepting direction and care while providing essential transport services across challenging arctic terrain. Their ability to break through ice pack and transform for land travel makes them invaluable for expeditions requiring movement between frozen sea and shore environments.

## Relationships

- **[[Basira al-Arshad]]**: Rode Akhlut across ice pack during [[Storvhit]] expedition, witnessed transformation (393 AC)
- **[[Filiki]]**: [[Basira al-Arshad]]'s deceased partner whose fascination with spectacles influenced her behavior
- **[[Storvhit]]**: Arctic region where Akhlut are native and serve expeditionary forces
- **[[The Ouroboros]]**: Airship whose route was being calculated after Akhlut transport
- **Expedition Guide**: Cared for and directed Akhlut pack during transport operations
