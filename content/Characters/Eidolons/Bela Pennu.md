## Overview

Bela Pennu is a solar deity associated with the sun and fire, revered by the Bravos Faction and invoked during moments of great power and decisive action.

## Manifestation Through Kojo

During the performance retelling the Battle of the Kraken, the storyteller Auraq described how Kojo Oduro gathered tremendous power for his final strike:

"As night falls, Kojo shines like a second sun. At this moment, Ra, Bela Pennu, and Helios have made him the vessel of their wrath!"

This dramatic description portrayed Kojo as a conduit for solar divine power, channeling the combined wrath of three sun deities to deliver the devastating blow that destroyed the Kraken.

## Cultural Significance

Bela Pennu represents solar power, divine wrath, and the life-giving yet potentially destructive force of the sun. The deity embodies the Bravos ideals of decisive action and overwhelming force when confronting threats to humanity.
