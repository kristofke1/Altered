# Ethel Waters

## Overview

Ethel Waters is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] singer, based on the historical American blues, jazz, and gospel vocalist (1896-1977).

## Summoning History

### 393 AC, June-September - BLISS! Rehearsal

[[Fen]] summoned Ethel Waters during [[BLISS!]] rehearsal at [[The Screed]]. Her voice faded in turn as [[Ole Bull]] drew his final note, and [[Fen]] dismissed them both in a swirl of [[Mana]] to prepare for [[Baishan]]'s appearance.

## Relationships

- **[[Fen]]**: [[Lyra]] Exalt who summoned Ethel Waters (393 AC)
- **[[Ole Bull]]**: Fellow [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] performer summoned alongside Ethel Waters
- **[[Baishan]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who performed after Ethel Waters was dismissed
