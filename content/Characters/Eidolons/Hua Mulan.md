# Hua Mulan

## Overview

Hua Mulan is an [[Eidolon]] representing the legendary Chinese warrior who disguised herself as a man to serve in the military. She was disarmed by [[Atsadi]] in single combat and later became one of his teachers.

## Timeline

### Date Unknown - Disarmed by Atsadi

[[Atsadi]] summoned Hua Mulan through [[Alteration]] to test his [[Galisgi]] fighting style. The battle culminated in Atsadi disarming her, demonstrating superior technique and control.

### 386 AC - Training Atsadi in Power Absorption

Hua Mulan taught [[Atsadi]] a technique that fundamentally changed his approach to combat, advising: "You have to feed on what others give you." She meant that Atsadi could consume part of [[Surge]]'s being to make his Alter Ego's powers his own, and that with every [[Eidolon]] summoned, a portion of that Eidolon's power could be transferred to him.

During a sparring session, Hua Mulan wielded her jian like a wasp's stinger, attacking with probing strikes to test Atsadi's defenses. Rather than fighting back, Atsadi stayed defensive while summoning multiple Eidolons he had previously fought—not to have them fight in his stead, but to ask them to pass along some of their power. As the Eidolons appeared, Hua Mulan smiled. Atsadi felt transcendence taking root within him, absorbing powers temporarily from each Eidolon.

## Relationships

- **[[Atsadi]]**: Disarmed her in combat; trained him in power absorption technique (386 AC)
- **[[Surge]]**: Atsadi's Alter Ego whose powers Atsadi learned to absorb through Hua Mulan's teachings
- **[[Excalibur]]**: Mythical sword Atsadi seeks, driving his quest to defeat legendary warriors
