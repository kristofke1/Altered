# Hua Mulan

## Overview
Hua Mulan is an [[Eidolon]] representing the legendary Chinese warrior who disguised herself as a man to serve in the military. She was disarmed by [[Atsadi]], the One-Armed Swordsman, as part of his quest to prove himself the world's greatest swordsman and become worthy of wielding [[Excalibur]].

## Encounter with Atsadi
[[Atsadi]] summoned Hua Mulan through [[Alteration]] to test his [[Galisgi]] fighting style against the legendary soldier. The battle culminated in [[Atsadi]] managing to disarm her - a technical victory that demonstrated his superior swordsmanship without necessarily requiring her total defeat.

Disarming an opponent in combat is considered a significant achievement, as it neutralizes their offensive capability and proves superior technique and control.

### 386 AC - Training Atsadi in Power Absorption

Hua Mulan provided crucial training to Atsadi, teaching him a technique that would fundamentally change his approach to combat. She advised: "You have to feed on what others give you."

Atsadi initially interpreted this advice as referring to lessons from instructors and experience from battles. However, Hua Mulan meant something more profound—that Atsadi could consume part of Surge's being to make his Alter Ego's powers his own through their bond. With every Eidolon Atsadi summoned, a portion of that Eidolon's power could be transferred to him, making him stronger.

During a demonstration sparring session, Hua Mulan took a combat stance with her jian, brandishing it like a wasp's stinger. Her eyes remained calm as a pond sheltered from wind, her body motionless as a marble statue. She came alive suddenly, dashing toward Atsadi and attacking with probing strikes—right, left, down—testing his defenses for weak spots.

Rather than fighting back as he would have days prior, Atsadi kept on the defensive, focused on countering or dodging each attack. He had always thought he needed to become the best sword fighter through sword skill alone, but he neglected the fact that he was an Alterer, which provided his true versatility.

As they sparred, Atsadi summoned multiple Eidolons he had previously fought—not to have them fight in his stead, but to ask them to pass along some of their power. As the Eidolons appeared around them, Hua Mulan smiled. Through the summoned warriors, Atsadi felt transcendence taking root within him. He felt the duende overtake him, absorbing powers temporarily from each Eidolon to enhance his own abilities.

This training revealed that Atsadi's true strength came from being an Alterer who could temporarily absorb and combine powers from multiple sources, rather than relying solely on physical swordsmanship.

## Significance
Successfully disarming Hua Mulan represents another milestone in [[Atsadi]]'s quest. The legendary warrior's reputation for strategic thinking, adaptability, and battlefield prowess made her a formidable test of [[Atsadi]]'s skills.

The specific nature of this victory - disarming rather than outright defeating - suggests a demonstration of technical precision and control that complements [[Atsadi]]'s victories over other legendary warriors.

## Nature as Eidolon
As an [[Eidolon]], Hua Mulan can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those familiar with her legend. [[Atsadi]]'s ability to materialize and overcome her in combat demonstrates his mastery of both Alteration magic and swordsmanship.

## Historical Legend
Hua Mulan represents a figure from Chinese folklore, a woman who disguised herself as a man to take her elderly father's place in the military. In the Altered universe, she exists as an [[Eidolon]] whose combat skills and strategic abilities can be invoked and challenged by those seeking to prove their martial excellence.
