# Gran Bwa

## Overview

Gran Bwa is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] associated with the [[Muna]] who provides patronage for agricultural endeavors. Under Gran Bwa's patronage, the [[Muna]] established the Farm at [[The Screed]] outpost in 393 AC.

## Patronage at The Screed

Under Gran Bwa's patronage, the area surrounding the largest willow tree in [[The Screed]] was transformed into golden wheat fields, vineyards, orchards, and vegetable gardens. The Farm provides fruits, tubers, and legumes essential for the [[Expeditionary Corps]]' daily meals.

## Relationships

- **[[Expeditionary Corps]]**: Force benefiting from Gran Bwa's patronage
- **[[Muna]]**: Faction Gran Bwa is associated with
- **[[The Farm]]**: Agricultural complex under Gran Bwa's patronage
- **[[The Screed]]**: Outpost containing The Farm
- **[[Rin]]**: [[Muna]] Exalt who tried to forage from orchard but was deterred by Gran Bwa's presence (393 AC)
