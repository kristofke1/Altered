# Snow Queen

Snow Queen is [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] associated with ice and cold who trains disciples in mental techniques for capturing ideas from [[The Empyrean]] while limiting [[Remanence]].

## Appearance and Presence

Lacy wisps of ice form around the Snow Queen and on everything she touches or brushes against. Her presence creates frozen formations on surfaces in her vicinity.

## Teaching Methods

Snow Queen instructs disciples in reaching a state of mental stasis where all that remains is the idea being worked upon. Students close their eyes and slow their breath, seeking to let go of parasitic thoughts. The training goal involves capturing ideas from [[The Empyrean]] while limiting [[Remanence]] effects.

## Abilities

### Mind Exploration

Snow Queen can explore disciples' minds, observing the mental constructs they build to restrain ideas hooked through [[The Veil]]. She evaluates students' techniques for capturing and refining ethereal ideas.

## Eidolon Adaptation

Snow Queen was among [[Eidolons]] recommended for diversified summoning to alleviate burden of [[Storvhit]]'s endemic cold during [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] operations. Along with [[Jack Frost]] and [[Skaði|Skadi]], Snow Queen represented lesser-used [[Eidolons]] being explored or rediscovered for adaptation to arctic environments.

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Training Lindiwe

Snow Queen trained [[Lindiwe]] alongside other disciples in techniques for capturing ideas from [[The Empyrean]], moving between rows of students as they practiced reaching mental stasis states.
