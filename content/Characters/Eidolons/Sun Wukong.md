# Sun Wukong

## Overview
Sun Wukong, known as the Monkey King, is an [[Eidolon]] representing the legendary warrior from Chinese mythology. He was defeated in single combat by [[Atsadi]], the One-Armed Swordsman, as part of his quest to prove himself the world's greatest swordsman and become worthy of wielding [[Excalibur]].

## Encounter with Atsadi
[[Atsadi]] summoned Sun Wukong through [[Alteration]] to test his [[Galisgi]] fighting style against the legendary Monkey King. Sun Wukong's reputation for supernatural combat abilities, magical powers, and mastery of the staff made him an extraordinarily challenging opponent.

Despite the Monkey King's legendary versatility and combat prowess, [[Atsadi]] managed to triumph through the application of his one-armed sword technique.

## Significance
Defeating Sun Wukong represents a particularly impressive achievement in [[Atsadi]]'s quest. The Monkey King's reputation spans multiple domains - martial arts, magical abilities, strategic thinking, and shapeshifting - making victory over his [[Eidolon]] form a demonstration of [[Atsadi]]'s comprehensive combat mastery.

The battle proved that [[Atsadi]]'s [[Galisgi]] style could overcome not just traditional swordsmen, but also warriors with supernatural abilities and non-traditional fighting methods.

## Nature as Eidolon
As an [[Eidolon]], Sun Wukong can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those familiar with his legend. [[Atsadi]]'s success in materializing and defeating the Monkey King demonstrates his ability to test himself against warriors of diverse fighting styles and supernatural capabilities.

## Historical Legend
Sun Wukong represents the central character from Journey to the West, one of the Four Great Classical Novels of Chinese literature. In the Altered universe, he exists as an [[Eidolon]] whose combat skills, magical abilities, and legendary prowess can be invoked and challenged by those seeking ultimate martial excellence.
