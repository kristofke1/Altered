# Sun Wukong

## Overview

Sun Wukong, known as the Monkey King, is an [[Eidolon]] representing the legendary warrior from Chinese mythology. He possesses shapeshifting abilities and can create clone armies from his hair.

## Timeline

### 385 AC - Duel with Atsadi

[[Atsadi]] summoned Sun Wukong through [[Alteration]] to test his [[Galisgi]] fighting style. During the battle, Sun Wukong transformed into various animals and created an army of miniature capuchin clones from his hair. [[Atsadi]] eventually won but had overdrawn from [[Surge]]'s strength, leaving his Alter Ego as a flickering spark that required [[Shenlong]]'s lightning to revive.

## Relationships

- **[[Atsadi]]**: Summoner who defeated Sun Wukong after intense battle (385 AC)
- **[[Surge]]**: Atsadi's Alter Ego reduced to flickering spark after battle
- **[[Shenlong]]**: Storm dragon summoned to revive Surge after the battle (385 AC)
- **[[Excalibur]]**: Mythical sword Atsadi seeks, requiring him to defeat legendary warriors
