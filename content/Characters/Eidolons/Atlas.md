# Atlas

## Overview
Atlas is a colossal titan Eidolon of gargantuan proportions whose silhouette obscures part of the sky and whose shoulders scramble clouds. He serves as massive-scale transportation, capable of tearing off entire rocky outcrops and carrying them leagues in just a few steps.

## Appearance
Atlas manifests as:
- Colossus whose silhouette obscures part of sky
- Shoulders that scramble clouds as he stretches
- Hands emerging from mist large enough to seize the Colosseum or pick up the Asterion
- Body creating hurricane-force winds with movements
- Steps that cause the world to quake with earthquake-like tremors

## Scale and Proportions
To Atlas, humans are described as:
- Mere ants compared to his size
- Ticks on a buffalo
- Insignificant gnats

His hands could:
- Seize the Colosseum
- Pick up the Asterion
- Tear off rocky outcrops like a quarryman taking stone
- Uproot earth clumps like a gardener pulling weeds

## Abilities

### Landmass Transportation
Atlas tears off entire geographic features including rocky plateaus, outcrops, and small islands. He carries these under his arm like bulky packages, transporting riders and terrain together across vast distances.

### Delicate Gestures
Despite his immense size, Atlas attempts delicate placement when setting down carried landmasses. However, his concept of delicacy differs dramatically from human scale - what he intends as gentle placement feels like terrible earthquake to riders, causing trees to fall, rocks to spring up, and ground to break apart and move like sea.

### Environmental Impact
Each of Atlas's movements creates:
- Violent tremors jolting countryside when hands contact ground
- Hurricane-force winds shaking trees
- Biting winds blasting across terrain
- Thick dust clouds rolling in after placement
- Quaking sensations with every step

## Summoning History

### 392 AC - Transporting Basira
Basira summoned Atlas while perched atop a cliff. The titan's silhouette obscured part of sky as one hand emerged from mist at close range, rumbling past Basira. The hand suddenly dropped down and plunged into the plateau side, causing violent tremors to jolt the countryside.

Like a quarryman taking stone, Atlas tore off the rocky outcrop where Basira stood. Like a gardener uprooting earth clump, he lightly swung the small island he had detached from ground. Trees around Basira shook as if hit by hurricane. The moor was blasted by biting wind as Atlas carried the landmass under his arm like bulky package.

With each of his steps, it felt like the world was quaking. Basira grabbed onto a stump to keep from getting blown away, laughing half-nervously and half-madly. She felt insignificant - nothing but a tick on a buffalo or gnat to the titan.

Suddenly, Atlas dropped the entire atoll to earth. Though he intended it as delicate gesture, to Basira it felt like terrible earthquake shattering the world. Trees fell at impact. Rocks sprang up from ground, which broke apart and started moving like sea. Thick dust rolled in around Basira before calm restored. In just a few steps, Atlas had transported her leagues across the landscape.
