# Atlas

## Overview

Atlas is a colossal titan [[Eidolon]] of gargantuan proportions whose silhouette obscures part of the sky and whose shoulders scramble clouds. He serves as massive-scale transportation, capable of tearing off entire rocky outcrops and carrying them leagues in a few steps.

## Abilities

Atlas tears off geographic features including rocky plateaus and outcrops, carrying them under his arm like packages and transporting riders across vast distances. His movements create violent tremors, hurricane-force winds, and earthquake-like effects. What Atlas intends as delicate placement feels like a terrible earthquake to riders.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Transporting Basira

[[Basira al-Arshad]] summoned Atlas while perched atop a cliff. The titan's hand emerged from mist, plunged into the plateau side causing violent tremors, and tore off the rocky outcrop where Basira stood. Atlas carried the landmass under his arm while Basira grabbed onto a stump to avoid being blown away by hurricane-force winds.

Atlas dropped the atoll to earth—though he intended it as a delicate gesture, to Basira it felt like a terrible earthquake. Trees fell, rocks sprang up, and thick dust rolled in. In just a few steps, Atlas had transported her leagues across the landscape.
