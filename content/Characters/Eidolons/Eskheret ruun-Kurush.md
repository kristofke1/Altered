# Eskheret ruun-Kurush

Eskheret ruun-Kurush is an Eidolon affiliated with the [[Yzmir]] Faction who served as a trusted intermediary for secure correspondence during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. She died in 86 AC during the rebellion against [[Ayxas]].

## Mortal Life and Death

### 86 AC - Final Assault on Elysium
During final assault on [[Elysium]], Eskheret breached fortress gates. [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] attempted to save her after she accomplished this feat, carrying her battered body away from battlefield. She succumbed to wounds. Her death plunged [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] into terrible frenzy, carrying him in whirlwind of blades to storm throne room where [[Ayxas]] awaited rebels.

## Role as Eidolon

### Role in Correspondence Network

### 392 AC - Secure Communications Intermediary

In December 392 AC, [[Waru Toowoom]] relied on Eskheret as one of his intermediaries for transmitting sensitive correspondence. As it became increasingly difficult to send dispatches through [[The Talaria]], [[Waru Toowoom]] took every precaution to ensure his letters were not intercepted.

Eskheret served alongside [[Mack]] as a trusted channel for secure communications, helping maintain the covert correspondence network between [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] during the expedition's operations in [[Caer Oorun]].

[[Waru Toowoom]] expressed uncertainty about how long these subterfuges would hold, indicating the communications network faced increasing surveillance or operational challenges.
