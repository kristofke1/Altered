# Heimdall

## Overview

Heimdall is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] representing the Norse guardian of the Bifrost rainbow bridge. He wields [[Gjallarhorn]], an instrument with multiple horns that plays four notes and generates powerful sonic vibrations imbued with rainbow-colored energy. Heimdall emanates vivid light and inner fire, capable of destroying stone barriers through sound waves.

## Appearance

Heimdall appears sparkly, gleaming, and colorful with all the power of the most vivid light emanating from him. He possesses inner fire similar to [[Ignescence]], radiating brilliant illumination. His chest and cheeks puff out when preparing to blow [[Gjallarhorn]], and his eyebrows furrow during focused performance. Heimdall maintains composure and focus, able to resist distractions during critical moments.

## Gjallarhorn

[[Gjallarhorn]] is Heimdall's signature instrument featuring multiple horns that play four notes simultaneously. The horn generates:
- Waves of pink and blue energy
- Vibrations that transform into ever-stronger sound
- Saturated hues that fill entire areas
- Colorful filaments resembling cotton-candy or fountains of color
- Rainbow effects when energy mingles with sunlight

The sound swells into powerful vibrations capable of exploding rock and collapsing stone walls, creating openings where none existed before.

## Summoning at Storvhit

### 393 AC - Cave Escape

[[Auraq]] summoned Heimdall while trapped in dead-end ice cave at [[Storvhit]]. [[Auraq]]'s [[Ignescence]] called to him and he replied quickly. [[Auraq]] smiled at Heimdall, encouraging him to blow harder into [[Gjallarhorn]]. Their eyes met and Heimdall understood what [[Auraq]] wanted.

Heimdall's chest puffed out, then his cheeks, and his eyebrows furrowed as sound swelled from [[Gjallarhorn]]. The waves emitted from the horn filled with range of saturated hues, getting into every corner of the cave. [[Auraq]] performed little dance steps at Heimdall's feet, brushing him with hands, but Heimdall remained focused and resisted distraction.

The sound grew stronger and the rock exploded, collapsing the stone wall. Sunlight finally mingled with colorful filaments of the rainbow. [[Auraq]] remained there for long time enjoying scene of hypnotic colors, wishing [[Fen]] had been present to appreciate the spectacle.

## Personality

Heimdall demonstrates focus and dedication to his purpose. He understands intent through eye contact and nonverbal communication. Despite his spectacular and colorful nature, he maintains discipline and cannot be distracted from tasks, even when performers attempt to engage him playfully. He knows how to resist charm and remain concentrated on objectives.
