# Heimdall

## Overview

Heimdall is an [[Eidolon]] representing the Norse guardian of the Bifrost rainbow bridge. He wields [[Gjallarhorn]], an instrument with multiple horns that plays four notes and generates powerful sonic vibrations imbued with rainbow-colored energy. Heimdall emanates vivid light and inner fire, capable of destroying stone barriers through sound waves.

## Appearance

Heimdall appears sparkly, gleaming, and colorful with vivid light emanating from him. He possesses inner fire similar to [[Ignescence]]. His chest and cheeks puff out when preparing to blow [[Gjallarhorn]], and his eyebrows furrow during focused performance.

## Gjallarhorn

[[Gjallarhorn]] is Heimdall's signature instrument featuring multiple horns that play four notes simultaneously. The horn generates waves of pink and blue energy, vibrations that transform into powerful sound, and colorful filaments resembling cotton-candy or fountains of color. When energy mingles with sunlight, it creates rainbow effects. The sound can explode rock and collapse stone walls.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Cave Escape at Storvhit

[[Auraq]] summoned Heimdall while trapped in a dead-end ice cave at [[Storhvit]]. Auraq's [[Ignescence]] called to him and he replied quickly. Heimdall blew [[Gjallarhorn]], filling the cave with saturated hues. The sound grew stronger until the rock exploded and the stone wall collapsed, allowing sunlight to mingle with the colorful filaments. Auraq wished [[Fen]] had been present to appreciate the spectacle.
