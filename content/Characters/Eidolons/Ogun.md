Ogun is an Eidolon master blacksmith who teaches Alteration and metalworking techniques at the Foundry.

## Teaching Methods

Ogun works at a forge and anvil, demonstrating metalworking techniques to students. Ogun places a vevè stamp on pieces worked at the forge. Those who call upon Ogun traditionally kiss a piece of steel.

## Work with Sierra

### 383 AC - Alteration Training

In 383 AC, Sierra worked with Ogun to learn metalworking and Alteration. Sierra used her Construct to raise and lower the temperature of metal while Ogun worked at his forge and anvil. Ogun observed Sierra's work and told her she had a talent for Alteration. This was a pivotal moment that led Sierra to consider becoming an Alterer as a path to join the Expeditionary Corps.


## Cultural Practices

- Uses a vevè stamp on metalwork
- Students kiss a piece of steel when calling upon Ogun
