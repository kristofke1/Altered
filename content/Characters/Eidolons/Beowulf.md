# Beowulf

Beowulf is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoned by [[Sol]], manifesting as the legendary Geatish warrior known as the slayer of Grendel, killer of witches and dragons.

## Appearance and Combat

Beowulf wields a blazing axe and shield. In combat, he bashes his shield with the flat of his axe before setting it ablaze. He can use his shield to send massive projectiles flying and delivers powerful guillotine-like strikes with his weapon.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Battle Against Halua

[[Sol]] summoned Beowulf during combat against [[Halua]]. The Geatish warrior's strike sliced through Halua's skin, leaving a bloody trail, and he slid across the Leviathan's slippery surface to deliver a fatal blow. Before completing the attack, a [[Woollyback]] sheep disrupted the confrontation, and Halua's tail struck Beowulf. The Eidolon blocked the blow but the opportunity to slay the Leviathan was lost.
