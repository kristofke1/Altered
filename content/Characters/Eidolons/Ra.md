# Ra

## Overview

Ra is a solar deity associated with the sun and fire, invoked by members of the [[Bravos]] Faction.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Invocation During Kraken Battle

During [[Auraq]]'s performance retelling the Battle of the [[Kraken]], she described [[Kojo Oduro]] gathering power for his final strike: "As night falls, Kojo shines like a second sun. At this moment, Ra, [[Bela Pennu]], and [[Helios]] have made him the vessel of their wrath!"

## Relationships

- **[[Kojo Oduro]]**: Warrior portrayed as channeling Ra's power (392 AC)
- **[[Helios]]**: Fellow solar deity invoked alongside Ra
- **[[Bela Pennu]]**: Fellow solar deity invoked alongside Ra
- **[[Auraq]]**: Storyteller who described Ra's invocation
