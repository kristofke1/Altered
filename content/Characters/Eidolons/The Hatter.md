# The Hatter

## Overview

The Hatter is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that can be summoned by [[Alterers]]. When summoned, The Hatter appears alongside the Cheshire Cat and March Hare.

## Timeline

### 391 AC - Tea Party and Flamebreaker Upgrade

[[Nevenka]] summoned The Hatter at a tavern in [[Arkaster]], covering the table with desserts and cocktails including a Stargazer for [[Fen]]; The Hatter also created an upgraded Flamebreaker for [[Crowbar]] that caused flames to burst from the scarecrow's eye sockets.

- **[[Fen]]**: [[Lyra]] [[Alterers|Alterer]] present at summoning (391 AC)
