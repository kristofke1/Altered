# Shiva

## Overview

Shiva is a multi-armed [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] bonded to [[Lindiwe]] (and by extension controlled by [[Maw]]). Shiva possesses a destructive third eye on his brow capable of vitrifying targets with a single glance.

## Appearance and Abilities

Shiva manifests with multiple arms that can dance in displays of power and defiance. His most dangerous feature is a third eye on his brow—when opened, it can reduce targets to ash or vitrify them instantly. The eye remains closed unless deliberately forced open.

## The Confrontation with Sylas (393 AC)

When [[Sylas]] confronted [[Maw]] through [[Lindiwe]]'s body, Shiva manifested his many arms in a display of defiance. After Sylas revealed that Lindiwe's psyche was being slowly digested by Maw, Lindiwe briefly seized control of her own body and attempted to force Shiva's third eye open to destroy the warlock. Despite focusing all her will on prying the eyelids apart, [[Maw]] regained control before the eye could open, preventing the attack.
