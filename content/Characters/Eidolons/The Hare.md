# The Hare

## Overview

The Hare is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] known for exceptional speed. Like all [[Eidolons]], it follows predictable patterns dictated by its stories—in The Hare's case, a tendency toward overconfidence that leads it to taunt opponents and stop during races.

## Behavior

The Hare taunts its racing opponents with somersaults, turns, and deliberate stops. This predictable behavior reflects the fable pattern where the Hare's overconfidence becomes its weakness.
