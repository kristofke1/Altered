# Sandman

## Overview

The Sandman is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that manifests in clouds of golden dust and creates ghostly figures from sand and half-formed dreams. It possesses the ability to induce sleep and is summoned to assist storytellers during bedtime rituals.

## Timeline

### 381 AC - Storytime on the Ouroboros

[[Auraq]] summoned the Sandman to help tell the story of [[Chiara Caceres]] and the [[Song of One]] to children in the dormitory aboard [[The Ouroboros]]. While Auraq served as storyteller and narrator, the Sandman acted as director and production designer, creating sand figures depicting palaces including [[The Asterion]], cathedrals of glass and greenery, and battlefields. The children gradually fell asleep as the Sandman's golden dust took effect.

## Relationships

- **[[Auraq]]**: Storyteller who summoned the Sandman for bedtime stories (381 AC)
- **[[Kasirga Clan]]**: Clan whose children the Sandman helped lull to sleep
