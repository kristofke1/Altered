Ganesha is an Elephant Eidolon who specializes in fitting Grafts and mechanical prostheses for injured workers. Despite imposing size, Ganesha works with delicate precision like a goldsmith.

## Appearance

Ganesha has an imposing size as an Elephant Eidolon. His trunk is strong enough to wrap around operation seats and move them aside. He has multiple arms that can extend to help people climb stairs. It's always hard to know what Ganesha is thinking based on his gaze.

## Work with Grafts

### 382 AC - Fitting Prostheses

Despite his imposing size, Ganesha leans delicately over patients, adjusting magnifier goggles. His hands move with a kind of slow precision, like a goldsmith working on a precious stone. He looks at Grafts and mechanical prostheses he has attached to workers, checking that everything looks right and is working properly. He adjusts a few nuts before standing upright, satisfied.

Ganesha smiles upon seeing tears in the eyes of patients, like the young man who had lost his limb in an unfortunate work accident. With his new mechanical arm, the worker can get on with his life, even though he'll need to rebuild it a little. The patient tests his new mechanical arm while Ganesha watches to ensure proper function.

## Working Relationship with Treyst

Treyst worked alongside Ganesha at the Foundry. Treyst's cane clacked against the stairs as he climbed onto the lifting platform to bring Ganesha the prosthesis for the next patient. Each step was painful for Treyst, but he had learned to live with it.

The Eidolon extended one of his arms toward Treyst, helping him climb the final steps. Treyst nodded to him in thanks as new patients arrived. Crafting these augmentations had given Treyst's life new purpose, and he enjoyed helping those in need alongside Ganesha.
