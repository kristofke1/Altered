# Ebenezer Scrooge

Ebenezer Scrooge is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] quartermaster summoned via [[Heka|Glyphs]] to efficiently administer and ration supplies during shortages.

## Physical Appearance

Ebenezer Scrooge appears as a small gentleman, dry and wrinkled as a vine in winter and shriveled like overcooked meat. He wears an ancient, battered top hat over a severe face framed by a bushy beard. He is wrapped in a moth-eaten, threadbare old overcoat that should have been retired years ago. He speaks in a hoarse, creaking voice.

## Abilities and Function

Ebenezer Scrooge is an inflexible old quartermaster who efficiently administers and rations remaining supplies during emergencies. He possesses the keen eye of a buzzard when it comes to optimizing, planning, and determining rations so groups can survive together. Until he loosens the purse strings, those under his management must tighten their belts.

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Supply Crisis at Storvhit

[[Waru Toowoom]] discovered an empty supply crate at [[Storhvit]] camp due to team disorganization, drew [[Heka|Glyphs]] in snow with his cane, and summoned Ebenezer Scrooge to ration remaining supplies.
