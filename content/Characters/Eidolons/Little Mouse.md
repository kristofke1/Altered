# Little Mouse

## Overview

The Little Mouse is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that collects baby teeth, serving as a rival to the [[Tooth Fairy]] in responding to the ritual of humans leaving teeth. It distributes coins to those who leave teeth and buries collected teeth in hidden locations.

## Appearance

The Little Mouse wears a red felt coat with turned-up collar and carries a large bag over its shoulder for collecting teeth.

## Behavior

The Little Mouse digs holes to bury collected baby teeth, carefully placing each tooth before covering the hole and pressing down the dirt with its hind legs. It carries coins to distribute at night to those who have left teeth. The entity is cautious and alert, scanning its surroundings before burying its collection.

## Tooth Collection Competition

When humans leave a tooth somewhere, it functions as a ritual summoning that both the Little Mouse and [[Tooth Fairy]] must answer. This competition has existed for a long time. Each tooth collected grants the collector more power, while stealing a rival's cached teeth weakens them.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Burying Teeth in The Undergrowth

The Little Mouse buried a collection of baby teeth in [[The Undergrowth]], unaware that the [[Tooth Fairy]] was observing from a hidden thicket and planning to steal the cache.

## Relationships

- **[[Tooth Fairy]]**: Rival Eidolon competing for tooth collection
- **[[The Undergrowth]]**: Location where the Little Mouse buried teeth (393 AC)
