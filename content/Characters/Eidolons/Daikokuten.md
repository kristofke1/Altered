# Daikokuten

## Overview

Daikokuten is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] deity of fortune from Japanese tradition. He can be summoned through the fukunusubi tradition, which involves stealing an idol depicting the deity from its owner and breaking it. When manifested, Daikokuten appears carrying a sack, a mallet, and bales of rice.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Summoned by Nevenka

[[Nevenka]] stole a Daikokuten effigy from [[Afanas Korovinovich]] and smashed it to summon the deity, following the fukunusubi tradition she had overheard [[Ordis]] scholars discussing.

## Relationships

- **[[Afanas Korovinovich]]**: [[Yzmir]] Exalt who possessed the Daikokuten effigy before [[Nevenka]] stole it (393 AC)
- **[[Nevenka]]**: [[Lyra]] Exalt who summoned Daikokuten by stealing and smashing his effigy (393 AC)
