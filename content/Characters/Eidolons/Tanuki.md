# Tanuki

Tanuki is a trickster [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] from Japanese folklore, known for mischievous behavior and association with [[Lyra]] performers.

## Abilities

### Teleportation

Tanuki can teleport using a beech leaf, disappearing and reappearing at different locations.

### Performance Role

Tanuki appears in theatrical performances, often cast as comedic villain or troublemaker characters. The Eidolon can be directed to perform specific actions during shows.

## Association with Lyra

Tanuki is one of several trickster [[Eidolons]] sought out by [[Lyra]] members for company and guidance. Along with [[Loki]] and Pan, Tanuki was among the [[Eidolons]] [[Nevenka]] sought for mischievous companionship.

## Timeline

### 385 AC - Cloth Cocoon Performance

During [[Ehuang]]'s aerial ribbons act, [[Auraq]] summoned Tanuki as the villain threatening scissors, which [[Ehuang]] wrapped in a silk cocoon as part of the performance.

### 388 AC - Dorayaki Stage Performance

[[Auraq]] summoned Tanuki with dorayaki (sweet treat) from her low-cut top during a performance. Tanuki teleported using a beech leaf to catch the flying treat.

### ~393 AC - Geyser Hot Springs

Tanuki was observed squabbling with a [[Magpengs|Magpeng]] over who would next use the geyser launch pad at the hot springs created by [[Atsadi]] at [[Storvhit]].

## Relationships

- **[[Auraq]]**: [[Lyra]] performer who summons Tanuki for theatrical performances (385 AC, 388 AC)
- **[[Nevenka]]**: [[Lyra]] who sought Tanuki along with other trickster [[Eidolons]]
- **[[Loki]]**: Fellow trickster Eidolon associated with Nevenka
- **[[Ehuang]]**: Aerial performer who wrapped Tanuki in silk cocoon during act (385 AC)
