# White Rabbit

White Rabbit is Eidolon associated with time whom Akesha Adhikari attempted to summon to steal temporal power.

## Association with Time

White Rabbit possesses watch that represents or controls temporal flow. Akesha theorized that if managed to summon White Rabbit and steal watch maybe could steal time itself.

This connection to temporal manipulation made White Rabbit target for mages seeking to extend available time or alter temporal constraints.

## Connection to Alice

White Rabbit exists within same narrative framework as Alice. Both originate from realm of imagination connected to Wonderland mythology. Alice showed Akesha "how far down rabbit hole went" suggesting shared thematic space.

## Akesha's Quest

### 387 AC - Attempted Summoning

Aether fragment pulsed against Akesha's chest making it easier for ideas to cross over from Empyrean. Used it to summon ideas from collective unconscious.

Training wasn't easy. Ever since Alice had shown how far down rabbit hole went every day had been full of new discoveries and surprises. World showed colors never sensed before.

Akesha concentrated feeling flow of ideas rush in. Didn't have time to be mesmerized - Kumari counting on her. Had no choice but to dig deeper and needed to be quick because sister's condition could get worse with each passing day.

If managed to summon White Rabbit and steal his watch maybe could steal time itself? Time was what didn't have but desperately needed.

Set trap and kept watch. May not get second chance in race against time.
