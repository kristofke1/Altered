# Arawn

## Overview

Arawn is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who presides over the Wild Hunt. He rides a deer with huge antlers and is followed by a pack of [[Cŵn Annwn]], spectral hounds with fearsome reputation that resemble corgis. He has jade eyes and a cold, severe demeanor.

## Appearance

Arawn rides a stag mount with large antlers and glowing hooves that pound the ground with thunderous force. His jade eyes carry a cold, severe look, and his silence demands answers from those who summon him.

## Role in Hunting

Traditionally, [[Hellequin]] Hunters call upon Arawn to hunt down wrongdoers. During the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], [[Teija]] adapted this practice to track missing expedition members. When given scent from personal belongings, Arawn and his pack can track targets across vast distances.

## The Cŵn Annwn

Arawn's pack consists of the Cŵn Annwn, spectral hounds with a terrifying reputation despite their corgi-like appearance. The hounds are known to occasionally bite those nearby, even allies.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Search for Missing Expedition

[[Teija]] summoned Arawn to track a missing expedition that had not reported for two days and was supposed to join the [[Mesektet]] at the entrance to the [[City of Scholars|Scholars' Vault]].
