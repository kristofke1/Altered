# Man in the Maze

## Overview

The Man in the Maze, also known as I'itoi, is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] based on the Tohono O'odham symbol representing the journey through life. He stands at the entrance to mazes and serves as an elder brother who teaches and protects. Each choice in life is a turning point leading toward the center; those who seek his counsel do so when their choices carry weight.

## Summoning History

### 393 AC, June-September - Counsel at City of Scholars
[[Auraq]] consulted the Man in the Maze at entrance to [[The Maze]] in [[City of Scholars]], seeking guidance about whether to support or redirect [[Fen]]'s quest for [[The Song of One]].
