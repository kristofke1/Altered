# Swift Jinn

## Overview

Swift Jinn is a fire elemental [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that manifests as a streak of fire and metal. It specializes in aerial attacks and explosive light-based destruction, capable of dispersing shadow entities through detonation.

## Appearance

Swift Jinn descends from above like a bird of prey, appearing as a streak of fire and metal. Its mouth contains steel fangs and blue flames, and it emits wordless shrieks during attack.

## Abilities

Swift Jinn's primary ability is explosive detonation that generates waves of bright light. This light flows into the smallest nooks and fissures of enclosed spaces, dispersing shadow-based entities upon contact.

## Summoning History

### 393 AC, June-September - Shadow Dispersal in City of Scholars

[[Atsadi]] summoned Swift Jinn during combat against shadow entities in a cavern within the [[City of Scholars]]. When conventional sword techniques proved ineffective against the roving shadows, Atsadi positioned himself near a well surrounded by engraved stones and summoned the Jinn as an ambush. Swift Jinn dropped from the ceiling and detonated, releasing a wave of light that caused the shadows to unravel, sizzle, and disperse. Atsadi absorbed the resulting [[Mana]] fog to replenish his power.
