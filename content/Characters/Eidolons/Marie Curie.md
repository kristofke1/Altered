# Marie Curie

## Overview

Marie Curie is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] scientist based on the historical figure who exposed the radioactive properties of certain substances. She assists with scientific research, methodically recording data on resonance, vibration, and friction.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Aerolith Research with Isaree

Marie Curie assisted [[Isaree Thongsuk]] with secret research on [[Aerolith]]'s electromagnetic and radioactive properties, recording data while Isaree was occupied with other duties.
