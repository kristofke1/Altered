# Valemon

Valemon is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] known as the Bear King, an old white bear with immense strength capable of breaking through ice barriers.

## Physical Appearance

Valemon appears as an old white bear with front paws extended. He can materialize within translucent ice walls to break through from within.

## Abilities and Function

Valemon possesses immense physical strength sufficient to twist and deform thick ice barriers through sheer force of will. When summoned via [[Heka|Glyphs]], Valemon can cause ice to sublimate in a sudden cloud of steam, opening passages through frozen obstacles.

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Ice Rescue at Storvhit

[[Waru Toowoom]] summoned Valemon during glacier cave exploration at [[Storvhit]] after hearing calls for help behind thick ice barrier, and the Bear King broke through the translucent wall by twisting and deforming ice until it sublimated in a cloud of steam.
