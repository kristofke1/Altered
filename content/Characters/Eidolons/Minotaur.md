# Minotaur

## Overview

The Minotaur is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoned by [[Teija]]. Unlike the aggressive creature of mythology, this Minotaur is calm and meditative, often resting beneath tree foliage.

## Mythological Connection

[[Teija]] discussed the Minotaur's mythological origins with historian [[Abiram Okech]], noting parallels between ancient myths and Asgarthan history. In mythology, Asterion was both an ancient king married to Europa and the name given to the Minotaur born from Pasiphaë and a white bull sent by Poseidon. [[Asgartha]]'s government building [[The Asterion]] shares this name.

## Symbolic Significance

The Minotaur represents the hybrid nature of civilization—tangible creatures shaped by ideas, a marriage between the realms of the imaginary and the real, [[Oneiros]] and mortals. If legends are true, an age once existed when imaginary characters and humans could unite in this way.
