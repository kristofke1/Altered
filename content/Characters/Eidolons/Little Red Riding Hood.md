# Little Red Riding Hood

## Overview

Little Red Riding Hood is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] bonded to [[Sol]]. She answered Sol's call during her [[Imhallat]]. Little Red Riding Hood was taken under [[The Huntsman]]'s wing before becoming Sol's Alter Ego.

## Relationships

- **[[Sol]]**: [[Exalts|Exalt]] whose [[Imhallat]] Little Red Riding Hood answered
- **[[The Huntsman]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who mentored Little Red Riding Hood
- **[[Imhallat]]**: [[Bravos]] ceremony during which Little Red Riding Hood bonded with [[Sol]]
