# Bessie Coleman

## Overview

Bessie Coleman is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] aviator who trains [[Axiom]] cadets in aerial combat maneuvers and evasion techniques for encounters with [[Leviathans]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Cadet Combat Training

Bessie Coleman performed aerobatic maneuvers including loop-the-loops, barrel rolls, split S turns, stall turns, chandelles, and Immelmann turns to demonstrate evasion tactics against [[Leviathans]] such as [[Lucan]] and [[Halua]], then executed a missile strike on a floating fragment before landing.

## Relationships

- **[[Treyst]]**: [[Axiom]] Exalt who observed Bessie's training demonstration (393 AC)
