# Drowsy Dodo

## Overview

Drowsy Dodo is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] resembling a large, colorful bird that leans on a cane and perpetually drifts in and out of sleep. In its half-sleeping state, it recites cryptic game rules in a croaky voice.

## Appearance

Drowsy Dodo appears as a large ball of colorful feathers with a hole in its beak from which sleep bubbles emerge. It leans on a cane resembling a twisted mast, barely staying upright as it nods off. Its plumage is thick enough for a small creature to nestle into like a quilted blanket.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Siesta with Spike

[[Arjun]] discovered Drowsy Dodo and [[Spike]] napping together on a secluded islet, with Spike wrapped in the Eidolon's plumage while it recited cryptic game rules in its sleep.
