# Kikimora

Kikimora is an Eidolon who performs cleaning and maintenance duties in the Sanctum, particularly in dusty and rarely visited areas of the sprawling library.

## Role in the Sanctum

Kikimora sweeps areas in the bowels of the Sanctum that have not been visited for years. The Eidolon maintains shelving areas that become progressively dustier the further one descends into the library's depths.

The Eidolon works in sections where books are more obscure and obviously less used. These areas contain older, less frequently accessed materials stored in the Sanctum's lower levels.

## Observed Activity

### 367 AC - Sweeping Restricted Area Approach

Waru and archivist walked through Sanctum's maze of shelving, then headed down spiral staircase descending into bowels of sprawling library. Further they walked, dustier shelves became, and books held were more obscure and obviously less used.

As Waru turned head, he saw Eidolon of Kikimora sweeping area that probably hadn't been visited for years. The archivist continued leading Waru toward gate preventing access to restricted area where Sanctum's most dangerous books are locked away.
