# Issitoq

Issitoq is a judge Eidolon who floats above court attendees and delivers verdicts. He is not known as a lenient judge.

## Appearance

Issitoq floats above attendees like an inquisitive eye. His eyes light up and emit blue rays that illuminate defendants from head to toe. When delivering judgments, his voice booms out like roar of thunder or rumbling of avalanche. His eyes close after verdict is announced, and like an eyeball, he turns to face court entrance before fading away.

## Judicial Process

Issitoq listens to cases put forward by defense and prosecutors before delivering judgment. He examines defendants with unrelenting stare. His blue rays scan accused individuals completely before verdict is announced.

His booming voice delivers sentences including removal from office, expulsion periods, and other punishments. After announcing punishment, his eyes close and he rotates toward entrance as if turning away from completed proceedings. He slowly fades away once judgment has been delivered.

## Reputation

Issitoq is not known as lenient judge. His harsh reputation serves judicial system well. Defendants place knees on ground when listening to verdicts.

## Judging Waru

### 365 AC - Removal and Expulsion Verdict

Judge turned and looked at Waru with unrelenting stare. Issitoq floated above attendees like inquisitive eye. He had listened to cases put forward by defense and Libra prosecutor.

His eyes lit up and blue rays illuminated Waru from head to toe. His voice boomed out like roar of thunder, like rumbling of avalanche. Waru placed knee on ground as he listened to verdict: immediate removal and expulsion for minimum of three years.

Behind Waru, his supporters protested and shouted this was disgrace. Issitoq's eyes closed and like eyeball, Eidolon turned to face entrance to Court. Having delivered judgment and announced punishment, he slowly faded away.

Waru silently rejoiced though he couldn't show it. By his sacrifice, he had given Avkan complete freedom to approve launch of Rediscovery Endeavor. Through scheming, he had silenced protest. Position he was vacating would be fought over, and resulting succession struggle would prevent hopefuls from effectively moving against Basileus.

Waru stood up and moved toward exit with head held high, feigning contrition while maintaining pride.
