# Santa Claus

## Overview

Santa Claus is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] who operates a flying sleigh pulled by reindeer for deliveries. He was considered as a candidate for [[Storvhit]] patronage following [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation but was deemed too human-centered for the role.

## Appearance

Santa Claus appears as a big, burly man with a long white beard. He wears a gold chain, sunglasses, and carries himself with the demeanor of a biker.

## The Sleigh

Santa Claus possesses a distinctive bright red sleigh with aerodynamic design streamlined like a race car. Features include red velvet quilted cushions, solid-gold handlebars and runners, and a finely sculpted figurehead. The sleigh can be operated without reindeer by bringing the figurehead to life through [[Alteration]]. The vehicle is pulled by a team of reindeer when Santa Claus operates it himself.

## Abilities

Santa Claus can climb into the sky and disappear into the clouds with his sleigh and reindeer team, moving unseen and unnoticed. He typically works one day a year for deliveries.

## Storvhit Patronage Consideration - 393 AC

Following [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation from [[The Nilam]], various [[Oneiros]] candidates were evaluated to replace the dragon as patronage for [[Storvhit]]'s arctic climate. Santa Claus was among those considered alongside Snow Queen, Baron Kelvin, and Dong Da Shen. All four were ruled out as being too human-centered for the biome survival role, which was ultimately given to [[Jack Frost]].

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Encounter with Nevenka at Talaria Warehouse

Santa Claus encountered [[Nevenka]] leaving [[The Talaria]] warehouse with stolen presents, spraying her with snow before gesturing for her to load the packages into his sleigh, then departed with his reindeer team into the clouds.
