# Melusine

## Overview

Melusine is a fairy [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] associated with water manipulation and retribution against those who pry into hidden affairs. She can transform floating water spheres into dragons under her command and attacks with lances of superheated liquid.

## Abilities

Melusine dances on the surface of floating water spheres and can burst them to form draconic creatures that pursue her targets. She wields boiling water as a projectile weapon, launching hissing jets capable of severing paper constructs on contact.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Ambush on Zhen

Melusine attacked [[Zhen]] with boiling water projectiles while the Rear Admiral scouted between floating water spheres, destroying one of Zhen's paper wings and pursuing her with conjured water dragons until Zhen escaped using a doppelgänger decoy.
