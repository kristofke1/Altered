# Eris

## Overview

Eris is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] goddess of discord from Greek tradition. She manifests with wings and a charming smile. When summoned as a gambling partner, Eris demonstrates exceptional luck, winning entire pots — though her presence invariably provokes arguments and near-brawls among the other players.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Gambling on Cloud Realm Island

[[Nevenka]] summoned Eris as her partner for a dice game organized on a [[Cloud Realm]] flying island, and the goddess won the entire pot in a few throws before the pair split the winnings while other players descended into argument.
