# Tāne

## Overview

Tāne is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] with the ability to restore devastated ecosystems through touch, causing color to return to lifeless bark, grass to shoot up in concentric waves, and branches to flower as if experiencing a new spring. Tāne can summon bird flocks through [[The Skein]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Restoring Aerolith Explosion Aftermath

Tāne walked among bent and bare tree trunks devastated by an [[Axiom]]-[[Bravos]] [[Aerolith]] explosion experiment, restoring the biome by caressing trees until color returned to bark and leaves grew, grass shot up around its feet, branches flowered, and summoned bird flocks alighted on the renewed foliage — witnessed by [[Teija]], who bowed to the Eidolon.

## Relationships

- **[[Teija]]**: [[Muna]] Exalt who witnessed and bowed to Tāne during restoration (393 AC)
