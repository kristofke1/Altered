# Inari

## Overview

Inari is a nature deity revered by the [[Muna]] Faction. Stylized statues of Inari can be found within the [[Bark Refuge]] sanctuary inside [[The Spindle|the Spindle Tree]], where offerings of flower garlands and trinkets are placed.

The [[Muna]] honor Inari alongside other nature deities including [[Cernunnos]], [[Freyja]], [[Yong-Su Tenzing|Yong-Su]], and [[Tlaloc]].

## Relationships

- **[[Muna]]**: Faction that worships Inari
- **[[The Spindle]]**: Location where Inari statues are found
- **[[Bark Refuge]]**: Sanctuary containing Inari statues
- **[[Cernunnos]]**, **[[Freyja]]**, **[[Yong-Su Tenzing|Yong-Su]]**, **[[Tlaloc]]**: Fellow nature deities honored by Muna
