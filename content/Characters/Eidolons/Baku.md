# Baku

Baku is an Eidolon at [[The Cloister]] with trunk who treats Remanence affliction by sucking up and digesting ideas flowing from patients' bodies. Relief provided is temporary.

## Remanence Treatment

### 385 AC - Treating Young Initiate

[[Kumari Adhikari]] had been bedridden for months. Crisis had passed but left her exhausted. She suffered from Remanence affliction that overwhelms those who flirt too much with imaginary. Her young age made case unusual, almost tragic. Little chance she would pull through.

She leaked and regurgitated ideas with no ability to control herself - Yzmir curse. Floating above herl, Baku continued using trunk to suck up stream of ideas flowing endlessly from body.

[[Lindiwe]] observed sensing if necessary would take over if Baku forced to leave leftovers.
