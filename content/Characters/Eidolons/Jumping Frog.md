# Jumping Frog

## Overview

The Jumping Frog is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] used for training and studying movement techniques.

## Characteristics

The Jumping Frog demonstrates seemingly chaotic jumps with sudden leaps, exceptional spring, and the ability to use its feet to stick to walls. These qualities make it useful for teaching concepts like reflexes and unpredictable movement.

## Relationships

- **[[Kaw]]**: [[Altrun]] champion who uses the frog to teach movement concepts to [[Kojo Oduro]] (393 AC)
- **[[Kojo Oduro]]**: [[Bravos]] Exalt who studies the frog's movement to improve his techniques (393 AC)
