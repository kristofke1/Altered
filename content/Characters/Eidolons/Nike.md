# Nike

## Overview

Nike is a goddess [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] associated with victory. She manifests with wings and a blazing flame, appearing comet-like when summoned through [[Heka]] verse. Her radiance is intense enough to capture the attention of [[Leviathans]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Diverting Sagitta

[[Zhen]] summoned Nike via [[Heka]] during an encounter with the [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] [[Sagitta]], and the goddess's brilliant flame drew the creature into pursuit away from endangered [[Expeditionary Corps]] transport barges.

## Relationships

- **[[Zhen]]**: [[Ordis]] Exalt who summoned Nike through [[Heka]] (393 AC)
- **[[Sagitta]]**: [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] diverted by Nike's radiance (393 AC)
