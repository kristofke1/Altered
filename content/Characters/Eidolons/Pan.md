# Pan

Pan is a trickster [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] known for irreverent behavior, associated with [[Lyra]] members.

## Association with Lyra

Pan was among the [[Eidolons]] [[Nevenka]] sought for companionship, valued for his irreverent side. Along with [[Loki]] and [[Tanuki]], Pan formed part of a gang of trickster Eidolons that provided Nevenka with company when other children avoided her.

## Appearance in Kauri's Dream

Pan appeared in one of [[Kauri]]'s dreamlike states, playing leapfrog and causing chaos among the [[Woollyback]] flock at [[The Screed]]. [[Eru]] was also present in the dream. Pan smiled at Kauri as though his antics were intended to provoke a realization, after which Kauri committed to the [[The Musubi|Musubi]] path.

## Relationships

- **[[Nevenka]]**: [[Lyra]] follower who sought Pan's irreverent companionship
- **[[Loki]]**: Fellow trickster Eidolon in Nevenka's gang
- **[[Tanuki]]**: Fellow trickster Eidolon in Nevenka's gang
- **[[Kauri]]**: [[Muna]] Exalt in whose dream Pan appeared, catalyzing his commitment to [[The Musubi]]
- **[[Eru]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] present alongside Pan during Kauri's dream
