# The Tortoise

## Overview

The Tortoise is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] representing methodical patience and strategic deliberation. He appears with clawed feet resting on the pommel of a cane and a friendly smile, moving slowly and deliberately.

## Summoning History

### 393 AC - Alelo Hunt Strategy

[[Moyo Chibuye]] summoned the Tortoise during the [[Bravos]] hunt of [[Alelo]] to impose patience and organize a tactical plan of action before intervening.

## Relationships

- **[[Moyo Chibuye]]**: [[Yzmir]] Exalt who summoned the Tortoise for strategic patience during [[Alelo]] hunt (393 AC)
- **[[Alelo]]**: [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] whose hunt prompted the summoning
