# Achilles

## Overview

Achilles is an [[Eidolon]] representing the legendary Greek hero and warrior, renowned for his martial prowess during the Trojan War. He can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those familiar with his legend.

## Timeline

### 382 AC - Duel with Atsadi

[[Atsadi]] summoned Achilles through [[Alteration]] to test his [[Galisgi]] fighting style. The duel took place under [[Bedivere]]'s watchful eye. Achilles fought with his lance, using its reach advantage against Atsadi, and at one point kicked him in the ribs. Through hard work and enduring failures, [[Atsadi]] eventually triumphed over Achilles, one of several legendary warriors he defeated in his quest to become worthy of wielding [[Excalibur]].

## Relationships

- **[[Atsadi]]**: Summoner who defeated Achilles in single combat (382 AC)
- **[[Bedivere]]**: Eidolon present as observer during Atsadi's duel with Achilles (382 AC)
- **[[Excalibur]]**: Mythical sword Atsadi seeks, requiring him to defeat legendary warriors
