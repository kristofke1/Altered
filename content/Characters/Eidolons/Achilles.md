# Achilles

## Overview
Achilles is an [[Eidolon]] representing the legendary Greek hero and warrior. He was defeated in single combat by [[Atsadi]], the One-Armed Swordsman, as part of his quest to prove himself the world's greatest swordsman and become worthy of wielding [[Excalibur]].

## Encounter with Atsadi
[[Atsadi]] summoned Achilles through [[Alteration]] to test his [[Galisgi]] fighting style against one of history's most renowned warriors. The Greek hero's legendary combat prowess made him a formidable opponent for testing [[Atsadi]]'s one-armed sword technique.

Through enduring failures and setbacks, and ultimately through hard work, [[Atsadi]] managed to triumph over this legendary opponent.

## Significance
Defeating Achilles represents a significant achievement in [[Atsadi]]'s quest. The Greek hero's reputation as one of the greatest warriors in Western mythology made victory over his [[Eidolon]] form a crucial demonstration of [[Atsadi]]'s mastery.

The battle proved that [[Atsadi]]'s [[Galisgi]] style - developed to compensate for his missing right arm - could overcome even the most celebrated warriors from classical legend.

## Nature as Eidolon
As an [[Eidolon]], Achilles can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those familiar with his legend. [[Atsadi]]'s success in materializing and defeating him demonstrates both his knowledge of legendary warriors and his ability to test himself against their combat techniques.

## Historical Legend
Achilles represents the hero of Greek mythology, renowned for his martial prowess during the Trojan War. In the Altered universe, he exists as an [[Eidolon]] whose fighting skills can be invoked and challenged by those seeking to prove their own martial excellence.
