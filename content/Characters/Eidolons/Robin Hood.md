# Robin Hood

Robin Hood is an Eidolon who serves as Isura financier. In old days, he was known as great thief who stole from rich to give to poor. He now holds position enabling him to ensure proper distribution of wealth through banking system.

## Background

Robin Hood's past involved highway robbery. Legends said he was great thief who stole from rich to give to poor. His past as outlaw became known enough that references to it can be used to provoke him. He managed to elevate his status among people despite criminal background.

## Current Role

Robin Hood became one of Isura's financiers. His position as banker allows him to ensure proper distribution of wealth through official channels rather than through theft. The transformation from outlaw to financier represents significant status change.

## Negotiations

### 364 AC - Meeting with Waru

Waru and Robin Hood held deep discussion in secluded alcove of breivikensveg tavern bar. Two hooded strangers and Mack the weasel in backroom drew little attention. Mack sipped beer and kept watch while listening through Waru's ears to negotiation.

Discussion covered how Rediscovery Endeavor would delay development of Protectorate's secondary cities in dire need of it. Robin Hood characterized endeavor as unnecessary risk and losing investment. Waru taunted Robin about his past as outlaw and how he managed to elevate status among people. Arguments were designed to stir up anger, which appeared to be working.

Mack noticed pair of eyes watching from counter - probably spy on Avkan's payroll. This indicated second phase of plan was bearing fruit. Waru was warned of spy's presence. Everything seemed to be running smoothly.
