Joseph Bologne, Chevalier de Saint-Georges, is an Eidolon swordmaster and one of the Seven Blades, the legendary warriors of the Lost Tribe who protected the rear guard during the Great Wandering. Joseph Bologne was a French composer and champion fencer whose name was lost to history for centuries until rediscovered by Atsadi.

## Historical Identity

Joseph Bologne, Chevalier de Saint-Georges, was a French composer and virtuoso violinist who also achieved renown as a champion fencer. The historical figure's exceptional skill with the sword made him worthy of inclusion among the Seven Blades, the mythical protectors who covered the advance of the seven tribes toward what would become Asgartha.

Bologne's combination of musical genius and martial mastery distinguished him as one of the illustrious sword fighters selected to protect the Lost Tribe. His title "Chevalier de Saint-Georges" reflected his aristocratic status and accomplishments.

Like the other members of the Seven Blades, Joseph Bologne's individual name was forgotten over the centuries following the Great Wandering. Only the collective title "The Seven Blades" survived in legends of the first Nomads.

## Rediscovery by Atsadi

### 392 AC - Training Target

Atsadi successfully identified Joseph Bologne as one of the Seven Blades through research and investigation. By 392 AC, the name "Bologne" had become part of a mantra in Atsadi's mind alongside the two other discovered names: Dei Liberi and d'Aubigny.

Atsadi worked intensively to learn enough about Joseph Bologne to materialize the Eidolon through Alteration. The goal was to challenge and defeat Bologne using the Galisgi style, as part of Atsadi's quest to prove himself worthy of wielding Excalibur.

During training sessions, Atsadi attempted to manifest Joseph Bologne while sparring with Surge. However, successfully materializing the Eidolon required substantial knowledge about the historical figure, making the process difficult and requiring continued study.

## Role in Atsadi's Quest

As one of the Seven Blades, Joseph Bologne represents one of the ultimate tests for Atsadi. The One-Armed Swordsman must:
1. Learn sufficient historical details to successfully materialize Joseph Bologne as an Eidolon
2. Challenge the legendary Chevalier de Saint-Georges in combat
3. Defeat him using the Galisgi style

Defeating all Seven Blades, including Joseph Bologne, would demonstrate Atsadi's mastery of the sword and prove him worthy to be considered the greatest swordsman in the world—the only one who can draw Excalibur from its sheath.

### 393 AC - Named as Target

After [[Atsadi]] defeated [[Fiore dei Liberi]] in the [[City of Scholars]], Fiore revealed Joseph Bologne's name as one of Atsadi's next targets among [[The Seven Blades]].

## Significance

Joseph Bologne's connection to the Lost Tribe and the Great Wandering makes him a symbol of sacrifice, duty, and martial excellence. His dual nature as both composer and champion fencer adds additional significance to his legend. By identifying and challenging Bologne, Atsadi honors the memory of the Lost Tribe's protectors while seeking to prove his own worthiness to join their legendary ranks.
