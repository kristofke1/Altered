# Joseph Bologne

## Overview

Joseph Bologne, Chevalier de Saint-Georges, is an [[Eidolon]] swordmaster and one of [[The Seven Blades]]—the legendary warriors of [[The Lost Tribe]] who protected the rear guard during [[The Great Wandering]]. He was a French composer and champion fencer whose individual name was lost to history for centuries until rediscovered by [[Atsadi]].

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Identified by Atsadi

[[Atsadi]] successfully identified Joseph Bologne as one of [[The Seven Blades]] through research and investigation. By 392 AC, the name "Bologne" had become part of a mantra in Atsadi's mind alongside the two other discovered names: Dei Liberi and d'Aubigny. Atsadi worked to learn enough about him to materialize the [[Eidolon]] through [[Alteration]] and challenge him using the [[Galisgi]] style.

### 393 AC - Named as Target

After [[Atsadi]] defeated [[Fiore dei Liberi]] in the [[City of Scholars]], Fiore revealed Joseph Bologne's name as one of Atsadi's next targets among [[The Seven Blades]].
