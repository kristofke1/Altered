# Vishvakarma

Vishvakarma is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] associated with the [[Axiom]] who specializes in vehicle repair and mechanical work. Based on the Hindu deity of architecture and engineering, Vishvakarma manifests with four robotic arms and demonstrates expertise in armored vehicle restoration.

## Appearance

Vishvakarma manifests with oversized robotic arms, possessing two pairs (four arms total). He sits cross-legged while working and displays a thin smile during repairs. His tools appear alongside him when summoned.

## Abilities

### Vehicle Repair

Vishvakarma excels at repairing armored vehicles and heavy machinery. He can summon assembly plans and scan them while working, using his four arms to perform complex repairs efficiently. His manifestation requires more [[Kelon]] than ordinary repairs but significantly reduces time needed.

### Equipment Fabrication

Vishvakarma can fashion additional equipment for vehicles, such as front-mounted snowplows for navigating arctic terrain.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Reprocessor Repair at Storvhit

[[Sierra]] summoned Vishvakarma at [[Storhvit]] to repair a damaged Reprocessor armored vehicle after ice stalactites and scree fell upon it while passing under an icy ledge, with the deity sitting cross-legged as his tools appeared and he scanned assembly plans before working in a shower of sparks.

### 393 AC - Snowplow Fabrication

Vishvakarma fashioned a front snowplow for the Reprocessor to help the armored vehicle navigate [[Storhvit]]'s snowy slopes.

- **[[Sierra]]**: [[Axiom]] Exalt who summons Vishvakarma for vehicle repairs

- **[[The Foundry]]**: Axiom facility where vehicle repair expertise is valued
