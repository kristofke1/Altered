# Pamola

Pamola is [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] whose domain encompasses cold and mountainous areas.

## Appearance

Pamola has an athletic human body with powerful elk-like antlers on his head, immense wings on his back, and eagle's claws for feet. The Eidolon appears ghostly and imposing, crouching like a bird of prey on rocky outcrops.

## Behavior

Pamola perches on mountain outcrops observing those who traverse his domain. When approached, the Eidolon stands tall and flaps immense wings with a crack before tearing through snow and freezing rain. Pamola extends his hand toward climbers, though whether to help or capture remains ambiguous.

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Encounter with Lindiwe at Cais Adarra

During [[Lindiwe]]'s ascent of a rock face at [[Cais Adarra]], Pamola appeared on an outcrop as a ghostly imposing silhouette during a blizzard and extended his hand toward her.
