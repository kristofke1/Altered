# Wanjiru

## Overview

Wanjiru was a [[Yzmir]] Horomancer who became a legendary hero during [[The Severance War]] in the early 3rd century AC. After leaving a profound mark on history as both a war hero and founder of [[The Qorgan]], he achieved the status of [[Eidolons|Eidolon]]/[[Oneiros]] upon death, allowing continued existence and influence.

## Background

At the outbreak of [[The Severance War]], Wanjiru was a young Horomancer stationed in [[Caer Arcolano]]. Despite [[Yzmir]]'s declaration of neutrality, he pledged allegiance to [[Telmo]], the Governor of Caer Arcolano. As the war worsened, Wanjiru secretly investigated, sensing hidden manipulation behind events. Through patient observation he uncovered [[The Isfet]], a chaos sect orchestrating the conflict.

Despite his status as an apostate for breaking [[Yzmir]] neutrality, Wanjiru alerted the [[Yzmir]] Magisters about the conspiracy. The Magisters intervened decisively, halting advancing armies while Wanjiru led strike teams against identified agitators. High-ranking officials in both loyalist and rebel factions were exposed as conspirators.

Celebrated as a hero after the war's end, Wanjiru remained uneasy, believing [[The Isfet]] had been backed by unknown outside forces. He founded [[The Qorgan]], a [[Yzmir]] cell specialized in tracking and eliminating threats to [[Asgartha]].

## Timeline

### Early 3rd Century AC - Severance War

Wanjiru uncovered [[The Isfet]] conspiracy during [[The Severance War]], led strike teams against agitators, and became celebrated as a hero of the reconciliation.

### Early 3rd Century AC - Qorgan Founded

Wanjiru founded [[The Qorgan]] to protect [[Asgartha]] from hidden threats, believing [[The Isfet]] had been backed by outside forces whose identity remained unknown.

### 392 AC - Suha Recruitment

Wanjiru arranged for [[Yanna]] to observe [[Suha]]'s investigation into the [[Kraken]] as a test, then revealed himself in [[The Magisterium]] and recruited Suha into [[The Qorgan]] after she concluded the Kraken was an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] rather than [[Leviathans|Leviathan]].

### 392 AC, October - Cebir Intervention

Wanjiru intervened at [[Cebir]] fortress in [[Caer Oorun]], after which [[Yzmir]] locked down the site citing quarantine to seize control from [[Bravos]]—complicating Admiral [[Temera Singh]]'s directive to reclaim Cebir for western coastal exploration.

### 393 AC, May - Afanas Recruitment at Cais Adarra

Wanjiru materialized at [[Cais Adarra]] with incarnation vessel [[Suha]] immediately after [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation, demonstrating [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]]'s advanced technique of creating [[Eidolons]] completely undetectable even to [[Irises]].

### 393 AC, May - Perjurers Theory Revealed

Wanjiru revealed to [[Afanas Korovinovich]] his 150-year investigation into [[The Perjurers]]—a secret organization working since [[Amahle Kalu|Kalu's Revolt]] to destabilize [[Asgartha]] through infiltrators in every Faction, with the [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]] embedded in [[Yzmir]].

### 393 AC, May - Kraken Theory Proposed

Wanjiru proposed that the [[Kraken]] was an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoning rather than [[Leviathans|Leviathan]], requiring immense [[Mana]] but "not entirely impossible," designed to keep [[Asgartha]] focused on survival rather than expansion into [[The Tumult|Terra Incognita]].

### 393 AC, May - Alliance Offer

Wanjiru offered [[Afanas Korovinovich]] the role of "bloodhound, judge, and executioner" against the [[Sylas|Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock]], admitting he had deliberately fueled disbelief of Afanas's claims over decades to prevent the warlock from going to ground.

## Legacy

Wanjiru's heroism is commemorated in tapestries depicting major turning points in Asgarthan history, displayed in the Basileus's chambers alongside moments like [[Chiara Caceres]] ending [[The Three Factions War]].
