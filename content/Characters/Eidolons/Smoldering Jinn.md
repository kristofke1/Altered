# Smoldering Jinn

## Overview

The Smoldering Jinn is a fire [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that provides warmth and protection.

## Appearance

The Smoldering Jinn has coal-like skin that smokes and splits, with embers glowing beneath the surface like fire smoldering in a hearth. Blue, searing flames can leap from its embers.

## Relationships

- **[[Atsadi]]**: [[Bravos]] Exalt who summons the Jinn for warmth and protection (393 AC)
