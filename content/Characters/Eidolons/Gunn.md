# Gunn

## Overview
Gunn is an [[Eidolon]] who collected essence of fallen warriors and recruited survivors to the rebellion against [[Ayxas]] during [[The Torment]].

## Activities During The Torment

### 74 AC - Ugetsu-Fenshen Massacre
After [[The Magan]] attacked [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] monastery in 74 AC, Gunn came to collect essence of fallen warriors from charred remains. She discovered [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] still clung to life despite being pierced by arrows and left for dead. She tended his wounds for days amidst smoldering ruins.

### Recruitment
Gunn offered [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]] pact to join rebellion and help put end to [[Ayxas]]'s tyranny. He accepted to avenge comrades. This recruitment brought one of rebellion's most legendary warriors into the fight against the Mad Kuningas.

## Relationships

- **[[Gericht vaan-Bracht]]**: Tended his wounds and recruited him to rebellion (74 AC)
- **[[Ugetsu-Fenshen]]**: Monastery where she collected fallen warriors' essence
- **[[Ayxas]]**: Mad Kuningas whose tyranny she opposed
- **[[The Torment]]**: Historical period during which she recruited rebels
