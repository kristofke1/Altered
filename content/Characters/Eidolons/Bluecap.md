# Bluecap

## Overview

A Bluecap is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that performs accounting and inventory calculations using an abacus, reacting with emphatic sounds of surprise or contentment as it works through figures.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Aerolith Stock Audit

A Bluecap tallied [[Aerolith]] stocks requested by [[Bravos]] for their spears while [[Waru Toowoom]] observed, confirming the supply figures balanced.

## Relationships

- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: Observed the Bluecap's audit of [[Aerolith]] supplies (393 AC)
