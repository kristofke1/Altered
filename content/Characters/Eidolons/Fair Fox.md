# Fair Fox

## Overview

Fair Fox is a colorful fox [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] with a magnificent bushy tail who serves as [[Teija]]'s [[Chimerae|Chimera]]. The Eidolon possesses a Hoshi-No-Tama, a sacred artifact that can be offered to others.

## Hoshi-No-Tama

Fair Fox carries a Hoshi-No-Tama, a ball-shaped artifact. In a rare occurrence, the Eidolon can offer this artifact to others by presenting it with outstretched paws. When accepted, the artifact's power resonates within the recipient.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, June-September - Summoning Arjun to Aid Teija
Fair Fox appeared to [[Arjun]] in [[The Undergrowth]] while the Alterer was collecting plant samples, presenting the Hoshi-No-Tama with outstretched paws—indicating [[Teija]] was nearby and needed help—then led Arjun through the undergrowth.

## Relationships

- **[[Teija]]**: [[Muna]] Exalt whom Fair Fox serves; summoned Arjun on Teija's behalf (393 AC)
- **[[Arjun]]**: [[Muna]] Exalt who accepted the Hoshi-No-Tama and followed Fair Fox (393 AC)
