# Yeti

Yeti is an Eidolon covered in thick white fur with a bright blue face and black horns like those of an ibex. The Yeti provides warmth and shelter to small creatures during harsh winter conditions.

## Appearance

Yeti appears as:
- Debonair fellow covered in thick white fur
- Bright blue face
- Pair of black horns like those of an ibex
- Broad grin revealing long canines
- Large enough to act as perch for multiple birds

## Behavior

Yeti demonstrates gentle, nurturing behavior toward small creatures seeking warmth. The Eidolon sits in snow with broad grin while acting as living shelter. Birds huddle together against Yeti's chest, and the Eidolon seems thrilled to keep them company while listening to their joyful songs. Yeti brings warmth to chilly woods through presence alone.

## Manifestation

### 393 AC - Discovered by Arjun and Spike During Rescue Mission

New layer of snow appeared overnight doubling snow blanket thickness. [[Arjun]] and [[Spike]] set out on rescue mission to help fragile flora and fauna including arctic poppies, blueberries, rockfoils, and cottongrass. Forest was unnaturally quiet with even birds seeming gone.

[[Spike]] led [[Arjun]] along narrow path. As they rounded bend, [[Arjun]] heard chirping of birds. Birds overwhelmed by cold had taken refuge in Yeti's arms. Yeti sat in snow with broad grin revealing long canines, acting as perch for tiny feathered friends huddling together against chest. Yeti seemed happy to keep birds company and listen to their joyful songs.

Reassured by discovery, [[Arjun]] left Yeti to bring warmth to chilly woods and resumed mission to help those who hadn't found such refuge.

## Comparison to Other Cold-Related Eidolons

Yeti was considered as potential candidate for [[Storvhit]] [[Oneiros]] role but ruled out because Yeti needed cold to exist but could not generate it. This contrasts with [[Jack Frost]] and [[Kuraokami]], who possess ability to generate and sustain cold rather than merely requiring it.
