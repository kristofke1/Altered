# Lernaean Hydra

## Overview

Lernaean Hydra is a multi-headed serpentine [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] based on the legendary creature from Greek mythology. Its heads can devour shadow entities.

## Manifestation

### 393 AC, June-September - Shadow Attack in City of Scholars

During a shadow attack in the [[City of Scholars]] caverns, [[Atsadi]] attempted to summon a bloodthirsty version of the Lernaean Hydra to combat the entities targeting the expedition's Alter Egos. However, [[Teija]] psychically intervened through [[The Skein]], implanting alternative ideas into Atsadi's imagination while he struggled to maintain focus on his original concept. The resulting manifestation differed from Atsadi's intended vision due to Teija's mental interference.

The Hydra's heads emerged from the ground one by one, their long necks unwinding to attack the shadow entities with sharp teeth.
