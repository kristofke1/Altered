# Bedivere

## Overview
Bedivere is an [[Altered/Concepts/Systems/Eidolons|Eidolon]] and master swordswoman who lost an arm, making her uniquely qualified to teach sword handling to those with similar handicaps. She trained [[Atsadi]] in the fundamentals of one-armed swordsmanship before he left [[Dusk]] to travel the world.

## One-Armed Mastery
Bedivere's defining characteristic as a sword instructor is that she too had lost an arm during her lifetime. This shared experience made her the most apt teacher to help [[Atsadi]] surpass his limits and compensate for his handicap after he lost his right arm trying to save [[Altered/Characters/Other/Aurora]].

The [[Lady of the Lake]] specifically recommended Bedivere to [[Atsadi]] because of this parallel, suggesting that only someone who had overcome the same challenge could properly guide him toward the mastery needed to wield [[Excalibur]].

## Teaching Atsadi
After [[Atsadi]] made his vow before the [[Lady of the Lake]] to become the world's greatest swordsman, he sought out Bedivere for training. Following Bedivere's teachings at [[Dusk]], [[Atsadi]] gained the foundational skills necessary to begin his journey.

The lessons from Bedivere prepared [[Atsadi]] to:
- Compensate for fighting with only one arm
- Develop his own techniques and adaptations
- Continue seeking tutelage from other masters
- Eventually create his own fighting style, the [[Galisgi]]

Bedivere also provided guidance on additional training opportunities. She suggested that [[Atsadi]] seek training from [[Kappa]], an Eidolon master trainer known for unconventional methods that help students overcome physical limitations. [[Atsadi]] followed this advice around 378 AC, undertaking mountain training with [[Kappa]].

## Legacy and Influence
Bedivere's teachings formed the bedrock upon which [[Atsadi]] built his martial prowess. After leaving [[Dusk]], he sought tutelage from numerous other swordsmen and blacksmiths, begging for lessons from countless fencing schools. But it was Bedivere's initial instruction that made all subsequent learning possible.

The fact that [[Atsadi]] eventually developed the [[Galisgi]] style and defeated legendary warriors like [[Tomoe Gozen]] and [[Achilles]] speaks to the quality of Bedivere's foundational instruction.

## Connection to Arthurian Legend
As an [[Eidolon]], Bedivere can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those familiar with her legend. Her connection to the [[Lady of the Lake]] and [[Excalibur]] suggests she is part of the Arthurian mythological cycle, though adapted within the Altered universe's cosmology.

## Nature as Eidolon
Bedivere exists as an [[Eidolon]] who can be invoked to provide instruction and guidance. [[Atsadi]]'s ability to receive her teachings suggests she responds to those who genuinely seek to master the sword despite physical limitations.
