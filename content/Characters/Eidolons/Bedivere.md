# Bedivere

## Overview

Bedivere is an [[Eidolon]] and master swordswoman who lost an arm during her lifetime. The [[Lady of the Lake]] recommended her to [[Atsadi]] because of this shared experience, making her uniquely qualified to teach one-armed swordsmanship.

## Training Atsadi

After [[Atsadi]] made his vow before the [[Lady of the Lake]] to become the world's greatest swordsman, he sought out Bedivere at [[Dusk]] for training. Her lessons prepared him to compensate for fighting with only one arm and provided the foundation for his subsequent development of the [[Galisgi]] style.

Bedivere also recommended that [[Atsadi]] seek training from [[Kappa]], an Eidolon master trainer known for unconventional methods.

## Timeline

### 378 AC - Kappa Referral

[[Atsadi]] undertook mountain training with [[Kappa]] following Bedivere's recommendation.

### 382 AC - Observing Achilles Duel

Bedivere watched as [[Atsadi]] dueled [[Achilles]], serving as observer during the bout.
