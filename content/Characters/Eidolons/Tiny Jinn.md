Tiny Jinn are a multitude of small Eidolons who serve as guardians of the Phoenix egg in the sacred chamber beneath Haven. They maintain the eternal flame that incubates the egg, ensuring it continues burning while waiting for the Phoenix to be reborn.

## Appearance

Tiny Jinn float in the air like will-o'-the-wisps. They appear as small luminous spirits that drift through the chamber. The Jinn are described as a multitude rather than individuals, working collectively to maintain their sacred duty.

## Fire Generation

Tiny Jinn generate fire through the pressure of their small palms. They can create flames with nothing but this palm pressure, requiring no external fuel or materials. This ability allows them to continuously feed the flames of the Phoenix's brazier without depleting resources.

## Sacred Duty

The Tiny Jinn serve as guardians of the sacred chamber beneath Haven. Their primary responsibility is keeping the fire continuously lit in the central brazier where the Phoenix egg rests. The Bravos have waited centuries for the Phoenix to be reborn.

## The Sacred Chamber

### 391 AC - Atsadi's Visit

When Atsadi descended the sandstone staircase beneath Haven in 391 AC, he encountered the sacred chamber where Rune was crowned the first Kuningas and where the Bravos Creed was enacted. The chamber contained tiers of seats and countless urns belonging to heroes of old, with incense sticks placed before each.

The central brazier held the Phoenix egg, its hissing column of smoke rising through an opening in the rocky ceiling. The Tiny Jinn floated throughout the chamber, tending the flames. Atsadi observed them generating fire to maintain the nest's continuous burning. The atmosphere was stifling with heat rising from the depths, carrying scents of sulfur and incense.
