# Three Little Pigs

The Three Little Pigs are [[Eidolons]] who serve as engineers and vehicle operators, commonly associated with [[Axiom]] technical operations.

## Roles and Functions

The Three Little Pigs perform various technical and supervisory functions including steering vehicles, supervising competitions for guild membership, and providing engineering support during expeditions.

## Timeline

### 381 AC - Fulcrum Competition Supervision

The Three Little Pigs supervised a competition for spots in [[The Fulcrum]] guild, replacing a Journeyman as overseers while candidates worked on machine design and assembly diagrams.

### 393 AC - Reprocessor Vehicle Steering

The Three Little Pigs steered the Reprocessor armored vehicle during [[Storvhit]] operations, reversing to safety when [[Sierra]] stabilized cracking rock with [[Alteration]] after the vehicle nearly slid off a cliff edge.

### ~393 AC - Hostile Forest Rescue

The Three Little Pigs were discovered by [[Teija]] struggling with voracious roots and grasping stalks in a dark clearing within a hostile reawakened forest at [[Storvhit]], with one pig engineer grabbed and lifted by vine before Teija intervened to free them.

### 393 AC - Creature Hunt Encounter

[[Sigismar Synd]] encountered the Three Little Pigs during a creature hunt and directed them toward camp.

## Relationships

- **[[The Fulcrum]]**: [[Axiom]] guild whose membership competition they supervised (381 AC)
- **[[Sierra]]**: [[Axiom]] Exalt who saved them from cliff disaster during Storvhit operations (393 AC)
- **[[Teija]]**: [[Muna]] Exalt who rescued them from hostile forest (~393 AC)
- **[[Sigismar Synd]]**: [[Ordis]] Exalt who directed them to camp during creature hunt (393 AC)
