# Oread

## Overview

An Oread is a mountain nymph [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoned by [[Nadir]] to assist with terrain traversal in mountainous environments. As an Exalt who had not yet achieved complete control over his [[Alteration]], [[Nadir]] was required to be supervised by an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] when using his abilities, making the Oread both guide and safeguard.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Controlled Landslide for Nadir
[[Nadir]] summoned an Oread to help him descend a mountain rapidly by surfing a controlled landslide, painting rocks in a style inspired by August Macke while the nymph maintained control of the rockfall.

- **[[Nadir]]**: [[Lyra]] Exalt who summoned the Oread for mountain descent (393 AC)
