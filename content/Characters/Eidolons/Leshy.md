# Leshy

Leshy is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] of enormous stature with tree-like characteristics who assists travelers by bridging impassable terrain.

## Appearance

Leshy has a flower-shaped face approximately fifteen feet across, topped with elk-like horns. His hair and beard are composed of moss, giving him a lichen-covered appearance. His man-like torso is covered with multiple layers of bark from different tree varieties. His arms resemble massive tree trunks capable of spanning wide distances.

## Abilities

Leshy can grow his body to immense size, stopping growth when half as wide as the gap he intends to bridge. His enormous arms stretch like tree trunks to grasp terrain on opposite sides of chasms, with his elbow serving as a crossing point for travelers.

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Bridging Expedition Crevasse

When [[Teija]]'s expedition halted at a wide crevasse spanning miles that could not be crossed with ropes and grappling hooks, Leshy emerged from the gorge bottom as a rumbling green tuft that grew into his full form, offering a conspiratorial smile before stretching his tree-trunk arms across the gap to serve as a makeshift bridge.

## Relationships

- **[[Teija]]**: [[Muna]] Exalt whose expedition Leshy assisted by bridging crevasse (~393 AC)
