# Lady of the Lake

Lady of the Lake, also known as Nimue, is an Eidolon guardian who wields Excalibur. 

## Excalibur

Lady of Lake holds Excalibur, legendary sword. Sword vibrates gently in her hand. She whispers words to calm it down. Excalibur perceives threats lurking in half-light of untouched sanctuaries.

## Guardian Role

Nimue serves as one of guardians of Megalith in [[The Well of Tears]] alongside [[Sakarabru]] and [[Marduk]].

## Sealing Process

When summoned via shattered Effigy statuette, Nimue's presence bursts forth from microscopic breach opened to Empyrean. She speaks into world and turns toward Aether meteor. Rock sparkles as though adorned with stars and constellations, comes to life as if crossed by galaxies and spinning celestial bodies.

Without saying word, Nimue plunges Excalibur into mass of Aether leaving it buried almost to hilt. Tremors suddenly stop and spectral glimmers fade away. This provides brief respite but requires additional guardians for complete seal.

## Encounter with Atsadi
[[Atsadi]], seeking a weapon capable of breaking [[Altered/Characters/Other/Aurora|Aurora]]'s crystal prison, learned of the Lady of the Lake from [[Lyra]] travelers. After [[Surge]] told him that only [[Excalibur]] could crack the crystal jail, [[Atsadi]] researched the Lady's story extensively to nourish his imagination sufficiently to invoke her.

When [[Atsadi]] invoked the Lady through [[Alteration]] and begged her to entrust him with [[Excalibur]], she could not bring herself to grant his request despite his pleading. She explained that only the greatest swordsman in the world could be considered worthy. She had been Excalibur's protector since time immemorial and could not absolve herself of her duty.

### Guidance to Bedivere
Touched by [[Atsadi]]'s determination and circumstances, the Lady of the Lake advised him to seek out [[Bedivere]] so that she could teach him the handling of the sword. She specifically recommended [[Bedivere]] because she too had lost an arm and would therefore be most apt to help [[Atsadi]] surpass his limits and compensate for his handicap.

This guidance set [[Atsadi]] on his path to become worthy of [[Excalibur]], suggesting the Lady believed he had the potential to achieve the necessary mastery despite his physical limitation.

### The Test of Worthiness
The Lady's requirement that only the greatest swordsman in the world can wield [[Excalibur]] represents a test of martial excellence. [[Atsadi]] made a vow before her to become that exceptional swordsman, to move heaven and earth challenging all the greatest masters of arms the world had known.

His quest to defeat legendary warriors like [[Tomoe Gozen]], [[Achilles]], [[Sun Wukong]], [[Hua Mulan]], and [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]], as well as his search for [[The Seven Blades]], all stem from this requirement to prove himself worthy in the Lady's eyes.

## Summoning Method

### 386 AC - Sealing Megalith Singularity

Afanas opened mouth letting Senka burst out in thick mauve plumes. Nighthawk spread wings while alabaster mask solidified to cover head. Afanas channeled Mana into Effigy placed at feet.

Megalith shook and ground trembled. Tumult oozed from floating crystal. Afanas needed to send it back to age-long slumber. Statuette of Nimue shattered. Afanas felt her presence burst forth from microscopic breach opened to Empyrean.

Lady of Lake manifested holding Excalibur. Eidolon turned to Afanas, then to Aether meteor. Her feet brushed ground generating ripples. Hair floated in halo. Walls became obsidian mirrors. In hand, Excalibur vibrated gently. She whispered to calm it.

Rock sparkled with stars and constellations. Nimue plunged sword into Aether mass buried to hilt. Tremors stopped and glimmers faded. Brief respite achieved but two more guardians needed.
