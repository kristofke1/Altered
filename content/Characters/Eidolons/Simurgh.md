# Simurgh

## Overview

Simurgh is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] with healing powers, capable of raining seeds of healing over large numbers of soldiers simultaneously. The entity manifests as a winged creature that breaks through clouds.

## Summoning History

### 393 AC, June-September - Mass Healing Above The Screed
[[Gulrang Oduro]] summoned Simurgh above [[The Screed]] after piloting [[Mesektet]] out of [[The Crow's Eye]] following an attack, healing over 200 soldiers in critical condition simultaneously.

## Relationships

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: [[Ordis]] Exalt who summoned Simurgh for mass healing (393 AC)
