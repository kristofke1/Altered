# Tomoe Gozen

## Overview
Tomoe Gozen is an [[Eidolon]] representing a legendary warrior who taught [[Atsadi]] that fights always start in opponents' heads. She was defeated in single combat by [[Atsadi]], the One-Armed Swordsman, as part of his quest to prove himself the world's greatest swordsman and become worthy of wielding [[Excalibur]].

## Encounter with Atsadi
As [[Atsadi]] developed his [[Galisgi]] fighting style, he began comparing it to the techniques of legendary [[Eidolons]]. He summoned Tomoe Gozen through [[Alteration]] and challenged her to combat.

The battle tested [[Atsadi]]'s newly developed one-armed sword technique against Tomoe Gozen's legendary skill. Through hard work and determination, [[Atsadi]] managed to triumph over this formidable opponent.

## Combat Philosophy

Tomoe Gozen taught [[Atsadi]] fundamental principle of combat psychology: "The fight always starts in the opponents' heads." This teaching emphasizes that mental warfare and psychological positioning precede physical exchanges in dueling. The lesson influenced [[Atsadi]]'s approach to subsequent duels, including his bout with [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] where he applied this principle by reading his opponent's intentions through muscle tension and positioning.

## Significance
Tomoe Gozen's defeat represents one of [[Atsadi]]'s major milestones in his quest to become the greatest swordsman. By besting this legendary warrior, he demonstrated that his [[Galisgi]] style could stand against even the most renowned fighters from history and legend.

The victory proved that [[Atsadi]]'s handicap - fighting with only one arm - could be overcome through superior technique, dedication, and the innovative combat style he had developed.

## Nature as Eidolon
As an [[Eidolon]], Tomoe Gozen can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those who know her legend. [[Atsadi]]'s ability to materialize and fight her demonstrates his mastery of Alteration magic alongside his swordsmanship.

## Historical Legend
Tomoe Gozen represents a figure from Japanese history and legend, a female warrior renowned for her combat prowess. In the Altered universe, she exists as an [[Eidolon]] who can be invoked to test the skills of aspiring swordmasters.
