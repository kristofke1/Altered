# Tomoe Gozen

## Overview

Tomoe Gozen is an [[Eidolon]] representing a legendary Japanese female warrior. She can be summoned through [[Alteration]] by those familiar with her legend.

## Timeline

### Date Unknown - Duel with Atsadi

[[Atsadi]] summoned Tomoe Gozen through [[Alteration]] as he developed his [[Galisgi]] fighting style. Through hard work and determination, [[Atsadi]] defeated her, one of several legendary warriors he bested in his quest to become worthy of wielding [[Excalibur]].

### Date Unknown - Teaching Atsadi

Tomoe Gozen taught [[Atsadi]] the principle that "the fight always starts in the opponents' heads." This teaching influenced his approach to subsequent duels, including his bout with [[Gericht vaan-Bracht]]'s [[Eidolon]] where he applied psychological positioning by reading his opponent's intentions through muscle tension.

## Relationships

- **[[Atsadi]]**: Student who defeated her in combat; taught him about psychological warfare in dueling
- **[[Gericht vaan-Bracht]]**: Legendary duelist whose combat Atsadi analyzed using Tomoe's teaching
- **[[Excalibur]]**: Mythical sword Atsadi seeks, requiring him to defeat legendary warriors
