# Kappa

## Overview

Kappa is an [[Eidolon]] master trainer known for unconventional and demanding teaching methods. Kappa specializes in helping students overcome physical limitations through patience-testing exercises.

## Teaching Methods

Kappa employs training techniques that appear antiquated or absurd but are designed to strengthen students. Training assignments include climbing mountains with heavy weights, performing repetitive tasks, and enduring seeming hardships. Kappa observes students casually while they struggle, often smoking a pipe and sipping cool tea during sessions.

## Timeline

### 378 AC - Training Atsadi

[[Bedivere]] suggested that [[Atsadi]] follow Kappa's training. Kappa assigned Atsadi to scale an endless mountain while wearing heavy weights strapped to his legs and left arm, with a bag of stones on his shoulders. The peak remained far off throughout the climb, with heat making each step a trial.

During the ascent, Kappa casually smoked his pipe and sipped cool tea while observing Atsadi's progress. [[Surge]] accompanied Atsadi during the training, utterly unaffected by gravity or distance—this contrast helped Atsadi realize that the goal was for him to overcome his own physical limitations rather than relying on his Alter Ego's abilities.
