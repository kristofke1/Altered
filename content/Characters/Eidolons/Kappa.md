Kappa is an Eidolon master trainer known for unconventional and demanding teaching methods. Kappa specializes in helping students overcome physical limitations through patience-testing exercises.

## Teaching Methods

Kappa employs training techniques that appear antiquated or absurd but are designed to strengthen students and test their patience. Training assignments include climbing mountains with heavy weights, performing repetitive tasks, and enduring seemingly pointless hardships. Kappa observes students casually while they struggle, often smoking a pipe and sipping cool tea during training sessions.

The mischievous Eidolon has gained a reputation for squeezing the best out of each apprentice, even transforming ordinary students into exceptional ones. Kappa's methods challenge both physical endurance and mental fortitude.

## Work with Atsadi

### 378 AC - Mountain Training

Bedivere suggested that Atsadi follow Kappa's training. Kappa assigned Atsadi to scale an endless mountain while wearing heavy weights strapped to his legs and left arm, with a bag of stones on his shoulders. The peak remained far off throughout the climb, with heat making each step a trial and each flight of stairs an ordeal.

During the ascent, Kappa casually smoked his pipe and sipped cool tea while observing Atsadi's progress. The stark contrast between Kappa's relaxation and Atsadi's suffering tested Atsadi's patience. As Atsadi struggled with the weights and sweat profusely, he questioned whether Kappa was merely testing patience or attempting to strengthen what he could to offset Atsadi's disability.

Surge accompanied Atsadi during the training, utterly unaffected by gravity or distance. This contrast helped Atsadi realize that the goal was for him to overcome his own physical limitations rather than relying on his Alter Ego's abilities. The training contributed to Atsadi's development as a one-armed swordsman.

## Personality

Kappa demonstrates a mischievous temperament, finding amusement in students' struggles while maintaining a casual demeanor. The Eidolon appears wise but remains enigmatic about the true purposes behind training methods. Kappa's relaxed attitude during intense training sessions serves as both a test and a teaching tool.
