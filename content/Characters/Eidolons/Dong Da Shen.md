# Dong Da Shen

Dong Da Shen is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] god of cold whose painted mask represents the face of winter. He is summoned via [[Heka]] using symbols traced in wind that unfurl a gigantic paper scroll.

## Physical Appearance

Dong Da Shen lounges on snow wearing a magnificent blue cape that whips in the wind. His painted mask is the face of winter. The [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] appears at the center of a gigantic paper scroll that unfurls in a circle, with ideograms written on the scroll blazing with azure flame.

## Abilities and Function

Dong Da Shen holds a document described as a statement of criminal charges and wields a scepter. His inquisitorial voice thunders as he points his scepter at enemies. As god of cold, Dong Da Shen commands authority over winter elements and can inspire fear in cold-adapted creatures.

## Timeline

### ~393 AC - Belisenki Dispersal at Storvhit

[[Waru Toowoom]] summoned Dong Da Shen during [[Belisenki]] pursuit at [[Storhvit]] by tracing [[Heka]] symbols in the wind, causing the paper scroll to unfurl with blazing ideograms and the [[Belisenki]] to disperse and return to the shelter of trees.

## Relationships

- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: [[Ordis]] Exalt who summoned Dong Da Shen during [[Belisenki]] attack (~393 AC)
- **[[Belisenki]]**: Moth-like creatures dispersed by Dong Da Shen's authority (~393 AC)
