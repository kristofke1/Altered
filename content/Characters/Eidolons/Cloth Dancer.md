# Cloth Dancer

## Overview

The Cloth Dancer is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] that appears as a suspended aerial dancer, twirling from clouds during performances.

## Summoning History

### 392 AC - Lyra Festival Performance

The Cloth Dancer appeared during [[Fen]] and [[Crowbar]]'s festival performance in [[Arkaster]], twirling above the performers' heads while suspended from a cloud. The [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] was summoned alongside [[The Sandman]] and [[Asmodeus]] as part of a spectacular multi-sensory concert experience.

## Relationships

- **[[Fen]]**: [[Lyra]] Exalt who summoned the Cloth Dancer during performance (392 AC)
- **[[Crowbar]]**: [[Fen]]'s Alter Ego present during summoning (392 AC)
- **[[The Sandman]]**: Fellow [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoned during same performance (392 AC)
- **[[Asmodeus]]**: Fellow [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoned during same performance (392 AC)
