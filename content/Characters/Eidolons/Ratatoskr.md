Ratatoskr is an Eidolon manifesting as a little red squirrel who serves as the mascot and emblem of the Talaria, Asgartha's postal system.

## Appearance

Ratatoskr appears as a little red squirrel. The Eidolon is bright and bubbly in demeanor.

## Role as Talaria Mascot

The little red squirrel has always been the mascot of the Talaria, Asgartha's postal system. Ratatoskr represents the guild's values of speed, agility, and reliability in delivering messages and parcels throughout Asgartha.

## 382 AC - Assisting Kojo's Delivery

During an urgent postal delivery, Kojo used Alteration to create stone steps in the air while suspended above a canal. He materialized Ratatoskr, the Talaria guild's emblem. The red squirrel Eidolon appeared bright and bubbly, hopping from surface to surface at exceptional speed. Ratatoskr created a slipstream effect that allowed Kojo to gain speed as he followed in the Eidolon's wake toward his delivery destination.

## Abilities

Ratatoskr demonstrates exceptional speed and agility:
- Hops rapidly from surface to surface
- Creates slipstream effect that allows followers to gain speed
- Causes world to blur around those following
- Moves even faster than skilled Altrun practitioners
- Can be materialized through Alteration by Talaria members

## Relationships

- **Kojo Oduro**: Summoned Ratatoskr for urgent delivery assistance (382 AC)
- **The Talaria**: Serves as guild mascot and emblem
