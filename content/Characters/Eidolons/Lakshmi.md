# Lakshmi

## Overview

Lakshmi is an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] goddess associated with wealth and good luck. Her manifestations symbolize eight sources of wealth: courage, spirituality, lineage, knowledge, royalty, material wealth, agriculture, and victory.

## Abilities

Lakshmi provides [[Sap]]-infused dice when requested. The dice are elongated cuboids rather than standard six-sided cubes. As a goddess of luck, she demonstrates superior fortune in games of chance.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, June-August - Dice Game with Nevenka

[[Nevenka]] summoned Lakshmi and requested Sap-infused dice, theorizing they would guarantee wins at gambling. Lakshmi sat cross-legged and provided the requested dice. When they played a wager game, Lakshmi won, demonstrating that challenging a goddess of luck was ill-advised.

## Relationships

- **[[Nevenka]]**: [[Lyra]] Exalt who summoned Lakshmi for Sap dice and lost gambling wager (393 AC)
