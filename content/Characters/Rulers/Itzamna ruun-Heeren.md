# Itzamna ruun-Heeren

## Overview

Itzamna ruun-Heeren was Basileus of [[Asgartha]] during Republic period, reigning from 301-305 AC. Perished in 305 AC tsunami that devastated [[Arkaster]], along with much of government and significant portion of urban population. Death occurred despite [[Aroro Niski]]'s warnings about impending cataclysm, which went unheeded during final hours before disaster.

## 305 AC Tsunami

### Failed Warning

[[Aroro Niski]], [[Yzmir]] mage who detected disturbance in [[The Aether]] flows heralding tsunami, sought audience with Itzamna at [[The Asterion]]. Aroro waited long hours in Palace halls hoping for meeting that never came. Tried to persuade [[The Aegis]] sentinels to let her through, but audience never granted. Said she turned back with face stricken with dread—perhaps glimpsed horizon through Palace windows, realized time running out.

### Death in Deluge

Itzamna perished when tsunami struck [[Arkaster]], devastating lakefront Districts and much of [[The Asterion]] palace. Died along with much of government, requiring hasty election of [[Ndidi Dewitt]] as successor Basilissa. Though [[Aroro Niski]]'s sacrifice drastically reduced human toll and extent of destruction, could not prevent deaths at highest levels of [[Asgartha]]n government.
