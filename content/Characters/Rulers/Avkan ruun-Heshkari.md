# Avkan ruun-Heshkari

## Overview
Avkan ruun-Heshkari is the current Basileus of [[Asgartha]], having held office since 361 AC. As of 392 AC, they have ruled for 31 years.

## Extended Mandate
Twenty-four years prior to 392 AC (approximately 368 AC), Avkan requested an extended mandate to realize an ambitious project of unprecedented scale.

## The Age of Rediscovery Proclamation
In 392 AC, Avkan delivered [[The Age of Rediscovery Proclamation]], announcing the launch of [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] and declaring the beginning of the Age of Rediscovery.

## Secret Collaboration with Waru

Avkan maintained secret friendship and political collaboration with Waru Toowoom spanning decades. While Waru publicly served as Avkan's most notorious opponent, he actually functioned as Avkan's most loyal ally working to enable Rediscovery Endeavor.

### 363 AC - Rediscovery Plan Development
Waru submitted Rediscovery Endeavor launch plan through official channels to Legate on Avkan's behalf. Plan faced scrutiny identifying funding issues, budget cuts, and continuity concerns. Basileus's mandate alone wouldn't be enough to make endeavor happen.

### 364 AC - Intelligence Gathering
Spy on Avkan's payroll observed Waru's meeting with Robin Hood at tavern. Waru deliberately stirred controversy about Rediscovery Endeavor to create controlled opposition. Intelligence operation indicated second phase of plan bearing fruit.

### 365 AC - Waru's Sacrifice
Waru was convicted of attempted corruption, threats, and kickbacks after trial. Prosecutor referenced Waru forcing Isura banking institution's hand. Waru received immediate removal and expulsion for minimum three years. By sacrifice, Waru gave Avkan complete freedom to approve Rediscovery Endeavor launch while silencing protest and creating succession struggle among opponents.

### 367 AC - Historical Research
Waru conducted research at Sanctum and consulted with Quetzalcóatl and Thoth. He examined Baird y Idris's logbook containing City of Scholars coordinates and translated Tumult Nomad memoir revealing "City of Scholars, where source of Tumult is engraved." Research provided compelling reason for Endeavor.

### 368 AC - Council Ratification
Four councilors ratified edict recommending Rediscovery Endeavor. Waru theatrically opposed on auditorium floor while political allies berated Council. Avkan nodded as opposing bench congratulated each other. Avkan gave Waru discreet nod but took care to project arrogant disdain to hide gratitude. Years of masquerade maintained secrecy.

### 386 AC - Exalt Appointment
Basileus's seal appeared on proclamation nominating Waru as Exalt. Frog Prince authenticated document making Waru and Mack official Exalt. After years as "opponent," now Waru's turn to bear burden of shared ambition.

### Peregrine Museum Meetings
Avkan met secretly with Waru at Peregrine Museum. Basileus spread arms approaching with grateful smile. Avkan embraced old friend patting shoulder while Mack perched on sovereign's arm. Before them stood old map of world as it was before. Two friends vowed to affix new map of current reality with Asgartha at center. After all these years, finally Waru's turn to carry dream further.

## Lineage
They are of the ruun-Heshkari lineage, connected to the founding family of [[Bravos]].

## Role
The Basileus, elected for a usual term of ten years, arbitrates the sessions of the Council. In cases of tie votes, the Basileus has the authority to decide the direction deemed most appropriate.

## Relationships

- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: Old friend and secret collaborator spanning 23+ years (363-386 AC)
- **[[Mack]]**: Waru's Alter Ego who gathered intelligence for Avkan
- **[[Robin Hood]]**: Isura financier whose meeting with Waru was monitored by Avkan's spy (364 AC)
- **[[Quetzalcóatl]]**: University professor who showed Waru logbook advancing Endeavor research (367 AC)
- **[[Thoth]]**: Scholar who translated memoir for Waru revealing City of Scholars importance (367 AC)
- **[[Frog Prince]]**: Notary who authenticated Waru's Exalt appointment with Avkan's seal (386 AC)
