# Assura ruun-Caellach

## Overview

Assura ruun-Caellach was Basileus of [[Asgartha]] during Republic period, reigning from 274-284 AC. During reign in 277 AC, [[Aroro Niski]] was born—future [[Yzmir]] mage who would master all Five Spheres and sacrifice herself defending [[Arkaster]] from 305 AC tsunami.
