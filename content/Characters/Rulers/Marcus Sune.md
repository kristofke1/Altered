# Marcus Sune

## Overview
Marcus Sune was Basileus of [[Asgartha]] during the late Federation period, reigning from 235-254 AC.

## Legacy
Marcus Sune dreamed of transforming the Kingdom into a Republic. This dream was realized by his successor, [[Morwen Durward]], in 254 AC.
