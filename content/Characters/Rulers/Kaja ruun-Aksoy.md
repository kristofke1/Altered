# Kaja ruun-Aksoy

## Overview
Kaja ruun-Aksoy was Basileus of [[Asgartha]] during the Federation period, reigning from 208-224 AC.

## Infrastructure Development
[[Svarga Station]] was built during the reign of Kaja ruun-Aksoy. This station was crucial for the mutual development of the capital and its Provinces.
