# Amaro Arundhani

Amaro Arundhani is a charismatic senator and skilled orator who served as an opposition voice before entering a strategic political alliance with [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]]. A protégé of [[Waru Toowoom]] and member of the new generation of politicians at [[The Agora]], he was positioned by Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] as an indispensable pillar of the [[New Exchequer]].

## Background

Amaro belongs to the new generation of politicians seated at [[The Agora]], though he is notably more moderate than his mentor [[Waru Toowoom]]. His chestnut hair is styled in artfully casual waves and meticulously combed back, with a neatly trimmed beard framing his disarming smile.

## Strategic Alliance with Anuncia

Despite positioning himself as an opposition candidate, Amaro entered a secret alliance with [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] that extended beyond politics into intimacy. In 392 AC, Anuncia pledged herself to Amaro, and they planned to announce their engagement. The alliance was part of [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]'s orchestrated succession planning—together their union would command nearly two-thirds of the political arena.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Oratorium Speech

Amaro delivered a speech at [[The Oratorium]] during the political frenzy preceding the 393 AC elections, critiquing the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]'s impact on ordinary Asgarthans. He argued that the time had come to look toward the future, emphasizing the "collective strain" and sacrifices made by the people. His core message: "Asgartha must remain our absolute priority. The people of Asgartha must feel that we will not abandon them." At least a third of the assembly applauded.

### 393 AC, May 28 - Statement at Oratorium

Following Senator [[Noya|Noya Kagama]]'s defense of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], Amaro emphasized the patience already demanded of the Asgarthan population, stating that the people had waited long enough. When [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun|Anuncia]] interrupted him during the [[Radio Guild]] broadcast defending expedition members, Amaro appeared unbothered and somewhat amused.

### 393 AC, August 6 - Joint Candidacy Announcement

Amaro and [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] formally announced their political union and candidacy as a couple for Basileus and Basilissa at [[The Asterion]]. Amaro declared that the burden placed upon the people must not have been in vain and that undoing what had been accomplished would be a loss, though the people must benefit from the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] or it would be nothing more than a narcissistic whim. Together they asked for votes as a couple rather than individuals—the first such request in Asgarthan history.
