# Amaro Arundhani

Amaro Arundhani is a charismatic senator and skilled orator who served as an opposition voice before entering a strategic political alliance with Anuncia ruun-Aysun. A protÃ©gÃ© of Waru Toowoom and member of the new generation of politicians at the Agora, he was positioned by Basileus Avkan ruun-Heshkari as an indispensable pillar of the New Exchequer.

## Background and Appearance

Amaro belongs to the new generation of politicians seated at the Agora, though he is notably more moderate than his mentor Waru Toowoom. He maintains a carefully cultivated appearance that exudes respectability while remaining understated and effective. His chestnut hair is styled in artfully casual waves and meticulously combed back, with a neatly trimmed beard framing his disarming smile.

## Political Career and Oratory

In 392 AC, Amaro delivered a significant speech at the Oratorium during the political frenzy preceding the 393 AC elections. His oratory skills were widely recognized - he deliberately scanned his audience before speaking, using calculated pauses and silences designed to let his audience fill the void and maximize dramatic impact.

His speech strategy combined humility with populist messaging, emphasizing certain words and playing with silences to ensure all remained captivated. He balanced confidence with emotion, grievance with gratitude, creating what observers described as eloquent and deliberate political theater. His gestures were purposeful, with clenched fists and resolute expressions reinforcing his words.

## Political Platform

Amaro's speech focused on critiquing the Rediscovery Endeavor's impact on ordinary Asgarthans. While acknowledging that he was still young when the project was proclaimed and that it had shaped the political and economic landscape for most of his life, he argued that the time had come to look toward the future.

His key arguments emphasized the "collective strain" and significant sacrifices made by the people, who had to tighten their belts so the Endeavor could take flight. He criticized the enormous funds invested at the expense of other vital programs - enterprises in education, healthcare, and social justice that were set aside as less glamorous but equally essential.

His core message declared: "Asgartha must remain our absolute priority. The people of Asgartha must feel that we will not abandon them." This populist stance garnered significant support, with at least a third of the assembly applauding his speech, though Amaro recognized this approach might be characterized as falling into populism by his opponents.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, May 28 - Statement at Oratorium

Following Senator [[Noya|Noya Kagama]]'s defense of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], Amaro emphasized the patience already demanded of the Asgarthan population, stating that the people had waited long enough and had every right to demand answers when promised deliveries were slow to arrive. When [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun|Anuncia Synd]] interrupted him during the Radio Guild broadcast defending expedition members, Amaro appeared unbothered and somewhat amused.

### 393 AC, August 6 - Joint Candidacy Announcement

Amaro and [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] formally announced their political union and candidacy as a couple for Basileus and Basilissa at [[The Asterion]]. Amaro spoke first, declaring that the burden placed upon the people must not have been in vain and that undoing what had been accomplished would be a loss, though the people of Asgartha must benefit from the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] or it would be nothing more than a narcissistic whim. He yielded the floor to Anuncia, who addressed the assembly on unity and bridge-building. Together they asked for votes as a couple rather than individuals - the first such request in Asgarthan history.

## Strategic Alliance with Anuncia

Despite positioning himself as an opposition candidate, Amaro entered a secret alliance with Anuncia ruun-Aysun that extended beyond politics into intimacy. The relationship was part of Avkan ruun-Heshkari's orchestrated succession planning.

In 392 AC, Anuncia pledged herself to Amaro, and they planned to announce their engagement soon. Anuncia cared for Amaro and considered the engagement the best thing for both of them.

During his Oratorium speech, Amaro locked eyes with Anuncia for a moment before shaking a colleague's hand. That glance revealed mutual awareness - both were studying each other's strategies and attempting to decipher the other's intentions.

The alliance represented a strategic calculation rather than romance. Amaro was positioned to represent strong opposition credentials while Anuncia represented Old Nobility aligned with the outgoing Basileus. Together, their union would command nearly two-thirds of the political arena.

## Political Considerations

In 392 AC, Amaro was reportedly hesitating between supporting Girsang or Muttunbaal for other political positions. The decision carried weight as Girsang's coffers ran deep while Muttunbaal possessed greater influence - demonstrating Amaro's careful consideration of both financial backing and political power.

The planned announcement of his union with Anuncia on the eve of the 393 AC elections was designed to strike like thunder, with various factions seeing different benefits: some viewing it as a gateway to higher Asterion positions, others as a means to resolve years of political discord, potentially symbolizing a renewed Concord.
