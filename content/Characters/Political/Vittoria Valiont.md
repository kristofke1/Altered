# Vittoria Valiont

Vittoria Valiont is a member of the [[Old Nobility]] and ally of [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] during the [[393 AC Basileal Elections]]. She shares the Valiont lineage with former Basilissa [[Annuska Valiont]].

## Relationships

- **[[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]]**: Political ally (393 AC)
- **[[Abenet ruun-Aksoy]]**: Fellow ally of Anuncia ruun-Aysun
- **[[Old Nobility]]**: Aristocratic class to which Vittoria belongs
- **[[Annuska Valiont]]**: Former Basilissa, shares Valiont lineage
