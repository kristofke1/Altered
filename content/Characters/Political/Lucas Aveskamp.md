# Lucas Aveskamp

Lucas Aveskamp is a politician and direct descendant of [[Geirr Aveskamp]], the fifth Basileus of Asgartha. He is a fierce opponent of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] and candidate in the [[393 AC Basileal Elections]].

## Political Position

Lucas Aveskamp advocates for reallocating funds granted to the [[Expeditionary Corps]] and redirecting them to the Asgarthan populace. He views the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] as "an indulgence from and for the elite" that "harms the people, who suffer every day."

## Rise to Prominence

Aveskamp gained traction among those who view the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] as a burden. He was particularly outspoken during the [[Caer Esvander]] suspended mines strike and the subsequent intervention of private militias, which increased his influence. He was chosen as leading candidate by defectors from [[Amaro Arundhani]]'s camp following Arundhani's alliance with [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Basileal Elections Candidacy

Lucas Aveskamp stood as candidate in the [[393 AC Basileal Elections]], representing opposition to the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] and advocating for domestic spending priorities.
