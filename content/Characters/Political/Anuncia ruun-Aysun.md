# Anuncia ruun-Aysun

Anuncia ruun-Aysun is a young senator and political strategist of the Old Nobility who was positioned by Basileus Avkan ruun-Heshkari as a successor candidate to continue the Rediscovery Endeavor. Born on the day the Endeavor was proclaimed, she represents the new generation of Asgarthan leadership.

## Background and Family

Anuncia is the younger sister of [[Sigismar Synd]] (seven years younger) and descends from Rune through her mother's lineage. Her family had long distanced itself from politics before Avkan ruun-Heshkari recognized the strategic value of her Old Nobility heritage combined with her father's unbridled ambition.

She was born on the very day the Rediscovery Endeavor was proclaimed, after a difficult labor witnessed by young Sigismar. Her father forbade Sigismar from seeing their mother during the confinement. When finally allowed inside, Sigismar saw their mother extend her hand, radiant with happiness despite exhaustion, holding the newborn Anuncia. The seven-year age gap between the siblings created a certain reservation between them.

Anuncia benefited from their father's leniency because Sigismar was the one destined to take his place. She had freedoms Sigismar didn't. When Sigismar joined the Ordis, their father lost interest in him and focused all attention on Anuncia. She learned charm and gentleness from her mother rather than inheriting her father's dourness and draconian nature, making her skilled at getting what she wants through persuasion rather than intimidation.

Her brother Sigismar's departure to join the Expeditionary Corps was part of a negotiation between her father and Avkan, with Anuncia serving as a bargaining chip in the arrangement - though she later acknowledged that her brother's "defection" was the best thing that ever happened to her political career.

## Political Career

Anuncia serves as a senator aligned with the continuation of Avkan ruun-Heshkari's policies and the Rediscovery Endeavor. She was taken under the mentorship of Abenet, who recognized her tactical acumen and ability to anticipate political moves. Her mentor saw in her the qualities of a chess master who could see moves coming from leagues away.

In 392 AC, Anuncia attended speeches at the Oratorium as the political establishment entered a frenzy ahead of the upcoming 393 AC elections. She carefully studied her political rivals, analyzing not just their words but their posture, diction, and tone to understand their strategies and anticipate their moves.

## Strategic Alliance with Amaro Arundhani

Anuncia's most significant political maneuver was her secret alliance with Amaro Arundhani, who belonged to the opposition and was one of Waru Toowoom's protÃ©gÃ©s. Despite recognizing Amaro as a skilled orator and charismatic opponent who would draw significant populist support, she saw the strategic value in forming a partnership rather than remaining adversaries.

The relationship extended beyond politics into intimacy, as Anuncia studied Amaro from every angle - both in public arenas and in private moments. She was uncertain whether love bound them together, but recognized that what she needed was support and an accomplice.

In 392 AC, shortly before Sigismar's departure with the Expeditionary Corps, Anuncia pledged herself to Amaro Arundhani. She informed Sigismar at their family villa in Kuna during the Season of Mists that she cared for Amaro and the engagement was the best thing for both of them, despite knowing how much this meant to Sigismar (implying Sigismar may have had feelings for Amaro that their father would have disapproved of). Anuncia demonstrated her independence, telling Sigismar she knew what she wanted and wouldn't let anyone divert her from her goals.

The plan, orchestrated by Avkan ruun-Heshkari, involved positioning Amaro as an indispensable pillar of the New Exchequer while grooming Anuncia to represent the Old Nobility. Together, their union would command nearly two-thirds of the political arena - Amaro representing a strong opposition candidate, Anuncia representing the Old Nobility aligned with the outgoing Basileus.

Their announcement of union was planned for the eve of the 393 AC elections, intended to strike like thunder. The strategic marriage would bridge the chasm that had fractured the upper echelons of Asgarthan politics, potentially symbolizing a renewed Concord. Some would see it as a gateway to higher positions in the Asterion, while others would view it as a means to resolve years of political discord.

## Political Philosophy

Anuncia approaches politics with calculated pragmatism rather than idealism. She recognized that defending the Rediscovery Endeavor required proving to the people that it was launched for their benefit, not detriment. She estimated that the Endeavor had two to three years to produce tangible results before public trust would erode completely.

While she acknowledged that Caer Oorun's reconquest was a significant first victory, she maintained it was far from enough compared to everything the Tumult still held. She understood that hope without concrete results was like building on quicksand, and that the Kraken's defeat was nothing compared to the challenges ahead.

Anuncia was aware of being a pawn in Avkan's schemes but recognized that she would soon cease to be a puppet and become the puppeteer herself. She understood the importance of knowing both adversaries and allies equally well.

## Succession Question

During the 392 AC political season, Anuncia discussed potential successors to Avkan ruun-Heshkari with Abenet. They considered various candidates:
- Noya was deemed too conservative, following Avkan's principles to the letter without adaptability
- Hadrian had minimal influence in the Assembly
- Girsang and Muttunbaal were being courted by Amaro for support, with Girsang's deep coffers balanced against Muttunbaal's greater influence

Abenet suggested that Anuncia herself was the clear heir, describing her as a master tactician needed to continue the Endeavor. He playfully invoked the title "Anuncia ruun-Aysun, fourteenth Basilissa of Asgartha," using her mother's name deliberately to downplay her father's lineage. Despite her protests about being too young and inexperienced, the symbolism of her birth coinciding with the Endeavor's proclamation combined with her strategic abilities made her a compelling candidate.

## Timeline of Activities

### 392 AC - Conversation with Sigismar at Kuna
During Season of Mists shortly before Expeditionary Corps departure, Anuncia found Sigismar at family villa in Kuna and revealed she had pledged herself to Amaro Arundhani, asking Sigismar to remember "those who stay behind."

### 393 AC, May 28 - Radio Guild Interruption
During live Radio Guild broadcast, Anuncia unexpectedly interrupted [[Amaro Arundhani]]'s statements to defend [[Expeditionary Corps]] members including her brother [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]], declaring that expedition members faced countless obstacles daily and deserved support, asking whether the idea of [[The Concord]] was built on unity rather than division between those at home and those on the front lines. She appeared visibly moved during the interruption while Amaro seemed unbothered and somewhat amused.

### 393 AC, August 6 - Joint Candidacy Announcement

Anuncia and [[Amaro Arundhani]] formally announced their political union and candidacy as a couple for Basilissa and Basileus at [[The Asterion]]. Amaro spoke first, declaring that the burden placed upon the people must not have been in vain and that undoing what had been accomplished would be a loss, though the people of Asgartha must benefit from the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] or it would be nothing more than a narcissistic whim. Anuncia then addressed the assembly, stating that the unity of the people was what a Basileus or Basilissa must embody, that all voices must be heard, and that Asgartha must stand united and mend its fractures. She took Amaro's hand and declared they asked for votes not for a single individual but for a couple - the first such request in Asgarthan history. She framed Asgartha as a home where everyone is family, proposing they move forward hand in hand.
