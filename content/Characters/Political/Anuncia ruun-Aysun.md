# Anuncia ruun-Aysun

Anuncia ruun-Aysun, born 368 AC, is a young senator and political strategist of the [[Old Nobility]] who was positioned by Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] as a successor candidate to continue the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. She has green eyes. Born on the day the Endeavor was proclaimed, she represents the new generation of Asgarthan leadership.

## Background and Family

Anuncia is the younger sister of [[Sigismar Synd]] (seven years younger) and descends from [[Rune]] through her mother's lineage. She was born on the day the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] was proclaimed after a difficult labor. Her brother's departure to join the [[Expeditionary Corps]] was part of a negotiation between her father and [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]], with Anuncia serving as a bargaining chip.

Her mentor [[Abenet ruun-Aksoy]] recognized her as a master tactician capable of seeing political moves from leagues away.

## Strategic Alliance with Amaro Arundhani

Anuncia's most significant political maneuver was her secret alliance with [[Amaro Arundhani]], who belonged to the opposition. In 392 AC, shortly before [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]]'s departure with the [[Expeditionary Corps]], Anuncia pledged herself to Amaro. She informed Sigismar at their family villa in [[Kuna]] that she cared for Amaro and the engagement was the best thing for both of them.

The plan, orchestrated by [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]], involved positioning Amaro to represent strong opposition credentials while Anuncia represented the [[Old Nobility]]. Together, their union would command nearly two-thirds of the political arena.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Conversation with Sigismar at Kuna

During Season of Mists shortly before [[Expeditionary Corps]] departure, Anuncia found [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]] at the family villa in [[Kuna]] and revealed she had pledged herself to [[Amaro Arundhani]], asking Sigismar to remember "those who stay behind."

### 393 AC, May 28 - Radio Guild Interruption

During live [[Radio Guild]] broadcast, Anuncia unexpectedly interrupted [[Amaro Arundhani]]'s statements to defend [[Expeditionary Corps]] members including her brother [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]], declaring that expedition members faced countless obstacles daily and deserved support. She asked whether the idea of [[The Concord]] was built on unity rather than division.

### 393 AC, August 6 - Joint Candidacy Announcement

Anuncia and [[Amaro Arundhani]] formally announced their political union and candidacy as a couple for Basilissa and Basileus at [[The Asterion]]. Anuncia stated that the unity of the people was what a Basileus or Basilissa must embody, that all voices must be heard, and that [[Asgartha]] must stand united. She took Amaro's hand and declared they asked for votes not for a single individual but for a couple—the first such request in Asgarthan history.

### 393 AC - Receiving Election Paper Herald

Anuncia burst into [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]]'s quarters aboard the expedition fleet, out of breath, carrying a [[Paper Herald]] bearing the [[Arkaster Echo]] election results.

### 393 AC, October - Basileal Elections

Anuncia received 105 votes (24%) in the [[393 AC Basileal Elections]], placing fourth among the four candidates. [[Somayeh Bahman]] won the election with 140 votes (31%).
