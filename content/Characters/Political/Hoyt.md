# Hoyt

## Overview

Hoyt is a politician who was considered as a potential candidate during discussions about the succession of [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] in 392 AC.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Rejected as Successor Candidate

[[Temesgen]] suggested Hoyt alongside [[Visala]] and [[Hamza]] as potential candidates to succeed [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]. [[Waru Toowoom]] rejected all three, stating they carried too much baggage, and expressed a preference for a new face.

## Relationships

- **[[Hamza]]**: Fellow candidate also rejected by Waru (392 AC)
- **[[Temesgen]]**: Political figure who suggested Hoyt as candidate (392 AC)
- **[[Visala]]**: Fellow candidate also rejected by Waru (392 AC)
- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: Politician who rejected Hoyt as candidate (392 AC)
