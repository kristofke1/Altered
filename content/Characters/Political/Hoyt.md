# Hoyt

Hoyt is a politician who was considered as a potential candidate during political discussions in 392 AC but was rejected due to excessive baggage.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Rejected as Candidate

During political discussions between [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Temesgen]], Hoyt's name was raised alongside [[Visala]] and [[Hamza]] as a potential candidate for [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]'s succession. Waru was seeking someone realistic who could inspire people and maintain harmony—someone representing a new face. Waru rejected all three, stating they carried "too much baggage."

## Relationships

- **[[Waru Toowoom]]**: Politician who rejected Hoyt as candidate
- **[[Temesgen]]**: Political figure who suggested Hoyt as potential candidate
- **[[Visala]]**: Fellow politician also rejected for having too much baggage
- **[[Hamza]]**: Fellow politician also rejected for having too much baggage
