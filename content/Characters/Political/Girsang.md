# Girsang

## Overview

Girsang is a wealthy politician considered a potential candidate for support during the 392–393 AC election cycle. His significant financial resources made him a sought-after figure among political actors seeking to build alliances ahead of the elections.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Election Maneuvering

[[Amaro Arundhani]] was reportedly weighing whether to support Girsang or [[Muttunbaal]] in the upcoming elections. Girsang offered superior financial backing while Muttunbaal possessed greater political influence. [[Anuncia ruun-Aysun]] expressed hope that Amaro would not align with Girsang, while advisor [[Abenet ruun-Aksoy|Abenet]] cautioned against underestimating the importance of monetary resources in political contests.
