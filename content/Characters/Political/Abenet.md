Abenet is a veteran senator and political advisor who served as mentor to Anuncia ruun-Aysun during the 392-393 AC election cycle. Characterized by his strategic thinking and dry wit, he played a key role in positioning Anuncia as a successor candidate to Basileus Avkan ruun-Heshkari.

## Role as Political Mentor

Abenet took Anuncia ruun-Aysun under his wing at the request of her father, who wanted to ensure she would not run from her responsibilities as her brother Sigismar had. However, Abenet recognized qualities beyond family obligation - he saw in Anuncia a master tactician with the ability to see political moves coming from leagues away, comparable to a chess player anticipating future strategies.

During the 392 AC political season, Abenet accompanied Anuncia to the Oratorium where they observed speeches and analyzed rival politicians. He provided strategic counsel on navigating the complex political landscape ahead of the 393 AC elections, offering both tactical advice and cautionary wisdom.

## Political Analysis and Strategy

Abenet demonstrated keen political insight in his assessment of various figures and situations:

He recognized Amaro Arundhani as a skilled orator belonging to the new generation of Agora politicians - one of Waru Toowoom's protégés, though thankfully more moderate than his mentor. When analyzing Amaro's populist speech about prioritizing Asgartha over the Rediscovery Endeavor, Abenet warned that such a stance would garner significant support and that Amaro might prove to be their main opponent.

He cautioned that Anuncia's challenge would be conveying to the people that the Rediscovery Endeavor was launched for their benefit rather than detriment, as Amaro would not hesitate to attack that vulnerable point. Abenet warned that basing discourse on hope was "like building on quicksand" - hope erodes quickly without concrete results.

When Anuncia gave a pessimistic timeline of two to three years to prove the Endeavor's worth, Abenet countered by noting that Caer Oorun's reconquest was a significant first victory, though he conceded that time would tell whether their gamble would pay off.

## Succession Planning

Abenet played a direct role in succession planning for the post-Avkan era. When discussing potential candidates to succeed Avkan ruun-Heshkari, he and Anuncia evaluated:
- Noya: too conservative, following Avkan's principles to the letter without adaptability ("bend like the reed")
- Hadrian: minimal influence in the Assembly at best

Abenet observed that no one seemed to have the stature to step out of Avkan's shadow, which represented their biggest problem. However, he pointedly suggested they already had a clear candidate - or rather, a clear heir - in Anuncia herself.

When Anuncia protested being too young and inexperienced, Abenet countered with strategic framing: "A young woman, born on the very day the Rediscovery Endeavor was proclaimed." He acknowledged that symbolism alone should not be the main argument, but emphasized that what they needed was a chess player and master tactician.

Abenet playfully invoked the title "Anuncia ruun-Aysun, fourteenth Basilissa of Asgartha," deliberately using her mother's name (ruun-Aysun) to downplay her father's lineage. When she protested his audacity, he laughed and declared it was her generation's duty to carry the flame while his generation sat on the sidelines commenting.

## Political Wisdom

Abenet shared several key insights with Anuncia:

He emphasized the symbolic potential of her brother's position: "His prestige should serve to bolster your own. He's out in the wilds; you're here, at the heart of civilization. Together, you form a bridge between the Known World and the Terra Incognita."

When discussing candidate support, he warned never to underestimate the weight of money when Anuncia expressed hope that Amaro wouldn't align with the wealthy Girsang over the more influential Muttunbaal.

He advised Anuncia to consider who might succeed Avkan before looking elsewhere, demonstrating his focus on immediate strategic priorities.

Most significantly, he told Anuncia directly: "I know how you operate, kid. That's exactly why I took you under my wing" - revealing that his mentorship was based on recognizing her abilities rather than merely fulfilling a family favor.

## Personal Characteristics

Abenet is described as scratching his beard when contemplating political matters, suggesting a thoughtful, deliberate demeanor. He refers to himself as an "old fool" in the context of generational politics, positioning himself as someone whose role is to advise the next generation rather than lead directly.

He demonstrates playful affection toward Anuncia, calling her "kid" while simultaneously treating her as a serious political player. He balances humor with hard political realism, willing to both encourage her ambitions and challenge her when playing the victim doesn't suit her character.
