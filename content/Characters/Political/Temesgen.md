# Temesgen

Temesgen is a politician and colleague of [[Waru Toowoom]] who was involved in succession planning discussions for the post-[[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] era.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Succession Planning with Waru

During the celebration at [[The Asterion]], Temesgen discussed successor candidates with [[Waru Toowoom]]. When Waru described seeking someone neutral who could inspire people and maintain harmony, Temesgen suggested [[Visala]], [[Hamza]], and [[Hoyt]] as potential candidates. Waru rejected all three as having "too much baggage."

When Temesgen jokingly suggested a [[Muna]] candidate, Waru took the idea seriously, reasoning that a mediator capable of fostering stability in [[Asgartha]] would be ideal. Temesgen acknowledged that qualified candidates would be few, but Waru suspected Temesgen already had names in mind.
