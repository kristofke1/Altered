# Temesgen

## Overview

Temesgen is a politician and colleague of [[Waru Toowoom]] who was involved in succession planning discussions for the post-[[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] era.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Succession Planning with Waru

During a gathering at [[The Asterion]], Temesgen discussed successor candidates with [[Waru Toowoom]]. When Waru described seeking someone neutral who could inspire people and maintain harmony, Temesgen suggested [[Visala]], [[Hamza]], and [[Hoyt]]. Waru rejected all three as having too much baggage and expressed a preference for a new face.

When Temesgen jokingly suggested a [[Muna]] candidate, Waru took the idea seriously, reasoning that a mediator capable of fostering stability would be ideal. Temesgen acknowledged that qualified candidates would be few, but Waru suspected he already had names in mind.
