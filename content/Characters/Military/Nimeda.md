# Nimeda

Nimeda is a General stationed aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Hunt Strategy Meeting

Nimeda attended the staff meeting aboard [[The Ouroboros]] to devise strategy for hunting the [[Kraken]] in [[Caer Oorun]]. When [[Vong]] proposed dividing forces into four cardinal contingents, Nimeda opposed the plan, arguing the [[Kraken]] was not running but waiting patiently for a mistake—and dividing troops was exactly the misstep it awaited. She speculated that if the creature was anything like a giant squid, it might be able to camouflage, explaining why [[Wingspan]]'s aerial reconnaissance had failed to locate it.
