# Sidqi

## Overview

Sidqi was a [[Bravos]] Pendekar and veteran warrior who died in 393 AC during an expedition in the [[City of Scholars]], sacrificing himself to allow younger members of his group to escape shadow entities.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, June-September - Sacrifice in City of Scholars
Sidqi jumped into combat against shadow entities to create an opening for retreat, suffering deep wounds while [[Aoife]] joined him and manifested the gift of the Phoenix to drive the shadows away.

## Relationships

- **[[Basira al-Arshad]]**: [[Bravos]] Exalt who led the expedition (393 AC)
- **[[Aoife]]**: [[Bravos]] warrior who joined his fight and manifested Phoenix powers (393 AC)
- **[[Kaizaimon]]**: [[Basira al-Arshad|Basira]]'s Alter Ego who fought alongside (393 AC)
