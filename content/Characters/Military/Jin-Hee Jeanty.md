# Jin-Hee Jeanty

Jin-Hee Jeanty is a Commodore in the [[Ordis]] forces responsible for air transport operations.

## Military Role

Jeanty holds the rank of Commodore and oversees air transport within the [[Ordis]] military structure.

## Timeline

### 391 AC - Recruit Inspection

Jeanty attended recruit inspection alongside Admiral [[Temera Singh]] and [[Sigismar Synd]], where Sigismar introduced recruit [[Nosk]] to Jeanty as recruits stood to attention in the mess hall.

### 393 AC - Crow's Eye Discovery

Commodore Jeanty presented [[The Crow's Eye]] discovery to Admiral [[Temera Singh]] aboard [[The Ouroboros]], having named the circular crater containing an abandoned underground city. Jeanty reported the deployment of approximately forty probes to map the city's depths and expressed confidence that the site was the City of Scholars, a key milestone for locating [[The Tumult]]'s source. Admiral Singh assigned Jeanty to personally supervise construction of a surface outpost in preparation for descent operations.

### 393 AC - Witnessing The Hunger's Destruction

Jeanty was present on [[The Ouroboros]] deck when [[The Hunger]] was destroyed in [[The Maze]] below. When a second column of light rose in the distance—leagues beyond [[The Screed]]'s cliffs—identical to the first pillar that had erupted from the abyss, Jeanty could not stifle a gasp as his eyes fixed on the horizon.
