# Sunisa

Sunisa is an [[Ordis]] Ensign who serves as aide-de-camp to [[Sigismar Synd]]. She is the daughter of a technomancer and grew up moving between [[Hadera]], [[Arkaster]], and [[Svarograd]]. She joined [[Ordis]] motivated by desire to protect others after witnessing [[Ordis]] troops defend striking miners during childhood.

## Background

### Family and Origins

Sunisa's family moved frequently during her childhood, spending time in [[Hadera]] and [[Arkaster]] before settling, though originally from [[Svarograd]]. Her father worked as a technomancer, giving her an analytical approach that she described as making her sound more like [[Axiom]] than [[Ordis]]. She admitted to not being particularly well-versed in magic.

### Hadera Strikes

As a child, Sunisa witnessed strikes in [[Hadera]] between the Quarrymen's Guild and miners that nearly spiraled out of control. She remembered heated discussions in the family kitchen, demands, and gatherings in warehouses during the tense period. When [[Ordis]] troops arrived, everyone feared they would crush the movement, but the opposite occurred—[[Ordis]] secured the area without raising a hand against strikers, and the negotiator sent by [[The Monolith]] witnessed working conditions and defended the miners. This experience motivated Sunisa to join [[Ordis]] with a specific desire to protect others, unlike those who joined for bureaucratic careers.

## Military Service

Sunisa holds rank of Ensign in [[The Aegis]] forces. She is described as cheeky and never at a loss for words, possessing a sharp tongue. Her file contained a record of insolence that would have disqualified her application with other reviewing officers, but her impertinence and independence were what won [[Sigismar Synd]] over when selecting an aide-de-camp. [[Wingspan]] also liked her. [[Sigismar Synd|Sigismar]] would jokingly ask what she was doing in [[Ordis]] when she could have applied to [[Bravos]], where her attitude would have been a better fit.

## Timeline

### 391 AC - Recruit Assessment and Selection

Instructor [[Gulrang Oduro]] evaluated Sunisa as having natural ease and officer potential alongside [[Amenisa]] and [[Osoyo]], and her top-of-class performance led [[Sigismar Synd]] to personally request her as his assistant from Admiral [[Temera Singh]].

### 391 AC - Transporter Study Mission

Sunisa accompanied [[Sigismar Synd]] for weeks as he studied transporters, buildings, and personalities at [[Wright Altiport]] for [[Alteration]] memory creation, observing two colossal craft from [[Verbera]] and [[Sojourn]] requisitioned for [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

### 392 AC - Tumult Creature Battle

Sunisa covered left flank during battle against [[The Tumult|Tumult]] creatures, using spear and targe shield while fighting alongside [[Sigismar Synd]] and [[Wingspan]].

### 393 AC - Cais Adarra Eastern Flank

Sunisa led troops up the eastern flank of [[Cais Adarra]] through intense blizzard conditions while [[Sigismar Synd]] covered the rear, using her shield for protection against glacial wind.

### 393 AC, February - Assignment to Yzmir Expedition at Storvhit

Sunisa received orders assigning her to [[Afanas Korovinovich]]'s [[Yzmir]] expedition at [[Storvhit]] despite serving as [[Sigismar Synd]]'s aide-de-camp. When questioned about the unusual cross-faction assignment, she responded that it was not her place to question orders. She understood the expedition objective was to pull a device from [[The Aether]] to detect ideas in the region—a Tumult Observatory—though she was not well-versed in magic. During downtime, she maintained camp duties and wrote reports on large parchment.

### 393 AC, February - Shared Camp with Bravos Expedition

Running into [[Atsadi]]'s rival [[Bravos]] expedition was accidental, but given harsh weather, both [[Alterers]] agreed to set up shared camp for the night. At the fire, Sunisa spoke with [[Bravos]] scout [[Sunniva Vavcarezza]], noting the similarity of their names. When asked about her background, Sunisa explained her family's geographic origins, her father's technomancer profession, the [[Hadera]] strikes, and her motivation for joining [[Ordis]]. She accepted half an energy bar from [[Sunniva Vavcarezza|Sunniva]] during their conversation.

### 393 AC, February - Belisenki Attack at Storvhit

When [[Belisenki]] triggered [[Yzmir]] alert wards around the camp perimeter, Sunisa immediately responded to the sound of countless tiny bells signaling a breach. She helped a companion fasten brigandine armor as [[Muna]] mages took positions. During the attack, temperature dropped several degrees instantly as the [[Belisenki]] swarm attacked and wind sent snow swirling, making it difficult to maintain weapon grip. Sunisa fought back-to-back with a [[Yzmir]] Initiate, drifting meters from the main group. When her companion was struck and dazed with [[Belisenki]] approaching, Sunisa slammed into the creature with her large targe shield, sending it tumbling into snow, then stood over the fallen scout extending her hand. Before the rescue could complete, [[Afanas Korovinovich]] unleashed a massive [[Alteration]] shockwave that hurled Sunisa and her companion into the air, and she was lost from sight as torrents of snow blasted in all directions while [[Yzmir]] mages raised magical barriers.

### 393 AC, June-August - Gestalt Communication with Sigismar at The Screed
Sunisa mentally connected with [[Sigismar Synd]] via [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]] during an Ordis roll call at [[The Screed]], ranting about ways to cook the annoying cockatoo Crier.

### 393 AC, June-August - Watching Recruitment Video Filming
Sunisa watched [[Sigismar Synd]] film a recruitment video with [[Nosk]] operating the camera, laughing when he flubbed the closing line.

### 393 AC - Demobilization and Recovery

Sunisa spent one month on involuntary demobilization after injuries from the avalanche and [[Afanas Korovinovich]]'s tornado-like shockwave, handling administrative requests at [[Town Square]] while her leg remained stiff, spending time at [[The Mess]] with [[Sunniva Vavcarezza]] and [[Catalina]]'s group before receiving a certificate of fitness to return to active duty.
