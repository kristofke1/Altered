# Kakoba

## Overview

Kakoba is the Legion Commander of [[The Aegis]], the military branch of the [[Ordis]]. He is a well-rounded soldier-scholar and strong supporter of Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]], known as a wise, bold, and stubborn politician with a tendency to provoke opponents and ambush them by exploiting their tactical mistakes.

## Timeline

### 386 AC - Exalt Selection Meeting

Kakoba attended a meeting at [[The Monolith]] as the Aegis representative alongside representatives from the other three branches of the [[Ordis]]: the [[Frog Prince]] (representing [[The Libra]]), [[Cestimir]] (Chief Curator of [[The Sanctum]]), and [[Nassefa]] (of [[The Armarius]]). The four representatives deliberated to confirm the selection of the first three Exalt pairs to begin the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] ahead of the [[Expeditionary Corps]]: [[Gulrang Oduro]] and [[Tocsin]], [[Sigismar Synd]] and [[Wingspan]], and [[Waru Toowoom]] and [[Mack]].

### 387 AC - Chess Match with Sigismar

Kakoba met with [[Sigismar Synd]] for a chess game that served as both interview and audition. The match was more than a mere game—it was a way for them to introduce themselves and reveal who they really were.
