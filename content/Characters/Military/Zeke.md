# Zeke

Zeke is a [[Bravos]] wingsuit jumper serving with [[The Ascalon]].

## Role

Zeke serves as a key part of [[The Ascalon]]'s training operations alongside [[Chalbo]] and [[Magda]]. Using [[Aerolith]] belts, jet packs, and parachutes, wingsuit jumpers act as both bait and beaters during Leviathan hunts.
