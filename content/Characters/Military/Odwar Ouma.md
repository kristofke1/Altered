# Odwar Ouma

## Overview

Odwar Ouma is a [[Bravos]] hunter serving with [[Afena]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October - Volunteering for Halua Hunt

Following the destruction of [[AES Sune]] by [[Halua]], Odwar Ouma declared that he and [[Afena]] would join the hunt when [[Sol]] rallied hunters in the mess hall.
