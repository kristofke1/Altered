# Nzinga

Nzinga is a boatwoman who operates a yawl in the canals of [[Arkaster]].

## Background

Nzinga navigates the waterways of [[Arkaster]] with expert skill, knowing which channels to take and which to avoid. She makes deliveries for the Svarog Market, including Amorgand silks. She is acquainted with [[Venka]] and they share a drinking history.

## Physical Description

Nzinga has an ebony face and closely cropped hair. She pulls back her hood to reveal her features when speaking with acquaintances.

## Timeline

### 368 AC - The Proclamation

Nzinga was at Drapers' Square when [[Venka]] spotted her during the commotion surrounding the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] announcement. She transported Venka to [[Congers' Wharf]] via her yawl, maneuvering through the [[Bagnante]] despite small craft being prohibited, slipping through narrow channels and beneath dock spans. Along the way, the two listened to Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]'s proclamation about the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. When Venka confirmed he would enlist to settle a score with [[The Tumult]], Nzinga remarked she would need to find a new drinking companion.

- **[[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]**: Basileus whose Proclamation Nzinga listened to (368 AC)
