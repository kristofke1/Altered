# Angus

## Overview

Angus is an [[Axiom]] demolition expert who lost one arm in a previous incident. He places explosive charges during [[Aerolith]] mining operations in the [[Cloud Realm]], analyzing deposits to determine optimal placement for revealing veins without triggering chain reactions.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Aerolith Mining Demolition

Angus placed charges along crevices in an [[Aerolith]] deposit, determining safe locations for detonation that would reveal veins without causing uncontrolled chain reactions.

### 393 AC - Controlled Detonation

Angus conducted a controlled [[Aerolith]] detonation, selecting a safe observation location far enough from the blast zone.
