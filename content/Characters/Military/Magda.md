# Magda

Magda, also known as Magdalena, is a [[Bravos]] wingsuit jumper and flier serving with [[The Ascalon]].

## Role

Magda serves as a wingsuit jumper alongside [[Zeke]] and [[Chalbo]]. The trio use [[Aerolith]] belts, jet packs, and parachutes to act as both bait and beaters during Leviathan hunts. They can open their sails to suddenly slow down, then discard them to become hunters instead of prey.

## Personality

When asked why she resents [[Leviathans]], Magda claims she has nothing against them and simply enjoys the thrill of the big jump and the euphoria of diving. [[Sol]] recognizes this as a protective shell to prevent dwelling on painful memories—armor worn to appear stronger than she truly is. During freefall, Magda pushes aside pain, doubts, and bad memories to focus only on the descent.
