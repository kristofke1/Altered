# Temera Singh

## Overview

Temera Singh is the Grand Admiral commanding the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], a pan-faction exploration initiative conceived by Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] and formally proclaimed in 392 AC. She directs the [[Expeditionary Corps]] operations into the [[Terra Incognita]], coordinating forces from all six factions aboard [[The Ouroboros]]. Singh is not a member of any Faction, a quality that was a decisive factor in her appointment, ensuring the neutrality of the Rediscovery Endeavor. She is implicated in a sealed conspiracy connected to the [[Mesektet]] crash that killed scientist [[Madelyn Moss]], with information about both [[Cebir|the Cebir]] and the Mesektet sealed by the admiralty.

## Background

### Mesektet Conspiracy

Prior to commanding the Rediscovery Endeavor, Singh was among high-ranking admiralty officials who regularly visited the home of [[Madelyn Moss]] and [[Bai Shan-shu]] in [[Hadera]] to discuss secret projects behind closed doors. Through [[The Musubi]] with [[Zephyr]], [[Zhen]] later witnessed fragmentary memories identifying Singh as one of the visitors involved in the conspiracy. Information about both the Cebir and the Mesektet has been sealed by the admiralty. Singh now leads the Rediscovery Endeavor alongside [[de Graaf]], making both figures difficult for Zhen to approach in her investigation.

## Timeline

### 390 AC - Recruitment by the Basileus

Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] personally recruited Admiral Singh to lead the [[Expeditionary Corps]] during a private meeting at [[The Asterion]]. [[Abelen Sundström]] put forward Singh's name, and the [[Trusted Council|Privy Council]] voted mostly in her favor among all candidates proposed. Her status as unaffiliated with any Faction tipped the scales, ensuring the neutrality of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

### 390 AC - Haven Visit

Admiral Singh conducted an official visit to [[Haven]] Bastion with a security cordon established to control access, during which a [[Phoenixian Guard]] messenger's attempt to deliver a message led to a confrontation with [[Silje]], a Haven bouncer maintaining the security perimeter.

### 392 AC - The Cebir Reclamation Directive

Admiral Singh issued a directive to reclaim [[Cebir]] fortress in [[Caer Oorun]] to plan exploration of the western coastal region beyond the [[Turbulent Shores]], and insisted that [[Waru Toowoom]] be accompanied by a small contingent during his journey to the [[Mesektet]] crash site in [[Irundu]].

### 392 AC, September - Battle of Madan Bay

Admiral Singh commanded operations from [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Battle of Madan Bay]], proposing use of The Ouroboros itself as bait to lure the [[Kraken]] into position while acknowledging the final decision rested with [[Esmeralda]], Shepherdess of the [[Kasirga Clan]], as the vessel hosted the civilian Sahanka population.

### 393 AC, January - Jayu Deployment Deliberations

Admiral Singh faced difficult decisions regarding force deployment to the [[Jayu Region]] as the [[Council of Factions]] hesitated given disturbing reports of strange phenomena including rings of light and beams that transformed objects into [[Red Salt|red salt]].

### 393 AC, January 15 - Storvhit Deployment

Admiral Singh documented the [[Storhvit]] deployment where the vanguard faced grueling progress with fatigue evident among [[Exalts]], identifying six distinct biome environments ([[Tundra]], [[Taiga]], [[Frozen Lake]], [[Canyon]], [[Caverns]], [[Spur]]) with the goal of reaching [[Cais Adarra]] as a secure haven against [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities.

### 393 AC, January - Avalanche Rescue Operation

Admiral Singh ordered [[The Ouroboros]] toward a distress signal from a snowy pass where an avalanche had descended into the valley, deploying [[Brassbugs]] from the main hangar via parachute to assist rescue efforts when troop contingents were spread thin due to [[Belisenki]] hostilities.

### 393 AC, February 21 - Belisenki Hostilities

Admiral Singh documented escalating conflict with [[Belisenki]] targeting Corps convoys at [[Storhvit]] using guerrilla warfare tactics, with [[Axiom]] zoologists revealing an eusocial moth-like species exhibiting soldier and field medic castes capable of wielding minor [[Alteration]] to manifest snow and ice phenomena.

### 393 AC, March 5 - Kuraokami Strategic Dilemma

Admiral Singh documented the discovery of [[Kuraokami]], a primordial dragon [[The Oneiroi|Oneiros]] imprisoned at the heart of [[Cais Adarra]], identifying an ethical conflict between [[The Covenant]] First Law requiring protection of all life and [[The Concord]] requiring aid to Oneiroi, and proposed consultation with Faction representatives and a popular vote.

### 393 AC, March 26 - Cais Adarra Base Camp Establishment

Drawing on theories of [[Moyo Chibuye]], the Expeditionary Corps successfully traversed [[Belisenki]] territory and established a makeshift camp at [[Cais Adarra]] foothills, with Admiral Singh remaining alongside ground forces rather than retreating to [[The Ouroboros]] while investigating [[Kuraokami]]'s imprisonment.

### 393 AC, April 2 - The Nilam Discovery

Admiral Singh documented the discovery that [[Cais Adarra]] contains a colossal dead world-tree named [[The Nilam]] with proportions rivaling [[The Spindle]], with [[Muna]] arborists proposing Tenth [[The Refuges|Refuge]] establishment while [[The Asterion]] desired [[Storhvit]] incorporation as [[Caer Nilam]] Province.

### 393 AC, May 15 - Kuraokami Liberation and Caer Nilam Governance

Admiral Singh documented [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation triggering a climate crisis as temperatures rose, selecting [[Jack Frost]] as replacement patronage while granting temporary governance of [[Caer Nilam]] to [[Ordis]] supported by [[Yzmir]] despite [[Muna]] advocacy for natural reserve status.

### 393 AC, May 28 - City of Scholars Arrival and Exploration Delay

The [[Expeditionary Corps]] reached the estimated location of the [[City of Scholars]] after a four-month journey across [[Caer Nilam]] (approximately 250 miles from [[Caer Oorun]] borders), establishing a forward outpost at [[The Screed]] around the [[The Crow's Eye|Crow's Eye]] chasm. Admiral Singh ordered a delay of underground exploration, a decision that generated significant political debate in [[Asgartha]] with Senator [[Muttunbaal]] criticizing the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]'s pace and Senator [[Noya|Noya Kagama]] defending its accomplishments.

### 393 AC, September - The Hunger Confrontation

Admiral Singh observed from [[The Ouroboros]] deck above [[The Crow's Eye]] while Exalts confronted [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]] below. When a pillar of light erupted from the abyss, Singh ordered medics and rescue crews deployed immediately. A second column of light then appeared on the distant horizon beyond [[The Screed]]'s cliffs.

### 393 AC, September 17 - Post-Hunger Aftermath

Admiral Singh documented the aftermath of [[The Hunger]]'s destruction, requesting reinforcements from [[Asgartha]] as the confrontation had proven grueling for Exalts following the exhausting events at [[Caer Nilam]]. The [[Tagmata]] deployed one new Exalt in response. Singh noted that [[Chimerae]] discovered in [[Caer Oorun]] and [[Caer Nilam]] could be bonded through [[The Musubi]], and that rumors spoke of an unregistered Exalt aboard [[The Wayfarer]].

Singh recorded [[The Sanctum|Sanctum]] historians' findings that the city was once called "Serdica" or "[[Sofia]]," named after the soul that manifested there, with [[Yzmir]] theorists identifying the [[Sap]] as a vital fluid responsible for Sofia's awakening. The outpost was renamed "Sofia" in the entity's honor, entrusted to [[Bravos]] for protection and [[Muna]] for provisions. Singh also documented a second luminous signal burning continuously to the southwest, potentially indicating another community or entity, though a violent [[The Tumult|Tumult]] storm blocked the path while the planned route continued northwest toward [[Avalon]].

### 393 AC, October 15 - The Turmoil Crossing

Admiral Singh documented the fleet's crossing of [[The Turmoil]], an endless sea of clouds with the [[The Tumult|Tumult]] roaring beneath. After nearly two weeks of traversal, the 18-ship armada had approximately 150 nautical miles remaining to reach the second signal's source. The cruiser [[AES Concord]] sustained severe hull corrosion from a Tumult pocket explosion and turned back for repairs.

Singh noted a Remanence-like affliction spreading among crews due to prolonged Aether exposure, with [[Rati]] physicians and [[Muna]] experimenters providing treatment. She documented her intention to appoint [[Saskia Averina]] to lead [[The Ouroboros]]' Greenhouse. Multiple lookouts reported a massive shadow beneath the cloud surface following the convoy, potentially a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]].

### 393 AC - The Whiplash

With the fleet only a few nautical miles from the [[pillar of light]], Admiral Singh documented a massive obstacle blocking the armada's final approach: [[The Whiplash]], a double-funnel cyclonic tornado with counter-rotating winds exceeding 500 kilometers per hour. All drones and probes sent through were destroyed, and no passage could be found—beneath lay [[The Tumult|Tumult]] winds of unprecedented magnitude, while the top of the funnel harbored the most violent forces. Singh suspected the barrier was deliberately erected as a protective measure. Major [[Dimuri]] subsequently announced an unidentified visitor requesting an audience with the Admiral concerning the Whiplash.

### 393 AC - Whiplash Crossing and First Contact

Admiral Singh commanded the fleet during a three-hour crossing of [[The Whiplash]], using [[Leviathans]] led by [[Halua]] as a vanguard to pierce the cyclone. The Leviathans consumed [[Mana]] along their path, creating a low-Tumult slipstream that allowed the armada to follow. [[Exalts]] aboard a skiff at the front of the formation protected an unnamed caller who lured the Leviathans through. Both [[The Ouroboros]] and [[The Wayfarer]] sustained superficial structural damage during the crossing, with one of the Wayfarer's balloons bursting.

Upon emerging into the cyclone's eye, the fleet discovered [[Asty]]—a living megalopolis built upon a floating island and a world-tree comparable in scale to [[The Nilam]] or [[The Spindle]]. Singh estimated the city's surface area may surpass [[Arkaster]]. When [[The Reka|Reka]] warships formed a defensive barrier, Singh ordered the Ouroboros, [[Mandjet]], [[Mesektet]], [[AES Horizon]], and [[AES Tempest]] to arm weapons and compute firing solutions. The standoff was resolved when the Reka broadcast a welcome message, translated by a [[Horomancy|Horomancer]] using [[Irises]], addressing the Asgartha fleet as "lost sons and daughters of humanity."
