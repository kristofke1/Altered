Temera Singh is the Grand Admiral commanding the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], a pan-faction collaborative initiative launched by the [[Council of Factions]] in 392 AC. She directs the [[Expeditionary Corps]] operations into the Terra Incognita, coordinating forces from all six factions in the largest exploration endeavor of the Age of Rediscovery.

## Leadership and Authority

Temera Singh commands the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] as Grand Admiral, overseeing operations and conducting official visits to strategic locations and faction representatives. Her rank commands respect within the multi-faction organization, with visits requiring security cordons and coordination among multiple personnel. She deals with sensitive and classified matters, balancing strategic priorities with ethical considerations when confronting difficult decisions.

## Timeline

### 390 AC - Haven Visit

Admiral Singh conducted an official visit to [[Haven]] Bastion with security cordon established to control access, during which a Phoenixian Guard messenger's attempt to deliver a message led to confrontation with [[Silje]], a Haven bouncer maintaining the security perimeter.

### 392 AC - The Cebir Reclamation Directive

Admiral Singh issued directive to reclaim [[Cebir]] fortress in [[Caer Oorun]] to plan exploration of the western coastal region beyond the [[Turbulent Shores]], and insisted that [[Waru Toowoom]] be accompanied by a small contingent during his journey to the [[Mesektet]] crash site in [[Irundu]].

### 392 AC - Kraken Battle Command

Admiral Singh commanded operations from [[The Ouroboros]] during the battle against [[Kraken]] in [[Caer Oorun]], making the bold strategic decision to use [[The Ouroboros]] itself as bait to lure the Leviathan into position while acknowledging final decision rested with [[Esmeralda]], Shepherdess of the [[Kasirga Clan]].

### January 393 AC - Jayu Deployment Deliberations

Admiral Singh faced difficult decisions regarding force deployment to the [[Jayu Region]] as the [[Council of Factions]] hesitated given disturbing reports of strange phenomena including rings of light and beams that transformed objects into [[Red Salt|red salt]].

### January 15, 393 AC - Storhvit Deployment

Admiral Singh documented [[Storvhit]] deployment where vanguard faced grueling progress with fatigue evident among [[Initiates|Exalts]], identifying six distinct biome environments ([[Tundra]], [[Taiga]], [[Frozen Lake]], [[Canyon]], [[Caverns]], [[Spur]]) with goal of reaching [[Cais Adarra]] as secure haven against [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularities.

### January 393 AC - Avalanche Rescue Operation

Admiral Singh ordered [[The Ouroboros]] toward a distress signal from a snowy pass where an avalanche had descended into the valley, deploying [[Brassbugs]] from the main hangar via parachute to assist rescue efforts when troop contingents were spread thin due to [[Belisenki]] hostilities.

### February 21, 393 AC - Belisenki Hostilities

Admiral Singh documented escalating conflict with [[Belisenki]] targeting Corps convoys at [[Storvhit]] using guerrilla warfare tactics, with [[Axiom]] zoologists revealing eusocial moth-like species exhibiting soldier and field medic castes capable of wielding minor [[Alteration]] to manifest snow and ice phenomena.

### March 5, 393 AC - Kuraokami Strategic Dilemma

Admiral Singh documented discovery of [[Kuraokami]], primordial dragon [[Oneiros]] imprisoned at heart of [[Cais Adarra]], identifying ethical conflict between [[The Covenant]] First Law requiring protection of all life and [[The Concord]] requiring aid to [[Oneiros]], proposing consultation with Faction representatives and popular vote.

### March 26, 393 AC - Cais Adarra Base Camp Establishment

Drawing on theories of [[Moyo Chibuye]], Expeditionary Corps successfully traversed [[Belisenki]] territory and established makeshift camp at [[Cais Adarra]] foothills, with Admiral Singh determined to stand alongside ground forces rather than retreating to [[The Ouroboros]] while investigating [[Kuraokami]]'s imprisonment.

### April 2, 393 AC - The Nilam Discovery

Admiral Singh documented discovery that [[Cais Adarra]] contains colossal dead world-tree named [[The Nilam]] with proportions rivaling [[The Spindle]], with [[Muna]] arborists proposing Tenth Refuge establishment while [[The Asterion]] desired [[Storvhit]] incorporation as [[Caer Nilam]] Province.

### May 15, 393 AC - Kuraokami Liberation and Caer Nilam Governance

Admiral Singh documented [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation triggering climate crisis as temperatures rose, selecting [[Jack Frost]] as replacement patronage while granting temporary governance of [[Caer Nilam]] to [[Ordis]] supported by [[Yzmir]] despite [[Muna]] advocacy for natural reserve status.

### May 28, 393 AC - City of Scholars Arrival and Exploration Delay

[[Expeditionary Corps]] reached estimated location of [[City of Scholars]] after four-month journey across [[Caer Nilam]] (approximately 250 miles from [[Caer Oorun]] borders), establishing forward outpost at [[The Screed]] around [[Crow's Eye]] chasm. Admiral Singh ordered delay of underground exploration, a decision that generated significant political debate in [[Asgartha]] with Senator [[Muttunbaal]] criticizing the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]'s pace and Senator [[Noya|Noya Kagama]] defending its accomplishments.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Witnessing The Hunger's Destruction
Admiral Singh paced the deck of [[The Ouroboros]] above [[The Crow's Eye]] while Exalts confronted [[The Hunger]] in [[The Maze]] far below. When a pillar of light erupted from the abyss, spearing the clouds and splitting the sky in yellow inquisitorial glow, Singh looked away from the incandescent column reaching heaven. As the light hung there, Singh experienced fleeting impressions: laughter, joyful dance, corridors gleaming with [[Sap]], inexhaustible happiness, love, freedom—and a little girl's fingers plucking a forget-me-not bloom. When the memory-fragments dissolved and darkness returned, Singh ordered medics and rescue crews deployed immediately. As she began weighing the implications, a second column of light identical to the first rose in the distance, far beyond [[The Screed]]'s cliffs—leagues away—piercing the heavens like a signal sent from an unknown source.

### September 17, 393 AC - Post-Hunger Aftermath Log
Admiral Singh documented the aftermath of [[The Hunger]]'s destruction. The confrontation proved grueling for many Exalts, especially following the exhausting events at [[Caer Nilam]], prompting a request for reinforcements from [[Asgartha]] despite the Exalts' reassuring claims about their condition. The [[Tagmata]] deployed one new Exalt, though normally those remaining in Asgartha defend the Peninsula or are still in training. Singh noted that [[Chimerae]] discovered in [[Caer Oorun]] and [[Caer Nilam]] could be bonded through [[The Musubi]], and that rumors spoke of an unregistered Exalt aboard [[The Wayfarer]] who might contribute to the Corps.

Singh recorded [[Sanctum]] historians' findings that the city was once called "Serdica" or "[[Sofia]]," named after the soul that manifested there. [[Yzmir]] theorists identified the [[Sap]] as a "vital fluid" responsible for Sofia's awakening as a conscious entity. [[Ordis]] historians had hoped to dialogue with Sofia, but the destruction of the [[Tesseracts|memory blocks]] ended that possibility.

The outpost was renamed "Sofia" in the entity's honor, entrusted to [[Bravos]] for protection and [[Muna]] for provisions. Singh also documented the strategic dilemma posed by a second luminous signal burning continuously to the southwest, hundreds of kilometers away, potentially indicating another human community or entity akin to Sofia. However, a violent [[The Tumult|Tumult]] storm blocked the path to the signal's source, while the planned route continued northwest toward [[Avalon]].
