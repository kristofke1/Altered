# Harshad

Harshad is an [[Ordis]] [[The Aegis|Aegis]] Sentinel who serves as a guard at [[The Asterion]].

## Timeline

### 390 AC - Guarding the Basileus's Chambers

Harshad opened the door and stepped aside to let Admiral [[Temera Singh]] pass into Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]'s chambers at [[The Asterion]], nodding in greeting. He announced her arrival in a ceremonial and poised tone: "Eminence, Admiral Singh has arrived." When the Basileus thanked him and dismissed him, Harshad clicked his heels in accordance with protocol, standing at attention before carefully closing the door behind him.

## Relationships

- **[[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]**: Basileus whose chambers Harshad guards
- **[[Temera Singh]]**: Admiral whom Harshad admitted to the Basileus's chambers (390 AC)
- **[[The Asterion]]**: Royal palace where Harshad serves
- **[[The Aegis]]**: Military force Harshad belongs to
