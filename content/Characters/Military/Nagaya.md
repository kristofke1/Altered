Nagaya is a Commodore serving aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Sector Seven Resupply Authorization

During a seven-hour stop at Sector Seven for resupply and decontamination operations, Commodore Nagaya authorized a landing of specialists to collect samples for [[Yzmir]] mages and [[Axiom]] lab technicians.
