General de Graaf serves aboard [[The Ouroboros]] as part of the High Command during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. He is a key figure in a sealed conspiracy connected to the [[Mesektet]] crash and is the subject of an ongoing investigation by [[Zhen]].

## Background

Prior to the Rediscovery Endeavor, de Graaf was among the high-ranking admiralty officials who regularly visited the home of [[Madelyn Moss]] and [[Bai Shan-shu]] in [[Hadera]] to discuss secret projects. Through [[The Musubi]] with [[Zephyr]], [[Zhen]] later witnessed fragmentary memories identifying de Graaf as one of the participants in a cabal — alongside [[Temera Singh]] and [[Basem Falani]] — whose activities were connected to the [[Mesektet]] crash. The information surrounding the crash and the [[Cebir]] was subsequently sealed.

De Graaf formerly employed a personal assistant who later became a prison officer aboard the [[Mandjet]]. This individual was not merely a secretary but a trained "strongbox" — a person conditioned to filter, encrypt, and protect crucial, secret, or illegal information from detection within [[The Gestalt]]. When [[Zhen]] probed the strongbox's subconscious, a discreet warning system installed by the conspiracy's architect was triggered, potentially alerting de Graaf and leading to subsequent attacks on Zhen's life, including an ambush by the Eidolon [[Melusine]] in the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]].

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Sector Seven Provisions Deployment

During the seven-hour stop at Sector Seven, General de Graaf deployed a small team to gather provisions while specialists collected samples for analysis.

### 393 AC - Departure from the City of Scholars

De Graaf was aboard [[The Ouroboros]] with [[Temera Singh]] and the rest of the High Command as the Asgarthan armada departed the [[City of Scholars]] to continue the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] toward the distant pillar of light.
