# Veora

## Overview

Veora is a [[Bravos]] lancer and harpooner serving with [[The Ascalon]]. She carries a kelonic harpoon and kelonic lance.

## Combat Role

Veora operates as an acrobatic harpooner alongside [[Sol]], eschewing ship-based turrets for mobility. Unlike harpooners who stay aboard ships and arm turrets, Veora and Sol operate exposed with minimal armor—at most a padded jacket that hinders movement without providing meaningful protection. Their primary defense is mutual trust and reliance on each other. They have access to various explosive ordinance: incendiary warheads, penetrating warheads, magnetic wave projectiles, and homing missiles.
