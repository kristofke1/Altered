# Chalbo

Chalbo is a [[Bravos]] wingsuit jumper serving with [[The Ascalon]].

## Role

Chalbo serves as a key part of [[The Ascalon]]'s training operations alongside [[Zeke]] and [[Magda]]. Using [[Aerolith]] belts, jet packs, and parachutes, wingsuit jumpers act as both bait and beaters during Leviathan hunts.

- **[[Zeke]]**: Fellow wingsuit jumper
