# Aoife

## Overview

Aoife is a [[Bravos]] Vanguard warrior who manifested the gift of the Phoenix during combat in the [[City of Scholars]], becoming a Fire Avatar and member of the [[Phoenixian Guard]]. She trained for over a decade under [[Basira al-Arshad]] before joining field operations with the [[Expeditionary Corps]].

## Background

Aoife trained under [[Basira al-Arshad]] for over a decade before reaching deployment status with the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. [[Basira al-Arshad|Basira]] identified Aoife as a potential successor capable of leading detachments and serving as an [[Alterers|Alterer]]. Her younger sister [[Catriona]] provides medical care and support within the unit, while [[Chaghagan]] also serves alongside them.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Charting Mission with Basira

Aoife participated in a terrain charting mission with [[Basira al-Arshad]], climbing to a peak elevation to survey and map the landscape while [[Kaizaimon]] and Basira provided [[Alterers|Alterer]] escort.

### 393 AC, June-September - Phoenix Manifestation and Phoenixian Guard Induction

During a shadow entity attack in the [[City of Scholars]], Aoife rushed to help [[Sidqi]] and manifested the gift of the Phoenix — her blades, hair, and eyes catching fire — banishing the shadows and earning induction into the [[Phoenixian Guard]].

### 393 AC, June-September - Inauguration Celebration

Aoife celebrated her [[Phoenixian Guard]] induction at [[The Mess]] with [[Basira al-Arshad]], [[Jubal Okech]], and other [[Bravos]], drinking [[Sap]] beer.
