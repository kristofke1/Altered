# Vassili

## Overview

Vassili is an [[Axiom]] Titan operator who controls heavy machinery using a steering gauntlet. He was too young to remember the [[384 AC Enosha Strikes]]. Despite his slight build, he possesses exceptional proprioception that enables precise control of [[Constructs|Titans]] and heavy machinery.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Titan Operation at Aerolith Quarry

Vassili operated a [[Constructs|Titan]] at an [[Aerolith]] quarry in the [[Cloud Realm]], using a steering gauntlet to control the machine's movements with precision.
