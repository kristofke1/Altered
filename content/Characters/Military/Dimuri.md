Dimuri is a Major stationed aboard [[The Ouroboros]], serving as an escort and briefing officer for Admiral [[Temera Singh]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Military Role

Major Dimuri provides operational briefings and escort services for Admiral [[Temera Singh]]. Dimuri coordinates with senior staff, manages logistics reports, and can invoke [[Alteration]] to summon personnel when needed.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Crow's Eye Briefing

Major Dimuri greeted Admiral [[Temera Singh]] upon her return to [[The Ouroboros]] above [[The Crow's Eye]], providing verbal status reports on kelonic generator output, repairs, resupply operations at Sector Seven, and decontamination procedures. Dimuri escorted the Admiral through the vessel's passageways and assisted with summoning [[Leocardius Sree]] via [[Alteration]].

### 393 AC - The Whiplash Visitor

Major Dimuri announced to Admiral [[Temera Singh]] that an unidentified individual had arrived requesting to speak with her regarding [[The Whiplash]], the double-funnel cyclone blocking the fleet's path to the [[Asty|pillar of light]].
