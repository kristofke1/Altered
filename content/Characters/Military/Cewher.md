# Cewher

Cewher is a General stationed aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Hunt Strategy Meeting

Cewher attended the staff meeting aboard [[The Ouroboros]] to devise strategy for hunting the [[Kraken]] in [[Caer Oorun]]. While Generals [[Vong]] and [[Nimeda]] debated tactics, Cewher remained mostly silent alongside [[Dalsgaard]]. When [[Vong]] compared the search to finding a needle in a haystack, Cewher muttered "A rather large needle."
