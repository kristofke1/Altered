# Rana

## Overview

Rana is an officer stationed aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. Rana was present on the command bridge when the [[Expeditionary Corps]] fleet emerged into [[The Whiplash]]'s eye and discovered [[Asty]] in 393 AC.

- **[[The Ouroboros]]**: Vessel where Rana is stationed
- **[[Temera Singh]]**: Admiral commanding the Ouroboros (393 AC)
