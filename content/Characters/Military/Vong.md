# Vong

Vong is a General stationed aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], noted for his strong political ties.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Hunt Strategy Meeting

Vong attended the staff meeting aboard [[The Ouroboros]] to devise strategy for hunting the [[Kraken]] in [[Caer Oorun]]. He proposed dividing [[The Aegis]] and [[Bravos]] fyrds into four cardinal contingents to survey the Province, arguing this would cover more ground. When [[Nimeda]] opposed the plan, Vong countered that continuing to wander aimlessly would exhaust the Exalts and force a shameful retreat to [[Asgartha]]. After [[Wingspan]]'s reconnaissance failure was reported, Vong sighed that if the best scouts returned empty-handed, they would have no better luck themselves. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] silenced the debate and redirected attention to strategy.

### 392 AC - Observed at Casino

[[Waru Toowoom]] spotted Vong lounging at a table with his close officers in the [[The Ouroboros|Ouroboros]] casino, noting the need to watch him closely given his political ties.
