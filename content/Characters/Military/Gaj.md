# Gaj

## Overview

Gaj is a Rear Admiral in the [[Asgarthan Navy]] and former mentor to [[Zhen]]. He served as [[Zhen]]'s superior during her time as Commodore before she rose to become his peer as Rear Admiral.

## Background

Gaj and [[Bai Shan-shu]], [[Zhen]]'s father, were close friends since their academy days, bound by mutual respect.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

Gaj stood alongside [[Zhen]] aboard [[Mandjet]] during the launch of the Asgarthan armada to investigate the pillar of light signal. He noted that all ships appeared accounted for and that the hour of migration had come. Gaj acknowledged that [[Zhen]] would honor her father and expressed regret that [[Bai Shan-shu]] was not present to see her consecration.

## Relationships

- **[[Zhen]]**: Former subordinate (as Commodore), now peer as Rear Admiral; former mentee
- **[[Bai Shan-shu]]**: Close friend since academy days (deceased)
- **[[Zephyr]]**: Noted that a part of Shan-shu lives on through the Chimera
- **[[Mandjet]]**: Ship from which Gaj observed fleet launch (393 AC)
- **[[Amiens]]**: Fellow Rear Admiral commanding [[Mesektet]]
