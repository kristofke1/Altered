# Dalsgaard

Dalsgaard is a General stationed aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Hunt Strategy Meeting

Dalsgaard attended the staff meeting aboard [[The Ouroboros]] to devise strategy for hunting the [[Kraken]] in [[Caer Oorun]]. While Generals [[Vong]] and [[Nimeda]] debated tactics, Dalsgaard remained mostly silent alongside [[Cewher]], rubbing his temples before shaking his head nonchalantly. When [[Sigismar Synd]] proposed using bait to lure the [[Kraken]], Dalsgaard noted that the strategy made sense but doubted anyone would relish being paraded like bait on a hook. He also observed that the Exalts' primary mission was to stabilize areas so scouts could operate there.
