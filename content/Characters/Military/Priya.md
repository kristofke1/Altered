# Priya

Priya is a [[Bravos]] lancer and signaler serving with [[The Ascalon]].

## Role

Priya locates [[Leviathans]] through cloud cover regardless of visibility, using her talents to track their positions. She deploys flares to signal operations and communicates with support vessels.

## Personal Effects

Priya wears a locket around her neck, hidden under multiple scarves, which she kisses before deployments. The locket likely contains a photo of a loved one and represents her personal reason for hunting Leviathans.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Aerolith Deposit Survey

Priya spent several days showing [[Axiom]] crews where flying stone deposits were located before being assigned to guide [[AES Tempest]] to an ambush site against [[Halua]].

### 393 AC - Supply Signal

Priya signaled an [[Axiom]] towboat's descent with a flare, marking the arrival of supplies for the next phase of operations against [[Halua]].
