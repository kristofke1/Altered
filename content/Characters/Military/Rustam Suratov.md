Rustam Suratov is the Ordis spokesperson who served aboard the Ouroboros during the 392 AC expedition to Caer Oorun.

## Role on the Ouroboros

Rustam Suratov served as the spokesperson for the Ordis Faction during the military operation to hunt and destroy the Kraken in 392 AC. As the Ordis representative in the command structure, Rustam participated in strategic planning meetings aboard the Ouroboros.

During a crucial staff meeting where Admiral Temera Singh proposed using the Ouroboros itself as bait to draw out the Kraken, Rustam spoke up to clarify the chain of command. Rustam reminded the assembly that while Admiral Singh commanded the vessel, the final decision rested with Esmeralda, the Shepherdess of the Kasirga Clan, as the Ouroboros was hosting the civilian Sahanka population.

This intervention highlighted the complex political dynamics aboard the vessel, where military authority intersected with the rights and autonomy of the Eidolon-led nomadic community.
