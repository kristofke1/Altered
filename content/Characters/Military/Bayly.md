# Bayly

Bayly is Commodore in Ordis forces responsible for air transport operations. He was annoyed by requisition of transporters for Rediscovery Endeavor.

## Military Role

Bayly holds rank of Commodore and oversees air transport operations. His responsibilities include management of colossal carrier transporters used for mass personnel and equipment movement.

## Transporter Fleet

### 391 AC - Requisition for Rediscovery Endeavor

Two colossal carrier craft were requisitioned as part of Rediscovery Endeavor. These transporters came from Verbera and Sojourn. Requisition occurred much to Commodore Bayly's annoyance.

Sigismar studied one transporter at altiport in preparation for summoning it inside Tumult. Transporter was described as gigantic shape - flying city of gargantuan proportions. It had stylized golden wings on hull and towers with angular edges.

In few months, transporter would escort Ouroboros and be home to hundreds of Expeditionary Corps adventurers. Three ships would face enormity of unknown together.

## Response to Requisition

Bayly's annoyance at requisition suggests transporters were needed for other operations or that removal disrupted existing air transport schedules. Despite annoyance, requisition proceeded as part of Rediscovery Endeavor preparations.
