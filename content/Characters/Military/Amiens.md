# Amiens

## Overview

Amiens is a Rear Admiral in the [[Asgarthan Navy]] who commands [[Mesektet]], the Night Barque.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Expeditionary Fleet Launch

Rear Admiral Amiens commanded [[Mesektet]] as it led the Asgarthan armada during the fleet launch to investigate the pillar of light signal over the [[Tumult Depression]]. Under Amiens' command, Mesektet took the lead position in the fleet formation.
