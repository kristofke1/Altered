# Itoro

## Overview

Itoro is [[Lyra]] calligrapher and inkcaster of [[Kasirga Clan]] who serves aboard [[The Ouroboros]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Tumult Wave Parasitization and Treatment
[[The Tumult|Tumult]] wave hit [[The Ouroboros]] causing parasitization to crew including Itoro, who sustained severe alterations from grafted ideas "Ribbon" and "Rubber"—leg became limp and elastic, left hand lost fingers replaced with laces of silky flesh. [[Akesha Adhikari]] treated Itoro using [[Sigils]] to stimulate metabolism, performed diagnostics through Irises to examine grafted ideas, then tapped into [[Esmeralda]]'s memories of Itoro's original appearance to guide restoration while [[Taru]] used tentacle dexterity to isolate and remove intrusive ideas.
