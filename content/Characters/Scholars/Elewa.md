Elewa was one of ten elite Battle Mages of [[The Kadigir]] who participated in the [[Battle of Madan Bay]] in 392 AC.

## Battle of Madan Bay

In 392 AC, Elewa was among the most powerful [[Initiates]] of [[The Kadigir]] deployed in the [[Battle of Madan Bay]]. Elewa joined nine other illustrious Battle Mages in forming a circle hovering around the imprisoned Leviathan, concentrating their Mana to call down a star from the sky.

The Mages traced complex Signs in the air to shield themselves with protective Sigils and imbued Senka with power to ascend toward the heavens and create a meteor strike. However, an unseen assailant from the horizon struck the comet mid-descent, causing it to disintegrate into blazing fragments that rained down on the assembled forces and freed the Kraken from its plant prison.

Elewa's fate following the meteor's destruction and the Kraken's violent counterattack remains uncertain.
