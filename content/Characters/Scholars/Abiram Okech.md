Abiram Okech is an [[Ordis]] historian and the twin brother of [[Jubal Okech]], a [[Bravos]] relic hunter.

## Background

Abiram and his twin brother [[Jubal Okech]] work in complementary capacities despite belonging to different factions. Jubal retrieves artifacts in the field while Abiram analyzes them, forming a collaborative partnership. Admiral [[Temera Singh]] encountered Abiram at refined gatherings at [[The Asterion]].
