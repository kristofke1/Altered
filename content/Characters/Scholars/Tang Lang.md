# Tang Lang

## Overview

Tang Lang is a professor at [[Arkaster]] University who delivered a public lecture on [[Sap]] applications in 393 AC. He compiled research from the [[City of Scholars]] expedition into a comprehensive presentation covering all faction uses of the substance.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Sap Lecture at Arkaster University

Tang Lang delivered a lecture at the Maxima Amphitheater of [[Arkaster]] University to a capacity crowd on the various applications of [[Sap]] discovered at the [[City of Scholars]]. Using photographic data from [[Lyra]] correspondents, he presented [[Axiom]] energy applications, [[Bravos]] culinary uses, [[Muna]] agricultural methods, [[Yzmir]] magical enhancements, and [[Lyra]] artistic applications. He noted [[Ordis]] caution regarding the substance and their theory that the ancient civilization's disappearance may have resulted from excessive Sap consumption. After the lecture, an [[Ordis]] bureaucrat confronted him about his eagerness to present first and suggested he showed inappropriate enthusiasm, warning him to exercise more restraint rather than praising Sap as a panacea.
