# Madelyn Moss

## Overview

Madelyn Moss was a scientist specializing in [[Chimerae|Chimera]] research who worked at [[Cebir|The Cebir]] and later in [[Hadera]]. She was the mother of [[Zhen]] and wife of [[Bai Shan-shu]]. Madelyn died in an explosion on the scientific bridge of [[Mesektet]] during an attack by [[Cingula]], though her husband's deathbed confession suggested the tragedy involved a deeper conspiracy.

## Research at The Cebir

Madelyn conducted studies on [[Chimerae|Chimeras]] at [[Cebir|The Cebir]] between 327 and 329 AC, when the creatures were considered dangerous. The remote outpost served as both military fortress and experimental laboratory for Asgarthan forces. There she met [[Bai Shan-shu]], an [[Ordis]] lieutenant beginning his military career. When the base was abandoned in 329 AC, Madelyn was permitted to keep one Chimera specimen—a swan that would become [[Zephyr]].

## Family Life in Hadera

The couple settled in [[Hadera]], [[Caer Esvander]], where their daughter [[Zhen]] was born in 330 AC. Madelyn attempted to introduce Zhen to science, but her daughter preferred outdoor adventures with her father.

Madelyn regularly locked herself in her workshop with [[Zephyr]] for experiments. These sessions involved Mana siphoning, leaving the Chimera exhausted and forced to rest for days. Young Zhen considered Zephyr a family member and objected to this treatment.

## Secret Projects

Madelyn received regular visits from high-ranking admiralty officials to discuss secret projects behind closed doors. These visitors included [[de Graaf]], [[Basem Falani|Falani]], and [[Temera Singh|Singh]]. The classified work created palpable tension between Madelyn and her husband that young Zhen could not understand.

## Death Aboard Mesektet

Madelyn received orders to board [[Mesektet]] to test her invention, while her husband's [[Tagmata]] squadron escorted the mothership. During an attack attributed to [[Cingula]], the Mesektet went down. Madelyn died in an explosion on the ship's scientific bridge.

Years later, [[Bai Shan-shu]] confessed on his deathbed that he had caused the Mesektet's crash and was responsible for all the deaths. He mentioned Madelyn's name when asked why, then refused to elaborate. Through [[The Musubi]] with [[Zephyr]], [[Zhen]] later witnessed fragmentary memories of experiments and conspiracy involving the admiralty officials who had visited their home.
