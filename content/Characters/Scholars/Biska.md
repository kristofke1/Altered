Biska was one of ten elite Battle Mages of [[The Kadigir]] who participated in the [[Battle of Madan Bay]] in 392 AC and was struck by burning debris when the meteor strike was intercepted.

## Battle of Madan Bay

In 392 AC, Biska was among the most powerful [[Initiates]] of [[The Kadigir]] deployed in the [[Battle of Madan Bay]]. Biska joined nine other illustrious Battle Mages in forming a circle hovering around the imprisoned Leviathan, concentrating their Mana to call down a star from the sky.

The Mages traced complex Signs in the air to shield themselves with protective Sigils and imbued Senka with power to ascend toward the heavens and create a meteor strike. However, an unseen assailant from the horizon struck the comet mid-descent, causing it to disintegrate into blazing fragments.

During the resulting storm of debris, Biska was smacked by a burning shard. The impact of the falling meteor fragments not only endangered the Battle Mages but also destroyed the plant prison that had bound the Kraken, allowing the Leviathan to break free and launch a devastating counterattack.

Biska's condition following this injury during the chaotic battle remains uncertain.
