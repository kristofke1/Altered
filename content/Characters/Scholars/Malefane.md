# Malefane

Malefane is the Magister of the Sphere of [[Eidomancy]] within the [[Yzmir]] Faction, sitting on [[The Magisterium]] High Council.

## Role

As Magister of [[Eidomancy]], Malefane presides over one of [[The Spheres|five Spheres]] of [[The Naali]], the [[Yzmir]] alteration style. Eidomancy focuses on the invocation of ideas using Effigies. Malefane oversees the Eidomancy Temple within [[The Kadigir]] and represents the discipline on the High Council that guides the entire Faction.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Summoning Vote

Following the defeat of the [[Kraken]], the [[Yzmir]] Magisters debated whether the creature should be added to the Faction's summoning arsenal or list of prohibitions. Malefane voted in favor of adding the [[Kraken]] to [[Yzmir]] Initiates' summoning capabilities. The vote passed 3-2, with Malefane joining [[Egon]] and [[Chambulla]] in favor, while [[Nasir]] and [[Tarjani]] opposed or advised caution.
