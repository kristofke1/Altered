# Baxsan

## Overview

Baxsan is a prominent [[Ordis]] Runic Scribe known for her extensive [[Glyph]] vocabulary. She is the daughter of a friend of [[Venka]].

## Timeline

### 387 AC - Instructing Gulrang

Baxsan trained [[Gulrang Oduro]] in Glyph vocabulary and practical Glyph dueling after [[Venka]] connected them.

## Relationships

- **[[Gulrang Oduro]]**: Student whom Baxsan instructed in Glyph vocabulary and dueling (387 AC)
- **[[Venka]]**: Friend of Baxsan's parent who connected her with Gulrang
