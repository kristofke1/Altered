# Sister Mathilde

## Overview

Sister Mathilde is an Auxiliary of [[The Cloister]], the [[Yzmir]] medical facility. She is credited with first identifying and naming [[Oblivion]], an affliction causing progressive memory loss among explorers of the [[City of Scholars]].

## Discovery of Oblivion (393 AC)

Sister Mathilde accompanied an expedition into [[The Crow's Eye]] during the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. On the second day of descent into the underground labyrinth, the group experienced intense emotions and [[Chimerae]] attacks. Near the outer limits of the last mapped levels, a malevolent presence manifested, accompanied by visceral fear. During the retreat, specters with emaciated limbs attacked the group. Several [[Alterers]] stood frozen, unable to summon their [[Eidolons]].

Following this expedition, Sister Mathilde conducted a thorough investigation. She consulted expedition reports at [[Town Square]], compiling similar incidents and interviewing affected individuals. Her research identified a pattern: those who ventured deepest into the Crow's Eye developed progressive memory loss, which she named "the Oblivion."

## Recommendations

Sister Mathilde formally recommended:
- Temporary suspension of all expeditions into the depths
- Full examination of all individuals returning from the City of Scholars
- Relocation of the outpost several hundred meters away to establish a quarantine zone

## Contributions to Treatment

Working with [[Rati]] surgeons and Cloister healers, Sister Mathilde documented the treatment protocol using memory [[Grafts]]—psychic fragments donated by loved ones that serve as patches for afflicted minds.
