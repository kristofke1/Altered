# Suha

## Overview

Suha is an Initiate of Third Circle of [[Horomancy]] within [[Yzmir]] Faction who joined [[The Qorgan]] intelligence organization in 392 AC after her investigation into the [[Kraken]]'s defeat uncovered evidence contradicting official accounts. She serves as [[Wanjiru]]'s incarnation vessel during field operations.

## Background

Suha specializes in [[Horomancy]], the magical discipline of temporal perception. She possesses the ability to activate [[Irises]], allowing her third eye to perceive magical dimensions of reality including [[The Aether]], [[Quintessence]], and [[Mana]] flows that anchor beings to the world.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Kraken Investigation

Suha left [[The Kadigir]] to conduct research in [[The Sanctum]] after becoming troubled by inconsistencies in reports about the [[Kraken]]'s fall—while other Leviathans like [[Annoba]] and [[Cadracal]] had documented remains and taxonomic origins, the Kraken supposedly left nothing and lacked proper naming documentation.

### 392 AC - Discovery with Yanna

Suha worked through the night with [[Yanna]], consulting [[Ariadne]] to locate relevant materials, and discovered "Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea" by Jules Verne which referenced "Krakens" as squids—literary rather than taxonomic origin led her to conclude the Kraken was an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] rather than [[Leviathans|Leviathan]].

### 392 AC - Qorgan Recruitment

[[Wanjiru]] intercepted Suha before she could warn a Magister, revealing that [[Yanna]] had been assigned to observe her investigation as a test, and recruited her into [[The Qorgan]] intelligence organization that operates across all six Factions.

### 393 AC, May - Cais Adarra Mission

Suha accompanied [[Wanjiru]] to [[Cais Adarra]] immediately after [[Kuraokami]]'s liberation, channeling [[Mana]] to enable his materialization as incarnation vessel while he recruited [[Afanas Korovinovich]]—Wanjiru's form became completely undetectable even to [[Irises]].
