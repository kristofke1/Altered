# Yanna

## Overview

Yanna is an [[Ordis]] cleric of [[The Sanctum]] and covert operative of [[The Qorgan]] who assisted [[Suha]]'s investigation of the [[Kraken]] in 392 AC while secretly reporting to [[Wanjiru]]. By 393 AC, she operates at [[The Screed]] managing [[Ariadne]]'s manifestation and conducting surveillance of [[City of Scholars]] expeditions.

## Background

Yanna served as a scribe in [[The Anthill]] for three years before her posting to [[The Sanctum]]. As an [[Ordis]] member, she bears the diamond [[Glyph]] tattoo on her shoulder connecting her to [[The Gestalt]]. She wears large glasses.

## Timeline

### 392 AC - Suha Investigation

Yanna approached [[Yzmir]] Initiate [[Suha]] who was researching the [[Kraken]] in [[The Sanctum]], assisting with the investigation while secretly reporting to [[Wanjiru]] as a test of Suha's abilities.

### 392 AC - Qorgan Reveal

[[Wanjiru]] revealed himself to [[Suha]] in [[The Magisterium]], with Yanna stepping forward from behind an obsidian column to confirm her role as [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] operative.

### 393 AC - Ariadne Surveillance Operation

Yanna materializes [[Ariadne]] at [[The Screed]], where the [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] serves as the central knot from which all expedition threads diverge into the [[City of Scholars]] depths, and placed a hidden [[Glyph|Rhombus of the Gestalt]] under Ariadne's tunic to monitor expedition movements through a secret [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]] frequency.

### 393 AC - Secret Legislative Influence

Yanna facilitated passage of secret laws by carefully selected individuals, transmitting directives to clerks ensuring maximum [[The Aegis|Aegis]] support for expedition teams to increase [[Ordis]] presence and [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]] coverage for [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] surveillance.

### 393 AC - Thread Snap Incident

Yanna detected an expedition thread snap after sensing distress, panic, and screams through her [[Coalescence]] connection, then contacted [[Suha]] by connecting through a cadet assigned to protect her.

### 393 AC - Pelagonian Quadrant Expedition

Yanna participated in a scholarly expedition organized by [[Leocardius Sree]] to a floating island in the [[Pelagonian Quadrant]], where ruins sharing architectural style with the [[City of Scholars]] were discovered. She connected to the college of linguists through an Espar to collectively translate four fresco panels documenting the City's inhabitants' escape—revealing they secretly gathered [[Sap]], built a tower with a [[The Nilam|Nilam]] seed, and used [[Aerolith]] to levitate the upper city into the sky. During the expedition, Yanna conducted surveillance on [[Nadir]] under [[The Qorgan|Qorgan]] orders and concluded he was not one of [[The Perjurers]].

### 393 AC - Surveillance of Zhen

Yanna covertly surveilled [[Zhen]], identified two assassination attempts against the Rear Admiral since her visit to [[Mandjet]]'s prison, intervened in the second by using a mind impulse to trip a butler carrying a poisoned drink, and consulted [[Suha]] on recruiting Zhen as an ally.

## Abilities

Yanna is trained in reading body language and non-verbal signals, enabling her to assess individuals' hidden intentions and emotional states through physical observation.

Yanna can push her [[Coalescence]] to descend along psychic links, perceiving through connected [[Ordis]] members' senses and speaking through their voices. She can also use mind impulses to influence physical actions at a distance.

Yanna possesses a secondary psychic bond with [[Suha]], separate from her [[The Gestalt|Gestalt]] connection. She can close her Gestalt link and open herself to this second bond, or mute Suha's presence by barricading it in a corner of her psyche.

Yanna serves as a linguist within [[Ordis]], capable of connecting to the college of linguists through [[The Gestalt]] and [[Heka]] to perform collective translation work. Through [[Coalescence]], multiple linguists can refine texts, debate phrasing, and reach consensus on interpretations.
