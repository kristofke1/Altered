# Edire Niski

## Overview

Edire Niski was engineer and member of Artificers' Guild who invented [[Constructs]], modules used by [[Axiom]] to control [[Alteration]]. Youngest sibling of Niski family alongside sisters [[Aroro Niski]] and [[Nanha Niski]], dedicated himself entirely to sciences while sisters pursued architecture and magic respectively. While often credited with unification of [[Asgartha]]'s technical Guilds under [[Axiom]] banner, achievement truly resulted from partnership with [[Vaike Swiftwater]]'s kelonic energy technology. Following 305 AC tsunami that took sister Aroro's life, Edire and [[Nanha Niski|Nanha]] tasked with rebuilding [[Arkaster]] capital.

## Avesta Energy Conference

Edire witnessed [[Vaike Swiftwater]]'s demonstration at energy conference in [[Avesta]] where she attempted to convince scientific symposium that Kelon could modernize aging Transasgarthan locomotives. This exposure to kelonic energy technology proved crucial to Edire's later vision for [[Asgartha]]'s modernization.

## Capital Rebuilding - 305+ AC

### Post-Tsunami Response

Following tsunami of 305 AC that reshaped economic landscape, Edire and sister [[Nanha Niski]] tasked with rebuilding [[Arkaster]] capital. Edire remembered [[Vaike Swiftwater|Swiftwater]] and [[Anuar Binbasaki|Binbasaki]]'s kelonic energy work from [[Avesta]] conference.

### Vision for New Age

To Edire, Kelon was key to ushering [[Asgartha]] into new age—one where science could meaningfully improve human life. He invited [[Vaike Swiftwater|Vaike]] to [[Arkaster]], to Guilds' headquarters, to lead transition of infrastructure—trams, lighting, and more—to kelonic energy. This partnership between Edire's [[Constructs]] and Vaike's kelonic technology enabled [[Axiom]] to emerge as full-fledged Faction.

## Legacy and Attribution

While Edire often attributed with unification of [[Asgartha]]'s technical Guilds under [[Axiom]] banner and driving Industrial Revolution, achievement truly resulted from [[Vaike Swiftwater]]'s work setting Guilds on path to becoming full-fledged Faction. Partnership between [[Constructs]] technology and kelonic energy created foundation for [[Axiom]]'s rise.
