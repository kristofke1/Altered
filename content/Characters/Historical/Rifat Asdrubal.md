# Rifat Asdrubal

## Overview

Rifat Asdrubal was an explorer who charted expeditions to [[Kirighai]], [[Enosha]], and [[Suspira]]. Along with [[Abelen Sundström]], Rifat Asdrubal is remembered as one of the great explorers whose exploits inspired the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

## Legacy

Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] cited Rifat Asdrubal alongside [[Abelen Sundström]] as explorers whose exploits inspired his childhood dream that eventually became the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].
