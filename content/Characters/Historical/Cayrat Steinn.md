# Cayrat Steinn

## Overview

Cayrat Steinn was the Shepherd of the [[Kasirga Clan]] and grandfather of [[Fen]]. He sought to find [[Mnemosyne]], believing only she could hold the memory of the world and reveal what humanity had forgotten. He died of illness and his body was entrusted to [[The Tumult]], after which he became an [[Eidolon]].

## Background

Cayrat served as Shepherd of the [[Kasirga Clan]], with his family's quarters located at the prow of [[The Ouroboros]]. His hair had turned gray and his skin had become parchment-like over the years. [[Esmeralda]], an [[Eidolon]], lived among the Clan and shared Cayrat's life, remaining perpetually manifested despite him being mortal.

## Death

Cayrat fell ill and became bedridden. When he died, [[Esmeralda]] was at his bedside. Many Clan members gathered at the doorstep, including [[Khaldun]], [[Jakob]], [[Chalinda]], [[Catalina]], [[Ajirai]], [[Boona]], [[Cao]], [[Bastien]], and [[Fen]]'s parents. The Matriarch and venerables came to offer condolences.

## Transmutation

The [[Kasirga Clan]] sailed toward a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity to entrust Cayrat's body to [[The Tumult]]. They laid his body at the edge of the singularity, where it fractured, dissipated, and was carried away by the erratic effluvia.

## Succession Crisis

Before his death, Cayrat named [[Esmeralda]] to take his place as Shepherd. The elders agreed, but the Matriarch of the [[Kasirga Clan]] opposed this decision. The Matriarchs of all Clans conferred and condemned making an [[Eidolon]] the Shepherd, as tradition reserved this role for mortals. They proclaimed an ultimatum: if the [[Kasirga Clan]] persisted, it would be banished from the [[Lyra]] alliance. The Clan was subsequently banished.

[[Esmeralda]] understood that continuing Cayrat's work meant finding [[Mnemosyne]] to rediscover the memory of the world. Through finding [[Mnemosyne]], Cayrat's memory might persist, allowing him to remain as an [[Eidolon]] rather than fading into nothingness.
