# Cayrat Steinn

## Overview
Cayrat Steinn was the Shepherd of the [[Kasirga Clan]] and grandfather of [[Fen]]. He was described as "the great Cayrat Steinn, loved by all within the Clan." He had set his mind on finding [[Mnemosyne]], the mother of the Muses, believing that only she could hold the memory of the world and shed light on all that humanity had forgotten.

## Physical Appearance
His hair had turned gray over the years, and his skin had become parchment-like. Even though he remained a force of nature, he bore the weight of the years. Recently, he had fallen ill to the point of being bedridden.

## Role as Shepherd
Cayrat served as Shepherd of the [[Kasirga Clan]]. His family's quarters were located at the prow of [[The Ouroboros]].

## Relationship with Esmeralda
[[Esmeralda]] lived like the [[Kasirga Clan]], perpetually manifested. She had become one of them by constantly mingling with the Clan. She shared their lives, and especially that of Cayrat, even though he was mortal and she a creature of the imagination. She was still as beautiful and hadn't aged a day.

## Death
When Cayrat died, [[Esmeralda]] was at his bedside. Many Clan members gathered at the doorstep with dull and tearful eyes, including [[Khaldun]], [[Jakob]], [[Chalinda]], [[Catalina]], [[Ajirai]], [[Boona]], [[Cao]], and [[Bastien]]. [[Fen]]'s father and mother were present. The Matriarch and venerables came to offer their condolences.

Cayrat lay there with eyes closed, not moving, with a dull complexion. [[Fen]] looked at her grandfather, waiting for him to sit up, to yawn loudly and burst into laughter at seeing all these mournful faces - but he did none of that.

## Transmutation
The [[Kasirga Clan]] set sail towards a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity to entrust Cayrat's body to [[The Tumult]]. They advanced towards the moiré waves and laid his body at the edge of the Singularity, where it fractured, dissipated, and was carried away by the erratic effluvia of [[The Tumult]].

## Becoming Eidolon
After his death, Cayrat became an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]]. However, his image would risk withering, and nothingness would eventually prevail unless the memory of humanity allowed him to endure in the world of imagination. For Cayrat to withstand the march of time, his memory had to persist in the world. But he was just a simple [[Lyra]] Shepherd, distinguished only by the tranquility of his office.

## Succession
Before his death, Cayrat specifically named [[Esmeralda]] (an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]]) to take his place as Shepherd. The elders agreed with this choice. However, the Matriarch of the [[Kasirga Clan]] opposed this decision.

The Matriarchs of all the Clans conferred and condemned the decision to make an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] the Shepherd. According to tradition, this role was reserved for a mortal, not an imaginary being. The Matriarchs proclaimed a stinging ultimatum: if the [[Kasirga Clan]] persisted in pursuing this path, it would be banished from the [[Lyra]] alliance. The [[Kasirga Clan]] was subsequently banished.

## Esmeralda's Continuity
When [[Esmeralda]] was chosen to take Cayrat's place, she understood that continuity meant finding [[Mnemosyne]], to rediscover the memory of a world that the Age of [[The Tumult]] had seen fade away. This was what Cayrat had always wanted. Through finding [[Mnemosyne]], Cayrat might be able to free himself from the grasp of nothingness, not be forgotten by his peers, and thus remain with [[Esmeralda]] for all eternity.
