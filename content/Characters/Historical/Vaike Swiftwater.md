# Vaike Swiftwater

## Overview

Vaike Swiftwater (259-333 AC) was engineer and pioneer widely regarded as catalyst behind Industrial Revolution and unification of [[Asgartha]]'s technical Guilds under [[Axiom]] banner. Developed kelonic energy technology enabling conversion of Kelon crystals into usable power source. Persists as [[Oneiros]] with [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] still manifesting to those in need sixty years after death. Measured and thoughtful temperament continues shaping [[Axiom]] toward balance and restraint despite showing little interest in politics during lifetime.

## Early Life and Family - 259-266 AC

### Birth and Upbringing

Born 259 AC in [[Sadracca]] shipyards, same year [[Asgartha]] became republic. Grew up far from political fervor of capital during post-[[Severance War]] period. Parents [[Wauna Swiftwater]] and [[Janek Janno]] were simple mechanics owning workshop JSW Mehaanika, preferring relative comfort of workspace to open air. Raised in environment nurturing curiosity and innovation with revolving door of clients - engineers and inventors staying weeks, months, or years developing ideas with couple's mechanical expertise.

### JSW Mehaanika Environment

Workshop hosted visitors from across Peninsula bringing stories from [[Amorgand]], [[Sojourn]], and capital. Life peaceful following years of war as people longed for stability. [[Wauna Swiftwater|Wauna]] and [[Janek Janno|Janek]] - once conscripted to build warships during Civil War - finally able to devote themselves to public works. Young Vaike helped at workshop prepping snacks for workers and assisting with tools, becoming jack-of-all-trades salvaging scrap or discarded parts for own use.

## Discovery of Kelon - 266 AC

### First Encounter

At age seven, Vaike saw Kelon nugget for first time belonging to inventor [[Anuar Binbasaki]]. Binbasaki hoped to use mineral to create new lightbulb type, aiming to modernize capital's street lighting and profit handsomely. Needed to determine whether mineral's natural luminosity could be enhanced. For weeks, [[Wauna Swiftwater|Wauna]] and [[Janek Janno|Janek]] built devices: mirror arrays to concentrate light, contraptions to "excite" material. One experiment caused short circuit frying every generator in workshop, leading Binbasaki to abandon project out of funds and patience.

### Pendant and Early Experiments

Vaike mesmerized by glowing stone received as gift from departing Binbasaki, turning crystal into pendant. Wore throughout childhood idly toying with it or using glow to secretly read at night. As learned trade, desire to uncover Kelon's secrets grew stronger, believing stone held story or mystery trapped inside. Discovered vibrations made it flicker, music caused it to pulse with changing intensity. Cobbled together tiny lab in garage annex, locking herself away whenever possible using pocket money to buy decorative Kelon crystals from local souvenir shops.

## Research Development - 266-292 AC

### Independent Laboratory

Created lab in garage annex bombarding crystals with sound waves and other vibrations. One experiment triggered energy surge nearly blowing up lab, leaving smoke, bent tools, and shattered furniture - confirming she was onto something. Eventually trinkets and paperweights insufficient, requiring more Kelon. After weeks pleading, convinced parents to help fund materials.

### Three Factions War Impact - 288 AC

Outbreak of [[Three Factions War]] in 288 AC stalled research. Conflict didn't reach [[Sadracca]] directly but disrupted supply lines causing severe shortages. Kelon designated low priority resource. Vaike sourced material on black market at steep prices, continuing experiments without pause despite war conditions.

### Breakthrough and Collaboration - 292 AC

After confirming Kelon's energy potential, Vaike reached out to [[Anuar Binbasaki]] asking if he'd like to assist in research. Aging inventor surprised girl still cared about material, agreed to join. In 292 AC with Binbasaki's help, Vaike built casing strong enough to contain Kelon's power and convert into usable energy. Using tanks, cylinders, and pressure vessels, succeeded in taming crystal's force.

### Ordis Occupation - 293 AC

When [[Ordis]] forces invaded [[Sadracca]] in 293 AC launching attack on capital via Cierna, Vaike continued work in secret knowing discoveries would be weaponized. Against all odds, managed to keep experiments hidden even as [[Ordis]] soldiers routinely visited parents' workshop. Maintained secrecy throughout occupation period protecting revolutionary technology.

## Post-War Struggle - 293-305 AC

### Initial Reception

Vaike revealed findings after war ended. Initially inventions gained little traction with industry leaders unconvinced by discoveries. For over decade traveled Peninsula visiting Guilds and showcasing work at invention fairs. Audiences and investors remained skeptical with same critique: cost and difficulty of switching to alternative energy source. Without support, Kelon energy might have faded into obscurity.

### Demonstration Circuit

Traveled extensively across [[Asgartha]] presenting at energy conferences and scientific symposiums. Attempted to convince audiences that Kelon could modernize aging Transasgarthan locomotives and other infrastructure. Faced consistent resistance from established industrial interests. Continued advocacy despite repeated rejections and limited funding.

## Breakthrough and Recognition - 305-333 AC

### 305 AC Tsunami Catalyst

Tsunami of 305 AC reshaped economic landscape creating opportunity for Kelon adoption. Young Artificers' Guild member [[Edire Niski]] had seen Vaike's demonstration at energy conference in [[Avesta]] where she tried convincing scientific symposium about Kelon's modernization potential. When [[Edire Niski]] and sister [[Nanha Niski]] tasked with rebuilding capital after tsunami, [[Edire Niski|Edire]] remembered Swiftwater and Binbasaki's work.

### Arkaster Invitation

[[Edire Niski]] believed Kelon was key to ushering [[Asgartha]] into new age where science could meaningfully improve human life. Invited Vaike to [[Arkaster]] to Guilds' headquarters to lead infrastructure transition - trams, lighting, and more - to kelonic energy. Vaike's measured temperament and technical expertise made her ideal figure to guide revolutionary transformation.

### Industrial Revolution

Vaike's work set Guilds on path to becoming full-fledged [[Axiom]] Faction. Led successful conversion of infrastructure throughout [[Arkaster]] and eventually broader [[Asgartha]]. Transasgarthan railway and other heavy transports converted to powerful sustainable resource. Achievement often attributed to [[Edire Niski]]'s [[Constructs]] invention, but truly Vaike's kelonic energy development enabled Guild unification under [[Axiom]] banner.

## Legacy and Oneiros Status

### Technological Impact

Sixty years after death (as of 393 AC), Vaike Swiftwater remains central figure in [[Axiom]] Faction. Cylinders and batteries she designed still power everything from [[Axiom]]'s smallest tools to airships and kelo-appliances found in everyday Asgarthan homes. Transasgarthan railway and heavy transports rely on her energy systems. Swiftwater's legacy embedded in every layer of society.

### Oneiros Manifestation

Enduring mark allows her to persist as [[Oneiros]], [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] still manifesting to those in need. Measured and thoughtful temperament continues shaping [[Axiom]] toward balance and restraint. Influence preserved through [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] manifestations guiding Faction despite showing little interest in politics during lifetime.

### Future Concerns

With Kelon supplies running low, energy empire may soon be at risk. Only [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] may hold solution to looming scarcity threatening technological infrastructure dependent on kelonic power. [[Suspira]] mining operations represent critical supply source, but deposits becoming increasingly rare according to prospectors.

## Technical Innovations

### Kelonic Energy System

Developed casing technology containing Kelon's power and converting into usable energy. Created tanks, cylinders, and pressure vessels taming crystal's force. Designed batteries and power distribution systems still used throughout [[Asgartha]]. Solved fundamental problem of harnessing Kelon's natural energy emissions for practical applications.

### Infrastructure Applications

Led conversion of:
- Transasgarthan railway locomotives
- Urban tram systems in [[Arkaster]]
- Street lighting throughout capital
- Airship propulsion systems
- Household kelo-appliances
- Workshop generators and tools

## Personality and Character

Measured and thoughtful temperament continuing to shape [[Axiom]] toward balance and restraint. Showed little interest in politics during lifetime, focusing on technical innovation and practical applications. Patient advocate persisting through decade of rejection before achieving recognition. Curious nature from childhood driving lifelong pursuit of understanding Kelon's mysteries. Pragmatic approach to problem-solving inherited from parents' mechanical workshop environment.

## Eidolon Activities

### ~393 AC - Storvhit Snowy Pass Expedition

Vaike's [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] led party through snowy pass at [[Storvhit]] using heat cannon to melt thick snow, and helped [[Treyst]] convert a kelonic heater into a more useful heat gun for the expedition.
