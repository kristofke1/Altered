# Vaike Swiftwater

## Overview

Vaike Swiftwater (259–333 AC) was an engineer who developed kelonic energy technology, enabling conversion of [[Kelon]] crystals into usable power. Her work catalyzed the Industrial Revolution and the unification of [[Asgartha]]'s technical Guilds under the [[Axiom]] banner. She persists as an [[Oneiros]], with her [[Eidolon]] still manifesting to those in need.

## Background

Born 259 AC in the [[Sadracca]] shipyards, the same year [[Asgartha]] became a republic. Her parents [[Wauna Swiftwater]] and [[Janek Janno]] owned workshop JSW Mehaanika, hosting engineers and inventors who stayed for weeks or months developing ideas. Young Vaike helped at the workshop and salvaged scrap parts for her own use.

## Timeline

### 266 AC - Discovery of Kelon

At age seven, Vaike saw a [[Kelon]] nugget belonging to inventor [[Anuar Binbasaki]], who hoped to use the mineral for a new lightbulb type. After experiments failed and Binbasaki abandoned the project, he gave Vaike the crystal as a gift. She wore it as a pendant throughout childhood and began experimenting with its properties, discovering that vibrations made it flicker and music caused it to pulse.

### 266–292 AC - Research Development

Vaike created a laboratory in a garage annex where she bombarded crystals with sound waves. One experiment triggered an energy surge that nearly destroyed the lab, confirming the crystal's potential. The [[Three Factions War]] (288 AC) disrupted supply lines, but she continued sourcing Kelon on the black market.

### 292 AC - Breakthrough

Vaike contacted [[Anuar Binbasaki]] to assist her research. Together they built a casing strong enough to contain Kelon's power and convert it into usable energy using tanks, cylinders, and pressure vessels.

### 293 AC - Ordis Occupation

When [[Ordis]] forces invaded [[Sadracca]], Vaike continued her work in secret, keeping her discoveries hidden from soldiers who routinely visited her parents' workshop.

### 293–305 AC - Advocacy Period

After the war, Vaike revealed her findings but gained little traction. For over a decade she traveled the Peninsula visiting Guilds and showcasing her work at invention fairs. Industry leaders remained unconvinced by the cost and difficulty of switching energy sources.

### 305 AC - Arkaster Invitation

The 305 AC tsunami reshaped the economic landscape. [[Edire Niski]], who had seen Vaike's demonstration at an energy conference in [[Avesta]], was tasked with rebuilding [[Arkaster]] alongside sister [[Nanha Niski]]. He invited Vaike to the Guilds' headquarters to lead the infrastructure transition—trams, lighting, and more—to kelonic energy.

### 305–333 AC - Industrial Revolution

Vaike's work set the Guilds on the path to becoming the [[Axiom]] Faction. The Transasgarthan railway and other heavy transports converted to kelonic power. The cylinders and batteries she designed still power everything from [[Axiom]]'s smallest tools to airships and household kelo-appliances.

### Post-333 AC - Oneiros Status

Sixty years after death (as of 393 AC), Vaike's [[Eidolon]] still manifests to those in need.

### ~393 AC - Storvhit Expedition

Vaike's [[Eidolon]] led a party through a snowy pass at [[Storhvit]], using a heat cannon to melt thick snow and helping [[Treyst]] convert a kelonic heater into a heat gun for the expedition.

## Future Concerns

With [[Kelon]] supplies running low, the energy infrastructure dependent on kelonic power faces potential scarcity. [[Suspira]] mining operations represent a critical supply source, but deposits are becoming increasingly rare.
