# Aroro Niski

## Overview

Aroro Niski (277-305 AC, awakened as [[Oneiros]]) was [[Yzmir]] mage who mastered all Five Spheres of [[The Naali]], becoming first to detect 305 AC tsunami threatening [[Arkaster]]. She sacrificed herself defending city's southwestern ramparts, holding back Aether-tainted deluge long enough for other [[Alterers]] to mobilize. Her heroic stand drastically reduced human casualties and destruction. Decades later awakened as [[Oneiros]] through collective remembrance, joining [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] to investigate tsunami's origins.

## Early Life and Family

Born 277 AC during reign of [[Assura ruun-Caellach]], Aroro demonstrated exceptional [[Alteration]] abilities from very young age alongside older sister [[Nanha Niski]] and younger brother [[Edire Niski]]. While Nanha dedicated talents to architecture and Edire to sciences, Aroro chose to join [[Yzmir]] seeking to explore hidden mechanisms of world and secrets of [[The Aether]].

## Magical Training and Mastery

### Kinemancy Virtuosity

Natural aptitudes first steered Aroro toward [[Kinemancy]]: with snap of fingers she summoned sparks, with flick of wrist conjured whirlwinds. Like peers, manipulated elements and primal forces of Creation through codified gestures and surgically precise movements. Virtuosity with which she made [[The Aether]] dance was unmatched—some believed she destined for brilliant future within Faction, perhaps even position aspiring to become Magister of Sphere of [[Kinemancy]].

### Multi-Sphere Mastery

Inquisitive and wandering spirit led Aroro to flit from school to school, ever hungry to learn and discover new things. While classmates rose through Faction ranks over years, she chose instead to dedicate herself studying other Spheres of magic, becoming perpetual student of [[The Kadigir]]. One by one mastered [[Gephyromancy]], [[Horomancy]], [[Eidomancy]]... until fully grasped all Five Spheres of [[The Naali]].

What some mockingly dismissed as intellectual gluttony—or refusal to take on real responsibility—ultimately gave her unparalleled understanding of magic. Where others saw dead ends, she envisioned thousand new paths. By weaving synergies between various Spheres, elevated each Discipline, unlocking full power and potential through skillful combination.

### Revolutionary Philosophy

To Aroro, dividing mystical arts into five branches made no sense. Only by studying them all in concert could one hope to uncover world's primordial truth. Hard to say how things might have changed within Faction had she been allowed to further explore mysteries of [[The Aether]]. Perhaps would have founded own school—or even entirely new Discipline.

## 305 AC Tsunami Defense

### Detection and Warning

In 305 AC at just 28 years old, Aroro first to detect disturbance in [[The Aether]] flows—at first just breeze, which she recognized as harbinger of cataclysm unlike anything [[Asgartha]] had endured since founding. Tried to warn peers who attempted to reassure her: archipelago had been protected from [[The Tumult]] for centuries; unthinkable such calamity could strike so deeply within stabilized zone.

Faced with Magisters' disbelief, sought audience with Basileus [[Itzamna ruun-Heeren]]. Waited long hours in halls of [[The Asterion]] hoping for meeting that never came. Tried to persuade [[The Aegis]] sentinels of urgency, begging them to let her through. Said that she turned back, face stricken with dread—perhaps glimpsed horizon through Palace windows, realized time running out.

### Final Stand

When tsunami struck, Aroro already standing on city's outer ramparts at southwestern edge of capital. For hours had been inscribing Sigils along walls, eyes glowing brightly as Irises scanned distance. Around her placed numerous Effigies ready to be summoned at last moment, while voice called to [[The Aether]] which swirled in spiraling eddies beyond [[The Veil]]. Had to work swiftly, sweat pouring from forehead and temples.

When wave of water and [[The Aether|Aether]] crashed into Vagheria foothills, reshaped rocky crests before cascading down mountain slopes and flooding into [[Muir Concordia]]. Around her shouts and cries echoed as people fled desperately seeking shelter. Began performing sequence after sequence of Signs; fingers, hands, entire body adopting postures and forming shapes. Voice thundered, crackling like lightning in clear sky.

### The Deluge

Aroro saw sea swell and ripple, cloaked in turn with vegetation, crystal, fur—cacophony of ideas made manifest. Furious ravenous surge carried thousand overlapping concepts, replacing, entwining, chasing one another in endless cycle of creation and destruction. Flood of ideas tore across land, reshaping terrain with wrath not seen since First City built. Aether-tainted foam rolled toward them, sliding like undertow, like ravenous shark come to devour.

As deluge bore down, Aroro stood alone before [[The Tumult]], drawing on last reserves to slow boiling waves. Summoned [[Eidolons]] with all might, shattering Effigies through sheer force of will. Ikenga, Caellach, Ganesh, Sakarabru, Achilles, Merlin, Hecate, Heshkari, Niavhe, Bastet, Amahle—in all sixteen [[Eidolons]] answered call, lending strength. They empowered voice, gave energy to continue frantic katas, poured power into hers.

### Death and Sacrifice

Tsunami struck with deafening roar. Sigils she layered around herself shattered one by one in blink of eye. Voice drowned and silenced under crashing wave. [[Eidolons]] around her swept away one after another, destroyed by overwhelming force of surge. Broken, on brink of annihilation, Aroro unleashed all energy—all [[Mana]] she gathered—in counterattack as blinding as it was fierce.

Held out several long minutes, chaining posture after posture in dizzying rhythm. Wave broke for time on shield she conjured. Brief time—because eventually silhouette disappeared into merciless flood. But that precious moment allowed other [[Alterers]] to mobilize and organize. Each took responsibility for small section of city, creating pockets of stability around which rolls of [[The Aether|Aether]] crashed and broke.

Though lakefront Districts and much of [[The Asterion]] devastated, Aroro's sacrifice drastically reduced human toll and extent of destruction.

## Legacy and Remembrance

### Immediate Aftermath

As waters receded and humanity began to heal, many survivors honored [[Yzmir]] Mage's sacrifice with thoughts, prayers, and tributes. Her feat spread like wildfire, passed from mouth to mouth, from tavern to temple.

Tsunami left cruel devastation. Basileus [[Itzamna ruun-Heeren]] perished in deluge along with much of government and significant portion of urban population. Rather than give in to despair, humanity proved resilience by celebrating joy of survival. Perhaps to exorcise trauma or honor fallen—Aroro became posthumously embodiment of that resilience, of survivors' will to proclaim to world they were stronger than grief and desperation.

### Memorial Construction

New Basilissa [[Ndidi Dewitt]], hastily elected, ensured statue erected in Aroro's honor towering over waters with one hand outstretched as if to hold back waves. Lighthouse built on small island that served as her final stand—transforming tragic memory into beacon of hope and restored safety.

## Awakening as Oneiros

Likely fervor of remembrance sowed seeds of rebirth as [[Oneiros]]. Memory of that dark day endured for decades, stories people told continued to be passed down through ages. At base of that lighthouse awakened once more to consciousness, recalled from death by power of remembrance—as icon, revered heroine. Turned to young woman who unwittingly summoned her, offering will to withstand all trials life had thrown her way.

### Dark Suspicions

Joining college of Oneiroï, harbored dark thoughts which eventually shared with Faction. Tsunami to her did not feel like random or natural occurrence—but possibly coordinated attack from beyond. This time [[Yzmir]] Magisters did not turn deaf ear and received warning with gravity. Indeed possible wave of destruction had been orchestrated—deliberately crafted to eradicate pocket of civilization.

### Rediscovery Endeavor Mission

Announcement of creation of [[Rediscovery Endeavor|Expeditionary Corps]] was signal she long awaited—chance to investigate tsunami's source: its origin, its architects. Could no longer sit idle in face of faceless enemy. Could not remain passive, vulnerable to another such strike. Though feared what they might find within [[Terra Incognita]], Aroro determined to shed light on event that cost her life—and so many others. Truth needed to come to light if humanity were to have hope of confronting threat.

## Magical Abilities

### Five Spheres Mastery

Aroro achieved rare distinction of mastering all Five Spheres of [[The Naali]]:
- **[[Kinemancy]]**: Elemental manipulation through codified gestures
- **[[Gephyromancy]]**: Bridge-building and connection magic
- **[[Horomancy]]**: Time and temporal manipulation
- **[[Eidomancy]]**: [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] summoning and manipulation
- **Fifth Sphere**: (Specific name not stated)

### Synergistic Casting

Revolutionary approach involved weaving synergies between various Spheres, elevating each Discipline by unlocking full power through skillful combination. Where others saw dead ends, envisioned thousand new paths. Believed dividing mystical arts into five branches made no sense—only by studying all in concert could one uncover world's primordial truth.

### Eidolon Summoning

During final stand summoned sixteen [[Eidolons]] simultaneously through shattering Effigies by force of will. [[Eidolons]] answered call included: Ikenga, Caellach, Ganesh, Sakarabru, Achilles, Merlin, Hecate, Heshkari, Niavhe, Bastet, Amahle. They empowered voice, provided energy to continue frantic katas, poured power into hers.

### Combat Techniques

Utilized Sigils inscribed along walls, Effigies placement for rapid summoning, Irises scanning for enhanced vision, and continuous sequences of Signs and postures. Demonstrated ability to channel massive amounts of [[Mana]], hold shield against tsunami for several minutes while chaining dizzying rhythm of defensive katas.

## Personality

Demonstrated inquisitive and wandering spirit, choosing breadth of knowledge over traditional advancement through Faction ranks. Exhibited determination and courage in face of institutional disbelief, taking unilateral action to defend city when warnings ignored. Willingness to sacrifice life for others reflected deep sense of responsibility despite being dismissed by peers. As [[Oneiros]], driven by need to uncover truth about tsunami and protect humanity from similar threats.
