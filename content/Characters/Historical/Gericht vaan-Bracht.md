# Gericht vaan-Bracht

## Overview
Gericht vaan-Bracht (55-86 AC) was a legendary swordsman known as the Five Blades Swordsman and the last remnant of the [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school. Orphaned at age five, he trained at the monastery until its destruction by [[The Magan]] in 74 AC. He joined the rebellion against [[Ayxas]] and became renowned for his uncanny speed, imposing frame, and skill with multiple blades. He died from wounds sustained during the final assault on the [[Elysium]] in 86 AC and later manifested as an [[Eidolon]].

## Early Life (60-69 AC)

Gericht's parents perished in a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity in 60 AC. At age five, he was placed in the care of the [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school, a secluded monastery on the edge of Vindmark, the eastern frontier of [[Caer Eidos]]. He was entrusted to aging swordsman [[Suddran]], who subjected the young orphan to monastic life of extreme rigor. Gericht performed labor including meal preparation and stable cleaning. He forged bonds with fellow orphans from across the Protectorate. In evenings, he listened to friend [[Nadra]] play lute, occasionally learning from her.

## Disciple Training (64-74 AC)

After four years, [[Suddran]] declared Gericht ready to become disciple. Training transformed to strengthening body and mind, learning blade techniques, and dueling. [[Nadra]] joined apprentice swordsmen's ranks one year after Gericht (65 AC).

## Ugetsu-Fenshen Massacre (74 AC)

Envoys of [[Ayxas]] arrived at school bearing royal decree outlawing the monastery's existence. [[Walela]], the Grandmaster, refused the Kuningas's "request." Attack came following dawn as entire [[The Magan|Magan]] contingent descended upon monastery. Flames consumed dwellings as monks fought fiercely. Resistance slowly crumbled beneath relentless zeal of assailants. [[Suddran]] fell, speared from all sides. [[Nadra]] retreated wounded. Gericht broke ranks attempting to lead [[Nadra]] to safety. Enemy archers struck both with arrows. [[Nadra]] died. Gericht was pierced by arrows and left for dead amid monastery's smoldering ruins.

## Recruitment and Rebellion (74-86 AC)

Eidolon [[Gunn]] tended Gericht's wounds for days amidst charred remains. She had come to collect essence of fallen warriors but stayed when she saw he still clung to life. She offered pact to join rebellion and help end [[Ayxas]]'s tyranny. Gericht accepted to avenge comrades. He mourned at monks' graves. He took [[Nadra]]'s lute, miraculously spared from flames despite scorch marks on wood. Within rebellion, Gericht led merciless raids against Kuningas forces, slaughtering [[The Magan|Magan]] disciples. With each battle, his legend grew as the Five Blades Swordsman, last remnant of [[Ugetsu-Fenshen]] school. He possessed imposing frame with uncanny speed, kohl-lined icy eyes, and swords that danced with unparalleled precision. He cut down Kuningas's finest duelists. Rebellion rallied around him. During rare moments between battles, he played lute with tenderness and skill.

## Death at Elysium (86 AC)

During final assault on [[Elysium]], Gericht fought at vanguard. He attempted to save [[Eskheret ruun-Kurush]] after she breached fortress gates, carrying her battered body from battlefield. She succumbed to wounds. Gericht stormed throne room, halting slaughter upon reaching [[Ayxas]] slumped at foot of dais. He laid down weapons, realized deep wounds lacerating his body, withdrew to quiet corner. He made no call for aid. By time others found him, it was too late. Smile rested upon his lips.

## Legacy as Eidolon

Years after death, Gericht's [[Eidolon]] appeared at fencing schools, drawing blade to test champions. Fencing schools know they will face his sudden visits to prove worth before Five Blades Swordsman.

As [[Eidolons|Eidolon]], Gericht established himself as master of his discipline, having claimed victory over each champion of the fencing schools. He is master of the steel dance, demonstrating virtuosity with unexpected combos and precise blade work. Gericht's signature attack involves drawing circles with his weapons while moving at exceptional speed.

### Dueling Technique

Gericht's [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] form evaluates opponents in slow back-and-forth motion, showing only profile as part of ritual dueling game. He slowly unsheathes two of his sabers then remains motionless before attacking. His signature attack draws circles with weapons, moving so quickly that opponents struggle to understand how he arrived at their position. He intercepts opponents where they land after evasive jumps, forcing reflex dodges and controlling the pace of combat.

During prolonged bouts, Gericht reads his opponents' moves while they attempt to read his, settling into sparring exchanges where both fighters try to land blows. His unexpected combinations test even skilled opponents' ability to counter. Opponents must watch his tensing muscles to anticipate attacks, searching for gaps in his guard while questioning whether apparent openings are genuine or traps.
