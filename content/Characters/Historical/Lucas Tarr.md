# Lucas Tarr

## Overview

Lucas Tarr was a writer who documented horrific accounts about encounters with the [[Kraken]]. These writings provided visceral, detailed descriptions of the creature's devastating impact on those who encountered it.

## Documentation of the Kraken

Lucas Tarr authored horrific accounts about the Kraken that were consulted by military strategists preparing for the 392 AC assault on the Leviathan.

In 392 AC, [[Sigismar Synd]] spent hours poring over everything written about the Kraken, including the horrific accounts of Lucas Tarr alongside the historical anecdotes of [[Gallas Devadas]], searching for weaknesses or advantages to exploit in the planned military operation.

The graphic nature of Lucas Tarr's accounts complemented the historical perspective provided by other writers, offering military planners a more immediate and terrifying understanding of what the Kraken was capable of inflicting on its victims.

## Relationships

- **[[Gallas Devadas]]**: Fellow writer whose historical anecdotes were studied alongside Lucas Tarr's horrific accounts
- **[[Kraken]]**: Subject of the horrific accounts
- **[[Sigismar Synd]]**: Paladin who studied Lucas Tarr's horrific accounts in preparation for the Kraken assault (392 AC)
