Lucas Tarr was a writer who documented horrific accounts about encounters with the Kraken.

## Documentation of the Kraken

Lucas Tarr authored horrific accounts about the Kraken that were consulted by military strategists preparing for the 392 AC assault on the Leviathan. These writings provided visceral, detailed descriptions of the creature's devastating impact on those who encountered it.

In 392 AC, Sigismar Synd spent hours poring over everything written about the Kraken, including the horrific accounts of Lucas Tarr alongside the historical anecdotes of Gallas Devadas, searching for weaknesses or advantages to exploit in the planned military operation.

The graphic nature of Lucas Tarr's accounts complemented the historical perspective provided by other writers, offering military planners a more immediate and terrifying understanding of what the Kraken was capable of inflicting on its victims.
