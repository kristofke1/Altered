# Nanha Niski

## Overview

Nanha Niski was architect and engineer, eldest sibling of Niski family alongside sister [[Aroro Niski]] and brother [[Edire Niski]]. Dedicated talents to architecture while siblings pursued different paths—Aroro joining [[Yzmir]] to master magic, Edire focusing on sciences. Following 305 AC tsunami that reshaped economic landscape and took sister Aroro's life, Nanha and brother tasked with rebuilding [[Arkaster]] capital, creating opportunity for adoption of kelonic energy technology at scale.

## Capital Rebuilding - 305+ AC

Nanha and brother [[Edire Niski]] tasked with rebuilding [[Arkaster]] capital after 305 AC tsunami. As chief-architect, Nanha directed the reconstruction and designed several new districts.

### District Designs

The districts of [[Nafitera]] and [[Kabatas]] were established during the reconstruction under Nanha's direction. Resolutely modern for their time, with wide thoroughfares and spacious streets, these districts served as experimental zones to persuade the rest of the capital to fully transition to [[Kelon]] technology.

[[Kladiver]] was the third district whose plans Nanha sketched during the reconstruction, designed as an extension of [[Sjena]] along [[Sforafjord]].

### Kelon Technology Transition

[[Edire Niski|Edire]] remembered [[Vaike Swiftwater]]'s and [[Anuar Binbasaki]]'s kelonic energy work from [[Avesta]] conference. [[Edire Niski|Edire]] believed Kelon was key to ushering [[Asgartha]] into new age where science could meaningfully improve human life.

Partnership led to invitation of [[Vaike Swiftwater|Vaike]] to [[Arkaster]] Guilds' headquarters to lead infrastructure transition—trams, lighting, and more—to kelonic energy. Rebuilding project served as proving ground for [[Vaike Swiftwater|Swiftwater]]'s kelonic technology, demonstrating practical applications that convinced skeptical industry leaders.
