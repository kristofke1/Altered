Chiziwa was a taxonomist responsible for naming the Leviathan Kaibara.

## Taxonomic Work

Chiziwa named the Leviathan Kaibara to highlight the beauty of its appearances when it would take flight to swim through the clouds. This poetic approach to taxonomic classification reflected both scientific documentation and aesthetic appreciation of the Leviathan's majestic movements.

The documentation of Chiziwa's naming of Kaibara was later cited by Yzmir Initiate Suha in 392 AC during her investigation of the Kraken. Suha used Chiziwa's work alongside Mamello's as evidence that proper taxonomic records should exist for all major Leviathans, noting that the absence of such documentation for the Kraken was highly suspicious.
