# Gallas Devadas

## Overview

Gallas Devadas was a writer who documented historical anecdotes about the [[Kraken]]. These writings provided historical context about the creature's behavior and impact over time.

## Historical Documentation

Gallas Devadas authored historical anecdotes about the Kraken that were consulted by military strategists preparing for the 392 AC assault on the Leviathan.

In 392 AC, [[Sigismar Synd]] spent hours poring over everything written about the Kraken, including the historical anecdotes of Gallas Devadas alongside the horrific accounts of [[Lucas Tarr]], searching for weaknesses or advantages to exploit in the planned military operation.

The historical perspective provided by Gallas Devadas complemented the more visceral accounts of other writers, offering military planners a fuller understanding of the Kraken's patterns and capabilities across different time periods.

## Relationships

- **[[Kraken]]**: Subject of the historical anecdotes
- **[[Lucas Tarr]]**: Fellow writer whose horrific accounts were studied alongside Gallas Devadas's historical anecdotes
- **[[Sigismar Synd]]**: Paladin who studied Gallas Devadas's historical anecdotes in preparation for the Kraken assault (392 AC)
