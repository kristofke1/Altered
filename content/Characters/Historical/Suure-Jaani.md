Suure-Jaani was a Master Archivist of the Ordis Faction who authored scholarly works on historical and cultural topics.

## Scholarly Work

Suure-Jaani authored the "Compendium IX of Hyperborean Folklore," a thick volume documenting stories, myths, and cultural knowledge from the Hyperborean tradition. This work was part of the Sanctum's extensive collection of preserved knowledge from various cultures and time periods.

In 392 AC, the Compendium was consulted by Yzmir Initiate Suha during her investigation of the Kraken. An Ordis cleric retrieved the volume at Suha's request while she researched discrepancies in the official accounts of the Leviathan's defeat. The work represented the type of comprehensive cultural and historical documentation maintained by the Sanctum's archivists.
