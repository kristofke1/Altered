# Calfuray

## Overview

Calfuray was a philosopher who authored Suspicionis Liber, a treatise on the nature of ideas and mortals' relationship with them. The work appears relevant to [[Yzmir]] practitioners who study [[The Aether]] and immaterial concepts.
