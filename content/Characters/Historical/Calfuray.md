# Calfuray

Calfuray is a philosopher who authored Suspicionis Liber, a treatise on the nature of ideas and mortals' relationship with them.

## Works

### Suspicionis Liber
Calfuray wrote Suspicionis Liber a philosophical treatise examining the nature of ideas and mortals' relationship with them. The work appears relevant to [[Yzmir]] practitioners who study [[The Aether|Aether]] and immaterial concepts.
