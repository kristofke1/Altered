# Yong-Su Tenzing

## Overview

Yong-Su Tenzing was a [[Muna]] Alterer who created the [[Mugen]], the sacred bridge linking [[Nisan]] and [[Kirighai]]. After death, Yong-Su became an [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] and continues to be honored by the [[Muna]].

## Creation of the Mugen

Yong-Su built the [[Mugen]] bridge when a [[The Tumult|Tumult]] singularity prevented [[Arkaster]] from being replenished with food and essential resources. The bridge provided a vital land route to the agricultural regions of [[Kirighai]] and the [[Katkera]], allowing supplies to reach the capital. What began as a practical solution became one of the most sacred sites in [[Asgartha]], with nineteen arched sections spanning numerous islets, each marked by a torii gate and altar.

## Veneration

Stylized statues of Yong-Su can be found within the [[Bark Refuge]] sanctuary inside [[The Spindle|the Spindle Tree]], where offerings of flower garlands and trinkets are placed. The [[Muna]] honor Yong-Su alongside other nature deities including [[Inari]], [[Cernunnos]], [[Freyja]], and [[Tlaloc]].

## Timeline

### 391 AC - Ryukkōsai Festival Manifestation

During the Ryukkōsai festival, [[Rin]] followed a procession crossing the [[Mugen]] bridge. Yong-Su Tenzing's [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] manifested floating in the air with lotus flowers blooming beneath each step.
