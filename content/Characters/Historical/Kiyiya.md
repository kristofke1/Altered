# Kiyiya

## Overview

Kiyiya was one of the nine disciples chosen by [[Niavhe]] to become keepers of her wisdom, dedicating body and soul to nurturing [[The Skein]]'s blossoming. Among the Nine, Kiyiya could make flames dance like no one else.

Together with Niavhe, Kiyiya and the eight others were granted permission by [[Kaibara]] to enter the [[Katkera]]—and even to dwell there.

## Relationships

- **[[Niavhe]]**: Founder of the Muna who chose Kiyiya as one of the Nine Keepers
- **[[Eru]]**: Fellow disciple among the Nine
- **[[Sewit]]**: Fellow disciple among the Nine
- **[[Kaibara]]**: Leviathan who granted permission for the Nine to dwell in Katkera
- **[[Katkera]]**: Sacred forest where Kiyiya dwelt
