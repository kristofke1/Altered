# Sewit

## Overview

Sewit was one of the nine disciples chosen by [[Niavhe]] to become keepers of her wisdom, dedicating body and soul to nurturing [[The Skein]]'s blossoming. Among the Nine, Sewit communed most easily with [[The Spindle]], the world tree at the heart of the [[Katkera]].

Together with Niavhe, Sewit and the eight others were granted permission by [[Kaibara]] to enter the [[Katkera]]—and even to dwell there.

## Relationships

- **[[Niavhe]]**: Founder of the Muna who chose Sewit as one of the Nine Keepers
- **[[Eru]]**: Fellow disciple among the Nine
- **[[Kiyiya]]**: Fellow disciple among the Nine
- **[[Kaibara]]**: Leviathan who granted permission for the Nine to dwell in Katkera
- **[[The Spindle]]**: World tree with which Sewit had a special communion
- **[[Katkera]]**: Sacred forest where Sewit dwelt
