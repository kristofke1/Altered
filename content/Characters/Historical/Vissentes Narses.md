# Vissentes Narses

Vissentes Narses was politician and contemporary of Ayxas who wrote attack pamphlet against the third Kuningas.

## Opposition to Ayxas

Vissentes Narses served as opponent of Ayxas during his reign as third Kuningas of Asgartha. As contemporary, Narses witnessed Ayxas's tyrannical regime and persecution of Protectorate inhabitants.

## Attack Pamphlet

Vissentes Narses authored attack pamphlet about Ayxas. Pamphlet documented criticisms and accusations against Kuningas during period when Ayxas turned kingdom into tyrannical regime shedding blood abundantly.

### 390 AC - Pamphlet Recovered by Hasret

Hasret stole scroll from Asterion archives for Sigismar. Scroll was Vissentes Narses's attack pamphlet about Ayxas. As Sigismar read it, he hoped pamphlet would hold clue to help understand why ancestor committed offenses.

Pamphlet had been preserved in Asterion archives, suggesting historical significance of document as record of opposition to Ayxas's rule.
