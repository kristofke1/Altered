# Abelen Sundström

## Overview

Abelen Sundström was a [[Bravos]] explorer celebrated as the most fearless explorer [[Asgartha]] has ever known. Daughter of [[Bravos]] messenger [[Gusta Sundström]], she made her fortune through geographic discoveries and used her wealth and fame to launch further expeditions.

## Background

Accompanied by her [[Lyra]] boatswain [[Maya Vastag]], Abelen discovered an island far off the coast of the Peninsula, which was named [[Abelena]] after her. She also found a new passage between [[Suspira]] and the capital that winds through the [[Undersea]] and connects to [[Abelena]], cutting journey time in half by passing between the reefs. She used her wealth and fame to launch expeditions to the east, where she mapped new shores which she named "[[Keleti]]".

Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] cited Abelen Sundström alongside [[Rifat Asdrubal]] as explorers whose exploits inspired his childhood dream that eventually became the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. Grand Admiral [[Temera Singh]] expressed deep admiration for Sundström's courage during a conversation with the Basileus about the Endeavor. The tale of Abelen, Maya, and [[Kacchena]] is remembered as a legendary story alongside other famous human-Leviathan relationships such as [[Niavhe]] and [[Kaibara]], and [[Nuur Tamrat]] and [[Annoba]].

## Timeline

### 271 AC - Sailing the Southern Coast of Caer Oorun

In 271 AC, Abelen was the first to defy the terror of the [[Kraken]] by sailing for several weeks along the southern coast of [[Caer Oorun]] without encountering the giant squid. More than a hundred years before the launch of the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]], her ship the Stormward paved the way for future explorers. It was by relying on her maps that the [[Bravos]] decided to replicate her feat in 327 AC, building [[Cebir]] with [[Ordis]] support in a frozen area where pack ice and icebergs could serve as natural defenses against the sea monster.
