# Abelen Sundström

## Overview

Abelen Sundström was a [[Bravos]] explorer celebrated as the most fearless explorer [[Asgartha]] has ever known. Daughter of [[Bravos]] messenger [[Gusta Sundström]], she made her fortune through geographic discoveries and used her wealth and fame to launch further expeditions.

## Discoveries

Accompanied by her [[Lyra]] boatswain [[Maya Vastag]], Abelen discovered an island far off the coast of the Peninsula, which was named [[Abelena]] after her. She also found a new passage between [[Suspira]] and the capital that winds through the [[Undersea]] and connects to [[Abelena]], cutting journey time in half by passing between the reefs.

She used her wealth and fame to launch expeditions to the east, where she mapped new shores which she named "[[Keleti]]".

## Legacy

Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]] cited Abelen Sundström alongside [[Rifat Asdrubal]] as explorers whose exploits inspired his childhood dream that eventually became the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]]. Grand Admiral [[Temera Singh]] stated that possessing even a fraction of Sundström's courage would content her.
