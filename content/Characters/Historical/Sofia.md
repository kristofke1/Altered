# Sofia

## Overview

Sofia was the living soul of the [[City of Scholars]] (originally called "Serdica" or "Sofia" before [[The Confluence]]), manifested through the collective worship of its inhabitants and awakened by the [[Sap]]. [[Yzmir]] theorists identified the Sap as a "vital fluid" capable of giving life, and it flowed through the city like blood—powering streetlights, ventilation systems, and machinery. Sofia manifested in the form of a joyful, mischievous, endlessly curious child shaped from golden light. Wall frescos throughout [[The Scholars' Tomb]] depict her in idealized adult form as a tall, serene woman with honey-gold features.

## The Golden Age

During the City of Scholars' height, Sofia watched over the people, her childlike form wandering the rooftops after dusk and casting her innate glow across the skyline. The inhabitants venerated her, and this worship sustained her existence. She was intrinsically connected to the world-tree and its Sap—the lifeblood of the civilization. The frescos depict scenes of inexhaustible happiness, love, and freedom under her guidance.

## The Fall

When the inhabitants over-consumed the Sap, draining the world-tree until it died, Sofia began to starve. As the City's soul, she required sustenance—and with the Sap depleted, she turned to the only remaining source: the memories and psychic essence of those around her. She became [[The Hunger]].

The people who had once worshipped her fled in terror from what their goddess had become. They sealed the remaining Sap reserves in deep cisterns, retreated to upper levels, and eventually abandoned the City entirely. The presence of stelae and coffins in upper chambers suggests some chose to die rather than leave their home.

## Ages of Isolation

Left utterly alone for ages—no worshippers to sustain her divine form, no Sap, nothing but darkness—Sofia's monstrous Hunger form consumed her original joyful nature. When [[Fen]] reached her consciousness in 393 AC, Sofia appeared as a little girl with honey-gold skin and hair, curled in fetal position within a golden bubble, dormant and dreaming. Her soul had been poisoned by bitterness, abandonment, and incomprehension, though deep within remained joyful recollections from the golden age.

## Liberation and Legacy

In 393 AC, [[Fen]] and [[Teija]] attempted psychic contact with The Hunger, seeking to reach whoever remained within. Fen extended mental tendrils into the creature's consciousness, breaking through layers of suffering and caustic memories. Using emotions of reunion, loyalty, and presence as armor against the entity's grief and madness, Fen discovered Sofia and called her to wake.

Sofia opened her eyes and, after a hesitant moment, reached back toward Fen. When asked her name, Sofia remembered and spoke it clearly.

During The Hunger's destruction, a column of light released Sofia's memories into the sky—impressions of joy, freedom, laughter, and happiness, including the image of a little girl's fingers plucking a forget-me-not bloom. These memory-fragments then dissolved like mist before dawn.

[[Ordis]] historians had hoped to open a dialogue with Sofia, to learn more about her, the City, and its inhabitants—perhaps to see her wandering the rooftops again, casting her glow across the skyline. The destruction of the [[Tesseracts|memory blocks]] ended that possibility.

In her honor, the relay outpost at [[The Screed]] was renamed "Sofia" and entrusted to the [[Bravos]] for protection and the [[Muna]] for provisions (September 17, 393 AC).
