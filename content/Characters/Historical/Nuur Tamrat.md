# Nuur Tamrat

## Overview

Nuur Tamrat was a legendary [[Bravos]] warrior who defeated the [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] [[Annoba]] using a cannon. The defeat stands as one of the great [[Bravos]] victories against Leviathans.

## Defeat of Annoba

When Nuur Tamrat took down [[Annoba]], it required an army of butchers to dismember the creature's carcass and clear the bay. This demonstrated the enormous scale of the Leviathan and established that defeated Leviathans leave substantial physical remains.

[[Yzmir]] Initiate [[Suha]] cited Nuur Tamrat's defeat of [[Annoba]] in 392 AC during her investigation into the [[The Kraken|Kraken]]. She noted that unlike [[Annoba]], which left a massive carcass requiring extensive cleanup, the Kraken supposedly left no physical remains after being vaporized—a discrepancy that contributed to her suspicions about the official account.
