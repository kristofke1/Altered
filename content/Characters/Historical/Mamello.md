# Mamello

## Overview

Mamello was a taxonomist responsible for naming the Leviathan [[Cingula]] in reference to a species of centipede. This taxonomic work represented standard practice for cataloging the great beasts that threatened [[Asgartha]].

## Taxonomic Work

Mamello's naming of Cingula established the precedent for scholarly documentation of Leviathan classifications. The documentation was later cited by [[Yzmir]] Initiate [[Suha]] in 392 AC during her investigation of the [[Kraken]]. Suha used Mamello's work as evidence that proper taxonomic records should exist for all major Leviathans, noting that the absence of such documentation for the Kraken was highly suspicious.
