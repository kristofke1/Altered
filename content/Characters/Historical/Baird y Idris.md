# Baird y Idris

Baird y Idris was last Shepherd of Sunset Tribe. He maintained detailed logbook recording coordinates and location of City of Scholars.

## Shepherd Role

Baird y Idris served as Shepherd of Sunset Tribe, one of Eight Tribes during Great Wandering. As last Shepherd, he held position during tribe's final period before arriving in Asgartha or disbanding.

## Logbook

Baird y Idris kept logbook religiously noting navigational information every day. Entries included:
- Azimuths (directional bearings)
- Coordinates (positional data)
- Location of City of Scholars

Logbook became old parchment with ink faded with time. Document preserved in cylindrical case made from metal and leather.

## City of Scholars Knowledge

Baird y Idris's annals provide the first and only historical references to the [[City of Scholars]]. He describes it as an underground metropolis nestled beneath a tall mountain, well sheltered from [[The Tumult]] currents. The city once served as a stopover for western [[Tumult Nomads]] traveling toward [[Asgartha]].

Baird y Idris offered several theories about the city's origins: he proposed it was a nerve center of the World Before, encircled by rocky foothills. Under pressure of The Tumult, these surrounding mountains allegedly surged over the city in waves and solidified, trapping the population in a stone casing shaped like frozen water. His logbook contained precise coordinate information that proved crucial for planning the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]].

### 367 AC - Logbook Shown to Waru

Quetzalcoatl opened chest and took out cylindrical case. He opened it and lifted out old parchment, placed it on table, and carefully unfurled it with talons. Document was logbook of Baird y Idris.

Waru put glasses on and slipped on gloves to avoid damaging precious relic. He read document where ink had faded. Every day Baird religiously noted azimuths, coordinates, and location of City of Scholars.

## Historical Significance

Baird y Idris's meticulous record-keeping preserved knowledge of City of Scholars location through generations. Logbook's survival in Sanctum's forbidden library enabled Rediscovery Endeavor to target specific destination rather than exploring blindly.
