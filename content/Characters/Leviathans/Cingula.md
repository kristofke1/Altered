## Overview

Cingula is a Leviathan that was formally named by the taxonomist [[Mamello]], who chose the name in reference to a species of centipede. This suggests the creature may have multiple segments or limbs similar to a centipede's appearance.

## Historical Significance

### Attack on Basira's Family

Cingula was responsible for taking the family of Basira al-Arshad, a Bravos warrior who later became an Exalt in the Expeditionary Corps. This personal loss drove Basira's desire for vengeance against Leviathans.

### Parallel to Afanas's Loss

Afanas Korovinovich, a Yzmir Battle Mage, noted the similarity between Basira's loss to Cingula and his own family being taken by the Kraken. His father had been a Bravos Leviathan harpooner. This shared tragedy created a bond between the two Exalts in their pursuit of the Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock.

## Naming Convention

The fact that Mamello formally named Cingula establishes the taxonomist tradition of giving Leviathans specific designations, unlike the Kraken whose original namer remains unknown—a detail that raised suspicions among scholars.
