# Koi

## Overview

Koi is a pisciform [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] inhabiting the [[Thessalian Quadrant]] of the [[Cloud Realm]]. Classified as moderate risk by the [[Expeditionary Corps]], Koi is gentle and peaceful by nature but its playful tendencies can inadvertently endanger fleet vessels.

## Physical Appearance

Koi resembles a giant koi carp approximately 1,400 meters in length. It possesses two pairs of long, slender fins resembling sails, a mouth adorned with two long appendages drifting like catfish whiskers, and a caudal fin composed of four fine petal-shaped lobes. Its coloration ranges from white to milky pink with scaly skin. The iridescent texture of its body reflects clouds and water surfaces, granting natural camouflage in the expanses it frequents.

## Behavior

Koi pays little heed to human activity and displays no malice. However, its playful tendencies pose risks to fleet operations—it will follow ships and inadvertently jostle them with often abrupt movements. At worst, it shows clumsiness rather than aggression.
