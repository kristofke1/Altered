## Overview

Annoba is a Leviathan that was defeated by the legendary warrior Nuur Tamrat in an earlier age. The creature's defeat stands as one of the great Bravos victories against Leviathans.

## Historical Significance

### Defeat by Nuur Tamrat

When Nuur Tamrat took down Annoba, the aftermath required an army of butchers to dismember the creature's carcass and clear the bay. This massive undertaking demonstrated the enormous scale of the Leviathan and established the precedent that defeated Leviathans leave substantial physical remains.
