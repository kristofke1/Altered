# Meander

## Overview

Meander is a Leviathan known as the Serpent Leviathan. Meander lives in symbiosis with [[Ventauri Clan]], one of the [[Lyra]] Clans.

## Symbiotic Arrangement

[[Kaibara]] tolerates Meander on its territory. The [[Lyra]] negotiated with the Whale Leviathan to allow Meander to continue roaming the Peninsula, in exchange for ensuring that it does not cause trouble within the [[Oases|Oasis]].
