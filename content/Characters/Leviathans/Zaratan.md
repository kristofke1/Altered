# Zaratan

## Overview

Zaratan is a reptilian [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] inhabiting the [[Rhodopean Quadrant]] of the [[Cloud Realm]]. Classified as low risk by the [[Expeditionary Corps]], Zaratan is docile, curious, and extremely sociable—behavior that contrasts starkly with its Asgarthan counterpart [[Kacchena]].

## Physical Appearance

Zaratan resembles a massive sea turtle suspended in the sky, approximately 1,500 meters in diameter. Its skin bears a stone-like, chalky hue. The surface of its shell is covered in lush vegetation that serves as nesting ground for a multitude of birds, making it an ecosystem unto itself. Its appearance allows it to go unnoticed in the heavily vegetated environment of the Rhodopean Quadrant.

## Behavior

Despite resembling [[Kacchena]], Zaratan displays contrasting temperament. It shows little mistrust of humans and has encouraged explorers to land upon its shell, positioning itself so ships could easily cast off their moorings. Deep gashes on some of its scales indicate that other [[Leviathans]] have targeted it in the past.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Whiplash Crossing

Zaratan joined a procession of [[Leviathans]] led by [[Halua]] through [[The Whiplash]], creating a low-Tumult slipstream for the [[Expeditionary Corps]] armada. Upon emerging into the cyclone's eye, [[Alelo]] snapped its jaws in defiance at Zaratan before the two parted without conflict.
