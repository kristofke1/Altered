# Scarab

## Overview

Scarab is a Leviathan known as the Beetle Leviathan. Scarab lives in symbiosis with [[Iju Clan]], one of the [[Lyra]] Clans.

## Symbiotic Arrangement

[[Kaibara]] tolerates Scarab on its territory. The [[Lyra]] negotiated with the Whale Leviathan to allow Scarab to continue roaming the Peninsula, in exchange for ensuring that it does not cause trouble within the [[Oases|Oasis]].
