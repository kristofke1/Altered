# Alelo

## Overview

Alelo is a pisciform [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] inhabiting the [[Macedonian Quadrant]] of the [[Cloud Realm]]. Named "demon" in Samoan after a Polynesian legend, Alelo remains the least observed Leviathan by the [[Expeditionary Corps]] due to both its elusive nature and [[Yzmir]] restrictions on further observation.

## Physical Appearance

Alelo resembles a moray eel or common eel in form. Like [[Mana Moths]], Alelo is a hybrid being that straddles the boundary between [[The Aether]] and reality, making it both elusive and potentially far more dangerous than any other Leviathan endemic to the [[Cloud Realm]].

## Behavior

Alelo rarely leaves the [[Macedonian Quadrant]], dwelling among the bones of deceased [[Leviathans]] in what [[Yzmir]] describe as an open-air cemetery. The creature reacts to any [[Alteration]] of reality, no matter how slight. This sensitivity has led [[Yzmir]] to forbid all use of [[Alteration]] within the region—the primary reason for the quadrant's restricted access.

Despite its dangerous classification, Alelo has demonstrated more curiosity than aggression in encounters with the [[Expeditionary Corps]]. During one incursion where it shadowed a convoy, explosive spears proved sufficient to drive it away.

## Timeline

### 393 AC - Whiplash Crossing

Alelo departed the [[Macedonian Quadrant]] to join a procession of [[Leviathans]] led by [[Halua]] through [[The Whiplash]], creating a low-Tumult slipstream for the [[Expeditionary Corps]] armada. Upon emerging into the cyclone's eye, Alelo coiled sharply and snapped its jaws in defiance at [[Zaratan]] before the two parted without conflict.
