## Overview

Cadracal is a Leviathan that was defeated by the Bravos Faction in an earlier age. The victory over this creature is commemorated in Bravos tradition.

## Historical Significance

### Defeat by Bravos

When Cadracal fell, the Bravos warriors stood atop its remains to commemorate the moment of victory. This practice of standing on the defeated Leviathan's carcass became part of Bravos martial tradition, symbolizing their triumph over the massive creatures that threaten humanity.

## Scholarly Significance

Like Annoba, Cadracal's defeat left substantial physical remains that could be stood upon and commemorated. This became a point of comparison for scholars investigating the Kraken's defeat in 392 AC, as the Kraken left no bones or flesh—only questions about its true nature.
