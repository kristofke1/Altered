# Garuda

## Overview

Garuda is a [[Leviathans|Leviathan]] known as the Buzzard Leviathan. Depending on its migrations, Garuda regularly disrupts the peace of the [[Asgartha|Asgarthan]] Peninsula in search of food.

## Physical Description

Garuda possesses six wings and flies over the [[Cierna]]. One of these wings was severed by [[Kasanji]] using a snare of steel cables.

## Behavior

Garuda pursues airships that venture too close to its territory.

## Known Victims

- **[[Saul]]**: Leviathan researcher killed during a reconnaissance mission tracking Garuda beyond [[Abelena Isle]]

## Relationships

- **[[Asgartha]]**: Peninsula Garuda disrupts during migrations
- **[[Cierna]]**: Sea over which Garuda casts its shadow
- **[[Abelena Isle]]**: Location northeast of which Garuda was sighted when Saul tracked it
- **[[Saul]]**: Researcher killed during reconnaissance mission
- **[[Sol]]**: Saul's sister who became a Leviathan hunter after his death
- **[[Kasanji]]**: Hunter who severed one of Garuda's six wings with steel cables
- **[[Kaibara]]**: Asgarthan Leviathan; Garuda is among the hostile Leviathans challenging the peninsula
