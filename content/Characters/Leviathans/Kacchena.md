# Kacchena

## Overview

Kacchena is a Leviathan known as the Moving Island, a Turtle Leviathan that resides in the [[Cierna]]. Kacchena is an ecosystem unto itself.

## Behavior

Kacchena is territorial, defending its domain against intruders. This behavior contrasts with [[Zaratan]], a similar turtle Leviathan inhabiting the [[Rhodopean Quadrant]] of the [[Cloud Realm]], which displays docile and curious behavior toward humans.

## Legendary Association with Maya Vastag

Kacchena is remembered alongside [[Maya Vastag]], the [[Lyra]] boatswain who explored the [[Cierna]] region with [[Abelen Sundström]], in legendary stories passed down through generations. The pairing of Maya and Kacchena appears in tales alongside other famous human-Leviathan relationships such as [[Niavhe]] and [[Kaibara]], and [[Nuur Tamrat]] and [[Annoba]]. The nature of their encounter remains undocumented.

## Relationships

- **[[Cierna]]**: Maritime territory
- **[[Zaratan]]**: Sky turtle Leviathan in [[Cloud Realm]] with contrasting docile behavior
- **[[Maya Vastag]]**: [[Lyra]] explorer with whom Kacchena shares a legendary association
- **[[Abelen Sundström]]**: [[Bravos]] explorer who sailed the [[Cierna]] with Maya Vastag
