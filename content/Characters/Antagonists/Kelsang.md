# Kelsang

Kelsang is body-hopping parasite and member of [[The Perjurers]] ancient conspiracy. He is associated with [[The Monolith]] and infiltrates [[Ordis]] faction.

## True Nature - The Perjurer

Kelsang is member of ancient conspiracy called [[The Perjurers]], body-hopping parasites who possess human hosts across centuries. Like fellow members [[Sylas]] and [[Saskia Averina]], Kelsang serially inhabits different bodies to maintain operations across generations.

## Operational Pattern

### Association with The Monolith

By 392 AC, [[Saskia Averina]] noted that Kelsang would "hole up in [[The Monolith]] as usual" during Expeditionary Corps departure. This suggests established pattern of remaining at Monolith location while other Perjurers undertake field operations.

### Role During Rediscovery Endeavor

While [[Sylas]] and [[Saskia Averina]] planned to infiltrate Expeditionary Corps (392 AC), Kelsang expected to remain behind in [[Asgartha]]. His role appears focused on maintaining conspiracy oversight at [[The Monolith]] rather than joining outward expansion.
