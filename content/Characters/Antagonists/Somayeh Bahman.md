# Somayeh Bahman

## Overview

Somayeh Bahman is a body-hopping parasite and member of [[The Perjurers]] ancient conspiracy. She infiltrates the [[Lyra]] faction, serving as a Chronicler

## Timeline

### 361 AC - Chronicler of the Ventauri Clan

Somayeh served as a chronicler among the [[Ventauri Clan]], using [[Alteration]] to extract stories from ancient frescoes and bas-reliefs through touch. She would summon the ideas contained in images, interpret the scenes that played out before her, then arrange and structure the narratives for oral retelling.

### 393 AC - Elected Basilissa

Somayeh Bahman was elected Basilissa

## Relationships

- **[[Lyra]]**: Faction Somayeh infiltrates as cover
- **[[The Perjurers]]**: Ancient conspiracy of body-hopping parasites Somayeh belongs to
- **Ventauri Clan**: [[Lyra]] clan where Somayeh was part of.
