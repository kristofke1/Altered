# Somayeh Bahman

## Overview

Somayeh Bahman is a member of [[The Perjurers]] conspiracy. She infiltrates the [[Lyra]] faction, serving as memorialist of the [[Ventauri Clan]]. In 393 AC, she was elected fourteenth Basilissa of [[Asgartha]].

## Political Position

Somayeh Bahman advocates for pursuing the [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] and intensifying existing efforts. She believes true resilience stems from Asgartha's ability to reclaim knowledge lost during the [[Coalescence|Confluence]], describing knowledge as "the most precious resource the [[Expeditionary Corps]] must recover." She stated that the Endeavor "is not an earthly initiative—it is a spiritual one" that "must take precedence over all else."

Her candidacy symbolized the schism dividing the [[Lyra]] Clans between those who claim their nature is rooted in journeying and those who seek to preserve their nomadic way of life within the Peninsula.

## Electoral Strategy

Somayeh won the [[393 AC Basileal Elections]] by appealing to emotion rather than reason. She stated that reason serves merely as a varnish people apply to ease their conscience, while emotion, desire, and impulse drive actual choices.

[[Wanjiru]] referred to Somayeh as "The Demagogue."

## Timeline

### 361 AC - Chronicler of the Ventauri Clan

Somayeh served as a chronicler among the [[Ventauri Clan]], using [[Alteration]] to extract stories from ancient frescoes and bas-reliefs through touch. She would summon the ideas contained in images, interpret the scenes that played out before her, then arrange and structure the narratives for oral retelling.

### 393 AC - Elected Basilissa

Somayeh Bahman received 140 votes (31%) in the [[393 AC Basileal Elections]], winning the office of Basilissa.

### 393 AC, October 22 - Investiture Ceremony

Somayeh was sworn in as the fourteenth Basilissa of [[Asgartha]] at [[The Asterion]] in the presence of outgoing Basileus [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari]]. She received the tarnished golden crown containing the Kiss of Aysun, an amethyst gem. That evening in her chambers, she examined portraits of [[Ayxas]] and [[Aysar]] that [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari|Avkan]] had kept as reminders of the temptation and corruption any ruler might succumb to.

### 393 AC, October 22 - Meeting with Shiramun

Following her investiture, Somayeh met privately with [[Shiramun]], Guildmaster of [[The Fulcrum]] and fellow [[The Perjurers|Perjurer]]. She revealed she had spent years training a replacement to watch over the [[Lyra]] Clans from within. Shiramun informed her that [[Kelsang]] had transmigrated to a new host and that the time had come for [[The Perjurers|The Dominion]] to reveal itself to the world.
