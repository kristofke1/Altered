# Shiramun

Shiramun is a member of [[The Perjurers]] conspiracy. He infiltrates the [[Axiom]] faction, serving as Guildmaster of [[The Fulcrum]].

## Physical Description

Shiramun's current host has hair that was once brown but is now almost entirely white, matching his finely combed mustache and goatee. His left arm is a mechanical [[Grafts|Graft]], as is his right leg. He carries a staff with a tip shaped like a complex key.

## Timeline

### 393 AC, October 22 - Meeting with Somayeh Bahman

Shiramun met privately with [[Somayeh Bahman]] in her chambers at [[The Asterion]] following her investiture as Basilissa. He congratulated her on her victory and reported that [[Kelsang]] had transmigrated and chosen a new host. He stated that the Perjurers had spent too long working in isolation without coordination, and that this needed to change. He revealed that additional directives had been received and that the time had come for [[The Perjurers|The Dominion]] to reveal itself to the world.
