# Sylas (Qaasin / Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock)

Sylas is a body-hopping parasite and member of [[The Perjurers]], an ancient conspiracy working to destabilize [[Asgartha]]. He currently inhabits a host body known as "Sylas" and previously inhabited body known as "Qaasin." The "Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock" that [[Afanas Korovinovich]] hunts is also Sylasâ€”an identity he assumed to stage Qaasin's death. As Qaasin, he served as [[Yzmir]] Battlemage who rescued and mentored young Afanas, maintaining deception for decades.

## True Nature - The Perjurer

Sylas is member of ancient conspiracy called [[The Perjurers]], body-hopping parasites who possess human hosts across centuries. His previous host body was called "Qaasin" (pre-356 AC). He faked Qaasin's death in 356 AC by using the identity "Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock" as intermediary persona, allowing him to stage his mentor's murder and assume new identity as "Sylas" while continuing operations. Afanas believed his mentor dead and spent decades hunting the warlock who "killed" him.

Afanas remained unaware of his mentor's true nature for decades, never suspecting that Qaasin, the Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock, and Sylas were all stages in same entity's existenceâ€”a body-hopping parasite manipulating him across his entire life.

### Host Preferences

Sylas prefers hosts with natural permeability to [[The Aether]]. This preference aligns with his [[Yzmir]] operations and magical capabilities.

### Workshop and Appearance

Sylas maintains workshop containing thaumaturgical tools, forbidden relics, and bound tomes. He wears enamel mask that conceals eyes glowing with unnatural hue. In workshop, he coaxes chrysanthemums into bloom.

When he removes mask, his glowing eyes reveal his parasitic nature. Through [[Irises]], he can see parasites moving on and beneath [[Saskia Averina]]'s opaline skin: translucent venomous slugs, multicolored leeches, invasive crustaceans.

## Qaasin Identity - Rescue of Afanas

Old man introduced himself as Qaasin when Afanas regained consciousness. This Yzmir mage had routed Kraken before retrieving young aspirant's unconscious body from icy waters.

Unfortunately rest of crew as well as Afanas's entire family had not survived shipwreck. Qaasin delivered devastating news that all others had perished.

Beside bed splendid night bird never left Afanas's bedside. Its plumage seemed made of very substance of shadows and head covered with mask of lunar pallor. When Afanas asked about it Qaasin revealed it was creature Afanas had saved from waters by freeing it from steel prison.

Just like Afanas bird had lost eye where mask had been split. Just like him seemed to have been battered by life. Afanas named it Senka and set about caring for it.

## Role as Mentor

Qaasin watched over Afanas throughout convalescence. He taught meditations and mantras, and demonstrated [[Yzmir]] Spheres to convince Afanas to embrace the path of magical arts.

## Faked Death and Identity Change

### 356 AC - Staged Death of Qaasin Identity

Afanas investigated Chrysanthemum Cloak Warlock, gathering clues over years. Qaasin warned Afanas that if he continued the hunt, the warlock would take measures.

Afanas and Senka leaped through vortex traveling thousand cable lengths to an estuary. No sign of warlock remained. Afanas found Qaasin's lifeless body sprawled nearby—skin cold and pale.

This staged death allowed the parasite to abandon the Qaasin host body and assume new identity as Sylas. The deception succeeded—Afanas never suspected his mentor was body-hopping parasite who continued operations under different name.

## Impact on Afanas

Staged death of Qaasin identity intensified Afanas's hunt for Chrysanthemum Cloak Warlock. Lost both family and mentor to same dark mage (unknowingly same entity).

36-year gap exists between Qaasin's staged death (356 AC) and Afanas's known activities in Kraken battle (392 AC).

## Chrysanthemum Cloaked Warlock Operations (~303-393 AC)

### Imprisonment of Kuraokami (~303 AC)

Sylas as CCW summoned [[Kuraokami]]'s [[Eidolons|Eidolon]] to base of already-dead world-tree at [[Cais Adarra]] under pretense of regenerating tree. Used "unholy artifice"—possibly lost [[Yzmir]] knowledge buried in [[The Mandates]]—to lure dragon into trunk and turn bark into prison walls.

Method excised [[Kuraokami]] completely from collective unconscious, similar to how Mirror trapped an [[Oneiros]], making dragon's summoning impossible. Technique represented advanced understanding of Eidomantic principles and manipulation of [[The Skein]].

### Triple-Walled Prison System

CCW constructed three-layered containment system:

**First Wall (Matter):** [[The Nilam]] itself—dead world-tree transformed into physical prison with opalescent bark unbreakable from within.

**Second Wall (Climate):** Engineered sudden glaciation using [[Kuraokami]]'s siphoned essence, transforming [[Cais Adarra]] from mild climate to polar wasteland. Pre-glaciation human settlement of several hundred people abandoned site. As [[Kuraokami]] stated: "Cold did not reside here—I brought it with me." CCW weaponized dragon's natural cold manifestation as deterrent.

**Third Wall (Species):** Created and deployed [[Belisenki]] as guardians—giant moth-like species "suited to cold, would do anything to keep outsiders away." Entire ecosystem construction deliberate, with species programmed to defend mountain from human intrusion.

### 305 AC Tsunami - Weaponized Essence

While [[Kuraokami]] remained imprisoned, CCW continuously siphoned dragon's essence over years. In 305 AC—only 2 years after imprisonment began—twisted accumulated power to forge "terrible catastrophe."

Created wave that "swept away everything in its path, surge that devoured mountains," devastating [[Arkaster]] capital. Killed [[Itzamna ruun-Heeren|Basileus Itzamna ruun-Heeren]], much of government, and thousands of civilians. [[Aroro Niski]] sacrificed herself defending capital, her theory about deliberate attack later validated by [[Kuraokami]]'s testimony (393 AC).

Attack resulted in:
- Civilization-threatening disaster
- Thousands killed including [[Itzamna ruun-Heeren|Basileus]] and leadership
- Weaponization of imprisoned dragon's essence
- 2-year timeline from imprisonment to attack

### Duration and Discovery

Operations spanned approximately 90 years (~303-393 AC):
- ~303 AC: Imprisoned [[Kuraokami]]
- 305 AC: Unleashed tsunami weapon
- 303-393 AC: Maintained triple-walled prison system
- 393 AC: Prison shattered by [[Exalts]], truth revealed

This 90-year campaign against [[Asgartha]] occurred while Sylas simultaneously operated as [[Yzmir]] Battlemage, maintained [[The Perjurers]] activities, and orchestrated [[Kraken]] puppet operations.

## Perjurer Conspiracy Activities

### The Kraken Operation

Sylas created [[Kraken]] as puppet for [[The Perjurers]] conspiracy purposes. Puppet allowed [[Saskia Averina]] to carry out experiments unhindered. By 392 AC, Kraken had "outlived its purpose."

During Kraken operations, there was "incident with the Strix" where Sylas failed to deliver something promised to Saskia. He later atoned for this failure.

### Destabilization Mission

After [[Amahle Kalu]]'s betrayal of [[The Perjurers]], Sylas was tasked with destabilizing Asgarthan regime. For nearly century, he worked to undermine stability and prevent outward expansion.

By 392 AC, Sylas claimed to be setting trap for [[Rediscovery Endeavor]] that would "snap shut" with all attention turned outward. He assured [[Saskia Averina]] to have patience.

### Protected Target

Sylas claimed female target in [[Yzmir]] domain as his to deal with, warning Saskia to stay away and consider target off-limits. Target's identity remains unspecified.

## Timeline of Activities

### 392 AC - Meeting with Saskia Averina

[[Saskia Averina]] visited Sylas's workshop, discussing [[The Perjurers]]' plans. Sylas revealed he had not heard from other Perjurers in decades. Saskia indicated "day of reckoning" was approaching and they would need to infiltrate Expeditionary Corps.

Sylas confirmed he intended to join Corps to ensure Asgarthan actions wouldn't undermine conspiracy interests.

### 393 AC - Proposal to Fly Over Tumult Depression

At a meeting in [[Nilam Spires]] observatory led by Satrap [[Zaj Ke Va]], Sylas proposed a solution to the [[Tumult Depression]] blocking the Expeditionary Corps' path southwest. While forecasters argued crossing was impossible due to mutagenic pressure 16 times stronger than any studied Singularity, Sylas suggested flying over the cyclone rather than around or through it.

Sylas noted the availability of four vessels: two [[Lyra]] Sahankas ([[The Ouroboros]] and [[The Wayfarer]]) and two [[Ordis]] flagships ([[Mesektet]], recently recovered with minor damage, and [[Mandjet]], with repairs nearly complete). He urged the assembly to advise Admiral [[Temera Singh]] to request reinforcements—troops, supplies, and resources—while [[Avkan ruun-Heshkari|Avkan]] remained in power before elections, warning that "the future is uncertain."

To persuade the Satrap, Sylas argued that flying over the cyclone's eye would provide [[Yzmir]] a "front-row seat to deepen understanding of the Tumult." The Satrap agreed to study the proposal further.

After the meeting, Sylas approached [[Moyo Chibuye]] cryptically, implying with a knowing smile that Moyo would "find out soon enough" what their bond with [[Silk]] meant—hinting at a potential [[The Musubi|Musubi]] ceremony.

### 393 AC - Observing Sap-Enhanced Sigil Session

Sylas observed a [[Gephyromancy]] Sigil engraver at [[Nilam Spires]] experimenting with [[Sap]] in the tattooing process, watching the engraver create emulsions from oil, pigments, and ideas from [[The Empyrean]], incorporate Sap which turned the liquid purple, and apply permanent Sigils to his own flesh that glowed blue phosphorescent and grafted onto his being unlike traditional fading Sigils, which Sylas sensed as unnatural and dangerous.
